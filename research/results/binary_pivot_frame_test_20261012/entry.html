The conditional frame law

Fix numerical binary matrices \(A\) of shape \(m\times n\) and \(B\) of shape \(n\times m\). Select \(k\) outer rows, \(1\le k<n\), and write \(R\) for that \(k\times n\) row matrix of \(A\). Choose \(U\) uniformly in \(\mathrm{GL}_n(2)\), put \[RU=[M\ C],\quad W_U=\det M,\quad X_U=\operatorname{adj}(M)C,\quad V_U=U(I+N_{X_U}),\] where \(N_X\) has the upper-right rectangular support of the preceding gauge. The output pair is \((AV_U,V_U^{-1}B)\). The shear is self-inverse even when \(M\) is singular, so this pair is defined for every \(U\).

Lemma (pivot frame fibers). If \(\operatorname{rank}R<k\), no frame has \(W_U=1\). Otherwise \[\Pr(W_U=1)= \frac{|\mathrm{GL}_k(2)|\,2^{k(n-k)}}{\prod_{i=0}^{k-1}(2^n-2^i)} =\frac{\prod_{i=0}^{k-1}(1-2^{i-k})}{\prod_{i=0}^{k-1}(1-2^{i-n})}.\tag{1}\] Conditional on success, \(V_U\) is uniform on the invertible matrices \(V\) such that \(RV=[M_V\ 0]\), with \(M_V\) invertible. Each such \(V\) has exactly \(2^{k(n-k)}\) successful preimages.

Proof. For full-row-rank \(R\), the matrices \(RU\) range over all full-row-rank \(k\times n\) matrices, with equal stabilizer sizes. There are \(\prod_{i<k}(2^n-2^i)\) such matrices, and exactly \(|\mathrm{GL}_k(2)|2^{k(n-k)}\) have an invertible first block. This proves (1). On success, \(\operatorname{adj}(M)=M^{-1}\), so \(RV_U=[M\ 0]\). Conversely fix such a \(V\). For each rectangular binary \(Z\), set \(U=V(I+N_Z)\). Then \(RU=[M_V\ M_VZ]\), \(X_U=Z\), and \(V_U=V\). Every preimage has this form, by solving \(V=U(I+N_{X_U})\), and distinct \(Z\) give distinct \(U\). This proves the fiber count and conditional uniformity. Rank deficiency makes every \(M\) singular. \(\square\)

Binary-sum consequence. For any \(\mathbb F_2\)-valued function \(f\) of the output pair, \[\sum_{U\in\mathrm{GL}_n(2)} W_U f(AV_U,V_U^{-1}B)=0.\tag{2}\] Indeed unsuccessful terms vanish, and each successful normalized \(V\) occurs an even \(2^{k(n-k)}\) times with identical arguments of \(f\). When \(R\) is rank deficient all terms vanish. This is a pointwise identity on the ambient matrix cube. No low-degree Boolean certificate for the sum is asserted. A real conditional expectation exists, but cannot be replaced by this binary sum. Distinct \(V\)'s may yield the same output pair; the output has the pushforward law, not an asserted uniform law on distinct pairs.

Symbolic source scope. For each fixed \(U\), the map \[\tau_U(A)=AU(I+N_{X_U}),\qquad \tau_U(B)=(I+N_{X_U})U^{-1}B\] is the predecessor's degree-\(k+1\) polynomial substitution after a constant linear basis change. It fixes \(AB\), fixes every fresh coefficient variable, and retains every shared input occurrence. Weighted replay keeps \(k+(k+1)B_{\rm src}\), including any earlier boundary cost in the original \(B_{\rm src}\). The class of \(W_U\) survives whenever \(2\le D\), \(k\le D\), and \(n-k\ge2D-1\): undo the fixed basis change and row permutation, then apply the identity-frame restriction from the predecessor. Linear Boolean images have degree-two certificates, so this does not increase the stated \(D\). This survival is not a satisfying-point probability for the inconsistent matrix base.

A minor-weighted repair

Work formally over \(\mathbb F_2\). Let \(\Gamma_{m,n}\) be the Boolean matrix equations \(AB=I_m\), with \(m>n\). Fix an outer-row set \(I\) of size \(k\), and use the same outer indices for columns of \(B\). For each inner-index set \(S\) of size \(k\), in increasing order, put \[p_S=\det A_{I,S}\det B_{S,I}.\] Let \(\sigma_S\) be the preceding rectangular gauge after moving \(I,S\) first by permutations and then returning the output to its original coordinate labels. Thus on \(\det A_{I,S}=1\), the selected rows of the output \(A\) are zero outside \(S\). Fresh variables remain fixed. Define the linear operator \[\Lambda(f)=\sum_{|S|=k}p_S\sigma_S(f).\tag{3}\] This is an explicit sum of polynomial substitutions, not itself a substitution.

Proposition (minor chart product defect). The identity \(\Lambda(1)-1=0\) has an ordinary rank-axiom certificate through degree \(2k\); also \(\deg\Lambda(f)\le2k+(k+1)\deg f\). Nevertheless, for \(k=1\), \(I=\{1\}\), \(f=A_{11}\), \(g=A_{12}\), \[\Lambda(fg)-\Lambda(f)\Lambda(g)\notin\mathcal C_D(\Gamma_{m,n})\tag{4}\] whenever \(D\ge8\), \(n\ge2D\), \(m>n\). Here \(\mathcal C_D\) is the degree-\(D\) PC consequence space. The defect has ordinary degree at most eight, so (4) is a substantive nonmembership in the stated space.

Unit and degree proof. Cauchy–Binet gives the formal identity \[\sum_{|S|=k}p_S=\det\bigl((AB)_{I,I}\bigr).\] Write \((AB)_{I,I}=I_k+E\), where each entry of \(E\) is an old rank axiom. In the determinant expansion of \(\det(I_k+E)-1\), every summand contains at least one \(E\) entry; assign such an entry as its generator and the remaining factors as its cofactor. There are at most \(k\) degree-two factors, giving the certificate ceiling \(2k\). Each \(p_S\) has degree \(2k\), and each gauge has degree at most \(k+1\), proving the second assertion. No division by the number of charts is used.

Defect proof in the hard quotient. For \(k=1\), \(p_{\{s\}}=A_{1s}B_{s1}\). Put \(r=(1,1,1,0,\ldots,0)\) and \(s=r^{\mathsf T}\), so \(rs=1\) in \(\mathbb F_2\). Choose a matrix \(K\) whose columns form a basis of \(\ker r\). Then \(Q=[s\ K]\) is invertible. Write \(Q^{-1}\) as the first row \(r\) followed by rows \(L\); thus \(LK=I_{n-1}\), \(Ls=0\), \(rK=0\). Substitute \[A=\begin{bmatrix}r\\ A'L\end{bmatrix}, \qquad B=\begin{bmatrix}s&KB'\end{bmatrix},\tag{5}\] where \(A'\) and \(B'\) have shapes \((m-1)\times(n-1)\) and \((n-1)\times(m-1)\). This affine substitution sends \(AB\) literally to \(\operatorname{diag}(1,A'B')\); Boolean axioms have degree-two certificates in the residual Boolean axioms.

Under (5), precisely the first three chart weights are one. In each of those charts the output's first row is the corresponding unit vector, by the gauge identity. Consequently \(\Lambda(f)\) and \(\Lambda(g)\) specialize to one, while \(\Lambda(fg)\) specializes to zero. The defect specializes to one, independently of \(A',B'\). A degree-\(D\) derivation of the defect would therefore give a degree-\(D\) refutation of \(\Gamma_{m-1,n-1}\). The recorded algebraic matrix baseline forbids that: \(m-1>n-1\ge2D-1\), \(D\ge2\). Finally \(\deg\Lambda(f),\deg\Lambda(g)\le4\), \(\deg\Lambda(fg)\le6\), giving the defect ceiling eight. \(\square\)

What this rejects. Formula (3) cannot be used as a homomorphism when replaying source products: the exact product defect is nonzero even modulo the old paid relations. This is a matrix-instance refinement of the warning in the compression-action control, not a refutation of every linear operator or of that source family. A proof-specific linear interpretation could retain the actual product/cofactor actions instead. It would have to prove their identities; unitality gives none of them for free.
