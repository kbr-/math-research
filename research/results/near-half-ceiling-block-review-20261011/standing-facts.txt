PROVED OR IMPORTED RESULTS (working proofs, one reviewer each unless said).
1. Transfer with the ambient rate (thm:bounded-depth-degree-transfer, cor:bounded-depth-size-bound,
   cor:frege-parity-size-bound; conditional on an imported rank theorem, itself reproved here): polynomial-
   length literal CNFs of width w, with auxiliary affine equations over F_2 allowed outside the formula, free
   dimension n, with no PC refutation over F_2 of degree floor(n/2 - s), have no polynomial-size refutation
   with l levels if s + w = o(n/(log n)^(l-2)); depth-d Frege with parity connectives is l = d + c_F >= d+2.
   prop:level-deficit-threshold: this inequality excludes balanced size n^A at l levels only if
   s < n/(4 A^(l-1) (ln n)^(l-2)). prop:constant-fraction-transfer-exponent: degree cn, c < 1/2 constant,
   gives n^e(c) at two levels and nothing beyond polylog at three or more.
2. Thin alternative (thm:level-size-degree-transfer, lem:kruskal-katona-growth): the same conclusion at every
   number of levels, with polylogarithmic degrees, from a closure C_D proper at cutoff D whose standard
   monomials at the levels j of a window [K - delta, K] just below the cutoff number at most binom(X, j),
   X <= 2(K-delta) + 1 + (K-delta+1)/delta. In the cube case (all monomials on n variables) this is near-half
   again. Not known attainable.
3. thm:truncated-kernel-dimension: a proper closure at D has a standard monomial on D-1 coordinates and
   quotient dimension at least 2^(D-1). Both endpoints force every set shattered through degree K to have
   size at most about 2K (entry shattered-dimension-bound).
4. Degree methods on record: boundary expansion (Alekhnovich-Razborov) gives at most about n/4 for narrow
   axioms; the sources read have nothing above a quarter for narrow axioms and a proved quarter ceiling for
   constant-width parity systems in odd characteristic; conditioning lies inside parity-resolution width;
   rank-expansion extensions of the expansion bound are false in three recorded forms; a system containing a
   monomial relaxation is refuted at its top standard degree plus one.
5. Ceilings on candidates: balanced paired modular source excluded in original instances for l >= 3
   (shared-pin ceiling, deficit of order v/k); counting family: two methods give, conditionally, at most
   about a quarter; block cover reduced to random parity clauses.
