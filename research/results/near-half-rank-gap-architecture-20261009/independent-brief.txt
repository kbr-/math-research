Open statements
Near-half Boolean PC family — open. For each fixed relevant approximation depth \(\ell\ge2\), construct a polynomial-size, preferably constant-width unsatisfiable Boolean CNF family on \(v\to\infty\) variables, independent of the proof-size exponent \(A\), with \(1\notin C_D\) over \(\mathbb F_2\) for \(D=\lfloor v/2-s\rfloor\), \(s=o(v/(\log v)^{\ell-2})\). A stronger near-square-root-deficit barrier also suffices. Growing width needs a new complete cost ledger, not automatic application of the fixed-input-degree theorem.
Complete Frege transfer — open. For that same family and every fixed Frege depth and polynomial exponent, verify a source/proof interpretation covered by Pang\'s argument or the moving-cutoff theorem, including encoding length, original axiom and approximation degrees, coefficient domains and coherent source grammar. Derive the superpolynomial bound in formula length.

thm:truncated-kernel-dimension
Over every field, a proper subspace J of the Boolean degree-D space, D>=2, containing all inputs of an unsatisfiable system and closed under variable multiplication of all its degree-at-most-D-1 elements, has a standard monomial of degree at least D-1. There is a (D-1)-coordinate Boolean algebra injecting linearly into the quotient, whose degree-j layer has dimension at least binom(D-1,j); total dimension is at least 2^(D-1).
Working two-slack stabilization and standard-divisor proof, including inputs of degree D. Linear freeness is not a full algebra representation or a hardness conclusion.
[Statement and proof](https://kbr.is-a.dev/math-research/branches/near-half-pc-degree/#entry-2026-10-09-kernel-dimension)

thm:filtered-shattering-ceiling
For an unsatisfiable canonical Boolean relation system over F of maximum width w, let rho_F be the largest coordinate set S for which each row monomial x_(S intersect X_a) has no representative of smaller degree on that row, or -1 if a row is empty. Then ordinary PC degree is at most max(w,2,rho_F+2), and rho_F is at most the old local projection-shattering number.
Working proof after medium review. Uses degree-compatible standardness of the cutoff kernel, including lower representatives in outside variables, plus original-width canonical derivation and final-line reuse. Equivalent row-retraction condition is local, not a global module assumption.
[Statement and proof](https://kbr.is-a.dev/math-research/branches/near-half-pc-degree/#entry-2026-10-09-filtered-specification)

prop:filtered-guarded-witness
The existing guarded width-five minimal UNSAT family on v=2N+1 variables has rho_F=v-1 over every field, retaining linear size, essential scopes, occurrence and branching conditions, no Horn polarity, full binary affine row hulls and positive-linear PC degree. Its row-specific filtered retractions fail termwise consistency on the existing identity (1-t)fg+tfg=fg through degree at most nine.
Working refinement after review. Constant guard sections witness local filtration; full selected algebra is not preserved by joint consequences. The old guarded product and positive-linear bound are explicitly reused, not claimed new.
[Statement and proof](https://kbr.is-a.dev/math-research/branches/near-half-pc-degree/#entry-2026-10-09-filtered-specification)

thm:filtered-row-retraction
For any field and Boolean relation R on disjoint retained S and discarded P, an F[S]-linear unital map from the full Boolean algebra to F[S] killing I(R) and preserving degree exists iff x_S has no representative of degree below |S| on R. Equivalently a supported trace weight has degree at most |P| and coefficient x_P equal to one.
Working proof after medium review; signed top-coefficient trace works in all characteristics and covers empty coordinate sets/relations. The map is linear and module-linear, not necessarily multiplicative.
[Statement and proof](https://kbr.is-a.dev/math-research/branches/near-half-pc-degree/#entry-2026-10-09-filtered-row-retraction)

thm:coefficient-fibre-barrier
A degree-D operator certificate over any field, with selected variables acting as a full Boolean coefficient algebra and all other variable operators linear over that algebra, gives uncharged degree-D certificates on every assignment fibre carrying nonzero unit. Each such fibre has dimension at least 2^(D-1); injectivity on the full selected-coordinate algebra implies all fibres survive and total dimension at least 2^(|S|+D-1).
Working fibre projection and truncated-kernel argument; outside operators need not commute or be idempotent globally. Conditional compatible sections alone do not satisfy the certificate hypotheses.
[Statement and proof](https://kbr.is-a.dev/math-research/branches/near-half-pc-degree/#entry-2026-10-09-coefficient-sections)

thm:conditional-modular-sections
For v=l2^l,k=16l,m=9v/k and prime q between v^2 and 2v^2, nine random partitions with uniform coefficients and central targets have all previous source/core properties with 2..9 terminal occurrences and O(n^17) encoding. With high probability some S has size at least v/2-9v exp(-l/64), and every pin on S leaves every row affine-full even after two further pins. Residual overlap moment maps are onto, giving compatible mass-one sections with local coefficient degrees at most k.
Working unbounded source modification with all actual variables paid. Width rises from 8l to 16l and encoding exponent from9 to17. Large S is asserted only in the original source. Pointwise section operators remain excluded as PC certificates; no degree-one filtered transport, near-half hardness or growing-width Frege transfer.
[Statement and proof](https://kbr.is-a.dev/math-research/branches/near-half-pc-degree/#entry-2026-10-09-parity-sections)

thm:independent-colour-degree-ceiling
For a non-four-colourable finite simple graph on N nonisolated vertices and any independent set I, compact two-bit canonical four-colouring has ordinary characteristic-two PC degree at most floor(3(N-|I|)/2)+2.
Working proof after review and a complete two-stage certificate. Star determinants derive through degree4; first-bit pin costs b+4, then final weighted restricted inputs are reused at b+min(|B0|,|B1|)+2. No flattened original-input NS ceiling or polynomial proof-size bound asserted.
[Statement and proof](https://kbr.is-a.dev/math-research/branches/near-half-pc-degree/#entry-2026-10-09-independent-colour-ceiling)

cor:near-half-rank-gap
At fixed odd modulus q and bounded original width, a canonical field-consistent Boolean-empty family with barrier through D=floor(v/2-s_v), s_v=o(v), eventually has t>=(D-2-sqrt(2q(q+1)v))/(2q-1), hence t>=v/[2(2q-1)]-o(v) and row rank r<=(q-1)v/(2q-1)+o(v).
Working necessary structural restriction excluding the whole rank-half fixed-modulus regime. A lower-rank, linear-deficit replacement remains provisional because its joint radius/projection/separator/formula requirements lack an unbounded witness.
