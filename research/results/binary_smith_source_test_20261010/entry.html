The minimal-precision aggregate has only one nonzero layer

Write \(A_s=(\mathbb Z/2^s)[\zeta]/(\zeta^2+\zeta+1)\), so \(A_s/(2)=\mathbb F_4\); an element is a unit exactly when its reduction is nonzero. Work with coordinate matrices after expanding in the fixed free character-class basis.

Lemma (top layer rank). Let \(v\ge0\) and let a rectangular matrix \(R\) over \(A_{v+1}\) have every entry in \(2^vA_{v+1}\). Let \(M=\operatorname{def}_v(R)\) and \(r=\operatorname{rank}_{\mathbb F_4}M\). Then invertible row and column operations over \(A_{v+1}\) transform \(R\) to a rectangular diagonal matrix with exactly \(r\) entries \(2^v\), and all other entries zero. In particular its nonzero local Smith valuations are all \(v\), with multiplicity exactly \(r\).

Proof. Choose a matrix \(B\) with \(R=2^vB\); its reduction is the well-defined \(M\). Gaussian elimination over \(\mathbb F_4\) gives invertible \(P_0,Q_0\) with \(P_0MQ_0=\operatorname{diag}(I_r,0)\). Lift those matrices entrywise. Their determinants reduce to nonzero elements, so their lifts \(P,Q\) are invertible over \(A_{v+1}\). The difference \(PBQ-\operatorname{diag}(I_r,0)\) is entrywise even. Multiplication by \(2^v\) kills it, proving the asserted form. Conversely invertible operations reduce to invertible operations on \(M\), so the multiplicity cannot change. \(\square\)

A selected domain needs no new generic primitivity hypothesis. If an old weighted multiplication matrix reduces to rank \(H\), choose \(H\) independent columns and \(H\) rows giving a nonzero minor. The same minor in any lift is a unit. Its inverse composed with projection to those rows is a left inverse for the chosen column map. Thus these columns give a split free copy of \(A_s^H\), using the same degree-bounded polynomial preimages and no new polynomial degree. The full lifted weight image can have additional torsion; it need not be declared free. Apply every error to the same selected columns.

For the cleared path aggregate in the deflation theorem, the lemma says that asking for fewer than \(H\) nonzero top-layer Smith entries is exactly asking for binary error rank below \(H\). It is not a new estimate. Higher precision may reveal additional valuations, but they do not determine the unit count without a separate source argument. A real source class has only unit pivots

Corollary (integral conjunction rank). In the recorded free charged character module over \(A_s\), multiplication by a label conjunction \(L_{T,a}\) on the degree-\(K\) class domain, with \(K+|T|\le D\), is either zero for incompatible local cut labels, or has Smith form consisting of \(q_T(K)\) units and zeroes. Here \(q_T(K)\) is the same cut-space-plus-coordinate orbit count as in the field-valued conjunction-rank theorem. This holds at every \(s\ge1\); no degree cost grows with precision.

Proof. Use the integral one-hot normalization, so the conjunction expansion is \(3^{-|T|}\sum_{\beta\in\mathbb F_3^T}\zeta^{-a\cdot\beta}m_\beta\). Collect terms with the same charged class exactly as in the earlier proof. The sum of a nontrivial character of a ternary vector space is zero, since \(1+\zeta+\zeta^2=0\); the trivial sum is a power of three, a unit in \(A_s\). Thus incompatible columns vanish, while compatible columns have unit coefficients on an entire orbit. Columns in each orbit are proportional by units, using the same small cut witnesses through \(K+|T|\). Distinct orbits have disjoint supports.

Choose one column per reached orbit and one of its rows. These rows give a diagonal unit minor. Scale its entries to one; eliminate the other rows in each orbit using that pivot, then eliminate the other proportional columns. This gives exactly \(q_T(K)\) unit diagonal entries and zeroes. All are operations on a fixed finite free matrix, not multiplications in a global charged algebra. \(\square\)

This recovers the same conjunction ranks over the residue field. It does not assert primitivity for arbitrary mixed error tuples or for multiplication by an arbitrary weight. Structured source relations are essential for the orbit argument.

Two natural lifts of one mixed source error

Proposition (source lift torsion). In any recorded charged graph window containing two edges \(e,f\) from a spanning-tree complement, let \(z=x_{e,a}\), \(t=x_{f,b}\), and take the actual binary input \(g=z+t\). Choose an accuracy-one complete block's coefficient zero, so its companion is \(g\), its field image is zero, and its original ceiling is \(B=3\). On the common degree-one multiplier domain \(\langle1,z,t\rangle\), compare the ordinary integer lift \(g_{\rm ord}=z+t\) with its Boolean-value lift \(g_{\rm bit}=z+t-2zt\). At every \(s\ge2\), their multiplication matrices in the free \(A_s\) quotient have Smith forms \[ \operatorname{diag}(1,1,2)\quad\text{and}\quad\operatorname{diag}(1,1,0), \] respectively, with any additional rows zero. Both reduce to the same binary error map of rank two, on a domain with a unit rank-three minor. The common original replay margin \(K+B=4\le D\) covers this control.

Proof. The cut space has no nonzero vector supported on these two spanning-tree-complement edges. Their nine local characters therefore occupy independent quotient coordinates. The ternary Fourier evaluation matrix is invertible over \(A_s\): three and differences of distinct third roots are units. It can be used as an invertible output-coordinate change on that nine-dimensional summand, without interpreting the inconsistent global quotient as a point algebra.

Each function here depends only on the two indicator bits \((z,t)\), and all four bit patterns occur among the nine local label assignments. Delete duplicate evaluation rows by invertible row subtraction, leaving four representative patterns. The source domain has evaluation rows \((1,z,t)\), with a unit three-by-three minor. The two multiplication matrices become \[ M_{\rm ord}=\begin{pmatrix}0&0&0\\1&1&0\\1&0&1\\2&2&2\end{pmatrix}, \qquad M_{\rm bit}=\begin{pmatrix}0&0&0\\1&1&0\\1&0&1\\0&0&0\end{pmatrix}. \] The first has a unit two-by-two minor and determinant \(-2\) on its three nonzero rows; pivoting those two units leaves a unit multiple of two. The second has exactly two unit pivots and zero remainder. This proves the displayed forms over every \(A_s\), \(s\ge2\). Both reduce to rank two. Products before local reduction have degree at most three, within the original margin four. The two input lifts agree modulo two but differ by \(2zt\). \(\square\)

Both lifts are legitimate matrices for comparison; this does not identify their ring-PC source presentations. The first is the ordinary coefficient lift used in deflation; the second is the parity-value lift already available from finite-precision replay. Their different positive valuations cannot be called an invariant of the original binary source. Conversely this is not a reason to discard integral methods: choosing a structured lift can lower its characteristic-zero rank. A proof of a useful lift for all actual errors, with its common domain and coherent source budget, is still needed.

The binary control itself is easy: the weight \(1+z+t\) kills \(g\) by Booleanity and survives locally. No new hard-family exclusion follows.
