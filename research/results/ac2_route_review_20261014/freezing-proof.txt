Paid coefficient freezing
Lemma. Work over \(\mathbb F_2\). Let \(J\subseteq\mathbb F_2[x]_{\le D}\) be a proper PC pseudoideal containing the old axioms and the local Boolean one-hot domain equations. Each categorical coordinate has \(q\ge2\) labels. Let \(F_1(x,r),\ldots,F_m(x,r)\) be a finite source's entire retained generator list, including all fresh fields, with original degrees at most \(B\ge2\). An old polynomial interpretation \(\sigma\) fixes \(x\), assigns every fresh variable an old polynomial of degree at most \(T\ge1\), and uses one assignment at every shared occurrence. Suppose \(W\notin J\), \(\deg W\le b\), and \(W\sigma(F_j)\in J\) for every \(j\). Select any subset of fresh variables whose images depend only on a common set \(S\) of \(s\) categorical old coordinates. If \[ b+s+TB\le D, \] then these selected images may all be replaced by binary constants, leaving the other images unchanged, so that the resulting coherent interpretation \(\tau\) satisfies \(W'\tau(F_j)\in J\) for every \(j\), for one \(W'\notin J\) of degree at most \(b+s\). Moreover \(W'=W\chi_a\) for one complete label pattern \(a\) on \(S\). All source fields and original degrees are retained.
Proof: choose a surviving cell. For \(a\in[q]^S\), let \(\chi_a=\prod_{e\in S}x_{e,a_e}\), of degree \(s\). The sum of these indicators equals one modulo the local sum equations through degree \(s\): expand \(\prod_{e\in S}\sum_t x_{e,t}\), then telescope each factor to one. After multiplication by \(W\), this identity fits \(b+s\le D\). If every \(W\chi_a\) belonged to \(J\), linearity and this identity would put \(W\) in \(J\). Choose \(a\) with \(W'=W\chi_a\notin J\). This is a bounded partition identity, not evaluation at a global solution of the inconsistent old system.
Proof: freeze coefficients, not inputs. For each selected image \(\beta_i=\sigma(r_i)\), put \(\lambda_i=\beta_i(a)\in\mathbb F_2\). Local-domain reduction gives \[ \chi_a(\beta_i-\lambda_i)\in J_{\rm local} \quad\text{through degree }s+T. \] Indeed reduce \(\beta_i\) by the sum, idempotence and pair-exclusion equations without increasing degree. Multiplying any remaining monomial by \(\chi_a\) sets its coordinate indicators to their selected values, through degree at most \(s+T\). Define \(\tau(r_i)=\lambda_i\) for these variables and \(\tau(r_i)=\sigma(r_i)\) otherwise.
In each monomial of each original \(F_j\), replace selected factors one occurrence at a time. This writes \(\sigma(F_j)-\tau(F_j)\) as a sum of terms \((\beta_i-\lambda_i)C_i\), where each cofactor has degree at most \(T(B-1)\). Repeated powers are handled as repeated occurrences. Old variables have degree one, bounded by \(T\); untouched earlier maps may be global polynomials. Multiply the local eigenidentity by \(WC_i\). Its certificate fits \(b+s+T+T(B-1)=b+s+TB\le D\), so \(W'(\sigma(F_j)-\tau(F_j))\in J\). Also multiply the assumed membership \(W\sigma(F_j)\in J\) by \(\chi_a\), paying the same bound. Subtract to get \(W'\tau(F_j)\in J\). This applies to fields as well as companions and keeps every shared map coherent. It uses bounded PC absorption, never an unrestricted ideal above \(D\). \(\square\)
