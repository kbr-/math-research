Affine support preserves the real reverse bound

Lemma (affine supported restriction). In the categorical restriction lemma, let the alphabet be a finite field \(F_q\), and replace the uniform full cube by any nonempty affine subspace \(\mathcal A\subseteq F_q^d\). Draw \(Y\) uniformly from \(\mathcal A\), independently of the Bernoulli-\(p\) live set \(T\), and fix \(\rho=Y|_{[d]\setminus T}\). The same \(\theta_q<1\) gives a signed expansion into width-\(b\) categorical CNFs on the conditional affine fiber, with expected absolute coefficient mass at most \((1-\theta_q)^{-1}\). Correlation is taken with uniform measure on that fiber. This is not a full-cube identity, a field-valued expectation or a degree-truncated PC design.

Proof. Conditional on \(T,\rho\), the remaining assignments form a nonempty affine fiber. Projection of its translation space to coordinates \(U\subseteq T\) has some rank \(r\le|U|\). Every compatible assignment on \(U\) therefore has probability exactly \(q^{-r}\ge q^{-|U|}\); incompatible assignments have probability zero. This follows by equal-sized fibers of a linear map, including deterministic coordinates.

Take the previous finite path expansion and marker-mixture construction, which are pointwise identities before restricting their domain. Delete paths whose cylinders do not meet the conditional affine fiber; their terms vanish there. For a remaining path, \(c_h\) is still constant on its cylinder, and \(\omega(P)=\prod_h1/c_h\) is unchanged. Its mass satisfies \[\omega(P)\le \mathbb E\!\left[q^{|U_j|}\mathbf1_{\mathcal Z_P}(Y)\prod_{h=1}^j\frac1{c_{U_h}(Y)}\ \middle|\ T,\rho\right].\] On the cylinder all denominators are positive; elsewhere the term is zero. Summing over compatible paths and then over \(T,\rho\) bounds expected length-\(j\) mass by the same full-assignment expression as before, now with \(Y\) uniform on \(\mathcal A\). For each fixed \(Y=y\), the live set is still independent Bernoulli. The reverse encoding and its one-step weight bound were pointwise in \(y\), so they again give \(\theta_q^j\). Summing proves the mass estimate; triangle inequality proves the conditional-fiber correlation inequality. No assumption of independence between the coordinates of \(Y\), nor between path compatibility and its count, was made. \(\square\)

The construction uses one cylinder-probability bound after conditioning on the entire fixed complement. It does not multiply unjustified independent probabilities along a path. A mixture over a separately revealed affine-support index can be averaged after applying the lemma to each support; an unrevealed arbitrary mixture need not have the same conditional cylinder bound.

A single-defect flow gives a concrete correlated law

Proposition (defect flow restriction). Let \(G=(V,E)\) be a finite connected loopless graph, oriented arbitrarily, and work over a finite field \(F_q\). Let the vertex charges \(c\) have nonzero total \(\sigma=\sum_vc_v\). Fix a root \(r\). The set \[\mathcal A_r=\{y\in F_q^E:\partial y=c-\sigma\mathbf1_r\}\] is a nonempty affine space of dimension \(|E|-|V|+1\). Draw \(Y\) uniformly from it independently of any live edge set \(T\), then fix edges outside \(T\) to their \(Y\)-values. Every connected component of \((V,T)\) not containing \(r\) has total residual charge zero; the root component has total residual charge \(\sigma\). Conditional on the fixed values, the corrected-flow affine fiber has dimension \(|T|-|V|+c(T)\), where \(c(T)\) counts all components, including isolated vertices.

Consequently independent Bernoulli live edges can be used with the preceding real signed bound, without the earlier exponentially many independent bad-vertex events. This is only charge localization. It neither proves that the root component is a hard expander nor makes the affine support satisfy the original root axiom.

Proof. On a connected graph the image of the oriented incidence map is the zero-sum vertex space. One elementary proof chooses a spanning tree, assigns its non-tree edges arbitrarily, then successively solves the tree-edge values from leaves to the root; the final root equation follows from total sum zero. There are \(|E|-|V|+1\) free non-tree labels, proving existence and dimension over every finite field.

Write \(b_v=c_v-\sum_{e\notin T}\partial_{v,e}Y_e\) for the residual charges. Then \[b=\partial_TY_T+\sigma\mathbf1_r.\] Summing over a component of the live graph cancels every live-edge contribution, giving exactly the asserted component totals. The conditional fiber is \(\partial_Tz=b-\sigma\mathbf1_r\), which is nonempty since it contains \(Y_T\). Incidence rank on that graph is \(|V|-c(T)\), by the same tree argument component by component. Rank-nullity gives its dimension. All nonroot components are solvable and can subsequently be fixed to solutions, leaving the root component with nonzero total charge. This last restriction alone does not assert a hard degree bound. \(\square\)

Small live cores. If the maximum graph degree is \(\Delta\) and \(T\) is independent Bernoulli-\(p\), then for every integer \(s\ge1\), \[\Pr(|\mathcal C_r(T)|\ge s)\le\min\{1,(4\Delta p)^{s-1}\}.\] Indeed a connected root component with at least \(s\) vertices contains a rooted tree on exactly \(s\) vertices. There are at most \(4^{s-1}\) plane rooted tree shapes and at most \(\Delta^{s-1}\) edge choices for an embedding from the fixed root. Restrict to injective embeddings; every resulting tree has \(s-1\) distinct edges and survives with probability \(p^{s-1}\). A union bound proves the estimate. Overcounting by plane trees is harmless. For fixed \(\Delta\), the signed lemma's regime \(b/k\to0\), with \(k\ge b+1\) eventually, forces \(p\to0\), so a growing hard core is not typically retained. Conditioning on a large core changes the available mass estimate and is not free; the bound does not rule out an exceptional useful restriction.

The affine support is not the old quotient. Let \(\Omega=\bigcup_r\mathcal A_r\), allowing any or all roots, and let \(V_v\) be the \(0/1\) predicate that vertex \(v\)'s charge fails. Every point of \(\Omega\) has exactly one failed vertex, so \(\sum_vV_v=1\) as functions on \(\Omega\), over any coefficient field. Thus no normalized linear functional on a function space containing one and all these predicates can annihilate every \(V_v\). In one-hot coordinates, \(V_v\) is the sum of its forbidden local pattern monomials, of degree at most \(\deg(v)\). Hence an arbitrary signed mixture of single-defect evaluation functionals cannot supply even the normalized old-axiom annihilation required at that local degree. This does not invalidate the real restriction lemma, and it is not a contradiction to the proper cut-code pseudoideal, which is not the function algebra on \(\Omega\).

A recovered modular seed allows many defects

Lemma (binary slice marginals). Let \(n=2^m-1\), \(k=2^r\), and \(1\le r<m\). Over any characteristic-two field, define \(\Lambda(f)=\sum_{S\subseteq[n],\ |S|=k}f(\mathbf1_S)\). For every Boolean polynomial of degree less than \(k\), \(\Lambda(f)=f(0)\). This is the elementary Lucas zero-marginal mechanism already used for the smaller blocks in one-row bounded-block spanning, not a new binomial-congruence theorem.

Proof. In characteristic two, \((1+z)^n=1+z+\cdots+z^n\), so every \(\binom na\) is odd. Counting pairs of an \(a\)-subset inside a \(k\)-subset gives \[\binom na\binom{n-a}{k-a}=\binom nk\binom ka.\] Thus \(\binom{n-a}{k-a}\equiv\binom ka\pmod2\). For \(1\le a<k\), the latter is zero because \((1+z)^k=1+z^k\). A squarefree monomial on \(a\) variables therefore has \(\Lambda\)-value zero, whereas \(\Lambda(1)=\binom nk=1\). Boolean reduction does not increase degree, proving the assertion. \(\square\)

For ternary edge flows with total charge \(\sigma\ne0\), choose \(\lambda=\sigma/k\in F_3\), which exists since a power of two is nonzero modulo three. For every \(k\)-subset \(S\), the charge vector \(c-\lambda\mathbf1_S\) has total zero and defines a nonempty affine flow space. The seed is normalized and has zero low-order moments on the defect indicators. It does not yet show that averaging flow evaluations annihilates old edge-axiom multiples: those depend on component-sum constraints after edge coordinates are fixed. That lift and its exact relation to the existing cut-code pseudoideal are the concrete next test, not an established new lower bound.
