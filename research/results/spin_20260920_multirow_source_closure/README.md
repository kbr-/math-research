# Shared multirow MP-fan projection

The [full notebook entry](https://kbr.is-a.dev/math-research/#entry-2026-09-20-multirow-source-closure)
contains the exact local projection theorem, its proof from the simultaneous
predicate-lifting theorem, the original-cost calculation and a symbolic
large-board obstruction to automatic cross closure.

No new numerical calculation was needed. The identity
`a_beta(B-C_alpha)-a_alpha(B-C_beta) = a_alpha H_beta-a_beta H_alpha`
is expanded in the record. A single signed matching trade perturbs an otherwise
consistent normalized point-evaluation fan. This preserves lower equations and
same-row boundaries but gives an explicitly nonzero cross contraction, over
every field and at arbitrary old degree on sufficiently large boards.

The claims are local formal moment statements, not a complete ENS source design.
The robust lifting theorem is used with its stated hypotheses; original module
costs and the distinction between known lower equations and the next equations
remain explicit. Metadata validation is preserved in `metadata-check.json`.
Timing and protected command output are in the corresponding session archive.
