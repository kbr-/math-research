- The proof needed K >= 2 Delta - 1 (for X >= K), not K >= Delta.

- "Necessary target" stated a sufficient condition. Point-set count: binom(X, K), not 4^K.
- Goal progress: "advanced" was admissible only with these disclosures; the entry now says "unchanged", since an
  added sufficient condition of unknown attainability does not change what must be proved.
