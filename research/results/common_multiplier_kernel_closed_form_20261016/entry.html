General statement. cor:hole-occupancy-kernels: in the product pigeonhole system over any field, for errors whose products with the window stay below the pigeon axioms' degree (\(K+e\le D<n\), or \(K+e\le n-1\) at \(D=n\)), every common kernel and stacked error rank is the restriction rank of low-degree functions on the hole-respecting assignments to the union of the errors' supports, with an explicit tensor formula when those supports are fixed by the occupancy of a set of holes; at \(K+e=D=n\) membership is membership of the restriction in the span of the indicators of unplaced pigeons.

Hole-occupancy kernels

Setting. Over a field, the product pigeonhole system with \(m\) pigeons and \(n\ge1\) holes in the multilinear ring: hole axioms \(x_{ij}x_{kj}\), \(i\ne k\), and pigeon axioms \(P_i=\prod_j(1-x_{ij})\) of degree \(n\). Let \(C\) be its degree-\(D\) closure with \(D\le n\), and \(Y\subseteq\{0,1\}^{m\times n}\) the hole-respecting assignments, at most one pigeon per hole; \(Y\) is the product over holes of the \(m+1\) states “empty” and “pigeon \(i\)”. Put \(U=V_K/(C\cap V_K)\). Let \(E_1,\ldots,E_s\) be polynomials of degree at most \(e\), \(S_j=\{y\in Y:E_j(y)\ne0\}\) and \(S=\bigcup_jS_j\). Call the range inert when \(K+e\le D<n\) or \(K+e\le n-1\) with \(D=n\).

Statement. (a) In the inert range, restriction to \(Y\) identifies \(U\) with \(V_K|_Y\), the functions on \(Y\) given by polynomials of degree at most \(K\), and the common kernel \(\{[u]\in U:E_ju\in C\ \forall j\}\) with the zero set of the restriction map \(V_K|_Y\to V_K|_S\); the stacked map \(u\mapsto([E_ju])_j\) has rank \(\dim V_K|_S\), and each \(u\mapsto[E_ju]\) rank \(\dim V_K|_{S_j}\). (b) If \(S=A\times Y'\), where \(A\) is a set of states of a set \(T\) of \(t\) holes and \(Y'\) all states of the other \(n-t\) holes, then \(\dim V_K|_S=\sum_{s\le K}\binom{n-t}{s}m^s\,d_A(K-s)\), where \(d_A(a)=\dim V_a|_A\) in the variables of the holes of \(T\); when \(A\) is all states, \(d_A(a)=\sum_{r\le a}\binom trm^r\). (c) In the inert range, for one monomial \(w\) on \(e\) distinct holes the kernel has dimension \(\sum_{k\le K}\bigl[\binom nk-\binom{n-e}k\bigr]m^k\); for the common kernel of \(x_{i0}\), \(i\in\Sigma\), with \(|\Sigma|=\sigma\ge1\) and \(K=1\), it is \(m+1-\sigma\). (d) At \(D=n\), for every polynomial \(c\) of degree at most \(n\), \(c\in C\) exactly when \(c|_Y\) lies in \(P_Y=\operatorname{span}\{1_{Z_i}\}\), \(Z_i\) the points of \(Y\) where pigeon \(i\) is unplaced. So for \(K+e\le n\) and \(K\le n-1\), where \(C\cap V_K=H\cap V_K\) and so \(U\cong V_K|_Y\), the common kernel is \(\{[u]:E_ju|_Y\in P_Y\ \forall j\}\); it contains the classes vanishing on \(S\), and modulo them it injects into \(\bigoplus_jP_Y^{S_j}\), where \(P_Y^{S_j}\) is the subspace of \(P_Y\) of functions vanishing off \(S_j\); hence the stacked rank lies between \(\dim V_K|_S-\sum_j\dim P_Y^{S_j}\) and \(\dim V_K|_S\).

Proof. (a) By lem:top-degree-axiom-inertness (b), the closure \(H\) of the hole axioms is the degree-\(D\) part of the vanishing ideal of \(Y\), and \(C\cap V_{D-1}=H\cap V_{D-1}\); for \(D<n\) no pigeon axiom has degree at most \(D\), so \(C=H\). In the inert range every product \(E_ju\) has degree at most \(K+e\), where \(C\) and \(H\) agree, and \(K\le K+e\) lies there too; so \(E_ju\in C\) exactly when \(E_ju\) vanishes on \(Y\), that is when \(u\) vanishes on \(S_j\), and \(C\cap V_K\) is the set of \(u\in V_K\) vanishing on \(Y\). So \(U\cong V_K|_Y\), and \([u]\) lies in the common kernel exactly when \(u|_S=0\). Restriction \(V_K|_Y\to V_K|_{S_j}\) is onto, so the rank of \(u\mapsto[E_ju]\) is \(\dim V_K|_{S_j}\); likewise for the stacked map with \(S\). This is lem:domain-model-stacked-rank for the point set \(Y\), whose \(B_D\) is \(C\) in this range. (b) The distinct-hole monomials of degree at most \(s\) on the free holes form a basis of the functions on \(Y'\) given by degree-\(\le s\) polynomials, by the triangularity argument of lem:top-degree-axiom-inertness (b) applied to \(Y'\), and \(\binom{n-t}{s}m^s\) of them have degree exactly \(s\); let \(B_s\) be their span. A monomial of degree at most \(K\) is the product of a monomial of degree \(a\) in the variables of \(T\) and one of degree \(s\) in the others, \(a+s\le K\), and on \(A\times Y'\) restricts to the tensor product of the restrictions; so \(V_K|_S=\sum_sV_{K-s}|_A\otimes B_s\), a direct sum since \(F^A\otimes\bigoplus_sB_s=\bigoplus_sF^A\otimes B_s\). For \(A\) all states the same basis count on \(T\) gives \(d_A\). (c) By (b) with \(t=n\), \(\dim V_K|_Y=\sum_{k\le K}\binom nkm^k\). For \(w\) on holes \(T\), \(|T|=e\), \(S\) fixes the states of \(T\), so \(A\) is one point, \(d_A\equiv1\), and \(\dim V_K|_S=\sum_{s\le K}\binom{n-e}sm^s\); the kernel is the difference. For the \(x_{i0}\), \(S\) is “hole \(0\) holds a pigeon of \(\Sigma\)”, \(t=1\), and \(A\) has \(\sigma\) states on which \(\sum_{i\in\Sigma}x_{i0}=1\) and the other \(x_{k0}\) vanish, so \(d_A(0)=1\), \(d_A(1)=\sigma\), and the kernel has dimension \((1+nm)-(\sigma+(n-1)m)=m+1-\sigma\). (d) By lem:top-degree-axiom-inertness (b) at \(D=n\), \(C=H+\operatorname{span}\{P_i\}\), with \(H\) the polynomials of degree at most \(n\) vanishing on \(Y\). If \(c\in C\), then \(c-\sum_i\lambda_iP_i\) vanishes on \(Y\) for some \(\lambda\), and \(P_i|_Y=1_{Z_i}\); conversely, if \(c|_Y=\sum_i\lambda_i1_{Z_i}\), then \(c-\sum_i\lambda_iP_i\) has degree at most \(n\) and vanishes on \(Y\), so lies in \(H\subseteq C\). For \(K+e\le n\) the products \(E_ju\) have degree at most \(n\), which gives the kernel. A class vanishing on \(S\) has every \(E_ju|_Y=0\in P_Y\). The map sending \(u\) in the kernel to \((E_ju|_Y)_j\) lands in \(\bigoplus_jP_Y^{S_j}\), as \(E_ju\) vanishes off \(S_j\); if all its components are zero, \(u\) vanishes on every \(S_j\); so the kernel modulo the classes vanishing on \(S\) injects there, and the bounds follow, since the stacked rank is \(\dim V_K|_Y\) minus the kernel's dimension.

Against the record. check:clique-factor-kernels at \(5\) pigeons, \(4\) holes, \(D=3<n\), \(K=1\): \(\operatorname{Ann}(x_{00})\) has dimension \(5\), \(\operatorname{Ann}(x_{00})\cap\operatorname{Ann}(x_{10})\) dimension \(4\), and the triple intersection dimension \(3\), which are \(m+1-\sigma\) for \(\sigma=1,2,3\). The check's family kernels, of the errors \(x_{i0}y\) with \(y\) outside hole \(0\), have union of supports \(S'\), the points with a pigeon of \(\Sigma\) in hole \(0\) and some other hole occupied; a function of degree at most \(1\) vanishing on \(S'\) vanishes on all of \(S\): with \(c+\sum a_{kl}x_{kl}\) restricted to \(S\), the points with exactly one further cell occupied give \(c+a=0\) for that cell's coefficient \(a\), and two further cells in different holes, available as \(n-1\ge2\), give \(c=0\) and then every \(a=0\); so these kernels also equal \(m+1-\sigma\), and the summed codimension \(165\) is (a) applied family by family: \(\dim V_1|_{S_j}=1+(n-2)m=11\) for \(S_j=\{x_{00}=y=1\}\) by (b) with \(t=2\), so \(165=15\times11\). At \(4\) pigeons and \(3\) holes only \(\operatorname{Ann}(x_{00})\), of dimension \(4=m\), lies in the inert range; the stacked kernels of the degree-\(2\) errors there, and those of check:truncated-kernel-beyond-support with ex:support-saving-not-inherited, have \(K+e=3=D=n\) and fall under (d), where the pigeon axioms act. Degenerate cases. No errors, or every \(E_j\) vanishing on \(Y\): \(S\) is empty and the kernel is all of \(U\). \(E_j\) a nonzero constant: \(S=Y\) and the kernel is \(0\). \(K=0\): the kernel is \(U\cong F\) if \(S\) is empty and \(0\) otherwise. A monomial \(w\) with two variables in one hole: it vanishes on \(Y\), \(S\) is empty, and the kernel is \(U\). \(D=n\) with \(K+e=n\): outside the inert range; (d) gives the description and bounds, not a count.
