General statement. lem:exposed-degree-occupancy, for every graph (multigraphs included), edge set \(T\) with \(2K+\tau\le2D\) and \(4K<m-\tau\), and exceptional set \(V_{\rm hi}\), in the charged character setting; cor:exposed-degree-tuple-ratio, for every tuple in lem:local-tuple-ratio's setting on such an edge set with \(\eta_{\rm ex}<1\) and \(c(K)>0\).

The setting and notation are lem:high-incidence-occupancy's: the charged character pseudoideal on a graph with edge expansion \(\varepsilon\), \(\max(4,2\Delta)\le D<\varepsilon n/8\), \(m\) edges, \(q_T(K)\) the number of cosets of the cut space \(\mathcal B\) plus \(\mathbb F_3^T\) met by the support-\(K\) ball, \(h(K)\) the number of cosets of \(\mathcal B\) it meets, \(B_j(k)=\sum_{i\le k}2^i\binom ji\), \(\beta_T=2\sqrt{K/(m-\tau)}\), and the small-potential remark (a cut of support at most \(2D\) has a potential of support below \(n/2\)). Lemma (exposed degree occupancy). Let \(T\) be an edge set, \(|T|=\tau\), with \(2K+\tau\le2D\) and \(4K<m-\tau\), and \(V_{\rm hi}\) any vertex set. For a vertex \(u\notin V_{\rm hi}\) let \(\theta(u)\) be the number of edges of \(T\) at \(u\) and \(\mu(u)\) the number of edges off \(T\) joining \(u\) to \(V_{\rm hi}\), and \(\nu=\max_{u\notin V_{\rm hi}}(\theta(u)+2\mu(u))\). Let \(X_0\subseteq V_{\rm hi}\) be the exposed vertices (those with an edge off \(T\)), \(h'=|X_0|\), and for \(x\in X_0\) let \(s(x)\) be the number of edges off \(T\) at \(x\), \(\kappa(x)\) the number of those whose other end lies in \(V_{\rm hi}\) (a loop at \(x\) counted there), and \(g(x)=s(x)-\kappa(x)\). Put \[\eta_{\rm ex}=\bigl(1+2\beta_T^{\,\varepsilon-\nu}\bigr)^n\prod_{x\in X_0}\bigl(1+2\beta_T^{\,g(x)}\bigr)-1.\] Then \(q_T(K)\ge(1-\eta_{\rm ex})B_{m-\tau}(K)\). The bound says nothing unless \(\eta_{\rm ex}<1\), which forces \(\varepsilon>\nu\) and \(g(x)\ge1\) for every \(x\in X_0\), each factor being at least \(3\) otherwise. With \(\theta,\mu\) the largest \(\theta(u),\mu(u)\), \(s_0\) the least \(s(x)\) and \(\kappa\) the largest \(\kappa(x)\), \(\nu\le\theta+2\mu\) and \(g(x)\ge s_0-\kappa\), so \(\eta_{\rm ex}\le(1+2\beta_T^{\varepsilon-\theta-2\mu})^n(1+2\beta_T^{s_0-\kappa})^{h'}-1\), the review's form.

Proof. Write a potential as \(s=s_{\rm lo}+s_X+s_{\rm in}\), its restrictions to the vertices outside \(V_{\rm hi}\), to \(X_0\) and to \(V_{\rm hi}\setminus X_0\). Every edge at a vertex of \(V_{\rm hi}\setminus X_0\) lies in \(T\), so the cut \(\partial s\) restricted to the edges off \(T\), \(\gamma'_s\) with support size \(d'_s\), depends only on \((s_{\rm lo},s_X)\). Let \(|\operatorname{supp}s|<n/2\) and \((s_{\rm lo},s_X)\ne0\), \(S=\operatorname{supp}s_{\rm lo}\), \(\sigma=|S|\), \(X=\operatorname{supp}s_X\). Edges from \(S\): as in lem:high-incidence-occupancy's proof, \(|\partial S|\ge\varepsilon\sigma\) since \(\sigma<n/2\); of these edges at most \(\sum_{u\in S}\theta(u)\) lie in \(T\) and at most \(\sum_{u\in S}\mu(u)\) are off \(T\) and end in \(V_{\rm hi}\); each other one, \(uv\) with \(u\in S\), is off \(T\) with \(v\notin S\cup V_{\rm hi}\), so \(s(v)=0\ne s(u)\), and lies in \(\operatorname{supp}\gamma'_s\). Edges from \(X\): each \(x\in X\) has at least \(g(x)\) off-\(T\) edges \(xv\) with \(v\notin V_{\rm hi}\); those with \(v\in S\) number at most \(\sum_{u\in S}\mu(u)\) in all, each being an off-\(T\) edge from a vertex of \(S\) into \(V_{\rm hi}\); each other one has \(s(v)=0\ne s(x)\) and lies in \(\operatorname{supp}\gamma'_s\). The two families are disjoint: an edge of the first has an endpoint in \(S\) and the other outside \(S\cup V_{\rm hi}\), one of the second an endpoint in \(X\subseteq V_{\rm hi}\) and the other outside \(S\cup V_{\rm hi}\). Hence \[d'_s\ \ge\ \varepsilon\sigma-\sum_{u\in S}\bigl(\theta(u)+2\mu(u)\bigr)+\sum_{x\in X}g(x)\ \ge\ (\varepsilon-\nu)\sigma+\sum_{x\in X}g(x).\] Call an off-\(T\) character \(\alpha\) of support at most \(K\) good if \(|\operatorname{supp}\alpha\cap\operatorname{supp}\gamma'_s|<d'_s/2\) for every such \(s\). If \(\alpha,\alpha'\) are good and in one coset, \(\alpha-\alpha'=\gamma+\omega\) with \(\gamma\) a cut and \(\omega\) on \(T\); \(|\operatorname{supp}\gamma|\le2K+\tau\le2D\), so \(\gamma=\partial s\) with \(|\operatorname{supp}s|<n/2\), and off \(T\), \(\alpha'=\alpha-\gamma'_s\). If \((s_{\rm lo},s_X)\ne0\), goodness of \(\alpha\) gives \(|\operatorname{supp}\alpha'|\ge|\operatorname{supp}\alpha|+d'_s-2|\operatorname{supp}\alpha\cap\operatorname{supp}\gamma'_s|>|\operatorname{supp}\alpha|\), and goodness of \(\alpha'\) with \(-s\) gives the reverse, a contradiction; so \((s_{\rm lo},s_X)=0\), \(\gamma'_s=0\) and \(\alpha'=\alpha\): a coset holds at most one good character. For \(\alpha\) uniform among the \(B_{m-\tau}(K)\) off-\(T\) characters, as in lem:high-incidence-occupancy's proof, the probability that it meets at least \(d'_s/2\) edges of \(\operatorname{supp}\gamma'_s\) is at most \(\beta_T^{d'_s}\), using \(\beta_T<1\), which is \(4K<m-\tau\), and at most \(\beta_T^{(\varepsilon-\nu)\sigma+\sum_{x\in X}g(x)}\) by the count above, whatever the signs of the exponents. The cut \(\gamma'_s\) is fixed by \((s_{\rm lo},s_X)\), with \(2^{\sigma+|X|}\) choices of values for given supports, so the probability that \(\alpha\) is not good is at most \[\sum_{(S,X)\ne(\emptyset,\emptyset)}\bigl(2\beta_T^{\varepsilon-\nu}\bigr)^{\sigma}\prod_{x\in X}2\beta_T^{g(x)}\ \le\ \eta_{\rm ex},\] the sum running over \(S\) outside \(V_{\rm hi}\) and \(X\subseteq X_0\), and expanding the product that defines \(\eta_{\rm ex}\) with \(n\) factors for \(S\). At least \((1-\eta_{\rm ex})B_{m-\tau}(K)\) characters are good, each in the support-\(K\) ball and no two in one coset. \(\square\)

Remark (closing part of the exceptional set). For \(Y\subseteq X_0\) let \(T_Y\) be \(T\) together with the off-\(T\) edges at \(Y\), \(\tau_Y=|T_Y|\). A coset of \(\mathcal B+\mathbb F_3^{T_Y}\) met by the ball contains a met coset of \(\mathcal B+\mathbb F_3^{T}\), and distinct ones contain distinct ones, so \(q_T(K)\ge q_{T_Y}(K)\). For \(T_Y\) and the same \(V_{\rm hi}\): a vertex \(u\notin V_{\rm hi}\) with \(a\) off-\(T\) edges to \(Y\) and \(b\) to \(X_0\setminus Y\) has \(\theta_Y(u)+2\mu_Y(u)=\theta(u)+a+2b\le\theta(u)+2\mu(u)\), so \(\nu_Y\le\nu\); the exposed vertices are \(X_0\setminus Y\), and each keeps its margin \(g(x)\), its edges to \(Y\) leaving \(s(x)\) and \(\kappa(x)\) together. So if \(2K+\tau_Y\le2D\) and \(4K<m-\tau_Y\), the lemma gives \(q_T(K)\ge(1-\eta_Y)B_{m-\tau_Y}(K)\), with \(\eta_Y\) its \(\eta_{\rm ex}\) for \(T_Y\), \(\beta_{T_Y}\) in place of \(\beta_T\). At \(Y=X_0\), \(T_Y\) is the star closure \(T^A\) of \(A=V_{\rm hi}\), \(\mu_Y=0\), \(\nu_Y=\max_u(\theta(u)+\mu(u))\), and this is lem:exposed-vertex-occupancy's case \(h'=0\) for \(T^A\), the closure route, under \(2K+\tau^A\le2D\) in place of its registered \(K+\tau^A\le D\).

Proof. Baseline. Without an idea, each exposed vertex is either a free coordinate of the potential, costing a factor three in the collision count and in the union bound (lem:exposed-vertex-occupancy), or closed by its star, costing its off-\(T\) edges in \(\tau\) (cor:star-closure-ratio).
