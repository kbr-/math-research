1. Exact restriction dimension on a flat

Lemma. Let \(F\subseteq\mathbb F_2^v\) be an affine flat of codimension \(r\). The restriction to \(F\) of the space of multilinear polynomials of degree at most \(k\) has dimension at most \(\sum_{j\le k}\binom{v-r}{j}\). Proof. Choose affine coordinates \(y\) in which \(F=\{y_1=\dots=y_r=0\}\); an affine change preserves degree and maps \(\mathbb F_2^v\) to itself. Multilinear reduction in \(y\) does not raise degree. Setting \(y_1,\dots,y_r=0\) leaves a multilinear polynomial of degree at most \(k\) in \(v-r\) variables. \(\square\)

For \(k\le(v-r)/3\) this is at most \(2\binom{v-r}{k}\le2e^{-rk/v}\binom vk\), since \(\binom{v-r}{k}/\binom vk=\prod_{i<k}\frac{v-r-i}{v-i}\le(1-r/v)^k\). The recorded estimate \(\binom{v-r+k}{k}\) gives only \(e^{-(r-k)k/v}\), which forces \(r\ge k+v\ln(8M)/k\ge2\sqrt{v\ln(8M)}\) for every \(k\); the exact count needs only \(r\ge v\ln(16M)/k\).
