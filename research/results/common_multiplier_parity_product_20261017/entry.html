General statement. prop:parity-product-repair: over any field of characteristic other than \(2\), on the cube \(\{0,1\}^n\) with \(1\le\ell\le n\) lines of protected class \(1\) and errors \(E_t=-2x_t\), every common repair of the errors has degree at least \(\ell\), attained when \(D\ge\ell+1\), while the product line's combined error has no repair of degree below \(\lceil\ell/2\rceil\) and one of that degree when \(D\ge\ell+\lceil\ell/2\rceil\), a saving in the repair's degree that needs the larger closure degree; on the pigeonhole matching domain, with diagonal errors \(-2x_{tt}\), both least degrees are \(\lceil\ell/2\rceil\).

Parity product repair

Setting. A field \(k\) of characteristic other than \(2\), \(R=k[x_1,\dots,x_n]\), the Boolean axioms \(x_i^2-x_i\), \(J\) the degree-\(D\) part of the closure they generate, and \(\ell\) lines, \(1\le\ell\le n\), line \(t\) holding \(1+E_t\) with \(E_t=-2x_t\) and protected class \(1\). The product line holds \(\prod_{t\le\ell}(1+E_t)\), with protected class \(1\) and combined error \(E_c=\prod_{t\le\ell}(1-2x_t)-1\), as in the combined-error reading of the multilinear repairs entry. A repair of errors \(E\) at parameter \(K\) is \(f\in V_K\) with \(a m f\in J\) for every axiom \(a\) and monomial \(m\) with \(\deg(am)\le D-K\), \(Ef\in J\) for each error, and \(f\notin J\).

Proposition (parity product repair). (a) If \(D\ge\ell+1\), the least degree of a common repair of \(E_1,\dots,E_\ell\) is \(\ell\), attained by \(\prod_{t\le\ell}(1-x_t)\). (b) If \(D\ge\ell+\lceil\ell/2\rceil\), the least degree of a repair of the combined error \(E_c\) is \(\lceil\ell/2\rceil\), attained by \(f_\ell=\prod_{i\le\lfloor\ell/2\rfloor}(1-x_{2i-1}-x_{2i})\), times \((1-x_\ell)\) when \(\ell\) is odd.

Proof. A polynomial \(g\) of degree at most \(D\) lies in \(J\) exactly when it vanishes on the cube: reducing \(x_i^2\) to \(x_i\) subtracts multiples \(m(x_i^2-x_i)\) of degree at most \(\deg g\), and a multilinear polynomial vanishing on the cube is zero. So for \(f\) of degree \(K\), soundness holds (each \(amf\) has degree at most \(D\) and vanishes on the cube), killing \(Ef\in J\) holds when \(\deg E+K\le D\) exactly when \(f\) vanishes where \(E\ne0\), and survival is \(f\) not vanishing everywhere on the cube.
(a) \(E_t(x)=-2x_t\) is nonzero exactly where \(x_t=1\), since \(2\ne0\). A common repair vanishes on the up-set with minimal sets \(\{1\},\dots,\{\ell\}\), whose transversal number is \(\ell\); by lem:transversal-killer-basis (c), with \(\ell+1\le D\), the least degree is \(\ell\), attained by \(\prod_t(1-x_t)\).
(b) On the cube \(1-2x_t=(-1)^{x_t}\), so \(E_c(x)=(-1)^{x_1+\dots+x_\ell}-1\) is \(-2\ne0\) where \(x_1+\dots+x_\ell\) is odd and \(0\) elsewhere; \(\deg E_c=\ell\), so killing is defined when \(\ell+K\le D\). A repair of \(E_c\) is therefore a polynomial nonzero somewhere on the cube that vanishes on the odd set \(O=\{x:x_1+\dots+x_\ell\text{ odd}\}\).
Upper bound. For \(a,b\in\{0,1\}\), \(1-a-b\) is \(0\) when \(a+b=1\) and \(\pm1\) otherwise. So \(f_\ell(x)\ne0\) exactly when \(x_{2i-1}=x_{2i}\) for each pair and, for odd \(\ell\), \(x_\ell=0\); then \(x_1+\dots+x_\ell\) is even. Hence \(f_\ell\) vanishes on \(O\), \(f_\ell(0)=1\), and \(\deg f_\ell=\lceil\ell/2\rceil\).
Lower bound. Let \(f\) be nonzero on the cube, vanish on \(O\), and have degree \(d\); its multilinear reduction \(g\) has degree at most \(d\) and the same values. Put \(y_i=1-2x_i\), an invertible affine change since \(2\) is a unit; on the cube \(y_i\in\{1,-1\}\), the functions are the multilinear polynomials in \(y\), uniquely, and a multilinear polynomial has the same degree in \(x\) and in \(y\) (the top part of \(y_S\) is \((-2)^{|S|}x_S\), and conversely). Write \(g=\sum_Sc_Sy_S\) and \(\chi=y_1\cdots y_\ell\), which is \(-1\) on \(O\) and \(1\) off it. Since \(g\) vanishes on \(O\), \(g=g\chi\) on the cube, and \(g\chi=\sum_Sc_Sy_{S\triangle[\ell]}\) because \(y_i^2=1\) there. By uniqueness \(c_S=c_{S\triangle[\ell]}\) for every \(S\). As \(g\ne0\), some \(c_S\ne0\), so \(c_{S\triangle[\ell]}\ne0\) and \(d\ge\max(|S|,|S\triangle[\ell]|)\ge\max(|S\cap[\ell]|,\ell-|S\cap[\ell]|)\ge\lceil\ell/2\rceil\). \(\square\)

Proposition, part (c): the matching domain. Let \(Y\) be the partial matchings of \(m\ge\ell\) pigeons into \(h\) holes, \(h\ge\ell\) for even \(\ell\) and \(h\ge\ell+1\) for odd \(\ell\), with variables \(x_{ij}\); let \(J\) be the degree-\(D\) part of the closure of the Boolean, hole (\(x_{ij}x_{i'j}\)) and functionality (\(x_{ij}x_{ij'}\)) axioms, and take \(\ell\) lines with protected class \(1\) and diagonal errors \(E_t=-2x_{tt}\). If \(D\ge\ell+\lceil\ell/2\rceil\), the least degree of a repair of the combined error and the least degree of a common repair of the \(E_t\) are both \(\lceil\ell/2\rceil\): on \(Y\) the cube's saving disappears.

Proof of (c). A polynomial of degree at most \(D\) lies in \(J\) exactly when it vanishes on \(Y\): multilinear reduction stays within degree, the non-matching monomials are multiples of hole or functionality axioms, and the matching monomials \(x_N\) are independent on \(Y\) (\(x_N(M)=1\) exactly when \(N\subseteq M\), a triangular system). So, as on the cube, a repair of an error is a function nonzero on \(Y\) vanishing where the error is nonzero, and every function has a unique representative \(\sum_Nc_Nx_N\) over matchings \(N\), whose degree \(\max\{|N|:c_N\ne0\}\) is at most that of any polynomial representing it. The combined error is nonzero exactly on the matchings with an odd number of the edges \((t,t)\), \(t\le\ell\).
Upper bound. \(g_\ell=\prod_{i\le\lfloor\ell/2\rfloor}x_{2i-1,2i}\), times \(x_{\ell,\ell+1}\) for odd \(\ell\), is nonzero only on matchings containing all its edges; such a matching uses pigeon \(2i-1\) and hole \(2i\) (and pigeon \(\ell\)), so it contains no \((t,t)\) with \(t\le\ell\). Hence \(g_\ell\) is a common repair, and a combined one, of degree \(\lceil\ell/2\rceil\), nonzero on the matching of its own edges.
Lower bound. Let \(f\ne0\) on \(Y\) vanish on the odd-diagonal matchings, with representative \(\sum_Nc_Nx_N\) of degree at most \(\deg f\). Pick \(N\) with \(c_N\ne0\); let \(B\) be its edges other than the \((t,t)\), \(t\le\ell\), and \(T_B\) the indices \(t\le\ell\) whose pigeon and hole \(B\) leaves free, so that \(|T_B|\ge\ell-2|B|\) (each edge of \(B\) uses one pigeon and one hole) and \(N=B\cup\{(t,t):t\in U\}\) with \(U\subseteq T_B\). For \(S\subseteq T_B\) put \(f_B(S)=\sum_{B'\subseteq B}(-1)^{|B\setminus B'|}f(B'\cup\{(t,t):t\in S\})\), every argument being a matching. Since \(f(M)=\sum_{N'\subseteq M}c_{N'}\), Möbius inversion gives \(f_B(S)=\sum_{U'\subseteq S}c_{B\cup\{(t,t):t\in U'\}}\): as a function on the cube \(\{0,1\}^{T_B}\), \(f_B\) has the multilinear coefficient \(c_{B\cup\{(t,t):t\in U'\}}\) at \(x_{U'}\), nonzero at \(U'=U\), and degree at most \(\deg f-|B|\). Each \(f(B'\cup\{(t,t):t\in S\})\) has \(|S|\) diagonal edges among the first \(\ell\), so \(f_B\) vanishes where \(|S|\) is odd. By the lower bound of (b) on the cube \(\{0,1\}^{T_B}\) with all its \(|T_B|\) coordinates, \(\deg f-|B|\ge\lceil|T_B|/2\rceil\ge\lceil\ell/2\rceil-|B|\) (when \(T_B=\varnothing\) the bound is \(0\)). So \(\deg f\ge\lceil\ell/2\rceil\). A common repair also vanishes on the odd-diagonal matchings, so the same bound holds for it. \(\square\)

Structural reason. The combined error sees only the parity of the errors' supports, because each factor \(1+E_t\) is a sign; a parity set is cut out by half as many pairwise conditions as the union of the supports needs, and the sign basis shows no polynomial does better, since a function supported on even parity has a spectrum closed under complementing \([\ell]\). On \(Y\) a single edge variable \(x_{2i-1,2i}\) vanishes on the supports of two diagonal errors, so covering the union costs \(\lceil\ell/2\rceil\), the parity price; the Möbius coefficient charges each blocking edge to \(f\)'s degree, so neither reading goes lower.

New mathematics. prop:parity-product-repair: on the cube in characteristic other than \(2\), a product of \(\ell\) lines with errors \(-2x_t\) has a combined-error repair of degree exactly \(\lceil\ell/2\rceil\) at \(D\ge\ell+\lceil\ell/2\rceil\), while every common repair of the factors' errors has degree \(\ell\); the saving is \(\ell-\lceil\ell/2\rceil\) in the repair's degree, unbounded in \(\ell\), at the cost of a closure degree \(\ell+\lceil\ell/2\rceil\) against \(\ell+1\), and it comes from cancellation of errors in the product, which characteristic \(2\) excludes. On the pigeonhole matching domain the same errors on diagonal edges have common and combined degrees both \(\lceil\ell/2\rceil\): blocking edges give the common repair the parity bound, so for these cancelling errors the saving holds on the cube but not on the matching domain.

Matching domain, first by hand. Before part (c) was proved, its first cases were worked by hand; they are kept as its checks.

  • At \(\ell=2\). A common repair must vanish on matchings containing \((1,1)\) or \((2,2)\). The linear \(f=x_{12}\) does: a matching containing \((1,2)\) uses pigeon \(1\) and hole \(2\), so it contains neither; and \(f\) is \(1\) on \(\{(1,2)\}\). So the common degree is \(1\), and a combined repair, which must vanish on \(\{(1,1)\}\), also has degree at least \(1\): no saving at \(\ell=2\).
  • For every \(\ell\) with \(\ell+1\) holes, \(\prod_{i\le\lfloor\ell/2\rfloor}x_{2i-1,2i}\), times \(x_{\ell,\ell+1}\) when \(\ell\) is odd, is a common repair of degree \(\lceil\ell/2\rceil\) on \(Y\): each blocking edge uses the pigeon of one diagonal edge and the hole of another. So on \(Y\) the common repair already reaches the cube's combined degree, and the question is whether the combined degree is lower still.
  • At \(\ell=3\), with at least three pigeons and four holes. Let \(f=c+\sum_ea_ex_e\) vanish on the matchings with an odd number of the edges \((1,1),(2,2),(3,3)\). The singletons \(\{(t,t)\}\) give \(c+a_{tt}=0\) and the triple gives \(c+a_{11}+a_{22}+a_{33}=0\), so \(-2c=0\), \(c=0\) and \(a_{tt}=0\).   • A general bound, by the same restriction: if \(f\ne0\) on \(Y\) vanishes on the odd-diagonal matchings, take \(M\) with \(f(M)\ne0\), remove its diagonal edges to get \(M_0\), and let \(T\) be the indices \(t\le\ell\) whose pigeon and hole \(M_0\) leaves free. The matchings \(M_0\cup\{(t,t):t\in S\}\), \(S\subseteq T\), form a cube in \(Y\) (with at least \(\ell\) pigeons and \(\ell\) holes) on which \(f\) restricts to a nonzero function vanishing where \(|S|\) is odd, so prop:parity-product-repair's lower bound gives \(\deg f\ge\lceil|T|/2\rceil\ge\lceil\ell/2\rceil-e\), with \(e\) the edges of \(M_0\) touching an index \(t\le\ell\). This bound left \(e\) unpaid; part (c) pays for it by extracting the coefficient of the blocking edges (Möbius inversion in \(B\)), whose degree \(|B|\) is charged to \(f\).
