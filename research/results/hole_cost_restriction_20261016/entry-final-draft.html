General statement. thm:binary-hole-cost-restriction, over every field, for all \(n\ge4\), \(m,r\) with \(\binom mr>n\), every inventory \(M\) of nonrecursive \(0/1\)-valued definitions of degree at most \(k\ge1\), and every refutation; part (b) for definitions whose monomials have fewer than \(r\) variables on any one hole, each touching at most \(\eta\le n/4\) holes.

The cleanup

Setting. Over a field \(F\), in the multilinear ring of variables \(x_{uv}\) (\(u\in[m]\), \(v\in[n]\)) and \(z_1,\ldots,z_M\), the ring Razborov's polynomial calculus works in (Boolean axioms built in), with \(t_{Iv}=\prod_{u\in I}x_{uv}\). Razborov's all-subsets system \(\mathrm{PHP}^{m,r}_n\), as imp:razborov-generic-pigeonhole-degree records it with every \(r\)-subset of \([m]\) its own family, has the axioms \(1-\sum_vt_{Iv}\) for \(|I|=r\), \(t_{I_1\cup I_2,v}\) for distinct \(r\)-subsets, and \(t_{Iv_1}t_{Iv_2}\) for \(v_1\ne v_2\); if \(\binom mr>n\) it has no refutation of degree at most \(n/2\), over any field. Extension axioms are \(z_i-Q_i\) with \(Q_i\) a multilinear polynomial in the \(x\)'s alone of degree at most \(k\), \(0/1\)-valued, that is \(Q_i^2=Q_i\) in the multilinear ring (automatic over \(\mathbb F_2\)); \(\eta_i\) is the number of holes \(v\) such that \(Q_i\) contains some \(x_{uv}\), and \(\eta=\max_i\eta_i\). The size \(s\) of a refutation is its total number of monomials over all lines.

Theorem (hole-cost restriction). Let \(\binom mr>n\) and \(k\ge1\). (a) Every refutation of \(\mathrm{PHP}^{m,r}_n\) together with the \(M\) extension axioms has degree at least \((n/2+1)/k\). (b) If moreover \(n\ge4\), \(\eta\le n/4\), and every monomial of every \(Q_i\) has fewer than \(r\) variables on any one hole (true when \(k<r\)), every such refutation has size \(s\) with \[\ln s\ \ge\ \frac{n^2}{64k(mn+M\eta)}-1.\]

Proof. Substitution, and part (a). Since \(Q_i^2=Q_i\) in the multilinear ring, \(z_i\mapsto Q_i\) is a ring homomorphism \(\varphi\) onto the \(x\)-ring, sending each extension axiom to \(0\) and fixing the \(\mathrm{PHP}^{m,r}\) axioms, which contain no \(z\). In a refutation of degree \(D\) an addition stays an addition; multiplication of a line \(f\), of degree at most \(D-1\), by \(x_{uv}\) stays one, of degree at most \(k(D-1)+1\le kD\), and by \(z_i\) becomes multiplication of \(\varphi(f)\), of degree at most \(k(D-1)\), by \(Q_i\), carried out monomial by monomial through at most \(k\) multiplications by variables and then additions, of degree at most \(kD\). This is a refutation of \(\mathrm{PHP}^{m,r}_n\) of degree at most \(kD\), so \(kD\ge n/2+1\).

Restrictions, for part (b). Fix \(d\ge0\), chosen below, and let \(H\) be the set of pairs (line, monomial of degree above \(d\)), \(|H|\le s\). A restriction, setting variables to constants, is a homomorphism of the multilinear ring, so it maps the refutation to a refutation of the restricted axioms; it never raises a line's monomial count or a monomial's degree, so the restricted \(H\) is no larger, and pairs with a monomial containing a variable set to \(0\) disappear. A restricted \(Q_i\) stays \(0/1\)-valued, and its monomials divide original ones. Whenever a definition \(Q_i\) becomes constant, substitute that constant for \(z_i\) everywhere; the axiom becomes \(0\), and \(H\) does not grow. So at each stage every remaining \(Q_i\) is nonconstant, and the axioms are those of \(\mathrm{PHP}^{m,r}\) on the remaining holes with the restricted extension axioms. A monomial of degree at least \(d+1\) has at least \((d+1)/2\) distinct \(x\)'s or at least \((d+1)/2\) distinct \(z\)'s, so one of the two kinds holds for at least half of \(H\).

Hole step, when at least \(|H|/2\) pairs have \((d+1)/2\) distinct \(x\)'s. Each hole carries \(m\) variables, so such a monomial meets at least \((d+1)/(2m)\) holes, and some remaining hole \(v\) is met by at least a \((d+1)/(4mn)\) share of \(H\). Set every remaining \(x_{uv}\) to \(0\): those pairs disappear, every \(t_{Iv}\) becomes \(0\), and the axioms are \(\mathrm{PHP}^{m,r}\) on the other holes. One hole is removed.

Extension step, otherwise. Some \(z_i\) occurs in at least a \((d+1)/(4M)\) share of \(H\). Choose an assignment \(\sigma\) of \(\mathrm{vars}(Q_i)\) with \(Q_i(\sigma)=0\): \(Q_i(0)\in\{0,1\}\); if it is \(0\), \(\sigma=0\); otherwise \(1-Q_i\) is nonzero with constant term \(0\), and for a monomial \(T\) of least degree in it, setting the variables of \(T\) to \(1\) and the rest to \(0\) gives \((1-Q_i)(\sigma)=\sum_{T'\subseteq T}\mathrm{coef}(T')=\mathrm{coef}(T)\ne0\), since no proper nonconstant divisor of \(T\) has nonzero coefficient, so \(Q_i(\sigma)\ne1\) and, \(Q_i\) being \(0/1\)-valued, \(Q_i(\sigma)=0\); and \(T\), dividing an original monomial, has fewer than \(r\) variables on each hole. Apply \(\sigma\), set \(z_i=0\), so the axiom \(z_i-Q_i\) becomes \(0\) and the pairs containing \(z_i\) disappear, and set to \(0\) every remaining variable of each of the at most \(\eta\) holes \(Q_i\) touches. Each such hole then carries fewer than \(r\) ones, so every \(t_{Iv}\) with \(|I|\ge r\) on it is \(0\), and the axioms are again \(\mathrm{PHP}^{m,r}\) on the other holes; at most \(\eta\) holes are removed.

Count. A step removes at least a share \(\alpha\) of \(H\), with \(\alpha\ge(d+1)/(4mn)\) or \(\alpha\ge(d+1)/(4M)\), and a step with \(\alpha\ge1\) is the last. While \(H\) is nonempty, \(s\prod(1-\alpha)\ge1\), so over the steps before the last \(\sum\alpha\le\ln s\): there are at most \(4mn\ln s/(d+1)\) hole steps and \(4M\ln s/(d+1)\) extension steps before the last, and with the last step at most \(4\ln s\,(mn+M\eta)/(d+1)+\max(1,\eta)\) holes are removed in all. Take \(d+1=\lceil8\ln s\,(mn+M\eta)/n\rceil\), positive as \(s\ge2\): at most \(n/2+\max(1,\eta)\le3n/4\) holes go, by \(n\ge4\) and \(\eta\le n/4\), \(n'\ge n/4\) remain, and \(\binom mr>n\ge n'\). The final refutation has inferences of degree at most \(d+1\), so by part (a) on \(n'\) holes, \(k(d+1)\ge n'/2+1>n/8\). With \(d+1\le8\ln s\,(mn+M\eta)/n+1\), \(\ln s>(n/(8k)-1)\,n/(8(mn+M\eta))\ge n^2/(64k(mn+M\eta))-1\), as \(n/(8(mn+M\eta))\le1/(8m)\le1\). \(\square\)

Hypotheses, each with its step. \(0/1\)-valued definitions: the substitution's ring map and the zero \(\sigma\); this is all either step needs, so the theorem holds over every field, more general than the characteristic-two form of an earlier draft, which copied the paper's split between \(p=2\) and \(p>2\) rather than reading the proof. Fewer than \(r\) variables of a monomial on one hole: only in the extension step, so that \(\sigma\)'s ones commit no \(r\)-set to a removed hole; it is more general than \(k<r\), which implies it, and restriction preserves it. \(\binom mr>n\): Razborov's degree bound at \(n'\le n\). \(k\ge1\): the degree count of the substitution. \(n\ge4\) and \(\eta\le n/4\): only that \(n'\ge n/4\); for \(n/4<\eta<n/2\) the same argument gives the bound with \(n/2-\eta\) holes left in place of \(n/4\), and for \(\eta\ge n/2\) it gives nothing as written. Nonrecursive definitions in the \(x\)'s: the substitution and the hole count of each \(Q_i\). Part (a) needs no condition on holes, and part (b) holds for every refutation of any degree.

Degenerate cases. \(M=0\): no extension step and no substitution occur, and the argument gives \(n/(64m)-1\), with no factor \(k\). \(k=0\): every definition is constant and is removed by the preliminary substitution, which is the case \(M=0\). A constant \(Q_i\): removed at no hole cost; \(\eta_i=0\) means \(Q_i\) constant. \(r=1\), ordinary PHP's form: the per-hole condition leaves only constant definitions, and \(mn>n^2\) makes the bound negative. A refutation has \(s\ge2\), and the bound says nothing when \(n^2\le64k(mn+M\eta)\).
