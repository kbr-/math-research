thm:turan-weak-lindelof-density
  Statement: Published (Turan 1984, Section 37, Theorem 37.1). Weak Lindelof hypothesis with constant A: there is delta < A such that zeta has fewer than A delta log tau0 zeros in the disk of radius delta about s0 = sigma0 + i tau0 whenever zeta has no zero in Re s > sigma0, |Im s - tau0| < log tau0, sigma0 >= 1/2 + 10 delta, tau0 large. If it holds with some A < 0.01 then N(alpha, T) < T^(2(1 - alpha) + 5 sqrt A) for 1/2 <= alpha <= 1 and T large.
  Assessment: Statement read as page images from a supplied scan; the proof read only in its first two pages; not audited. The book adds, citing an earlier paper of its author, that the count holds unconditionally with A = 0.71 (a constant read from a small scan and uncertain) for all delta < 1/10, and with every A at sigma0 = 1.

prop:moment-extraction-exponents
  Statement: Let k, m >= 1, beta > 0, H = D^beta, and complex A, A_u (u in P) with |P| >= H^(1/k) D^-eps / C, |A - A_u| <= E and sum over P of |A_u|^(2m) <= C D^(m+eps) H. Then |A| <= C^(1/m) D^(1/2 + beta(1-1/k)/(2m) + eps/m) + E. With E <= D^(1-beta/k) the best exponent over beta is (k-1+m)/(k-1+2m); for beta >= m the first exponent is at least 1 - 1/(2k) for every m.
  Assessment: Working elementary proof with exponent arithmetic. Applied to the imported sextic family (k = 6, m = 1, beta slightly above 1) it reproduces the source's 11/12. For beta >= m the exponent is at best 1 - 1/(2k). For beta < m the moment hypothesis alone, from one row, gives every member the exponent 1/2 + beta/(2m), so the extraction only improves a saving the hypothesis already contains. For Moebius coefficients the defect unfolds into sums of the same kind with the same excluded set, so an induction over scales removes it.
  Status: working_proof
  Record: [Moment extraction exponents](https://kbr.is-a.dev/math-research/branches/riemann/#entry-2026-10-11-moment-extraction-exponents)

prop:one-sided-zero-set-equivalence
  Statement: Fejer kernel K_L(w) = 2(1 - cos Lw)/(L w^2); a conjugation-closed multiset of points z with |Im z| <= Delta and sum m_z/(1 + (Re z)^2) finite, infinite allowed (real points and off-line pairs gamma_j +- i delta_j); D_L(tau) = sum m_z K_L(tau - z). (a) The sum converges absolutely, and if all delta_j <= theta then L D_L(tau) >= -4 S_off e^{theta L} for every real tau and L > 0, S_off = sum over the points z of the pairs of m_z/|tau - z|^2. (b) Let tau be real, not a real point, and d the largest distance of a pair at height tau (0 if none). If L D_L(tau) >= -C L^A e^{theta L} for all L >= L_1, with theta >= 0, then every delta_j <= max(theta, d). In particular for tau at no pair's height the bound for all large L holds with exponent theta if and only if no pair is farther than theta from the line; no hypothesis on distinct heights or on the supremum of the distances being attained. Conditional application, not part of the proved statement: if the zeros of zeta (z = (rho - 1/2)/i) form such a configuration, which follows from three classical facts recalled and not read in a source (symmetry about the critical line, Delta = 1/2, the count N(T+1) - N(T) << log T), the one-sided bound with theta = 0 at one such tau is the Riemann hypothesis restated.
  Assessment: Working proof of the statement for configurations: the Laplace transform in L of L K_L(w) is 2/(s(s^2 + w^2)); the transform of L D_L is a meromorphic function whose poles off the imaginary axis are at +-delta_j +- i(tau - gamma_j) with residues that cannot cancel, and Landau's lemma moves the abscissa of convergence to theta. Its application to the zeros of zeta is conditional on three classical facts recalled and not read in a source.
  Status: working_proof

lem:gram-form-localization
  Statement: phi real, even, supported in [-L/2, L/2], continuously differentiable on the line with phi' absolutely continuous, B = int |phi''|; grid tau_k = tau_0 + 2 pi k/L; c real supported on 0 <= k < d; H_c = sum c_k phihat(. - tau_k); I = [tau_0, tau_{d-1}]. (i) |phihat(r)| <= B/r^2. (ii) For R > 0 the integral of H_c^2 over dist(tau, I) >= R is at most |c|^2 t(R), t(R) = B^2 (2/(3R^3) + L/(6 pi R^2)). (iii) For bounded measurable nu the matrix of Q_nu(c) = int H_c^2 nu on the indices 0 <= k < d has largest eigenvalue at most 2 pi L ||phi||_inf^2 max(sup_{I_R} nu, 0) + ||nu||_inf t(R) and smallest at least 2 pi L ||phi||_inf^2 min(inf_{I_R} nu, 0) - ||nu||_inf t(R), I_R the R-neighbourhood of I. (iv) The same with ||nu||_inf t(R) replaced by B^2 (N_R (2/(3R^3) + L/(6 pi R^2)) + a (2/(9R^3) + L/(12 pi R^2))) when nu is bounded on I_R and |nu(tau)| <= N_R + a log(dist(tau, I)/R) outside I_R. (v) For nu = P_X, the prime cosine sum -(1/pi) sum_{n <= X} Lambda(n) n^{-1/2} cos(tau log n): ||P_X||_inf <= (2/pi) sqrt(X) log X, so the eigenvalues of its matrix are at most 2 pi L ||phi||_inf^2 sup_{I_R} |P_X| + (2/pi) sqrt(X) log X t(R) in modulus.
  Assessment: Working elementary proof: two integrations by parts, Cauchy-Schwarz over the grid at each tau, a sum compared with an integral, and the identity Q_nu(c) = int H_c^2 nu of the prime-side form bound. Its use for the density source's window needs that window's taper to be continuously differentiable with an absolutely continuous derivative, which was not read; the flat window and the triangle are excluded.
  Status: working_proof
  Record: [Gram form localization](https://kbr.is-a.dev/math-research/branches/riemann/#entry-2026-10-11-gram-form-localization)
