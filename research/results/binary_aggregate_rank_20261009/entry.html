The paired block on one surviving domain

Let \(J_D\) be a binary Boolean pseudoideal containing an unsatisfiable old family \(\Gamma\), let \(w\notin J_D\), \(\deg w\le b\), and let \(D-b\ge\max(2,\max\deg\Gamma)\). Choose \(N\le D-b-1\) coordinates from the free cube of the weighted restriction theorem. All Boolean monomials on these coordinates have independent weighted images. Write them as \(x_1,y_1,\ldots,x_h,y_h,z_1,\ldots,z_t\), with \(h\ge1\), \(N=2h+t\), \(t\ge0\). Fix an integer \(K\ge0\) with \[ b+K+2h+1\le D. \tag{1} \] Let \(U_K\) be the binary multilinear polynomials on these \(N\) coordinates of degree at most \(K\), and write \(H_n(k)=\sum_{j=0}^{\min(n,k)}\binom nj\), with \(H_n(k)=0\) for \(k<0\).

Take one complete binary ENS block of accuracy \(h\), actual inputs \(g=(x_1,y_1,\ldots,x_h,y_h)\), original product cost \(2h\), and original companion cost \(2h+1\). Set row \(i\) to select \(x_i+y_i\), all other coefficients zero. All fresh coefficient fields vanish, and \[ P=\prod_{i=1}^h(1+x_i+y_i),\qquad E_{x_i}=x_iP,\quad E_{y_i}=y_iP. \tag{2} \] Keep every companion. These are legal simultaneous scalar choices for an actual block, not independent choices per error.

Corollary (rare prefix rank). In this setup the domain has weighted dimension \(H_N(K)\). The individual map \(f\mapsto[wE_{x_1}f]\) has rank \[ H_{N-h-1}(K), \tag{3} \] and the complete companion map has rank \[ H_{N-h}(K)-H_t(K-h). \tag{4} \] Both ranks are in the same \(J_D\) quotient. Under the uniform binary cube law the individual error has probability \(2^{-h-1}\), and the union of all companion errors has probability \(2^{-h}(1-2^{-h})\). Each individual error is a width-two Boolean CNF function. When \(K\le N-h-1\), \[ \frac{H_{N-h-1}(K)}{H_N(K)} \ge 1-\frac{(h+1)K}{N}. \tag{5} \] Thus for \(h\to\infty\) and \(hK/N\to0\), individual rank fraction tends to one while its error probability tends to zero.

Proof of the exact ranks. Free-cube injectivity identifies every weighted image with the corresponding Boolean function on these selected coordinates. All products and Boolean reductions used here fit (1); in particular their unreduced degree is at most \(b+K+h+1\), below the original-cost reserve. The equality conditions \(P=1\) are \(x_i=y_i\). On that subspace identify each pair with one bit \(u_i\), leaving \(h+t=N-h\) free bits. Restricting \(U_K\) gives exactly all degree-at-most-\(K\) polynomials on those bits: substitute \(x_i=y_i=u_i\), and conversely lift each monomial using one coordinate per pair.

The first error additionally requires \(u_1=1\). Restriction onto its support therefore has rank \(H_{h+t-1}(K)\), proving (3). The stacked map vanishes precisely where all \(u_i=0\), or where the restricted multiplier vanishes. Its kernel within the restricted polynomial space is the functions supported on \(u_1=\cdots=u_h=0\). Every such function has unique multilinear form \[ \prod_{i=1}^h(1+u_i)\,q(z). \] If \(q\ne0\), its degree is \(h+\deg q\), since its top \(u_1\cdots u_h\) coefficient is \(q\). This kernel therefore has dimension \(H_t(K-h)\), giving (4). Free-cube injectivity prevents any extra relations from the unsatisfiable old base; this is why the transfer is valid here and not in an arbitrary chosen coordinate set.

The binary probability counts follow by pairing independent bits. The first support has the two unit clauses \(x_1=1,y_1=1\) and, for each other pair, the two width-two clauses expressing equality. This proves the width claim without identifying CNF width with polynomial degree. For (5), choose uniformly a subset of size at most \(K\) from the \(N\) coordinates. The left side is the probability it avoids a specified \(h+1\) coordinates. Symmetry and the union bound give failure at most \((h+1)\mathbb E|S|/N\le(h+1)K/N\). This proves the inequality and the asymptotic assertion. \(\square\)

The ternary hard-family interpretation and its limits

In the canonical charged ternary one-hot system, selected free coordinates must come from distinct edges: two distinct indicators of one edge have zero product in \(J_D\), contradicting free-cube injectivity. Relabel each selected indicator's distinguished edge label as zero. Under the uniform full ternary label law, each selected bit has probability \(1/3\) of being one, independently. The individual error probability is consequently \[ \frac19\left(\frac59\right)^{h-1}, \tag{6} \] and the complete error support has probability \((5/9)^h-(4/9)^h\). Each pair equality forbids four of the nine two-edge patterns, giving four width-two categorical clauses. The individual support also fixes the first two edge labels. It is therefore a genuine narrow categorical CNF, not merely a width-two formula in dependent variables. The ranks (3)–(4) are unchanged: rank came from free-coordinate injectivity, not from this real distribution.

This fits a small-cutoff regime of the growing-degree budget. For example, if \(N=\Theta(n^{1+\alpha-\beta})\), \(h=\Theta(n^\alpha)\), and \(K=\Theta(n^{\alpha d})\), then \(hK/N\to0\) whenever \(\alpha d+\beta<1\), and (1) can hold with room. These are choices of a calibration domain, not a restriction forcing the goal to use that \(K\). A larger cutoff may have a substantially smaller rank fraction and must be assessed with its full source-degree reserve. No new hard graph construction is asserted.

Changing the weight repairs this block. The surviving polynomial weight \(w(1+x_1+y_1)\) is not an annihilator of all errors, but \(w(x_1+y_1)\) is: it kills \(P\) by the Boolean identity \((x_1+y_1)(1+x_1+y_1)=0\). Its survival follows from the free cube, and the field certificate fits the same original reserve. This is an explicit cheap repair, not evidence that arbitrary source inventories share one such repair. In particular (3) is a bad rank-control example for these fixed rows and common domain; it is not an impossibility theorem for coefficient optimization or a lower bound on required weight degree.
