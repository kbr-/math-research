Exact filtered elimination

Let \(\mathbb F\) be any field, let \(R=\mathbb F[x_1,\ldots,x_n]\), and choose finitely many \(z_j(x)\) with positive integer bounds \(d_j\ge\max(1,\deg z_j)\). In \(S=R[Z_1,\ldots,Z_s]\), give \(x_i\) cost one and \(Z_j\) cost \(d_j\). Write \(S_{\le K}^{\mathrm{wt}}\) for the span of monomials of total cost at most \(K\), and \(R_{\le K}\) for the ordinary old degree space. Define \(\psi(x_i)=x_i\), \(\psi(Z_j)=z_j(x)\); old inclusion \(\iota:R\to S\) is a right inverse of \(\psi\).

Lemma (weighted feature retraction). For every integer \(K\ge0\), \[ \psi(S_{\le K}^{\mathrm{wt}})=R_{\le K}. \] Every \(F\in S_{\le K}^{\mathrm{wt}}\) has a graph-elimination certificate \[ F-\iota\psi(F)=\sum_j A_j(x,Z)(Z_j-z_j(x)),\qquad \deg_{\mathrm{wt}} A_j\le K-d_j, \] with negative-bound coefficient spaces zero. In particular this identifies the filtered quotient by the graph equations with the original filtration, without assuming feature independence.

Let also \(J_D\subseteq R_{\le D}\) be a degree-\(D\) pseudoideal, \(w\in R_{\le b}\), and let \(A_1,\ldots,A_q\) be fixed old error factors of degrees at most \(a\), where \(b+K+a\le D\). Then \[ \operatorname{im}\bigl(F\in S_{\le K}^{\mathrm{wt}}\mapsto([w\psi(F)A_i])_i\bigr) =\operatorname{im}\bigl(f\in R_{\le K}\mapsto([wfA_i])_i\bigr). \] The induced maps on the common weighted base quotient have identical kernels and ranks. In particular \(S_{\le K}^{\mathrm{wt}}/\{F:w\psi(F)\in J_D\}\) is canonically \([wR_{\le K}]\). Survival is preserved in both directions. Properness, Booleanity and a nonzero weight are not needed for the linear identities; they are needed only when using the quotient as a nontrivial binary lower-bound domain.

Proof. Substitution does not increase the assigned cost, so the image is contained in \(R_{\le K}\). Old inclusion supplies the reverse containment. For the certificate, replace \(Z_1,\ldots,Z_s\) in succession and telescope the differences. On a monomial, replacing \(Z_j^e\) uses \[ Z_j^e-z_j^e=(Z_j-z_j)\sum_{u=0}^{e-1}Z_j^{e-1-u}z_j^u. \] Every cofactor has cost at most the original monomial cost minus \(d_j\); preceding substitutions do not increase cost. Sum over monomials and replacement positions. This proves the displayed certificate and shows that the full kernel is the graph ideal with the indicated bounded representations.

The error-image equality follows from the first equality by applying the same old linear map to both sides. The weighted base quotient identification is the first isomorphism theorem applied to \(F\mapsto[w\psi(F)]\), with old inclusion giving surjectivity. Each error map factors through it: if \(wf\in J_D\), multiply by each monomial of \(A_i\); all degrees fit \(b+K+a\le D\), so bounded absorption gives \(wfA_i\in J_D\). The induced maps on this identified domain are literally the same, proving the kernel and rank assertion. \(\square\)
