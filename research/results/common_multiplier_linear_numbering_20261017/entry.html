Setting

As in the functional-transfer entry: the functional pigeonhole system over a field \(F\) (Booleanity, hole and functionality axioms, pigeon axioms \(Q_i=1-\sum_jx_{ij}\), multilinear ring) with \(m\) pigeons and \(N\) holes, \(C\) its degree-\(D\) closure and \(H\) that of the hole and functionality axioms alone, \(D\le\lceil N/2\rceil\). A numbering orders pigeons and holes; a dance moves the pigeons of a matching in increasing order, each to a strictly larger hole unoccupied at that moment (Impagliazzo–Pudlák–Sgall, Definition 2.3, as that entry quotes it); \(T_d\) are the matching monomials of degree at most \(d\) and \(B_d\) the basis of that entry. From that entry, used below: “\(C\) is \(H\) plus the lift of \(K_D\)”, with \(K_D=\operatorname{span}(B_D\setminus T_D)\), and \(B_D\) is a basis of \(\operatorname{span}(T_D)\) for \(D\le\lceil N/2\rceil\) (Proposition 2.8).

Polynomial numbering transfer

Statement. Lemma (polynomial numbering transfer). Let \(D\le\lceil N/2\rceil\), let \(\mathcal E\) be a set of polynomial errors \(f\) of degree at most \(D-K\), and let \(M\) be the set of matching monomials \(x_E\) occurring in some error of \(\mathcal E\). If one numbering has, for every \(x_E\in M\) and every \(x_t\in T_K\cap B_K\) with \(t\cup E\) a matching, \(x_{t\cup E}\in B_D\), then \(\bigcap_{f\in\mathcal E}\operatorname{Ann}_C(f)\cap V_K=\bigcap_{f\in\mathcal E}\operatorname{Ann}_H(f)\cap V_K+(C\cap V_K)\).

Proof. It is the proof of lem:admissible-numbering-transfer with the monomial \(x_E\) replaced by a polynomial, as the band review's Decision proposed. \(\supseteq\): \(H\subseteq C\), and \((C\cap V_K)f\subseteq C\) since the product has degree at most \(D\). \(\subseteq\): let \(u\in V_K\) with \(uf\in C\) for every \(f\in\mathcal E\). As in that proof, \(u\equiv k+r\) modulo \(H\) with \(k\in K_K\), so \(k\in C\cap V_K\), and \(r=\sum_{t\in T_K\cap B_K}c_tx_t\). Then \(kf\in C\) and the member of \(H\cap V_K\) times \(f\) lies in \(H\) (non-matching monomials of degree at most \(D\)), so \(rf\in C\). Write \(f=\sum_{E'}c_{E'}x_{E'}\). Modulo \(H\), every product \(x_tx_{E'}\) that is not a matching vanishes, and every other one is \(x_{t\cup E'}\in T_D\cap B_D\) by the property, \(x_{E'}\in M\). So \(rf\) is modulo \(H\) a combination of elements of \(T_D\cap B_D\). Since \(C\) is \(H\) plus the lift of \(K_D=\operatorname{span}(B_D\setminus T_D)\) and \(B_D\) is a basis, that combination lies in \(K_D\) only if it is zero: \(rf\in H\). So \(r\in\bigcap_f\operatorname{Ann}_H(f)\), and \(u\) is \(r\) plus members of \(C\cap V_K\). \(\square\)

Linear errors

Statement. Corollary (linear error transfer). Let \(2\le D\le\lceil N/2\rceil\), \(K=1\), any number of pigeons, and let \(\mathcal E\) be a set of polynomials of degree at most \(1\) with \(S\) the set of edges \(x_{ph}\) occurring in them. For a numbering with hole ranks \(0,\dots,N-1\), the property of the lemma holds for \(M=\{1\}\cup S\) exactly when (i) no edge of \(S\) lies in the hole of rank \(N-1\); (ii) every edge of \(S\) in the hole of rank \(N-2\) has its pigeon first in the pigeon order; (iii) every edge of \(S\) in the hole of rank \(N-3\) has its pigeon last. When some numbering satisfies (i)–(iii), \(\bigcap_{f\in\mathcal E}\operatorname{Ann}_C(f)\cap V_1=\bigcap_{f\in\mathcal E}\operatorname{Ann}_H(f)\cap V_1+(C\cap V_1)\). In particular this holds when the holes of \(S\) miss three holes, or miss one hole while the edges of \(S\) in two further holes have one pigeon each, distinct.

Proof. The monomial \(1\) has the property for every \(t\). For \(x_E=x_{ph}\) and \(t=\varnothing\): \(x_E\in B_D\) exactly when the one pigeon can move, that is when \(h\) is not the top hole: clause (i). For \(t=\{(a,b)\}\) with a dance (so \(b\) is not the top hole), \(a\ne p\), \(b\ne h\): \(t\cup E\) has two pigeons, in holes of ranks \(r_1\) (the pigeon moving first) and \(r_2\), both at most \(N-2\). Statement. Corollary (two-factor kernels). Over any field, let \(N\ge5\), \(m\ge4\), \(K=1\), \(q_1=x_{00}+x_{11}\), \(q_2=x_{22}+x_{33}\). Then (a) for \(2\le D\le\lceil N/2\rceil\), \(\operatorname{Ann}_C(1+q_1)\cap\operatorname{Ann}_C(1+q_2)\cap V_1=C\cap V_1\); and (b) for \(3\le D\le\lceil N/2\rceil\), the common kernel in \(V_1\) of any set of errors of degree at most \(D-1\) that contains, for each \(\nu\), the errors \((1+q_\nu)x_{ij}\) over every hole \(j\) of some pigeon \(i\), is \(C\cap V_1\); the errors \((1+q_\nu)x_e\) over every edge are such a set.

Proof. (a) The linear error transfer holds at every \(2\le D\le\lceil N/2\rceil\); the argument holds at any such \(D\). \(S=\{x_{00},x_{11},x_{22},x_{33}\}\) uses holes \(0,1,2,3\); rank the holes \(0,1,\dots,N-1\) in order. For \(N\ge7\) the ranks of \(S\) are at most \(3\le N-4\), so (i)–(iii) hold with any pigeon order. For \(N=6\), hole \(3\) has rank \(N-3\) and holds only \(x_{33}\): put pigeon \(3\) last. For \(N=5\), hole \(3\) has rank \(N-2\) and hole \(2\) rank \(N-3\): put pigeon \(3\) first and pigeon \(2\) last, possible since \(m\ge4\) gives other pigeons and \(3\ne2\). So by the linear error transfer the kernel is \(\operatorname{Ann}_H(1+q_1)\cap\operatorname{Ann}_H(1+q_2)\cap V_1+C\cap V_1\). For \(u=c+\sum_ea_ex_e\), \(u(1+q_1)\in H\) exactly when every matching monomial's coefficient vanishes, \(H\cap V_3\) being spanned by the non-matching monomials. The constant gives \(c=0\); the edge \(x_e\), \(e\notin\{00,11\}\), gives \(a_e=0\); \(x_{00}\) gives \(2a_{00}+c=0\), \(x_{11}\) gives \(2a_{11}+c=0\), and \(x_{00}x_{11}\) gives \(a_{00}+a_{11}=0\). So \(\operatorname{Ann}_H(1+q_1)\cap V_1\) is \(\operatorname{span}\{q_1\}\) in characteristic \(2\) and \(0\) otherwise, likewise for \(q_2\), and the two intersect in \(0\). (b) \(C\cap V_1\) lies in the common kernel of any errors of degree at most \(D-1\), the products having degree at most \(D\). Conversely, if \(u\in V_1\) kills \((1+q_\nu)x_{ij}\) for every hole \(j\) of a pigeon \(i\), then summing over \(j\), \(u(1+q_\nu)(1-Q_i)\in C\), and \(u(1+q_\nu)Q_i\in C\) (degree \(3\le D\)), so \(u(1+q_\nu)\in C\) for both \(\nu\), and \(u\in C\cap V_1\) by (a). \(\square\)

Degenerate cases. \(\mathcal E\) empty: both sides are \(V_K\).
