Working mathematical context

Counts. \(v\) is the number of old Boolean variables, \(\Lambda\) the formula encoding length, \(\ell\) the approximation-level count, \(A\) a fixed inventory/proof-size exponent, and \(D\) the PC ceiling. Keep these distinct from holes, edges and graph vertices.

Exact sufficient source. \(\Gamma_v\) contains Booleanity and has \(1\notin C_D(\Gamma_v)\) at the moving cutoff. At most \(v^A\) complete binary ENS blocks have strictly lower-level product-only dependencies: each input is an old polynomial of fixed degree at most \(r\) plus a constant linear combination of earlier whole products. Earlier coefficients occur through those products, not separately. With uniform \(h=\lfloor v^\epsilon\rfloor\), \(0<\epsilon<1/(3\ell)\) the theorem excludes original PC degree \(\lfloor v^{1/3}\rfloor\) for large \(v\). Use \(K=D-\lceil v^{1/3}\rceil\) and one final multiplier; the last approximation degree is \(v^{o(1)}\). Repeated weight-selection buffers are unnecessary in this particular construction.

Original costs. A complete block of accuracy \(h\) and input ceiling \(\delta\) has product ceiling \(h(\delta+1)\) and companion ceiling \(h(\delta+1)+\delta\). All companions and fresh Boolean coefficient equations count; zero padding never resets these costs. The binary PC compiler has its own coherent-profile and literal-CNF hypotheses; the ordinary NS compiler separately retains a proof-height factor. Neither is automatic for a new encoding.

Certificate limitations. Respectful expansion certifies at most \(v/3\) in its nontrivial range; the recorded immunity criterion at most \(v/4\). These are not upper bounds on actual PC degree. The generic-set-system certificate also stays at most \(1/3+o(1)\) of the variable count.

Actual graph upper bounds. Boolean modular graph constraints in characteristic coprime to the modulus have degree at most \(m/4+O_\Delta(\sqrt{n\log n})\) on fixed-degree families, with one Boolean variable per edge. The full ternary encoding has the same edge-count upper bound, with its different variable count. For arbitrary unsatisfiable Boolean constraints local to graph vertex stars, matched frontier elimination gives \((1/2-1/(4\Delta-2))m+O_\Delta(\sqrt{m\log m})\). The canonical positive-flow corollary excludes polynomial canonical encodings for \(\ell\ge3\); fixed degree also excludes \(\ell=2\). Growing-degree depth two and noncanonical encodings are not settled by that corollary. Do not transfer its upper bound to arbitrary CNFs.

Encoding warning. Unary PHP has \(v=N(N+1)\) and bit PHP \(v=(N+1)\log_2N\). Their half-hole lower bounds are not near half the variable count. A larger input width or auxiliary encoding can erase apparent normalization gains. The family may change; the full size/degree comparison may not.
