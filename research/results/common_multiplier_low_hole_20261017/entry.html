Setting

The functional pigeonhole system with \(m\) pigeons and \(N\) holes (Booleanity, hole and functionality axioms, pigeon axioms \(Q_i=1-\sum_hx_{ih}\), multilinear ring), over any field; \(C\) its degree-\(D\) closure, \(H\) that of the hole and functionality axioms, \(W=C\cap V_K\); a numbering of holes and pigeons, \(B_d\) the basis of lem:admissible-numbering-transfer's record (partial maps whose matching part has a dance: pigeons moved once each, in increasing order, each to a larger hole, every step one-to-one) and \(T_K\) the matching monomials of degree at most \(K\). The edges of a polynomial of degree at most \(1\) are the variables with nonzero coefficient.

Low-hole linear transfer

Statement. Lemma (low-hole linear transfer). Over any field, on the functional pigeonhole system with any number of pigeons and \(N\) holes, let \(D\le\lceil N/2\rceil\) and \(K+1\le D\), and let \(F\) be a set of polynomials of degree at most \(1\) whose edges all lie in a set \(L\) of holes and have their pigeons in a set \(P\), with \(N-|L|\ge\max\bigl(2K-1,\;K+1+\min(K,|P|-1)\bigr)\). Then the common kernel of \(F\) in \(V_K\) modulo \(C\) is the common kernel modulo \(H\) plus \(W\). In particular \(N-|L|\ge2K+1\) suffices for every \(P\), and for edges on two pigeons \(N-|L|\ge\max(2K-1,K+2)\).

Proof. By lem:polynomial-numbering-transfer, whose errors have degree at most \(D-K\) (here \(1\le D-K\)), it suffices to give one numbering under which, for every matching monomial \(x_E\) of a member of \(F\) and every \(x_t\in T_K\cap B_K\) with \(t\cup E\) a matching, \(t\cup E\) has a dance. Rank the holes of \(L\) lowest, \(1,\dots,\ell\) with \(\ell=|L|\), the others in any order, and the pigeons of \(P\) last, the others first, each group in any order, as the nested-hole corollary of lem:functional-killer-transfer's record ranks its error pigeons. The matching monomials of a polynomial of degree at most \(1\) are \(1\) and its edges. For \(E=\varnothing\), \(t\cup E=t\) has a dance. Let \(E=\{(p,j)\}\) with \(j\le\ell\), and \(t\) with a dance \(\delta\), \(p\notin t\), hole \(j\) not used by \(t\). Write \(h_q\) and \(g_q\) for the hole and the target of a pigeon \(q\) of \(t\) in \(\delta\); the targets are distinct, and at most one pigeon \(q_0\) has \(g_{q_0}=j\), with \(h_{q_0}<j\). Run the pigeons of \(t\cup E\) in increasing order. A pigeon \(q\ne q_0\) of \(t\) moves to \(g_q\): at its turn \(g_q\) holds no pigeon of \(t\), as in \(\delta\), and not \(p\), which is at \(j\ne g_q\) or at the hole \(g\) chosen below, which is no target of \(\delta\). If \(q_0\) comes after \(p\), it moves to \(j\), vacated by \(p\). If \(q_0\) comes before \(p\), it moves to a hole \(g_0>h_{q_0}\) that is not \(j\), holds no pigeon at that turn and is no target of \(\delta\): the holes above \(h_{q_0}\) number at least \(N-j+1\ge N-\ell+1\), and the excluded ones at most \(1+(K-1)+(K-1)=2K-1\) (the hole \(j\), the holes and targets of the other pigeons of \(t\), a moved pigeon's hole being its target), so one is left as \(N-\ell\ge2K-1\). At its turn \(p\) moves to a hole \(g>j\) that holds no pigeon and is no target of \(\delta\) nor \(g_0\). Since \(p\in P\) and \(P\) is ranked last, the pigeons of \(t\) after \(p\) lie in \(P\setminus\{p\}\), at most \(a=\min(K,|P|-1)\) of them, each excluding its hole and its target; the at most \(K-a\) pigeons of \(t\) before \(p\) have moved and exclude one hole each, their target (\(g_0\) for \(q_0\)). The holes above \(j\) number \(N-j\ge N-\ell\), and the excluded ones at most \((K-a)+2a=K+a\), so one is left as \(N-\ell\ge K+1+a\). Every move goes to a larger hole and keeps the map one-to-one, so \(t\cup E\) has a dance. \(\square\)

Hypotheses, each with its step. The reduction uses \(D\le\lceil N/2\rceil\) and \(K+1\le D\); the dance uses \(N-|L|\ge K+1+\min(K,|P|-1)\) at the move of \(p\), the weakest form that step needs with \(P\) ranked last, and \(N-|L|\ge2K-1\) at the re-target of \(q_0\); the conclusions hold over every field, for every number of pigeons and every set of polynomials of degree at most \(1\) with edges in \(L\) on pigeons of \(P\):

  • \(D\le\lceil N/2\rceil\), any field, any number of pigeons: lem:polynomial-numbering-transfer's own hypotheses, used in the reduction.
  • \(K+1\le D\): the errors' degree \(1\le D-K\) in that lemma.
  • \(N-|L|\ge K+1+\min(K,|P|-1)\): the free hole for \(p\) above \(j\le\ell\), with \(K-a\) holes excluded by moved pigeons and \(2a\) by the at most \(a=\min(K,|P|-1)\) pigeons of \(t\) after \(p\), all in \(P\).
  • \(N-|L|\ge2K-1\): the re-target of \(q_0\), above \(h_{q_0}<j\), avoiding \(j\) and the holes and targets of the other \(K-1\) pigeons.
  • Edges in \(L\), ranked lowest, and their pigeons \(P\) ranked last: \(j\le\ell\) in both counts and \(a\le\min(K,|P|-1)\). One numbering serves every member of \(F\) and every edge, so the statement is for sets.

Without the pigeon order the construction needs \(2K+1\), and that is exact for it: with \(N-\ell=2K\), free holes \(f_1<\dots<f_{2K}\) above \(\ell\), \(t=\{(q_i,f_i):i\le K\}\) dancing by \(q_i\to f_{K+i}\), and an edge \((p,\ell)\) with \(p<q_1<\dots<q_K\), the pigeon \(p\) moves first to a hole above \(f_K\), and then each \(q_i\) needs a hole above \(f_i\) other than the vacated ones below it, \(K\) pigeons for \(K-1\) holes (the reviewer's example, checked by hand). With \(P\) ranked last the room at \(p\)'s move is exact too: with \(N-\ell=K+a\), \(a\) pigeons of \(P\setminus\{p\}\) at \(f_1,\dots,f_a\) and \(K-a\) pigeons outside \(P\) at the holes \(\ell-(K-a),\dots,\ell-1\), the dance of \(t\) sends the low ones to \(f_{a+1},\dots,f_K\) and the others to \(f_{K+1},\dots,f_{K+a}\), while with the edge \((p,\ell)\) the \(K-a\) low pigeons and \(p\) need holes above \(f_a\) and the \(a\) pigeons of \(P\) need \(a\) more: \(K+1\) pigeons for \(K\) holes (the reviewer's second example, checked by hand).

Degenerate cases. \(L=\varnothing\): every member is a constant \(c\); for \(c\ne0\) both sides are \(W\) (\(H\cap V_K\subseteq W\)), for \(c=0\) both are \(V_K\). \(K=0\): the room is \(N-|L|\ge1\); both sides are sets of constants \(c\), on the left those with \(cf\in C\) for every \(f\in F\), on the right those with \(cf\in H\), plus \(W=C\cap V_0=0\) (no refutation at \(D\le\lceil N/2\rceil\)): so for \(c\ne0\) the lemma says \(f\in C\) only when \(f\in H\); a polynomial of degree at most \(1\) lies in \(H\) only when it is \(0\), so the case says that a nonzero \(f\) whose edges miss a hole is not in \(C\). At \(K=0\) and two pigeons the room is \(\max(-1,1)=1\), where the corollary's \(\rho=\max(2K-1,K+2)\), written for \(K\ge1\), would give \(2\). A polynomial of degree \(1\) with an edge in every hole: outside the hypothesis, as ex:top-degree-linear-killer's error \(x_{10}+x_{21}+x_{22}\) at three holes, where the transfer fails.

Nonvacuity. Seven pigeons and six holes, \(D=3\le\lceil6/2\rceil\), \(K=2\), \(F=\{E_A+Q_2\}\) with \(|A|=2\): \(L=A\), \(P=\{1,2\}\), \(N-|L|=4\ge\max(2K-1,K+2)=\max(3,4)=4\), while \(2K+1=5>4\), so the pigeon order is the step used; check:partition-sizes-seven-pigeons computed excess \(0\) there over \(\mathbb F_2\) (\(535\) three times). cor:linear-error-transfer, stated at \(K=1\), does not cover \(K=2\). Plainest bound. The containment \(\supseteq\) holds always, so the common kernel modulo \(C\) is at least the domain side plus \(W\); the single-edge lemma lem:functional-killer-transfer gives each monomial \(x_{pj}\) alone, not the polynomial \(1+\sum_{j\in A}(x_{1j}-x_{2j})\), whose kernel is not the intersection of its monomials' kernels.

Baseline. cor:linear-error-transfer's in-particular clause, at \(K=1\): an edge set missing three holes transfers. At \(K=1\) the lemma's room is \(\max(1,2+\min(1,|P|-1))\le3\), so it contains that clause, and for one pigeon (\(|P|=1\)) asks only \(N-|L|\ge2\); the corollary's exact criterion (i)–(iii) is stronger there. The lemma adds every \(K\ge2\), by the dance in which the error's pigeon, ranked last, takes a hole outside \(t\)'s dance.

Partition errors at every K

Statement. Corollary (partition transfer at every K). On the functional pigeonhole system over any field, with two distinct pigeons written \(1\), \(2\) among any number, \(N\) holes, \(D\le\lceil N/2\rceil\) and \(1\le K\le D-1\), write \(\rho=\max(2K-1,K+2)\). If \(N-|A|\ge\rho\), then \(\operatorname{Ann}_C(E_A)\cap V_K=\operatorname{Ann}_H(E_A+Q_2)\cap V_K+W\), and if \(|A|\ge\rho\), the same with \(E_A+Q_1\); in both cases the single excess of \(E_A\) is \(0\). For a set of partition errors \(E_{A_1},\dots,E_{A_r}\) and a choice of \(R_i=E_{A_i}+Q_2\) or \(E_{A_i}+Q_1\), with \(L_i=A_i\) or \(A_i^c\) accordingly, if \(N-|L_1\cup\dots\cup L_r|\ge\rho\), the common kernel in \(V_K\) modulo \(C\) is \(\bigcap_i\operatorname{Ann}_H(R_i)\cap V_K+W\): with \(a\) the largest, over choices of one representative per error, of their domain common kernel plus \(W\) (the window comparison's \(a\), taken over \(r\) errors), \(\dim J=a\), one choice explaining it, with no mixing. At the top degree \(D=\lceil N/2\rceil=K+1\) with \(N\le8\) (so \(K\le3\) and \(\rho=K+2=D+1\)), \(E_A\) has single excess \(0\) whenever \(|A|\le\lfloor N/2\rfloor-1\) or \(|A|\ge\lceil N/2\rceil+1\): the only-if direction of conj:partition-excess-middle at \(n\le8\), over every field and every number of pigeons; for \(N\ge9\) there, \(\rho=2D-3\), and the single excess is \(0\) for \(|A|\le N-2D+3\) or \(|A|\ge2D-3\).

Proof. \(uQ_2\in C\) for \(u\in V_K\), of degree \(K+1\le D\), so \(\operatorname{Ann}_C(E_A)\cap V_K=\operatorname{Ann}_C(E_A+Q_2)\cap V_K\); the edges of \(E_A+Q_2=1+\sum_{j\in A}(x_{1j}-x_{2j})\) lie in \(L=A\), and the lemma applies; for \(E_A+Q_1\), \(L=A^c\). The domain side of every representative plus \(W\) lies in the closure kernel, so the best single representative reaches it. For the set, the same step for each \(i\) replaces \(E_{A_i}\) by \(R_i\) without changing the common kernel modulo \(C\), and the \(R_i\) have their edges in \(L_1\cup\dots\cup L_r\), so the lemma applies to \(\{R_1,\dots,R_r\}\), their edges lying on the two pigeons \(1\), \(2\). Always \(a\le\dim J\), every choice's domain common kernel plus \(W\) lying in \(J\); the lemma gives \(J=\bigcap_i\operatorname{Ann}_H(R_i)\cap V_K+W\), whose dimension is at most \(a\), so \(\dim J=a\). At the top degree, \(\rho=K+2=D+1\) when \(K\le3\), and \(N-|A|\ge D+1\) reads \(|A|\le N-\lceil N/2\rceil-1=\lfloor N/2\rfloor-1\), the \(Q_1\) side \(|A|\ge\lceil N/2\rceil+1\); for \(K\ge4\), \(\rho=2K-1=2D-3\). \(\square\) At seven pigeons, \(K=2\), \(\rho=4\), the pair clause needs \(|L_1\cup L_2|\le2\): it decides the zero-excess pair orbits of two distinct singletons and of a singleton inside a pair, \(\dim J=a\) on both, and leaves the others; at nine pigeons and eight holes, \(D=4\), \(K=2\) (the top degree is \(K=3\)), it needs \(|L_1\cup L_2|\le4\).

Nonvacuity. Seven pigeons and six holes, \(D=3\), \(K=2\), \(\rho=4\): \(|A|=1\) and \(|A|=2\) satisfy \(N-|A|\ge4\), and check:partition-sizes-seven-pigeons computed excess \(0\) at both over \(\mathbb F_2\) (\(519\) and \(535\) three times), which the corollary proves over every field and pigeon count; \(|A|=3\) has \(N-|A|=3<4\), not covered, where the computed excess is \(15\). Against check:top-degree-partition-kernels's other boards: \(n=5\), \(K=2\), \(\rho=4\): \(|A|\le1\) or \(\ge4\), computed \(0\) there and \(12\) at \(|A|=2,3\); \(n=4\), \(K=1\), \(\rho=3\): \(|A|\le1\) or \(\ge3\), computed \(0\), and \(1\) at \(|A|=2\); \(n=3\): \(|A|\in\{0,3\}\), computed \(0\), and \(1\) at \(|A|=1,2\).

The conjecture at one and two holes

Statement. Refutation (middle excess on small boards). conj:partition-excess-middle, registered by the partition sizes entry for every \(n\), is false at \(n=1\) and \(n=2\): over every field, with \(n+1\) pigeons, \(n\) holes, \(D=\lceil n/2\rceil=1\) and \(K=0\), a middle-size partition error has single excess \(0\). Corrected conjecture (middle excess from three holes): its statement for \(n\ge3\), the range of its evidence.

Proof. At \(D=1\) the closure \(C\) is the span of the pigeon axioms, the other axioms having degree \(2\), and \(W=C\cap V_0=0\): \(1=\sum_ic_iQ_i\) forces every \(c_i=0\) by the coefficients of the variables, and then \(1=0\). At \(n=1\), \(|A|=0\) is a middle size (\(\lfloor1/2\rfloor=0\)) and \(E_\varnothing=x_{20}=1-Q_2\): a constant \(c\) has \(cE_\varnothing\in C\) exactly when \(c\in C\), that is \(c=0\), so the closure kernel is \(0=W\), and the excess is \(0\). At \(n=2\), \(|A|=1\) with \(A=\{a\}\) and the other hole \(b\): \(E_A+Q_2=1+x_{1a}-x_{2a}\), and \(c(1+x_{1a}-x_{2a})=\sum_ic_iQ_i\) with \(c\ne0\) forces \(c_1=0\) by the coefficient of \(x_{1b}\), \(c_2=0\) by that of \(x_{2b}\), \(c_3=0\) by \(x_{3a}\), and then \(c=0\); so again the closure kernel is \(0=W\) and the excess is \(0\) (the lemma at \(K=0\), room \(1\), gives the same). \(\square\)

Nonvacuity. The instances themselves: two pigeons and one hole, three pigeons and two holes. Plainest bound. The excess is at least \(0\); the coefficient count above decides it.

General statement. lem:low-hole-linear-transfer: over any field, for \(D\le\lceil N/2\rceil\), \(K+1\le D\) and any set of polynomials of degree at most \(1\) with edges in a set \(L\) of holes and on a set \(P\) of pigeons, \(N-|L|\ge\max(2K-1,K+1+\min(K,|P|-1))\), the common kernel in \(V_K\) modulo \(C\) is the one modulo \(H\) plus \(C\cap V_K\); cor:partition-transfer-every-k gives the only-if direction of conj:partition-excess-middle at \(n\le8\).
