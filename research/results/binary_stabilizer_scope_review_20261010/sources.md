# Source scope for the stabilizer review

Read on 10 October 2026; statements are paraphrased, no third-party full text saved.

- Stacks 00J9, Lemma 10.32.6:
  https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/00J9
  In a commutative ring, reduction modulo a locally nilpotent ideal gives a
  bijection on idempotents. Both proofs were read. The constructive correction
  improves the square-error ideal power; it supplies no polynomial-degree ledger.
- Stacks 00DV, Lemma 10.20.1:
  https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/00DV
  A finite module M with IM=M is zero when I lies in the Jacobson radical.
  Nilpotence gives the analogous conclusion without finite generation.
  The determinant proof and listed variants were read.
- Stacks 00IN, Lemma 10.51.2:
  https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/00IN
  For finite N subset M over Noetherian R and ideal I, there is a constant c
  such that I^n M intersect N = I^(n-c)(I^c M intersect N) for n>=c.
  Full short Rees-module proof read; no quantitative bound imported.
- Aharonov, Arad, Landau, Vazirani, arXiv:1011.3445v5:
  https://arxiv.org/html/1011.3445
  Read introduction's Hamiltonian/Hilbert-space hypotheses and Lemma 1.1
  (lines 59–86 in web extraction). Frustration-free, gapped 1D local
  Hamiltonians admit contraction on the orthogonal complement of the ground
  space via alternating layers of ground-space projections.
  No full proof/paper audit. No finite-field analogue imported.
- Robin Evans, Graphical Models, Chapter 7, Proposition 7.2:
  https://www.stats.ox.ac.uk/~evans/gms/_book/jt.html
  Read definitions, running-intersection Proposition 7.1 and proof,
  consistent nonnegative potentials, and marginal-gluing Proposition 7.2
  with its proof and separator normalization. No field-valued support theorem
  is asserted. The countercontrol is already in our record.

Search detours: Fitting decomposition was considered but not used; no result
from the search excerpts is relied upon. Search of signed marginal gluing
found the existing acyclic support obstruction before any duplicate lemma was drafted.
