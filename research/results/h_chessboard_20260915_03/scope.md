# H10 scope

For a≥1 and b≥2, on the (a+1)-by-b board, prove first-row star intersections have a vertex exactly for proper column subsets, while the full intersection has only the empty face. Identify the nerve with simplexBoundary univ. Reuse H05 to prove its ExactAt k for k+1<b, and AcyclicThrough n for n<b. H08 cone closure will be explicitly connected to H04 cone_exact for convenient assembly. H12 arithmetic/induction and H13 filling are not undertaken here.
