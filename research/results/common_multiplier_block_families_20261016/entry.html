General statement. lem:block-family-multiplier-degree: on the cube, for coordinate families on the blocks of a hypergraph \(H\), with approximators nonzero at some nonzero pattern of their block, the least degree of a surviving common multiplier is at least the transversal number \(\tau(H)\), and Pang's own factors on a chain of forms attain it over every prime field; over \(\mathbb F_2\) with \(|B|-1\) factors per block every choice satisfies the hypothesis, so on all \((t+1)\)-subsets the degrees over choices range from \(n-t\) to \(n\) (the upper end, through \(\delta_0\), when \(K\ge n\) and \(n+a+1\le D\)), both attained at \(t=1\), while Pang's summed criterion with \(t\) factors certifies nothing at any \(K\) once \(\binom n{t+1}\ge2^t\).

Block-family multiplier degree

Statement. Over a field \(F\), let \(Q=\{0,1\}^n\), let \(\mathcal C_D\) be the degree-\(D\) part of the ideal of the Boolean axioms \(x_i^2-x_i\) (so a polynomial of degree at most \(D\) lies in \(\mathcal C_D\) exactly when it vanishes on \(Q\)), and let \(H\) be a family of nonempty blocks \(B\subseteq[n]\), with transversal number \(\tau(H)\), the least size of a set meeting every block, equal to \(n-\alpha(H)\) for \(\alpha(H)\) the largest size of a set containing no block. For each \(B\in H\) take the family \((x_i)_{i\in B}\) and an approximator \(P_B\in F[x_i:i\in B]\) of degree at most \(a\); call \(f\) of degree at most \(K\) a common multiplier when \(fP_Bx_i\in\mathcal C_D\) for all \(B\in H\), \(i\in B\), with \(K+a+1\le D\), and surviving when \(f\) is nonzero on \(Q\) (equivalently \(f\notin\mathcal C_D\): the class \(1\) is kept alive). (a) If each \(P_B\) is nonzero at some nonzero \(v_B\in\{0,1\}^B\), every surviving common multiplier has degree at least \(\tau(H)\). (b) Over \(\mathbb F_p\), with an ordering \(b_1,\ldots,b_{|B|}\) of each block, the product of Pang's factors \(P_B=\prod_{j<|B|}\bigl(1-(x_{b_j}-x_{b_{j+1}})^{p-1}\bigr)\), of degree \(a=(p-1)(\max|B|-1)\), is nonzero on \(Q\) exactly at the patterns \(0\) and \(1_B\), and when \(\tau(H)+a+1\le D\), \(\pi_T=\prod_{i\in T}(1-x_i)\) for a least transversal \(T\) is a surviving common multiplier of degree \(\tau(H)\). (c) Over \(\mathbb F_2\), every product \(P_B=\prod_{j=1}^s(1+q_j)\) of \(s\le|B|-1\) of Pang's factors, \(q_j\in\operatorname{span}\{x_i:i\in B\}\), satisfies the hypothesis of (a); with \(s\ge|B|\) factors \(q_j=x_{b_j}\) give \(P_B=[x_B=0]\), and \(f=1\) is a common multiplier of degree \(0\). (d) Over \(\mathbb F_2\), for \(H\) all \((t+1)\)-subsets of \([n]\), \(t+1\le n\), and \(t\) factors per block: every surviving common multiplier has degree at least \(n-t\), and when \(D\ge n+1\) the least degree over all choices is \(n-t\); Pang's criterion \(S(1-\eta)^t<1\) with these \(t\) factors, \(S=\binom n{t+1}\), \(d=1\) and \(\eta=\tfrac12N_n(K-1)/N_n(K)\), fails as an inequality at every \(1\le K\le n\) when \(\binom n{t+1}\ge2^t\), in particular for every \(n\ge2t+1\). (e) Over \(\mathbb F_2\), for \(H\) all pairs of \([n]\), \(n\ge4\), and one factor per pair, some choice forces every surviving common multiplier to be a multiple of \(\delta_0=\prod_i(1+x_i)\), of degree \(n\); and \(\delta_0\) is a surviving common multiplier for every approximator when \(K\ge n\) and \(n+a+1\le D\).

Proof. Since \(\deg(fP_Bx_i)\le K+a+1\le D\), the condition \(fP_Bx_i\in\mathcal C_D\) says that \(fP_Bx_i\) vanishes on \(Q\): \(f(x)P_B(x_B)=0\) at every \(x\in Q\) with \(x_B\ne0\). (a) At a point with \(x_B=v_B\ne0\), \(P_B(v_B)\ne0\), so \(f\) vanishes on the cylinder \(\{x:x_B=v_B\}\), whose coordinate set is \(B\). The cube is the product domain of lem:cylinder-killer-degree with factors \(\{0,1\}\), base state \(0\) and \(x_{j,1}=x_j\), whose degree is the multilinear degree; by its part (a), a nonzero function vanishing on the union of these cylinders has degree at least \(\tau(H)\). A polynomial of degree at most \(K\) represents a function of degree at most \(K\), so \(\deg f\ge\tau(H)\). (b) On \(Q\), \(x_{b_j}-x_{b_{j+1}}\in\{-1,0,1\}\), and its \((p-1)\)th power is \(1\) exactly when it is nonzero, so \(P_B=1\) exactly where \(x_B\) is constant and \(0\) elsewhere; hence \(P_Bx_i\) is, as a function on \(Q\), the indicator of \(x_B=1_B\). If \(\pi_T(x)\ne0\) then \(x_T=0\), and \(T\) meets \(B\), so \(x_B\ne1_B\): \(\pi_TP_Bx_i\) vanishes on \(Q\), its degree is at most \(\tau(H)+a+1\le D\), and it lies in \(\mathcal C_D\). \(\pi_T(0)=1\), so \(\pi_T\) survives; this is the basis element of lem:transversal-killer-basis (a) for the up-set generated by the blocks. (c) The forms \(q_1,\ldots,q_s\) on \(\mathbb F_2^B\) have a common kernel of dimension at least \(|B|-s\ge1\), which contains a nonzero \(v_B\), and \(P_B(v_B)=1\). With \(q_j=x_{b_j}\) for every \(j\le|B|\), \(P_B=1\) only at \(x_B=0\), where every \(x_i\), \(i\in B\), vanishes. (d) A set containing no \((t+1)\)-subset has at most \(t\) elements, so \(\tau(H)=n-t\); by (a) and (c), every choice of \(t=|B|-1\) factors forces degree at least \(n-t\), and over \(\mathbb F_2\) the chain of (b) has \(t\) factors and \(a=t\), so it attains \(n-t\) when \(n-t+t+1\le D\). For the criterion, \(N_n(K-1)\le N_n(K)\) gives \(\eta\le\tfrac12\), so \(S(1-\eta)^t\ge\binom n{t+1}2^{-t}\ge1\) when \(\binom n{t+1}\ge2^t\); for \(n\ge2t+1\), \(\binom n{t+1}\ge\binom{2t+1}{t+1}=\binom{2t+1}t\ge\binom{2t}t\ge2^t\). (e) Take \(q_{\{i,i+1\}}=x_{i+1}\) for \(i\in[n]\), indices modulo \(n\), so that \(f\) must vanish where \(x_i=1\) and \(x_{i+1}=0\); take \(q_{\{1,3\}}=x_1+x_3\), which forces vanishing where \(x_1=x_3=1\) (the pair \(\{1,3\}\) is not a cycle pair since \(n\ge4\)); give the other pairs any nonzero form. A nonzero point other than \(1_{[n]}\) has a cyclic step from a \(1\) to a \(0\), and \(1_{[n]}\) has \(x_1=x_3=1\); so \(f\) vanishes at every nonzero point and is a multiple of \(\delta_0\) on \(Q\), of degree \(n\) unless zero. Conversely \(\delta_0P_Bx_i\) vanishes on \(Q\) since \(\delta_0\) is supported at \(0\), where \(x_i=0\).

Hypotheses, each with its step. \(K+a+1\le D\) turns membership into vanishing on the cube, in the first step; one nonzero pattern at which \(P_B\) is nonzero, per block, forces the cylinder in (a), and this is its weakest form: without it, as for \(s\ge|B|\) factors in (c), the family imposes nothing; the Boolean axioms make the domain the cube, as lem:cylinder-killer-degree needs; nonempty blocks make the cylinders proper and \(\tau(H)\) finite; the field enters only in (c) to (e), where Pang's factors \(1-q^{p-1}\) with \(p>2\) need not be nonzero at a nonzero Boolean pattern (over \(\mathbb F_3\), \(q=x_1+x_2\) gives \(P_B=1\) only at \(x_B=0\), so one factor already kills a pair family), so (c) to (e) are stated over \(\mathbb F_2\), and (b) holds over every \(\mathbb F_p\); \(1\le K\le n\) and \(d=1\) are Pang's in (d). The conclusions hold for the protected class \(1\) in the cube's Boolean ideal, the domain model, and are not shown for any proper closure of an unsatisfiable system.

Degenerate cases. \(H\) empty: \(\tau=0\) and \(f=1\). A block of size \(1\): its only nonzero pattern is \(1\), and \(f\) must vanish where \(x_b=1\). A block whose approximator vanishes at every nonzero pattern imposes nothing and leaves (a) for the others. \(t+1=n\): one block, \(\tau=1\), and \(n-t=1\). \(n=3\) in (e): the cyclic pairs are all pairs and \(1_{[3]}\) is not forced, so (e) needs \(n\ge4\).

Nonvacuity. Over \(\mathbb F_2\), \(n=4\), \(H\) all pairs (\(t=1\), the families of lem:marginal-refinement-gain (d)), chain approximators \(1+x_a+x_b\), \(a=1\), \(K=3\), \(D=5\), so \(K+a+1=5\le D\): the multiplier \((1+x_1)(1+x_2)(1+x_3)\) has degree \(3=n-t\) and is nonzero at \(0\); by (a) no choice of one-factor approximators admits one of degree at most \(2\); by (e) the cyclic choice admits none below degree \(4\), and \(\delta_0\) has degree \(4\) with \(4+1+1=6>5\), so at \(D=5\) that choice admits no surviving multiplier at all; \(\binom42=6\ge2\), and the inequality of Pang's criterion fails at every \(K\), while his theorem's range \(\Delta+K+d\le D\) is \(K\le3\) there. No parameter ledger applies: the witness verifies no budget of the goal.

Baseline. The indicator of the origin \(\delta_0=\prod_i(1-x_i)\), of degree \(n\), kills every error for every approximator, since every error vanishes at \(0\), and survives; the lemma adds the matching lower bound \(\tau(H)\) for every admissible choice, the chain attaining it (a saving of \(t\) degrees on all \((t+1)\)-subsets), and, in (e), that a choice can lose that saving entirely.

Relations. (a) is lem:cylinder-killer-degree (a) applied to the cylinders forced by the approximators; (b) is the basis element of lem:transversal-killer-basis (a) after the approximators turn the errors into the monomials \(x_B\), and that lemma's last clause, the vertex-cover number for Pang's \(t=1\) pair families with \(q=x_a+x_b\), is the case of (b) with blocks of size \(2\). The elementary baseline is \(\delta_0\), of degree \(n\), which survives for every choice; what (a) and (b) add is that over \(\mathbb F_2\) with at most \(|B|-1\) factors per block no approximator choice goes below the transversal number, and the chain reaches it, a saving of \(t\) degrees on all \((t+1)\)-subsets, while (e) shows that a choice can also lose them all. It strengthens part (d) of lem:marginal-refinement-gain, which shows one round from \(P=1\) gaining nothing at \(K=1\) for the six pairs on four coordinates: by (a), no one-factor choice admits a multiplier below degree \(3\). The Sauer–Shelah threshold named in that entry, impossibility when \(\sum_{j\le t}\binom nj<2^{n-K}\), is weaker than (d)'s \(K<n-t\), since \(\sum_{j\le t}\binom nj\ge2^t\). lem:block-additive-degree concerns errors local to the blocks of a product domain; the forced cylinders here overlap, and it does not give (a). cor:monomial-code-approximation, read by statement, concerns the disjoint-block mixture model with codes chosen per block and \(K/v\) below \(\tfrac12\); its families are not the complete \((t+1)\)-uniform ones, and the two do not compare directly.
