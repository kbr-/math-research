superadditive for aligned gradients, so the superposition is worth sum_m V(kappa_m), V the net dual
value of one scaled Leighton-Shor function. V peaks at the balanced aspect kappa* = (log N)^(-(beta_1
- beta_2)); the class-test lower bound has the form sqrt(kappa N)(log N)^(3/4 - beta_2) with
kappa <= kappa* forced by the other coordinate's penalty, indicating V(kappa) <~ (kappa/kappa*)^(1/2) V*
below and (kappa*/kappa)^(1/2) V* above (after rescaling the gradient). Aspects differing by
(log N)^gamma are off balance by a power of log N; aspects at ratios 2^k give sum 2^(-|k|/2) V* = O(V*):
a bounded factor, not log log N. Opposite-sign overlap is one test function, i.e.
conj:conjugate-class-discrepancy itself. Unless V decays only polylogarithmically in kappa/kappa*, the
refutation architecture fails; make the decay the next entry's lemma. Rigorous gaps: an upper bound on
