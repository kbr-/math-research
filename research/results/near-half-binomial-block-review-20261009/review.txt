Verdict: pass with corrections. The audit of (A) is sound; the decisive test first proposed (evenly covered
set) is answered by two short arguments and should be replaced.
1. Audit: reading of the corollary right (conditions on K, shifts, t_i = ceil(ln(l n^A)/eta_i)+1); eta_i is
the theorem's rate, bounded below by rho^(2 Delta)/4 at shift Delta (reword); binomial ratio at K = alpha n is
of order (alpha/(1-alpha))^Delta; pigeonhole gives 2^(-n Delta/(D-K0)), exp(-Theta(Delta)) only when D-K0 is
of order n; "no better in general" justified by the cube, not by c(D) >= 2^(D-1); varying c_P makes selection
weaker (no common level over n^A families; a random level good for a fraction at the same rate);
gamma*A = O(1), hence gamma = o(1), correct; last sentence correct for small eps (gamma about 4 eps).
2. "Exactly the condition" should be "a sufficient condition"; "low-degree definitions lower them together"
unsupported.
3. Hereditary condition correct; "easy on suitable cosets" has the wrong quantifier: mark or delete.
4. Decisive test: well posed, |E|/2^n <= delta; the window 1+O(sw/n) is from the static span bound. The
statement is true in three lines for any U containing E cut out by forms l_i: g l_i = 0 multilinearly, so
multiplying a degree-d_U refutation of rows plus l_i by g derives g in degree d_U + w: d_U <= d <= d_U + w.
The restricted system is arbitrary: any cover C of a codimension-w subspace U plus the w-row partition of the
complement has covering sum 1 + 2^-w (Sigma_C - 1) and degree at least d(C). No ceiling and no construction;
rows inside U have width w + w'.
5. "Unchanged" right; title's "closed at a quarter" holds for the near-half endpoint only; dropping binomial
closures is a judgment (the n/8 bound is unverified).
6. Different test: which families c_P the prefactors actually produce; if each is the standard-monomial count
of an ideal such as C_D : P, the plateau is needed only for those Hilbert functions. Ends if one P gives an
arbitrary down-closed subfamily.
Suggestions (unverified by the author): rows crossing U are the only way to keep width, then parity off U
must be repaired (pairs of rows agreeing off U and differing on U); compute c_P for one explicit round on the
n = 10 orbit cover.
