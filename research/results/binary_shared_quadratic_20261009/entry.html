Paired parent normalization

Corollary (paired parent normalization). Work over \(\mathbb F_2\). Let a proper Boolean degree-\(D\) pseudoideal \(J_D\) contain an unsatisfiable old family \(\Gamma\), of maximum degree at most \(u\ge2\), including the old Boolean axioms. Let \(w_0\notin J_D\), \(\deg w_0\le b_0\). Choose integers \(\delta\ge1\), \(h\ge\delta\), \(k\ge0\), and a source proof ceiling \[ B\ge\max(u,2h^2+3h),\qquad b_0+\delta B+2\delta\le D. \tag{1} \] Select \(2\delta\) coordinates from the free cube supplied by the weighted restriction theorem for \(w_0\), and split them into two groups of \(\delta\). Make distinguished accuracy-\(h\) bottom blocks on the literal tuples \((1-x_i)\) for these groups. Call their whole products \(P_a,P_b\). Make any further \(2k\) bottom blocks \(P_{s_i},P_{t_i}\), each on at most \(\delta\) old literals; their supports may overlap arbitrarily, including outside the free cube. Every block is complete, with private coefficients and their fields.

In the original source ring put \[ \mathsf U=(P_a,P_{s_1},\ldots,P_{s_k},P_{t_1},\ldots,P_{t_k}),\quad \mathsf V=(P_b,P_{t_1},\ldots,P_{t_k},P_{s_1},\ldots,P_{s_k}). \] For any finite collection of binary alternating \((2k+1)\)-square matrices \(M\) (symmetric with zero diagonal), include an accuracy-\(h\) parent block on the actual tuple \(\mathsf V+M\mathsf U\). Reuse each bottom once coherently, and include all companions and fields. Then this complete two-level source has no ordinary degree-\(B\) PC or NS refutation. More specifically, a single old-only coefficient map of degree at most \(\delta\) transfers every such proof of an old target \(q\) to an old proof of \(w_0abq\) through \(b_0+\delta B+2\delta\), where \(a,b\) are the respective products of the selected old coordinates. No parent-count term appears.

Exact bottoms and survival. Assign one bottom row to each listed literal, with its coefficient one and others zero; zero-pad to \(h\). A bottom on literals \(\ell_j\) maps literally to \(\prod_j(1-\ell_j)\). Its companions vanish on the entire old Boolean cube, and its coefficient fields are zero. Thus the images of \(P_a,P_b\) are \(a,b\), while the other images \(s_i,t_i\) have degree at most \(\delta\), regardless of their outside support. The free-cube reserve is available: \(D-b_0\ge\delta B+2\delta\ge u\) and \(D-b_0-1\ge2\delta\). The selected monomial \(ab\) is nonzero in that coordinate algebra, so \(w_0ab\notin J_D\). No evaluation on a satisfying assignment of \(\Gamma\) is used.

Literal common certificate. Write \(U=(a,s,t)\), \(V=(b,t,s)\) for the images. In the ordinary commutative polynomial ring, \[ U^{\mathsf T}V=ab+\sum_i(s_it_i+t_is_i)=ab,\qquad U^{\mathsf T}MU=0. \tag{2} \] The second identity uses zero diagonal and cancellation of each symmetric pair in characteristic two, before any Boolean reduction. Hence \[ U^{\mathsf T}(V+MU)=ab \] for every parent simultaneously. Assign its first row the coefficient vector \(U\) and all later rows zero. This is one legal old-only substitution, once per fresh parent coefficient. Its whole product becomes \(1-ab\). Every weighted parent companion is \[ w_0ab\,g_{M,j}(1-ab), \] which vanishes on the full old Boolean cube. Its degree is at most \(b_0+5\delta\); its Boolean certificate fits that ceiling. Every weighted parent coefficient field \(w_0ab(U_j^2-U_j)\) vanishes there and has degree at most \(b_0+4\delta\). Neither the literal identity nor its field certificates use a bound on \(k\) or the number of parents.

Original replay. Each original bottom product has degree at most \(2h\), each parent input at most \(2h\), and each parent companion at most \(h(2h+1)+2h=2h^2+3h\). These are upper ceilings: cancellations or shorter bottom tuples may lower actual degrees, but never reset a carried proof cost. Every source variable maps to degree at most \(\delta\); old variables are fixed and bottom coefficients are constants. For any original companion or field generator \(E\) of actual degree \(e\), the polynomial \(w_0ab\Phi(E)\) vanishes on the entire cube and has degree at most \(b_0+2\delta+\delta e\). Monic division by the Boolean equations gives an old certificate at that actual ceiling. For old \(\gamma\in\Gamma\), multiplication by \(w_0ab\) costs at most \(b_0+2\delta+\deg\gamma\), which also fits.

An NS cofactor of degree at most \(B-e\) adds at most \(\delta(B-e)\). For PC simulate each line \(p\) by \(w_0ab\Phi(p)\); a multiplication predecessor has degree at most \(B-1\), and multiplying by a degree-\(\delta\) variable image fits (1). The final old target is \(w_0abq\). A refutation would put the surviving \(w_0ab\) in \(J_D\), contradiction. This repeats the proved semantic common-weight replay with explicit maps; no unproved equivalence of augmented PC and NS is used. Earlier weighted memberships, if being retained in an application, must separately fit multiplication by \(ab\) and use the same coefficient images; (1) alone does not certify unspecified earlier obligations. \(\square\)
