Assessment of this line. The primal side has narrowed, this period, to two properties of a flow with flow-bound value \(O(N)\): at pairs with both exponents finite and above \(2\) it is not given by a rule linear in the counts (thm:linear-flow-obstruction), and if it is level-by-level, adapted and local it must change the means of squares at a shift \(r\) beyond the reach of the coarse-mean proof, which by this review's Attempt and its reviewer reaches every \(r\le r^*-c_0\), \(r^*=(\frac12-\frac1\alpha)\log_2\log N\), \(c_0\) a constant (\(10.92\) levels at \(\alpha=4\), \(L=1\), \(\theta=6\cdot10^{-4}\)).
