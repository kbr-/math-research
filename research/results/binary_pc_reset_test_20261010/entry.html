The actual two-degree datum

Read the original criterion and the full quotient refinement. For original supplied equations, homogenized to their actual degrees without changing the source, let \(A=F[z,y]/J\), \(E=A_{D-1}\), \(F'=A_D\). Write \(Z:E\to F'\) for multiplication by \(z\), \(X_i:E\to F'\) for the other variable maps, and \(u=[z^D]\). The exact closure is \[ U_0=0,\qquad U_{t+1}=U_t+\sum_iX_i(Z^{-1}U_t). \tag{1} \] The notation \(Z^{-1}U\) means the full linear preimage, not inversion of an injective map. The marked unit survives PC precisely when \(u\notin U_\infty\). This formulation retains the original fixed ceiling and permits multiplication after a cancellation has lowered actual degree.

A forward-only representation on \(E\oplus F'\) sends \(e\in E\) to \(Ze\) or \(X_ie\) in \(F'\), and kills \(F'\). Every product of two such arrows is zero, for every source whatsoever. Their span is a square-zero ideal of the unital algebra they generate, not of the full endomorphism algebra. This proves nothing about (1): resetting from a class in \(F'\) to its possible lower-degree representatives in \(E\) is missing. It is precisely the preimage operation in (1).

Identify the matrix-space operation, with its scope

Let \(\mathcal B=\operatorname{span}(Z,X_1,\ldots,X_v)\). Ivanyos–Karpinski–Qiao–Santha, Definition 4 calls \[ W_0=0,\qquad W_{t+1}=\mathcal B(Z^{-1}W_t) \] the second Wong sequence for \((\langle Z\rangle,\mathcal B)\). It is increasing, so it equals (1): inductively \(W_t\subseteq\mathcal B(Z^{-1}W_t)\); adding \(W_t\) changes nothing, and the extra \(Z\)-image is \(W_t\cap\operatorname{im}Z\), already contained in \(W_t\). Thus this is the existing PC closure in a standard matrix-space setting, not an additional invariant.

The paper's Lemma 9 relates \(W_\infty\subseteq\operatorname{im}Z\) to a singularity witness of deficiency \(\dim\ker Z\), and implies maximum rank of \(Z\) in \(\mathcal B\) under that containment. It does not say that the particular vector \(u\) is outside \(W_\infty\). Lemma 10 makes the reset explicit using a pseudoinverse while the sequence remains in \(\operatorname{im}Z\). The definition, relevant proofs and published-author version were checked. No rank-one spanning or triangularizability theorem is applied to arbitrary sources, and no large-field constructive algorithm is silently invoked over \(\mathbb F_2\).

These tools could help construct or test a source-specific invariant, but their polynomial-time matrix algorithms are measured in \(\dim E+\dim F'\), not the compiler's formula count or degree budget. No affordable bound for the marked source follows from the identification alone.

A complete reset control

Over \(\mathbb F_2\), use the original quadratic system \[ x^2,\qquad x^2-x,\qquad xy-1,\qquad y^2-y. \tag{2} \] At ceiling \(D=2\), its homogeneous ideal is \[ J=(x^2,xz,xy-z^2,y^2-yz). \] There are no degree-one relations. In degree two these four independent relations leave basis \(u=z^2,v=yz\). In particular \(u\ne0\) in \(A_2\): the ordinary degree-two NS space does not refute the system. Ordinary PC does refute it at degree two: subtract the first two generators to derive \(x\), multiply the derived polynomial \(x\) by \(y\) to derive \(xy\), and subtract \(xy-1\) to derive one.

With columns corresponding to the basis \(z,x,y\) of \(E\), and rows to \(u,v\) of \(F'\), the actual maps are \[ Z=\begin{pmatrix}1&0&0\\0&0&1\end{pmatrix},\quad X=\begin{pmatrix}0&0&1\\0&0&0\end{pmatrix},\quad Y=\begin{pmatrix}0&1&0\\1&0&1\end{pmatrix}. \] Here \(\ker Z=\langle x\rangle\), so \(U_1=\langle Yx\rangle=\langle u\rangle\); the marked unit is already lost. Next \(Z^{-1}U_1=\langle z,x\rangle\), and \(Yz=v\), giving \(U_2=F'\). The dimension sequence is \(0,1,2,2\).

Choose the actual section \(S:F'\to E\) with \(S(u)=z,S(v)=y\), so \(ZS=I\). The reset followed by multiplication has \[ YS(u)=v,\qquad YS(v)=v. \] Thus \(YS\) is a nonzero idempotent, not a nilpotent operator, although all forward arrows in \(E\oplus F'\) square to zero. Any two-sided ideal containing this reset loop is not nilpotent. This rejects treating all reset transitions as the desired nilpotent defect. A smaller ideal of correction errors, relative to an already controlled action, remains possible.

Also \(Z\) is surjective and has maximum possible rank two in \(\mathcal B\). The Wong containment \(U_\infty\subseteq\operatorname{im}Z\) holds automatically, and \(E\) is a deficiency-one singularity witness: \(\dim E-\dim\mathcal B(E)=3-2=1=\dim\ker Z\). Nevertheless \(u\in U_1\). This directly tests why the literature's unmarked rank witness cannot replace the marked PC condition. It does not contradict its theorem.

What remains available
