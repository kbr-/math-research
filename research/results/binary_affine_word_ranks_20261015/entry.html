The factor-position decomposition

For one ordered product \(A=\prod_{j=1}^{q}(c_j+\ell_j)\), write \(A_{<j}\) and \(A_{>j}\) for its prefix and suffix, with empty product one. Expansion chooses a linear part in exactly \(r\) factor positions for its degree-\(r\) part. Group by the position \(j\) of the \(p\)-th chosen linear part. Distributivity gives the formal identity \[ A_r=\sum_{j=1}^{q} (A_{<j})_{p-1}\,\ell_j\,(A_{>j})_{r-p}. \tag{2}\] Constants in every unchosen factor remain in the corresponding prefix or suffix component. This partitions labeled expansion choices even when different choices produce the same word, so cancellations do not invalidate the identity.

The left factor of each summand in (2) has degree \(p\), and the right one degree \(r-p\). Its contiguous coefficient matrix is the outer product of their coefficient vectors, of rank at most one. Summing over \(j\), then over \(a\), proves (1). No coefficient extraction is implemented by this analysis decomposition.

The interleaved control

The coefficient of a word in \((G_r)_{2r}\) is one exactly when its \(2r\) letter indices strictly increase. Each such word selects a unique subsequence of the displayed distinct factors. In its odd/even flattening restrict the odd letter in pair \(i\) to \(X_{3i-2}\) or \(X_{3i-1}\), and the even letter to \(X_{3i-1}\) or \(X_{3i}\). Distinct triples have strictly ordered indices automatically. Within a triple, the strict-increase test is \[ B=\begin{pmatrix}1&1\\0&1\end{pmatrix}. \] Therefore the restricted coefficient matrix is \(B^{\otimes r}\). The matrix \(B\) is invertible over every field, with determinant one, so its tensor power is invertible and has rank \(2^r\). A submatrix's rank is at most the full matrix's rank, proving the lower bound.

The description of \(G_r\) contains \(3r\) distinct sum gates and one product gate, with \(O(r)\) occurrences and wires. Its depth is two under the same ordered unbounded-product convention as the matched simulation. It can also be seen as the affine shift of \(\prod_jX_j\). The component degree grows with original size; no conflict with fixed-degree coefficient bounds follows. The control defeats the proposed generic interleaved-rank upper bound, without asserting that \(G_r\) or its component is a certificate.
