Disjoint pairs outside characteristic two

This is the functional pigeonhole system with \(m\ge2\) pigeons and \(N\) holes over a field \(k\), with \(C\), \(H\), \(1\le K<D\le\lceil N/2\rceil\), \(W=C\cap V_K\), \(\rho=\max(2K-1,K+2)\) and \(E_A\) as in the partition kernels in every field entry, and \(s_B=\sum_{j\in B}x_{1j}\). A set \(A\) of holes has a single room when \(N-|A|\ge\rho\) or \(|A|\ge\rho\).

Statement. Proposition (disjoint pairs outside characteristic two). Let \(k\) have characteristic other than \(2\), and let \(A\), \(B\) be disjoint sets of holes such that \(A\) has a single room and \(\max(N-|B|,|B|)\ge\max(2K-1,K+1)\), or the same with \(A\) and \(B\) exchanged. Then \(J=\operatorname{Ann}_C(E_A)\cap\operatorname{Ann}_C(E_B)\cap V_K=W\): the pair has no common surviving killer of degree at most \(K\). In particular, every disjoint pair has \(J=W\) when \(N\ge2\rho-1\).

Proof. Let \(u\in J\), with \(A\) the set with the single room. This argument is the reviewer's, checked here step by step.

  • Reduce to \(E_A\)'s zero set. By prop:partition-kernels-every-field (a) with \(r=1\), \(\operatorname{Ann}_C(E_A)\cap V_K=\Phi_A+W\). If \(N-|A|\ge\rho\), \(\Phi_A\) consists of the polynomials vanishing off \(Z_A=\{2\text{ in }A,\ 1\text{ not in }A\}\), the \(Q_2\) representative's zero set outside characteristic \(2\). If \(|A|\ge\rho\), it uses the \(Q_1\) representative's zero set \(\{1\text{ in }A^c,\ 2\text{ not in }A^c\}\). In both cases pigeon \(2\) is never in \(B\subseteq A^c\) on the zero set. Write \(u=a+w\) with \(w\in W\) and \(a\in\Phi_A\).
  • Rewrite \(aE_B\). Since \(wE_B\) has degree at most \(K+1\le D\) and \(w\in C\), \(wE_B\in C\), so \(aE_B=uE_B-wE_B\in C\). Now \(E_B=s_B+\sum_{j\notin B}x_{2j}=s_B+1-Q_2-\sum_{j\in B}x_{2j}\). So \(aE_B=a(1+s_B)-aQ_2-\sum_{j\in B}a\,x_{2j}\). Here \(aQ_2\in C\), at degree \(K+1\le D\). Each \(a\,x_{2j}\) with \(j\in B\) vanishes at every partial matching: at one containing \((2,j)\), pigeon \(2\) is in \(B\), outside the zero set, so \(a\) vanishes there. By the first step of the partition common kernels entry, \(a\,x_{2j}\in H\). Hence \(a(1+s_B)\in C\).
  • Transfer and evaluate. If \(N-|B|\ge\max(2K-1,K+1)\), lem:low-hole-linear-transfer applies to \(1+s_B\), whose edges lie in \(B\) on the one pigeon \(1\): its room for \(|P|=1\) is \(\max(2K-1,K+1+\min(K,0))=\max(2K-1,K+1)\). It gives \(a\in\operatorname{Ann}_H(1+s_B)\cap V_K+W\). If instead \(|B|\ge\max(2K-1,K+1)\), the lemma applies to \(1+s_B+Q_1=2-\sum_{j\notin B}x_{1j}\), with edges in \(B^c\) on pigeon \(1\); \(aQ_1\in C\), so \(a(1+s_B+Q_1)\in C\) as well. At a partial matching, \(1+s_B\) takes the values \(1\) and \(2\), and so does \(2-\sum_{j\notin B}x_{1j}\). Both are nonzero outside characteristic \(2\), so the \(\operatorname{Ann}_H\) part vanishes at every partial matching and lies in \(H\subseteq W\). So \(a\in W\), and \(u\in W\).
  • The last sentence. If \(N\ge2\rho-1\), then \(\max(N-|A|,|A|)\ge\lceil N/2\rceil\ge\rho\), so every set has a single room. Also \(\max(N-|B|,|B|)\ge\lceil N/2\rceil\ge\rho\ge\max(2K-1,K+1)\). \(\square\)

Hypotheses, each with its step. Characteristic other than \(2\) is used by the zero set of \(E_A\)'s representative and by the nonvanishing of \(1+s_B\); disjointness by the vanishing of \(a\,x_{2j}\), \(j\in B\); the single room of \(A\) by the single-error step; the room \(\max(2K-1,K+1)\) of \(B\) by the one-pigeon transfer; \(K<D\le\lceil N/2\rceil\) by the degrees and both transfers. The conclusion \(J=W\) holds for the class of such pairs, at every \(m\ge2\). In detail:

  • Characteristic other than \(2\): the zero set of the representative of \(E_A\), on which pigeon \(2\) is never in \(B\) (in characteristic \(2\) the zero set contains pigeon \(1\) in \(A\) with pigeon \(2\) in \(B\)), and the nonvanishing of \(1+s_B\).
