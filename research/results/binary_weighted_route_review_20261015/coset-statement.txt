prop:bounded-coset-probes
  Statement: Let A=F_2[x_1,...,x_v], v>=1, and F in A^r generate the unit ideal and include B=x_1^2+x_1. Let H(x,Z) have placeholder degree at most k and a size-S depth-d division-free unbounded-fanin binary arithmetic circuit counting gates and wires. Suppose H(x,c(x)) is the same polynomial for every polynomial certificate F dot c=1. That common value has a binary circuit constructed from H and B alone of size O_k((S+r+v+1)^((k+2)4^k)) and depth at most 4^k(2d+8). Thus fixed k preserves polynomial size and bounded depth without a supplied certificate. Proof uses polynomial-parameter Koszul translations over the infinite domain A, rational specialization to e_B/B, truncation to k in one placeholder, and 2k exact divided differences. No polynomial guarantee for growing k, no derivative/evaluation probe or simulation-image-only invariance theorem, and no Frege lower bound.
