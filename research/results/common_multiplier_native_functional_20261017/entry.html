Setting

The functional pigeonhole system over a field, \(m\) pigeons, \(N\) holes, Booleanity, hole and functionality axioms and pigeon axioms \(Q_i=1-\sum_hx_{ih}\) in the multilinear ring; \(C\) its degree-\(D\) closure and \(H\) that of the hole and functionality axioms, the span of the non-matching monomials of degree at most \(D\). Following lem:native-detection-count, an old functional modulo a closure \(J\) is a linear functional on \(V_D\) vanishing on \(J\); for an error \(E\) of degree \(e\), \(T(u)=[Eu]\) on \(V_K\) and slack \(L\) with \(e+K+L\le D\), old functionals \(\mu_1,\dots,\mu_c\) detect \(\ker T\) when \(\ker T\) is the set of \(u\in V_K\) with \(\mu_j(Euv)=0\) for all \(j\) and \(v\in V_L\), and \(m_{\min}(J)\) is the least such \(c\). For an edge \(e\), \(B_e\) is the reduced board, \(e\)'s pigeon and hole removed, with \(H_e\) its hole and functionality axioms and \(Q_i'=1-\sum_{h\ne h_e}x_{ih}\); \(\rho_e\) sends a polynomial to its restriction at \(x_e=1\) and \(0\) on the other edges in \(e\)'s row and column, so that \(x_ew\equiv x_e\rho_e(w)\) modulo \(H\) for \(w\) of degree below \(D\). \(W_k(B_e)\) is the span of the \(x_sQ_i'\) with \(|s|+1\le k\) modulo \(H_e\), counted by the degree \(x_sQ_i\) has before restriction (at \(N=1\), \(Q_i'=1\) has degree \(0\), and counting by degree after restriction would put \(1\) in \(W_0\)). The functional-transfer entry records, for \(D\le\lceil N/2\rceil\), that “\(C\) is \(H\) plus the lift of \(K_D\)” with \(K_D=\operatorname{span}(B_D\setminus T_D)\), \(B_D\) the dance basis (Impagliazzo–Pudlák–Sgall, Proposition 2.8), and that “\(C\cap V_K\) is \(H\cap V_K\) plus the lift of \(K_K\)”.

Native restriction

Statement. Lemma (native restriction). Over any field, let \(D\le\lceil N/2\rceil\), any number \(m\) of pigeons, an edge \(e\), and \(K,L\ge0\) with \(1+K+L\le D\). Then (a) \(1\notin C\); (b) \(m_{\min}(H)\) for the error \(x_e\) is the least number of linear functionals \(\lambda_j\) on \(V_{K+L}(B_e)\) modulo \(H_e\) such that no nonzero \(\bar u\in V_K(B_e)\) has \(\lambda_j(\bar uv)=0\) for all \(v\in V_L(B_e)\) and all \(j\); (c) \(m_{\min}(C)\) for \(x_e\) is the same least number with \(V_k(B_e)\) replaced by \(A_k=V_k(B_e)/W_k(B_e)\) for \(k=K,L,K+L\), the product \(A_K\times A_L\to A_{K+L}\) being well defined; (d) hence \(\lceil\dim A_K/\dim A_L\rceil\le m_{\min}(C)\le\dim A_K\), and the same for \(H\) with \(V_k(B_e)\) in place of \(A_k\); (e) in the \(H\)-model at \(K=2\), \(L=1\), with \(p\) pigeons and \(n\) holes on \(B_e\), the lower bound of (d) is \(1+\lceil(p-1)(n-1)/2\rceil\).

Proof. (a) The empty matching has a dance, so \(1=x_\varnothing\in T_D\cap B_D\); \(B_D\) is a basis and \(K_D=\operatorname{span}(B_D\setminus T_D)\), so \(1\notin K_D\), and \(1\notin H\), so \(1\notin C=H+K_D\). (b) For \(w\) of degree at most \(D-1\), each monomial of \(w\) sharing a pigeon or hole with \(e\) other than \(e\) itself gives a non-matching product with \(x_e\), and the others give \(x_e\) times their restriction, so \(x_ew\equiv x_e\rho_e(w)\) modulo \(H\). The map \(V_{K+L}(B_e)\to V_D/H\), \(\bar w\mapsto[x_e\bar w]\), is injective, distinct matchings \(t\) of \(B_e\) giving distinct matchings \(t\cup\{e\}\). So an old functional modulo \(H\) gives the forms \((u,v)\mapsto\lambda(\rho_e(u)\rho_e(v))\) for a functional \(\lambda\) on \(V_{K+L}(B_e)\), and every \(\lambda\) arises, extended from the injective image; \(\rho_e\) is multiplicative and onto \(V_k(B_e)\), and \(\ker T=\operatorname{Ann}_H(x_e)\cap V_K=\ker\rho_e\). So \(\mu_1,\dots,\mu_c\) detect \(\ker T\) exactly when the \(\lambda_j\) leave no nonzero \(\bar u\). (c) By lem:functional-killer-transfer, for \(k=K\) and \(k=K+L\) (\(1+k\le D\le\lceil N/2\rceil\)), \(\operatorname{Ann}_C(x_e)\cap V_k=\operatorname{Ann}_H(x_e)\cap V_k+C\cap V_k=\ker\rho_e+C\cap V_k\). And \(\rho_e(C\cap V_k)=W_k(B_e)\): by the recorded sentence, \(C\cap V_k\) is \(H\cap V_k\) plus a span of products \(x_s\) times factors \(Q_i\), each in the span of the \(x_sQ_i\), and \(\rho_e(x_sQ_i)\) is \(0\) when \(s\) meets \(e\)'s row or column away from \(e\) or when \(i\) is \(e\)'s pigeon (then \(\rho_e(Q_i)=1-1=0\)), and \(\rho_e(x_s)Q_i'\) otherwise, with \(|\rho_e(s)|+1\le|s|+1\le k\); conversely, for \(s\) in \(B_e\) and \(i\) not \(e\)'s pigeon, \(x_sQ_i\in C\cap V_k\) and \(\rho_e(x_sQ_i)=x_sQ_i'\). So the kernel of \(w\mapsto[x_ew]\in V_D/C\) on \(V_{K+L}\) is \(\rho_e^{-1}(W_{K+L})\), and it induces an injection \(A_{K+L}\to V_D/C\); the old functionals modulo \(C\) give exactly the forms \(\lambda(\rho_e(u)\rho_e(v))\) with \(\lambda\) a functional on \(A_{K+L}\). And \(\ker T=\operatorname{Ann}_C(x_e)\cap V_K=\rho_e^{-1}(W_K)\). The product is well defined since \(x_sQ_i'\cdot v\) for \(v\) of degree at most \(L\) lies in \(W_{K+L}\), \(x_sQ_i'\,x_g\) being \(x_{s\cup g}Q_i'\), \(0\), or, for \(g\) in row \(i\), \(x_sx_gQ_i'\equiv0\) modulo \(H_e\). So detection is the condition of (b) on the \(A_k\). (d) Each \(\lambda\) gives the map \(A_K\to A_L^*\), \(\bar u\mapsto\lambda(\bar u\,\cdot)\), whose kernel has codimension at most \(\dim A_L\), and the \(c\) kernels must meet in \(0\); this is lem:native-detection-count's bound, here \(r=\dim A_K\) and \(Z_L\) of dimension \(\dim A_L\). For the upper bound, \(A_K\to A_{K+L}\) is injective. At \(N=1\), \(K=L=0\) and the map is the identity of \(A_0\). For \(N\ge2\), let \(C'\) be the degree-\((K+L)\) closure of the reduced board, whose \(N-1\) holes have \(K+L\le D-1\le\lceil(N-1)/2\rceil\); then \(W_k(B_e)=C'\cap V_k\) modulo \(H_e\) for \(k\le K+L\), since by the recorded sentence \(C'\cap V_k\) is \(H_e\cap V_k\) plus the span of the basis elements \(x_t\prod_{i\in I}Q_i'\) with \(|t|+|I|\le k\), each of which, expanding all factors \(Q_i'\) but one, is a combination of \(x_sQ_i'\) with \(|s|+1\le k\), while each such \(x_sQ_i'\) lies in \(C'\) by multiplication; so \(W_{K+L}\cap V_K(B_e)=C'\cap V_K=W_K\), and a dual basis of \(A_K^*\) extended to \(A_{K+L}\) detects with \(v=1\). (e) In the \(H\)-model with \(p\) pigeons and \(n\) holes on \(B_e\), \(P=pn\) and \(d=(p-1)(n-1)/2\): \(\dim V_1(B_e)=1+P\) and \(\dim V_2(B_e)=1+P+Pd\), the pairs of edges sharing nothing numbering \(\binom p2n(n-1)=Pd\); so at \(K=2\), \(L=1\) the ratio is \(\lceil1+Pd/(1+P)\rceil=1+\lceil d\rceil\), as \(d-d/(1+P)\) lies in \((d-1,d)\) for integer \(d\ge1\) and above \(\lfloor d\rfloor\) for half-integer \(d\), \(2d<1+P\); at \(d=0\) (\(p\le1\) or \(n\le1\)) the ratio is \(1\) directly. \(\square\)
