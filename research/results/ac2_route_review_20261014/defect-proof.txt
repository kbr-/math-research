Theorem. Let \(2\le K\le v\), \(t\ge1\), and set \[T_0=u-1,\qquad T=T_0+re,\qquad \eta=\frac{N_v(K-2)}{2N_v(K)},\quad N_v(k)=\sum_{j=0}^k\binom vj.\] Suppose \[h\ge\max(r,t+1),\quad B\ge\max(u,2h^2+3h),\quad M2^e(1-\eta)^t<1,\quad K+TB\le D.\tag{1}\] Then one binary old weight \(W\notin J_D\), of degree at most \(K\), and one coherent degree-at-most-\(T\) assignment to every source coefficient kill all weighted source generators in \(J_D\). Row reduction and actual branches. Fix a parent. Constant invertible operations on its input list put its coefficient matrix into independent pivot rows and zero rows.
