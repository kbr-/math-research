# Chessboard extension and the uniform design candidate

The [notebook entry](https://kbr-.github.io/math-research/#entry-2026-09-12-chessboard-extension-and-uniform-candidate)
contains the exact source application and complete probability argument.

BLVZ Theorem 1.1 gives (k-2)-connectivity for the k-by-N chessboard complex
when N>=2k-1. It supplies precisely the homology vanishing required by the
recorded moment-boundary argument. Every quadratic functional-PHP design
therefore extends through R when N>=2R-1, including R=floor(N/2).
Uniform higher-degree designs have the full uniform quadratic marginal.

Combining that result with the covariance and distinct-map bounds rules out
the natural uniform residual-design candidate over F2 for the survival rate
needed by the ENS theorem, at logarithmic accuracy and polynomial companion
count S>=2. The query tree has degree two.

This does not refute the source theorem or the weaker printed diagonal
probability problem. It gives no ENS upper bound or final Frege lower bound,
and leaves nonuniform design distributions open.
