\begin{abstract}
Let \(D(n,k)\) be the least degree of a polynomial
\(P\in\Ft[x_1,\ldots,x_n]\) that vanishes with (Hasse) multiplicity at least
\(k\) at every nonzero point of \(\Ft^n\) and with multiplicity less than \(k\)
at the origin. Bishnoi, Boyadzhiyska, Das and M\'esz\'aros asked whether
\(D(n,k)\ge2k-\lfloor k/2^{n-1}\rfloor\) when \(k\ge2^{n-2}\); this bound
follows for all \(n,k\) from the multiplicity Schwartz--Zippel lemma of Dvir,
Kopparty, Saraf and Sudan. We determine \(D(n,k)\) exactly for all
\(1\le k<3\cdot2^{n-1}\):
\[
 D(n,k)=2k+n-2-\lfloor\log_2k\rfloor .
\]
So the bound above is attained exactly when \(2^{n-2}\le k<3\cdot2^{n-1}\).
Below \(2^{n-2}\) it is not attained, and each further dimension costs exactly
one degree. For \(k<2^{n-1}\) the lower bound combines the
multiplicity Schwartz--Zippel lemma with a one-dimension reduction that
lowers the degree by one while keeping the origin order. The upper bound is a
characteristic-two specialization of a Catalan truncation of Menezes, who
determined the minimum for \(n\ge k-1\); a telescoping identity makes the
construction work in every dimension. The main theorem and all its
dependencies, including the multiplicity Schwartz--Zippel lemma, are
formalized in Lean~4, and every statement links to its formal proof.
\end{abstract}
