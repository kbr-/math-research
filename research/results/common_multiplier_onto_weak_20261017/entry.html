Setting

This is the functional pigeonhole system with \(m\ge2\) pigeons and \(N\) holes over a field \(k\): Booleanity, hole and functionality axioms, pigeon axioms \(Q_i=1-\sum_hx_{ih}\), in the multilinear ring. \(C\) is its degree-\(D\) closure and \(H\) that of the hole and functionality axioms, with \(1\le K<D\le\lceil N/2\rceil\), \(W=C\cap V_K\) and \(\rho=\max(2K-1,K+2)\). Two distinct pigeons are written \(1\) and \(2\), and \(E_A=\sum_{j\in A}x_{1j}+\sum_{j\notin A}x_{2j}\). For errors \(E_{A_1},\dots,E_{A_r}\), \(J=\bigcap_i\operatorname{Ann}_C(E_{A_i})\cap V_K\). A choice \(\chi\) takes for each \(i\) the representative \(R_i=E_{A_i}+Q_2=1+\sum_{j\in A_i}(x_{1j}-x_{2j})\), with edge set \(L_i=A_i\), or \(R_i=E_{A_i}+Q_1=1+\sum_{j\notin A_i}(x_{2j}-x_{1j})\), with \(L_i=A_i^c\); its room is \(N-|\bigcup_iL_i|\). “Pigeon \(p\) is in \(S\)” means that \(p\) is matched to a hole of \(S\). \(S_T=\sum_{j\in T}(x_{1j}+x_{2j})\).

Partition kernels with room in every field

Statement. Proposition (partition kernels in every field).

  • (a) Over every field, for a choice \(\chi\) with room at least \(\rho\), \(J=\Phi_\chi+W\), with \(\Phi_\chi\) the \(u\in V_K\) vanishing at every partial matching outside \(Z_\chi=\bigcap_iZ(R_i)\). Outside characteristic \(2\), \(Z(E_A+Q_2)=\{2\text{ in }A,\ 1\text{ not in }A\}\) and \(Z(E_A+Q_1)=\{1\text{ in }A^c,\ 2\text{ not in }A^c\}\). In characteristic \(2\), \(Z(E_A+Q_2)\) is the set where exactly one of pigeons \(1\), \(2\) is in \(A\), and \(Z(E_A+Q_1)\) the set where exactly one of them is in \(A^c\).
  • (b) For a pair \(A\), \(B\), the four choices have rooms \(N-|A\cup B|\) (both \(Q_2\)), \(|A\cap B|\) (both \(Q_1\)), \(|B\setminus A|\) (\(Q_2\) for \(A\), \(Q_1\) for \(B\)) and \(|A\setminus B|\) (\(Q_1\) for \(A\), \(Q_2\) for \(B\)).
  • (c) In characteristic \(2\), \(E_A+E_B=S_{A\triangle B}\) as polynomials, so \(J=\operatorname{Ann}_C(E_A)\cap\operatorname{Ann}_C(S_{A\triangle B})\cap V_K\) for every pair and every \(K\).
  • (d) Monomial killers, without room, from \(K\ge2\). For holes \(h\ne j\), modulo \(H\), \(x_{1h}x_{2j}E_A=([h\in A]+[j\notin A])\,x_{1h}x_{2j}\). So in characteristic \(2\), \(x_{1h}x_{2j}\) is a common surviving killer of \(E_{A_1},\dots,E_{A_r}\) exactly when every \(A_i\) separates \(h\) and \(j\) (contains exactly one of them); outside characteristic \(2\), exactly when \(j\) lies in every \(A_i\) and \(h\) in none. In characteristic \(2\) every pair of nonempty proper sets of holes has such a killer.

Proof. (a) The corollary's common-kernel clause, quoted in the partition common kernels entry, holds over any field for one representative per error with room: “the common kernel modulo C is the intersection of the \(\operatorname{Ann}_H(R_i)\cap V_K\) plus \(C\cap V_K\)”. That entry's first step shows, over any field, that a polynomial of degree at most \(D\) lies in \(H\) exactly when it vanishes at every partial matching. Since \(uR_i\) has degree at most \(K+1\le D\), \(u\in\operatorname{Ann}_H(R_i)\) exactly when \(u\) vanishes wherever \(R_i\) does not. At a partial matching \(M\), \((E_A+Q_2)(M)=1+[1\text{ in }A]-[2\text{ in }A]\), with the values \(1,2,0,1\) at \(([1\text{ in }A],[2\text{ in }A])=(0,0),(1,0),(0,1),(1,1)\). Outside characteristic \(2\) only \((0,1)\) gives \(0\); in characteristic \(2\), \((1,0)\) gives \(2=0\) too, which is exactly one of the pigeons in \(A\). \((E_A+Q_1)(M)=1+[2\text{ in }A^c]-[1\text{ in }A^c]\) takes the same four values with the pigeons' roles exchanged and \(A^c\) for \(A\). So \(J=\Phi_\chi+W\).

(b) The complement of \(A\cup B\) has \(N-|A\cup B|\) holes; that of \(A^c\cup B^c\) is \(A\cap B\); that of \(A\cup B^c\) is \(B\setminus A\); that of \(A^c\cup B\) is \(A\setminus B\).

(c) The coefficient of \(x_{1j}\) in \(E_A+E_B\) is \([j\in A]+[j\in B]\), and that of \(x_{2j}\) is \([j\notin A]+[j\notin B]=2-[j\in A]-[j\in B]\). Modulo \(2\) both are \([j\in A\triangle B]\). A \(u\) killing \(E_A\) and \(E_B\) kills their sum; a \(u\) killing \(E_A\) and \(S_{A\triangle B}\) kills \(E_B=S_{A\triangle B}-E_A\).

(d) \(x_{1h}x_{2j}E_A=\sum_{k\in A}x_{1h}x_{2j}x_{1k}+\sum_{k\notin A}x_{1h}x_{2j}x_{2k}\). Modulo the functionality axioms a term of the first sum survives only for \(k=h\), present when \(h\in A\), and one of the second only for \(k=j\), present when \(j\notin A\); each surviving term is \(x_{1h}x_{2j}\), since \(x^2=x\). So the product is \(([h\in A]+[j\notin A])x_{1h}x_{2j}\), of degree \(3\le D\), as \(K\ge2\) and \(K<D\). The coefficient takes the values \(0,1,1,2\); it is \(0\) outside characteristic \(2\) only for \(h\notin A\), \(j\in A\), and in characteristic \(2\) also for \(h\in A\), \(j\notin A\), that is, when \(A\) separates \(h\) and \(j\). Survival is the argument of thm:partition-killer-degree (d): with holes \(h\), \(j\) ranked lowest both pigeons dance to higher holes, since \(N\ge5\) (from \(3\le D\le\lceil N/2\rceil\)), so \(x_{1h}x_{2j}\in T_2\cap B_2\) is not in \(C\). Conversely, if some coefficient \(c\) is nonzero, then \(x_{1h}x_{2j}E_{A_i}\equiv c\,x_{1h}x_{2j}\) modulo \(H\subseteq C\), so \(x_{1h}x_{2j}E_{A_i}\in C\) would put \(x_{1h}x_{2j}\) in \(C\), against its survival: the killer conditions are exact. For a pair of nonempty proper sets: if \(A\setminus B\) and \(B\setminus A\) are nonempty, take \(h\in A\setminus B\), \(j\in B\setminus A\); if \(A\subseteq B\), take \(j\in A\) and \(h\notin B\); symmetrically if \(B\subseteq A\). \(\square\)
