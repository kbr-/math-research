
## Actual residual-certificate application

Let Q(x,Z)=sum_j q_j(x)Z_j with Q(x,b(x))=P for the rank residual.
Because P is in I^2, every first derivative of P vanishes at every Boolean a.
Differentiating the identity and evaluating there gives
partial_j P(a)=q_j(a), since b_j'=1 over F2 and every b_l(a)=0.
Thus every q_j(a)=0 and Q is zero on ALL binary (x,Z) assignments.
The scalar-oracle obstruction therefore applies to every such complete Q,
not only the artificial determinant-times-field-equation example.
This is the existing coefficient-cube-traces identity plus the new exact
order result. It excludes F2 scalar-oracle extraction of a hard Boolean
function, not white-box circuit transformations or extension-field arguments.
