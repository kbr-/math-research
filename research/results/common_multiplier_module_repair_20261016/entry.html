Linearly certified module repairs are scalar

16 October 2026 · Working proof · scope: certificates semilinear over a ring map · Module and matrix repairs.

Picked. The user picked this item: “On next cycles, prioritize that modules + matrices idea at least for some time (give it the usual 4 cycles + extensions if deserved treatment)”. It came from the user's question whether repair can be abstracted from multiplication to maps with several inputs, multilinear in some of them, and their remark that the modules may carry relations among their generators; it bears on joint-error-savings, since a repair acting on a module might kill several errors where no single polynomial of the same degree does.

Best outcome. At the goal's own parameters, a repair by an element of a module, with survival still following from the old system's hardness, that kills Pang's errors below the least degree of any scalar repair of them; thm:certified-repair-composition and thm:finite-grid-repair construct one at Pang's \(K\), which it must beat.

General statement. lem:module-repair-factorization: a repair of the old proof system into any filtered module \(N\), free or with relations, is an element \(\nu\in N\) (modulo the module's closure); if its survival is certified by a map from \(N\) into a filtered commutative algebra \(S\), compatible with multiplication modulo the target closure up to a ring map \(\phi:R\to S\), then that map's value at \(\nu\) is an \(S\)-valued repair of the \(\phi\)-image system, a scalar repair of the old system when \(S=R\) and \(\phi\) is the identity; first-order differential certificates into the old closure, on systems whose differentiated variables carry Boolean axioms, yield scalar repairs on the window \(\deg(a_jm)\le D-K\); and every repair, scalar or module, implies that the protected class lies outside the closure of the axioms with the weighted errors added.

Module repair factorization

Statement. Over a field \(k\), let \(R=k[x_1,\ldots,x_n]\), \(V_d\) the polynomials of degree at most \(d\), and axioms \(a_1,\ldots,a_r\). Let \(N\) be an \(R\)-module, given by any generators and relations, with a filtration \(F_0N\subseteq F_1N\subseteq\cdots\), \(x_iF_jN\subseteq F_{j+1}N\), and \(J_N\subseteq F_DN\) a degree-\(D\) pseudo-submodule (closed under each \(x_i\) within \(F_DN\)). Let errors \(E_1,\ldots,E_s\), a protected \(w\) of degree at most \(b\), and \(\nu\in F_KN\) with \(b+K\le D\) and \(\deg(wE_i)+K\le D\). Call \(\nu\) a module repair when \(a_jm\nu\in J_N\) for every axiom and monomial \(m\) with \(\deg(a_jm)\le D-K\) (soundness), \(wE_i\nu\in J_N\) for all \(i\) (killing, as in thm:certified-repair-composition) and \(w\nu\notin J_N\) (survival). (a) A linear \(T:V_{D-K}\to F_DN\) with \(T(x_iq)-x_iT(q)\in J_N\) whenever \(\deg q<D-K\) satisfies \(T(q)-q\,T(1)\in J_N\) for all \(q\): compatible repairs, exactly or modulo \(J_N\), are multiplications by \(\nu=T(1)\) (exactly: lem:cyclic-repair-map). (b) Let \(S\) be a commutative \(k\)-algebra with a filtration by subspaces \(S_0\subseteq S_1\subseteq\cdots\) (for \(S=R[\varepsilon]/(\varepsilon^2)\), \(\varepsilon\) of degree \(0\)), \(\phi:R\to S\) a \(k\)-algebra map with \(\phi(x_i)\in S_T\), \(J'\subseteq S_{D'}\) a subspace closed under multiplication by each \(\phi(x_i)\) within \(S_{D'}\), and \(\beta:F_DN\to S_{D'}\) linear with \(\beta(F_jN)\subseteq S_{Tj+\sigma}\), \(TD+\sigma\le D'\), \(\beta(J_N)\subseteq J'\) and \(\beta(x_iu)-\phi(x_i)\beta(u)\in J'\) for \(u\in F_{D-1}N\). If \(\beta(w\nu)\notin J'\), then \(f=\beta(\nu)\in S_{TK+\sigma}\) is a repair of the image system: \(\phi(a_jm)f\in J'\), \(\phi(wE_i)f\in J'\), \(\phi(w)f\notin J'\). With \(S=R\), \(\phi\) the identity, \(T=1\) and \(J'\) the old closure at degree \(D+\sigma\), it is a scalar repair of the old system at parameter \(K+\sigma\). For \(N=R/I\), the maps of (b) with \(\phi\) the identity are given by their value \(f\) at \(1\), which must satisfy \(If\subseteq J'\) within degree. (c) If \(N=R^m\) with coordinate shifts \(\sigma_j\) and \(J_N=B^{-1}\bigl(\bigoplus_lJ_D\bigr)\cap F_DN\) for a matrix \(B\) whose rows have shift at most \(0\), every module repair yields a scalar repair \(f=(B\nu)_l\) at parameter \(K\); for a shifted direct sum, \(f=\nu_j\). (d) For a derivation \(\partial=\sum_kp_k\partial/\partial x_k\) and \(\phi_\partial:R\to R[\varepsilon]/(\varepsilon^2)\), \(g\mapsto g+\varepsilon\,\partial g\), the maps of (b) over \(\phi_\partial\) are the first-order differential certificates, \(\beta(gu)\equiv g\beta_0(u)+\varepsilon\bigl(g\beta_1(u)+\partial g\,\beta_0(u)\bigr)\). Take \(J'=J+\varepsilon J\) for the old closure \(J\subseteq V_{D'}\), containing the Boolean axioms \(a_k=x_k^2-x_k\) and closed under variables within degree \(D'\). If \(f=f_0+\varepsilon f_1\) is a repair of the image system as in (b), soundness reaches \(a_k\) itself, and \(\deg(p_k(2x_k-1)^2f_0)\le D'\), then \(f_0\) and \(f_1\) each satisfy soundness and killing as scalar repairs against \(J\), and one of them survives: a differential certificate into the old closure yields a scalar repair of degree at most \(\deg f\). (e) For every module repair, \(A=\{g\in V_{D-K}:g\nu\in J_N\}\) is a degree-\((D-K)\) pseudoideal containing every \(a_jm\) and every \(wE_im\) within degree \(D-K\), and not \(w\): \(w\) lies outside the degree-\((D-K)\) closure of the axioms with the weighted errors \(wE_i\) added, and some functional vanishing on that closure is nonzero at \(w\).

Proof. (a) By induction on \(\deg q\), for monomials: \(T(x_im)\equiv x_iT(m)\equiv x_i(m\,T(1)+j)\) with \(j\in J_N\), and \(x_ij\in J_N\) since \(\deg(x_imT(1))\le D\) places it in \(F_DN\) and \(J_N\) is closed there; linearity extends it. (b) The same induction with \(\phi\): \(\beta(x_im\nu)\equiv\phi(x_i)\beta(m\nu)\equiv\phi(x_i)(\phi(m)f+j')\) with \(j'\in J'\), and \(\phi(x_i)j'\in J'\) by the closure of \(J'\), its degree being at most \(T(D-K)+TK+\sigma\le D'\); so \(\beta(g\nu)-\phi(g)f\in J'\) for \(\deg g\le D-K\). Soundness and killing give \(a_jm\nu,\ wE_i\nu\in J_N\), so \(\beta\) carries them into \(J'\), and \(\phi(a_jm)f,\ \phi(wE_i)f\in J'\); \(\phi(w)f\equiv\beta(w\nu)\notin J'\). For \(N=R/I\), \(\beta(g\cdot1)\equiv gf\) and \(\beta(I\cdot1)=\beta(0)=0\), so \(If\subseteq J'\) within degree. (c) If \(w\nu\notin J_N\), then \(B(w\nu)\notin\bigoplus_lJ_D\), so some coordinate \(\beta=\pi_l\circ B\), \(R\)-linear of shift at most \(0\), has \(\beta(w\nu)\notin J_D\), and \(\beta(J_N)\subseteq J_D\) by the definition of \(J_N\); (b) applies with \(\phi\) the identity, \(\sigma\le0\), \(J'=J_D\). For the direct sum take \(B\) the identity. (d) \(\phi_\partial\) is a ring map because \(\partial\) is a derivation, and expanding \(\phi_\partial(g)(\beta_0+\varepsilon\beta_1)\) gives the displayed form. For \(g\in R\), \(\phi_\partial(g)f=gf_0+\varepsilon(gf_1+\partial g\,f_0)\), which lies in \(J+\varepsilon J\) exactly when \(gf_0\in J\) and \(gf_1+\partial g\,f_0\in J\). Soundness at \(a_k\) gives \(a_kf_1+p_k(2x_k-1)f_0\in J\); \(a_kf_1\in J\), as a multiple of an axiom within degree, so \(p_k(2x_k-1)f_0\in J\); multiplying by \(2x_k-1\) and using \((2x_k-1)^2=4a_k+1\) and \(4p_ka_kf_0\in J\) gives \(p_kf_0\in J\) (in characteristic \(2\), \(2x_k-1=1\) and this is immediate). Hence \(\partial g\,f_0=\sum_k\partial_kg\,(p_kf_0)\in J\) within degree, and the conditions become \(gf_0\in J\) and \(gf_1\in J\): each of \(f_0,f_1\) satisfies soundness (\(g=a_jm\)) and killing (\(g=wE_i\)); survival, \(\phi_\partial(w)f\notin J+\varepsilon J\), means \(wf_0\notin J\) or \(wf_1+\partial w\,f_0\notin J\), that is \(wf_0\notin J\) or \(wf_1\notin J\). (e) \(A\) is closed under each \(x_i\) within degree \(D-K\) because \(g\nu\in F_DN\) there and \(J_N\) is closed; soundness and killing put the \(a_jm\) and \(wE_i\) in it, so it contains their closure at degree \(D-K\), and \(w\notin A\) by survival; a functional separating \(w\) from \(A\) exists by finite dimension.

Hypotheses, each with its step. Compatibility of \(\beta\) modulo \(J'\), up to \(\phi\), is the whole of (b): it turns \(\beta(g\nu)\) into \(\phi(g)f\); the closure of \(J'\) under each \(\phi(x_i)\) carries the induction; the degree bounds \(T\), \(\sigma\) and \(TD+\sigma\le D'\) keep every product in \(S_{D'}\); \(\beta(J_N)\subseteq J'\) is what makes survival follow from the target system's hardness; the pseudo-submodule closure of \(J_N\) is used in (a) and (e); (d) uses the Boolean axioms \(a_k\) of every variable with \(p_k\ne0\), soundness reaching each \(a_k\) (\(D-K\ge2\)) and the degree room \(\deg(p_k(2x_k-1)^2f_0)\le D'\), which follows from the window when \(T(D-K)\ge T+2\) and binds only at its edge. The field is arbitrary. The conclusions hold for every protected class \(w\), the base class \(1\) included. For the old system's own hardness \(J'\) must be its closure at degree \(D'\); another \(J'\) gives a repair of another system. In (b) for \(N=R/I\), \(\beta\) is determined by \(f\) only modulo \(J'\), and on the image of \(V_D\) when \(F_DN\) is the induced filtration.

Degenerate cases. \(N=R\), \(\beta\) the identity: (b) is the scalar repair itself. \(\nu=0\), or \(J_N=F_DN\): no survival. No errors: (e) gives the old hardness at degree \(D-K\). A satisfiable domain model: a design of the extended system exists if some point is a common zero of the axioms and the errors with \(w\ne0\), by evaluation there. \(N=R/I\) with \(I\ni1\): \(N=0\), no survival.

Nonvacuity. Over \(\mathbb F_2\), \(n=1\), the Boolean axiom \(x^2+x\), \(D=2\), \(N=R^2\) with \(J_N=J_2\oplus J_2\), \(J_2=\operatorname{span}\{x^2+x\}\), error \(E=x\), \(w=1\), \(\nu=(1+x,0)\), \(K=1\): soundness asks \(a_jm\nu\in J_N\) for \(\deg(a_jm)\le1\), vacuous here since the axiom has degree \(2\); \(wE\nu=(x+x^2,0)\in J_N\); \(w\nu=(1+x,0)\notin J_N\); (c) with \(B\) the identity returns \(f=1+x\). This witness exercises neither soundness nor a non-diagonal \(B\); the general hypotheses are met. No parameter ledger applies: the witness verifies no budget of the goal.

Baseline. Every scalar repair \(f\) is a module repair with \(N=R\), \(\nu=f\), so module repairs are at least as strong; the lemma adds that with survival certified by a map into \(R\) compatible up to a substitution, or by a differential certificate into the old closure on a Boolean system, they are no stronger than substitution followed by a scalar multiplier, and that every repair needs the extended system's hardness at degree \(D-K\).

New mathematics. lem:module-repair-factorization: repairs into any filtered module, free or with relations, are elements of it modulo its closure; a survival certificate into an algebra \(S\) compatible with multiplication up to a ring map turns a module repair into an \(S\)-valued repair of the image system, a scalar repair of no larger degree when \(S=R\), in particular on free modules with product or matrix-preimage closures; first-order differential certificates into the old closure, on systems whose differentiated variables carry Boolean axioms, yield scalar repairs on the window \(\deg(a_jm)\le D-K\); every repair implies the extended system's hardness at degree \(D-K\).
