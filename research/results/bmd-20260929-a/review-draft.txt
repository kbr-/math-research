
29 September 2026 — Review: one discriminant decides the first-step support off collisions
Working criterion with computer-assisted certificates; statement 3 stays open on collision divisors and for N≥8. Wronskian discriminant review.
The line and the goal. Open statement 3 asks for \(\alpha_N=\ell_0(3,p_N+1)=\lambda_N=3\binom N4\) for every \(N\ge7\). By the exact Ext formula this follows once the kernel \(L_N\) of \(C_N^+\to D_{22}(-4)\) has codimension at least three; the invariant-prefix theorem already removes every \(\delta_0\)-invariant height-two component. The line of the last cycles tried to exclude the remaining non-invariant components through generic normal rank two, and the dual-jet construction made that normal map explicit without bounding its rank. This review replaces the pointwise rank question by a global one: the ramification weight of the quadratic series at the mark, read along the Möbius flow, turns the whole off-collision obligation into squarefreeness of one invariant, the discriminant of the Wronskian covariant. That invariant is computable, and at \(N=7\) it is squarefree.
General statement. thm:cube-wronskian-discriminant-criterion holds for every characteristic-zero \(N\ge5\): if the part of the Wronskian discriminant prime to the collision forms is squarefree, then \(C_N^+\) has no codimension-two support off the discriminant divisor and the boundary maximal-minor ideal is reduced at every height-two prime meeting the separated locus. It rests on prop:cube-mark-weight-dictionary, valid for all \(N\ge3\). The target for all \(N\) is conj:cube-offcollision-contact-bound. The finite certificates (check:cube-wronskian-discriminant-certificates) test this conjecture at \(N=5,6,7\); they are not the general statement.

Mark weight dictionary

For a root vector \(a\in k^N\) with distinct entries, let \(X_a\) be the curve of the dual-jet theorem with branch points \(-a_1,\ldots,-a_N\): its function field is generated over \(k(z)\) by \(w_A^2=\prod_{i\in A}(z+a_i)\), \(|A|\) even. Transfer to the actual prefix
Lemma. In the deflated core, with \(\ell_1=2N-3\) and \(Q_i=\sum_{j\le i}SD_F^jv\), there are graded isomorphisms \(F/Q_i\cong V/\langle b_1,\ldots,D^{\ell_1+i}b_1\rangle\) for every \(i\ge0\). In particular \(C_N^+\cong V/\langle b_1,\ldots,D^{R+1}b_1\rangle\), and the boundary maximal-minor ideal \(J_N\) is the zeroth Fitting ideal of \(V/\langle b_1,\ldots,D^Rb_1\rangle\).
Proof. By the block presentation, \(B_1=\bigoplus_{j<\ell_1}SD^jb_1\) is a free summand of \(V\) with complement the remaining strings, identified with \(F\); let \(\pi:V\to F\) be the projection. Each feedback term \(C_rb_1\) has order at most \(w_r-4<\ell_1\), so it lies in \(B_1\). Hence, for \(x\in V\) with coefficient \(x_{\ell_1-1}\) on \(D^{\ell_1-1}b_1\), \(\pi(Dx)=D_F\pi(x)+x_{\ell_1-1}v\), because \(D^{\ell_1}b_1\equiv v\) modulo \(B_1\) by \(L_1b_1=v\). Induction gives \(\pi(D^{\ell_1+j}b_1)\in D_F^jv+\sum_{i<j}SD_F^iv\). Since the prefix contains \(B_1\), \(V/\langle b_1,\ldots,D^{\ell_1+i}b_1\rangle=F/\pi(\ldots)=F/Q_i\). With \(i=q+1\), \(\ell_1+q+1=R+1\). The Fitting statement follows because \(J_N=I_q(v_0,\ldots,v_q)\) is the zeroth Fitting ideal of \(F/Q_q\). \(\square\)
On the separated labeled-root locus the pair evaluation used in the marked normal-polynomial lemma identifies the pair-hull prefix \((b_1,\ldots,D^Lb_1)\) with \(A_L\), up to unit row and column factors. With the proposition this gives, over the finite étale root extension of \(S\) off the discriminant, \(\operatorname{Supp}C_N^+=\{\operatorname{rank}A_{R+1}<R\}\), the locus of a section with contact at least \(R+4\), and \(J_N=I_R(A_R)\). Such contact forces \(n_{R+1}\ge R+4\), hence \(\operatorname{wt}_P\ge3\).

Wronskian discriminant criterion
Definition. Clearing the denominators of \(F_{\rm sep}(b(z))\) by a power of \(\prod_i(a_i-z)\) and removing every factor \(z-a_i\) gives \(W\in k[a][z]\). By part (3) the roots of \(W_a=W(a;\cdot)\) are exactly the \(z\) whose fibre over \(-z\) is ramified, with multiplicity equal to the weight. Let \(\Delta_W\in k[a]\) be the discriminant of \(W_a\) as a binary form of degree \(\deg_zW\), and \(\Delta_W^{\rm nc}\) its part prime to the collision forms \(a_i-a_j\). At \(N=5,6,7\) the computation below finds \(\deg_zW=2\lambda_N\), with no root at any \(a_i\).
Theorem. Let \(N\ge5\). Suppose \(\Delta_W^{\rm nc}\) is nonzero and squarefree. Then on the separated locus \(U\):

the locus \(\operatorname{wt}_P\ge3\) has codimension at least three; in particular so does \(\{\operatorname{rank}A_{R+1}<R\}\), and \(C_N^+\) has no codimension-two support off the discriminant divisor;
every irreducible component of \(V(F_{\rm sep},\delta F_{\rm sep})\cap U\) has codimension two and weight exactly two at its generic point, and \((F_{\rm sep},\delta F_{\rm sep})\) is reduced there;
\(J_N=I_R(A_R)\) is reduced at every height-two prime meeting \(U\). Equivalently, by the normal-map criterion, every height-two component of the boundary off the discriminant divisor has generic corank one and normal rank two.

Proof. The vector fields \(\sum\partial_{a_i}\), \(\sum a_i\partial_{a_i}\) and \(\delta\) span a copy of \(\mathfrak{sl}_2\) acting on \(U\) by Möbius motions, with three-dimensional orbits. Translation and scaling preserve \(\operatorname{wt}_P\). Along a \(\delta\)-orbit the curve is fixed and only the mark moves, so the root sets of \(W\) correspond under a Möbius map with multiplicities. The two binary forms are therefore proportional with a nonvanishing factor, and \(\Delta_W\) is invariant up to units; its zero set in \(U\) is a union of orbits. No orbit lies in \(\{\operatorname{wt}_P\ge1\}\), since \(W_a\ne0\).
(1) Let \(Y\) be an irreducible component of \(\{\operatorname{wt}_P\ge3\}\cap U\), and let \(\Gamma\) be the closure of its flow-out, of dimension \(\dim Y+1\). On \(Y\) the form \(W_a\) has a root of multiplicity at least three at the mark position, so \(\Delta_W\) vanishes on \(\Gamma\). As \(\Delta_W\ne0\), \(\Gamma\) is a divisor and \(\operatorname{codim}Y\ge2\). Suppose \(\operatorname{codim}Y=2\). Then \(\Gamma\) is an irreducible divisor, not a collision divisor, along which \(W_a\) has a root of multiplicity \(m\ge3\). Take a curve germ \(y(\varepsilon)\) meeting \(\Gamma\) transversally at a smooth generic point. Hensel's lemma factors \(W_{y(\varepsilon)}=g_\varepsilon h_\varepsilon\) near that root, with \(g_0=(z-\zeta_0)^m\) and \(h_0(\zeta_0)\ne0\), so \(\operatorname{Disc}(g_\varepsilon)\) divides \(\Delta_W(y(\varepsilon))\) up to a unit. After translating \(z\) by \(\zeta_0\), every nonleading coefficient of \(g_\varepsilon\) is \(O(\varepsilon)\). The discriminant of a monic degree-\(m\) polynomial is isobaric of weight \(m(m-1)\) with coefficient weights at most \(m\), so each monomial has degree at least \(m-1\). Hence \(\operatorname{ord}_\Gamma\Delta_W\ge m-1\ge2\), contradicting squarefreeness. The contact locus and the support of \(C_N^+\) lie in \(\{\operatorname{wt}_P\ge3\}\) by the transfer paragraph.
(2) By part (2) of the proposition, \(X=V(F_{\rm sep},\delta F_{\rm sep})\cap U=\{\operatorname{wt}_P\ge2\}\). A codimension-one component would flow out to an open set and force \(\Delta_W=0\), so every component \(Z\) has codimension two, and by (1) weight exactly two at its generic point \(a_0\). Reducedness at \(a_0\) implies reducedness at the generic point, and may be checked in the completion \(\widehat{\mathcal O}\), since the local rings are excellent. Choose flow-box coordinates \((s,y)\) with \(\delta=\partial_s\). Then \(F_{\rm sep}(a_0)=\partial_sF_{\rm sep}(a_0)=0\ne\partial_s^2F_{\rm sep}(a_0)\), and Weierstrass preparation gives \(F_{\rm sep}=u\,(s^2+bs+c)\) with \(u\) a unit and \(b,c\in k[[y]]\). Hence
\[
(F_{\rm sep},\delta F_{\rm sep})\widehat{\mathcal O}=(s+b/2,\ \Delta),\qquad \Delta=b^2-4c,\qquad \widehat{\mathcal O}/(F_{\rm sep},\delta F_{\rm sep})\cong k[[y]]/(\Delta).
\]
For fixed \(y\), the two roots of \(s^2+bs+c\) are the two mark positions near \(a_0\) at which the flow line meets \(\{\operatorname{wt}_P\ge1\}\). By part (3) they correspond, under a map with nonzero derivative, to the two roots of \(W_{a(y)}\) that meet at \(y_0\). If a second root coincidence persisted along \(\Gamma\), the factorization argument would give \(\operatorname{ord}_\Gamma\Delta_W\ge2\). So near \(y_0\), \(\Delta_W=(\text{unit})\cdot\Delta\). A squarefree polynomial stays squarefree in the completion of an excellent reduced ring, so \(\Delta\) is squarefree and the quotient is reduced.
(3) The locus \(V(J_N)\cap U=\{\operatorname{rank}A_R<R\}\) has all components of codimension at most two, by the Eagon–Northcott bound for maximal minors. It lies in \(X\), by part (1) of the proposition, since contact at least \(R+3\) gives weight at least two. So its height-two components are components \(Z\) of \(X\). At the generic point the weight is two, and the vanishing sequence containing an order at least \(R+3\) is \((0,\ldots,R,R+3)\). Only one section has order at least \(R+1\), so \(\operatorname{rank}A_{R-2}=R-1\), and part (4) gives \(J_N=(F_{\rm sep},\delta F_{\rm sep})\), reduced by (2). The pair evaluation and root extension are étale off the discriminant, so reducedness passes to \(J_N\) over \(S\). \(\square\)
The proof also gives the converse direction used in the certificates: a codimension-two component of \(\{\operatorname{wt}_P\ge3\}\) forces \(\Delta_W\) to vanish at least twice along its flow-out. Squarefreeness is sufficient, not necessary, for (1). A repeated factor can instead come from weight-three points of type \((0,\ldots,R-1,R+1,R+3)\), where the contact is only \(R+3\) and \(C_N^+\) still vanishes; six roots exhibit exactly this.

Discriminant certificates
Tested statement and prediction. bmd_cube_wronskian_discriminant.c restricts to the line \(a_i(u)=\alpha_i+u\beta_i\) over \(\mathbb F_p\), \(p=2^{61}-1\), with seeded random \(\alpha,\beta\). It computes \(W(a(u);z)\) on a grid, recovers it by bivariate interpolation, evaluates \(D(u)=\operatorname{Disc}_zW\) at \(4\lambda(2\lambda-1)+6\) good points, and removes every collision factor \((u-u_{ij})\) to its full multiplicity. It then tests the remainder \(D^{\rm nc}\) for full degree and squarefreeness. Controls: \(F_{\rm sep}/\prod(a_i-a_j)^{N-2}\) restricted to the line must have degree \(\lambda\) and be squarefree (the proved boundary squarefreeness); every row must have exact \(z\)-degree \(2\lambda\) with no root at a branch point; the \(z^k\)-coefficient must have \(u\)-degree at most \(3\lambda-k\); the leading coefficient must be a constant multiple of \(F\); and \(D\) must stay within its degree bound. Before the \(N=7\) run we recorded the prediction of conj:cube-offcollision-contact-bound: either \(D^{\rm nc}\) is squarefree, or every repeated factor carries only multiple roots with \(\operatorname{rank}A_{R+1}=R\). A repeated factor of contact type \((0,\ldots,R,R+4)\) would have falsified it. Each certificate is a single decisive outcome, not a sample.

All controls passed at every size. Full degree and uniform collision multiplicities show that \(D^{\rm nc}\) is the restriction of \(\Delta_W^{\rm nc}\). The reduction is of a \(\mathbb Z[1/2]\) identity, so the usual argument applies: a square factor of the characteristic-zero invariant would reduce, by primitivity, to a nonconstant square factor on a full-degree line. Six roots. The exponent-four factor \(G\), of degree 45, splits over \(\mathbb F_p\) into sixteen linear, four quadratic and seven cubic factors. It is neither the involution invariant \(I_{15}\) (the product over the fifteen perfect matchings of the pencil determinants, \(\gcd=1\)) nor the boundary polynomial (\(\gcd=1\)). bmd_cube_repeated_root_type.gp examines all sixteen \(\mathbb F_p\)-rational roots of \(G\). At each, \(W\) has exactly two multiple roots, both triple, over \(\mathbb F_p\) or a quadratic extension. Moving the mark to either gives \(\operatorname{rank}A_{R-2}=13\), \(\operatorname{rank}A_R=14\), \(\operatorname{rank}A_{R+1}=15\), the type \((0,\ldots,R-1,R+1,R+3)\). Two triple roots give order at least four, matching the exponent. So at \(N=6\) weight-three marks of this type fill a divisor of unmarked configurations. There the criterion's hypothesis fails and \((F_{\rm sep},\delta F_{\rm sep})\) is not reduced: Weierstrass preparation gives a cubic whose ideal with its derivative lies in \((s^2,\gamma)\). The contact stays \(R+3\), so the tested points give no support of \(C_6^+\). The check covers these sixteen points of one line, not the roots of \(G\) over larger extensions.
Corollary (seven roots). For \(N=7\), \(C_7^+\) has no codimension-two support off the discriminant divisor, and \(J_7\) is reduced at every height-two prime not contained in it. With the invariant-prefix theorem, \(L_7\) has no codimension-two component except possibly non-invariant components inside the collision divisor. Thus \(\alpha_7=\lambda_7=105\) now follows from excluding such collision-supported components, or from bounding their Ext degree as in (WG).
Conjecture (off-collision contact bound). For every characteristic-zero \(N\ge5\), the separated root vectors at which some nonzero quadratic section has contact at least \(R+4\) with the mark form a set of codimension at least three; equivalently, \(C_N^+\) has no codimension-two support off the discriminant divisor. It holds at \(N=5,7\) by the certificates and is consistent at the tested six-root points. The squarefree form of the hypothesis is stronger and fails at \(N=6\).
Falsification attempt. Three tests could have broken the line. The five-root control could have produced a repeated non-collision factor even though five-root radicality is proved; it did not. The six-root run did produce one, and it was classified rather than set aside: it refutes uniform squarefreeness and boundary radicality at \(N=6\), but not the contact bound. The seven-root run, the first size in the range of statement 3, could have produced a contact-type repeated factor; it produced none.

Obstacle
Off collisions the first-step obligation is now one uniform statement, the off-collision contact bound. It is certified at \(N=7\) but has no proof for general \(N\), and the six-root excess shows that generic-dimension reasoning alone is unreliable. On the collision divisor, non-invariant height-two components of \(L_N\) remain unexcluded even at \(N=7\). The confluent limit of the first-step matrix supplies a relation with constant top coordinate at the generic point of the two-root collision, but it says nothing about codimension-one loci inside that divisor.

Outside leads

Discriminants of covariants (Sylvester and Cayley; Grace–Young, The Algebra of Invariants, Ch. VII). The discriminant of a covariant of a binary form is an invariant, so the Wronskian covariant's discriminant is one polynomial on configuration space whose divisor records every coincidence of ramification fibres. Targets open statement 3 off collisions. It breaks where a repeated factor has a non-contact origin, as at six roots. Answers. Squarefreeness of one invariant replaces the pointwise normal-rank question on every non-collision component at once, and is decidable per \(N\) by a full-degree line. Test. Passed: \(N=5\) and \(N=7\) are squarefree and the criterion is proved; at \(N=6\) the uniform squarefree form fails, while the contact bound survives at the tested points.
Eisenbud–Harris weight-two Weierstrass points and limit linear series (Invent. Math. 87, 1987). Their dimension counts for exceptional ramification loci proceed by degenerating to reducible curves, where the conditions split into Schubert conditions on components. Targets the all-\(N\) contact bound. It breaks where the admissible-cover limit of the deck-equivariant quadratic series is not a refined limit series. Answers. \(M_{0,N}\) is affine (a hyperplane-arrangement complement), so the projective closure of every excess divisor of dimension \(N-4\ge1\) meets the boundary of the Knudsen–Kapranov compactification. An inductive limit analysis at collision boundaries that excluded weight-three contact would prove the contact bound for all \(N\). Test. Passed for the boundary-meeting step, which is immediate from affineness; the limit analysis is the development task and was not attempted in this review.
Kleiman's transversality of a general translate (Compositio Math. 28, 1974). If a group acted transitively on the jet data, general members would meet each Schubert stratum in the expected codimension. Targets the contact bound. Answers. Homogeneity would give the codimension of every ramification type at once, with no computation. Test. Falsified: only a three-dimensional Möbius group acts, which cannot be transitive on the jet Grassmannian; moreover the six-root excess divisor violates the expected codimension of the type \((0,\ldots,R-1,R+1,R+3)\).


Absurd bridges

Wigner–von Neumann non-crossing (quantum chemistry). Translate the roots of \(W_a\) into energy levels of a real one-parameter family of symmetric operators, with real branch points as real parameters. Level repulsion puts crossings in codimension two, and discriminants of symmetric matrices are sums of squares (Ilyushechkin). Answers. A hyperbolic structure would say which divisors carry even multiplicity, as the six-root divisor does, and would bound triple crossings far below codimension two. Test. Falsified: bmd_cube_wronskian_real_roots.gp finds that \(W_a\) is not real-rooted at \(N=5\). The configurations \(0,1,3,7,12\) and \(-5,-2,1,4,11\) give ten real roots out of thirty; \(0,1,2,3,4\) gives nine real roots of a degree-29 form, one root being at infinity.
Thom–Arnold catastrophe theory (versal unfoldings of \(A_k\) singularities). Along each Möbius flow line, \(s\mapsto F_{\rm sep}(e^{s\delta}a)\) has an \(A_{w-1}\) zero at weight \(w\), and the curve moduli unfold it. In a versal unfolding the cusp \(A_2\), weight three, has codimension two in parameter space, which is exactly the contact bound. Answers. A Kodaira–Spencer surjectivity theorem for the Möbius-flow unfolding would prove the contact bound for every \(N\). Test. Falsified as a uniform principle: at \(N=6\) weight-three zeros fill a divisor, so the unfolding is not versal there. The seven-root certificate is consistent with versality in the range of statement 3.

Assessment and next step. The line changed character this cycle. The pointwise question, whether a surviving generic kernel of the normal map exists, is replaced by squarefreeness of one invariant. That invariant settles the whole off-collision part of the first-step obligation at seven roots, the first size in the range of statement 3. The evidence is a proved criterion, a positive certificate where it was needed, and a classified failure at six roots that explains why squarefreeness is only sufficient. What remains is concrete. At \(N=7\), exclude non-invariant height-two components of \(L_7\) inside the collision divisor. For every \(N\), prove the off-collision contact bound. On cube-odd-characteristic, open statement 3, the next cycle develops the collision part at seven roots. Carry the Möbius-flow dictionary to the two-root confluent limit, whose first-step matrix already has a relation with constant top coordinate at the generic collision point, and decide the codimension-one loci inside that divisor where the limit prefix can fail to be onto. A larger discriminant run would not address either remaining obligation, and none is proposed.
