## Result

Original module witness costs determine which old moment degrees can actually
be queried for each selector support. Once those active component functionals
annihilate the old base and satisfy the module equations jointly, each may be
extended independently to its full old window by the existing matching
extension theorem. No conditioning-compatible choice of extension maps is
needed during this final completion.

Conversely, any full joint solution restricts to an active one. The result
does not prove that active solutions exist.

For the binary modular compiler, choose its uniform OR witness ceiling 3L.
The target weight is at most 2L, so the full degree-D legal OR multiples only
query weighted polynomial degree at most E = D-L. Source Booleanity is handled
by the old and selector Boolean support normal forms.

The active problem must still include the original degree-D legal cofactor
set. Reindexing that set at cutoff E with a formal cost 2L is bookkeeping,
not a new concrete 2L OR witness.
