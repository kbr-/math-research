Coefficient-fed division

Lemma. Fix a monic \(d(z)\in\mathbb F_2[z]\) of degree \(b\ge0\), and \(\nu\ge b\). Suppose a joint binary division-free unbounded-fanin arithmetic circuit of gate/wire size \(S\), depth \(\delta\), supplies the complete coefficient list \(a_0(y),\ldots,a_\nu(y)\) of \(g(z,y)=\sum_{i=0}^{\nu}a_i(y)z^i\), with the promise \(d\mid g\) in \(\mathbb F_2[y,z]\). There is a binary circuit for \(h=g/d\) of size \(O(S+(\nu+1)^2)\), depth at most \(\delta+3\). The divisor, degree bound and all transform constants are fixed nonuniform parameters, not circuit inputs. No circuit for the coefficient list is constructed from a supplied circuit for \(g\). For nonzero \(g\), actual joint degree satisfies \(\deg h=\deg g-b\); this is not a PC-degree or simulation-image claim.

Proof. Let \(u=\nu-b\), \(d^*(t)=t^bd(1/t)\), and let binary constants \(c_0,\ldots,c_u\) be the coefficients through degree \(u\) of \(1/d^*(t)\). These exist because \(d^*(0)=1\). Reversing the identity \(g=dh\) with the degree bounds gives \[ t^\nu g(1/t,y)=d^*(t)t^u h(1/t,y). \] The rightmost polynomial has degree at most \(u\). Multiplying by the truncated inverse therefore proves, for \(0\le j\le u\), \[ h_j(y)=\sum_{l=0}^{u-j}c_l a_{b+j+l}(y),\qquad h(z,y)=\sum_{j=0}^u h_j(y)z^j.\tag{1} \] Each inner transform is a sum of supplied coefficient outputs, with shared original circuits. There are at most \((\nu+1)^2\) wires. Construct every \(z^j\) with its ordinary \(j\) wires and one product gate; the total is also quadratic. The coefficient sums, output products and final sum give \(\delta+3\) as a conservative depth bound. No extension constants, inverse gates, tests or succinct powers are required. Total degree is additive for nonzero polynomial products in the domain, and \(\deg d=b\), proving the degree assertion. This is ordinary monic division with fixed scalar coefficients, not a new division algorithm for implicit inputs. \(\square\)

Nonvacuity. For every \(b,u\ge0\), take any monic binary divisor and \(h=z^u\prod_{i=1}^v(1+y_i)\). The coefficient list of \(g=dh\) consists of shared copies of that displayed product and zeros, and satisfies the assumptions for \(\nu=b+u\), at unbounded counts. In particular \(d=(z^2+z)^k\) has \(b=2k\) and arbitrary growing \(k\). This is a supplied-coefficient witness, not a hard formula or an algorithm obtaining such inputs.

Degenerate cases. Zero \(g\) gives zero output by (1); the degree equality is stated only for nonzero \(g\). For \(b=0\), monicity gives \(d=1\) and the construction returns \(g\). Padded leading zero coefficients are allowed. A nonmonic, variable-coefficient or zero divisor is outside the statement. The restriction \(\nu\ge b\) is harmless: if a nonzero multiple has smaller degree in \(z\) than a monic divisor, polynomial division in \(\mathbb F_2[y][z]\) contradicts that degree. No certificate hypothesis is used by this elementary interface lemma.
