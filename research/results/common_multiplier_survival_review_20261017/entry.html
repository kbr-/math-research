Obstacle

The line is stuck on lower bounds modulo the proper closure. Every recorded tool gives the joint action of errors from above: domain models give the stacked rank \(\dim U|_S\), and the proper closure can only lower it (saving monotonicity, the support bound). What the composition needs, through lem:exact-common-kernel-selection, is a common killer of degree at most \(K\) outside the closure, that is \(\operatorname{rank}(\rho,q)>\operatorname{rank}\rho\) modulo \(C\): a statement that a killer survives. The domain model supplies killers (lem:pang-family-killer, the blocking-edge products of prop:parity-product-repair, transversal products). Their survival in a proper closure is known in special cases: at the product system's top degree, where the closure is the domain model plus span conditions the support absorbs (lem:top-degree-killer-transfer, prop:top-degree-native-count), and for one Pang family, whose Fermat killer \(q\) survives unless it lies in the closure's degree-one part, a one-line membership. What has no general statement is survival where the summed condition fails, for several families at once, below the top degree, where the pigeon axioms act in every window; and no recorded result says which closure killers exist beyond the domain's there, except one identity (ex:top-degree-linear-killer). Reading the recorded transfer results for this, and the period's six entries, found none.

Falsification attempt. The falsification stays open; it is the Decisive test below in another form.

Extra requirements. The route demands more than the target in one way the blind stage made plain: it measures savings, a difference of ranks, where the composition needs only the survival of one common killer, a one-sided statement. A saving modulo the closure is bounded above by the domain's, so a saving-based route needs a lower bound on a difference of ranks; a survival-based route needs a lower bound on one rank, \(\operatorname{rank}(\rho,q)\), against an upper bound already recorded, \(\operatorname{rank}\rho\le\dim U|_S\). The demand for savings can be weakened to the demand for survival, provided the survival is where the summed condition fails (\(\sum_ar_a\ge h\)) and by a mechanism the endpoint admits; otherwise it is met trivially, by the summed condition or by one family's Fermat killer.
