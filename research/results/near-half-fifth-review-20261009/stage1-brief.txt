GOAL. Superpolynomial proof-size lower bounds for every fixed depth of AC0[2]-Frege.

NOW PROVED IN THE RECORD (working proofs, each reviewed once; no imported premise):
T. Transfer. Fix a Frege system F in the DeMorgan language with unbounded MOD_2 connectives (Krajicek's
   F_d(MOD_2)), depth d, exponent A; l = d + c_F, c_F an uncomputed constant >= 1. If CNFs F_n of width w_n and
   auxiliary affine equations Lambda_n over F_2 (not part of the formula), n = (number of variables) - rank
   Lambda_n, have no polynomial-calculus refutation over F_2 of degree floor(n/2 - s_n) from clause polynomials,
   Boolean axioms and Lambda_n, with (s_n + w_n)(log n)^(l-2) = o(n), then F_n has no F_d(MOD_2)-refutation with
   n^A symbols for large n. A single family with s_n + w_n <= n^(1-eps) serves every fixed depth.
   Inside the proof the dimension n enters in exactly one place: for the down-closed family B of standard
   monomials (squarefree, in the n non-pivot variables) of certain kernels Z containing C_D, the count
   (j+1) b_{j+1} <= (n-j) b_j, giving rate eta = (1/2) N_n(K-delta)/N_n(K) per approximation factor, K the cutoff,
   delta the degree of the approximated inputs (polylog below the top level). Earlier entries proved that the
   same engine works if instead the level sizes of the standard monomials are at most C(X, j) for X about 2K
   (Kruskal-Katona), or each standard monomial has few standard extensions; both force every set shattered by
   the closure through degree K to have size at most about 2K.

STILL OPEN: the family. No polynomial-size CNF (with any auxiliary affine equations) is known with PC degree over
F_2 within n^(1-eps), or even o(n/(log n)^k), of half its free dimension.

WHAT IS KNOWN ABOUT THE FAMILY QUESTION (all from the record):
F1. Actual ceilings: regular even modular-counting sources with canonical rows of width k: degree <=
    v/2 - (8/9 - o(1)) v/k; fixed-width systems: occurrence ceilings with linear deficit unless many variables
    occur often; several structured sources (colourings, codes of fixed modulus) are bounded away from half.
F2. Method ceilings: Alekhnovich-Razborov expansion bounds give at most v/4 (single-tuple) and 0.034 v (immunity)
    when every variable is in two rows. A hypothetical rank version for rows on linear forms would have deficit
    of order n/k at full expansion.
F3. An attempt to prove a general ceiling "degree <= max(k, n/2 - c n/k) + O(1) for relations on k affine forms"
    failed at its first step, and even "degree <= n/2 + O(k)" for all such systems has no proof in the record;
    no bounded-width example needing degree well above n/2 over F_2 is known to us either.
F4. Local screens: rows on dense random parities pass every local obstruction near n/2. Measured: 3n random
    products of two affine forms have exact degree 3 at n <= 16.
F5. Clause width is now paid in the same budget as the deficit, so width up to n^(1-eps) is allowed; a
    polynomial-size unsatisfiable CNF still has a clause of width <= log2(number of clauses). Non-affine
    auxiliary variables count in n.
F6. Tool: exact refutation degree by truncated Buchberger completion (pure Python, n up to about 16-20).
