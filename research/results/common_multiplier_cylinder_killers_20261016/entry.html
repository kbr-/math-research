Cylinder killer degree

Statement. Over a field \(F\), let \(X=\prod_{j\in[n]}A_j\) with finite nonempty \(A_j\) and a base state \(0_j\in A_j\); for \(a\in A_j\setminus\{0_j\}\) let \(x_{j,a}(s)=[s_j=a]\), and call the degree of a function on \(X\) the least degree of a polynomial in these variables that represents it. A cylinder is a partial assignment \(\alpha\) on a set \(\operatorname{dom}\alpha\) of coordinates; let \(\mathcal C\) be a family of cylinders and \(U\) the union of the points extending them. (a) Every nonzero function on \(X\) vanishing on \(U\) has degree at least \(\tau=\tau(\{\operatorname{dom}\alpha:\alpha\in\mathcal C\})\), the least size of a set of coordinates meeting every \(\operatorname{dom}\alpha\). (b) If \(T\) is such a set of size \(\tau\) and every \(j\in T\) has a state \(b_j\) that no cylinder assigns at \(j\), then \(\prod_{j\in T}[s_j=b_j]\), of degree at most \(\tau\), is nonzero and vanishes on \(U\). (c) For the product pigeonhole system, with \(Y\) the hole-respecting assignments as the product over holes of “empty” and the pigeons, base state “empty”, and monomial errors on distinct holes, whose supports are cylinders assigning pigeons, the least degree of a nonzero function on \(Y\) vanishing on the union of supports is the transversal number \(\tau\) of the errors' hole sets; when \(\tau\le K\) and \(K+e\) lies in the inert range of cor:hole-occupancy-kernels, or \(D=n\) with \(K+e\le n\) and \(K\le n-1\) by the transfer below, it is the least degree of a surviving common killer of \(1\), and when \(\tau>K\) there is none of degree at most \(K\).

Proof. On \(X\), \(x_{j,a}x_{j,b}=[a=b]\,x_{j,a}\), so every polynomial reduces, without raising its degree, to a combination of the monomials \(x_S=\prod_{j\in\operatorname{dom}S}x_{j,S(j)}\) over partial assignments \(S\) with non-base values; these are a basis of the functions on \(X\), since on each factor \(1\) and the \(x_{j,a}\) are a basis of \(F^{A_j}\) and the monomials are their tensor products. So the degree of a function is the largest \(|\operatorname{dom}S|\) with a nonzero coefficient. (a) Let \(f\ne0\) vanish on \(U\), and let \(T\) be the domain of a monomial \(x_S\) with nonzero coefficient and \(|T|\) largest. Fix any states \(z\) of the coordinates outside \(T\); in the restriction \(g_z\) of \(f\) to the fibre \(\{s:s|_{[n]\setminus T}=z\}\), a function on \(\prod_{j\in T}A_j\), the part of degree \(|T|\) comes only from monomials with domain exactly \(T\), by maximality, and is unchanged, so \(g_z\ne0\). If some cylinder had \(\operatorname{dom}\alpha\cap T=\emptyset\), choose \(z\) extending \(\alpha\): the whole fibre over \(z\) lies in \(U\), so \(g_z=0\), a contradiction. Hence \(T\) meets every \(\operatorname{dom}\alpha\), and \(\deg f=|T|\ge\tau\). (b) \([s_j=b]\) is \(x_{j,b}\) for \(b\ne0_j\) and \(1-\sum_ax_{j,a}\) for \(b=0_j\), of degree at most \(1\); the product is the indicator of \(\{s:s_j=b_j\ \forall j\in T\}\), nonempty, and every cylinder has a coordinate \(j\in T\) at which it assigns a state other than \(b_j\), so the product vanishes on it. (c) The support of \(\prod x_{i_kj_k}\) with distinct holes is the cylinder assigning pigeon \(i_k\) to hole \(j_k\); “empty” is assigned by none, so (b) applies with \(b_j\) empty, and (a) gives the lower bound. In the inert range, by cor:hole-occupancy-kernels (a), a common killer of the errors that keeps \(1\) is a function of degree at most \(K\) on \(Y\) vanishing on the union of supports and nonzero; at \(D=n\) the transfer below gives the same conditions, and \(f\notin C\) means \(f|_Y\ne0\) by the clause \(C\cap V_{n-1}=H\cap V_{n-1}\) of lem:top-degree-axiom-inertness (b), as \(K\le n-1\).

Relations. It is the reviewer's proof in the goal review (Attempt checked), stated on any product domain. On the cube with base state \(1\) for each coordinate it is the first-degree part of lem:transversal-killer-basis, without its basis count; lem:greedy-transversal-certificate, in Braun's row-linear setting with a greedy count, and historical lem:columnconditioning build products of the kind in (b). prop:polynomial-majority-rank-floor uses the same tensor support-degree argument for majority inputs on disjoint blocks.

Top-degree killer transfer

Statement. For the product pigeonhole system with \(m\) pigeons and \(n\ge1\) holes over a field, at \(D=n\), let \(E\) have degree \(e\), \(K+e\le n\), \(S_E\) its support on \(Y\) and \(1_{Z_i}\) the indicator of “pigeon \(i\) unplaced”. (a) If the restrictions of the \(1_{Z_i}\) to \(Y\setminus S_E\) are linearly independent, then for \(f\in V_K\), \(fE\in C\) exactly when \(f\) vanishes on \(S_E\). (b) The independence holds when \(Y\setminus S_E\) contains the empty assignment and, for each pigeon \(k\), an assignment placing only \(k\); it also holds for every monomial error of degree \(e\ge1\) on distinct holes, for all \(m\) and \(n\), by that condition when \(n\ge2\) and by a separate argument when \(n=1\). (c) For \(K+e\le n-1\) the conclusion of (a) holds with no hypothesis, by cor:hole-occupancy-kernels (a); the content of the lemma is the case \(K+e=n\).

Proof. (a) By cor:hole-occupancy-kernels (d), \(fE\in C\) exactly when \((fE)|_Y=\sum_i\lambda_i1_{Z_i}\) for some \(\lambda\), as \(\deg(fE)\le n\). The left side vanishes on \(Y\setminus S_E\), so \(\sum_i\lambda_i1_{Z_i}\) does, and independence gives \(\lambda=0\); then \(fE\) vanishes on \(Y\), and as \(E\ne0\) on \(S_E\), \(f\) vanishes there. Conversely, if \(f\) vanishes on \(S_E\), then \((fE)|_Y=0\). (b) At the empty assignment every pigeon is unplaced, so a combination vanishing there has \(\sum_i\lambda_i=0\); at an assignment placing only \(k\) it takes the value \(\sum_{i\ne k}\lambda_i=-\lambda_k\), so \(\lambda_k=0\). For a monomial error of degree \(e\ge1\) on distinct holes, the empty assignment lies off \(S_E\), which needs a placed pigeon; the assignment placing only \(k\) in hole \(h\) lies in \(S_E\) only if \(E=x_{kh}\), and then placing \(k\) in another hole works when \(n\ge2\), while for \(n=1\) the other pigeons' points give \(\lambda_i=0\) for \(i\ne k\) and \(\sum\lambda_i=0\) gives \(\lambda_k=0\). (c) For \(K+e\le n-1\), \(fE\) has degree below \(n\), where \(C\) agrees with the vanishing ideal of \(Y\).

Against the record. check:truncated-kernel-beyond-support lies at the top degree: \(4\) pigeons, \(3\) holes, \(D=3=n\), \(K=1\), errors \(Pg_i\) of degree \(2\) with \(P=1+g_1+g_2\), which on \(Y\) over \(\mathbb F_2\) agree with \(g_1g_2\). For the four families with \(g_1g_2\) on distinct holes, a monomial of degree \(2\), the transfer (b) applies, so each kernel is the zero set of restriction to the support: for \(S_E=\{x_{00}=x_{01}=1\}\) the degree-\(\le1\) functions on \(S_E\) are spanned by \(1\) and the four variables of hole \(2\), so the rank is \(5\), the recorded codimension, and the four ranks sum to the recorded \(20\); on the union of the supports the nine functions \(1\), \(x_{i1}\), \(x_{i2}\) stay independent, the hole-\(0\) variables being constant there, giving the recorded stacked rank \(9\). The two families in one hole have \(g_1g_2\) vanishing on \(Y\), codimension \(0\), as recorded. So for these errors the proper closure's kernels equal those of the domain model on \(Y\), and ex:support-saving-not-inherited compares the cube's support ranks with that domain model, although the closure at this degree is \(H\) plus the span of the pigeon axioms.

Degenerate cases. No cylinders: \(\tau=0\) and the constant \(1\) is a nonzero function vanishing on \(U=\emptyset\). A cylinder with empty domain: \(U=X\), no set meets \(\emptyset\), only \(0\) vanishes on \(U\), and \(\tau\) is infinite. A coordinate of a least transversal where every state is assigned by some cylinder: (b) does not apply there, and (a) still bounds the degree from below. In the transfer, a constant error \(e=0\): its support is all of \(Y\) when it is nonzero, the independence fails, and (a) does not apply; any number of pigeons \(m\): (b) needs no relation between \(m\) and \(n\).

New mathematics. lem:cylinder-killer-degree: on product domains the least degree of a nonzero function vanishing on a union of cylinders is the transversal number of their coordinate sets, attained by state indicators where a free state exists; lem:top-degree-killer-transfer: at the top degree of the product pigeonhole system, the condition on a multiplier from an error reduces to vanishing on its support whenever the unplaced-pigeon indicators stay independent off it, which holds when the support misses the empty assignment and a single placement of each pigeon, and for every monomial error of positive degree. Together they decide the review's test and show that monomial errors gain no killer from the pigeon axioms.
