Word coefficient access

Proposition (word coefficient access). For every integer \(n\ge1\), there is a free polynomial \(Q_n\in\mathbb F_2\langle x_0,x_1\rangle\) with an ordered unbounded-fan-in formula of size \(O(n^4)\), depth at most four and degree at most \(n^2+2n\), such that \[ [x_{b_1}\cdots x_{b_n}]Q_n =\mathbf1\!\left\{\sum_{i=1}^n b_i\equiv0\pmod3\right\} \quad(b\in\{0,1\}^n). \tag{1} \] Every bipartition flattening of its degree-\(n\) word tensor has binary rank at most three. Nevertheless the Boolean query function in (1) has no polynomial-size fixed-depth \(\mathrm{AC}^0[2]\) circuits. In particular there is no generic guarantee, uniform in growing query length and polynomial degree, that a size-\(S\), fixed-depth binary noncommutative formula has a word-coefficient query circuit of size polynomial in its size, degree and query length and depth bounded only by its original depth. The bound is uniform over parameters; even nonuniform query circuit families are excluded.

Proof. Define \[ Z_n=\sum_{j=0}^n x_0^j,\qquad Q_n=\sum_{t=0}^{\lfloor n/3\rfloor} Z_n(x_1Z_n)^{3t}. \tag{2} \] Every power denotes its full ordered product; repeated-factor wires and formula copies are counted. A word with \(3t\) occurrences of \(x_1\) has a unique decomposition into its \(3t+1\) runs of \(x_0\), including empty runs. For total length \(n\), every run length is at most \(n\). It therefore occurs exactly once in that summand of (2), and in no other summand. Words whose \(x_1\) count is not divisible by three occur nowhere. This proves (1) as an exact free coefficient identity, with no parity cancellation hidden in the count.

Each \(Z_n\) is a sum of products, with \(\sum_{j\le n}j=O(n^2)\) input wires and depth two. Flatten the outer products in (2) into alternating copies of \(Z_n\) and \(x_1\). Their total number of \(Z_n\) copies is \(\sum_{t\le n/3}(3t+1)=O(n^2)\); hence full tree size is \(O(n^4)\), with one further product and sum layer. The maximum degree is at most \((3t+1)n+3t\le n^2+2n\). No formula sharing or exponentiation gate is free.

For any partition of the \(n\) positions into \(A,B\), a matrix entry depends only on the number of ones modulo three on each side. There are at most three distinct row vectors, so its rank is at most three over \(\mathbb F_2\). This concerns the degree-\(n\) word tensor, not all components or all ranks of the whole inhomogeneous polynomial. Finally the recorded Smolensky consequence excludes polynomial-size fixed-depth binary circuits for MOD-3. A generic query guarantee applied at original depth four and size/degree polynomial in \(n\) would supply exactly such a circuit, contradicting (1). This proves the proposition.

Nonvacuity. The construction uses two free letters at every \(n\) and grows without changing the binary field. The all-zero word has coefficient one; a word with exactly one \(x_1\) has coefficient zero. Thus the query is nonconstant already at \(n=1\), while the circuit lower bound is asymptotic. Degenerate cases. Empty runs and the \(t=0\) summand are included. For \(n=1,2\), only that summand is needed. The zero-length query is outside the stated family and has constant answer one here. Empty-sided flattenings have rank at most one. A fixed query length is not ruled out by this argument. No hardness of evaluating \(Q_n\) at Boolean scalar values is asserted; its supplied formula already gives cheap evaluation.

This is different from the earlier extension-field coordinate obstruction: (2) already has a small formula over \(\mathbb F_2\). It is also different from the interleaved rank control in thm:affine-component-ranks: the queried tensor here has bounded matrix rank but a hard Boolean coefficient function. Neither comparison proves an all-certificate lower bound. The source is the elementary run decomposition plus the existing circuit lower bound; no new literature novelty is asserted.
