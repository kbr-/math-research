General statement. lem:leading-form-killer-bound: in a degree-\(D\) pseudoideal \(C\) of the multilinear ring, the top parts of members form a graded pseudoideal \(T\) of \(F[x]/(x_v^2)\); within the window \(\deg f+\max_je_j\le D\), a surviving common killer can be reduced to one whose leading form lies outside \(T\) and multiplies every error's leading form into \(T\), so the least degree \(\beta'\) of such a graded killer bounds every surviving killer from below; errors spanning a unit modulo \(C\) leave no surviving killer at all, which \(\beta'\) can miss, since the graded algebra drops lower-degree parts.

Leading-form killer bound

Statement. Over a field \(F\), in the multilinear ring with the degree filtration, let \(G=F[x]/(x_v^2)\), \(\operatorname{top}(h)\in G_{\deg h}\) the top-degree part, \(C\) a degree-\(D\) pseudoideal, \(T_k\subseteq G_k\) the top parts of the members of \(C\) of degree exactly \(k\), with \(0\), and \(T=\bigoplus_kT_k\). Let \(E_1,\dots,E_r\) have degrees \(e_j\), \(e=\max_je_j\). (a) If \(fE\in C\) with \(\deg f=a\), \(\deg E=e'\), then \(\operatorname{top}(f)\operatorname{top}(E)\in T_{a+e'}\) (product in \(G\); \(T_k=0\) for \(k>D\)). (b) \(T\) is a graded pseudoideal of \(G\): for \(c\in C\) of degree \(k<D\), \(x_v\operatorname{top}(c)\in T_{k+1}\). (c) Reduction: if \(f\notin C\), \(\deg f=a\le D-e\), \(fE_j\in C\) for all \(j\), and \(\operatorname{top}(f)\in T_a\), there is \(f'\notin C\) of degree below \(a\) with \(f'E_j\in C\) for all \(j\). (d) Let \(\beta'\) be the least \(a\le D-e\) with some \(g\in G_a\setminus T_a\) such that \(g\operatorname{top}(E_j)\in T_{a+e_j}\) for every \(j\), or none. Every surviving common killer of degree at most \(D-e\) has degree at least \(\beta'\), and if \(\beta'\) is none there is none. (e) Unit combinations: if \(1-\sum_ja_jE_j\in C\) for scalars \(a_j\), there is no surviving common killer of degree at most \(D-e\).

Proof. (a) In the multilinear ring \(x_Sx_T=x_{S\cup T}\), so the degree-\((a+e')\) part of \(fE\) comes only from pairs of top monomials with disjoint supports, which is \(\operatorname{top}(f)\operatorname{top}(E)\) in \(G\). If it is nonzero it is the top part of the member \(fE\); otherwise it is \(0\in T\). (b) \(x_vc\in C\) for \(\deg c<D\), and apply (a) with \(E=x_v\). (c) \(T_a\) is the image of the members of degree at most \(a\) in \(G_a\), a subspace, so some \(c\in C\) of degree \(a\) has \(\operatorname{top}(c)=\operatorname{top}(f)\). Since \(\deg c+e_j\le D\), \(cE_j\in C\): multiply \(c\) by the variables of each monomial of \(E_j\) one at a time, every intermediate product having degree at most \(D\), and add. So \(f'=f-c\) kills every \(E_j\), is not in \(C\), and has degree below \(a\). (d) Iterate (c) from a surviving killer of degree at most \(D-e\); it stops at a surviving killer \(f'\) with \(\operatorname{top}(f')\notin T_{\deg f'}\), nonzero, and (a) puts \(g=\operatorname{top}(f')\) in the set defining \(\beta'\). (e) For \(f\) of degree at most \(D-e\) killing every \(E_j\), \(f=f\bigl(1-\sum_ja_jE_j\bigr)+\sum_ja_jfE_j\); the first term is in \(C\) by the closure argument of (c), as \(\deg(1-\sum a_jE_j)\le e\); so \(f\in C\).

Where it can miss. In (e) the relation \(1-\sum a_jE_j\in C\) has a constant term, which the graded algebra drops: \(T_e\) contains \(\sum_{e_j=e}a_j\operatorname{top}(E_j)\) when it is nonzero, a relation of positive degree, and no graded condition forces \(g\in T\). So \(\beta'\) can be finite where (e) excludes every survivor, as for the variables of pigeons \(0\) and \(1\) below. It is not always blind there: \(T\) contains the top parts of the inhomogeneous axioms, \(\sum_jx_{ij}\in T_1\), and for all thirty variables, another unit case, \(\beta'\) certified that nothing survives.

Relations. (d) refines lem:graded-domain-pairing (c): with \(D\ge\deg f+e\) and \(C\) the degree-\(D\) part of the vanishing ideal of a domain, \(T_k\) is the initial ideal in degree \(k\), and a nonzero function has leading form outside it. On domain models lem:cylinder-killer-degree gives the exact degree. (e) is the special case of a unit in the span of the errors; for one pigeon's variables over \(F_2\) it is \(f=fP_i+\sum_jfx_{ij}\).

Degenerate cases. \(1\in C\): \(T_0=G_0\), nothing survives, and \(\beta'\) is none. A constant error \(E=1\): (e) with \(a=1\); \(\beta'\) is none as well, since \(g\cdot1\in T_a\) contradicts \(g\notin T_a\). An error in \(C\): it imposes nothing. \(C\) with no member of degree at most \(D-e\): \(T_a=0\) below that, and \(\beta'\) is the least degree of a nonzero graded killer.

Computation

beta_prime.py (script, committed before each run with its expectations) computes \(T\), \(\beta'\) and the least surviving killer degree \(k^*\) for the functional pigeonhole system over \(F_2\) with \(6\) pigeons and \(5\) holes at \(D=3\), closure dimension \(4056\). Second run: for the single variable \(x_{00}\), the pairs \(\{x_{00},x_{11}\},\{x_{00},x_{10}\},\{x_{00},x_{01}\}\), the product \(x_{00}x_{11}\) and the pair \(\{x_{00}x_{11},x_{22}x_{33}\}\), \(\beta'=k^*=1\), the trivial bound, since each has a linear killer from a monomial axiom; for all thirty variables both are none up to degree \(2\); for the ten variables of pigeons \(0\) and \(1\), \(\beta'=2\) while no surviving killer of degree at most \(2\) exists, which (e) proves. Third run, after the review, adding \(\{x_{00},x_{11},x_{22}\}\) and \(\{x_{00},x_{11},x_{22},x_{33}\}\), whose common linear kernel is \(C\cap V_1\) (check:functional-factor-kernels): \(\beta'=k^*=2\) for both, the bound tight and above the trivial one. The constant-error control gave none for both in every run. The first run, on the first six sets, gave the same values (output).

Nonvacuity. The first six sets were controls that could not fail: by (d) \(\beta'\le k^*\), \(\beta'=0\) would need the error's top part in \(T\), and \(T_1\) is spanned by the \(\sum_jx_{ij}\), so only \(\beta'=k^*=1\) could occur. The informative sets have \(D-e=2\) and \(k^*\in\{2,\text{none}\}\): the run could show \(\beta'=1<k^*\), \(\beta'=k^*=2\), or \(\beta'\) finite with \(k^*\) none; the second occurred for the three- and four-variable sets, the third for pigeons \(0\) and \(1\). The lemma's hypotheses hold there: a field, a proper degree-\(3\) pseudoideal, errors of degree \(1\) within the window. No parameter ledger applies: the witness verifies no budget of the goal.
