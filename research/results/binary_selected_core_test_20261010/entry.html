Closing under selected words recovers the handoff construction

A direct way to make selected features \(a_1,\ldots,a_t\), each of degree at most \(\delta\), act invariantly is to include all their Boolean words. Those words have representatives through degree \(t\delta\), and multiplication by a feature stays in their span after Boolean reduction. This is the historical conditioning construction in operator notation: it pays the same feature-degree sum. In a pseudoideal, every word multiplication and Boolean reduction additionally needs its paid cutoff; treating this as a full ambient Boolean algebra is not justified.
