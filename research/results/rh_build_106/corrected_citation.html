What it says on Turán's line

In the model and to leading order only; nothing here is a statement about \(\zeta\). In the last entry's reading, Turán's premise at a height \(\tau\) bounds \(|P(u)|e^{-\beta_0u}/a(\beta_0)\) on \(u\ge2\log\tau\) by \(\tau^{-1}e^{(1-\beta_0)u}\) up to powers of \(u\); its most demanding point is the window's start \(U=2\log\tau\), where it asks \(\tau^{-(2\beta_0-1)}\). The comb with \(\Lambda=(2\beta_0-1)\log\tau+O(\log\log\tau)\) meets that on the whole window \([U,U+R]\). Its number of points is at most \(\tfrac{4\Lambda}d\sqrt{1+r}+3\), and the largest admissible \(d\) is \(2\pi/(2+r)\), giving \(\tfrac{2\Lambda}\pi(2+r)\sqrt{1+r}+3\); with \(g=d/U\) the heights lie within \(Jg\le2\Lambda\sqrt{1+r}/U+d/U\) of \(\tau\). For \(\beta_0=0.6\) and the longest window of that reading, \(R=\tfrac12\log\tau\) and \(r\) near \(\tfrac14\): about \(0.32\log\tau\) points within about \(0.23\) of the height. A local count of the form "fewer than \(\log\tau\) zeros within one of the height", the form used in prop:tilted-local-count-reach, does not exclude that many. So in the model: a point strictly rightmost in the configuration can be hidden from the premise's window by a number of near-tied points proportional to \(\log\tau\) with a constant below what a local count allows; a deduction of an exclusion from the premise must therefore assume fewer near-tied points near the height, as cor:sparse-off-line-exclusion does with its small constant.
