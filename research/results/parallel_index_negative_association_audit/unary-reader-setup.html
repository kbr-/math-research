1. Setting

A unary reader is a disjunction \(F=\bigvee_{(i,x)\in P_F}[\text{row }i\text{ pinned to }x]\) over a set \(P_F\) of pairs; \(\deg(x)\) is the number of rows pinned to \(x\). In the random-flat family \(\rho'=(Q,R',\mu')\), a pair is satisfied if \(\mu'(i)=x\), alive if \(i\in R'\) and \(x\in Q\), and otherwise falsified (\(\mu'(i)\ne x\)) or annihilated (\(i\in R'\), \(x\notin Q\): every \(X_{ix}\) with \(x\) matched to another row is zero after the restriction). The alive pairs form the bipartite graph \(B(\rho')\subseteq R'\times Q\) with matching number \(\nu(B)\). Fix \(k_1\ge1\), \(k_2\ge2\), and a source size \(S\); put

\[ D^\ast=\frac{k_1(n-k_1)}{2e(N+1)N},\qquad \Delta_0=N+\frac{2(n-N)\ln(8S)}{D^\ast}, \]

and assume \(D^\ast\ge2(N+1)\). Call a label dense if \(\deg(x)\ge D^\ast\), sparse otherwise, and let \(D\) be the set of dense labels.
