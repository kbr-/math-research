The action and its transfer

Let the field have characteristic two. Write \(A\) for an \(m\times n\) matrix of indeterminates and \(B\) for an \(n\times t\) matrix. The upper-unitriangular group \(U_n(\mathbb F_2)\) acts on polynomials by substituting \((AU,U^{-1}B)\). Its transfer is \(\operatorname{Tr}_U(f)=\sum_{U\in U_n(\mathbb F_2)}U\cdot f\), as a formal polynomial. Total degree includes both matrices; no Boolean reduction is made in this paragraph. Variables fixed by the action can be included.

Proposition (unipotent transfer degree). For \(n\ge2\), the transfer is identically zero on every polynomial of degree less than \(\lfloor n^2/4\rfloor\). For \(1\le k<n\), the rectangular subgroup \(H_k=\{I+N_X:X\in\mathbb F_2^{k\times(n-k)}\}\), with \(N_X\) supported in the upper-right rectangle, kills all degrees below \(r=k(n-k)\). If \(m\ge k\), its threshold is sharp: for \(w=\prod_{i\le k<j}A_{ij}\), \[\operatorname{Tr}_{H_k}(w)=\det(A_{[k],[k]})^{n-k}.\] If \(m\ge n\), the full-group transfer is nonzero on some polynomial of degree \(n(n-1)/2\). No exact full-group threshold is asserted.

Proof. Since \(N_X^2=0\), both \((I+N_X)\) and its inverse are affine in the \(r\) independent binary entries of \(X\). A degree-\(d\) polynomial after substitution has parameter degree at most \(d\). If \(d<r\), each parameter monomial omits some entry; summing over that entry multiplies it by two and gives zero. This proves the rectangular assertion as a formal identity, even over a larger characteristic-two field. Partition the full group into cosets so each inner sum is a rectangular transfer of a fixed linear transform of \(f\). That transform preserves degree, so each sum is zero. Maximize \(k(n-k)\).

For the sharp witness, the sum in each column \(j>k\) selects the coefficient of all \(k\) parameters in \(\prod_{i=1}^k(A_{ij}+\sum_{a=1}^k A_{ia}X_{aj})\). It is the permanent of the leading \(k\times k\) matrix, equal to its determinant in characteristic two. Distinct columns use disjoint parameters, giving the stated power. This power is a formal polynomial and is not replaced by the determinant. Finally take \(\prod_{i<j}A_{ij}\) and evaluate the first \(n\) rows of \(A\) at the identity. The transformed polynomial is the product of all free upper-triangular entries of \(U\). Its sum is one, proving nonvanishing. \(\square\)

Window comparison. A weighted transfer \(R_w(f)=\operatorname{Tr}_U(wf)\) has normalization \(R_w(1)=0\) if \(\deg w<\lfloor n^2/4\rfloor\), before any quotient. For \(n\ge4\) this covers every input weight within the recorded matrix-PC window \(D\le(n+1)/2\). It excludes this low-input-degree trace construction, not higher-degree input weights with independently proved cheaper representations, smaller groups, or nonlinear substitutions. The earlier odd-order certificate average does not apply to this even-order group.

A multiplicative partial normalization

Now work over \(\mathbb F_2\), let \(B\) have shape \(n\times m\), and assume \(1\le k<n\), \(m\ge k\). In the first \(k\) rows of \(A\), put \(M=A_{[k],[k]}\) and \(C=A_{[k],\{k+1,\ldots,n\}}\). Set \[ W=\det M,\qquad X=\operatorname{adj}(M)C,\qquad H_X=I+N_X,\qquad \sigma(A)=AH_X,\quad\sigma(B)=H_XB. \tag{1}\] All fresh source variables are fixed. This is a polynomial substitution, not an assertion that \(\sigma\) is invertible. Let \(I_q(F)\) denote polynomials with ordinary certificates from generators \(F\) whose individual summands have degree at most \(q\); let \(\mathcal C_D(F)\) denote degree-\(D\) PC closure. We use explicit certificates for Boolean identities, not merely equality at field points.

Theorem (weighted rectangular gauge). The map (1) has degree at most \(T=k+1\), fixes \(AB\) and \(W\) literally, and satisfies \(\sigma(C)=(1+W)C\). For a fixed binary rectangular matrix \(Y\) and \(h_Y=(I+N_Y)\), every polynomial \(f\) of degree at most \(s\ge1\) satisfies \[ W\bigl(h_Y\cdot\sigma(f)-\sigma(f)\bigr)\in I_{k+(k+1)s}(\mathrm{Bool}). \tag{2}\] Here \(h_Y\) acts on the output old variables, fixing fresh variables. Also \(W\sigma(C)\in I_{2k+1}(\mathrm{Bool})\). On \(\Gamma_{m,n}=\{AB-I_m,\mathrm{Bool}(A,B)\}\), with \(m>n\), the class of \(W\) is nonzero modulo \(\mathcal C_D(\Gamma_{m,n})\) whenever \[2\le D,\qquad k\le D,\qquad n-k\ge2D-1.\tag{3}\] An original algebraic source proof through degree \(B_{\rm src}\ge2\) transfers through \(TB_{\rm src}\), and its weighted replay through \(k+TB_{\rm src}\), with complete source blocks and sharing retained. This is a transfer of the proof and source, not a derivation of its remaining companions from the old base.

Proof of the identities. The adjugate has degree \(k-1\), including \(\operatorname{adj}(M)=1\) when \(k=1\), so \(\deg X\le k\). Since \(H_X^2=I\) as a polynomial matrix, \(\sigma(A)\sigma(B)=AB\). The first \(k\) columns of \(A\) are unchanged, giving \(\sigma(W)=W\). The leading upper-right block becomes \(C+MX=(1+W)C\).

Under \(h_Y\), the matrix \(M\) is unchanged and \(C\) becomes \(C+MY\); hence \(X\) becomes \(X+WY\). Multiplication of rectangular shears adds their rectangles. Therefore the exact coordinate differences between the normalized transformed point and the normalized point are \[ \sigma(Ah_Y,h_YB)_A-\sigma(A,B)_A=(1+W)AN_Y,\quad \sigma(Ah_Y,h_YB)_B-\sigma(A,B)_B=(1+W)N_YB.\tag{4}\] The notation in (4) means evaluation of the coordinate formulas defining \(\sigma\) at the transformed point. It proves output invariance in (2), not \(W(\sigma(h_Y\cdot f)-\sigma(f))=0\).

For any binary-coefficient polynomial \(q\) of degree \(a\), \(q^2+q\) has a Boolean certificate through \(2a\): expand the Frobenius square monomial by monomial and telescope each monomial's square against itself, reducing each variable power by \(x^2+x\). In particular \(W^2+W\in I_{2k}(\mathrm{Bool})\). Multiplying (4) by \(W\) gives coordinate certificates through \(2k+1\). Telescope a monomial of \(f\) of degree at most \(s\) one coordinate at a time. The other factors have degree at most \(T(s-1)\), so the total certificate degree is at most \(2k+1+T(s-1)=k+Ts\). Fresh coordinates contribute zero difference. This proves (2), and the same Boolean certificate gives \(W\sigma(C)=0\) at \(2k+1\). Constants have zero difference without this bound.

Proof of survival. Restrict the first \(k\) outer rows and inner columns to a common identity frame in both matrices: set their diagonal blocks to \(I_k\), every off-diagonal block incident to the frame to zero, and leave the \((m-k)\times(n-k)\) and \((n-k)\times(m-k)\) remaining blocks free. The rank equations become \(\Gamma_{m-k,n-k}\), with tautological discarded equations; \(W\) becomes one. A degree-\(D\) derivation of \(W\) would therefore refute this residual matrix base at degree \(D\). The recorded algebraic baseline in the quadratic rank construction excludes this whenever \(m-k>n-k\ge2D-1\) and \(D\ge2\). Its proof identifies the residual two matrices with transposed copies of functional PHP variables; each matrix axiom has a degree-two derivation from that harder-to-refute PHP base. The condition \(k\le D\) ensures that \(W\) is in the truncated space under discussion. No satisfying assignment of the inconsistent rank base is asserted.

Proof of the source ledger. Apply one global substitution, including every repeated old occurrence in shared input expressions. Old rank equations are fixed; images of old Boolean equations have certificates through \(2T\). Fresh coefficient fields remain unchanged. Each complete block retains every original input position and becomes the same complete block on the substituted inputs, because substitution preserves sums and products. A degree-\(B_{\rm src}\) PC proof substitutes to a proof through \(TB_{\rm src}\): linear combinations commute with substitution, and multiplication by one source variable is simulated by multiplying by its image polynomial one monomial at a time. Every partial product fits the original multiplication step's degree times \(T\). Multiplying the replay by \(W\) pays another \(k\); the old Boolean certificates fit because \(B_{\rm src}\ge2\). This gives a derivation of \(W\) from the retained transformed companions and the old base, not from the old base alone. All original degree ceilings remain in this calculation. If starting at the recorded CNF/basis-change boundary, first pay its separate \(\widehat B=\max(2B,6)\), then \(k+(k+1)\widehat B\). \(\square\)

What the partial symmetry leaves

Unbounded calibration. Take \(m=2n\), \(k=\lfloor n^\epsilon\rfloor\) for a fixed \(0<\epsilon<1\), \(D=\lfloor n/4\rfloor\), and any fixed polylogarithmic original source ceiling \(B_{\rm src}\). For sufficiently large \(n\), (3) holds and \(k+(k+1)B_{\rm src}=o(n)<D\). The subgroup has \(k(n-k)\) independent binary shear parameters. Thus a nontrivial unbounded normalization fits the hard window independently of source inventory. This asserts a degree budget, not a small-depth implementation of the adjugate or arbitrary error annihilation.

Residual-source control. Under the identity-frame restriction used above, \(C=0\), hence \(X=0\), \(W=1\), and \(\sigma\) is the identity on the residual two matrices and all fresh coefficients. Any complete source on \(\Gamma_{m-k,n-k}\) can be written in those remaining entries and recovered by this restriction after normalization. Therefore partial invariance alone gives no automatic small input profile: the normalized class still has every source on a smaller rank base as a constant restriction. This is an exact residual section, not a general impossibility theorem for exploiting symmetry together with the original proof's structure.

Functional-PHP control. Now make the recorded stronger identification \(\psi(A_{ij})=\psi(B_{ji})=z_{ij}\) and add functional PHP row exclusions \(z_{ia}z_{ib}=0\) for \(a\ne b\). In characteristic two, \(W\) is the sum of the matching monomials on the first \(k\) rows and holes. For \(i\le k<j\), every term of \(Wz_{ij}\) contains a same-row exclusion, so \(Wz_{ij}\) has a certificate through \(k+1\). Thus \(W\psi(C)=0\) through \(k+1\). The coordinate corrections in (1) are entries of \(A_{\cdot,[k]}\operatorname{adj}(M)C\) and \(\operatorname{adj}(M)C B_{\{k+1,\ldots,n\},\cdot}\). After weighting and applying \(\psi\), each is zero through \(2k+1\), by multiplying those certificates by at most \(k\) more degrees. Telescoping as before proves \[W\bigl(\psi\sigma(f)-\psi(f)\bigr)\in I_{k+(k+1)s}(\mathrm{functional\ PHP})\] for \(\deg f\le s\), including fresh variables fixed by both maps. The transformation is therefore a weighted identity after this stronger base. Same-row exclusions are essential to this argument; they were not silently available in the bare matrix base.

Degenerate cases. Constant polynomials have zero transfer for a nontrivial group and zero invariance defect. The empty rectangle \(k=0\) or \(k=n\) is excluded because its transfer is the identity; \(n=1\) is likewise excluded from the transfer obstruction. Singular \(M\) gives \(W=0\), so weighted identities say zero equals zero; the survival argument is necessary to prevent that stratum from being the only content. When \(m\le n\), the algebraic identities still hold, but no rank-base hardness consequence is claimed. No map is required to be invertible. At \(k=1\) the adjugate convention is the scalar one and all displayed identities apply.

Nonvacuity. The unbounded calibration supplies weights surviving a proper PC closure on unsatisfiable bases. For the formal transfer, the rectangular determinant power and the full-unitriangular identity-frame witness are nonzero, so the operator is not vacuous in all degrees. Finite Boolean gauge checks use the unconstrained ambient matrix cube, not purported satisfying assignments of \(AB=I_m\) when \(m>n\).
