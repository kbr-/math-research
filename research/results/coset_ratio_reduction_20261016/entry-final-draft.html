The coset form

Setting as in lem:local-tuple-ratio: the charged character pseudoideal \(J_D\) in its window, components \(E_i=P_im_{\gamma_i}\) with \(P_i\) a function of the states of \(T\), \(|T|=\tau\), \(e_{\max}+K\le D\), \(K+\tau+|\operatorname{supp}\gamma_i|\le D\); \(\mathcal F_j\) the span of the \(T\)-characters \(m_\psi\), \(\psi\in W=\mathbb F_3^T\), of support at most \(j\); \(R_j=\operatorname{span}\{(R_D(E_ig))_i:g\in\mathcal F_j\}\), \(r_j=\dim R_j\), so \(R_\tau\) is the span over all \(T\)-functions; \(q_T(k)\) the number of cosets of \(\mathcal B+W\) met by the support-\(k\) ball.

Lemma (coset ratio reduction). (i) For \(0\le k\le K\), \(c(k)\le r_\tau\,q_T(k)\). (ii) If \(c(K)>0\), then for \(0\le J\le K\) with \(r_J>0\) and \(0\le d\le K\), \[\frac{c(K-d)}{c(K)}\ \le\ \frac{r_\tau}{r_J}\cdot\frac{q_T(K-d)}{q_T(K-J)}\ =\ \frac{r_\tau}{r_J}\cdot\frac{h_{G-T}(K-d)}{h_{G-T}(K-J)},\] the equality by lem:pin-rank-transfer (b); with \(J=j^*\), the least \(j\) with \(r_j=r_\tau\), when \(j^*\le K\) (automatic when \(\tau\le K\), as \(j^*\le\tau\)), the factor \(r_\tau/r_J\) is \(1\).

Proof. (i) lem:local-tuple-ratio's proof writes the image at level \(k\) as "\(I_k=\sum_{\alpha'\text{ off }T,\ |\operatorname{supp}\alpha'|\le k}M_{\alpha'}(R_{k-|\operatorname{supp}\alpha'|})\)". Group the \(\alpha'\) by their coset \(\kappa\) of \(\mathcal B+W\) and fix in each a representative \(\alpha'_\kappa\) off \(T\) of least support. For \(\alpha'\in\kappa\) write \(\alpha'-\alpha'_\kappa=\beta-\beta_T\) with \(\beta\in\mathcal B\) and \(\beta_T\) its part on \(T\), both \(\alpha'\) and \(\alpha'_\kappa\) being off \(T\). For \(g\in\mathcal F_j\), \(P_ig\) is a combination of \(m_\psi\), \(\psi\in W\), and each exponent \(\psi+\gamma_i+\alpha'\) differs by \(\beta\) from \(\psi-\beta_T+\gamma_i+\alpha'_\kappa\), both of support at most \(\tau+|\operatorname{supp}\gamma_i|+k\le D\). By lem:cut-code-classes, "Two characters of degree at most \(D\) have the same quotient coordinate exactly when their exponents differ by \(\mathcal B\)", and by the translation remark in lem:local-tuple-ratio's setting, "the relative phase depends on the difference only", here \(\beta\), the same for every \(\psi\) and every \(i\); the supports are at most \(\tau+|\operatorname{supp}\gamma_i|+k\) because \(|\psi-\beta_T|\le\tau\) and \(|\alpha'_\kappa|\le|\alpha'|\le k\), which is why the representative of least support is taken. So \((R_D(E_im_{\alpha'}g))_i\) is that scalar times \((R_D(E_im_{\alpha'_\kappa}m_{-\beta_T}g))_i\), and \(m_{-\beta_T}g\) is a \(T\)-function: \(M_{\alpha'}(R_j)\subseteq M_{\alpha'_\kappa}(R_\tau)\). Hence \(I_k\subseteq\sum_\kappa M_{\alpha'_\kappa}(R_\tau)\) over the \(q_T(k)\) cosets met by the ball, each summand of dimension at most \(r_\tau\). (ii) lem:local-tuple-ratio's lower bound gives \(c(K)\ge\sum_{j\le K}\Delta r_j\,q_T(K-j)\ge r_J\,q_T(K-J)\), as \(q_T\) does not decrease in its level; divide (i) at \(K-d\) by it. \(\square\)

The count \(r_\tau\). For one component \(E=Pm_\gamma\): writing \(P=\sum_aP(a)1_{T=a}\) over labels, \(1_{T=a}\) a constant times the conjunction \(L_{T,a}\) of thm:conjunction-quotient-rank, \(R_\tau\) is spanned by the images of the \(L_{T,a}m_\gamma\) with \(P(a)\ne0\). Incompatible labels have zero image. For a compatible label the image of \(L_{T,a}\) on the class of \(\beta\in W\) is \(\kappa_ac_\beta\,\omega^{-a\cdot\beta}\), a common nonzero function \(c_\beta\) of the class times \(\omega^{-(a-a_0)\cdot\beta}\) for a fixed compatible \(a_0\), and the differences \(a-a_0\) annihilate \(\mathcal B_T\), so distinct compatible labels give distinct characters of \(W/\mathcal B_T\), independent; translation by \(\gamma\), injective by the translation remark, keeps them independent. So \(r_\tau\) is the number of compatible labels at which \(P\) is nonzero, at most \(3^{\tau-\dim\mathcal B_T}\), and (i) is the decomposition into conjunctions with that exact count.

Meta. The habit is closing a sentence on the strongest available verb: an upper bound becomes "is", a sufficient condition "reduced", a reviewer's guess "suggests".
