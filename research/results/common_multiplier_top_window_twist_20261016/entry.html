Top-window twist scalarity

Setting. As in lem:twist-window-trade: \(R=k[x]\) over a field, \(\rho:R\to\operatorname{Mat}_k(R)\) an algebra map given by commuting \(\rho(x_i)\) of entry degree at most \(T\ge1\), and \(\nu\in R^k\) a twisted repair at window \(D-K\) with room \(R_0\):

  • soundness, \(\rho(a_jm)\nu\in J^k\) for \(\deg(a_jm)\le D-K\);
  • killing, \(\rho(wE_i)\nu\in J^k\) for \(\deg(wE_i)\le D-K\);
  • survival, \(\rho(w)\nu\notin J^k\);
  • room, \(T(D-K)+\deg\nu\le R_0\le D'\).
Here \(J=C\) is the degree-\(n\) closure of the product pigeonhole system with \(m\) pigeons and \(n\) holes together with the Boolean axioms \(x_{ij}^2-x_{ij}\): hole axioms \(x_{ij}x_{kj}\), \(i\ne k\), and pigeon axioms \(P_i=\prod_j(1-x_{ij})\) of degree \(n\). \(Y\) is the set of hole-respecting assignments. A variable \(v\) is twisted when \(\rho(v)\ne vI\).
  • Membership. By lem:top-degree-axiom-inertness, whose statement covers Boolean axioms among the generators, the closure \(H\) of the hole and Boolean axioms is the degree-\(n\) part of the vanishing ideal of \(Y\). The proof of cor:hole-occupancy-kernels (d) then applies word for word: a polynomial \(c\) of degree at most \(n\) lies in \(C\) exactly when \(c|_Y\in P_Y=\operatorname{span}\{1_{Z_i}\}\), \(Z_i\) the points of \(Y\) where pigeon \(i\) is unplaced.
  • Cylinders. For an untwisted \(x_{kb}\) and \(f\) of degree at most \(n-1\): \(x_{kb}f\in C\) exactly when \(f\) vanishes on \(\{s_k=b\}\). Indeed \((x_{kb}f)|_Y\) vanishes off that cylinder, and a combination \(\sum_i\lambda_i1_{Z_i}\) vanishing off it is zero: at points with \(s_k=\bot\), placing one other pigeon \(i\) or none gives \(\lambda_i=0\) for \(i\ne k\) and then \(\lambda_k=0\). So the membership forces \(x_{kb}f|_Y=0\).

Lemma (top-window twist scalarity). Let \(n\ge2\), \(m\ge2\), \(D'=n\), and let \(\nu\) be a twisted repair at window \(D-K=n\) whose twisted variables are hole variables on distinct pigeons and distinct holes. Then:

  • \(T=1\) and \(\deg\nu=0\);
  • \(\rho(x)\nu=x\nu\) for every twisted \(x\), and \(\rho(g)\nu=g\nu\) for every \(g\in R\);
  • such a repair exists exactly when the weight \(w\) is a repair: \(w\notin C\) and \(wE_i\in C\) for every error \(E_i\) within the window.

Proof. The room \(Tn+\deg\nu\le n\) with \(T\ge1\) gives \(T=1\) and \(\deg\nu=0\), so \(\nu\) is a constant vector. Fix a twisted \(x=x_{ab}\) and a coordinate \(f=c+\sum\beta_{ij}x_{ij}\) of \(\rho(x)\nu\), of degree at most \(1\). Pigeon \(a\) carries no other twisted variable, so \(\rho(P_a)\nu=\prod_{j\ne b}(1-x_{aj})(\nu-\rho(x)\nu)\), \(\rho\) being an algebra map. Its coordinate \(h=\prod_{j\ne b}(1-x_{aj})(\nu_c-f)\) has degree at most \(n\), and soundness puts it in \(C\). Modulo the Boolean axioms within degree \(n\), \(h\) may be taken multilinear. On \(Y\) the product is the indicator of \(s_a\in\{b,\bot\}\), and \(h|_Y=\sum_i\lambda_i1_{Z_i}\).

  • At points with \(s_a=j\ne b\), \(h=0\). With the other pigeons unplaced, \(\sum_{i\ne a}\lambda_i=0\). Placing one pigeon \(i\ne a\) in a hole other than \(j\), which \(n\ge2\) allows, gives \(\lambda_i=0\). So \(h|_Y=\lambda_a1_{Z_a}\).
  • At \(s_a=\bot\): \(\nu_c-f(y)=\lambda_a\) for every placement of the others. A single pigeon \(i\ne a\) in a hole \(j\), the rest unplaced, gives \(\beta_{ij}=0\) for all \(i\ne a\) and all \(j\). So \(f=c+\sum_j\beta_{aj}x_{aj}\).
  • Cylinders. For a pigeon \(k\ne a\), which \(m\ge2\) gives, the hole axiom \(xx_{kb}\) has degree \(2\le n\), and \(x_{kb}\) is untwisted, hole \(b\) carrying no other twisted variable. So soundness gives \(x_{kb}f\in C\), and \(f\) vanishes on \(\{s_k=b\}\). The point with only \(k\) placed, in \(b\), gives \(c=0\). Adding \(a\) in a hole \(j\ne b\) gives \(\beta_{aj}=0\).
  • At \(s_a=b\): \(h=0\), so \(\nu_c-\beta_{ab}=0\). Hence \(f=\nu_cx\), that is \(\rho(x)\nu=x\nu\).
  • Every \(g\). For a scalar polynomial \(p\) and any variable \(v\), \(\rho(v)(p\nu)=p\,\rho(v)\nu=pv\nu\), the twisted case by the above and the untwisted case by definition. By induction on the factors of a monomial, \(\rho(v_1\cdots v_r)\nu=v_1\cdots v_r\nu\), and by linearity \(\rho(g)\nu=g\nu\).
  • Existence. Survival gives a coordinate with \(\nu_cw\notin C\), so \(\nu_c\ne0\) and \(w\notin C\). Killing at that coordinate gives \(\nu_cwE_i\in C\), so \(wE_i\in C\). Conversely, if \(w\) is a repair, \(k=1\), \(\nu=1\), \(\rho(g)=g\) is one, untwisted. \(\square\)

Consequence on the instance. Three pigeons, two holes, twisted \(x_{00}\) and \(x_{11}\), weight \(w=x_{00}\), error \(x_{11}\). By the lemma, a repair exists exactly when \(x_{00}x_{11}\in C\). It is not: if \(x_{00}x_{11}|_Y=\sum_i\lambda_i1_{Z_i}\), the point \(s=(0,1,\bot)\) gives \(\lambda_2=1\). The points \((0,\bot,\bot)\), \((\bot,1,\bot)\) and \((\bot,\bot,\bot)\) give \(\lambda_1+\lambda_2=0\), \(\lambda_0+\lambda_2=0\) and \(\lambda_0+\lambda_1+\lambda_2=0\), hence \(\lambda_2=0\), a contradiction. So at window \(2\) and room \(2\) no twisted repair of this kind with weight \(x_{00}\) kills \(x_{11}\), and no scalar one does either.

Hypotheses, each with its step.

  • Window \(n\): the pigeon-axiom step uses soundness at \(P_a\), of degree \(n\). The room at \(D'=n\) then forces \(T=1\) and \(\deg\nu=0\). Below window \(n\) the conclusion fails: at window \(n-1\) with \(D'=n\), the substitution \(x_{11}\mapsto0\) is sound and not scalar (Suggestion checked).
  • \(n\ge2\): the hole axioms of degree \(2\) lie in the window, and the pigeon step places a pigeon in a second hole. At \(n=1\) the conclusion is false: with \(m\ge2\), \(k=1\), \(\nu=1\) and \(\rho(x_{00})=x_{10}\), the window \(1\) holds only the pigeon axioms \(1-x_{i0}\). \(\rho(1-x_{00})=1-x_{10}\) lies in \(C\), and the room is \(1\le1\), but \(\rho(x_{00})\nu\ne x_{00}\nu\).
  • \(m\ge2\): the cylinder step needs a pigeon \(k\ne a\). At \(m=1\) there are no hole axioms, and \(f\) keeps the free terms \(\beta_{aj}x_{aj}\).
  • Distinct pigeons: \(\rho(P_a)\) factors with one twisted factor. Distinct holes: the partner \(x_{kb}\) in the cylinder step is untwisted. When both fail, a pigeon permutation \(\rho(x_{ij})=x_{\sigma(i)j}\) with \(\nu=1\) is a sound, non-scalar map, by the reviewer's example; whether each alone is needed was not checked.
  • The field: none. The Boolean axioms enter the Membership step, where the closure of the hole and Boolean axioms is the degree-\(n\) part of the vanishing ideal of \(Y\).
  • Commutation of the \(\rho(x_i)\) is used only through \(\rho\) being an algebra map.
The conclusion holds for every twisted repair in this class, every weight and every error set.

Degenerate cases.

  • No twisted variable: \(\rho(g)=gI\), and the lemma is the definition.
  • \(\nu=0\): survival fails, so there is no repair.
  • \(k=1\): \(\rho(x)\) is a linear substitution, and the lemma forces \(\rho(x)\nu=x\nu\). A linear substitution of \(x\) is sound at window \(n\) only as the identity.
  • \(m=1\) and \(n=1\): see the Hypotheses; at \(n=1\) the conclusion is false.

Nonvacuity. Three pigeons, two holes, \(k=2\), \(\nu=(1,0)\), \(\rho(x_{00})=x_{00}I+x_{20}E_{12}\) and \(\rho(x_{11})=x_{11}I+x_{21}E_{12}\), other variables scalar, weight \(x_{00}\), error \(x_{10}\).

  • The matrices commute, since \(E_{12}^2=0\), and have entry degree \(1\). Both are twisted, on distinct pigeons and holes.
  • \(E_{12}\nu=0\), so \(\rho(g)\nu=g\nu\), and soundness holds, the axioms, Boolean ones included, lying in \(C\).
  • Killing holds since \(x_{00}x_{10}\) is a hole axiom.
  • Survival: \(x_{00}|_Y\notin P_Y\). The points \((\bot,\bot,\bot)\), \((0,\bot,\bot)\) and \((1,\bot,\bot)\) would force \(\lambda_0+\lambda_1+\lambda_2=0\), \(\lambda_1+\lambda_2=1\) and \(\lambda_1+\lambda_2=0\).
  • Room: \(1\cdot2+0=2\).
