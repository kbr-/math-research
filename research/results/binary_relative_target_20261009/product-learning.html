Affine product learning

Work over \(\mathbb F_2\). Let the old base \(\mathcal F(x)\) contain Booleanity. Retain a complete family of old-affine bottom blocks, with products \(P_c\), inputs \(g_{c,j}\), and all companions \(g_{c,j}P_c\) and coefficient fields. Denote this retained base by \(\Gamma_0\), and write \(w_c=\deg P_c\).

Add a terminal family of fresh complete parent blocks with actual inputs \(q_{a,i}\), positive accuracies \(h_a\), maximum ordinary input degree \(\delta_a\ge1\), and companion costs \[ e_{a,i}=h_a(\delta_a+1)+d_{a,i},\qquad d_{a,i}=\deg q_{a,i}. \] Zero inputs may be ignored, and constant unit tuples may be removed directly. Parents do not read one another. For each parent supply old-affine \(K_{a,j},\lambda_{a,c}\), and constant representations of both \(K_{a,j}\) and \(P_c+\lambda_{a,c}\) in its actual input span, for selected bottoms \(c\in C_a\). Extra arbitrary parent inputs are allowed. Require \(w_c\le\delta_a\) for selected products.

Suppose one old polynomial \(f\), of actual degree \(s\le\kappa\), has for every parent a supplied identity \[ f=\sum_j u_{a,j}K_{a,j} +\sum_{c\in C_a,j}v_{a,c,j}\lambda_{a,c}g_{c,j}+E_a, \quad E_a\in\mathcal I_\kappa(\mathcal F), \] where all coefficients are old-only, \(\deg u_{a,j}\le\kappa-1\), \(\deg v_{a,c,j}\le\kappa-2\), and \(\kappa\ge2\). The quadratic terms retain cost two even when they simplify or vanish; the displayed cofactor bound is part of the premise.

Lemma (affine product learning). A degree-\(D\) ordinary NS or PC refutation of the complete retained-plus-parent system implies, respectively, \[ f\in\mathcal I_{TD+s}(\Gamma_0) \quad\hbox{or}\quad f\in\mathcal C_{TD+s}(\Gamma_0),\qquad T=\kappa-1. \] Consequently any such \(f\) separated from that retained consequence space excludes the refutation. All original parent coefficients are mapped; every bottom variable and its equations remain retained.

Proof. The actual bottom companions give the exact identity \[ \lambda_{a,c}g_{c,j} =g_{c,j}(P_c+\lambda_{a,c})+g_{c,j}P_c. \] Substitute it in the supplied old identity and use the constant parent-span representations. This produces \[ f=H_a+R_a,\qquad H_a=\sum_i A_{a,i}q_{a,i}, \quad\deg A_{a,i}\le\kappa-1, \] where \[ R_a=E_a+\sum_{c,j}v_{a,c,j}g_{c,j}P_c \in\mathcal I_{\kappa+\delta_a-1}(\Gamma_0). \] Indeed a bottom summand has certificate cost at most \(\kappa-2+1+w_c\le\kappa+\delta_a-1\), and the old residual costs at most \(\kappa\). These are ordinary cofactor certificates, not semantic agreement on the full inconsistent base.

Map the first coefficient row of parent \(a\) to the old polynomials \(A_{a,i}\), and all its other rows to zero. Fix every retained variable. The parent product becomes \(1-H_a=1-f+R_a\). Its weighted actual companion is \[ f q_{a,i}(1-H_a) =q_{a,i}(f-f^2)+f q_{a,i}R_a. \] The first term has an old Boolean certificate through \(2s+d_{a,i}\). The second has a retained certificate through \[ \kappa+\delta_a-1+s+d_{a,i} \le T e_{a,i}+s. \] For the last inequality use \(h_a\ge1\) and \[ (\kappa-1)(\delta_a+1+d_{a,i}) -(\kappa+\delta_a-1+d_{a,i}) =(\kappa-2)(\delta_a+d_{a,i})\ge0. \] The Boolean term also fits, since \(s\le\kappa\le T(\delta_a+1)\). Weighted parent fields have old certificates through \(2T+s\); retained generators are unchanged and weighted by \(f\).

In NS a cofactor of original degree at most \(D-e_{a,i}\) maps to degree at most \(T(D-e_{a,i})\), so every complete summand fits \(TD+s\). The same argument covers fields and retained generators. In PC first derive the weighted axiom images, then replay a line \(v\) as \(f\Phi(v)\). Multiplication by a source variable is multiplication by an old polynomial of degree at most \(T\), or by one retained variable; a nonzero predecessor has original degree at most \(D-1\). Expansion into primitive multiplications fits \(TD+s\), and linear steps commute. The final line is \(f\). \(\square\)

The premise \(w_c\le\delta_a\) is automatic for a genuinely exposed \(P_c+\lambda_{a,c}\) when \(w_c\ge2\), since an old affine term cannot cancel its highest degree; for \(w_c\le1\) it follows from \(\delta_a\ge1\). It is stated explicitly to keep the original-degree dependency visible. The coefficients and the common target, not only membership in unrestricted ideals, must be supplied.

What a mixture matrix does and does not expose

For a parent \(q=L+A P\), where \(L\) is old-affine and \(A\) has full column rank, choose a constant left inverse \(B\), \(BA=I\). Then \[ P+BL=Bq,\qquad (I+AB)L=(I+AB)q. \] Thus its input span exposes \(\lambda=BL\) and the affine residuals \(K=(I+AB)L\). Conversely \(q=A(P+\lambda)+K\), so these spans coincide. For genuine independent affine-bottom blocks, the product weights satisfy the preceding observation. The new lemma can therefore use old affine \(K\)'s and old quadratics \(\lambda_c g_{c,j}\), with no truth-table expansion or separate product-variable definitions.

Full column rank is only a sufficient way to find exposed directions, not a requirement of the lemma. The actual compiler allows rank-deficient matrices: \(q=P_1+P_2\) exposes neither individual product plus an old affine polynomial when the product names are formally independent. This is the recorded span obstruction, not a new lower-bound example.

Even full column rank does not make the old quadratics an equivalent replacement for the original tuple. With \(A=I,L=0\), they are all zero while the parent tuple is \(P\). At an old point where a bottom input tuple is zero, its genuine product is one under every coefficient choice, so this parent's zero condition fails although every extracted quadratic vanishes. Over a Boolean-only old base every supplied certificate then places \(f\) already in \(\mathcal I_\kappa(\mathrm{BOOL})\), so it supplies no separated old target. Relative to an inconsistent old base, a surviving low-degree common certificate still cannot be assumed. This blocks applying the old quadratic source endpoint merely because quadratic consequences were extracted.

Setting every \(\lambda_c=1\) recovers the companion identity behind the complement-exposed endpoint, with a possibly looser charged certificate convention. The full-base relative-learning lemma already owns the weighted-specialization principle. The additional content here is the concrete affine-valued product exposure and its original parent-degree absorption; no independent new learning principle is claimed.
