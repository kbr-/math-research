Pins and ranks

Setting as in the edge-supported error ratio: one-hot coordinates over \(F\supseteq\mathbb F_4\), \(J_D\) the charged character pseudoideal of \((G,c)\) in its window, \(h(k)\) the number of cut-code classes met by the support-\(k\) ball, \(B_m(k)=\sum_{j\le k}2^j\binom mj\), and for a tuple \(E=(E_1,\ldots,E_r)\) of degree at most \(e_{\max}\), \(e_{\max}+K\le D\), \(c_E(k)\) the rank of \(f\mapsto(R_D(E_if))_i\) on \(V_k\). The record's lem:weighted-restriction-transport: for an edge set \(F\) with \(G'=G-F\) of supplied expansion \(\varepsilon'>0\), adjusted charge \(c'\), and \(\max(4,2g')\le D'\le D\), \(D'<\varepsilon'n/8\), "every pin assignment rho sends J_D intersect ordinary-degree<=D' into canonical residual J'_{D'}".

Lemma (pin rank transfer). (a) For \(F\), \(\rho\), \(D'\) as in lem:weighted-restriction-transport, and a tuple with \(e_{\max}+K\le D'\), \(c_E(K)\ge c'_{E|_\rho}(K)\), the rank of \(f\mapsto(R'_{D'}(E_i|_\rho f))_i\) on the degree-\(K\) space \(V'_K\) of \(G'\)'s one-hot coordinates. (b) For every edge set \(T\), the conjunction count of thm:conjunction-quotient-rank, \(q_T(K)\), the number of cosets of the cut space plus \(\mathbb F_3^T\) met by the support-\(K\) ball, equals \(h_{G-T}(K)\), the number of cut-code classes of \(G-T\) met by its support-\(K\) ball.

Proof. (a) \(V'_K\subseteq V_K\), the one-hot coordinates of \(G'\) being those of \(G\) off \(F\), in the binary one-hot encoding lem:weighted-restriction-transport is stated for. The rank is that of \(f\mapsto(E_if)_i\) modulo \(J_D\), since \(R_D\)'s kernel on degree at most \(D\) is \(J_D\) there. If \(f\in V'_K\) has \(E_if\in J_D\) for every \(i\), then \(E_if\) has degree at most \(e_{\max}+K\le D'\), so \(\rho(E_if)\in J'_{D'}\); and \(\rho\), a substitution homomorphism fixing the variables off \(F\), gives \(\rho(E_if)=E_i|_\rho f\). So the kernel \(\ker\) of the original map meets \(V'_K\) inside the kernel \(\ker'\) of the pinned one, and \(c_E(K)=\dim V_K/\ker\ge\dim V'_K/(\ker\cap V'_K)\ge\dim V'_K/\ker'=c'_{E|_\rho}(K)\), the first step because \(V'_K/(\ker\cap V'_K)\) injects into \(V_K/\ker\). (b) The projection \(\pi:\mathbb F_3^{E(G)}\to\mathbb F_3^{E(G)\setminus T}\) has kernel \(\mathbb F_3^T\), so cosets of \(C+\mathbb F_3^T\), \(C\) the cut space of \(G\), correspond to cosets of \(\pi(C)\); \(\pi\) sends the cut \(\delta_G(S)\) to \(\delta_{G-T}(S)\), so \(\pi(C)\) is the cut space of \(G-T\); and \(\pi\) maps the support-\(K\) ball of \(G\) onto that of \(G-T\), a vector of support at most \(K\) off \(T\) extending by zero. \(\square\)

Corollary (pin transfer cost). For \(F\), \(\rho\), \(D'\) as in (a) with \(|F|=\tau\), any bound on \(c(K-d)/c(K)\) obtained from \(c(K-d)\le h(K-d)\) and (a), that is, \(c(K-d)/c(K)\le h(K-d)/c'_{E|_\rho}(K)\), is at least \[\frac{h(K-d)}{h(K)}\cdot\frac{h(K)}{h_{G-F}(K)}\ \ge\ \frac{h(K-d)}{h(K)}\cdot\lambda^{-a(F)},\qquad\lambda=\frac{K+2m}{3K+2m},\quad a(F)=\tau-\dim\mathcal B_F,\] with \(a(F)\ge(1-1/\varepsilon)\tau\) when \(\varepsilon>1\) and \(\tau\le D\). On the power-growing charged family (\(n\Delta/8\le m\le n\Delta\), expansion at least \(\Delta/4\), \(D=\lfloor n\Delta/64\rfloor\), \(K=\lfloor D/8\rfloor\), \(\log N=\Theta(\Delta)\)), \(\lambda^{-1}=1+2K/(K+2m)\ge1+(1-o(1))/513\), so for \(\tau\le D\) the factor over canonical is at least \(e^{c\tau}\) with an absolute \(c>0\), superpolynomial in \(N\) whenever \(\tau/\Delta\to\infty\).

Proof. The pinned rank is at most \(\dim V'_K/(J'_{D'}\cap V'_K)\): an \(f\in J'_{D'}\cap V'_K\) has \(E_i|_\rho f\in J'_{D'}\), the pseudoideal absorbing multiplication within degree \(D'\) as in cor:edge-supported-error-ratio (ii); and that dimension is \(h_{G-F}(K)\), the record's identification of the quotient with the cut-code classes met by the support-\(K\) ball (lem:cut-code-classes), in \(G-F\)'s window. So \(h(K-d)/c'\ge(h(K-d)/h(K))\cdot h(K)/h_{G-F}(K)\). By (b), \(h_{G-F}(K)=q_F(K)\), and thm:punctured-quotient-contraction gives "\(q_T(K)/h(K)\le\lambda^{a(T)}\)", with "\(a(T)\ge(1-1/\varepsilon)|T|\)" when "\(\varepsilon>1\) and \(|T|\le D\)". On the family, \(K\ge n\Delta/512-2\) and \(m\le n\Delta\), and \(N^{A}=e^{\Theta(A\Delta)}\), while \(\varepsilon\ge\Delta/4>2\) gives \(a(F)\ge\tau/2\). \(\square\)

Hypotheses, each with its step. (a) uses only lem:weighted-restriction-transport's inclusion of \(J_D\) in degree at most \(D'\) into \(J'_{D'}\), and \(e_{\max}+K\le D'\) so that every \(E_if\) lies in that range; it holds for every tuple and every pin that lemma admits, its expansion hypothesis being the lemma's, not a closure condition. lem:weighted-restriction-transport also assumes nonzero total ternary charge, a standing hypothesis of (a). (b) uses nothing about \(T\), the window or the charge: it is a statement about cosets. The corollary uses \(c(K-d)\le h(K-d)\) (absorption, as in cor:edge-supported-error-ratio (ii)), the absorption and lem:cut-code-classes in \(G-F\)'s window for \(c'\le h_{G-F}(K)\), and thm:punctured-quotient-contraction with (b); its first inequality needs nothing else, and the expansion and \(\tau\le D\) serve only the bound on \(a(F)\), the family's constants only its last sentence.

Degenerate cases. \(F=\emptyset\): (a) is equality and the corollary's factor is \(h(K)/h(K)=1\). \(T=\emptyset\) in (b): \(q_\emptyset=h\). A pin assignment violating a residual charge at a vertex all of whose edges are pinned makes \(J'_{D'}\) contain one; lem:weighted-restriction-transport's expansion hypothesis then fails for that vertex's isolation, and the lemma is not invoked. A pin with \(\tau>D\) still has the factor \(\lambda^{-a(F)}\), without the bound on \(a(F)\).

Nonvacuity. On the power-growing family take \(F\) a matching of \(\tau=\lceil\Delta\log n\rceil\) non-bridge edges, incidence one at every vertex, \(D'=D\), \(K=\lfloor D/8\rfloor\), any tuple of degree at most \(7D/8\). The transport lemma's maximum-incidence case gives \(G-F\) expansion \(\varepsilon'\ge\Delta/4-1\); its window needs \(D<\varepsilon'n/8\), and \(n\Delta/64<(\Delta/4-1)n/8\) holds for \(\Delta>8\); the total charge is nonzero, since a charged Tseitin instance on a connected graph is satisfiable exactly when its total charge is zero and the family is unsatisfiable. Then (a) applies, \(\tau\le D\), and the factor is at least \(\lambda^{-\tau/2}\ge e^{c\Delta\log n}\), superpolynomial since \(\log N=\Theta(\Delta)\). Plainest bound. On this pin the bare count gives \(h_{G-F}(K)\le B_{m-\tau}(K)\le B_m(K)\), so the pinned rank can never exceed the original quotient's dimension; it fixes no loss, and the instance tests the contraction, which the bare count does not give. This lies in the pin ledger.

Baseline. The plainest bound for a component outside \(J_D\) with \(s\) nonzero quotient coordinates is lem:sparse-weight-survival's ratio at most \(s\,h(K-d)/h(K)\); for a component that is a function of an edge set with a low-incidence closure inside the window, cor:star-closure-ratio gives a factor \(2r\,e^{9.5\tau d/(n\Delta)}\), with \(\tau d=O(n\Delta^2)\) allowed. The two quantities are of different kinds: the star closure's factor is an upper bound proved for the components it reaches, the pin factor a lower bound on what the pin bookkeeping can prove, about \(e^{\tau K/m}\), with \(K\) in place of \(d\). Where both apply, a low-incidence closure existing inside the window, the closure's upper bound is the smaller, because it bounds both levels in the reduced space (lem:local-tuple-ratio's upper bound \(c(k)\le\sum_j\Delta r_jB_{m-\tau}(k-j)\)), while a pin bounds the level \(K-d\) in the original quotient only.
