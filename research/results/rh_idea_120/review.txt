Suggestions (unverified)
1. Necessity fails in the model without a cancellation hypothesis. The trapezoid kernel is e^(z(a-l)) times a 
transform of a nonnegative weight on [0, b-a+2l], so the comb applies: window U = 2 log tau, r = 
1/(2(1-beta_0)) - 1, d(2+r) <= 2 pi, Lam = (2 beta_0 - 1) log tau. For beta_0 near 1/2 this needs about 
(4/pi)(2 beta_0 - 1) log tau points within O(1) of tau, inside the count; W_tau >= tau^(2 beta_0 - 1), yet 
the zeros' part stays below y/tau on the whole range; beyond it y^beta_0 < y/tau.
2. W is an l^1 quantity; generic phases give square-root cancellation. A necessary condition, if any, is l^2: 
mean square over log y of the zeros' part, by Montgomery-Vaughan for points spaced at least 2 pi over the 
range; zeta's zeros are not so spaced, which is where it breaks.
