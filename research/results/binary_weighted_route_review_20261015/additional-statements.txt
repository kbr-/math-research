conj:general-column-claim
  Statement: Every column reader (classes I x {x}, I in the pinned rows, single-row light tails, role-separated) of a source of size n^c has fewer than N/48 heavy rounds on every path of the both-endpoints tree before a 1-leaf except with probability O(n^(-c-1))
  Assessment: Conjecture; proved for two-tier or activation-bounded labels; three reader families (private labels, shared labels, one hosting row) fail to refute it: rounds need activated edge mass of order n^2/N or a rank coincidence, and that mass forces a satisfied term; proved 2026-09-18 for row-spread readers (each light row hosts fewer than n^(1/3) labels); the remaining case is a row hosting many labels with many patterns ([theorem](https://kbr.is-a.dev/math-research/#row-spread-theorem), [remaining case](https://kbr.is-a.dev/math-research/#row-spread-scope)); settled 2026-09-18 for all widths w <= l/(2 log_2(2l+1)) and for any reader with at most H_max patterns per light row ([theorem](https://kbr.is-a.dev/math-research/#pattern-bounded-theorem)); open only for wide patterns crowded on one light row; for tails on any rows it holds at every size n^c, c < 2-eps, by `cor:multirow-size-switching`, and above that size under the light-mass hypothesis of `thm:multirow-mass-switching`, and with at most n^(1-eps)/N^4 light rows at every size by `thm:few-light-rows-switching`, and for sizes n^c, c < 3-2eps, with tails on boundedly many rows by `cor:crowded-size-switching`
  Status: conjecture

cor:presentation-polynomial-size-bound
  Statement: For the fixed bounded-degree charged-mod3 Tseitin CNF family over F2, fix formula depth in the recorded binary Frege presentation and a constant ell>=2 bounding coherent source dependency levels. For every 0<rho<1/(2^(ell-1)-1), proofs of the flat CNF-negation tautology require more than n^(1+rho) total formula-node occurrences for large n. In old bit-encoding length this is exponent1+rho-o(1). Choose fixed h>=clause width, use constant compiled PC ceiling, linear block inventory and fixed-level exclusion with a slightly larger rho and a small positive beta.
  Assessment: Working polynomial SYMBOL-size lower bound for the audited presentation. Not a proof-line count bound, arbitrary-presentation exponent, superpolynomial bound, or claim of independent literature novelty. The full superpolynomial goal remains open.
  Status: working_proof
