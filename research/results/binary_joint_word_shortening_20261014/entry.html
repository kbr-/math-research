Joint word shortening

Statement. Work over \(\mathbb F_2\) with a degree-\(D\) pseudoideal \(J\) containing Booleanity, and \(Q=V_D/J\). Fix \(M\ge1\) tuples \(g_{a,i}\) of degree at most \(\delta\ge1\), a weight \(w\) of degree at most \(b\), binary fixed prefixes \(P_a\) of degree at most \(p_0\), and an ordinary common domain \(U\subseteq V_K\), \(K\ge0\). Let \(V\) be a finite-dimensional binary seed space with basis \(e_1,\ldots,e_d\), and surjective linear maps \(\pi_a:V\to G_a=\operatorname{span}(g_{a,i})\). Put \(q_{a,l}=\pi_ae_l\). Each has a fixed constant representation in its parent's original input positions. Assume

\[b+p_0+K+(h+2)\delta\le D,\qquad h\ge1.\]

For \(0\le t\le h\), define the fixed space

\[\mathcal Z_t=\operatorname{span}\left\{\left([wP_ag_{a,i}f\prod_{\nu=1}^{r}q_{a,l_\nu}]\right)_{a,i}:f\in U,\ 0\le r\le t,\ l_\nu\in\{1,\ldots,d\}\right\}.\]

The empty word is included. The same multiplier \(f\) and the same word indices are used across all parents. Write \(Y=\mathcal Z_0\). Draw independent uniform seeds \(R_1,\ldots,R_h\in V\), use \(q_{a,j}=\pi_aR_j\), and set \(R_a=\prod_{j=1}^h(1+q_{a,j})\). Then

\[\mathbb E\operatorname{rank}\left(f\in U\mapsto([wP_aR_ag_{a,i}f])_{a,i}\right)\le2^{-h}\dim\mathcal Z_h.\]

If \(H=\dim[wU]>0\) and \(2^{-h}\dim\mathcal Z_h<H\), one coherent family of these legal constant rows and an \(f\in U\) have \(wf\notin J\) and every selected weighted companion in \(J\). Earlier obligations encoded by \(U\) persist. All original source and replay charges remain separate.

Shared row on a fixed space. Choose constants \(\beta_{a,i,l}\) with \(q_{a,l}=\sum_i\beta_{a,i,l}g_{a,i}\). For a seed with coordinates \(\xi_l\), define the constant-coordinate map

\[S_\xi:Q^{\sum_a r_a}\to Q^M,\qquad (S_\xi z)_a=\sum_i\left(\sum_l\xi_l\beta_{a,i,l}\right)z_{a,i}.\]

The \(F_a\) can differ after taking joint words; they do not replace the original domain from which the final multiplier is selected. Two constant representations of the same \(q_{a,l}\) induce the same map on this multiplication-image space, because their input combination is literally zero.

\[q_a(\lambda)=\sum_k\alpha_{a,k}(\lambda)v_{a,k},\]

where the \(\alpha_{a,k}\) are linearly independent binary linear forms in the \(\lambda\)'s. Extend them to a basis of all seed-coordinate linear forms; they are algebraically independent over \(\mathbb F_2\). Relations between forms belonging to different parents do not alter this assertion for an individual parent.

Suppose \(S_\lambda z=0\), and fix rational-coefficient representatives \(F_a\) of the indicated degrees. With \(A_a=wP_a\), this says \(A_aL_{a,0}F_a\in J_E\), where \(L_{a,j}=\sum_k\alpha_{a,k}^{2^j}v_{a,k}\). Multiply a completed relation by \(L_{a,j}\) and subtract \(A_aF_a(L_{a,j}^2+L_{a,j+1})\). Binary input Boolean certificates through \(2\delta\) give the next relation at degree at most \(b+p_0+K+(h+2)\delta\). Scalar extension retains the paid pseudoideal closure; the same \(F_a\) is used throughout, without applying Frobenius to it. The Moore determinant on the independent \(\alpha_{a,k}\) is nonzero, with distinct exponent monomials. Inversion recovers all \(A_av_{a,k}F_a\in J_E\), hence every \(z_{a,i}=0\). The same multiplicity rank-specialization bound gives \(\mathbb E_\xi\operatorname{rank}(S_\xi|L)\ge\dim L/2\), because the matrix pencil is linear in the common seed coordinates. Therefore \(\mathbb E\dim(L\cap\ker S_\xi)\le\dim L/2\). Joint row escape

Statement. For a tuple space \(Y\subseteq Q^I\) with representatives through degree \(d_0\), let componentwise multiplication \(G_R\) use a binary polynomial \(q_a\) of degree at most \(\delta\) in every coordinate of parent \(a\). Assume \(d_0+2\delta\le D\). Put \(c=\dim Y\), \(r_R=\dim G_RY\), and \(e_R=\dim((Y+G_RY)/Y)\). Then the actual updated tuple space \(Y_R=(I+G_R)Y\) has

\[\dim Y_R=c-r_R+e_R.\]

Proof. The map is well defined on the paid space \(Y+G_RY\): any zero relation represented through \(d_0+\delta\) can be multiplied by the corresponding \(q_a\) within \(D\). Booleanity gives \(G_R^2y=G_Ry\) for \(y\in Y\) through \(d_0+2\delta\), so \(G_R\) fixes \(G_RY\). A vector \(y\in Y\) lies in the kernel of \(I+G_R\) exactly when \(y=G_Ry\), equivalently when \(y\in Y\cap G_RY\): for the reverse implication, \(y=G_Rz\) is fixed by \(G_R\). Thus rank-nullity gives \(\dim Y_R=c-\dim(Y\cap G_RY)=c-r_R+e_R\). This is the primal proof of the recorded lem:dual-escape-identity, applied componentwise with its full degree charge; no adjoint or stronger pairing window is used.
