General statement. lem:surviving-branch-removal uses one surviving member of a finite polynomial decomposition, with reference input degree controlling the contraction rate and actual input degree controlling the transfer buffer. lem:weighted-chain-cells supplies such a decomposition from weighted nested idempotents. thm:chain-source-exclusion excludes degree-\(v^{1/3}\) PC refutations of the stated complete two-level sources on any old family with a linear variable-relative degree barrier, when the bottom input spaces have \(o(v/\log v)\) chains. What the direct learning attempt leaves unpaid
The existing nested theorem expresses a current input \(f\) in each later tuple, modulo a supplied target-annihilator space. In a nonnested tuple the residual \(r_b\) instead leaves a weighted companion proportional to \(wfg_{b,i}r_b\). Charging only the number of blocks is unnecessary in a chain, but the old proof supplies no bound for those residuals on arbitrary overlaps. The raw spread calculation still calibrates that criterion only; its complete normalization already handles the displayed source. The stronger relative-membership learning interface was also recovered; it needs bounded certificates in every input ideal and does not provide them automatically.
The alternative below does not force the parent spaces to become nested. It uses weighted bottom relations to make their nonlinear coordinates constant on one branch. Unlike coefficient interpolation on all branches, it selects the branch before the legal constant coefficients. Its common decomposition requirement is stronger than having separate partitions for each block; its cost and old-pseudoideal setting are different.
Remove errors on one surviving branch
Lemma (surviving branch removal). Work over \(\mathbb F_2\). Let \(J\subseteq\mathbb F_2[x_1,\ldots,x_v]_{\le D}\) be a degree-\(D\) pseudoideal containing the Boolean equations. Fix \(M\ge1\) finite input tuples \(g_{\nu,i}\) of degree at most \(\delta\). Suppose finitely many branch weights \(w_a\), of degree at most \(b\), satisfy \(\sum_a w_a\notin J\), and for every branch there are reference tuples \(q^a_{\nu,i}\), of degree at most \(r\), with \[ w_a(g_{\nu,i}-q^a_{\nu,i})\in J\quad\text{for all }a,\nu,i. \] Assume integers \(1\le r\le\delta\), \(r\le K\le v\), \(t\ge1\), and \[ b+K+(t+1)\delta\le D,\qquad \eta=\tfrac12\frac{N_v(K-r)}{N_v(K)},\qquad M(1-\eta)^t<1. \] There is one branch \(a\), one binary coefficient matrix with \(t\) rows for each original tuple, and a multilinear \(f\) of degree at most \(K\), such that \(w_af\notin J\) and \[ w_af\,g_{\nu,i}\prod_{u=1}^t\left(1-\sum_j c_{\nu,u,j}g_{\nu,j}\right)\in J \quad\text{for every }\nu,i. \] If source accuracies exceed \(t\), the other rows may be zero, retaining their original costs. Neither idempotence nor orthogonality of the branch weights is required.
Proof. Some \(w_a\notin J\), since otherwise their sum would belong to \(J\). Fix such a branch. Apply the weighted approximation proof to its degree-\(r\) reference tuples and weight \(w_a\). That proof applies here to a Boolean pseudoideal: the recorded conditional contraction on the full domains \(V_k\), together with their squarefree downward-closed kernel bases, gives \[ c_{P(1-\ell)}(K)\le(1-\eta)c_P(K),\qquad c_P(k)=\operatorname{rank}\bigl(F\mapsto([w_aPq^a_{\nu,i}F])_i\bigr). \] All reference inputs have their degree-\(2r\) Boolean certificates. Before row \(u\), the required ceiling is \(b+(u-1)r+K+2r\le D\). Initially each component rank is at most \(H=\operatorname{rank}(F\mapsto[w_aF],F\in V_K)>0\), by bounded absorption. The final maps all kill the kernel of that same weight map, and their summed ranks are at most \(MH(1-\eta)^t<H\). Hence one common multilinear \(f\) survives and kills all reference companions. This argument uses a full multiplier degree space; it does not substitute the invalid ambient bound for an arbitrary conditioned subspace.
Use the selected reference coefficients in the same indexed positions of the actual tuples. Write \(G_u=\sum_jc_{u,j}g_j\), \(Q_u=\sum_jc_{u,j}q_j\), \(P_g=\prod_u(1-G_u)\), and \(P_q=\prod_u(1-Q_u)\). Linearity gives \(w_a(G_u-Q_u)\in J\). The identity \[ g_iP_g-q_iP_q=(g_i-q_i)P_g+q_i(P_g-P_q) \] and product telescoping express its weighted image as supplied comparison relations multiplied by at most \(t\) input factors. After multiplying by \(f\), every such polynomial has degree at most \(b+K+(t+1)\delta\). Bounded absorption therefore gives \(w_af(g_iP_g-q_iP_q)\in J\). The reference companion vanishes, proving the actual one does as well. No input position or coefficient domain has been changed. \(\square\)
The branch count does not enter the degree or contraction bound: only one branch is used. It may have a small multiplication image; its positive dimension suffices because the rank count is relative to that image. This is not one coefficient choice working on every branch. The high degree \(\delta\) remains in the comparison buffer, but the rate uses \(r\).
Weighted chains supply a common decomposition
Lemma (weighted chain cells). Let \(w\notin J\), \(\deg w\le b_0\), and let \(q\ge1\) finite nonempty chains of polynomials \(z_{a,1},\ldots,z_{a,m_a}\) have degrees at most \(s\ge1\). Suppose \[ w(z_{a,i}z_{a,j}-z_{a,j})\in J\qquad(1\le i\le j\le m_a) \] and \(b_0+(q+1)s\le D\). Put \(z_{a,0}=1,z_{a,m_a+1}=0\), and for \(0\le k\le m_a\) set \(\chi_{a,k}=z_{a,k}-z_{a,k+1}\). For a multi-index \(\mathbf k\), set \(w_{\mathbf k}=w\prod_a\chi_{a,k_a}\). Then \[ \sum_{\mathbf k}w_{\mathbf k}=w,\qquad \deg w_{\mathbf k}\le b_0+qs,\qquad w_{\mathbf k}(z_{a,j}-\mathbf1_{j\le k_a})\in J. \] Consequently every family \(g_{\nu,i}=A_{\nu,i}(x)+\sum_{a,j}\lambda_{\nu,i,a,j}z_{a,j}\), with old parts of degree at most \(r\), has reference degree at most \(r\) on these same branches.
Proof. Each chain's differences sum literally to one, proving the sum and degree assertions. For one chain, subtract the two relations for \(z_kz_j\) and \(z_{k+1}z_j\). If \(j\le k\), their weighted difference is \(w(z_k-z_{k+1})\); if \(j>k\), it is zero. The diagonal case uses the stated idempotence relation; endpoint constants give the same identities directly. Each comparison has degree at most \(b_0+2s\). Multiplying by the other \(q-1\) differences fits \(b_0+(q+1)s\), establishing the displayed memberships. For a parent input, replace each \(z_{a,j}\) by the indicated constant and take the same linear combination. No global evaluation model of \(J\) is used, and chains need not be independent of one another. \(\square\)
When these \(z\)'s are actual selected bottom ENS products, the weighted nesting can be proved from bottom companion annihilation. Suppose bottom input spaces in a chain satisfy \(V_i\subseteq V_j\) for \(i<j\), all selected products have degree at most \(s\), their supplied prefix expansions have input-weighted degree at most \(s\), and \(wz_jg_{j,k}\in J\). Then \(wz_jg_{i,k}\in J\) by the exact constant span representation. The ordinary prefix identity \(1-z_i=\sum_k U_{i,k}g_{i,k}\), with its input-weighted degree at most \(s\), yields \(wz_j(1-z_i)\in J\) through \(b_0+2s\). Booleanity supplies the diagonal case through the same ceiling. Thus the chain relations are consequences of actual complete bottom blocks; they are not imposed on unrelated independent selectors.
A complete two-level source consequence
Theorem (chain source exclusion). Fix constants \(0<\gamma<1\), \(A>0\), integers \(r_0,r_1\ge1\), and \(0<\epsilon<1/6\). Let \(\Gamma_v\) be an old system over \(\mathbb F_2\) in \(v\) variables, containing all Boolean axioms, with \[ 1\notin\mathcal C_D(\Gamma_v),\qquad D=\lfloor\gamma v\rfloor. \] Take a complete two-level product-only binary ENS source, with at most \(v^A\) bottom blocks and at most \(v^A\) parent blocks, all of accuracy \(h=\lfloor v^\epsilon\rfloor\). Bottom inputs are old polynomials of degree at most \(r_0\). Their exact \(\mathbb F_2\)-linear input spaces admit a cover by \(q=q(v)\) nested chains with \(q=o(v/\log v)\). Every parent input is a degree-at-most-\(r_1\) old polynomial plus an arbitrary constant binary linear combination of the genuine bottom products. Coefficients of earlier blocks occur only through those products, and shared products refer to the same block. Then, for sufficiently large \(v\), the full source has no PC refutation through \(B=\lfloor v^{1/3}\rfloor\).
All tuples are finite; no restriction on parent arity, overlap or input-space nesting is added. The count assumption is on bottom chains, not parent blocks or the number of bottom values. Products are the actual polynomials in their old variables and coefficient variables; an additional named-value encoding would require its own definitions and ledger. Empty levels reduce to the corresponding one-level argument, so the proof treats both level inventories as positive.
Bottom selection. Set \(J=\mathcal C_D(\Gamma_v)\) and \(K=\lfloor\gamma v/4\rfloor\). For fixed \(r\), \(\tfrac12N_v(K-r)/N_v(K)\) is bounded below by a positive constant depending only on \(\gamma,r\). For example, bound \(N_v(K)\) above by \(\binom vK/(1-K/(v-K+1))\) and \(N_v(K-r)\) below by \(\binom v{K-r}\); their ratio has a positive limit lower bound since \(K/v\to\gamma/4<1/2\). Choose \(t_0=O_{A,r_0,\gamma}(\log v)\) so the bottom rank sum is below half its domain dimension. The weighted approximation lemma with initial weight one supplies one constant assignment to \(t_0\) rows per bottom block, zero padding to \(h\), and a multilinear \(w\notin J\), \(\deg w\le K\), annihilating all specialized bottom companions. Its ceiling \(K+(t_0+1)r_0\le D\) holds eventually, and \(t_0\le h\). Let \(s=r_0t_0\); every specialized bottom product has degree at most \(s=O(\log v)\).
Chain selection and parent removal. Order the bottom blocks in their supplied chains. If a block appears in more than one chain, discard its redundant appearances by assigning it to one chain; the remaining subsequences remain nested. The preceding prefix argument gives all weighted chain relations through \(K+2s\). The chain-cell lemma gives a finite common decomposition of \(w\), each branch of degree at most \(K+qs\), on which every parent input agrees, after weighting, with its old part plus a binary constant. Thus the reference degree is \(r_1\), even when parent spans have large rank or form an antichain.
Choose \(t_1=O_{A,r_1,\gamma}(\log v)\) so the parent rank sum is below half, and put \(\delta=\max(s,r_1)\). Apply surviving branch removal with multiplier cutoff \(K\). Its total comparison ceiling is \[ 2K+qs+(t_1+1)\delta \le \gamma v/2+o(v)<D, \] because \(qs=o(v)\) and \(t_1\delta=O((\log v)^2)\). It chooses one branch, one constant assignment to every parent block (with zero padding to \(h\)), and a final multiplier \(f\) of degree at most \(K\). The combined weight \(W=w_{\mathbf k}f\notin J\) kills every parent companion. It also preserves every bottom companion membership: multiplying its previous certificate by the chosen cell and \(f\) costs at most \(2K+qs+s+r_0\), which fits the same reserve. Earlier coefficient choices never change.
Replay with original source degrees. Suppose a degree-\(B\) source refutation existed. Apply the single global constant coefficient assignment to the whole proof. This never raises degree and makes every coefficient-domain axiom zero. Old axioms and inferences can be multiplied by \(W\), because \[ \deg W+B\le2K+qs+B<D. \] The bottom and parent companion memberships were just established. For an old-variable multiplication use bounded absorption; multiplication by an assigned coefficient is scalar multiplication. Induction puts the final polynomial \(W\) in \(J\), a contradiction.
The original bottom product weight is at most \(h(r_0+1)\). The original parent input ceiling is \(\max(r_1,h(r_0+1))\), so parent companions have original degree \(O_{r_0,r_1}(h^2)\). Since \(2\epsilon<1/3\), these degrees are below \(B\) eventually. The replay uses the original proof ceiling \(B\), not a degree reset after padding. For a fixed old family and fixed accuracy exponent, these inequalities hold for every fixed inventory exponent \(A\); the row-count constants and sufficiently-large-\(v\) threshold may depend on \(A\). The bound \(\epsilon<1/6\) ensures that all original companion degrees fit below \(B\); the weighted replay itself only needs its displayed ceiling. \(\square\)
Scope and next test
The theorem uses the actual parent grammar rather than requiring a bounded number of product summands. It is not subsumed merely by saying that parents have few input directions: their directions may be numerous, while the common chain cells are cheap after weighting. Conversely a polynomial-size source is not known to have a small bottom chain cover. The old small-internal-inventory theorem works on its unary-PHP family with a different count and degree normalization; no comparison across their variable parameters is asserted. The earlier complete partition transfer permits separate partitions but pays coefficient-interpolation degree; this result uses one common weighted decomposition and one chosen branch. Neither theorem supplies the other's missing hypotheses automatically.
