Necessary endpoint: a polynomial-size UNSAT Boolean family on all actual v variables, independent of polynomial proof exponent, ordinary PC over F2 with 1 absent through D=floor(v/2-s), s=o(v/(log v)^(ell-2)) for each fixed relevant ell. A proper kernel containing active axioms and closed under legal degree-D multiplications suffices. A normalized functional annihilating only bounded original-input multiples is NS, not dynamic PC. One surviving unit suffices; no separately large image or full global algebra is needed. Growing input width needs its own complete transfer ledger.

Available statements:
1. A width-four linear-size minimal UNSAT family has PC degree >=c v for fixed c>0 over every field. Essential scopes, >=2 occurrences, branching fraction >=2/5, no common Horn polarity, and joint binary local-affine consistency coexist. This is not a near-half lower bound.
2. For compact four-colouring on two bits per vertex, ordinary binary PC degree <=v/2+1. Independent-star elimination yields degree-four boundary determinants; matching centres add degree-six conflicts, with forward replay max(D,6), no reverse/truncated-closure equality. Minimal non-four-colourable Kneser subgraphs yield unbounded width-four sources with growing odd girth, but no PC lower bound.
3. Fixed odd-modulus, fixed-row-width canonical field-consistent systems have paid character bounds. Near-half hardness requires row-code covering radius, smallest Boolean-empty projection and puncture separation >=v/2-o(v). An indecomposable F3 code family meets rank/radius/projection screens within O(sqrt v) and every accepted structural condition, but a small hub puncture yields O(sqrt v) PC degree. Exact maximal-radius ternary codes and graphical leader charts with sublinear radius deficit are excluded; wider-column near-deficit codes remain open.
4. Independent Bernoulli-half random allowed tables on k=ceil(2log2 v) scopes, m=v+k, have binary local immunity through floor(k/2-sqrt k), and random scopes retain a linear number of variables in every normalized minimal core. The actual upper bound on original instances is v/2+3v/sqrt k+O(1), above target; no reverse core transfer. Whole-row monomial-support absorption fails near half. Recorded immunity and respectful-expansion certificates top out at quarter and third fractions in their ranges, not actual PC upper bounds.
5. A growing-modulus source has v=l2^l,k=8l,m=5v/k, prime v²<q<2v², five random partitions and uniform supported coefficients/targets. With high probability: full Fq row rank and consistency, Boolean emptiness, pins through floor(k/64), full binary affine hull in every row. Every normalized minimal core retains n>=e^-512 v/256, essential width-O(log n) rows, 2..5 occurrences, no common Horn polarity, length O(n^9). Full affine hull persists under projection, giving no affine local vanishing functions; neither higher polynomial immunity nor any near-half PC lower bound follows. Terminal proofs replay original through max(k,D), only one direction.
6. General method warning: for local ideals J_a in the common Boolean algebra, K_d=sum(J_a cap B_<=d). At cutoff D, the maps (K_D cap B_<=D-1)/K_D-1 -> B_<=D/K_D induced by multiplying variables vanish iff K_D is closed under legal multiplications. Unit survival is separate. This is a test, not a construction. Nonzero falls can have zero next action; free spectator padding can turn a prior fall into a later defect. Even all proper jointly satisfiable members can miss a legal multiplication.
