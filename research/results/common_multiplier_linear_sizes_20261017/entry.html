General statement. lem:saving-monotonicity: over any field, enlarging the kernels of a family of error maps never raises its saving, so modulo a closure \(C\) containing a subspace \(H\) the saving of errors is at most the saving of the spaces \(\operatorname{Ann}_H(E_i')\cap V_K\), for every choice of representatives. ex:top-degree-linear-killer refutes conj:linear-kernel-transfer at three holes. conj:representative-sum-transfer: over \(\mathbb F_2\), below half the holes, a linear error's kernel modulo \(C\) is the sum over its linear representatives of their domain kernels, plus \(C\cap V_K\). check:top-degree-partition-kernels gives the excesses and the sum equality at the cases computed.

A refutation at three holes

Setting. The functional pigeonhole system (Booleanity, hole and functionality axioms, pigeon axioms \(Q_i=1-\sum_jx_{ij}\), multilinear ring) with four pigeons and three holes, at \(D=2=\lceil3/2\rceil\) and \(K=1\); \(C\) is the degree-\(2\) closure of all axioms, \(H\) that of the hole and functionality axioms, spanned by the non-matching monomials, and \(C\cap V_1=C_1=\operatorname{span}\{Q_i\}\) by lem:closure-degree-locality (\(2\le\lceil3/2\rceil\)).

Refutation (top-degree linear killer). Over any field, \((1-x_{12}-x_{22})(x_{10}-x_{21}+c\,x_{22})\equiv x_{10}Q_2-x_{21}Q_1\) modulo \(H\) for every scalar \(c\). Over \(\mathbb F_2\), with \(E=x_{10}+x_{21}+x_{22}\) and \(u=1+x_{12}+x_{22}\), this gives \(u\in\operatorname{Ann}_C(E)\); the kernel \(\operatorname{Ann}_C(E)\cap V_1\) has dimension \(7\), while the largest \(\operatorname{Ann}_H(E')\cap V_1+C\cap V_1\) over the \(16\) linear representatives \(E'\equiv E\) is \(6\), and \(u\) lies in none of them. So conj:linear-kernel-transfer, an \(\mathbb F_2\) statement whose evidence was one size with five holes, is false at three holes.

Proof. Modulo \(H\), a product of two variables in one row or one column is zero, and \(x^2=x\). The factor \(1-x_{12}-x_{22}\) kills \(x_{22}\): \(x_{22}-x_{12}x_{22}-x_{22}^2=0\). On the rest, \((1-x_{12}-x_{22})(x_{10}-x_{21})=x_{10}-x_{21}+x_{12}x_{21}-x_{10}x_{22}\), the products \(x_{12}x_{10}\) and \(x_{22}x_{21}\) being zero. Also \(x_{10}Q_2=x_{10}-x_{10}x_{21}-x_{10}x_{22}\) (dropping \(x_{10}x_{20}\)) and \(x_{21}Q_1=x_{21}-x_{21}x_{10}-x_{21}x_{12}\) (dropping \(x_{21}x_{11}\)), so \(x_{10}Q_2-x_{21}Q_1=x_{10}-x_{21}-x_{10}x_{22}+x_{12}x_{21}\), the terms \(x_{10}x_{21}\) cancelling. Both products have degree \(2\le D\) and lie in \(C\). The dimensions are computed over \(\mathbb F_2\): witness.json replays the error from linear_sizes.py's seed (there \(E+Q_3\) and \(u+Q_3\), which leave every kernel unchanged), checks \(uE\in C\) by an elimination independent of tools/pc_closure.py, finds \(u\) in none of the \(16\) representatives' domain sides, and gives \(C\)-side \(7\) by both routes against the best domain side \(6\). \(\square\)

Structural reason. The killer uses two pigeon axioms at once: the cross term \(x_{10}x_{21}\) occurs in both \(x_{10}Q_2\) and \(x_{21}Q_1\) and cancels between them, while a single representative \(E+q\) absorbs a combination of pigeon axioms into the error only once. So the reason applies to every transfer statement that compares with one representative at a time, such as conj:linear-kernel-transfer; it does not apply to single monomial errors, where lem:functional-killer-transfer proves transfer at every \(D\le\lceil N/2\rceil\), the top included, nor to monomial families with one admissible numbering (lem:admissible-numbering-transfer), nor to the sum over representatives, which the computed cases match.

Saving monotonicity

Lemma (saving monotonicity). Let \(V\) be a finite-dimensional space over any field and \(A_i\subseteq B_i\subseteq V\) subspaces, \(i=1,\dots,M\). Write \(s(A)=\sum_i\operatorname{codim}A_i-\operatorname{codim}\bigcap_iA_i\). Then \(s(B)\le s(A)\); for \(M=2\), \(s(A)=\operatorname{codim}(A_1+A_2)\). Consequently, let \(C\) be a degree-\(D\) PC closure, \(H\subseteq C\) any subspace, \(E_i\) errors of degree at most \(D-K\), and \(E_i'\) representatives with \(E_i-E_i'\in C\) of degree at most \(D-K\). Then the saving of the multiplication maps \(V_K\to V_D/C\) (summed ranks minus stacked rank) is at most \(s\) of the spaces \(\operatorname{Ann}_H(E_i')\cap V_K+C\cap V_K\), and so at most \(s\) of the spaces \(\operatorname{Ann}_H(E_i')\cap V_K\), where \(\operatorname{Ann}_H(E')=\{u:uE'\in H\}\).

Proof. It suffices to enlarge one space, \(A_1\) to \(B_1\), by \(\delta=\dim B_1-\dim A_1\). Then the first codimension falls by \(\delta\). Writing \(R=\bigcap_{i\ge2}A_i\), the map \((B_1\cap R)/(A_1\cap R)\to B_1/A_1\) is injective, so the intersection grows by at most \(\delta\) and its codimension falls by at most \(\delta\); hence \(s\) does not rise. For \(M=2\), \(\operatorname{codim}A_1+\operatorname{codim}A_2-\operatorname{codim}(A_1\cap A_2)=\operatorname{codim}(A_1+A_2)\). For the consequence, the rank of \(u\mapsto uE_i\) modulo \(C\) is \(\operatorname{codim}\operatorname{Ann}_C(E_i)\) in \(V_K\), and the stacked rank is \(\operatorname{codim}\bigcap_i\operatorname{Ann}_C(E_i)\). Then \(\operatorname{Ann}_H(E_i')\cap V_K\subseteq\operatorname{Ann}_H(E_i')\cap V_K+C\cap V_K\subseteq\operatorname{Ann}_C(E_i)\): for \(uE_i'\in H\subseteq C\), \(uE_i=uE_i'+u(E_i-E_i')\), the second term a closure member times \(u\) within degree \(D\); and \(cE_i\in C\) for \(c\in C\cap V_K\), the closure being closed under multiplication by variables up to degree \(D\). Two applications of the first part give both bounds. \(\square\)

Against the record. ex:support-saving-not-inherited says that the containment of the domain closure in the proper one “gives only that each rank and the stacked rank can drop”. The lemma corrects that sentence: the saving drops too, for every family and every choice of representatives. Its recorded figures obey it, \(11\le54\). lem:domain-model-stacked-rank's clause that domain savings “carry into Pang's proper quotient” was corrected by that example; the lemma gives the general inequality in the other direction. lem:factor-ideal-domination and lem:restriction-rank-overlap, which the resemblance check listed, are other statements on kernels and restriction ranks (antitone ranks and submodularity in ideals); neither is a more general version or a special case. The band review's Rank normalization stated an equality with a nonnegative correction under joint transfer; the inequality needs no transfer.

Hypotheses, each with its step.

  • \(A_i\subseteq B_i\): the injectivity step. The field and \(M\): any.
  • For the consequence, \(C\) a closure (closed under multiplication by variables within degree \(D\)), \(H\subseteq C\) any subspace, \(\deg E_i\le D-K\) and \(\deg(E_i-E_i')\le D-K\): the products \(uE_i'\), \(u(E_i-E_i')\) and \(cE_i\) stay in degree \(D\).

Degenerate cases. \(M=1\): \(s=0\) on both sides. \(A_i=B_i\): equality. \(B_i=V\): \(s(B)=0\le s(A)\).

The corrected conjecture

Conjecture (representative sum transfer). Over \(\mathbb F_2\), on the functional pigeonhole system with \(n+1\) pigeons and \(n\) holes, at \(D\le\lceil n/2\rceil\) and \(K+1\le D\), every single linear error \(E\) has \(\operatorname{Ann}_C(E)\cap V_K=\sum_{E'}\operatorname{Ann}_H(E')\cap V_K+C\cap V_K\), the sum over its linear representatives \(E'\equiv E\) modulo \(C\cap V_1\). The containment \(\supseteq\) always holds. Both sides are unchanged under \(E\mapsto E+q\), \(q\in C\cap V_1\), and \(E\equiv0\) gives \(V_K\) on both. Evidence: the equality wherever the single excess is \(0\) (trivially) and at the \(23\) errors with positive single excess on which the sum was computed, at four sizes. It refines conj:linear-kernel-transfer, which took the largest single representative, covered families of errors and is refuted; this one covers single errors only.

Plainest bound. The containment \(S(E)\subseteq\operatorname{Ann}_C(E)\) is the plainest bound; it gives one direction only. Own statements. \(H\) is the degree-\(D\) vanishing space of the partial matchings \(X\) in the multilinear ring: every non-matching monomial vanishes on \(X\); and if a multilinear \(f=\sum c_Tx_T\) of degree at most \(D\) vanishes on \(X\), then for each matching monomial \(m\), \(0=f(1_m)=\sum_{T\subseteq m}c_T\), the sum over matching \(T\) since non-matching ones vanish at \(1_m\), and \(X\) being downward closed, induction on \(|m|\) gives \(c_m=0\); the quotient \(U=V_K/(H\cap V_K)\) is the same in this presentation. With that, lem:saving-monotonicity and lem:domain-model-stacked-rank together answer the savings question for the pure \(H\)-kernels: the \(H\)-savings are union-of-supports counts and bound the savings modulo \(C\). They do not decide the conjecture, which is about kernels, nor whether the mixed spaces \(\operatorname{Ann}_H(E')+C\cap V_K\) have savings of another kind.
