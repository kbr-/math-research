## 1. Architectures, as different as I could make them

- **A. Killers that are not domain killers (pigeon-axiom killers).** Build common killers of degree at most \(K\) from proper-closure memberships that no evaluation model has. `ex:top-degree-linear-killer` is the one example on record: \(u=1+x_{12}+x_{22}\) kills \(E=x_{10}+x_{21}+x_{22}\) through the identity \(x_{10}Q_2-x_{21}Q_1\). The joint version is a parametrised family of such identities in which one \(u\) uses the \(Q_i\) to kill many errors at once. Each independent killer lowers the stacked rank by one. This avoids all three excluded mechanisms, but no family is on record (unverified).
- **B. Overlap of the dual row spaces (designs).** The saving equals \(\sum\dim R_i-\dim\sum R_i\), where \(R_i=\{\lambda(E_i\,\cdot):\lambda\text{ an old functional}\}\). Overlap is certified by intertwiners: \(E_i\equiv aE_j\) gives \(R_i\subseteq R_j\) when \(\deg a+K+e\le D\). More generally, a design transport \(T\) with \(\lambda E_i=(T\lambda)E_j\) on \(V_K\) does the same. If a few generators span the errors modulo \(C\), the stacked rank is at most the sum over the generators. This reaches across several generators, not one element as in factor ideal domination. It is close to "duplicating errors", so whether it is admitted is a reading question (unverified).
- **C. Symmetry (isotypic) counting.** If \(C\) is invariant under a group \(G\) (for pigeonhole, \(S_m\times S_n\)) and the errors form a \(G\)-orbit, then \(\sum R_i\) is the \(G\)-module generated by \(R_1\). Its dimension is at most \(\sum_\chi\dim\chi\cdot\min\bigl(m_\chi(U^*),\,\dim R_1\cdot\dim\chi\bigr)\). This beats \(h\) only when the orbit misses whole isotypic components of \(U^*\). It is structural and checkable, and none of the exclusions name it (unverified).
- **D. Gorenstein / inverse-system duality.** In an Artinian Gorenstein quotient, \(\dim A/\operatorname{ann}(I)=\dim I\). The stacked rank then becomes the Hilbert function of \(\sum E_iA\), and the saving becomes the intersections \(E_iA\cap E_jA\), which syzygies control. The truncated pseudoideal \(C\) is not an ideal, and its socle structure is unknown. That breaks the plain form; one would need a windowed duality (unverified).
- **E. Measure on \(S\), not on \(S/J\).** `lem:exact-common-kernel-selection` uses \(\operatorname{rank}(\rho,q)-\operatorname{rank}\rho\). Its Hermitian instance (\(\dim S/J=1\), summed and joint conditions fail, exact criterion holds) gains exactly because there \(\rho\) does not kill \(J\). An architecture for observations that do not factor through the quotient would bound this mixed rank structurally. The open statement's clause "measure every rank in the same protected quotient" forbids it as written.
