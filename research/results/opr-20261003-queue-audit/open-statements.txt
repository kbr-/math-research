Open statements
The goal follows from W by the proved logarithmic-prefix reduction, and from S1 by the proved compiler. By Taylor glue, W follows from Tor vanishing for the clause tops, open along three axes, highest risk first. The window route to S1 is a partial alternative (fate review).
W, wide clauses. Over \(\mathbb F_3\), for fixed \(K\) and large \(n\): for every family of \(M=n^K\) blocks of affine forms \(L_{b,1},\dots,L_{b,h_b}\) there are prefix sets \(S_b\), \(|S_b|=\min(k,h_b)\), such that weak unary PHP plus the clauses \(g_{b,i}\prod_{j\in S_b}L_{b,j}^{2}\), \(i\notin S_b\), \(g=1-L^2\), with \(k\approx2.5(K+1)\log n\), has no PC refutation of degree \(22k\). Status: open; a randomized-prefix weakening suffices (W_RS). It follows from row freeness on the allowed set (thm:global-closure-gluing), conjectured for column forms at density \(\ge\frac12\) (conj:closed-row-freeness) and, weakest, for a top-lift subset (lift review).
A1, adversarial forms. W for all families. Status: open, highest risk. Proved for cell, sparse, row-sparse and frozen-cell blocks (cells, sparse, frozen cells, small residual); under random prefixes (W_RS) several structured classes are settled (spare pigeon, pinned rank, pins). Open: the dense core beyond joint capacity, where no pullback exists (A1 subset review). Every certificate there needs a Nullstellensatz design. A point design on the allowed set cannot be found by density or spread and meets the capacity barrier at few free columns; local designs, a measure on each block's allowed set, bypass the union (thinness equivalence, review). The certificate half needs a lagged-lift subset (conj:lagged-lift-subset) or, for random tops, lower-degree Taylor conditions of A3's kind (relation excess).
A2, width. Tor vanishing for tops of width \(k=\Theta(\log n)\). Status: open for adversarial forms (A1). For designed forms settled with A3: about \(n^{K-o(1)}\) explicit clauses with \(\Theta(k^2)\)-cell supports on a column design glue on the weak base through \(22k\) (bounded clusters).
A3, degree. Tor vanishing through degree \(22k=\Theta(\log n)\) (conj:semi-regular-relative-to-base), or at one degree \(D'\ge22k\) given a design at \(D'-1\) (single degree). Status: open; core: Koszul range \(s\ge2k+4\). Designed clauses: Taylor generation holds at every degree when supports pairwise share few cells and columns (column Wilson, column-disjoint, bounded clusters); random tops act like random matrices (check, generic directness). For W_RS: randomized Taylor glue with members glued whole (single-block falls, pairs); early cross falls conjecturally need a shared piece, rare under random prefixes (fill-point falls, flat difference, shared pieces, review).
S1, window route. Weak unary PHP plus any \(n^K\) complete accuracy-one ENS blocks has no PC refutation of degree 11. Status: proved outside the window (graph criterion). In the window its gluing needs, for dense inventories, the lag bound \(C_{11}(\text{base}+M\text{ blocks})\cap\mathbb F_3[\text{board}]\subseteq C_{11+O(\log M)}(\text{base})\), whose known proof path is the prefix reduction to W.
