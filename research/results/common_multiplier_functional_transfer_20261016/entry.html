General statement. lem:admissible-numbering-transfer: on the functional pigeonhole system at \(D\le\lceil N/2\rceil\), over any field and for any number of pigeons, a set of monomial errors gains no killer from the pigeon axioms whenever one numbering of pigeons and holes keeps every product of a dance-admissible matching monomial with an error admissible or zero. lem:functional-killer-transfer: every single monomial error has such a numbering. cor:nested-hole-joint-transfer: sets of errors whose hole sets are nested have one, under a hole-room condition.

Setting

Setting. The functional pigeonhole system with \(m\) pigeons and \(N\ge1\) holes over a field: Booleanity, hole axioms \(x_{ij}x_{i'j}\), functionality axioms \(x_{ij}x_{ij'}\), pigeon axioms \(Q_i=1-\sum_jx_{ij}\), in the multilinear ring. \(V_K\) is the space of multilinear polynomials of degree at most \(K\). \(C\) is the degree-\(D\) PC closure of all axioms, and \(H\) that of the hole and functionality axioms alone, which is the span of the non-matching multilinear monomials of degree at most \(D\). For \(X\in\{C,H\}\) and a polynomial \(E\), \(\operatorname{Ann}_X(E)=\{u:uE\in X\}\), for \(u\in V_K\) with \(K+\deg E\le D\).

  • Modulo \(H\), every polynomial of degree at most \(D\) is a combination of matching monomials \(x_t\), \(t\) a partial matching, \(T_d\) those of degree at most \(d\) (IPS, Definition 2.1). The product \(x_tx_E\) is \(x_{t\cup E}\) when \(t\cup E\) is a matching and lies in \(H\) otherwise.
  • From Impagliazzo, Pudlák and Sgall (ECCC TR97-042, pages 3–6, read in this cycle from the rendered pages), for a fixed numbering of pigeons and holes:
    • A pigeon dance on a matching moves the pigeons in increasing order, each to a larger hole, every step staying one-to-one (Definition 2.3).
    • \(B_d\) consists of the \(x_a\), \(a\) a partial map to holes or to \(0\) (the factor \(Q_i\)), whose matching part admits a dance (Definition 2.5). Membership depends only on \(a\), so \(B_K=\{x_a\in B_D:|a|\le K\}\).
    • For \(d\le\lceil N/2\rceil\), \(B_d\) is a basis of \(\operatorname{span}(T_d)\) (Proposition 2.8).
    • The degree-\(d\) closure of the \(Q_i\) in that span is \(K_d=\operatorname{span}(B_d\setminus T_d)\). Theorem 2.9's proof gives the inclusion of the closure in that span. The elements of \(B_d\setminus T_d\) are multiples of axioms derivable in degree \(d\), which gives the reverse inclusion.
    • No step of these uses more pigeons than holes: Lemma 2.6 uses only \(y+2x\le n\).
  • Since \(B_K\subseteq B_D\) and Proposition 2.8 holds at \(K\) and at \(D\), an element of \(\operatorname{span}(T_K)\) has the same unique expansion in \(B_K\) and in \(B_D\). Hence \(K_D\cap\operatorname{span}(T_K)=\operatorname{span}(B_K\setminus T_K)=K_K\).
  • So \(C\) is \(H\) plus the lift of \(K_D\), and \(C\cap V_K\) is \(H\cap V_K\) plus the lift of \(K_K\). A member of \(H\cap V_K\) times \(x_E\) is a combination of non-matching monomials of degree at most \(D\), so it lies in \(H\).

The criterion

Lemma (admissible numbering transfer). Let \(D\le\lceil N/2\rceil\), let \(F\) be a set of monomial errors with \(K+|E|\le D\) for each \(E\in F\), and suppose one numbering of pigeons and holes has the following property. For every \(E\in F\) that is a matching and every \(x_t\in T_K\cap B_K\) with \(t\cup E\) a matching, \(x_{t\cup E}\in B_D\). Then \(\bigcap_{E\in F}\operatorname{Ann}_C(x_E)\cap V_K=\bigcap_{E\in F}\operatorname{Ann}_H(x_E)\cap V_K+(C\cap V_K)\).

Proof. Every relabelling of pigeons and holes maps the axiom sets to themselves, so \(C\) and \(H\) do not depend on the numbering, while \(B_d\) is taken for the given one.

  • The inclusion \(\supseteq\) holds since \(C\supseteq H\) and \((C\cap V_K)x_E\subseteq C\), the product having degree at most \(D\).
  • For \(\subseteq\), let \(u\in V_K\) with \(ux_E\in C\) for all \(E\in F\). Modulo \(H\), \(u\) is a combination of \(T_K\). By Proposition 2.8 at degree \(K\), \(u\equiv k+r\) with \(k\in K_K\) and \(r=\sum_{t\in T_K\cap B_K}c_tx_t\).
  • For each \(E\), \(kx_E\in K_D\), so \(rx_E\in K_D\) modulo \(H\). A non-matching \(E\) has \(x_E\in H\) and constrains nothing.
  • For a matching \(E\), by the property, \(rx_E=\sum_tc_tx_tx_E\) is modulo \(H\) a combination of elements of \(T_D\cap B_D\). Distinct \(t\) with the same \(t\cup E\) may cancel, which is harmless. Since \(B_D\) is a basis and \(K_D=\operatorname{span}(B_D\setminus T_D)\), the combination lies in \(K_D\) only if it is zero, that is \(rx_E\in H\).
  • So \(r\in\bigcap_E\operatorname{Ann}_H(x_E)\), and \(u\) is \(r\) plus \(k\) plus a member of \(H\cap V_K\), the last two lying in \(C\cap V_K\). \(\square\)

The operator form. In the notation of prop:exact-operator-criterion, \(r=R(u)\), and the property says \(R(x_Er)\equiv x_Er\) modulo \(H\). The restricted-board form holds in one direction: \(x_E\) times the closure of the restricted board, whose pigeon axioms are the \(Q_i'\) with the holes of \(E\) removed, lies in \(K_D\). Indeed \(x_EQ_i'=x_EQ_i\) modulo \(H\) for pigeons \(i\) outside \(E\).

Single errors

Lemma (functional killer transfer). For every \(N\ge1\), every \(m\), \(D\le\lceil N/2\rceil\), every set \(E\) of variables and \(K+|E|\le D\): \(\operatorname{Ann}_C(x_E)\cap V_K=\operatorname{Ann}_H(x_E)\cap V_K+(C\cap V_K)\).

Proof. If \(E\) is not a matching, both sides are \(V_K\); if \(E=\varnothing\), both are \(C\cap V_K\). Let \(E\) be a matching of size \(e\ge1\).

  • Case \(N\ge2\). Number so that \(E=\{(p_k,e+1-k):k=1,\dots,e\}\), with \(p_1<\dots<p_e\) the last \(e\) pigeons. Let \(x_t\in T_K\cap B_K\) with \(t\cup E\) a matching, and \(t_0=t\setminus E\).
    • A sub-matching of a matching with a dance has one: restrict each step to the remaining pigeons. So \(t_0\) has a dance.
    • \(t_0\) uses no pigeon of \(E\) and no hole in \(\{1,\dots,e\}\). Its dance moves upward, so it never enters those holes, the pigeons of \(E\) never block it, and its pigeons precede \(p_1\).
    • Then \(p_1\), at hole \(e\), moves to an unoccupied hole above \(e\): there are \(N-e-|t_0|\ge N-K-e\ge1\) of them, since \(K+e\le D\le\lceil N/2\rceil\le N-1\). Then \(p_k\), at hole \(e+1-k\), moves to hole \(e+2-k\), vacated by \(p_{k-1}\).
    • So \(t\cup E\) has a dance, and \(x_{t\cup E}\in B_D\). The admissible numbering transfer lemma gives the claim.
  • Case \(N=1\). Then \(D\le1\), so \(K=0\) and \(E=\{(i,1)\}\). The degree-\(1\) closure is \(\operatorname{span}(Q_1,\dots,Q_m)\), the hole and functionality axioms having degree \(2\), and \(x_{i1}=1-Q_i\) is not in it: \(x_{i1}=\sum_jc_jQ_j\) forces \(c_i=-1\) and \(c_j=0\) for \(j\ne i\), from the coefficients of the variables, and the constant term is then \(-1\ne0\). So \(cx_{i1}\in C\) only for \(c=0\), while \(\operatorname{Ann}_H(x_{i1})\cap V_0=0\) and \(C\cap V_0=0\). Here the property fails, since \(x_{i1}\) has no dance, so the case is checked directly. \(\square\)

Nested hole sets

Corollary (nested hole joint transfer). Let \(D\le\lceil N/2\rceil\) and let \(F\) be a set of matchings whose hole sets form a chain under inclusion, \(\Pi\) the set of their pigeons. Suppose that for each \(E\in F\), with \(s_E=|E|\), \(K+s_E\le D\) and \(K+2s_E+\min(|\Pi|-s_E,K)\le N\). Then the joint transfer of the admissible numbering transfer lemma holds for \(F\).

Proof. Number the holes so that each hole set in the chain is an initial segment: the smallest first, then each larger set's new holes. Number the pigeons so that \(\Pi\) comes last, in any order. Fix \(E\in F\), with hole set \(\{1,\dots,s\}\), \(s=s_E\), and \(x_t\in T_K\cap B_K\) with \(t\cup E\) a matching.

  • As before, \(t_0=t\setminus E\) has a dance, uses no pigeon of \(E\), and has no hole in \(\{1,\dots,s\}\). Its dance stays above \(s\), and the pigeons of \(E\) never block it.
  • Run that dance in pigeon order. The pigeons of \(t_0\) outside \(\Pi\) all move before any pigeon of \(\Pi\). At its turn, a pigeon of \(E\) moves to a hole above \(s\) that is unoccupied and is not the target of a later move of a pigeon of \(t_0\) in \(\Pi\).
  • Holes above \(s\): \(N-s\). Excluded: the current positions of the pigeons of \(t_0\), at most \(K\); the future targets of the pigeons of \(t_0\) in \(\Pi\setminus E\), at most \(\min(|\Pi|-s,K)\); the holes taken by earlier pigeons of \(E\), at most \(s-1\). The room condition leaves at least one.
  • The pigeons of \(t_0\) still reach their targets, which no pigeon of \(E\) takes. So \(t\cup E\) has a dance, and the property holds for this one numbering and every \(E\in F\). \(\square\)
The room condition is this bookkeeping's bound, which reserves every current position and future target. It is not shown to be needed: for one error the single-error lemma needs only \(K+s\le N-1\), using vacated holes of \(E\).

Hypotheses, each with its step.

  • \(D\le\lceil N/2\rceil\): Proposition 2.8 and Theorem 2.9 at degrees \(K\) and \(D\). The single-error lemma's free hole needs only \(K+|E|\le N-1\), which this implies for \(N\ge2\).
  • \(K+|E|\le D\): the products \(x_tx_E\) and \((C\cap V_K)x_E\) must lie in the degree-\(D\) space.
  • The number of pigeons: none. No cited step uses \(m>N\), so the statements hold for every \(m\). For \(m\le N\) the system is satisfiable, and the statements still describe its truncated closure.
  • The corollary's nested hole sets: the dance of \(t_0\) avoids \(E\)'s holes because \(t_0\) has none of them and they form an initial segment. Its room condition is the count of free holes above \(s_E\).
  • The field: none; the cited basis is over any field.
The criterion and the single-error lemma hold for every \(K\le D-|E|\), the corollary for every \(K\) meeting its room condition.

Degenerate cases.

  • \(E=\varnothing\) (\(x_E=1\)): both sides are \(C\cap V_K\).
  • A non-matching \(E\): \(x_E\in H\), and both sides are \(V_K\).
  • \(K=0\) and \(E\) a nonempty matching: \(cx_E\in C\) only for \(c=0\).
  • \(N=1\): proved directly above.
  • A corollary set with one element: the single-error lemma under a stronger room condition.
  • \(F\) empty: both sides are \(V_K\).
