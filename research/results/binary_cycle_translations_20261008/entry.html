Circulations preserve the charged space

Use the exact charged character construction: a simple connected oriented graph on \(n\) vertices and \(m\) edges, maximum degree \(\Delta\), edge expansion \(\varepsilon\), arbitrary charges \(c\in\mathbb F_3^V\), coefficient field \(F=\mathbb F_4=\mathbb F_2(\omega)\), and \[ \max(4,2\Delta)\le D<\varepsilon n/8. \] The nonzero total-charge condition is needed for the intended inconsistent family, not for the identities below. Let \((\partial s)_e=s_u-s_v\) for an edge oriented from \(u\) to \(v\), let \(\mathcal B=\operatorname{im}\partial\), and write \(J_D\) for the constructed degree-\(D\) pseudoideal and \(R_D\) for its quotient map. The local normal forms use characters \(m_\alpha=\prod_{\alpha_e=1}U_e\prod_{\alpha_e=-1}V_e\). Local relations include \(U_e^2=V_e\), \(V_e^2=U_e\), \(U_eV_e=1\). Phase-adjusted differences identify \(\alpha-\beta=\partial s\), with the prescribed small witness and phase \(c\cdot s\). No globally consistent charged function algebra is assumed.

Lemma (circulation differences). For any \(z\in\ker\partial^{\mathsf T}\), the substitution \[ T_z(U_e)=\omega^{z_e}U_e,\qquad T_z(V_e)=\omega^{-z_e}V_e \] preserves every degree filtration and \(J_D\), with inverse \(T_{-z}\). It permutes one-hot indicators by \(T_z(x_{e,a})=x_{e,a-z_e}\), so also acts over their binary form. Whenever \(\deg f+K\le D\), the multiplication maps for \(f\) and \(T_zf\) on degree at most \(K\) have equal quotient rank. If \(C_1,\ldots,C_r\) are pairwise edge-disjoint simple cycles and \(\kappa_i\) their oriented unit circulations, then \[ \prod_{i=1}^r(T_{\kappa_i}-I)H=0\quad\text{for every }\deg H<r. \] The individual differences preserve degree; they need not lower it.

Proof. Local relations are carried to scalar multiples of themselves: for example \(\omega^{2z_e}=\omega^{-z_e}\). In a phase-adjusted character difference with \(\alpha-\beta=\partial s\), both terms acquire the same scalar since \(z\cdot(\alpha-\beta)=(\partial^{\mathsf T}z)\cdot s=0\). Such differences and local reductions generate the kernel on its own monomial support, so \(T_zJ_D=J_D\); applying the inverse gives equality, not just containment. The indicator formula follows from \(x_{e,a}=1+\omega^{-a}U_e+\omega^aV_e\). Conjugating multiplication by the invertible induced maps on the domain and quotient proves rank invariance within the stated cutoff. Finally a degree-below-\(r\) monomial touches fewer than \(r\) disjoint cycle supports. Some translation fixes it, so its difference kills it; the commuting operators kill every such monomial. \(\square\)

A single-coordinate label shift need not be a circulation and need not preserve a charged vertex relation. The graph condition is doing the work. This lemma is the standard character action checked on the actual truncated, phase-adjusted kernel, not a claim of a new general symmetry theorem.

A cost witness that retains the multiplier space

Choose \(bt\) pairwise edge-disjoint simple cycles, where \(b\ge2\) and \(t\ge1\). Choose one distinguished edge from each cycle and partition these edges into \(t\) sets \(B_1,\ldots,B_t\) of size \(b\). Select \(e_*\in B_1\), put \(x_e=x_{e,0}\), and define the reduced companion \[ F=x_{e_*}\left(\sum_{e\in B_1\setminus\{e_*\}}x_e\right) \prod_{j=2}^t\left(1+\sum_{e\in B_j}x_e\right). \] It has degree at most \(t+1\). Modulo local Boolean relations it equals \(x_{e_*}\prod_{j=1}^t(1+\sum_{B_j}x_e)\), one companion of a legal constant specialization of a complete affine-input ENS block of accuracy \(t\). Original product and companion degrees are \(2t\) and \(2t+1\), including fresh coefficients. They remain charged in any source replay. The original specialized companion reduces through degree \(t+1\); this does not erase the original charge.

Theorem (cycle conjunction cost). Assume \(\varepsilon>1\), integers \(0\le K<K'\), and \(K'+t+1\le D\). Set \[ \kappa=1-1/\varepsilon,\quad \lambda(u)=\frac{u+2m}{3u+2m},\quad \sigma=\lambda(K')^\kappa,\quad \tau=\lambda(K)^\kappa,\quad \mu=\log(\tau/\sigma)>0. \] For every identity \[ F-H-\sum_\ell c_\ell L_\ell\in J_D,\qquad \deg H\le t,\quad c_\ell\in F^*,\quad d_\ell:=|\operatorname{supp}L_\ell|\le D-K', \] where each \(L_\ell\) is a label conjunction on distinct arbitrary graph edges, one has \[ \mathcal C_\tau:=\sum_\ell\tau^{d_\ell} \ \ge\ \frac{(b-1)b^{t-1}e^\mu(e\mu)^t}{4^{t+1}}. \] In particular \(b\ge\max(2,\lceil32/(e\mu)\rceil)\) implies \(\mathcal C_\tau>1\). The real number \(e=\exp(1)\) is used in these numerical inequalities; the integers counting terms or ranks are not field scalars. The theorem permits arbitrary sharing and cancellation among terms. It is stated for positive label conjunctions, not unexpanded negative-indicator literals.

Proof. For each set \(S\) consisting of \(e_*\), one other edge of \(B_1\), and one edge of each \(B_j\) for \(j\ge2\), apply the mixed difference along the \(r=t+1\) corresponding cycles. There are \(N=(b-1)b^{t-1}\) such sets. The lemma kills \(H\). In \(F\), the mixed difference kills every monomial except \(\prod_{e\in S}x_e\), and therefore \[ \Delta_SF=\prod_{e\in S}(T_{\kappa_{C_e}}-I)x_e. \] Every factor is a sum of exactly two nonzero character terms, one in \(U_e\) and one in \(V_e\), with no constant term. Moreover no nonzero cut vector is supported in \(S\). If \(\alpha\in\mathcal B\) were so supported, pairing it with each selected cycle circulation would isolate the corresponding \(\alpha_e\), forcing all coordinates to vanish. Thus the \(2^r\) characters in this product represent distinct cut-code cosets and have nonzero coefficients. The product survives \(J_D\).

The sparse-weight bound, applied at \(K'\) with weight degree \(r\), gives \[ \operatorname{rank}(f\mapsto R_D((\Delta_SF)f),\ \deg f\le K')\ge h(K')/2^r. \] All terms of the representation difference have degree at most \(D-K'\), because \(r+K'\le D\). The circulation operator preserves that degree and \(J_D\); bounded absorption then permits multiplication by the whole degree-\(K'\) domain. This is why the larger multiplier cutoff is paid explicitly.

If \(L_\ell\) misses any selected cycle, \(\Delta_SL_\ell=0\). Otherwise its mixed difference is a sum of at most \(2^r\) translated label conjunctions, each on the same \(d_\ell\) edges. The puncturing theorem bounds each map's rank by \(\sigma^{d_\ell}h(K')\), including incompatible labels, whose rank is zero. Rank subadditivity, followed by summation over \(S\), therefore gives \[ \frac{Nh(K')}{2^r}\le2^rh(K')\sum_\ell a_\ell\sigma^{d_\ell}, \] where \(a_\ell\) is the number of selected transversals whose cycles all meet \(L_\ell\).

If the distinguished cycle is missed, \(a_\ell=0\). Otherwise let \(k_j\) count the cycles in group \(j\) that meet its support. Then \[ a_\ell=(k_1-1)\prod_{j=2}^tk_j \le\left(\frac{d_\ell-1}{t}\right)^t, \] since the cycles are edge-disjoint and \(\sum_jk_j\le d_\ell\). A positive \(a_\ell\) requires \(d_\ell\ge t+1\). Putting \(u=d_\ell-1\), the elementary maximum of \(u^te^{-\mu u}\) at \(u=t/\mu\) gives \[ a_\ell\left(\frac\sigma\tau\right)^{d_\ell} \le e^{-\mu}(u/t)^te^{-\mu u} \le e^{-\mu}(e\mu)^{-t}. \] Cancel the positive \(h(K')\) and rearrange to obtain the stated cost. Finally \(b-1\ge b/2\) implies that when \(be\mu\ge32\), the lower bound is at least \(e^\mu8^{t-1}>1\). \(\square\)

The projection-padding obstruction is not contradicted. That projection sends padded conjunctions to an unchanged small witness. Here padding lowers the full quotient multiplication rank, and the normalization \(h(K')\) can be exponentially large, exactly as the rank-witness dimension warning requires. Conversely, the theorem does not cover conjunctions in the last \(K'-K\) degrees up to \(D-K\); setting \(K'=K\) gives \(\mu=0\) and loses the estimate.

The reserved band can be polylogarithmic

Corollary (cycle cost window). Let a given family of simple connected \(\Delta\)-regular graphs have fixed \(\Delta\ge5\) and fixed expansion lower bound \(\varepsilon>1\). For any integer sequence \(1\le t\le(\log n)^a\) with fixed \(a\ge0\), all sufficiently large graphs admit the preceding construction with \(D=\lfloor\varepsilon n/16\rfloor\), \(K=\lfloor D/4\rfloor\), \(K'=K+O(t\log n)\), and a cost lower bound greater than one for every represented term degree at most \(D-K'\). This is conditional on the stated graph family, not an existence claim for every choice of degree and expansion constants.

Proof. Write \(L_n=2\lceil\log_2n\rceil+3\). Any graph with more than \(2n\) edges has a nonempty subgraph of minimum degree at least three: repeatedly deleting a vertex of degree at most two cannot remove more than \(2n\) edges in total. Such a subgraph contains a cycle of length at most \(L_n\). Indeed, if the ball of radius \(\lceil\log_2n\rceil\) at a vertex has no collision creating a cycle of length at most twice that radius plus one, its breadth-first tree has at least \(1+3(2^{\lceil\log_2n\rceil}-1)>n\) vertices, a contradiction. Remove a short cycle's edges and repeat while more than \(2n\) edges remain. This gives at least \[ \left\lfloor\frac{m-2n-1}{L_n}\right\rfloor=\Omega_\Delta(n/\log n) \] edge-disjoint cycles for all sufficiently large \(n\), since \(m=\Delta n/2\). Choose \(b=\lfloor c n/(t\log n)\rfloor\), for a sufficiently small fixed positive \(c=c(\Delta)\), so that \(bt\) cycles fit. Then \(b\ge2\) eventually.

Set \(K'=K+\lceil C t\log n\rceil\), with a sufficiently large fixed \(C=C(\Delta,\varepsilon)\). Polylogarithmic \(t\) ensures \(K'\le D/2\), \(K'+t+1\le D\), and \(2t+1\le D\) eventually. On this interval \(u\le m\), and \[ -\frac{d}{du}\log\lambda(u)=\frac{4m}{(u+2m)(3u+2m)}\ge\frac4{15m}. \] Consequently \(\mu\ge4\kappa(K'-K)/(15m)\), and \(b\mu\) is bounded below by a positive constant times \(C\). Choose \(C\) to make \(b\mu\ge32/e\) eventually. The theorem applies. All original degree charges fit as well; the resulting cost statement is for terms through \(D-K-O(t\log n)\), not for the final reserved band. \(\square\)

The number of distinguished inputs is \(bt=O(n/\log n)\); choosing polylogarithmic accuracy gives a polynomial written source inventory and the stated original degree. This illustrates a real asymptotic range, not a test on a graph too small to admit a proper charged quotient. It does not establish that this adverse specialization must occur in a proof.

A surviving weight defeats the single-companion witness

Put \(W=1+x_{e_*,0}=U_{e_*}+V_{e_*}\). The distinguished edge is on a cycle, so its unit vector is not in the cut space. Thus these are two distinct nonzero quotient coordinates, and \(W\notin J_D\). The sparse-weight lemma gives \(h_W(K)\ge h(K)/2\) when \(K+1\le D\). But \(WF=0\pmod{J_D}\) through degree \(t+2\), simply from \(x_{e_*}(1+x_{e_*})=0\) and local Booleanity. In the displayed graph range this degree fits. This kills the selected companion, not necessarily every companion of the block. It directly prevents promoting the unweighted cost bound to a universal obstruction for surviving weights.
