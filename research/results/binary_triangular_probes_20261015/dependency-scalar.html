Let \(r\ge1\) be the number of original clause, Boolean and commutator placeholder labels. After commuting old variables and placeholders, denote the coefficient vectors of the placeholder-linear certificates by \(c_\pi,c_{\widehat\pi}\).

Let a division-free unbounded-fan-in \(\mathbb F_2\) circuit \(H(x,Z_1,\ldots,Z_r)\) have gate/wire size \(U\) and depth \(u\), of arbitrary degree. Assume only \[ H(a,c_\pi(a))=H(a,c_{\widehat\pi}(a))\quad\text{for every Boolean old point }a. \] Then \(H(x,c_\pi(x))\)'s Boolean function has an unbounded-fan-in \(\mathbb F_2\) arithmetic circuit of size \(O(U+(r+1)R^{48})\) and depth at most \(u+16\). This does not compute its formal polynomial or follow from invariance on a class that excludes \(\widehat\pi\).

Proof. For each placeholder label \(j\), form \[ a_{t,j}(x)=A_t(x,e_j)-A_t(x,0) \] after commutative specialization. This selects its old coefficient because \(A_t\) is placeholder-linear. Subtracting the constant part makes no extra homogeneity assumption. Copies of the given size-\(O(R^{48})\), depth-at-most-fourteen formula, with placeholder inputs set to zero/one, and one output subtraction layer, give all \(r\) coefficients in size \(O((r+1)R^{48})\), depth at most fifteen. The constant-part output can be shared. Field constants remain binary.

The used-node theorem states \(C_{\widehat\pi}-A_t\in\mathfrak m_a^t\), where \(\mathfrak m_a\) is the two-sided old-point ideal, for every Boolean \(a\). Evaluating old variables at \(a\) kills that ideal. Substituting either \(e_j\) or zero for placeholders therefore gives \(c_{\widehat\pi,j}(a)=a_{t,j}(a)\). Its value at \(a\) equals \(H(a,c_{\widehat\pi}(a))\), hence the assumed original value. Shared circuit composition costs the stated size and conservative depth. \(\square\)
