General statement. lem:twist-window-trade: with the variables beyond \(x_1,\dots,x_t\) untwisted, a twisted repair on Boolean twisted variables splits into substitution repairs of \(x_1,\dots,x_t\), losing for each split the room does not pay one window degree: soundness and killing above the reduced window are dropped. Twisted variables that the closure defines linearly in untwisted ones cost nothing, with no Boolean axiom: the repair is scalar on the window. In a domain model the split keeps the full window, measured in the degree-\((D'+T)\) part of the same vanishing ideal.

Twist-window trade

Statement. Over a field, let \(R=k[x]\), \(J\) the degree-\(D'\) closure of axioms \(a_j\), and \(\rho:R\to\operatorname{Mat}_k(R)\) given by commuting \(\rho(x_i)\) of entry degree at most \(T\ge1\). Call \(\nu\in R^k\) a twisted repair at window \(D-K\) with room \(R_0\) when:

  • soundness: \(\rho(a_jm)\nu\in J^k\) for \(\deg(a_jm)\le D-K\);
  • killing: \(\rho(wE_i)\nu\in J^k\) for the errors with \(\deg(wE_i)\le D-K\);
  • survival: \(\rho(w)\nu\notin J^k\);
  • room: \(T(D-K)+\deg\nu\le R_0\le D'\).
Put \(u_0=\nu-\rho(x_1)\nu\) and \(u_1=\rho(x_1)\nu\). (a) Let \(x_1,\dots,x_t\) carry Boolean axioms \(x_i^2-x_i\), and let \(\rho(x_i)=x_iI\) for \(i>t\).
  • For \(g\) of degree at most \(D-K-1\), \(\rho(g)\nu\in J^k\) implies \(\rho(g|_{x_1=0})u_0\in J^k\) and \(\rho(g|_{x_1=1})u_1\in J^k\).
  • For \(w\) multilinear in \(x_1\), \(\rho(w)\nu=\rho(w|_{x_1=0})u_0+\rho(w|_{x_1=1})u_1\). For other \(w\) this holds modulo \(J^k\), when soundness reaches \(\deg w\).
  • So one of the vectors \(u_\varepsilon\) is a twisted repair of the substituted map \(g\mapsto\rho(g|_{x_1=\varepsilon})u_\varepsilon\), measured in \(J\). Its window is \(D-K-1\): soundness and killing of degree \(D-K\) are dropped. Its vector degree is at most \(\deg\nu+T\), and the room is unchanged.
  • After all \(t\) splits the action is scalar, and some coordinate of some \(u_\sigma\), \(\sigma\in\{0,1\}^t\), is a substitution repair of \(x_1,\dots,x_t\mapsto\sigma\), at window \(D-K-t\).
  • With room \(R_0\ge T(D-K)+sT+\deg\nu\), the first \(s\) splits keep the window and the result has window \(D-K-(t-s)\).
  • The conclusion has content only when \(D-K>t-s\).
(b) Let \(x_1,\dots,x_s\) be twisted and \(\rho(x_i)=x_iI\) for \(i>s\). Let each \(x_i-\ell_i\), \(i\le s\), be an axiom, with \(\ell_i\) of degree at most \(1\) in the untwisted variables, and \(D-K+\deg\nu\le D'\). Then \(\rho(g)\nu\equiv g\nu\pmod{J^k}\) for every \(g\) of degree at most \(D-K\): the twisted repair is the scalar repair with the coordinates of \(\nu\) as multipliers, at the full window. No Boolean axiom is needed, and (b) uses neither the entry bound \(T\) nor the room of the definition. (c) Let \(J\) be the space of polynomials of degree at most \(D'\) vanishing on a finite set \(X\) (a domain model), let \(x_1^2-x_1\) be an axiom, and let \(D-K\ge2\). For every \(g\) of degree at most \(D-K\) with \(\rho(g)\nu\) vanishing on \(X\), both \(\rho(g|_{x_1=\varepsilon})u_\varepsilon\) vanish on \(X\), with degree at most \(T(D-K)+T+\deg\nu\le D'+T\). So the split keeps the full window, measured in the degree-\((D'+T)\) part of the same vanishing ideal. Survival is unchanged, since a polynomial not vanishing on \(X\) lies in no degree part.

Proof. The identity: for \(g=g_0+x_1g_1\) with \(g_0,g_1\) free of \(x_1\), \(\rho(g)\nu=\rho(g_0)u_0+\rho(g_0+g_1)u_1=\rho(g|_{x_1=0})u_0+\rho(g|_{x_1=1})u_1\), since \(\rho\) is an algebra map. A \(g\) of degree at most \(D-K\) has this form modulo multiples of \(x_1^2-x_1\) of no higher degree, which soundness sends into \(J^k\). (a)

  • Separation. If \(\deg g\le D-K-1\) and \(\rho(g)\nu\in J^k\), its entries have degree at most \(T(D-K-1)+\deg\nu\). Multiplying by the entries of \(\rho(x_1)\) stays in \(J\) within \(T(D-K)+\deg\nu\le D'\), so \(\rho(x_1g)\nu\in J^k\). Since \(x_1g=x_1g|_{x_1=1}+(x_1^2-x_1)g_1\) with \(\deg((x_1^2-x_1)g_1)\le D-K\), soundness gives \(\rho(g|_{x_1=1})u_1\in J^k\), and the identity gives \(\rho(g|_{x_1=0})u_0\in J^k\).
  • The new repair. Applied to axiom multiples and weighted errors of degree at most \(D-K-1\), this is soundness and killing of the substituted maps. The identity at \(w\) splits survival, so one of the two survives.
  • Room. The new vectors have degree at most \(\deg\nu+T\), and \(T(D-K-1)+\deg\nu+T=T(D-K)+\deg\nu\), so the next split, on \(x_2\) acting on \(u_\varepsilon\) by commuting, has the same room.
  • Paid splits. With room \(T(D-K)+sT+\deg\nu\), the first \(s\) splits are made at the full window by lem:twisted-certificate-reduction (f)'s argument. That argument holds with the other variables still twisted, since \(\rho(x_1^2-x_1)\rho(g_1)\nu=\rho(g_1)\rho(x_1^2-x_1)\nu\) by commuting, at degree at most \(T(D-K+1)+\deg\nu\). The \(i\)-th paid split needs \(iT\le sT\) of extra room. The unpaid ones follow by the separation step.
  • The last split. Once every twisted variable is substituted, \(\rho\) acts by scalars, and the coordinates separate.
(b) Substituting \(x_1\mapsto\ell_1\), then \(x_2\mapsto\ell_2\), and so on, writes \(g=\bar g+\sum_{i\le s}(x_i-\ell_i)h_i\), where \(\bar g\) is \(g\) after all \(s\) substitutions and \(\deg h_i\le\deg g-1\). No step raises degree, since \(\deg\ell_i\le1\). Each \((x_i-\ell_i)h_i\) is an axiom multiple of degree at most \(D-K\), so soundness gives \(\rho((x_i-\ell_i)h_i)\nu\in J^k\). Then \(\rho(g)\nu\equiv\rho(\bar g)\nu=\bar g\,\nu\), since \(\bar g\) involves only untwisted variables. Finally \(g\nu-\bar g\nu=\sum_i(x_i-\ell_i)h_i\nu\) is a sum of axiom multiples of degree at most \(D-K+\deg\nu\le D'\), so it lies in \(J^k\). (c) At a point \(p\in X\):
  • \((\rho(x_1g)\nu)(p)=\rho(x_1)(p)\,(\rho(g)\nu)(p)=0\);
  • \((\rho((x_1^2-x_1)g_1)\nu)(p)=\rho(g_1)(p)\,(\rho(x_1^2-x_1)\nu)(p)=0\), by soundness at \(x_1^2-x_1\), of degree \(2\le D-K\).
So \(\rho(g|_{x_1=1})u_1\) vanishes on \(X\), and then so does \(\rho(g|_{x_1=0})u_0\). Their degrees are at most \(T(D-K)+T+\deg\nu\). In a domain model membership is vanishing plus a degree bound.

Hypotheses, each with its step.

  • Part (a). That \(\rho\) is an algebra map is used in the identity, and commuting in the later splits. The Boolean axiom on \(x_i\) makes \((x_i^2-x_i)g_1\) a sound multiple. The room is the closure step. \(\rho(x_i)=x_iI\) for \(i>t\) makes the last action scalar, so that coordinates separate. \(D-K>t-s\) is nontriviality only, used by no step.
  • Part (b). Soundness at the axioms \(x_i-\ell_i\), \(\deg\ell_i\le1\), and \(\ell_i\) untwisted are the steps. \(D-K+\deg\nu\le D'\) is the final membership. A closure member \(x_i-\ell_i\) that is not an axiom needs soundness extended to closure members, which lem:twisted-certificate-reduction (a) gives within its room.
  • Part (c). The domain model is membership as vanishing. Soundness at \(x_1^2-x_1\), with \(D-K\ge2\), is the second vanishing.
  • The field is arbitrary throughout.

Degenerate cases.

  • \(t=0\) in (a), or \(s=0\) in (b): nothing to split or substitute.
  • \(\rho(x_1)=x_1I\): \(u_1=x_1\nu\), and the split is the scalar repair split by cases.
  • \(u_1=0\) or \(u_0=0\): one piece only, already substituted.
  • \(D-K\le t-s\) in (a): the reduced window is empty and (a) says nothing.
  • \(\ell_i\) constant in (b): \(x_i\) is fixed by an axiom, and the substitution is by that constant.
