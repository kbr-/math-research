\begin{abstract}
For \(0\le\ell<k\), let \(\delta(n,k,\ell)\) be the least degree of a polynomial
\(P\in\Ft[x_1,\ldots,x_n]\) that vanishes with (Hasse) multiplicity at least \(k\)
at every nonzero point of \(\Ft^n\) and with multiplicity exactly \(\ell\) at the
origin. We prove that for all \(n\ge1\),
\[
 \delta(n,k,\ell)=n+2\ell+\sum_{j=0}^{n-1}\Bigl\lfloor\frac{k-\ell-1}{2^j}\Bigr\rfloor .
\]
Menezes determined \(\delta\) over every field for \(k\ge2\) and \(n\ge k-1\); in
characteristic two his value is \(n+2k-2-s_2(k-\ell-1)\), which our formula
recovers whenever \(k-\ell-1<2^n\). When \(k-\ell-1\ge2^n\) the value is smaller:
each block of \(2^n\) in \(k-\ell-1\) is paid for by the square of the product of
the affine forms \(1+u\cdot x\), \(u\ne0\), of the \(2^n-1\) hyperplanes avoiding the
origin. The lower bound inverts \(x^2+x\) by an additive power series and reduces
modulo the vanishing polynomial of the span of \(x_1^2+x_1,\ldots,x_n^2+x_n\).
Minimizing over \(\ell\) gives the least degree \(D(n,k)\) of a polynomial with
multiplicity at least \(k\) off the origin and less than \(k\) at the origin:
\(D(n,k)=2k+n-2-\lfloor\log_2k\rfloor\) for \(k<2^{n-1}\), and
\(2k-\lfloor k/2^{n-1}\rfloor\) for \(k\ge2^{n-1}\). So the multiplicity
Schwartz--Zippel bound \(2k-\lfloor k/2^{n-1}\rfloor\), which Bishnoi,
Boyadzhiyska, Das and M\'esz\'aros asked about for \(k\ge2^{n-2}\), where their
hyperplane covers attain it, is not attained for any \(k<2^{n-2}\). Both values hold
over every field of characteristic two, by a descent to \(\Ft\). Every theorem, lemma and corollary is formalized in
Lean~4, and an appendix maps each to its formal proof.
\end{abstract}
