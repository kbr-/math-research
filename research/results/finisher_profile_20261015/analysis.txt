Actual checkpoint profile, 15 October 2026

The powered-prefix checkpoint reported 6.4 seconds of checks under cProfile;
the preceding unprofiled GCD checkpoint reported 5.1 seconds. The profile
includes instrumentation overhead and is not a speed comparison.

profile.txt preserves the built-in pstats cumulative report from the completed
real checkpoint. Its 12.543-second overall interval also includes timing,
archival, next-cycle guidance and the external stand-in attempt. These are not
all inside the check-warning interval.

Within checks, check_revision accounts for 2.411 seconds, full registry schema
validation for 1.098 seconds, validate_queue for 1.180 seconds, and two calls to
validate_marker for 0.809 seconds. Existing registry parsing is cached; the
profile does not justify adding another parse cache. The two marker validations
are not automatically redundant: one runs on the draft and one after timing
replacement. Removing either requires verifying which state it protects.

The existing queued checkpoint-check-cost build already owns reducing these
checks without weakening validation. This evidence narrows its next profiling
work to changed-evidence maintenance and repeated notebook scans; it proves no
speedup and changes no limit or default. A same-check unprofiled measurement is
still needed before attributing an improvement. No framework implementation was
changed in this administrative evidence checkpoint.
