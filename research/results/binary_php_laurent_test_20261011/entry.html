PHP extension certificates

Theorem. Let \(n\ge1\), \(M=n(n+1)\), and use the weak unary PHP CNF on \(x_{ij}\), \(1\le i\le n+1\), \(1\le j\le n\). Its original generators are row violations \(R_i=\prod_{j=1}^n(1-x_{ij})\) and column collisions \(Q_{ikj}=x_{ij}x_{kj}\), \(i<k\), with the displayed orders. Include Boolean and variable-commutator generators. There is a placeholder-linear noncommutative IPS formula of size \(O(n^8)\) and constant ordered unbounded-fan-in depth over \(K=\mathbb F_{2^a}\), with \(|K|=O(n^2)\). There is a binary-coefficient certificate polynomial with a constant-depth \(K\)-formula of size \(O(n^8\log(n+2))\). The formulas use the original inputs; no row functionality clauses or new proof variables are added. This is not a constant-depth \(\mathbb F_2\)-formula claim.

Local polynomials. Let \(z\) be a central Laurent parameter and put \(u_{ij}=1+(z-1)x_{ij}\), \(v_{ij}=1+(z^{-1}-1)x_{ij}\). Define \[ T_i=z^{-1}\prod_j u_{ij},\quad G_i=T_i-z^{-1}R_i =\sum_{j=1}^n\left(\prod_{k<j}u_{ik}\right)x_{ij}\left(\prod_{k>j}(1-x_{ik})\right). \] The last equality is ordered product telescoping between \(u_{ij}\) and \(1-x_{ij}\), whose difference is \(zx_{ij}\). It proves that \(G_i\) has only nonnegative powers of \(z\), of degree at most \(n-1\), using only \(n\) terms. For evaluation at a nonzero scalar one may use the shorter difference expression. The row correction is exactly \(T_i-G_i=z^{-1}R_i\).

For a column set \(b=z^{-1}-1\) and \[ S_j=z\prod_{i=1}^{n+1}(1+bx_{ij}),\qquad H_j=z+(1-z)\sum_{i=1}^{n+1}x_{ij}. \] An exact free-algebra identity is \[ S_j-H_j=zb^2\sum_{i<k}Q_{ikj}\prod_{l>k}(1+bx_{lj}). \] Indeed every term of degree at least two in the ordered product has unique first two selected positions \(i,k\); all remaining selected positions lie after \(k\). The degree-zero and degree-one terms are precisely \(H_j\). This identity uses the original collision generators, with right cofactors, and \(O(n^2)\) terms per column. Each \(H_j\) is a polynomial in \(z\) of degree at most one. No expansion into all column assignments is used.

Global cancellation. Let \[ W=T_1\cdots T_{n+1}S_1\cdots S_n,\qquad \Gamma=G_1\cdots G_{n+1}H_1\cdots H_n. \] Replacing factors one at a time by their good versions, with old factors in the prefix and good factors in the suffix, expresses \(W-\Gamma\) from the row and collision generators by the local identities above. The total scalar \(z\)-power in \(W\) outside the affine edge factors is \(z^{-1}\). Reorder its \(2M\) affine factors into \(z^{-1}\prod_{ij}u_{ij}v_{ij}\), using at most \(\binom{2M}{2}\) adjacent swaps. A swap of two affine factors has difference equal to the product of their linear coefficients times a variable commutator; scalar terms commute. Finally, \[ u_{ij}v_{ij}-1=(z-1)(z^{-1}-1)(x_{ij}^2-x_{ij}). \] Product telescoping now expresses \(W-z^{-1}\) from Boolean and commutator generators. Subtracting from the first expression gives a placeholder-linear Laurent formula \(E(z;X,Y)\) with \[ E(z;X,0)=0,\qquad E(z;X,F)=z^{-1}-\Gamma(z). \] All identities are in the free algebra with central Laurent scalars. In particular every rearrangement, including those between rows and columns, is explicitly paid.

Range and cost. The good product \(\Gamma\) has only nonnegative exponents, so its coefficient at \(z^{-1}\) is zero. For the full certificate use the conservative interval \([-L,L]\), \(L=4(M+n+1)\). Every term of the displayed construction contains at most \(2M\) affine \(u,v\) factors, at most \(2n+1\) exterior row/column powers \(z^{\pm1}\), and at most two extra powers from one local correction. Expanding a good factor for this exponent bound does not expand it in the formula. Each such term therefore fits \([-L,L]\).

Choose the least power of two \(q>2L+1\), let \(K=\mathbb F_q\), and take \(2L+1\) distinct nonzero nodes. Vandermonde interpolation on \(z^LE(z)\), of degree at most \(2L\), extracts its degree-\(L-1\) coefficient. The resulting linear combination of \(E(\alpha)\) is a certificate for one. There are \(O(n^4)\) swap corrections, each of size \(O(n^2)\); local row/column corrections fit the same \(O(n^6)\) total bound per specialization. All good factors remain compact. The \(O(n^2)\) interpolation copies give \(O(n^8)\) size and constant depth; scalar constants are counted as leaves. By the proved coefficientwise trace argument, choose \(\beta\in K\) with trace one and sum the \(a\) Frobenius conjugates of \(\beta D\). This gives a binary-coefficient certificate, with the asserted \(O(\log n)\) copy factor over \(K\). Coefficientwise Frobenius fixes all indeterminates; no binary circuit implementation of this scalar-field computation is inferred. \(\square\)

Nonvacuity. Weak PHP is unsatisfiable for every \(n\ge1\): the row constraints require at least \(n+1\) ones, while collision-free columns permit at most \(n\). Its original literal encoding has size \(\Theta(n^3)\), so all displayed costs are polynomial in that encoding. Degenerate cases. For one hole, the two positive row clauses and their collision clause already contradict. Zero holes give an empty clause and need no construction. Removing a pigeon eliminates the scalar deficit \(z^{-1}\), so the same extraction no longer proves one. No stronger functional PHP assumptions are used.

Certificate cube erasure

Lemma. Let \(F_1,\ldots,F_m\) be old-variable binary formulas of total size \(s\) and depth at most \(\delta\), with no common Boolean zero. Include any other generators, and let \(C(X,Y)\) be a supplied noncommutative IPS formula for the full tuple, of size \(S\) and depth \(d\). Define ordered polynomials \[ P=\prod_{i=1}^m(1-F_i),\quad H=\sum_{i=1}^m\left(\prod_{j<i}(1-F_j)\right)Y_i,\quad D=H+PC. \] Then \(D\) is a certificate of size \(O(S+(m+1)(s+m+1))\), depth at most \(\max(d,\delta+3)+2\), preserving placeholder-linearity if \(C\) has it. For every Boolean old point \(a\), the free polynomial in placeholders satisfies \(D(a,Y)=H(a,Y)\). In particular all Boolean-point coefficient values of a placeholder-linear certificate can simultaneously be made independent of the supplied certificate. No formal coefficient extraction, derivative control or exact matched-image membership is asserted.

Proof. Ordered telescoping gives \(H(X,F)=1-P\), without commutativity. Hence \(D(X,F)=1-P+P=1\) and \(D(X,0)=0\). At a Boolean point, each \(F_i(a)\) is zero or one, and some is one by the hypothesis; thus \(P(a)=0\) and the claimed equality follows even with noncommuting placeholders. Write all prefix products directly: they contain at most \(m\) copies of each input description in total across the sum. One final copy of \(C\) suffices. Complement, product and outer-sum layers give the stated bounds. If the starting polynomial is linear in placeholders, so is the result.

This is exactly the ordered iteration of the old axiom-cut operation, applied in reverse input order and then written as explicit prefix products. The earlier coefficient-cube result already made the commutative normalization's values cheap by a different fixed-clause normalization; this is a short all-input noncommutative version. Here the formal clause coefficients are not fixed exactly, so there is no contradiction with the forced derivative values of Boolean coefficients in that earlier fixed-clause form. Multiplication by \(P\) can add the sum of the input degrees; no low-PC-degree transfer is claimed.

Nonvacuity. Contradictory inputs \(x,1-x\) and \(C=Y_1+Y_2\) give \(H=Y_1+(1-x)Y_2\), with \(D(0,Y)=Y_1+Y_2\) and \(D(1,Y)=Y_1\). Adding unused variables preserves the example at unbounded ambient size. Degenerate cases. A unit generator kills the residual immediately. Without a supplied certificate the construction supplies none, and on a common Boolean zero the conclusion \(P(a)=0\) is false. Exact-depth overhead must be charged. For literal CNFs, \(H\) has polynomial size and absolute constant depth; an efficiently computable Boolean restriction of this function cannot by itself force a hard Boolean output. Formal derivatives and non-Boolean evaluations are outside that assertion. \(\square\)
