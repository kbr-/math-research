Single-input killers enlarge truncated error kernels

Question. For a proper degree-\(D\) closure \(C_D\) over \(\mathbb F_2\) and two-input errors \(E_i=(1+g_1+g_2)g_i\) with \(\deg E_i+K\le D\), is \(\ker_C=\{u\in V_K:E_iu\in C_D\}\) equal to \(\ker_B+(C_D\cap V_K)\), the support kernel of lem:domain-model-stacked-rank plus the quotient's relations, and if not, what enlarges it?

General statement. lem:two-input-kernel-enlargement: over \(\mathbb F_2\), for a proper degree-\(D\) closure containing Booleanity, inputs \(g_1,g_2\) of degree at most \(d\), \(P=1+g_1+g_2\) and \(2d+K\le D\), the kernel of either error \(Pg_i\) on \(V_K\) contains \(\ker_B+(C_D\cap V_K)+A_1+A_2\), \(A_i=\{u\in V_K:g_iu\in C_D\}\); when \(g_1g_2\) is a closure member of degree at most \(D-K\), the kernel is \(V_K\), and it strictly contains \(\ker_B+(C_D\cap V_K)\) exactly when \((C_D\cap V_K)|_S\ne V_K|_S\), \(S\) the support of \(g_1g_2\), so the support-plus-relations hypothesis fails in every such instance. The finite check check:truncated-kernel-beyond-support finds the inclusion an equality for the other families of one pigeonhole instance.

Two-input kernel enlargement

Statement. Let \(C\) be the degree-\(D\) PC closure of a system over \(\mathbb F_2\) containing the Boolean axioms, with \(1\notin C\); let \(g_1,g_2\) have degree at most \(d\ge1\), \(P=1+g_1+g_2\), \(E_i=Pg_i\), and \(2d+K\le D\). Put \(\ker_B=\{u\in V_K:E_iu\text{ vanishes on the cube}\}\) and \(A_i=\{u\in V_K:g_iu\in C\}\). Then (a) \(E_1\equiv E_2\equiv g_1g_2\) modulo Boolean consequences of degree at most \(2d\); (b) \(\ker_B+(C\cap V_K)+A_1+A_2\subseteq\{u\in V_K:E_iu\in C\}\) for \(i=1,2\); (c) if moreover \(g_1g_2\in C\) and \(\deg g_1g_2\le D-K\), then \(\{u:E_iu\in C\}=V_K\), and this strictly contains \(\ker_B+(C\cap V_K)\) exactly when \((C\cap V_K)|_S\ne V_K|_S\) for the support \(S\) of \(g_1g_2\) on the cube; in the multilinear ring a sufficient condition is \(\dim(C\cap V_K)<\dim V_K|_S\). The hypotheses \(1\notin C\) and \(d\ge1\) are not used in (a) and (b), and in (c) \(1\notin C\) follows from the condition. Here \(C\) is the degree-\(D\) PC closure in the convention of the computation below (members of degree below \(D\) multiplied by variables); that Pang's \(C_D\) is this closure is an assumption of the application.

Proof. (a) \(Pg_1=g_1+g_1^2+g_1g_2\), and \(g_1^2+g_1\) vanishes on the cube, so it is a Boolean consequence of degree at most \(2d\) by degree-nonincreasing reduction (historical lem:fieldreduction); likewise for \(g_2\). (b) A member of \(C\) times a polynomial stays in \(C\) when the intermediate degrees of the variable-by-variable multiplication stay below \(D\). For \(u\in\ker_B\), \(E_iu\) has degree at most \(2d+K\le D\) and vanishes on the cube, so it is a Boolean consequence, in \(C\). For \(r\in C\cap V_K\), multiplying by the monomials of \(E_i\) keeps the intermediate degrees at most \(K+2d-1<D\). For \(u\in A_j\), the member \(g_ju\) has degree at most \(d+K\), and multiplying it by the monomials of the other input keeps the intermediate degrees below \(2d+K\le D\), so \(g_1g_2u\in C\); by (a), \(E_iu-g_1g_2u=(g_i^2+g_i)u\) is a Boolean consequence of degree at most \(2d+K\le D\), so \(E_iu\in C\). (c) Every \(u\in V_K\) gives \(g_1g_2u\in C\), multiplying the member \(g_1g_2\) of degree at most \(D-K\) by \(u\), and then \(E_iu\in C\) as in (b). On the other side, \(E_i\) and \(g_1g_2\) are the same function by (a), so \(\ker_B=\{u:u|_S=0\}\) is the kernel of restriction to \(S\); hence \(\ker_B+(C\cap V_K)=V_K\) exactly when restriction maps \(C\cap V_K\) onto \(V_K|_S\). The sufficient condition follows from \(\dim(C\cap V_K)|_S\le\dim(C\cap V_K)\).

Pigeonhole families. For the pigeonhole system with pigeon axioms \(\prod_j(1+x_{ij})\) and hole axioms \(x_{ij}x_{i'j}\), write \(x_{ij}\) for pigeon \(i\) in hole \(j\). Single-variable inputs \(g_1=x_{ij}\), \(g_2=x_{i'j'}\) have \(d=1\). Each \(A\) contains the variables \(x_{kj}\), \(k\ne i\), for \(g_1\), since \(x_{ij}x_{kj}\) is a hole axiom, and \(x_{kj'}\), \(k\ne i'\), for \(g_2\). When the two inputs share a hole, \(j=j'\), \(g_1g_2\) is a hole axiom, and (c) applies whenever \(C\cap V_K\) is small.

The finite check

The computation. The committed script truncated_kernels.py computes the closure exactly in the multilinear ring (Booleanity built in) by echelon elimination on the monomials of degree at most \(D\), multiplying every echelon row whose leading monomial has degree below \(D\) by every variable until nothing new enters, and the three kernels on \(V_K\) by elimination over \(\mathbb F_2\); variables are indexed \(x_{3i+j}\) for pigeon \(i\), hole \(j\). Controls: the empty system gives equal sides, and \(\ker_B+(C\cap V_K)\subseteq\ker_C\) is asserted in every case. A first version had a non-canonical remainder and a closure that missed members of degree below \(D\) arising from cancelling top terms; it failed the containment assertion, and both were fixed before any result was used (commit history).

Result. From truncated_kernels.json: \(3\) pigeons in \(2\) holes have largest proper degree \(2\), below the window. With \(4\) pigeons and \(3\) holes the largest proper degree is \(D=3\) (so \(1\in C_4\)); at \(K=1\), \(\dim V_1=13\), \(C_3\cap V_1=0\), \(H=13\). The script took the first six pairs containing \(x_{00}\): \((x_{00},x_{01})\), \((x_{00},x_{02})\), \((x_{00},x_{10})\), \((x_{00},x_{11})\), \((x_{00},x_{12})\), \((x_{00},x_{20})\). Kernel codimensions are \(5,5,0,5,5,0\) against \(11\) each for the support kernel plus relations; summed \(20\) against \(66\); stacked \(9\) against \(12\). The stacked numbers depend on this arbitrary choice of families.

The trace. The pairs \((x_{00},x_{10})\) and \((x_{00},x_{20})\) share hole \(0\): \(g_1g_2\) is a hole axiom, and (c) gives codimension \(0\), with \(0<11=\dim V_1|_S\). For the other four, (b) accounts for the whole kernel: for \((x_{00},x_{01})\), \(\ker_B+A_1+A_2\) contains \(1+x_{00},1+x_{01}\) (in \(\ker_B\)) and \(x_{10},x_{20},x_{30},x_{11},x_{21},x_{31}\) (in \(A_1+A_2\) by hole axioms), eight independent elements, so it equals the computed kernel of dimension eight; for \((x_{00},x_{11})\), \(1+x_{00},1+x_{11}\) and \(x_{10},x_{20},x_{30},x_{01},x_{21},x_{31}\), again eight; the pairs with \(x_{02}\) and \(x_{12}\) are the same with hole \(2\). The stacked kernel is the intersection of the six, spanned by \(1+x_{00},x_{10},x_{20},x_{30}\), dimension \(4\), as computed.

Nonvacuity. In Pang's matched ledger: \(p=2\), \(d=t=1\), \(\Delta=1\), \(K=1\), \(D=3\), so \(\Delta+K+d=3\le D\) and \(2d+K=3\le D\); the system is unsatisfiable and \(C_3\) proper; the errors are \((1+g_1+g_2)g_i\), one fixed choice of approximation row; \(H=13\). The lemma's (c) applies to the two same-hole pairs.

Degenerate cases. The empty system: \(C\) holds only Booleanity; in (b) \(A_i\subseteq\ker_B\), since the support of \(g_1g_2\) lies in that of \(g_i\), so (b) reduces to \(\ker_B\), as the control asserts; (c) is vacuous there, since \(g_1g_2\in C\) forces \(S\) empty. Errors that are themselves members: (c). \(D\) at or above the refutation degree: the question is void.

New mathematics. lem:two-input-kernel-enlargement: in a proper truncated quotient, a two-input Pang error's kernel contains the multipliers that kill either input alone, and is everything when the inputs' product is a member, which refutes in every such instance the hypothesis that proper error kernels are support kernels plus relations; check:truncated-kernel-beyond-support finds this inclusion an equality for the four non-member families of one pigeonhole instance and traces it to hole axioms.

Selection gain. Unchanged as a theorem. Measured at the instance: proper-quotient ranks are smaller than support ranks, summed (\(20\) against \(66\)) and stacked (\(9\) against \(12\)), and the joint saving differs from the support count (\(11\) against \(54\)).
