A joint decomposition in the same quotient

Use the exact charged setup and binary quotient \(Q_d=V_d/(J_{2,D}\cap V_d)\) from the preceding entry. Each cutoff has its nondegenerate canonical form from the global \(\mu\). Let \(q_1,\ldots,q_t\) be binary old polynomials of degree at most \(\delta\ge1\), where \(t\ge1\), and let \(K\ge0\) satisfy \[ K+(t+1)\delta\le D. \tag{1} \] Keep \(\delta\) positive as an original source-cost ceiling, even for constant predicates. Set \(U=Q_K\), \(W=Q_{K+t\delta}\), and let \(E:U\to W\) be inclusion. For \(z\in\{0,1\}^t\), define \[ F_z=\prod_{j:z_j=1}q_j\prod_{j:z_j=0}(1-q_j),\qquad A_z[f]=[F_zf]. \] Write \[ Z=\operatorname{span}\{[q_Sf]:S\subseteq[t],\ f\in V_K\}\subseteq W, \qquad q_S=\prod_{j\in S}q_j. \tag{2} \]

Corollary (joint branch extension). There are pairwise orthogonal selfadjoint projections \(R_z\) on \(W\), summing to the identity, such that \(R_zE=A_z\). Consequently the projections \[ P_j=\sum_{z:z_j=1}R_z \] commute and satisfy, for every function \(a:\{0,1\}^t\to\mathbb F_2\), \[ \left(\sum_z a(z)R_z\right)E[f] =\left[\sum_z a(z)F_z f\right]. \tag{3} \] In particular every word in the \(P_j\)'s and \(I-P_j\)'s agrees on \(E(U)\) with the corresponding Boolean polynomial in the \(q_j\)'s. The ranks obey the exact identity \[ \sum_z\operatorname{rank}(R_zE) =\dim Z =\dim\sum_z\operatorname{im}A_z. \tag{4} \] The equality concerns the images of the original domain under these projections, not the dimensions of their enlarged ambient summands.

Proof: orthogonality and a paid separation. Each \(A_z\) is well-defined by bounded absorption through \(K+t\delta\). Pointwise Booleanity gives \(F_zF_{z'}=0\) for \(z\ne z'\), so their images are mutually orthogonal for the global \(\mu\). We must prove directness, since orthogonality alone does not suffice.

Suppose \(\sum_z F_zf_z\in J_{2,D}\), with all \(f_z\in V_K\). For any selected bit \(j\), multiply this relation by either \(q_j\) or \(1-q_j\). This uses degree at most \(K+(t+1)\delta\). For each term, replace its doubled bit factor using \(q_j^2=q_j\); the Boolean certificate has degree at most \(2\delta\), and the other \(t-1\) factors and \(f_z\) give the same ceiling. The result is the subrelation consisting only of branches with the chosen bit, now represented again at degree at most \(K+t\delta\). Repeat this operation for each bit, always on that reduced subrelation. At no stage do original simultaneous products exceed (1). It follows that each \(F_zf_z\in J_{2,D}\), proving directness. This is repeated use of the truncated pseudoideal, not a claim of globally closed multiplication.

Apply orthogonal branch extension to these \(2^t\) images in the nondegenerate \(W\). It gives orthogonal ambient projections fixing their own image and killing every other branch image. The literal identity \(\sum_zF_z=1\) gives \(\sum_zA_z=E\), hence \(R_zE=A_z\). Orthogonality of the projections proves their commutation and (3). For a word with repeated factors, Boolean reduction of the operators yields (3); its right side is the reduced Boolean function expression of degree at most \(t\delta\). This does not claim an unrestricted-degree PC replay of the unreduced word.

Proof: the span and rank identity. Expansion of \(F_z\) shows that each \(A_z(U)\) lies in the space (2). Conversely, \[ q_S=\sum_{z:z_j=1\text{ for all }j\in S}F_z \] holds literally, by expanding \(\prod_{j\notin S}(q_j+(1-q_j))=1\). Thus the two spans agree. Directness proved above makes the dimension of their sum the sum of their ranks. This proves (4). \(\square\)
