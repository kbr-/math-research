19d55bc9 [New mathematics] New mathematics. lem:top-coefficient-quotients: the quotient of a dividend of \(z\)-degree at most \(b+D\) by a divisor of degree \(b\) with a unit leading coefficient is a fixed linear combination of the dividend's top \(D+1\) coefficients; for sums of products of factors \(u+cz\) each such coefficient is a sum over subsets of order at most \(E+D\) of each product's \(z\)-carrying factors, \(E\) the excess of the products' \(z\)-degrees over \(b+D\); so for affine factors with constant coefficients of \(z\) and \(d=(z^2+z)^k\), over any commutative ring, the quotient is \(\Sigma\Pi\Sigma\) of size \(O((D+1)Q^{E+D}S)\) at every \(k\), polynomial when \(E+D\) is bounded, and over \(\mathbb F_2\) in the regime \(D\le1\), \(E=0\), \(h_0=\sum_{m_a=2k}U_a+\sum_{m_a=2k+1}U_a(e_{1,a}+k)\). prop:frobenius-pair-obstruction: a dividend \(G\in\mathbb F_2[y][z]\) with a Boolean point where it is \(1\) at \(z=0\) and \(z=1\) and one where its two values differ is not made divisible by \(z^2+z\) by any one product of binary affine factors; so \(\prod_{i\le n}(z+y_i)^M+z^{nM}\) plus one product never is, for every \(n\ge2\), \(M\ge1\).
