Strict degree reduction still requires a hitting monomial

Lemma (monomial compression cost). Over any field, let \(\mathcal H\) be a nonempty family of nonempty subsets of \([v]\), and write \(z_T=\prod_{i\in T}x_i\). In the Boolean function algebra, suppose a nonzero weight \(W\) and polynomials \(q_T\) satisfy \[ W(z_T-q_T)=0\quad(T\in\mathcal H),\qquad \deg q_T<|T|. \] Then every maximum-degree monomial of the unique multilinear representative of \(W\) meets every \(T\in\mathcal H\). In particular \(\deg W\ge\tau(\mathcal H)\), where \(\tau\) is the hitting number. The minimum possible degree, when the references may also be chosen, is exactly \(\tau(\mathcal H)\). Spectator variables and references mixing all variables are allowed.

Proof. Multilinearize \(W\), let its degree be \(b\), and select a degree-\(b\) monomial \(x_S\) with nonzero coefficient. If \(S\cap T=\varnothing\), the coefficient of \(x_{S\cup T}\), of degree \(b+|T|\), in the Boolean reduction of \(Wz_T\) is precisely that coefficient. Indeed a contributing monomial \(x_R\) of \(W\) must satisfy \(R\cup T=S\cup T\). It must contain \(S\), and \(|R|\le|S|\), so \(R=S\). But \(\deg(Wq_T)\le b+\deg q_T<b+|T|\), and reduction cannot raise degree. This contradicts the functional identity. Thus \(S\) is a hitting set.

Conversely, for a hitting set \(S\) of minimum size, take \(W=\prod_{i\in S}(1-x_i)\) and every \(q_T=0\). The weight is nonzero and annihilates each \(z_T\), proving equality. \(\square\)

The zero-reference minimum was already contained in the conjunction-cover filtration. The earlier independent-flat proof already isolates a highest-degree monomial against arbitrary lower-degree offsets. The new formulation records that the same single-edge argument enforces a transversal for an arbitrary overlapping family. It does not recover that earlier theorem's full offset-image entropy bound, and it is not a replacement for the stronger-scope costed PHP-domain or matching-partition results, whose additional hypotheses and proofs were reread.

A polynomial inventory with linear hitting cost

Fix any integer \(A\ge3\), put \(\beta=2^{2-A}\) and \(\alpha=1-\beta\). For every sufficiently large \(v\), let \(d=\lceil\log_2v\rceil\). There is a family \(\mathcal H_v\) of at most \(v^A\) distinct \(d\)-subsets with \(\tau(\mathcal H_v)>\lfloor\alpha v\rfloor\). Here is a complete existence proof, not a sampled experiment. Draw \(v^A\) independent uniform \(d\)-subsets, allowing repetition. For a fixed set \(S\) of size \(\lfloor\alpha v\rfloor\), one draw avoids it with probability at least \[ \frac{\binom{v-|S|}{d}}{\binom vd} \ge\beta^d\left(1-\frac{d-1}{\beta v}\right)^d \ge\frac{\beta}{2}v^{2-A} \] for all sufficiently large \(v\). Indeed \(\beta^d\ge\beta v^{2-A}\), and Bernoulli's inequality applies once \(d(d-1)/(\beta v)\le1/2\). The probability that \(S\) hits every draw is at most \(\exp(-\beta v^2/2)\). A union bound over at most \(2^v\) sets has total at most \(\exp(v\log2-\beta v^2/2)<1\). Smaller hitting sets extend to one of this size. A successful family therefore has no such hitting set, and deleting repeated edges changes no hitting set. The threshold depends on fixed \(A\); no uniform assertion for growing \(A\) or efficient deterministic construction is made. At \(A=3\), the hitting number exceeds \(\lfloor v/2\rfloor\).

Complete bottom blocks do not evade the compression bound

Accuracy \(h\) means \(P=\prod_{u=1}^h(1-\sum_i r_{u,i}g_i)\), with every companion \(g_iP\) and every fresh coefficient equation \(r_{u,i}^2-r_{u,i}\) retained. Original total degree counts the coefficient variables.

Proposition (compression cleanup separation). Over \(\mathbb F_2\), fix an integer \(A\ge3\), take the preceding family with \(\alpha=1-2^{2-A}\), and common accuracy \(h\ge d\). For each \(T\in\mathcal H_v\), introduce a complete bottom ENS block on the old affine tuple \((1-x_i)_{i\in T}\), with whole product \(P_T\). Let one complete parent block read the tuple of all these products. For any legal constant choices of the bottom rows and any nonzero Boolean weight \(W\), if \[ W(1-x_i)P_T=0\quad(i\in T),\qquad W(P_T-q_T)=0,\quad\deg q_T<d\quad(T\in\mathcal H_v), \] then \(\deg W>\lfloor\alpha v\rfloor\). Nevertheless there are one coherent constant assignment to the full source and a nonzero degree-\(d\) weight that annihilate every specialized bottom and parent companion. The weighted identities have Boolean certificates through degree at most \(3d\).

Proof of the obstruction. Fix a Boolean point where \(W\ne0\). If any \(x_i=0\) with \(i\in T\), its complete companion forces \(P_T=0\). If all \(x_i=1\), every bottom input is zero, so every factor is one and \(P_T=1\), irrespective of the selected coefficients. Therefore \(WP_T=Wz_T\) as Boolean functions for every \(T\). The reference agreements imply \(W(z_T-q_T)=0\), and the lemma gives the required degree bound. This argument quantifies over all legal constant bottom assignments, not one adverse prefix. It uses neither the parent rows nor \(h\ge d\); that inequality enables the cleanup assignment below. Characteristic two specifies the intended source setting, rather than a requirement of these Boolean-algebra identities.

Proof of the direct cleanup. In every bottom block select each input once in the first \(d\) rows and zero-pad the rest; thus \(P_T=z_T\). Choose one edge \(T_0\), put \(W=z_{T_0}\), and select that input in the first parent row, zeroing the others. The parent product is \(1-z_{T_0}\). Bottom companions obey \[ (1-x_i)z_T=-(x_i^2-x_i)\prod_{j\in T\setminus\{i\}}x_j, \] through degree \(d+1\); multiplying by \(W\) costs at most \(2d+1\). Parent weighted companions are \[ Wz_T(1-z_{T_0})=-z_T(z_{T_0}^2-z_{T_0}), \] with Boolean certificates through \(3d\). The weight is a nonzero multilinear monomial of degree \(d\). Every coefficient-domain equation specializes to zero. \(\square\)

The source has at most \(v^A\) bottom blocks and one parent, each bottom with \(d\) literal inputs. It has a constant-depth OR/negation realization: bottom clauses on negated literals, followed by a parent reading their negations. This is polynomial inventory and polynomial written Boolean-formula size, not a claim that expanding every ENS polynomial into monomials has polynomial size. No essential occurrence in a hypothetical hard proof is asserted. Taking \(h=d\) gives original bottom companion degree \(2d+1\) and original parent companion degree \(d(2d+1)+2d\). These charges remain in any proof replay; the smaller specialized identities do not replace them.

For any fixed linear degree budget \(\gamma v\) with \(\gamma<1\), choosing fixed \(A\) so \(1-2^{2-A}>\gamma\) defeats automatic Boolean-only compression within that budget. This is the relevant all-polynomial-inventory quantifier, not an exponent varying with \(v\).

The positive control is the familiar distinction between annihilating actual errors and reconstructing all inputs at lower degree. The historical one-block elimination already removes the remaining parent with an additive input-degree charge after exact bottom specialization. We are not presenting this one-parent system as a new hard proof family.

What changes when old relations are allowed

The negative statements concern the full Boolean function algebra, or comparisons justified by Boolean identities alone. They do not imply the same lower bound modulo an arbitrary proper old pseudoideal. The existing relative-learning control already demonstrates why: after relabelling its satisfiable old relation as \(xy-z=0\), the nonzero weight one makes \(xy\) agree with the affine reference \(z\) in the old quotient. The single-edge hypergraph has hitting number one, but no positive-degree weight is needed there. Thus old relations can remove the very top term used by the Boolean proof.

The earlier PHP-relative theorem gets a genuine old-base conclusion only with its explicit signed-cube, weight and reduction-excess budgets. Its matching-domain successor has a different partition hypothesis and degree bound. Neither licenses silently applying this new unrestricted-weight Boolean argument in the charged quotient. A successful use of the branch lemma beyond the chain class may need such old-system structure; a common branch is still only sufficient.
