General statement. prop:top-degree-native-count: for the product pigeonhole system at its top degree \(D=n\), over any field, and an error of degree \(e\ge1\) whose support satisfies the independence hypothesis of lem:top-degree-killer-transfer, the least number of old functionals that detect the error's kernel on \(V_K\) in rectangle forms at slack \(L\), with \(e+K+L\le n\), is the same modulo the proper closure as modulo the domain model; for a monomial error on distinct holes it lies between \(\lceil N(K)/N(L)\rceil\) and \(N(\max(K-L,0))\), \(N(d)=\sum_{k\le d}\binom{n-e}km^k\) for \(m\) pigeons, at every \(D\) with \(e+K+L\le D\le n\); at five pigeons, four holes, \(E=x_{00}\), \(K=2\), \(L=1\) it is \(6\).

Top-degree native count

Setting. Over a field \(k\), the product pigeonhole system with \(m\) pigeons and \(n\) holes has variables \(x_{ih}\), the Boolean axioms, the hole axioms \(x_{ih}x_{i'h}\) (\(i\ne i'\)) and the pigeon axioms \(Q_i=\prod_h(1-x_{ih})\) of degree \(n\). \(C\) is its degree-\(D\) closure and \(H\) the degree-\(D\) closure of the Boolean and hole axioms. \(Y\) is the set of hole-respecting assignments, each hole empty or holding one pigeon, a product domain \(\{0,1,\dots,m\}^n\). An old functional modulo a degree-\(D\) closure \(J\) is a linear functional on \(V_D\) vanishing on \(J\). Following lem:native-detection-count, for an error \(E\) of degree \(e\), \(U=V_K\) and slack \(L\) with \(e+K+L\le D\), old functionals \(\mu_1,\dots,\mu_c\) detect \(\ker T\), \(T(u)=[Eu]\), when \(\ker T\) is the set of \(u\in V_K\) with \(\mu_j(Euv)=0\) for all \(j\) and \(v\in V_L\); \(m_{\min}(J)\) is the least such number. \(S_E\subseteq Y\) is the support of \(E\) on \(Y\).

Proposition (top-degree native count). (a) At \(D=n\), \(1\notin C\); and if \(e\ge1\), the unplaced-pigeon indicators \(Q_1,\dots,Q_m\) restricted to \(Y\setminus S_E\) are linearly independent, and \(e+K+L\le n\) (so \(K+e\le n\)), then \(\ker T\) is \(\{u:u|_{S_E}=0\}\) modulo \(C\) and modulo \(H\), and the families of rectangle forms \(u,v\mapsto\mu(Euv)\) given by old functionals modulo \(C\) are exactly those given by old functionals modulo \(H\); hence \(m_{\min}(C)=m_{\min}(H)\). (b) If \(E\) is a monomial of degree \(e\ge1\) on \(e\) distinct holes, then at every \(D\) with \(e+K+L\le D\le n\), with \(N(d)=\sum_{k\le d}\binom{n-e}km^k\), \(\lceil N(K)/N(L)\rceil\le m_{\min}(C)\le N(\max(K-L,0))\). (c) At \(m=5\), \(n=4\), \(E=x_{00}\), \(K=2\), \(L=1\), \(D=4\), \(m_{\min}(C)=6\) over every field.

Upper bound. For each \(y\in S_E\) with a set \(F_y\) of at most \(\max(K-L,0)\) nonempty free holes, let \(B_y\) be the points of \(S_E\) that agree with \(y\) on \(F_y\) and have at most \(L\) nonempty holes in \(F\setminus F_y\), and \(\nu_y=\sum_{z\in B_y}\delta_z\), extended to an old functional as above. A point with \(s\le K\) nonempty free holes lies in \(B_y\) for \(y\) its restriction to \(\min(s,\max(K-L,0))\) of them, so the \(B_y\) cover the determining set of degree-\(K\) functions, and the forms vanishing gives \(u|_{S_E}=0\). The number of centres \(y\) is \(N(\max(K-L,0))\).
(c) By (b), \(m_{\min}\ge\lceil91/16\rceil=6\). Structural reason. At the top degree the pigeon axioms are \(m\) functionals' worth of constraints, the unplaced indicators, and when those stay independent off the error's support, the points there absorb them; the rectangle forms of an error never look off its support, so for such errors the proper closure costs a native certificate nothing over the domain model. The bound in (b) is the cube's, with two states per coordinate replaced by \(m+1\) per free hole; and at \(K=2\), \(L=1\) the count is a decomposition of the complete multipartite graph of quadratic coefficients into pieces whose star sums are injective, as on the cube.
