Native functional growth

Statement. Over \(\mathbb F_2\), let \(J_{D'}\) be the degree-\(D'\) part of the Boolean ideal in \(n\) variables, a family of linear forms \(g_1,\ldots,g_s\) with approximator \(P=\prod_{k=1}^t(1+q_k)\), \(q_k\in\operatorname{span}\{g_i\}\) (kept to match the family's form; the proof works for any linear \(q_k\)), errors \(E_i=Pg_i\) (the form of Pang's approximators and errors, as the marginal-refinement entry read them from his Section 4 and lem:marginal-refinement-gain states them), \(U=V_K\), and slack \(L\) with \(t+1+K+L\le D'\). If \(g_1\notin\operatorname{span}\{q_1,\ldots,q_t\}\), then the stacked rank \(r\) of \(u\mapsto([E_iu])_i\) is at least \(N_{n-t-1}(K)\), and every \(m\) old functionals whose rectangle forms \(\mu_j(E_iuv)\), \(v\in V_L\), detect the stacked kernel satisfy \(m\ge N_{n-t-1}(K)/(s\,N_n(L))\). Since detection at slack \(L\) implies it at every larger admissible slack, the bound is to be read at the largest admissible \(L\); for \(d=1\) at the least degree Pang's simulation allows, \(D=K+\max(2t+2,\,t+w)\), \(\Delta=t\), that is \(L_{\max}=\max(t+1,\,w-1)\) (a larger \(D\) raises it), and for fixed \(s\), \(t\), \(K\) the bound grows with \(n\) exactly when \(K>L_{\max}\).

Proof. On the cube \(E_1\) is the indicator of \(A=\{q_1=\cdots=q_t=0,\ g_1=1\}\), nonempty and affine of dimension at least \(n-t-1\) because \(g_1\) lies outside the span of the \(q_k\). Within degree, membership in \(J_{D'}\) is vanishing on the cube, so \(E_1u\in J\) exactly when \(u\) vanishes on \(A\), and the rank of \(u\mapsto[E_1u]\) on \(V_K\) is the dimension of the restrictions of \(V_K\) to \(A\). An affine change of coordinates of \(\mathbb F_2^n\) making \(A\) a coordinate subcube preserves degree, and restriction of degree-\(\le K\) polynomials to a subcube of dimension \(a\ge n-t-1\) gives all its functions of degree at most \(K\), of dimension \(N_a(K)\ge N_{n-t-1}(K)\). The stacked rank is at least this one error's rank. By lem:native-detection-count (a), detection by \(m\) functionals at slack \(L\) forces \(r\le m\dim Z_L\le m\,s\,N_n(L)\). If detection holds at slack \(L\), it holds at every admissible \(L'\ge L\): \(V_L\subseteq V_{L'}\) gives \(\ker\Phi_{L'}\subseteq\ker\Phi_L\), and \(C\subseteq\ker\Phi_{L'}\) by lem:native-detection-count (a). For \(d=1\), \(\Delta=t\) and \(L=D-\Delta-d-K\) at \(D=K+\max(2t+2,t+w)\) give \(L_{\max}=\max(t+1,w-1)\); the ratio \(N_{n-t-1}(K)/N_n(L)\) grows like \(n^{K-L}\).

Hypotheses, each with its step. The cube domain model is used for membership as vanishing and for the rank as a restriction count; \(\mathbb F_2\) and linear inputs are used in the one step that makes the support of \(E_1\) an affine subspace, the factor \(1+q_k\) being Pang's \(1-q_k^{p-1}\) at \(p=2\); \(g_1\) outside the span of the \(q_k\) makes \(A\) nonempty of dimension at least \(n-t-1\); \(t+1+K+L\le D'\) is lem:native-detection-count's window for errors of degree \(t+1\). Over any field with the Boolean axioms in \(J\), \(r\ge\dim V_K|_{\operatorname{supp}E_1}\) still holds; only the affine count needs \(\mathbb F_2\). The conclusion concerns detection of the stacked kernel, with no protected class.
