Correction to the flat-shadow threshold

Corollary. In the earlier flat-shadow proposition, the equivalence in equation (1), for \(r>m\) and \(q=2m-r\ge K\), must read \[q\le 2K-2\delta+1+(K-\delta+1)/\delta.\] Its right side was too large by \(\delta\). The graded cap and complementary-flat control remain valid; the \(r\le m\), \(K<m/4\) exclusion remains valid.

Proof and control. Rearranging \((X-K+\delta)/(K-\delta+1)\le1+1/\delta\) gives exactly the corrected right side as an upper bound on \(X\). When \(q\ge K\), the degree-\(K\) cap forces \(X\ge q\), and \(X=q\) suffices for all caps. For \(r\le m\), the cap still forces \(X\ge m\), while the corrected upper bound is at most \(3K-1<m\). The old iff fails at \(K=2,\delta=1,m=9,r=12,q=6\): the old right side is six, but the correct one is five and the minimal \(X=6\) gives \(\rho=5/2>2\). The pair-counting polynomial \((1+z)^6\) is realized by six single-pivot and three double-pivot pairs. This is a control for the majorant's threshold, not a charged hard instance. \(\square\)
