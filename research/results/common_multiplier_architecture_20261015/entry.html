Curve locator budget

Statement. Let \(X/k\) be a smooth projective geometrically integral curve of genus \(g\), \(A\) a divisor, and \(E\) an effective divisor of degree \(e\). If \(\deg A\ge e+g\), then \(L(A-E)\ne0\). If a rational point \(P\) lies outside the supports of \(A,E\) and \(\deg A\ge e+2g\), there is \(f\in L(A-E)\) with \(f(P)=1\).

Proof. Riemann–Roch gives \(\ell(A-E)\ge\deg A-e+1-g\ge1\). Under the second bound, the dual divisor \(K_X-(A-E-P)\) has degree at most \(-1\), hence no nonzero section. Duality gives \(H^1(A-E-P)=0\); the point-evaluation exact sequence makes \(L(A-E)\to k\) surjective. This is a standard consequence of Riemann–Roch and the negative-degree section criterion, not a new curve theorem. Vanishing along \(E\) is vanishing as a section of \(\mathcal O(A)\); the representing rational function vanishes there when the supports of \(A,E\) are disjoint. Evaluation at the stipulated \(P\) supplies protected survival.

Nonvacuity. On the projective line over any field with distinct finite points \(\alpha_1,\ldots,\alpha_e,P\), take \(A=e\infty\), \(E=\sum_i\alpha_i\); the product \(\prod_i(x-\alpha_i)\), divided by its nonzero value at \(P\), realizes the bound. There are examples for every \(e\) over an infinite field, and over finite fields large enough to contain the named distinct points. For \(e=0,g=0\), the constant one works. At higher genus the stated degree bounds are sufficient, not asserted minimal. This is a function/section theorem; it does not identify a bounded PC quotient with a curve.

Repair cost controls

Native rectangle. For every \(n\ge3\), over the Boolean-only degree-four consequence space in \(\mathbb F_2[x_1,\ldots,x_n]\), take \(K=2,d=t=1\), family \((x_1,x_2)\), and any \(q\in\operatorname{span}(x_1,x_2)\). Let \(P=1+q\) and \(T:f\mapsto([Px_1f],[Px_2f])\) on multilinear \(U=V_2\) modulo Booleanity. No single scalar native rectangle \(B(f,h)=\mu(Efh)\), with \(E\) a scalar combination of the two written errors, full left space \(U\), full right space of degree at most \(L\), and reserve \(\deg E+2+L\le4\), has radical on the left equal to \(\ker T\).

Proof. If \(q\ne0\), the three possibilities are \(x_1,x_2,x_1+x_2\). A nonzero error component takes value one respectively at \((0,1),(1,0),(1,1)\). Varying the spectator \(x_3\) proves that its products by \(1,x_3\) are independent modulo Booleanity, so \(\operatorname{rank}T\ge2\). Every nonzero written error combination equals \((1+q)(a x_1+b x_2)\), of degree two, even over a scalar extension. Thus \(L=0\), and a single scalar rectangle has rank at most one. A zero combination has rank zero. If \(q=0\), its nonzero combinations have degree one and \(L\le1\), so rectangle rank is at most \(n+1\). A degree-two Boolean function killed by both \(x_1,x_2\) is a scalar multiple of \((1+x_1)(1+x_2)\): its function support lies at \(x_1=x_2=0\), and multiplying any nonconstant spectator term by that indicator has degree at least three. Hence \(\operatorname{rank}T=\dim U-1=n(n+1)/2>n+1\). Equal left kernels would require equal ranks, a contradiction. The original Pang selection window \(1+2+1=4\) holds. The result concerns the displayed single rectangle, not several functionals, a changed left domain, or arbitrary constrained pairings.

Artin–Rees delay. For every \(m\ge1\), take \(R=k[t]\), \(I=(t)\), \(M=R\), \(N=(t^m)\). The Artin–Rees equality \(I^nM\cap N=I^{n-c}(I^cM\cap N)\) needs \(c\ge m\): at \(n=m\) and \(c<m\), the left ideal is \((t^m)\) while the right is \((t^{2m-c})\). Conversely \(c=m\) works for every \(n\ge m\). Thus the theorem's eventual stabilization has no presentation-independent constant delay.

Observability horizon. On \(k^m\), let \(Ae_i=e_{i-1}\) for \(i>1\), \(Ae_1=0\), and \(C=e_1^*\). The first \(h\le m\) observations \(C,CA,\ldots,CA^{h-1}\) are the first \(h\) coordinate rows. Their common kernel has dimension \(m-h\), and it becomes zero only at \(h=m\). This is the sharp elementary shift control for the observability horizon; stabilization alone does not give a short degree budget.

Degenerate cases. The rectangle assertion excludes \(n<3\); its rank comparison uses a spectator. The zero error combination never increases rank. Boolean-only quotients are proper and contain the actual products through degree four. Artin–Rees at \(m=0\) has zero delay and is excluded only for a positive-delay family. Observability at \(m=1\) is settled by one observation. None of these controls refutes common multipliers themselves: for instance \(q=x_1,f=x_1\) is a surviving multiplier for the rectangle example.
