# H05 dependency and scope

Prove reduced exactness of a simplex boundary below its top degree, reusing
H04 contraction. For a nonempty finite vertex set T of cardinal b, the boundary
consists of proper subsets S of T. At cell count k the safe range is k+1<b,
which corresponds to reduced degree k-1≤b-3. The top degree b-2 is excluded.
For b=1 the safe range is empty; for b=2 only augmentation is covered.
A full-simplex cone filler has k+1 cells, hence remains a proper face in this
range. No connectivity of the top sphere, or empty-face-only exactness, is claimed.
