Fix a binary prefactor \(A\) of degree at most \(a\), an allowed binary linear space \(U\subseteq\mathbb F_2[x]_{\le K}\), and inputs \(g_1,\ldots,g_r\) of degree at most \(\delta\ge1\). Earlier obligations, if any, are encoded by this fixed \(U\); no larger domain is silently substituted. If \(S=0\) there is no surviving domain and the intended lower-bound use is unavailable; assume \(S\ne0\). Define maps into \(Y=Q_L^r\) by \[ Tf=([g_if])_i,\qquad M_jf=([g_ig_jf])_i, \qquad C=\operatorname{im}T,\quad c=\dim C. \] For a legal row \(t\in\mathbb F_2^r\), write \(q=\sum_jt_jg_j\), \(M_q=\sum_jt_jM_j\) and \(T_q=T-M_q\). These are the original current, multiplied and new companion maps on exactly \(S\). Original source/replay ceilings remain separate from (1).

The exact identity

Lemma (dual escape identity). Under these hypotheses let \(\pi:Y\to Y/C\) be the quotient map and put \[ r_q=\operatorname{rank}M_q,\quad e_q=\operatorname{rank}(\pi M_q), \quad c_q=\operatorname{rank}T_q. \] Then, for every legal binary row, \[ c_q=c-r_q+e_q =c-\operatorname{rank}M_q^\dagger +\operatorname{rank}\bigl(M_q^\dagger|_{\ker T^\dagger}\bigr). \tag{2} \] For formal independent variables \(\lambda_j\), scalar extension to \(\mathbb L(\lambda)\) also gives \[ \operatorname{rank}(\pi M(\lambda)) =\operatorname{rank}\bigl(M(\lambda)^\dagger|_{\ker T^\dagger}\bigr), \quad M(\lambda)=\sum_j\lambda_jM_j. \tag{3} \] Both specialized and generic escape ranks are therefore independent of the permissible nonsingular moment choices. Formula (2)'s first equality only needs the forward window \(s+3\delta\le D\); the extra reserve in (1) supplies the common-moment realization.

Proof: primal loss. Bounded absorption gives \(\ker T\subseteq\ker M_q\) and hence \(\ker T\subseteq\ker T_q\). We claim \[ T(\ker T_q)=C\cap\operatorname{im}M_q. \] One inclusion is immediate: \(T_q f=0\) means \(Tf=M_qf\). Conversely, suppose \(Tf=M_qh\), with \(f,h\in S\). In every coordinate \(g_if-qg_ih\in J\), with degree at most \(s+2\delta\). Multiply this relation by \(q\), legal through \(s+3\delta\). The Boolean identity \(q^2-q\), multiplied by \(g_i h\), has the same paid degree, giving \(qg_if=qg_ih\) in the quotient. Combine with \(g_if=qg_ih\) to obtain \(T_q f=0\). Thus the claim holds. Rank-nullity on the two kernels gives \[ c-c_q=\dim T(\ker T_q) =\dim(C\cap\operatorname{im}M_q)=r_q-e_q. \] This includes \(c=0\), when all the maps vanish by absorption.

Proof: dual loss. Under the nondegenerate form on \(Y\), the dual of \(Y/C\) identifies with \(C^\perp=\ker T^\dagger\). The dual of \(\pi M_q\) is therefore \(M_q^\dagger\) restricted to that subspace. A linear map and its dual have equal rank, which proves \(e_q=\operatorname{rank}(M_q^\dagger|_{\ker T^\dagger})\); also \(r_q=\operatorname{rank}M_q^\dagger\). The same reasoning over the rational-function field proves (3). Neither \(C\) nor any reverse kernel needs a nondegenerate restricted form or a multiplication-module structure. \(\square\)

What happens to the proposed improvement

Averaging (2) over the same shared binary row gives the exact identity \[ \mathbb E c_q=c-\mathbb E r_q+\mathbb E e_q. \] The full partial-image proof gives \(\mathbb E r_q\ge c/2\), and specialization gives \(e_q\le e\), where \(e\) is (3)'s generic rank. These reproduce \(\mathbb E c_q\le\min(c,c/2+e)\). The adjoint representation changes neither expectation. One could improve the bound by proving a smaller actual average escape or a larger actual multiplied rank for the intended sources, but the new form does not prove either. A shared weighted objective uses the same coefficients across contexts; choosing a different best row per context is still invalid.

The recorded relative-intersection family has \(c=21t,e=14t\), and residual rank \(18t\) for every nonzero row. It forbids the universal improvement \((c+e)/2\). It can be placed in its full Boolean algebra at any larger \(D\), so the stronger pairing reserve (1) does not evade the example. Equation (2) shows that every permissible common form retains its escape loss. This is an analytic application of the proved all-parameter control, not a rerun of its finite search or a claim that its multiplier space is forced by a hard proof.

At the opposite extreme, for a single Boolean input choose its coefficient one. Then \(M_q=T\), since \(g^2=g\) through the paid window; thus \(r_q=c,e_q=0,c_q=0\). The dual gives exactly the same cheap repair. The existing prefix plateau also remains: several useful rows can precede the first rank drop. Consequently a one-row decrease is not a mandatory condition for a useful batch. No larger numerical instances are needed to decide this representation question.
