The actual update, with its paid window

Specialize the partial-image setup to characteristic two. Let \(A\) have degree at most \(a\), let binary-valued inputs \(g_i\) have degree at most \(\delta\ge1\) and certified \(g_i^2-g_i\in J_D\), and let \(U\subseteq V_K\) be the common allowed multiplier subspace. Require \(a+K+3\delta\le D\). Earlier errors may define \(U\); no invariant basis or full multiplication closure is assumed. Put \[Tf=([Ag_if])_i,\quad C=T(U),\quad M_qf=([Ag_iqf])_i,\quad q=\sum_jt_jg_j,\quad t_j\in\mathbb F_2.\] The updated map is literally \(T'_q=T-M_q\), on the same \(U\), and its old kernel is contained in its new one.

The partial-image proof gives a lift into \(\ker T'_q\) for every vector in \(C\cap\operatorname{im}M_q\). Conversely, if \(r\in\ker T'_q\), then \(Tr=M_qr\), so \(Tr\) lies in that intersection. Its kernel as a map from \(\ker T'_q\) is exactly \(\ker T\). Hence the existing proof in the binary case identifies \[\ker T'_q/\ker T\ \simeq\ C\cap\operatorname{im}M_q, \qquad \dim\ker T'_q-\dim\ker T =\operatorname{rank}M_q-\operatorname{rank}(\pi M_q),\] where \(\pi\) is projection modulo \(C\). For completeness the lift is degree-paid: if \(Tr=M_qf\), multiply each relation \(Ag_i(r-qf)\in J_D\) by \(1-q\), and use \(Ag_if(q-q^2)\in J_D\). Both fit \(a+K+3\delta\). This is the very argument of the cited proof, with its immediate converse, not a new contraction mechanism.
