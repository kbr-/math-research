Local fiber argument

Charge fibers. Work temporarily over \(\mathbb F_4\), with ternary edge-label characters. On \(d\) star edges an oriented charge fiber is an affine hyperplane with normal vector nonzero on all \(d\) coordinates. Distinct character monomials of support at most \(t\), with \(2t<d\), stay distinct on that hyperplane: a difference in its one-dimensional normal space would have full support \(d\), whereas their difference has support at most \(2t\). Restricted distinct characters are linearly independent, by character orthogonality on the ternary affine hyperplane; its order is odd and hence invertible in characteristic two. The indicator-to-character basis change preserves local degree. Therefore restriction to each individual charge fiber is injective through degree \(t\), over \(\mathbb F_4\) and then over \(\mathbb F_2\). The charge-satisfied fiber and the union of the other two fibers both have this property.

Fix a value \(\xi_i\in\mathbb F_2\) of \(g_i\). On each fixed state of the private edge, the condition \(g_i=\xi_i\) permits either the satisfied charge fiber or the two violating fibers, so at least one individual charge fiber. Write a normal polynomial on the enlarged block as \(p_0+x_{e_i,1}p_1+x_{e_i,2}p_2\). If it vanishes on \(g_i=\xi_i\), the private zero state and charge-fiber injectivity force \(p_0=0\); the two nonzero states then force \(p_1=p_2=0\). All coefficients have degree at most the original polynomial degree. Thus both \(g_i\)-fibers are injective through every \(t\) with \(2t<d_i\).

Set \(s=\lfloor\Delta/8\rfloor\), \(t=s-1\). Since \(d_i\ge\Delta/4\), \(2t<d_i\). For every \(C\) with \(h<q\), choose a nonzero \(\xi\in\ker C\), and an index with \(\xi_i=1\). On the nonempty product fiber \(g=\xi\), \(P_C=1\) and that error coordinate equals one. Tensor the just-proved degree-\(t\) block injections and the full unused-edge spaces. The error map is injective on their total-degree-\(K\) subspace, giving \[ \operatorname{rank}E_C(V_K)\ge [u^{\le K}](1+2u)^{m-\sum_i(d_i+1)} \prod_i\left(\sum_{j=0}^{t}2^j\binom{d_i+1}{j}u^j\right). \] This is the same fixed-fiber mechanism as the majority row floor, with a different local fiber proof; it is not a majority identity.

Among normal monomials through degree \(K\), the support is uniform conditional on its size. A specified \(s\)-set is included with probability at most \((K/m)^s\). A union bound over blocks and their \(s\)-subsets bounds the omitted fraction by \(q\binom{2\Delta+1}{s}(K/m)^s\). Eventually \(\Delta\ge64\), \((2\Delta+1)/s\le20\), \(e<3\), and \(K/m\le1/64\). Thus \(\binom{2\Delta+1}{s}(K/m)^s\le(15/16)^s\), proving the local bound and its limit. No point-density inference is used.
