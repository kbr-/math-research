Exact counts after fixing edges

Use the full setup of defect slice design: \(n=2^m-1\), \(k=2^r\), \(1\le r<m\), ternary total charge \(\sigma\ne0\), \(\lambda=\sigma/k\), \(\max(4,2\Delta)\le D<\varepsilon n/8\), and \(2D/\varepsilon<k\). Let \(F\subseteq E\) be the fixed edge set, \(t=|F|\), with labels \(a\in F_3^F\). Put \(H=(V,E\setminus F)\), with \(c(H)\) components, and residual charges \[b_v=c_v-\sum_{e\in F}\partial_{v,e}a_e.\] Let \(L_{F,a}\) be its one-hot pin indicator of degree \(t\). Write \(\Omega_k\) for the disjoint union of corrected-flow spaces in the preceding construction.

Theorem (conditional slice count). For arbitrary \(F,a\), the number of points of \(\Omega_k\) extending the pins is \[N(F,a)=3^{|E\setminus F|-n+c(H)}\, \#\{S\subseteq V:|S|=k,\ \lambda|S\cap C|=\sum_{v\in C}b_v\text{ in }F_3\text{ for every component }C\}.\tag{1}\] If \(H\) is connected, every \(S\) is compatible, every pin assignment has odd fiber size, and its probability under the uniform real slice-flow law is \(3^{-t}\).

If \(t\le D\), there is one component containing more than half the vertices. Let \(Z\) be the union of all other components. Then \(|Z|\le t/\varepsilon<k\), and \[\mu(L_{F,a})=N(F,a)\bmod2 =\mathbf1\left[\sum_{v\in C}b_v=0\text{ for every component }C\subseteq Z\right].\tag{2}\] When (2) is one and \(d=D-t\ge\max(2,\Delta)\), the space \[J^{a}_d=\{f\in F_2[x_{E\setminus F}]_{\le d}:L_{F,a}f\in J_{2,D}\}\tag{3}\] is a proper degree-\(d\) pseudoideal containing every restricted old one-hot generator. The functional \(f\mapsto\mu(L_{F,a}f)\) is normalized and annihilates (3). Thus the restricted old system has no PC refutation through \(d\). This assertion pays the conditioning degree \(t\), even when the real fiber is large.

Proof of (1). For a fixed \(S\), the remaining edge labels solve \[\operatorname{div}_H y=b-\lambda\mathbf1_S.\] Incidence image on each connected component consists exactly of vectors of total zero. Thus the displayed compatibility constraints are necessary and sufficient. In the compatible case, rank-nullity gives exactly \(3^{|E\setminus F|-n+c(H)}\) solutions, independently of \(S\) and the pin values. Sum over the disjoint defect supports. If \(H\) is connected, its sole sum condition follows from \(\sum b_v=\sigma=\lambda k\). Dividing the count by \(|\Omega_k|=\binom nk3^{|E|-n+1}\) gives \(3^{-t}\), and the fiber size is odd.

Proof of (2). If all components of \(H\) had size at most \(n/2\), summing their expansion boundaries would give \(\varepsilon n\le2t\le2D\), impossible. Thus the large component exists and is unique. Its complement \(Z\) has size below \(n/2\), and its boundary lies in \(F\), giving \(\varepsilon|Z|\le t\). Compatibility for the large component follows from the smaller ones and the total charge. The indicator of the remaining constraints is therefore a Boolean function \(A(z_Z)\) of at most \(|Z|<k\) defect bits, of multilinear degree at most \(|Z|\) over \(F_2\). The binary-slice identity gives \[\sum_{|S|=k}A(\mathbf1_{S\cap Z})=A(0),\] which is exactly the right side of (2). The power of three in (1) is odd. This proves the parity formula, including incompatible empty fibers.

More generally, when \(A(0)=1\), the characteristic-two conditional sum retains the zero-defect moments of any Boolean polynomial \(Q\) with \(\deg Q+|Z|<k\): apply the same seed to \(AQ\). This is a defect-indicator statement; it is not an uncharged degree statement for arbitrary edge polynomials.

Proof of (3). The old pseudoideal is linear and absorbs multiplication up to its degree ceiling. Since \(\deg L_{F,a}=t\), (3) is linear and closed under multiplying members of degree at most \(d-1\) by a remaining variable. Its unit is absent: otherwise \(L_{F,a}\in J_{2,D}\), contradicting \(\mu(L_{F,a})=1\).

Remaining-edge row, exclusion and Boolean generators multiplied by \(L_{F,a}\) are old generator multiples through \(D\). Fixed-edge domain generators specialize to zero. For an original forbidden vertex monomial \(g\), an inconsistent pinned label makes its restriction zero. Otherwise write \(g=g_{\rm pin}g_{\rm rem}\). Multiply this old generator by the pin indicator on fixed edges not in its star. The resulting polynomial is literally \(L_{F,a}g_{\rm rem}\), with degree \(t+\deg g_{\rm rem}\le D\); no duplicate pinned factor is inserted. Thus the restricted vertex generator lies in (3). The stated lower bound on \(d\) ensures all residual old generators are included. Proper pseudoideal closure excludes a degree-\(d\) PC refutation. Finally normalized annihilation by \(\mu(L_{F,a}\,\cdot)\) follows directly from its definition and old annihilation. This proves the claim without imposing a smaller ordinary NS certificate degree. \(\square\)

The connected-complement statement holds even far beyond \(t\le D\); the proper old-window conclusion does not. Nor is the scalar conditional functional asserted to have kernel exactly (3). The parity test in the small window is the old conjunction compatibility criterion expressed by the actual slice-flow counts.

Many defects still obstruct typical independent thinning

Proposition (many-defect survival). In the same slice-flow law, independently retain each edge live with probability \(p\in[0,1]\). If \(G\) has maximum degree \(\Delta\), then the probability that the resulting restriction creates no fully fixed wrong-charge vertex is at most \[\left(1-(1-p)^\Delta\right)^{\lceil k/(\Delta+1)\rceil}.\tag{4}\] For fixed \(\Delta\) and \(p\) bounded away from one, this is exponentially small in \(k\). In contrast, fixing all edges outside any spanning tree always gives an odd slice-flow fiber for every label assignment, while the residual one-hot Tseitin system has a PC refutation through \(\max(2,\Delta)\). Thus odd fiber mass alone does not retain old hardness.

Proof of (4). Condition on any defect set \(S\) and any flow point drawn from its affine space. Exactly the vertices of \(S\) have incorrect original charges, by the nonzero correction \(\lambda\). Choose an independent subset \(I\subseteq S\) of size at least \(\lceil k/(\Delta+1)\rceil\), using the maximum-degree greedy bound on the induced graph. Incident edge sets of vertices in \(I\) are disjoint. Consequently their events of having no live incident edge are independent under the Bernoulli live-set law, each with probability \((1-p)^{\deg(v)}\ge(1-p)^\Delta\). Such a vertex remains wrongly charged after all its edges are fixed and yields a constant forbidden-tuple axiom. Avoiding all immediate contradictions requires avoiding these events. Multiply their complementary probabilities and average over the original \(S,y\). No independence among the labels is used.

Proof of the tree control. A spanning tree is connected, so (1) gives an odd fiber of exactly \(\binom nk\) points after its complementary edges are fixed. Residual total charge remains \(\sigma\ne0\). At a leaf, the degree-one forbidden-label axioms and row sum derive that its unique incident edge equals its required label. At its neighbor, every newly forbidden remaining pattern \(R\) is derived from the old pattern \(x_{e,a}R\) and the learned unit \(x_{e,a}-1\) using \[R=x_{e,a}R-(x_{e,a}-1)R.\] All products have degree at most the original maximum vertex degree \(\Delta\). Reuse the learned final lines and prune leaves repeatedly. The last vertex has nonzero charge and no remaining edge, giving one. Including the Boolean/domain convention yields the safe ceiling \(\max(2,\Delta)\); the standard exactly-one CNF version pays the already recorded degree-three row conversion, hence \(\max(3,\Delta)\). There is no proof-height multiplication. This is the familiar tree elimination control, not a new tree lower-bound result. \(\square\)

The control does not contradict (3): fixing a cotree can consume more than the entire old degree budget. Conversely, (4) does not establish that the actual real signed mass on the rare hard-survival event is large. It shows that the unconditioned mass theorem and the odd full denominator do not by themselves supply a useful joint event. Conditioning needs its own bound, and arbitrary rare restrictions are not ruled out.

Route consequence

Thus this representation of the old functional does not automatically turn the source's real reverse paths into a hard-preserving restriction argument.
