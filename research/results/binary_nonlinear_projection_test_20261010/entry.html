Canonical flow depth

Proposition. Let an oriented graph have maximum degree at most \(\Delta\ge2\). Let \(S\) induce a connected graph, with \(k=|\delta S|\ge2\Delta\), and give its vertices arbitrary charges in \(\mathbb F_3\). On valid one-hot boundary labels, the side CNF projects to \[H_S(x):\quad \sum_{e\in\delta S}\eta_e x_e=c(S),\] where \(\eta_e\in\{1,-1\}\) is the outward-divergence sign. Fix any spanning tree of \(G[S]\), set every private chord to zero, and solve the private tree edges. This gives a unique canonical flow on \(H_S\). Substitution by that flow commutes with every Boolean connective, parity gate and repeated occurrence. Nevertheless some private tree-edge indicator, restricted to \(H_S\), computes MOD3-zero on \(q=\lfloor k/(2\Delta)\rfloor\) independent Boolean inputs by constant-size substitutions into the boundary indicators.

Consequently, when \(\Delta\) is fixed and \(k=\Omega(n)\), no family of polynomial-size fixed-depth \(\mathrm{AC}^0[2]\) circuits represents all these substituted private literals, even if correctness is required only on \(H_S\). The charge choice and the spanning tree are arbitrary; no source proof is assumed.

Flow proof. Summing the side's vertex equations cancels private edges and yields \(H_S\). Conversely, set non-tree private labels to zero. Remove leaves of the tree successively: each leaf equation uniquely determines its remaining incident private edge. The root equation is then exactly the summed condition. This proves existence and uniqueness on \(H_S\), recovering the earlier connected-side projection. Evaluating all occurrences at this one assignment is ordinary simultaneous substitution, so it commutes with AND, OR, NOT and MOD2 and preserves the truth of a pointwise MP implication. This is a semantic statement about one side, not a joint satisfying assignment of both inconsistent sides.

A hard tree coordinate. Weight each vertex by its number of incident boundary edges. The total weight is \(k\), each vertex weight is at most \(\Delta\), and the tree degree is at most \(\Delta\). A weighted centroid has each component after its deletion of weight at most \(k/2\): repeatedly move toward a component of weight greater than \(k/2\); the next move cannot reverse, so the finite tree process ends. At the centroid, component weights sum to at least \(k-\Delta\). Some component \(T\) therefore has weight \[k/(2\Delta)\le (k-\Delta)/\Delta\le w(T)\le k/2.\] Its joining tree edge is denoted \(e\), with sign \(\eta\) outward from \(T\). Summing its vertex equations, with private chords zero, gives \[\eta y_e=c(T)-\sum_{f\in\delta S,\ f\text{ incident to }T}\eta_f x_f.\] There are at least \(q\) boundary edges incident to \(T\) and at least \(q+1\) outside \(T\): the latter follows from \(w(T)\le k/2\), \(q\le k/4\) and \(k\ge4\). Pair \(q\) distinct edges in \(T\) with \(q\) outside it. For Boolean inputs \(u_i\), set their signed boundary contributions to \(u_i\) and \(-u_i\), respectively. Set one additional outside edge's signed contribution to \(c(S)\), and all other boundary labels to zero. The total is always \(c(S)\), so every one of these Boolean inputs lands inside \(H_S\).

The displayed tree-edge equation becomes \(\eta y_e=c(T)-\sum_i u_i\). Thus the private indicator \([y_e=\eta c(T)]\) is exactly \([\sum_i u_i=0\bmod3]\). Each original one-hot boundary indicator becomes a constant, \(u_i\), or \(1-u_i\); no MOD3 computation is hidden in the restriction. The established Smolensky circuit bound, at the scope used in the charge barrier, proves the fixed-depth consequence. This does not transfer a complex or ternary arithmetic gate into a binary-modular circuit for free. \(\square\)

A connected partition of the actual expander

The review initially fixed a labelled half-vertex partition, which need not have connected sides. For that partition, the exact projection has one charge condition per component; no single large-boundary condition is automatic. Here is a supported change of partition, available before seeing any proof, rather than an assumption that the original split was connected.

For any connected graph with \(n\ge4\) and maximum degree at most \(\Delta\ge2\), root a spanning tree and choose a minimal subtree containing at least \(n/(2\Delta)\) vertices. Each child subtree has fewer than that many vertices, so its size is less than \(1+n/2\), hence at most \(\lceil n/2\rceil\). It is not the whole tree since \(1+n/2<n\). Deleting its parent edge gives two connected vertex sets, each of size at least \(n/(2\Delta)\), since the complement has at least \(\lfloor n/2\rfloor\) vertices. Their induced graphs remain connected. Choose the tree and minimal subtree deterministically from the graph labels, independently of a proof or size exponent.

For edge expansion at least \(\varepsilon_0>0\), the shared boundary has \(k\ge\varepsilon_0 n/(2\Delta)\). The previous proposition applies to either side for all sufficiently large \(n\), with \(q\ge\varepsilon_0 n/(4\Delta^2)-1\). The standard bounded-degree one-hot CNF has polynomial encoding length, so its consequence is in the actual required size parameter. The full charge-one system is inconsistent, but each side's compatibility promise is nonempty. No satisfying assignment of the full system is used.

Why projecting formulas separately is not the repair

For a side formula \(A(a,x)\), the operation \(\Pi(F)(x)=\exists a\,(A(a,x)\land F(a,x))\) preserves disjunction, but does not generally preserve conjunction, negation or MP. This is the same common-witness issue as the recorded join control, not a new general quantifier theorem. It occurs inside this side model whenever \(G[S]\) has an internal cycle. For any compatible boundary assignment, adding an arbitrary circulation around that cycle leaves the vertex equations unchanged and lets a chosen cycle edge take every ternary value. Hence \(\Pi([a_e=0])=\Pi(\neg[a_e=0])=H_S\), while their conjunction projects to false. In the actual valid inference \(F,(F\to\bot)\vdash\bot\), the first two projected predicates can both be true and the conclusion false. This is a failure of the proposed formula-by-formula projection rule, not a claim those premises follow simultaneously from the satisfiable side.

If the induced side is a tree, this witness ambiguity disappears, but the canonical-flow depth obstruction still applies. Choosing one common flow also repairs ambiguity when cycles exist; it simply incurs the hard literal found above. Neither the failure nor its repair depends on an enumeration of special degrees. A proof-dependent extraction might avoid representing that literal at all, or simplify complete annotations before compiling them. Those possibilities are outside this generic literalwise substitution.
