Identify functions on \(\{0,1\}^L=\mathbb F_2^L\) with values in a field \(F\) with multilinear polynomials over \(F\), and write \(\deg\) for the degree of that representative. (a) Over any field \(F\), let \(f_1,\ldots,f_k\) be functions on \(\{0,1\}^n\) with values in \(F\) and common zero set \(Z\), let \(A:\{0,1\}^L\to\{0,1\}^n\) be any map and \(T\ge0\). If \(\deg(1_Z\circ A)\le T\), then \(A^{-1}(Z)\) is empty or has at least \(2^{L-T}\) points. Over \(\mathbb F_2\), \(1_Z\circ A=\prod_j(1+f_j\circ A)\), so the same holds for the OR \(1+\prod_j(1+f_j\circ A)\). Parts (b) to (e) are over \(\mathbb F_2\). (b) Let \(\ell_1,\ldots,\ell_k\) be independent uniform affine forms on \(\mathbb F_2^n\), and \(A:\mathbb F_2^{L'}\to\mathbb F_2^n\) an affine map whose image \(V\) has dimension \(L\), parametrized by an injective affine \(P:\mathbb F_2^L\to V\). Then \(\deg(f\circ A)=\deg(f\circ P)\) for every function \(f\); the forms \(\ell_j\circ P\) are independent uniform affine forms on \(\mathbb F_2^L\); the conjunction indicator \(\prod_j(1+\ell_j\circ P)\) is \(0\) when the restricted system is inconsistent and otherwise has degree equal to the rank \(r\) of the restricted linear parts; and \[\Pr\bigl[\deg\textstyle\prod_j(1+\ell_j\circ A)\le k-1\bigr]=1-\prod_{i=0}^{k-1}(1-2^{i-L})\le(2^k-1)2^{-L}.\] Hence for every probability distribution \(\mu\) on affine maps whose images have dimension at least \(L\) there are fixed forms \(\ell_1,\ldots,\ell_k\) with \(\Pr_{A\sim\mu}[\deg\le k-1]\le(2^k-1)2^{-L}\). (c) Let \(n\ge1\), \(1\le k<L_0\le n\) and \[M\ge\frac{(n-L_0)(n+1)+n}{L_0-k}.\] Then \(M\) independent conjunctions, each of \(k\) independent uniform affine equations, have, except with probability at most \((n-L_0+1)2^{-n}\), no affine subspace \(V\) of dimension at least \(L_0\) on which every one of them has degree at most \(k-1\). (d) Lines. Let a line be a disjunction of \(r\ge1\) members, the \(i\)-th the conjunction of \(k_i\ge1\) affine equations, all \(K=\sum_ik_i\) forms independent and uniform, and let its falsity indicator be \(\prod_i(1+1_{Z_i})\), \(Z_i\) the member's zero set. Under every \(A\) as in (b), except with probability at most \((2^K-1)2^{-L}\), no member becomes constant and the restricted falsity indicator has degree exactly \(K\); so the existence statement of (b) holds for lines with \(K\) in place of \(k\), and (c) holds for \(M\) independent such lines with \(K\) in place of \(k\) and the condition \(K<L_0\). (e) Padding. Let \(F\) be a CNF on \(n\) variables with clauses \(C_1,\ldots,C_N\) of width at most \(w\), and \(R\) a refutation of \(F\) in PCK\(^1_{\mathbb F_2}\) of size \(S\), with \(F_R\) the clauses it uses as initial cedents. For each \(C\in F_R\) and \(t\le M_0\), let \(\Lambda_{C,t}=\{C,m^{C,t}_1,\ldots,m^{C,t}_r\}\), its members as in (d) with \(K\) forms, fresh for each \((C,t)\). There is a refutation \(R'\) of \(F\) of size \(S+O(NM_0r(w+Kn))\) in which every \(\Lambda_{C,t}\) is an ancestor of the final line. (i) For \(M_0=1\) and every distribution \(\mu\) on affine maps whose images have dimension at least \(L\ge w+K+1\), there are fixed forms with \[\Pr_{A\sim\mu}\bigl[\text{every }\Lambda_C\circ A\text{ has degree below }K\bigr]\le(2^K-1)2^{-(L-w)}.\] (ii) For \(w+K<L_0\le n\) and \(M_0\ge((n-L_0)(n+1)+n)/(L_0-w-K)\), except with probability at most \((n-L_0+1)2^{-n}\) over the forms, every affine subspace of dimension at least \(L_0\) carries a padded line of degree at least \(K\).
