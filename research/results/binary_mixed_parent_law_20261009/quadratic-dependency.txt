20 September 2026 — Complete binary ENS on arbitrary quadratic old inputs
Status: working end-to-end degree lower bound for complete old-only quadratic-input families, with an exact low-case certificate control; full multilevel Frege remains open. Produced by Codex (gpt-6-astra, high reasoning).
Question and stopping point. Audit the complete source consequence of the new structural dichotomy. The required endpoint is an old proof below its degree barrier, with one common weight and actual maps of every source coefficient. This cycle supplies that ledger. It is a theorem about arbitrary quadratic inputs in the old bits, not about arbitrary later ENS inputs involving earlier coefficient variables.
1. Finite theorem and asymptotic consequence
Work over \(\mathbb F_2\), with \(n=2^\ell\), \(\ell\ge2\), \(v=(n+1)\ell\), and the compact old base \(\mathcal Q_n\). Let \(\mathcal E\) contain at most \(M\ge1\) complete ENS blocks whose inputs are arbitrary polynomials of ordinary degree at most two in the old bits only. Input tuples may overlap arbitrarily and may contain arbitrary sums. Each block has fresh own coefficients, all companions and all coefficient Booleanity equations. Its positive accuracy may be chosen independently of the other blocks.
Let \(C_{\rm BR}\) be an absolute constant valid in the imported binary Bogolyubov–Ruzsa theorem. For integers \(a\ge1,k\ge2\), put
\[ d=\lceil\log_2(v+1)\rceil,\quad t_0=16a^2d^2,\quad c_0=\lceil C_{\rm BR}(a+1)^4\rceil, \quad T=t_0+2k+2c_0+1,\quad B=TD+k, \tag{GQ-costs} \]
\[ \theta=\frac{16(n+1)}n\left[\left(1+\frac{3k}v\right)^\ell-1\right]. \]
Working finite theorem. No ordinary NS or PC refutation of \(\mathcal Q_n\cup\mathcal E\) has degree at most \(D\), provided
\[ D\ge\max(\ell,5),\quad 2a\le v,\quad k\le n/16,\quad \theta\le1/2,\quad B\le n/4, \tag{GQ-room} \]
\[ M(v+1)e^{-ak^2/v^2}\le1/8,\qquad M2^{t_0}e^{-k^2/v}\le1/8. \tag{GQ-images} \]
Asymptotic corollary. For every fixed polynomial block-inventory exponent and every fixed polylogarithmic degree bound, such refutations are excluded for all sufficiently large \(n\). One parameter choice has \(T=O(n^{8/9}(\log n)^{3/2})\); the exact finite statement, rather than this asymptotic notation, owns the original-degree budget. This result is binary; no odd-prime extension is asserted.
2. A common weight for every high case and every low-case branch
Restrict all blocks to their first coefficient row, setting later rows to zero. This does not increase ordinary proof degree, regardless of their original accuracies. Constant tuples take scalar coefficient maps. For each remaining tuple apply the structural dichotomy to the span of its multilinear representatives, modulo the affine part.
High case. The dichotomy supplies \(a\) constant input combinations with disjoint quadratic leading pairs after a binary coordinate change. The leading-pair reduction, pulled back to the original old bits, has image dimension on degree-at-most-\(k\) polynomials bounded by
\[ \binom vk(v+1)e^{-ak^2/v^2}. \]
Its kernel gives an ordinary identity \(f=\sum_i c_iq_i+E\), with \(\deg c_i\le k-2\) and \(E\in\mathcal I_k({\rm BOOL}_{\rm old})\). Every selected generator is a constant combination of the actual input tuple.
Low case. There are \(t_j\le t_0\) independent binary linear probes and a subspace \(W_{j,0}\) of the actual input-function span of codimension \(s_j\le c_0\), all of whose functions become affine on each probe branch. Choose at most \(s_j\) exceptional quadratic functions completing that subspace to the full input span. For each \(\sigma\in\mathbb F_2^{t_j}\), let \(A_{j,\sigma}\) be the affine span of the restricted \(W_{j,0}\), extended as affine polynomials in the original old coordinates. Let \(G_{j,\sigma,1},\ldots,G_{j,\sigma,s_j}\) be the restricted exceptional functions, of degree at most two. They need not be independent after restriction. Together these functions span every original branch input, with constant representations in both directions modulo old Booleanity.
There are at most \(M2^{t_0}\) such affine spaces. A space containing one will be handled by a constant prefix; the zero space needs none. For every proper one of rank at least \(2k+3\), use ordinary restriction to its affine zero flat. Its degree-at-most-\(k\) image dimension is at most \(\binom vk e^{-k^2/v}\), by the same dimension calculation as before.
The cube-volume space \(\mathcal S_k\) has dimension at least \(\binom vk/2\). The two total image bounds in (GQ-images) consume at most \(\binom vk/4\). Thus a nonzero \(f\in\mathcal S_k\) lies in all high-quadratic and high-affine kernels. The weight remains a polynomial in the original pigeonhole bits. Coordinate changes used to construct individual reduction maps do not change the old PHP axioms or its separator space.
3. Simultaneous old-only coefficient maps
On a high tuple, use its kernel coefficients \(c_i\). Its accuracy-one product is \(1-f+E\), hence zero at every old Boolean point with \(f=1\).
For a low tuple and branch \(\sigma\), choose the affine coefficient map on \(A_{j,\sigma}\). A proper high-affine space uses its kernel representation of \(f\), giving affine-core value \(A=1-f\). A low-affine space of rank at most \(2k+2\) uses a telescoping flat indicator. These coefficients have degree at most \(2k+1\), and \(\deg A\le2k+2\). Take \(A=1\) for a zero affine space. A unit affine span gives a constant representation of one and value zero, as a Boolean-function identity.
For the remaining exceptional functions, use the exact basis-prefix expression
\[ V_{j,\sigma}=A\prod_{u=1}^{s_j}(1-G_{j,\sigma,u}),\qquad 1-V_{j,\sigma} =\sum_\nu\gamma_\nu A_\nu +\sum_{u=1}^{s_j} A\prod_{b<u}(1-G_{j,\sigma,b})G_{j,\sigma,u}. \tag{GQ-branch-prefix} \]
Pull the coefficients back to every original input position using the constant branch representations. Their degrees are at most \(2k+2s_j+1\), including the case \(s_j=0\). At Boolean points with \(f=1\), all branch companions vanish: a high-affine core is already zero, while in every other case a nonzero basis input forces the displayed product to zero. Working with representatives introduces only Boolean-function equalities here, which will be certified at the final actual degree.
Let \(\chi_{j,\sigma}\) be the product of the appropriate binary probe literals. Interpolate each original coefficient by
\[ \beta_{j,i}=\sum_\sigma\chi_{j,\sigma}\,\beta_{j,\sigma,i}. \tag{GQ-map} \]
Exactly one selector is one at every old Boolean point. Consequently the mapped original input tuple agrees there with the selected branch tuple, and every mapped original companion vanishes on \(f=1\). All these coefficient images are binary old-only polynomials of degree at most \(t_j+2k+2s_j+1\le T\). Different blocks use their own maps simultaneously; no coefficient image depends on another source coefficient. The potentially exponential branch count is charged in (GQ-images), while the degrees in (GQ-map) add. No repeated proof substitution or hidden factor for the number of branches is used.
4. Every original axiom and proof step is covered
Weight the substituted proof by the same \(f\). On the full old Boolean cube every weighted companion is zero, as proved above. Every coefficient image \(\beta\) also satisfies \(\beta^2-\beta=0\) there, since its coefficients and values are binary. Ordinary division by the old Boolean equations therefore supplies NS certificates through each polynomial's actual total degree. This argument uses the full Boolean cube, not the empty set of full PHP models.
For a nonconstant accuracy-one tuple let \(\delta\in\{1,2\}\) be the actual maximum input degree and \(\mu_i\) the degree of a nonzero input. Fresh coefficient variables make its original companion degree \(e_i=\delta+1+\mu_i\). The ledger is
\[ \begin{array}{c|c|c} \text{source generator}&\text{original degree}&\text{weighted old NS ceiling}\\\hline q_iP & e_i & k+T+\delta+\mu_i\le k+Te_i\\ r_i^2-r_i &2&k+2T\\ F\in\mathcal Q_n&\deg F&k+\deg F\le k+T\deg F. \end{array} \tag{GQ-ledger} \]
Zero inputs contribute no companion; actual constant tuples use their exact scalar cleanup. Inputs whose Boolean representatives vanish or become affine are still charged at their actual original polynomial degree. Thus cancellations never lower a carried source cost without justification.
A source NS cofactor through \(D-e\) maps to degree at most \(T(D-e)\); adding the certified weighted generator gives ceiling \(TD+k\). For PC, represent every line \(p\) by \(f\Phi(p)\). Addition is linear, and a primitive multiplication is replayed by multiplying the previous weighted image by the corresponding degree-at-most-\(T\) variable image. Its predecessor degree is at most \(D-1\), so the total never exceeds \(TD+k\). All axiom preludes satisfy that bound. A refutation would therefore derive \(f\) in ordinary old PC through \(B\), or in old NS through the same bound.
But \(f\ne0\) belongs to \(\mathcal S_k\), and (GQ-room) gives \(k\le B\le n/4\). The cube-volume separation lemma excludes that old consequence. This proves the finite theorem. No equality between augmented PC and NS spaces has been assumed.
5. Parameter choice and the source corollary
For \(M\le n^c\) with fixed \(c\), choose \(a=\lceil n^{2/9}\rceil\), and
\[ k=\left\lceil\max\left\{2, v\sqrt{\frac{\ln(16M(v+1))}{a}}, \sqrt{v(\ln(16M)+t_0\ln2)}\right\}\right\rceil. \tag{GQ-choice} \]
The two image bounds then hold. As \(n\to\infty\),
\[ c_0=O(n^{8/9}),\quad t_0=O(n^{4/9}(\log n)^2),\quad k=O(n^{8/9}(\log n)^{3/2}),\quad T=O(n^{8/9}(\log n)^{3/2}). \]
In particular \(k=o(n)\), \(\theta=o(1)\), and \(TD+k=o(n)\) for every fixed polylogarithmic \(D\). All finite conditions hold for sufficiently large \(n\). Smaller proof ceilings can be enlarged to \(\max(D,\ell,5)\) before applying the statement.
Working actual-source corollary. A complete binary two-level family with old-affine bottoms of rank at most two and arbitrary parent sums of their actual products, with old-affine offsets allowed, has no polylogarithmic-degree ordinary NS or PC refutation over \(\mathcal Q_n\), at polynomial block inventory. Bottoms and parents may overlap arbitrarily, and no independence, fixed summand bound, special tensor shape, or common probe set is required. Positive accuracies may vary by block.
Proof. Apply the complete bottom map once per reused bottom. At rank two, the first-success coefficient map at accuracy one has degree one; at larger accuracy the two scalar rows produce the exact product of the two affine factors. Rank-one and scalar-clean cases have the corresponding constant/affine maps. Each bottom axiom image fits its original degree, so the whole substitution preserves NS/PC degree. Every parent input becomes its actual old quadratic sum plus affine offset. All parent coefficients and field equations remain until applying the theorem just proved. The restriction to first coefficient rows may then be made independently for every parent. This gives the contradiction without adding separate summand generators.
This removes the feature-independence condition from the preceding unbounded-sum source class, at a weaker exponent in the old-proof bound. Earlier stronger quantitative results on their narrower classes remain valid. Arbitrary-rank bottom affine tuples, later inputs depending on uneliminated coefficients, higher-degree old inputs, and odd primes remain outside the new theorem.
6. Exact low-case certificate control
The new bounded C++ checker uses seven Boolean old variables, four genuine quadratic inputs
\[ e=x_5x_6,\quad f=x_1+x_2,\quad g_i=x_0x_i+e\quad(i=1,2,3,4), \quad \beta_1=1+x_0+x_0f,\quad \beta_2=x_0,\quad \beta_3=\beta_4=0. \]
This is a hand-supplied low-case decomposition, not an execution of Bogolyubov–Ruzsa. At \(x_0=0\), all inputs reduce to the exceptional quadratic \(e\); at \(x_0=1\), the affine difference \(g_1+g_2=f\) supplies the weighted clamp. The coefficient map combines those branches. The checker expands ordinary polynomials before reduction and emits full NS cofactors for all four weighted companions and all four weighted coefficient-field equations. All eight reconstruct exactly, with zero Boolean remainder and the original-degree ceilings for \(T=2,\deg f=1\). Removing either the affine-branch contribution or the exceptional low-branch contribution gives a nonzero companion remainder.
All certificates and both controls, reproduction and encoding, and provenance are retained. This finite Boolean-base check does not prove the theorem or test a full PHP instance; it checks the new simultaneous interpolation and degree ledger. The preceding cycle's structural checks are reused without calling them fresh runs.
