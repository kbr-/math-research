Corollary, recovered support. In the coherent balanced binary modular NS compiler, and its recorded literal-CNF boundary at \(h\ge\max(1,w)\), the supplied NS identity can use just old Boolean equations, old clause generators after the boundary, and retained ENS companions. Every fresh-coefficient field generator has coefficient zero in this chosen identity. The original ceiling \(B=(C_2+H_\pi)L\), \(C_2=\max(M_{\rm fr}+2,12)\), and polynomial factored representation are unchanged. This is a choice of witness in that compiler, not elimination of fields from an arbitrary NS or PC proof.

Proof. Follow the complete proof of the all-prime modular compiler at \(p=2\), rather than its earlier domain-only alternative. For a block \(P=\prod_u(1-\sum_i r_{u,i}g_i)\), its prefix identity \(1-P=\sum_i U_i g_i\) is literal. Thus \(P^2-P=-\sum_iU_i(g_iP)\), through twice the original product weight, uses companions only. Old atoms use old Booleanity, negation preserves it, and an affine binary MOD value \(t=c+\sum_i a_i\) satisfies \(t^2-t=\sum_i(a_i^2-a_i)\). Recursing therefore introduces no coefficient-field generator. The MOD frame's formal \(t^2-t\) is expanded into those argument Booleanity witnesses; it is not a new fresh-variable domain axiom.

The three-product OR comparison is an exact ring identity in companions, with non-OR singleton inputs paid by the same Booleanity witnesses. Boolean frames use those modules, and MP multiplies the supplied identities by their actual conclusion values. These operations do not add generators. Each module retains the already proved \(2L\), \(3L\), and leaf \(C_2L\) ceilings, including its outer cofactor. The literal boundary assigns clause coefficients constants, maps clause companions to old Boolean consequences, and maps goal companions to old clause generators. Their original-degree certificates likewise need no fresh fields. This proves the statement. The older cleanup instead trades companions for domain equations; it does not force that choice here. \(\square\)

Nonvacuity. Contradictory unit clauses have a balanced proof and the stated boundary; adding unused Boolean variables gives unbounded ambient size. The corollary is conditional on a supplied proof for a hard family, not an assertion that the hard family's short proof exists. Degenerate cases. An empty clause already gives one; constant and all-zero-input products have the direct values used in the compiler. No arbitrary presentation conversion is added.

Companion-only map descent

Theorem. Let \(J_2\subseteq\mathbb F_2[x]_{\le D}\) be a degree-\(D\) pseudoideal containing old Booleanity, and let \(J_E=E\otimes J_2\), \(E=\mathbb F_{2^e}\). Consider an acyclic binary ENS source. An input \(g_{a,i}\) is a binary polynomial in old variables and earlier named products, of structural weighted degree at most \(\delta_{a,i}\); write \(\delta_a=\max_i\delta_{a,i}\), \(s_a=h_a(\delta_a+1)\), \(h_a\ge1\). Old variables have weight one and product \(P_a\) weight \(s_a\); own coefficient variables are fresh. Let \(L\ge\max(1,\max_a s_a)\). An all-zero tuple is assigned its literal product one and may be removed.

Let \(\Phi\) fix old variables and map every original coefficient variable to an old \(E\)-polynomial of degree at most \(T\ge1\). Suppose \(W\notin J_E\), \(\deg W\le b\), and \[ W\Phi(g_{a,i}P_a)\in J_E\quad\text{for every companion},\qquad b+3TL\le D. \] No image of a fresh-field equation is assumed. Fix any coefficientwise unital projection \(\pi(z)=\operatorname{Tr}_{E/\mathbb F_2}(cz)\), \(\operatorname{Tr}(c)=1\). Let \(\Phi_0(r)=\pi(\Phi(r))\), and rebuild every product with these same globally assigned binary maps. Then \[ W(\Phi(P_a)-\Phi_0(P_a))\in J_E,\qquad W\Phi_0(g_{a,i}P_a)\in J_E. \] All new coefficient maps have degree at most \(T\), and \(W\Phi_0(r^2-r)\in J_E\). Some binary coefficient component \(w\) of \(W\), of degree at most \(b\), survives in \(J_2\) and satisfies all these new companion and fresh-field memberships (the product-difference assertion is for \(W\), not necessarily for each component against the original nonbinary product). No rank-retention statement is needed or asserted.

Weighted factor projection. Write \(\sigma\) for coefficientwise squaring with old indeterminates fixed. For any old polynomial \(f\) of degree at most \(R\), \(f^2-\sigma f\) is an old Boolean consequence through \(2R\). If \(Kf\in J_E\), \(\deg K\le q\), and \(q+2R\le D\), multiplication by \(f\) followed by subtraction of that Boolean consequence times \(K\) gives \(K\sigma f\in J_E\). Repeat with \(\sigma f\), always at the same ceiling; \(K\) is neither squared nor projected. Summing with the coefficients \(\sigma^j(c)\) gives \(K\pi(f)\in J_E\). The Boolean consequence follows termwise from \(m^2-m\) for old monomials, with degree at most twice their degree. This is reuse in a PC pseudoideal, not a claim that ordinary NS cofactor degrees stay fixed under iteration.

For one tuple of binary old polynomials \(g_i\) of degree at most \(\delta\), let \(f_u=1-\sum_i a_{u,i}g_i\), \(\deg a_{u,i}\le T\), \(R=T+\delta\), and \(P=\prod_{u=1}^h f_u\). Assume all \(Wg_iP\) lie in \(J_E\). To replace row \(u\), apply the preceding argument to \(K=Wg_i\prod_{v\ne u}f_v\), using the already replaced rows where applicable. Its required ceiling is \[ b+\delta+(h+1)R. \] Because the inputs are binary, \(\pi(f_u)=1-\sum_i\pi(a_{u,i})g_i\). The same projection works for every companion and every row. Hence the projected product \(P_0\) also has all weighted companions zero. Its prefix and the original prefix give \[ W(P-P_0)=WP(1-P_0)-WP_0(1-P). \] Each right-hand term is a sum of paid multiples of these companions. The prefixes have degree at most \((h-1)R+T\), so the additional value-comparison ceiling is \(b+2hR\). This proves the one-block assertion, with the maximum of these two ceilings.

Acyclic reconstruction. Induct on blocks. Let \(G_i=\Phi(g_{a,i})\), \(G_i^0=\Phi_0(g_{a,i})\), and temporarily retain the original coefficient images \(a_{u,i}=\Phi(r_{a,u,i})\) while rebuilding the inputs: \[ P_* = \prod_u(1-\sum_i a_{u,i}G_i^0). \] Earlier product differences, multiplied by monomial telescoping factors, give \(W(G_i-G_i^0)\in J_E\) through \(b+T\delta_{a,i}\). More generally, replacing only already-compared product atoms in an expression of weighted degree \(q\), while holding every raw coefficient image fixed, pays \(b+Tq\): its one changed product has weight \(s_v\), and all the other factors have combined weight at most \(q-s_v\). Original coefficient images, when present in these factors, have degree at most their weight times \(T\). This does not compare \(\Phi\) and \(\Phi_0\) on arbitrary raw-coefficient expressions; the inputs \(g_{a,i}\) contain no such raw coefficients.

It follows that \(W(\Phi(P_a)-P_*)\) lies in \(J_E\) through \(b+Ts_a\), and \(WG_i^0P_*\in J_E\) through \(b+T(s_a+\delta_a)\), using the assumed original companion. The inputs \(G_i^0\) are now genuinely binary old polynomials. Apply the one-block argument with input ceiling \(T\delta_a\) and row ceiling \(R_a=T(\delta_a+1)\). Its costs are \[ b+T\delta_a+(h_a+1)R_a\le b+3Ts_a,\qquad b+2h_aR_a=b+2Ts_a. \] The first inequality uses \(\delta_a\le\delta_a+1\) and \(h_a+2\le3h_a\). Both fit \(D\). Thus \(W(P_*-\Phi_0(P_a))\) and every new weighted companion lie in \(J_E\). This proves the inductive product comparison without making separate assignments for shared occurrences.

Every \(\Phi_0(r)\) is binary, so its Booleanity is an old Boolean consequence through \(2T\), and its weighted version fits \(b+2T\le D\). Decompose \(W\) in any binary basis of \(E\). All new source images are binary, so their weighted memberships hold componentwise. At least one component survives because \(W\notin J_E\). This proves the theorem. \(\square\)

Nonvacuity. Over \(\mathbb F_4\), take one old Boolean bit, bottom input \(x\) and accuracy two, with coefficient images \(1,\alpha\), where \(\alpha^2+\alpha+1=0\). Use parent input \(1-P\), also accuracy two and the same two constant images. With \(W=1\), both companions are old Boolean consequences, but the fresh coefficient image \(\alpha^2-\alpha=1\) does not vanish. The structural weights are four and ten; \(T=1,b=0,L=10,D=30\) suffice. The old Boolean pseudoideal is proper. This witnesses the newly allowed premise, including an actual later product. Unused old variables preserve it at unbounded size.

Degenerate cases. With no blocks the assertion reduces to componentwise survival. A zero tuple has product one; constants are covered by weight zero inputs. Accuracy one uses the full \(3s_a\) ceiling. For \(E=\mathbb F_2\) the projection is the identity. A nonbinary input cannot commute with projection in the one-block step; acyclic rebuilding is what makes its input binary. A trace with \(\pi(1)=0\) is not allowed. The theorem does not assert that \(\pi\) is multiplicative or that the whole old quotient is an algebra outside its paid degree range.
