Simultaneous dense basis

Proposition (separated reader source), algebraic part. Let \(W=\mathbb F_2^L\) have its fixed coordinate basis, and let \(U_1,\ldots,U_M\) be disjoint coordinate blocks of dimension \(p\), where \(M\ge2,p\ge1,Mp\le L\). For a linear map \(\phi:W\to E\) and subspace \(S\le E\), write \(q=\operatorname{rank}\phi\), \(s=\dim(\operatorname{im}\phi\cap S)\), and \(t=\lceil\log_2M\rceil\). If \[ p+t+1\le L/16,\qquad q-s\ge2p+2t+3, \] there is \(T\in\mathrm{GL}(L,2)\) such that \(G_a=TU_a\) are a literal direct sum, every nonzero vector in each \(G_a\) has coordinate Hamming weight greater than \(L/4\), and \[ (G_a+G_b)\cap\phi^{-1}(S)=0\quad(a\ne b). \] In particular their individual images are injective and disjoint from \(S\), and their images in \(E/S\) are pairwise disjoint. Global independence of all those quotient images is not asserted.

Proof. Choose \(T\) uniformly in the finite group \(\mathrm{GL}(L,2)\). Each fixed nonzero vector has uniform nonzero image: extend it and any desired target to bases, giving an invertible change between them. The number of binary words of weight at most \(L/4\) is at most \(3^{L/4}(4/3)^L\), by weighting the binomial sum with \(3^{-j}\). There are fewer than \(M2^p\) nonzero vectors in the individual blocks, and \(2^L-1\ge2^{L-1}\). Thus the probability that any individual block has a forbidden low-weight image is at most \[ 2M2^p(2/3^{3/4})^L \le (2^{17/16}/3^{3/4})^L <1/4. \] Here the first hypothesis gives the middle inequality and \(L\ge16\); the base to the sixteenth power is \(2^{17}/3^{12}<1/4\), since \(4\cdot131072<531441\). The bad subspace \(\phi^{-1}(S)\) has codimension \(q-s\). Each fixed nonzero image lands in it with probability at most \(2^{1-(q-s)}\). Fewer than \(M^2 2^{2p}\) vectors occur among the pair-block sums. The second union bound is consequently at most \[ 2^{2p+2t+1-(q-s)}\le1/4. \] With positive probability neither event occurs. Invertibility preserves the literal direct sum. Avoidance for each pair includes each individual block, and gives all the stated quotient properties. This proves the algebraic part without an extension field or an efficient construction algorithm.
