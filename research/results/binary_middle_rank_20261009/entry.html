A dimension test in the actual hard quotient

Work over \(\mathbb F_2\) in the Boolean degree filtration on \(v\) old variables. Let \(J_D\) be a proper degree-\(D\) pseudoideal containing an unsatisfiable old family \(\Gamma\), with \(u=\max(2,\max_{g\in\Gamma}\deg g)\). Fix integers \(b\ge0\), \(0\le q<r\le a=D-b-1\), with \(D-b\ge u\), and write \(N_v(q)=\sum_{j\le q}\binom vj\). The source list contains \(x_T=\prod_{i\in T}x_i\) for every \(r\)-subset \(T\subseteq[v]\).

Proposition (hard quotient compression gap). If \[ \binom ar>N_v(q), \tag{1} \] there is no surviving binary \(w\), \(\deg w\le b\), and collection of arbitrary old references \(Q_T\), \(\deg Q_T\le q\), with \[ w(x_T-Q_T)\in J_D\quad\text{for every }|T|=r. \tag{2} \] Moreover introduce complete accuracy-\(h\) bottom blocks, \(h\ge r\), on inputs \((1-x_i)_{i\in T}\), with actual products \(P_T\). If \(b+2h+r\le D\), the same impossibility holds for references to every \(P_T\), under any legal constant bottom-row assignment satisfying all weighted bottom companions.

Proof of the dimension obstruction. Suppose \(w\) and the references exist. The weighted cube supplies a set \(S\) of \(a\) old coordinates whose weighted monomials are all independent modulo \(J_D\). Therefore the \(\binom ar\) classes \([wx_T]\), for \(T\subseteq S\), \(|T|=r\), are linearly independent. By (2) they all lie in \([wV_q]\), whose dimension is at most the number \(N_v(q)\) of all old multilinear monomials through degree \(q\). This contradicts (1). The set \(S\) may depend on \(w\); the source included every \(r\)-subset before that choice. References may use variables outside \(S\), already accounted for in \(N_v(q)\). No module structure beyond the paid window is assumed.

Proof of the complete-source transfer. The earlier prefix comparison applies without its circulation argument. Telescoping \(1-x_T\) and using the weighted companions gives \(wP_T(1-x_T)\in J_D\). The literal prefix \(1-P_T\) is a combination of \((1-x_i)_{i\in T}\), so multiplying by \(wx_T\) gives \(wx_T(1-P_T)\in J_D\) by Booleanity. Their sum yields \(w(P_T-x_T)\in J_D\). Constant row assignments make the actual product degree at most \(h\); the conservative original-charge buffer \(b+2h+r\) covers both comparisons. Thus reference agreement for every \(P_T\) would imply (2), already impossible. All indexed companions, rather than a chosen subset, are required for this comparison. \(\square\)

This is a dimension obstruction for a specified degree target \(q\), not a claim of a sharp hitting-number bound or a general lower bound on every degree reduction. The previous circulation theorem gives stronger strictly-lower-degree and sharp hitting conclusions for its edge-disjoint-cycle family. The two hypotheses differ; no universal dominance is asserted.

The intermediate interval contains such a source

The necessary control: one parent is still easy

Add one complete parent whose tuple consists of all the genuine \(P_T\)'s. There is nevertheless a cheap simultaneous cleanup. Apply the weighted cube at weight one and choose \(T_0\) of size \(r\) in its free coordinate set; \(x_{T_0}\) survives. Specialize every bottom exactly, with its \(r\) literal rows and zero padding, giving \(P_T=x_T\). In the parent choose its first row to select \(P_{T_0}\), and all other rows zero. With \(W=x_{T_0}\), every parent companion is \[ x_{T_0}(1-x_{T_0})x_T, \] which vanishes by Booleanity through degree at most \(3r\); all bottom companions have their corresponding zero factor through \(2r+1\le3r\). Fields vanish at the binary constants. The weight has degree \(r\), and \(3r\le D\). This is the earlier one-parent cleanup with survival now provided by the weighted cube, not a new source-removal engine.

Original bottom and parent companion ceilings remain \(2h+1\) and \(2h^2+3h\). Replaying any source refutation still requires its original ceiling plus \(r\) to fit \(D\). This positive control demonstrates why demanding all values become low-degree is stronger than the actual goal: the parent errors can vanish while the simultaneous reference demand is impossible.
