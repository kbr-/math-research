The actual full-face action has a nilpotent lift

Let \(m\ge1\) and \(A_m=\mathbb F_2[r_1,\ldots,r_m]/(r_i^2-r_i)\), with monomial basis \(r_S=\prod_{i\in S}r_i\). Write \(e_H\in A_m^*\) for extraction of the coefficient of \(r_H\). The recorded exact action of multiplication by \(r_i\) is \[ M_i e_H=\begin{cases}e_H+e_{H\setminus\{i\}},&i\in H,\\0,&i\notin H.\end{cases} \] Put \(D_i e_H=\mathbf1_{i\in H}e_H\), and define \[ Ze_H=\sum_{S\subseteq H}e_S. \] The incidence algebra here consists of endomorphisms sending each \(e_H\) into the span of its subfaces. Its strictly subface-lowering endomorphisms form a two-sided ideal \(\mathfrak n\), with \(\mathfrak n^{m+1}=0\), since a nonzero product of \(m+1\) strict steps would require a strictly decreasing chain of that length inside an \(m\)-element set. Each \(M_i-D_i\) and \(Z-I\) belongs to this ideal. This is an actual finite algebra on the full fresh space; it is not yet an algebra acting on the paid source annihilator. It is generally noncommutative, so no commutative-ring uniqueness theorem is being imported for it.

Proposition (Boolean face conjugation). On this binary full-face space, \[ Z^2=I,\qquad (Z-I)^2=0,\qquad M_i=ZD_iZ\quad(1\le i\le m). \] Moreover, \(Z\) is the unique invertible binary intertwiner satisfying \(M_iR=RD_i\) for all \(i\). Its transpose under the coefficient pairing acts on polynomials by \[ Z^\vee(r_S)=r_S\prod_{j\notin S}(1+r_j). \tag{1} \] Every displayed polynomial has unique multilinear degree exactly \(m\), including \[ Z^\vee(1)=\prod_{j=1}^m(1+r_j)=:\Delta_0. \tag{2} \] Consequently an active correction \(\lambda\mapsto Z\lambda\) has \((Z\lambda)(1)=\lambda(\Delta_0)\), not generally \(\lambda(1)\). For the complete accuracy-\(m\) block with single constant input \(g=1\), its companion is exactly \(\Delta_0\). Every normalized full Boolean functional annihilating that source therefore has \((Z\lambda)(1)=0\).

Proof. In \(Z^2e_H\), the coefficient of \(e_T\), for \(T\subseteq H\), counts intermediate subsets \(T\subseteq S\subseteq H\). It is \(2^{|H\setminus T|}\), equal to one modulo two only for \(T=H\). This gives \(Z^2=I\), and characteristic two gives \((Z-I)^2=0\).

The vector \(Ze_H\) is evaluation at the Boolean assignment with support \(H\). Thus \(M_iZe_H=\mathbf1_{i\in H}Ze_H\), giving \(M_iZ=ZD_i\) and the conjugation. Conversely a joint eigenfunctional \(\lambda\) with eigenvalues \(\mathbf1_{i\in H}\) satisfies \[ \lambda(r_S)=\left(\prod_{i\in S}\mathbf1_{i\in H}\right)\lambda(1) \] by repeated full-algebra multiplication. It is zero if \(\lambda(1)=0\), and otherwise it is exactly evaluation at \(H\), since the only nonzero binary scalar is one. Each column \(Re_H\) of an invertible intertwiner is a nonzero vector in that one-dimensional joint eigenspace. Hence \(R=Z\). This uniqueness is for the labelled full set of operators and this specified diagonal model, not for arbitrary source corrections.

For (1), the coefficient of \(r_H\) in \(Z^\vee r_S\) is \[ (Ze_H)(r_S)=\mathbf1_{S\subseteq H}. \] Summing the corresponding monomials yields (1). Its full monomial has coefficient one, proving exact degree \(m\). Equation (2) gives the normalization formula. The constant-input block has original product and companion degree \(m\), and fresh-field degree two; these degrees are not reset. Its source is satisfiable, for example at \(r_1=1\). Its annihilators exist, but must kill \(\Delta_0\), proving the final assertion. \(\square\)

Why this does not yet repair the source

The square-zero correction is genuine, but its nilpotence index alone gives no useful normalization or degree guarantee. If \(Z\) is treated as a physical correction on the original functional domain, even testing its new value on one asks for the degree-\(m\) polynomial \(\Delta_0\). When the available fresh degree is less than \(m\), that test is outside the domain. If instead \(Z\) is only a change of coordinates and the marked unit, relation subspace and degree filtration are all transported, there is no intrinsic degree inflation from the coordinate change; the problem is equivalent to the original one and no source equation has been solved. The proposition does not claim that every nilpotent method costs \(m\).

The transform is not the dual of affine translation \(r\mapsto r+1\). That translation preserves polynomial degree and fixes the unit, but its coefficient-dual action goes in the opposite subset direction: \[ e_H\longmapsto\sum_{T\supseteq H}e_T. \] Its conjugation exchanges multiplication by \(r_i\) with multiplication by \(1+r_i\), rather than diagonalizing to \(D_i\). Confusing those two transposes would falsely make (2) cost-free.

The natural source domain has an additional constraint. A degree-\(B\) polynomial in old variables \(x\) and fresh variables \(r\) has \[ P=\sum_H p_H(x)r_H,\qquad \deg p_H\le B-|H|. \] Its component functional at face \(H\) is defined only through old degree \(B-|H|\). Sending a high-face component to a proper subface asks it to act on a larger old domain. Such extensions are not canonically supplied by the incidence formula. Supplying them independently does not prove the mixed equations or PC compatibility. The supported handoff ansatz additionally carries its original input factors and their degrees.

Likewise the actual dual PC multiplication is a map between consecutive cutoffs, as lem:filtered-dual-transport states. Restricting the full-face matrices to an arbitrary finite list and treating them as uncharged endomorphisms replaces that interface. The earlier Boolean-overflow control and the one-bit source annihilator show why this is unsafe: for the source \(1+r=0\), the normalized evaluation vector \(e_\varnothing+e_{\{1\}}\) is fixed by the combined multiplication action, but \(Z\) sends it to \(e_{\{1\}}\), which has zero normalization and does not annihilate the unchanged source polynomial.

The existing source-cover theorem was reread to avoid reintroducing a larger but empty interface: a compatible filtered cover exists exactly when the observation annihilates actual bounded PC closure. Adding nilpotent matrix coordinates alone does not establish that condition. The known path-system NS design fails a legal derived-line multiplication; no basis change with all tests transported removes that failure. The positive prescribed graph substitution remains valid because it supplies the map and its genuinely fresh-degree-decreasing contraction in advance.
