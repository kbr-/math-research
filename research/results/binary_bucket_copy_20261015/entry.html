A copied CNF and a complete certificate

Theorem (bucket copy lift). Let \(\Gamma(X)\) be a binary CNF on \(r\ge1\) declared old variables \(X=(x_1,\ldots,x_r)\). Let \(F(X)\) be its ordered clause-falsity tuple together with every Boolean and variable-commutator generator. Write \(T\) for the total gate/wire size of explicit formulas for that tuple, and \(\delta=\max(2,\operatorname{width}\Gamma)\). Let a binary noncommutative formula certificate \(C(X,Y)\) have size \(S\ge2\), depth \(d\), and every nonzero monomial contain exactly one original placeholder. Let \(D_0\) be its original syntactic degree, with every old letter and placeholder of degree one, sums taking maxima and products adding degrees.

Add one copy \(W=(w_1,\ldots,w_r)\) and the two clauses \(\neg w_j\vee x_j\), \(\neg x_j\vee w_j\) for each \(j\). The resulting fixed CNF \(\Gamma^{\rm cp}\) retains \(\Gamma(X)\) and has encoding length \(N+O(r\log(r+2))\) if \(N\) is the original indexed encoding. Retain all Boolean and commutator generators on both copies. There is a placeholder-linear formula certificate \(\widehat C(X,W,\widehat Y)\) for this full tuple of size \[ O(S^3+ST),\qquad \text{depth}\le d+6,\qquad \text{joint syntactic degree}\le D_0+\delta-1. \tag{2}\] Every monomial is an \(X\)-word, one original or added placeholder, then a \(W\)-word. Thus its polynomial is abecedarian in the three buckets \(X;\widehat Y;W\). This is a semantic output property; (2) does not assert that every intermediate gate has the source's interval type. A syntactically abecedarian formula of polynomial size and logarithmic depth is available by the preceding fixed-bucket construction.

There is also a constant-leaf substitution taking any certificate for the full copied tuple back to a certificate for \(F\), with no increase in formula size or depth. It takes this constructed \(\widehat C\) back to \(C\) as a formal polynomial. Thus polynomial-size fixed-depth placeholder-linear certificate existence for the original family is equivalent to such existence with semantically three-bucket output for the copied family, with the forward depth allowance (2). No syntactic-abecedarian fixed-depth equivalence or exact sequent-compiler-image membership is asserted.

First marked leaf. Expand only the part of placeholder degree one of the supplied formula. For every physical placeholder leaf occurrence \(a\), its parse contexts give \[ C(X,Y)=\sum_a L_a(X)Y_{i_a}R_a(X). \tag{3}\] At every other placeholder leaf substitute zero. Along the marked leaf's path, multiply the old sibling subformulas before it into \(L_a\) and those after it into \(R_a\), in inherited order; additions merely select the branch containing the marked occurrence. Scalar edge weights can be put in \(L_a\). Every expanded term containing exactly one placeholder chooses exactly one such leaf, so (3) holds despite cancellations. There are at most \(S\) terms, each pair of contexts has size \(O(S)\) and depth at most \(d+1\). No proof DAG is being unfolded here: the input is already the full formula tree.

Copy difference. For any old formula \(R\), define a placeholder-linear difference certificate \(E_R(X,W,U)\) by \[ E_c=0,\quad E_{x_j}=U_j,\quad E_{P+Q}=E_P+E_Q, \quad E_{P_1\cdots P_t}=\sum_{j=1}^t\left(\prod_{ij}P_i(W)\right). \tag{4}\] Induction, or ordinary ordered product telescoping, gives \[ E_R(X,W,W+X)=R(W)+R(X). \tag{5}\] Equivalently mark one old variable leaf in \(R\), evaluate its left parse context at \(X\) and its right context at \(W\), and place \(U_j\) between them. This flattened marked-leaf expression has size \(O(|R|^2)\), depth at most \(\operatorname{depth}R+2\), and syntactic degree at most that of \(R\). It does not recursively substitute large already-built certificates down every level. Every term has the required left/middle/right bucket form. Constants and zero contexts are harmless.

Original equality labels. Write the new clause polynomials in the displayed literal order as \[ A_j=w_j(1+x_j),\qquad B_j=x_j(1+w_j),\qquad K_j=x_jw_j+w_jx_j. \] The third is the corresponding retained cross-copy commutator. In the free binary algebra, \(A_j+B_j+K_j=w_j+x_j\). Define \(U_j=Y_{A_j}+Y_{B_j}+Y_{K_j}\), using precisely these original labels of the copied source. No equality polynomial is silently added as a new axiom. All other added Boolean/commutator labels remain in the tuple, even when unused by the constructed formula.

Forward identity. Set \[ \widehat C=\sum_a L_a(X)Y_{i_a}R_a(W) +\sum_a L_a(X)F_{i_a}(X)E_{R_a}(X,W,U). \tag{6}\] At the full copied tuple, (5) makes each summand pair equal to \(L_aF_{i_a}R_a(X)\); hence (6) specializes to \(C(X,F)=1\). At all placeholders zero, every term is zero. Every term contains exactly one placeholder. Original clause, Boolean and commutator polynomials \(F_{i_a}(X)\) in the second sum are old-variable factors, not omitted or renamed placeholders. All their letters lie in the left bucket, so the semantic three-bucket assertion holds term by term.

Reverse identity. Identify \(W=X\). Original clause/Boolean/commutator placeholders stay the same. Each added equality-clause placeholder maps to the original Boolean placeholder for \(x_j^2+x_j\), each copied Boolean placeholder to its original one, and every commutator placeholder maps to the original unordered-pair commutator placeholder or zero if the two indices coincide. Characteristic two removes orientation signs. These are exact generator identities under the identification, so this substitution takes every copied-source certificate back to an original-source certificate. It sends \(U_j\) to two identical Boolean placeholders plus zero, hence zero. Formula (6) therefore retracts exactly to (3), proving the stronger formal retraction for this construction.

Full ledger. In (3), each context pair costs \(O(S)\). Applying (4) to each right context costs \(O(S^2)\), with at most \(S\) contexts. Copying its corresponding original generator costs at most \(T\) per context. This proves \(O(S^3+ST)\), with no shared subtree counted free. A right context has depth at most \(d+1\); its flattened difference has depth at most \(d+3\). Substituting the three-term \(U_j\)'s adds at most one layer. Multiplying by \(L_a\) and \(F_{i_a}\), whose clause/field/commutator formulas have depth at most two, and summing gives \(d+6\). For each marked term, \(\deg L_a+\deg R_a\le D_0-1\). Its correction has joint degree at most \(D_0-1+\delta\). Before cancellation, substituting the full original generators into all placeholders costs at most \(D_0+\delta\): an original placeholder in the first sum contributes at most \(\delta\), and a new placeholder in the second sum contributes degree two. No equality cancellation resets that original substituted ceiling. The source construction uses exactly one copy per old variable and is independent of \(S\) or its proposed exponent. For \(r\le N\), its encoding and \(T=O(N+r^2)\) are polynomial in the original encoding.

Further sorting need not be inexpensive

Proposition (bucket sorting gap). There are unbounded copied unit-CNF families of encoding \(\Theta(t\log(t+2))\) with complete placeholder-linear, semantically three-bucket binary certificates of size \(O(t)\) and depth at most six, such that a specified stable refinement to five buckets produces another complete certificate requiring at least \(\binom{2t}{t}\) variable leaves in every noncommutative arithmetic formula over \(\mathbb F_2\), of any depth. The same lower bound holds after adding any polynomial every monomial of which contains a commutator placeholder. It is a lower bound for these chosen sorted representatives and this correction class, not for all certificates of the CNF; that CNF has a constant-size certificate.

Family and sorting. Use old variables \(g,u_1,v_1,\ldots,u_{2t},v_{2t}\), original unit clauses \(g,\neg g\) and all \(u_i,v_i\), then their copied source as above. Write \(g'\) for the copy of \(g\), \(J_g=g^2+g\), \(U_g\) for its three-label equality certificate, and \(Y_{J_g}\) for the retained original Boolean placeholder. Put \[ P_t=\prod_{i=1}^{2t}(u_i+v_i),\qquad D_t=Y_{g+}+Y_{g-}+P_t\bigl(gY_{J_g}+Y_{J_g}g'+J_gU_g\bigr). \tag{7}\] This is a specialization of the copy construction. At the full generator tuple, the parenthesis becomes \(gJ_g+J_gg'+J_g(g'+g)=0\), since \(g\) commutes with its own powers, and the first two terms become one. All placeholders zero give zero. The complete original tuple, including all unused labels, is retained.

Refine the left bucket into all \(u_i\)'s, then all \(v_i\)'s, then \(\{g\}\), followed by the placeholder bucket and the whole right-copy bucket. The stable sorting operator sorts each word by those bucket indices, preserving relative order inside each bucket, and extends linearly. On (7) it replaces precisely \(P_t\) by \[ P_t^{\rm sort}=\sum_{S\subseteq[2t]}\left(\prod_{i\in S}^{\uparrow}u_i\right) \left(\prod_{j\notin S}^{\uparrow}v_j\right). \tag{8}\] The same complete certificate identity holds with (8), because the parenthesis in (7) still specializes to zero. Thus sorting here preserves the identity but can change formula complexity. The unbounded-fan-in formula for (7) has linear size and depth at most six; all powers are written products.

A free constant restriction. Set \(Y_{J_g}=1\), every other placeholder to zero, \(g=0\), \(g'=1\), and all other right-copy variables to zero. The sorted certificate becomes exactly (8). Every correction supported on commutator placeholders becomes zero. This is an arithmetic restriction of the formal certificate polynomial; it is not a satisfying assignment or a legal restriction of the original CNF. It increases no formula size.

Cut-rank bound, with proof. For any free polynomial computed by a formula with \(L\) variable leaves, its degree-\(2t\) coefficient matrix at the contiguous length-\(t\) cut has rank at most \(L\). To see the standard marked-leaf argument directly, mark the leaf supplying the \(t\)-th letter of a parse word. If its old left/right parse contexts are \(L_a,R_a\) and the leaf letter is \(z_a\), then the degree-\(2t\) component is \[ \sum_a (L_a)_{t-1}\,z_a\,(R_a)_t. \tag{9}\] Every expansion word of degree \(2t\) has exactly one such leaf, so this equality survives cancellations and allows inhomogeneous intermediate gates. Each summand's cut matrix is an outer product, of rank at most one. Sum over at most \(L\) leaves. This is the usual noncommutative cut-rank method, not a new lower-bound technique or a cheap component-extraction algorithm.

In (8), restrict rows to \(u\)-words on subsets \(S\) of size \(t\) and columns to \(v\)-words on subsets \(T\) of size \(t\), each in increasing order. An entry is one exactly when \(T=[2t]\setminus S\). The submatrix is a permutation matrix of order \(\binom{2t}{t}\), over every field. Apply (9) to the restricted output formula to prove the lower bound. Literal expansion of (8) gives an upper bound \(O(t4^t)\), so the exponential scale of this particular control is determined up to polynomial factors.

Sorting ledger. The original source has \(4t+1\) variables and its copied CNF has \(8t+2\); both indexed encodings are \(\Theta(t\log(t+2))\). The full generator inventories are quadratic, not silently omitted from the interface. Formula (7) has joint degree at most \(2t+3\), and full raw generator substitution costs at most \(2t+4\), before its cancellations. The lower bound \(\binom{2t}{t}=2^{\Omega(t)}\) is superpolynomial in the original encoding. Yet \(Y_{g+}+Y_{g-}\) is already a constant-size certificate for this source, and is bucket-ordered for the same refinement. Hence the bound rejects this normalization/correction operation; it is not the required universal certificate hardness.
