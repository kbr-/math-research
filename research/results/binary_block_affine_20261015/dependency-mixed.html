Mixed feature space

Keep the proper binary charged graph pseudoideal \(J_D\), one-hot encoding and exact phase-adjusted cut-code dictionary. Fix one spanning tree. Outside it choose \(u\ge0\) distinct unused edges, and \(s\ge0\) pairwise disjoint nonempty blocks disjoint from those edges. On unused edge \(i\), use its two independent label indicators \(x_i,y_i\); the third indicator is \(1+x_i+y_i\), and \(x_iy_i=0\) in the local three-state algebra. On block \(j\), use a nonconstant binary polynomial \(z_j\) of ordinary degree at most \(1\le\delta\le h\). Every support is off the fixed tree. Put \(v=2u+s\ge1\).

Let \(X_i,Y_i,Z_j\) be formal Boolean variables and let \(\psi\) substitute \(x_i,y_i,z_j\). A standard monomial is squarefree and contains at most one of \(X_i,Y_i\) for each \(i\). Let \(\mathcal S_k\) be the homogeneous degree-\(k\) standard-monomial span. Its dimension is \[ a_k=[t^k](1+2t)^u(1+t)^s. \] For \(v\ge2\), counting the \(k\)-subsets containing a specified forbidden pair gives \[ a_k\ge\binom vk-u\binom{v-2}{k-2} =\left(1-\frac{uk(k-1)}{v(v-1)}\right)\binom vk. \] The union bound counts some bad subsets repeatedly, so it is a lower bound in the required direction. For \(k=1\) the subtracted term is zero; for \(u=0\) every squarefree monomial is standard.

Faithfulness. Every nonzero linear combination of standard monomials of degree at most \(k\) has \(\psi(F)\notin J_D\) if \(\delta k\le D\). Locally, \(\{1,x_i,y_i\}\) is an independent basis of the three-state edge functions. Each \(\{1,z_j\}\) is independent because \(z_j\) is nonconstant. Their tensor products on disjoint supports are independent. Thus a nonzero standard polynomial has a nonzero local-function image. In its ternary Fourier expansion at least one coefficient is nonzero; every exponent has edge support at most \(\delta k\), off the tree. No two distinct such exponents differ by a nonzero cut, because a vertex potential constant on every tree edge is constant everywhere. The cut-code dictionary therefore prevents quotient cancellation. Extension to \(\mathbb F_4\) and descent to binary coefficients preserve nonzeroness. This uses the local function space to choose independent vectors, not a globally consistent charged point algebra.

The full formal Boolean cube is not faithful: \(X_iY_i\) pulls back to zero. We use its Boolean certificates but select the weight from the standard subspace. Every Boolean certificate through degree \(c\) pulls back through \(\delta c\): for each binary feature \(b\) of degree at most \(\delta\), successive square reductions give a certificate for \(b^2+b\) through \(2\delta\), and a degree-\(c-2\) cofactor costs at most \(\delta(c-2)\). No certificate for \(X_iY_i\) is needed in the construction.

Finite cleanup theorem

Statement (mixed feature transfer). Take a complete two-layer accuracy-\(h\) source with a coherent constant bottom interpretation whose bottom companions are Boolean-zero within their original ceilings. Every actual parent input after that interpretation is an affine binary combination of \(1,x_i,y_i,z_j\). There are at most \(M\ge1\) parents, no raw coefficient readers or dependencies between parents. Keep all original companion generators and coefficient fields. Set \(L=\ln(4M)\), and \[ k=\begin{cases} \max(1,\lceil v/h\rceil),&v\le256hL,\\ \lceil2\sqrt{vL/h}\rceil,&v>256hL, \end{cases} \qquad T=\delta k. \] In the large regime require additionally \[ 2u k(k-1)\le v(v-1). \] If the original degree ceiling \(B\) covers every fresh generator and proposed source proof, with \(B\ge2h^2+3h\) and \(T(B+1)\le D\), there is one binary weight \(W\notin J_D\), \(\deg W\le T\), and one global old coefficient map of maximum degree at most \(T\), annihilating all weighted fresh generators and all used old axioms through \(T(B+1)\). Hence that complete source has no degree-\(B\) PC or ordinary NS refutation. The theorem gives no expanded-size or circuit bound on its interpretation polynomials. The initial weight is one; arbitrary incoming weighted faithfulness is not assumed.

Proof: choosing the common polynomial. Work first in the formal \(v\)-variable Boolean cube. Normalize zero input spans by zero rows and any span containing one by a constant row giving a unit; all their companions vanish. In every other span the homogeneous parts of an affine basis are independent, so its zero set is a nonempty affine flat of codimension equal to its rank.

In the small regime, every proper affine rank is at most \(v\le h(k+1)\); choose \(F=1\) and pack every parent as below. No pair-exclusion condition is required. In the large regime let \(r_0=h(k+1)\). The inequalities \(v>256hL\), \(L>1\) imply \[ k<v/(8h)+1<v/6,\quad r_0<v/8+2h<v/4, \quad k\le(v-r_0)/3. \] Call a proper parent high when its rank exceeds \(r_0\). By affine coordinates and degree-preserving Boolean reduction, restriction through degree \(k\) to any high zero flat has image dimension at most \[ \sum_{j\le k}\binom{v-r_0}{j} \le2e^{-r_0k/v}\binom vk. \] The first inequality is the exact flat restriction count; the second follows by comparing consecutive binomial terms and \(\binom{v-r_0}{k}/\binom vk\le e^{-r_0k/v}\). Restricting the domain to \(\mathcal S_k\) cannot increase this image rank. Summing over at most \(M\) high flats gives at most \[ 2M e^{-r_0k/v}\binom vk\le2M e^{-4L}\binom vk =\frac{1}{128M^3}\binom vk<\frac12\binom vk\le a_k. \] The last inequality is the pair-exclusion hypothesis. Therefore a nonzero \(F\in\mathcal S_k\) vanishes on every high flat. This is vanishing on the entire ambient Boolean flat, not merely its intersection with the ternary feature domain. Faithfulness supplies its later surviving pullback.

Proof: simultaneous certificates. A low proper parent has affine basis \(f_1,\ldots,f_r\) with \(r\le h(k+1)\), each a constant binary combination of its actual input list. Split these forms into \(h\) bins of size at most \(k+1\). In a bin, the exact telescoping identity \[ 1+\sum_{i=1}^t f_i\prod_{j<i}(1+f_j)=\prod_{i=1}^t(1+f_i) \] assigns coefficient polynomials of degree at most \(k\) to its one original factor row. Consequently its complete product is \(P=\prod_{i\le r}(1+f_i)\). Each input is in the span of the \(f_i\)'s, so its companion \(gP\) is a sum of \(f_i(1+f_i)\prod_{j\ne i}(1+f_j)\). For an affine binary form, \(f_i^2+f_i\) is a linear combination of the feature Boolean axioms. The resulting certificate is through \(r+1\), and multiplying by \(F\) costs at most \[ h(k+1)+1+k\le k(2h+1)+k. \]

For a high parent, an affine coordinate change and multilinear reduction give \[ F-H_a\in I_k(\mathrm{BOOL}),\qquad H_a=\sum_i A_{ai}g_{ai},\qquad\deg A_{ai}\le k-1. \] Indeed after choosing the constrained coordinates, every monomial left after subtracting the zero restriction contains one constrained coordinate; factoring it gives degree at most \(k-1\). Affine substitution and Boolean square reduction preserve the degree ceiling, including for redundant original inputs. Use those cofactors in the first row and zero in all others, making \(P_a=1+H_a\). Then \[ Fg_{ai}(1+H_a)=g_{ai}\bigl(F(1+F)+F(F+H_a)\bigr) \] has a Boolean certificate through \(2k+1\). Binary \(F\) has \(F^2+F\) certificates through \(2k\). The high-parent bound fits the preceding common ceiling, and all weighted coefficient-field images fit \(3k\). These are simultaneous maps on the same original feature variables, with one common \(F\); costs from different parents are not added or multiplied.

Proof: original replay. Pull back by \(\psi\). The selected standard \(F\) gives \(W=\psi(F)\notin J_D\), degree at most \(T\), and each parent coefficient image has degree at most \(T\). Pulled-back parent companion certificates cost at most \(T(2h+1)+T\); weighted fields cost at most \(3T\). These fit the original parent ceiling \(2h^2+3h\) and field degree two under the conservative weighted substitution ledger. Bottom coefficients are constants; their supplied certificates through their original ceilings, multiplied by \(W\), fit as well. Since \(B\) covers all original ceilings, every weighted source axiom is in \(J_D\) through \(T(B+1)\). Old axioms are multiplied by \(W\). No equality between an input-degree upper bound and an actual joint degree is needed.

For PC map every line \(p\) to \(W\Phi(p)\). An original predecessor of degree at most \(B-1\) has weighted substituted degree at most \(T(B-1)+T\); multiplication by a substituted variable of degree at most \(T\) fits \(T(B+1)\). Old variables stay fixed, also of degree at most \(T\). Bounded-degree absorption and linearity preserve membership in \(J_D\). An ordinary degree-\(B\) NS refutation first gives a same-degree PC refutation: expand each cofactor into monomials, multiply its axiom successively by their variables within the original summand degree, and sum the terms. Applying the just-proved PC replay handles those lines, without substituting a loose degree ceiling for an actual cofactor degree. A final one would put \(W\) in \(J_D\), a contradiction. This proves thm:mixed-feature-transfer with original product degrees, shared bottom choices, every coefficient field and whole-source costs retained. \(\square\)
