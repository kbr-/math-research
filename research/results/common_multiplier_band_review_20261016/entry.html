General statement. The line's general claim, from its proved results: on the proxy systems studied, product and functional pigeonhole closures and domain models, every joint saving found is a union of supports, a domination by one annihilator or a product of killers, and every module repair reduces to scalar or substitution repairs.

  • lem:functional-killer-transfer and lem:admissible-numbering-transfer: in the functional system below half the holes, the pigeon axioms add no killer to monomial errors, singly, or jointly under one admissible numbering.
  • cor:nested-hole-joint-transfer and check:six-by-five-numberings: such numberings exist for nested hole sets with room, and for three of the four recorded six-by-five sets.
  • lem:twist-window-trade, lem:top-window-twist-scalar and cor:twist-open-band: twisted repairs reduce to substitutions or scalars outside one band.
What the goal needs is a structural saving outside those kinds on a nontrivial unbounded family, measured in the protected quotient, that improves a multiplier budget after composition costs.

Obstacle

The line is stuck on the absence of a mechanism. The one place where its proxies differ from domain models is the pigeon axioms in a proper closure. In the functional system below half the holes, those axioms were proved transparent to monomial errors: with an adapted numbering, the projection onto the dance basis commutes with multiplication by the error, so every killer is the domain model's plus the closure's low-degree part. Module repairs were proved to reduce to the scalar and substitution classes everywhere but one band. So the proxies supply no saving of the admitted kind where they have been analysed. The remaining places are:

  • joint monomial sets with no admissible numbering, such as a triple whose hole sets cover all holes, where the computed excess is still zero;
  • the twist band, \(W\le n-1\) with \(TW+tT+\deg\nu\ge n+1\);
  • errors that are not monomials, which is where Pang's actual errors lie.

By lem:exact-common-kernel-selection the composition needs only that the common kernel is not contained in \(J\), so the certificate, not a rank count, is the endpoint. Downstream. Extra requirements. The route demands more than the target in two ways.

  • It works on pigeonhole proxies with monomial errors, chosen because their closures are computable. The goal asks about actual paid errors. Wherever joint transfer holds, the proxies' errors give at most support savings (Rank normalization), so this demand should be weakened: the errors tested should be ones the transfer results do not reach.

Rank normalization. Losses and survivors are measured in the same quotient, and the comparison bounds the route. Work modulo \(H\), whose degree-\(D\) part in the multilinear ring is the degree-\(D\) vanishing ideal of the set \(M\) of partial matchings, by Möbius inversion over the down-closed set \(M\). Suppose the joint identity \(\bigcap_E\operatorname{Ann}_C(E)=\bigcap_E\operatorname{Ann}_H(E)+C_K\) holds for a set of errors, with \(C_K=C\cap V_K\), \(K+e\le D\le\lceil N/2\rceil\), supports \(S_i\) on \(M\) and \(S=\bigcup S_i\). Then, with \(Z_i\) the functions vanishing on \(S_i\) and \(C_K/(Z_i\cap C_K)\cong C_K|_{S_i}\), each rank is \(\dim V_K|_{S_i}-\dim C_K|_{S_i}\), and the stacked rank is \(\dim V_K|_S-\dim C_K|_S\). The saving modulo \(C\) is therefore \(\bigl[\sum_i\dim V_K|_{S_i}-\dim V_K|_S\bigr]-\bigl[\sum_i\dim C_K|_{S_i}-\dim C_K|_S\bigr]\). The first bracket is the domain model's union-of-supports saving, an excluded kind. The second is nonnegative, since \(C_K|_S\) injects into \(\bigoplus_iC_K|_{S_i}\), a function vanishing on every \(S_i\) vanishing on \(S\). So under joint transfer the closure can only lower the support saving. The argument is the reviewer's, checked here; it is not registered, and the next research entry registers it. Relative contraction does not apply: no absolute smallness is claimed.

Decision. Core: joint-error-savings stays the worked statement, in the user's order, with the test moved to errors that the transfer results do not reach. The proof of lem:admissible-numbering-transfer passes to polynomial errors. Its step reads, in the functional-transfer entry: “for a matching \(E\), by the property, \(rx_E=\sum_tc_tx_tx_E\) is modulo \(H\) a combination of elements of \(T_D\cap B_D\)”. Summing over the monomials of \(E=\sum c_Ex_E\), each with the property under one numbering, \(rE\) is such a combination, and it lies in \(K_D\) only if it is zero. So the joint transfer holds for any polynomial errors of degree at most \(D-K\) whose matching monomials share an admissible numbering. This is an argument for the next research entry to register, not a claim of this review. Missing a hole is necessary for such a numbering, not sufficient. An error's representative modulo the closure's degree-one members also matters, as the Decisive test's Attempt shows.

Decisive test. Test disposition. New operation. Operation. The kernel comparison for an error with no representative to which the numbering criterion applies, where no recorded result describes the closure's killers. Prediction. If \(\operatorname{Ann}_C(E_1)\) exceeds \(\operatorname{Ann}_H(E')+C\cap V_1\) for every representative \(E'\), the pigeon axioms add killers to this error beyond the domain model, and the route continues on such errors with the Koszul bound. One size decides nothing about the range. Trivial cases. Duplicating \(E_1\) changes neither kernel. Adding a member of \(C\cap V_1\) to \(E_1\) leaves \(\operatorname{Ann}_C\) unchanged, the room being \(1+1+1\le3\), but changes \(\operatorname{Ann}_H\), so the comparison is taken over representatives. Members of \(C\cap V_2\) also leave \(\operatorname{Ann}_C\) unchanged, the room being \(1+2\le3\); the test takes linear representatives only, so its positive branch shows an excess beyond the domain model relative to them. The deciding structure of the system is its degree-one members, the pigeon axioms \(Q_i=1+\sum_jx_{ij}\), taken here as \(C\cap V_1=\operatorname{span}\{Q_i\}\), as check:functional-factor-kernels records at this size. \(E_1\) is not in that span: every element of the span has constant coefficients along each pigeon's row, while row \(0\) of \(E_1\) is \(x_{00}\) alone. Instance check. \(D=3\le\lceil5/2\rceil=3\) and \(K+e=1+1=2\le3\), so the transfer setting applies. Clearing hole \(h\) from \(E_1\) by adding \(\sum_ic_iQ_i\) forces \(c_i=[i=h]\), so at most one hole can be cleared, and every linear representative uses at least four of the five holes. Attempt. Tried before posing, over the linear representatives.

  • A representative that clears no hole uses all five holes, and missing a hole is necessary for an admissible numbering: at \(t=\varnothing\) a monomial with a pigeon in the top hole has no dance.
  • The representatives that clear one hole are \(E_1+Q_h\), \(h\le4\), images of \(E'=E_1+Q_4\) under the simultaneous permutation of pigeons and holes \(0\) to \(4\), which fixes \(E_1\) and \(C\). So \(E'\) stands for all of them.
  • In any admissible numbering for \(E'\) the cleared hole \(4\) is the top hole: any other top hole holds the pigeon of some monomial of \(E'\), which at \(t=\varnothing\) cannot move.
The dance rule (Impagliazzo, Pudlák and Sgall, ECCC TR97-042, Definition 2.3, page 4) moves the pigeons in increasing order, each to a strictly larger hole, every step staying one-to-one. The matching monomials of \(E'\) are \(1\), \(x_{jj}\) and \(x_{4j}\) for \(j\le3\). Two pigeons in the top two used hole positions, with the top hole free, therefore dance only if the one in the higher position moves first: the other, moving first, takes the top hole and leaves no larger hole for it. The single-variable \(t\) below lie in \(B_1\), each having a free larger hole. With \(h\) the hole at the fourth position and \(h'\) at the third, the monomial \(x_{4h}\) with \(t=x_{hh'}\) needs pigeon \(4\) before pigeon \(h\); the monomial \(x_{hh}\) with \(t=x_{4h'}\) needs pigeon \(h\) before pigeon \(4\). So no numbering has the property for \(E'\), and the criterion decides no representative. The Attempt stops there: no recorded argument gives \(\operatorname{Ann}_C(E_1)\). Record search.
