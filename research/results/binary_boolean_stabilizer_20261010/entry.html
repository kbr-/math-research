A colon transformation in the actual quotient

Let \(J\subseteq\mathbb F_2[x]_{\le D}\) be a proper degree-\(D\) pseudoideal containing all old Boolean equations. Write \(Q_i=\mathbb F_2[x]_{\le i}/(J\cap\mathbb F_2[x]_{\le i})\), with the natural inclusions into \(Q_D\). Fix integers \(\delta\ge1\), \(K\ge0\), \(s\ge1\) with \(K+3\delta\le D\). Let \[ 1\in U\subseteq Q_\delta,\qquad 0\ne V\subseteq Q_K^s, \qquad Y=\operatorname{span}(UV)\subseteq Q_{K+\delta}^s. \] Products are coordinatewise scalar multiplication, always through the displayed old cutoff. No unit is required in \(V\); for \(s>1\) it is not an algebra at all. Set \(L=K+\delta\).

Lemma (paid stable core). There exist \(1\in C\subseteq U\) and \(V\subseteq Z\subseteq Y\) with \[ CZ\subseteq Z,\qquad \dim C+\dim Z=\dim U+\dim V. \] In particular \[ \operatorname{codim}_U C\le q:= \min\{\dim U-1,\ \dim Y-\dim V\}. \] There are a nonzero \(z\in Z\) and a linear map \(\chi:C\to\mathbb F_2\), \(\chi(1)=1\), such that \(az=\chi(a)z\) for every \(a\in C\). All coordinates of \(z\) have representatives of degree at most \(L\).

Proof: one step. Start with \(U_0=U,V_0=V\). Maintain \(1\in U_i\subseteq U\), \(V\subseteq V_i\subseteq Y\), and \(U_iV_i\subseteq Y\). Every \(V_i\) is represented in degree at most \(L\), and every \(U_i\) in degree at most \(\delta\). If \(U_iV_i\not\subseteq V_i\), choose \(b\in V_i\) for which \(U_i b\not\subseteq V_i\), and define \[ U_{i+1}=\{a\in U_i:ab\in V_i\},\qquad V_{i+1}=V_i+U_i b. \] Here \(b\) is a tuple, so the condition is a vector-space preimage, not division by an element of a ring. The map \(a\mapsto ab\bmod V_i\) has kernel \(U_{i+1}\) and image \(V_{i+1}/V_i\). Rank-nullity gives \[ \dim U_{i+1}+\dim V_{i+1}=\dim U_i+\dim V_i. \] Also \(1\in U_{i+1}\) because \(b\in V_i\), and \(U_{i+1}\) is proper in \(U_i\) by choice of \(b\).

Proof: the uniform degree reserve. The nontrivial invariant is \(U_{i+1}V_{i+1}\subseteq Y\). For \(a\in U_{i+1}\), \(v\in V_i\), \(a'\in U_i\), the product \(av\) lies in \(Y\). For the other summands, \[ a(ba')=(ab)a', \] and \(ab\) has a representative in \(V_i\), so the right side lies in \(U_iV_i\subseteq Y\). This identity must be paid in the pseudoideal: \(b\) is represented through \(L\), each scalar through \(\delta\), and replacing \(ab\) or \(ba'\) by its degree-\(L\) representative first creates a relation through \(L+\delta\). Multiplying that relation by the other scalar costs at most \(L+2\delta=K+3\delta\). Bounded absorption then justifies the displayed reassociation. Thus every new \(V_i\) can again be represented in the fixed original \(Y\), of degree at most \(L\). The cutoff does not increase with the number of transformations.

Proof: terminate and split. Each nonterminal step reduces \(\dim U_i\), retaining the unit, so after at most \(\dim U-1\) steps reach \(C=U_i,Z=V_i\) with \(CZ\subseteq Z\). Rank-nullity throughout gives the dimension identity, and \(Z\subseteq Y\) gives the codimension bound. The action \(M_a:z'\mapsto az'\) is an endomorphism of \(Z\). These endomorphisms commute and satisfy \(M_a^2=M_a\): compare both products using degree-\(L\) representatives and the same \(L+2\delta\) reserve. The old Boolean polynomial \(a^2-a\) has a degree-at-most-\(2\delta\) Boolean certificate, whose product with a representative of \(z'\) fits that reserve.

A finite family of commuting idempotents over \(\mathbb F_2\) has a common nonzero eigenspace. Split \(Z\) successively into kernel and image of a basis of these operators, retaining a nonzero part; commutation preserves previous eigenspaces. Choose nonzero \(z\) there. Linearity of the action gives the linear eigenvalue map \(\chi\), and the unit acts as identity. No positivity, extension field, simultaneous polynomial selector of high degree, or unrestricted quotient multiplication is used. \(\square\)

The transformation is inspired by Beck–Lecouvey's Lemma 4.2. The direct preimage formulation here avoids inverses and works on tuple modules over the binary field. The preceding graded-envelope obstruction is respected: these are genuine Boolean actions on a represented stable space, not nilpotent degree-shift operators in a truncated ring.

Turn the core into simultaneous legal source maps

Consider one complete old-only level with arbitrary finite input tuples \(g_{a,i}\), all of degree at most the declared \(\delta\), and common accuracy \(h\ge1\). The old axioms \(\Gamma\subseteq J\) include Booleanity. Let \(B\) cover the old generator degrees, all fresh fields, and the uniform original companion ceiling \(h(\delta+1)+\delta\). Preserve this ceiling even after cancellations. Define the actual joint input map and input space by \[ \mathcal T:Q_K\longrightarrow Q_{K+\delta}^{\,s}, \qquad [f]\longmapsto([g_{a,i}f])_{a,i}, \qquad U=\operatorname{span}(1,[g_{a,i}]:a,i), \] where \(s\) is the total number of displayed inputs. Choose any nonzero binary subspace \(X\subseteq\mathcal T(Q_K)\), and put \[ Y=\operatorname{span}(UX),\qquad q=\min\{\dim U-1,\dim Y-\dim X\},\qquad T=\max\{1,\delta(\lceil q/h\rceil-1)_+\}. \] All dimensions are in the same coordinatewise old quotient.

Corollary (stable core exclusion). If \[ \max\{K+4\delta,\ K+\delta+TB\}\le D, \] there are an old binary polynomial \(f\notin J\), \(\deg f\le K+\delta\), and one coherent assignment of all fresh coefficients to binary old polynomials of degree at most \(T\), for which every source generator image multiplied by \(f\) belongs to \(J\). Consequently the complete source has no degree-at-most-\(B\) PC refutation, and no such NS refutation. This is a conditional criterion in the actual additive growth \(q\), not an estimate of \(q\) from source size.

Proof: get a recoverable tuple eigenvector. Apply the lemma with module input degree \(K+\delta\) and domain \(X\); its reserve is \(K+4\delta\). It gives a stable core \(C\subseteq U\) of codimension at most \(q\) and a nonzero joint eigenvector \(z\in Y\). Every vector of \(Y\) has a lift \(f\in\mathbb F_2[x]_{\le K+\delta}\) under the natural extension of \(\mathcal T\) to that degree: take a linear section of \(X\) through degree \(K\), multiply its lifts by degree-\(\delta\) representatives of \(U\), and take linear combinations. Thus \[ z_{a,i}=[fg_{a,i}],\qquad [a\,fg_{b,i}]=\chi(a)[fg_{b,i}]\quad(a\in C). \] If \(f\in J\), bounded absorption by every input would make \(z=0\), a contradiction. Notice that \(f\) itself is not asserted to be an eigenvector; the eigenvector lives in the actual joint error image.

Proof: split each genuine input tuple. Let \(G_a=\operatorname{span}([g_{a,i}])\) and \(C_a=G_a\cap C\). Then \(\dim(G_a/C_a)\le q\). If \(\chi\) is nonzero on \(C_a\), choose \(c\in C_a\) with \(\chi(c)=1\), lifting it as a constant linear combination of that block's actual inputs. Use these constants in its first row and zero in later rows. The product becomes \(1-c\). For every input of that block the eigenvector identity gives \[ [fg_{a,i}(1-c)]=0. \] No input is added, and this does not require \(f(1-c)\in J\).

If instead \(\chi(C_a)=0\), every actual polynomial \(c\) of degree at most \(\delta\) representing a class in \(C_a\) has \(fc\in J\). Indeed express its class as a constant combination of the block inputs, so \([fc]\) is the corresponding combination of the coordinates of \(z\). Its eigenvalue under \(c\) is zero, giving \(fc^2\in J\). Boolean reduction of \(f(c^2-c)\) through degree \(K+3\delta\) gives \(fc\in J\). Any discrepancy between representatives already belongs to \(J\) and is absorbed within the same reserve.

Choose residual representatives \(v_1,\ldots,v_r\) from the actual tuple whose classes form a basis of \(G_a/C_a\), \(r\le q\). For each input write, in the quotient, \[ [g_{a,i}]=[c_i]+\sum_j d_{ij}[v_j],\qquad [c_i]\in C_a, \] using the actual difference polynomial for \(c_i\). The historical packing identity on these \(r\) selected inputs gives coefficient maps of degree at most \(\delta(\lceil r/h\rceil-1)_+\), with product \(Z_a=\prod_j(1-v_j)\). They are legal coefficients in the original input positions. At \(r=0\), take all coefficients zero and \(Z_a=1\). Then \[ fg_{a,i}Z_a=fc_iZ_a+ f\Big(\sum_jd_{ij}v_j\Big)Z_a\in J. \] The first term uses the already obtained final membership \(fc_i\in J\); the second is a bounded Boolean consequence. The cores \(C_a\) need not be nested: one common tuple eigenvector handles all of them.

Proof: preserve original costs and replay. Put \(L=K+\delta\). A substituted product has degree at most \(h(T+\delta)\), and the preceding weighted companion calculations fit \[ L+\delta+h(T+\delta)\le L+TB. \] The inequality uses \(T\ge1\) and \(B\ge h(\delta+1)+\delta\). The first-row constant case fits \(L+2\delta\). Every fresh-field image \(\beta^2-\beta\) has an old Boolean certificate through \(2T\), and weighting costs at most \(L+2T\le L+TB\). Weighted old axioms fit the same budget. Apply all maps simultaneously; they depend only on old variables and there is no within-level composition. A degree-\(B\) PC proof, under this substitution and weight, stays through \(L+TB\): a multiplication step pays at most \(T\) for the image of its variable. Its conclusion would put \(f\) in \(J\), contradicting survival. \(\square\)

If \(\mathcal T(Q_K)=0\) and \(B\le D\), \(f=1\) and all-zero fresh coefficients already give weighted companion membership and the degree-\(B\) exclusion. The displayed nonzero-\(X\) criterion handles the other case. Earlier prefixes, supplied weights and later dependent levels are not silently included in this one-level statement; their ledgers require a separate composition.

Comparison and what remains open

The present route uses absolute growth: for such an \(X\), \(\dim UX-\dim X=(\rho-1)\dim X\). A small relative increase can still be an enormous \(q\), so neither statement automatically supplies the other's affordable hypothesis. Here the stable core is constructed rather than assumed; the new unproved quantitative task is to find \(X\) with small enough absolute growth for the actual source.

At \(q=0\), all inputs in the generated \(U\) act on the selected joint image already, so the corollary gives constant coefficient maps for any number of old-only blocks. For nonzero \(q\), historical packing pays only the residual bound, with one common weight. This positive construction goes beyond another abstract Boolean representation, but it must not make an exact invariant domain or small \(q\) a mandatory condition on every possible lower-bound route. Arbitrary compiled sources are not shown to satisfy it.
