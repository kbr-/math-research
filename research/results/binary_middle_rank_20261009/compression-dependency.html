Degree-compatible circulation grading

Use the charged \(\mathbb F_4\) pseudoideal \(J_D\) on a simple connected oriented graph, with \(n\) vertices, maximum degree \(\Delta\), expansion \(\varepsilon>0\), and \[ \max(4,2\Delta)\le D<\varepsilon n/8. \] Nonzero total charge specifies the intended inconsistent system. Let \(C_1,\ldots,C_r\) be pairwise edge-disjoint simple cycles. Choose one edge \(e_i\) on each, a label \(a_i\in\mathbb F_3\), and an oriented circulation \(\kappa_i\) with \((\kappa_i)_{e_i}=1\). Put \(x_i=x_{e_i,a_i}\). The group \(H=\langle\kappa_i\rangle\cong\mathbb F_3^r\) acts by the recorded degree-preserving translations.

Over \(\mathbb F_4\), its character projections are \[ \Pi_\alpha=3^{-r}\sum_{u\in\mathbb F_3^r}\omega^{-\alpha\cdot u}T_{\sum_i u_i\kappa_i}, \qquad\alpha\in\mathbb F_3^r. \] The scalar \(3^r\) is nonzero (indeed one) in this field. Orthogonality follows by multiplying the one-coordinate sums \(1+\omega+\omega^2=0\). These are degree-preserving projections on the polynomial space and on its quotient, because every translation preserves \(J_D\). A degree-\(b\) monomial touches at most \(b\) of the edge-disjoint cycles, so each of its eigencomponents has character support at most \(b\). Consequently every nonzero eigencomponent of a degree-\(b\) quotient class has support at most \(b\). Within a paid product window, multiplying components adds their characters. This is a filtered partial multiplication statement, not a ring structure on the entire quotient.

Each selected indicator is \(x_i=1+\omega^{-a_i}U_{e_i}+\omega^{a_i}V_{e_i}\). Its three terms have eigencharacters \(0,e_i,-e_i\) in the cycle index space, with nonzero coefficients. A product on a set \(T\subseteq[r]\) therefore has exactly one Fourier monomial for every character supported in \(T\). All such selected-edge characters are distinct modulo the cut code: pairing a cut supported on the selected edges with \(\kappa_i\) forces each of its coordinates to vanish.

Compression needs a hitting support

Lemma (circulation compression cost). Let \(\mathcal H\) be a nonempty family of nonempty subsets of \([r]\), \(d=\max_{T\in\mathcal H}|T|\), and \(z_T=\prod_{i\in T}x_i\). Suppose \(\deg W\le b\), \(W\notin J_D\), \(b+d\le D\), and for arbitrary old polynomials \(q_T\) over \(\mathbb F_4\), \[ \deg q_T<|T|,\qquad W(z_T-q_T)\in J_D\quad(T\in\mathcal H). \] Every maximum-support nonzero circulation eigencomponent of \([W]\) has support meeting every \(T\). In particular \(b\ge\tau(\mathcal H)\). If \(\tau(\mathcal H)+d\le D\), this minimum weight degree is attained, when references may be chosen, by binary \(W\) with every \(q_T=0\).

Proof. Let \(s\) be the maximum support size of a nonzero eigencomponent of \([W]\), and choose one with character \(\alpha\) and support \(S\), \(|S|=s\). Its polynomial representative \(W_\alpha=\Pi_\alpha W\) has degree at most \(b\) and survives. Components lying in \(J_D\) may be discarded when multiplying within \(b+d\le D\), by bounded absorption.

If \(S\cap T=\varnothing\), choose \(\beta\) with value one on \(T\) and zero elsewhere. In the eigencomponent of \(Wz_T\) at \(\alpha+\beta\), any contributing nonzero component \(W_\gamma\) must agree with \(\alpha\) outside \(T\). It already has the \(s\) nonzero coordinates of \(S\), so maximality of \(s\) forces its coordinates on \(T\) to be zero. Thus \(\gamma=\alpha\), and the only contributing term of \(z_T\) is its nonzero scalar multiple of \(\prod_{i\in T}U_{e_i}\). Multiplication by that degree-\(|T|\) character is injective on degree at most \(b\) in the charged quotient, since it translates distinct cut-code classes without merging them and \(b+|T|\le D\). The resulting component of \(Wz_T\) is therefore nonzero.

But every component of \(q_T\) has support at most \(|T|-1\). Every nonzero component of \(Wq_T\) consequently has support at most \(s+|T|-1\), whereas \(\alpha+\beta\) has support \(s+|T|\). Its component there is zero, contradicting the supplied membership. Hence \(S\) hits every \(T\), so \(b\ge s\ge\tau(\mathcal H)\).

For attainability choose a minimum hitting set \(S_0\) and \(W=\prod_{i\in S_0}(1-x_i)\). Every product \(Wz_T\) has a local Boolean zero factor, with certificates through \(\tau(\mathcal H)+|T|\le D\). Its weight has \(2^{|S_0|}\) nonzero selected-edge characters, distinct modulo cuts, hence survives. Its ordinary degree is \(|S_0|\), and references zero work. \(\square\)

The lemma covers arbitrary noninvariant weights and references mixing edges on and off the cycles. It is not a deduction from a pointwise model of the inconsistent base. The orbit-kernel obstruction is not contradicted: no kernel is required to be invariant under all translations. Projections are used to examine an asserted polynomial relation, not to replace its common kernel by an invariant one.

All bottom choices, and a cheap complete cleanup

Proposition (charged compression cleanup). Let \(\mathcal H\) be \(d\)-uniform in the preceding setup, and take common accuracy \(h\ge d\). For every \(T\), introduce the complete bottom block with inputs \((1-x_i)_{i\in T}\), product \(P_T\), and every companion and fresh binary domain. Let one complete parent read all these actual products. For any legal constant bottom-row assignment, any \(W\notin J_D\), \(\deg W\le b\), and any old references \(\deg q_T<d\), if \[ W(1-x_i)P_T\in J_D,\quad W(P_T-q_T)\in J_D,\quad b+2h+d\le D, \] then \(b\ge\tau(\mathcal H)\). Nevertheless, if \(3d\le D\), exact bottom specialization and a parent row selecting one \(P_{T_0}\) give a binary surviving degree-\(d\) weight annihilating all extension companions, with actual weighted Boolean certificates through \(3d\). Original bottom and parent source charges remain \(2h+1\) and \(h(2h+1)+2h\).

Proof of the lower bound. The identity \(1-z_T=\sum_{i\in T}(1-x_i)\prod_{j\in T,\ j<i}x_j\) puts \(WP_T(1-z_T)\) in \(J_D\) by the supplied complete companions and bounded absorption. The prefix identity for \(P_T-1\) expresses it as a combination of the same inputs \(1-x_i\), whose products with \(z_T\) vanish by Booleanity. Thus \(W(P_T-1)z_T\in J_D\) as well. Their sum gives \(W(P_T-z_T)\in J_D\), with a conservative original-charge bound \(b+2h+d\). Subtract the supplied reference agreement to obtain \(W(z_T-q_T)\in J_D\); the lemma applies. This argument is uniform in the legal constant rows. In particular it does not assume the exact assignment used for the positive statement.

Proof of cleanup. In every bottom block select each of its \(d\) literal inputs once and zero-pad the rest, giving \(P_T=z_T\). Choose any \(T_0\in\mathcal H\); in the parent select \(P_{T_0}\) in its first row and set the other coefficients to zero. Put \(W=z_{T_0}\). Bottom weighted companions have the zero factor \((1-x_i)x_i\) and degree at most \(2d+1\). Parent weighted companions are \(z_{T_0}(1-z_{T_0})z_T\), with Boolean certificates through \(3d\). All coefficient domains vanish at the assigned constants. The weight survives by independence of the selected-edge character classes. The assignments are global and coherent. Their smaller specialized degrees do not replace the original source charges in any replay. \(\square\)

These are genuine lower-degree-reference comparisons, stronger than annihilating the actual errors. The earlier one-parent elimination and Boolean cleanup construction already explain why the positive part is easy. The new part is the negative statement modulo the actual charged old relations, including arbitrary surviving weights in its paid window.

A linear old window with many disjoint cycles

The hypothesis is not restricted to a sublinear number of cycles. Given a simple cubic expander \(H\) on \(N\) vertices with edge expansion \(\varepsilon_0>0\), replace each vertex by a triangle, attaching its three incident old edges at the three distinct triangle vertices. The resulting simple cubic graph has \(n=3N\) vertices and \(r=N\) vertex-disjoint triangles. It has expansion at least \[ \varepsilon'=\frac{2\varepsilon_0}{9+2\varepsilon_0}<1. \] Here is a proof of that bound. For a vertex set \(S\) of size at most \(3N/2\), let \(f\) count full triangles and \(p\) partial triangles. Then \(f\le N/2\) and \(|S|\le3f+2p\). Its boundary size \(B\) is at least \(2p\). At least \(\varepsilon_0f-3p\) old edges go from full to empty triangles whenever that quantity is positive; including the internal boundaries gives \(B\ge\varepsilon_0f-p\) in every case. Hence \(p\le B/2\), \(\varepsilon_0f\le B+p\le3B/2\), and \(|S|\le(9/(2\varepsilon_0)+1)B\), proving the claim. No proof-calculus encoding is changed; the earlier graph-uniform theorem applies to this graph.

Take any such fixed expander family, arbitrary charges of nonzero total, and \(D=\lfloor\varepsilon'n/16\rfloor\). For large \(n\), the old degree window is valid and \(D<r/2\). The proved hypergraph construction, with its variable count now \(r\) and exponent three, supplies at most \(r^3\) distinct \(d=\lceil\log_2r\rceil\) supports with \(\tau(\mathcal H)>\lfloor r/2\rfloor>D\) for sufficiently large \(r\). Take \(h=d\). No surviving weight with \(b+3d\le D\) can make all bottom values strictly lower-degree modulo \(J_D\), under any legal constant bottom assignment annihilating the complete weighted companions. Yet the explicit degree-\(d\) cleanup works, and all original source charges are \(O((\log n)^2)\), below this linear \(D\) eventually. The fixed graph family is independent of the inventory exponent; no new existence theorem for cubic expanders is claimed.
