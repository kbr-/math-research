General statement. lem:twisted-certificate-reduction: a repair into \(R^k\) with \(R\) acting through commuting matrices \(\rho(x_i)\), whose survival is certified in a coordinate of \(J^k\), is certified by a matrix coefficient \(\tau(g)=e_l^{\mathsf T}\rho(g)\nu\); a change of basis \(P\in GL_k(R)\) of degree \(p\) diagonalizing \(\rho\) into substitutions gives a substitution repair of degree at most \(\deg\nu+p\); and when only \(x_1\) is twisted and carries a Boolean axiom, over any field and for any \(k\), every repair splits into a substitution repair by \(x_1\mapsto1\) or \(x_1\mapsto0\), at room cost \(T\), a result that covers the settings of the triangular twist and the quadratic twist \(t^2-x_2\), both also split directly.

Twisted certificate reduction

Statement. Over a field \(k\), let \(R=k[x_1,\ldots,x_n]\), axioms \(a_j\), \(J\subseteq V_{D'}\) the old degree-\(D'\) closure (a closure: closed under multiplication by polynomials within degree), and \(\rho:R\to\operatorname{Mat}_k(R)\) the algebra map given by commuting matrices \(\rho(x_i)\) with entries of degree at most \(T\). Let \(M_\rho\) be \(R^k\) with \(g\cdot u=\rho(g)u\), so that \(J^k\) is closed under each \(\rho(x_i)\) within degree. Let \(\nu\in R^k\), errors \(E_i\) with \(\deg(wE_i)\le D-K\), protected \(w\), with \(\rho(a_jm)\nu\in J^k\) for \(\deg(a_jm)\le D-K\) (soundness), \(\rho(wE_i)\nu\in J^k\) (killing), \(\rho(w)\nu\notin J^k\) (survival), and \(T(D-K)+\deg\nu\le D'\). (a) Some coordinate \(l\) has \(\tau(g)=e_l^{\mathsf T}\rho(g)\nu\) with \(\tau(w)\notin J\), while every coordinate of \(\rho(g)\nu\) lies in \(J\) for \(g\) in the closure of the axiom multiples and weighted errors at degree \(D-K\); this is the last part of lem:module-repair-factorization for \(N=M_\rho\), \(J_N=J^k\). (b) If \(P\in GL_k(R)\) with \(\deg P,\deg P^{-1}\le p\) makes \(P\rho(x_i)P^{-1}=\operatorname{diag}(\phi_1(x_i),\ldots,\phi_k(x_i))\), and \(T(D-K)+\deg\nu+2p\le D'\), then some \(b\) gives a substitution repair \(\phi_b(a_jm)f,\ \phi_b(wE_i)f\in J\), \(\phi_b(w)f\notin J\) with \(f=(P\nu)_b\), of degree at most \(\deg\nu+p\). (c) For \(\rho(x_1)=\bigl(\begin{smallmatrix}x_1&q\\0&0\end{smallmatrix}\bigr)\), \(q\in R\), and \(\rho(x_i)=x_iI\) for \(i\ge2\), \(\rho(g)=\bigl(\begin{smallmatrix}g&q\,\Delta_1(g)\\0&\sigma(g)\end{smallmatrix}\bigr)\), with \(\Delta_1(g)=(g-g|_{x_1=0})/x_1\) and \(\sigma\) the substitution \(x_1\mapsto0\). (d) In (c), if \(x_1^2-x_1\) is an axiom, \(D-K\ge2\) and \(D-K+\deg q+\deg\nu_2\le D'\), then every repair yields either the substitution repair \(\nu_2\) with \(\sigma\) (when \(\sigma(w)\nu_2\notin J\)) or the scalar repair \(f_1=\nu_1+q\nu_2\), of degree at most \(\max(\deg\nu_1,\deg q+\deg\nu_2)\). (e) Over any field with \(n\ge2\), the Boolean axiom \(x_1^2-x_1\), \(\rho(x_1)=\bigl(\begin{smallmatrix}0&x_2\\1&0\end{smallmatrix}\bigr)\) (characteristic polynomial \(t^2-x_2\), whose eigenvalues lie outside \(R\), though not outside \(R/J\) when \(x_2\) is Boolean) and \(\rho(x_i)=x_iI\) for \(i\ge2\), with \(D-K\ge2\) and degree room \(D-K+\deg\nu+2\le D'\), every repair yields a substitution repair by \(x_1\mapsto1\) with multiplier \(x_2\nu_2\) or by \(x_1\mapsto x_2\) with multiplier \(\nu_2\). (f) Over any field, for any \(k\) and any \(\rho(x_1)\) commuting with \(\rho(x_i)=x_iI\), \(i\ge2\), with the Boolean axiom \(x_1^2-x_1\), \(D-K\ge2\) and room \(T(D-K)+T+\deg\nu\le D'\): with \(u_1=\rho(x_1)\nu\) and \(u_0=\nu-u_1\), \(\rho(g)\nu\equiv g|_{x_1=1}u_1+g|_{x_1=0}u_0\pmod{J^k}\) for \(g\) in the window, and every repair yields a substitution repair by \(x_1\mapsto1\) with a coordinate of \(u_1\) or by \(x_1\mapsto0\) with a coordinate of \(u_0\); (f) covers the settings of (d) and (e) at its own room, with repairs of its own form; (e)'s \(x_1\mapsto x_2\) form, which needs no Boolean \(x_2\), is a combination of (f)'s two forms when \(x_2\) is Boolean, since \(g|_{x_1=x_2}\equiv x_2\,g|_{x_1=1}+(1-x_2)\,g|_{x_1=0}\).

Proof. (a) As in the last part of lem:module-repair-factorization: \(\{g\in V_{D-K}:\rho(g)\nu\in J^k\}\) is closed under each \(x_i\) within degree, since \(\rho(x_ig)\nu=\rho(x_i)(\rho(g)\nu)\) and \(J^k\) is closed under \(\rho(x_i)\), of degree at most \(T(D-K)+\deg\nu\le D'\); it contains the axiom multiples and weighted errors, hence their closure; survival gives a coordinate \(l\) with \(\tau(w)\notin J\). (b) \(P\rho(g)P^{-1}=\operatorname{diag}(\phi_b(g))\), both sides being algebra maps agreeing on generators. For \(g\) with \(\rho(g)\nu\in J^k\), \(P\rho(g)\nu=\operatorname{diag}(\phi_b(g))P\nu\) has coordinates \(\phi_b(g)(P\nu)_b\) in \(J\), \(P\) carrying \(J^k\) into \(J^k\) within the degree room; and if every \(\phi_b(w)(P\nu)_b\) were in \(J\), then \(\rho(w)\nu=P^{-1}\operatorname{diag}(\phi_b(w))P\nu\) would be in \(J^k\); so some \(b\) survives. (c) \(\rho(x_1)^j=\bigl(\begin{smallmatrix}x_1^j&qx_1^{j-1}\\0&0\end{smallmatrix}\bigr)\) for \(j\ge1\), and \(\rho(x_i)\), \(i\ge2\), are scalar; for \(g=x_1^jg'\), \(g'\) free of \(x_1\), \(\rho(g)=g'\rho(x_1)^j\), whose entries are \(g\), \(qg/x_1=q\Delta_1(g)\), \(0\) and \(0\) for \(j\ge1\), and \(g'I\) for \(j=0\), where \(\Delta_1(g)=0\) and \(\sigma(g)=g\); linearity gives the formula. (d) The second coordinate of \(\rho(g)\nu\) is \(\sigma(g)\nu_2\), which lies in \(J\) for every axiom multiple and every \(wE_i\) by soundness and killing; if \(\sigma(w)\nu_2\notin J\), \(\nu_2\) with \(\sigma\) is a substitution repair. Otherwise: soundness at \(a=x_1^2-x_1\) gives \(a\nu_1+q(x_1-1)\nu_2\in J\), and \(a\nu_1\in J\), so with \(h=q\nu_2\), \((x_1-1)h\in J\) and \(h\equiv x_1h\); then \(q\Delta_1(g)\nu_2=\Delta_1(g)h\equiv\Delta_1(g)x_1h=(g-\sigma(g))h\), and \(\sigma(g)h=q\,\sigma(g)\nu_2\in J\) whenever \(\sigma(g)\nu_2\in J\), within the degree room; so the first coordinate \(g\nu_1+q\Delta_1(g)\nu_2\equiv g(\nu_1+h)\) modulo \(J\) for every axiom multiple, every \(wE_i\) and \(g=w\). Hence \(f_1=\nu_1+h\) kills and is sound, and \(wf_1\equiv e_1^{\mathsf T}\rho(w)\nu\); survival, with \(\sigma(w)\nu_2\in J\), puts the first coordinate outside \(J\). Equivalently, the involution \(Q=\bigl(\begin{smallmatrix}1&q\\0&-1\end{smallmatrix}\bigr)\) gives \(Q\rho(x_1)Q=\bigl(\begin{smallmatrix}x_1&(x_1-1)q\\0&0\end{smallmatrix}\bigr)\), diagonal modulo the relation soundness supplies. (e) Identify \((u_0,u_1)\) with \(u_0+u_1y\) in \(S=R[y]/(y^2-x_2)\): \(\rho(x_1)(u_0,u_1)=(x_2u_1,u_0)\) is multiplication by \(y\), and \(J^2\) becomes \(J\oplus Jy\); write \(f=\nu_1+\nu_2y\). Soundness at \(x_1^2-x_1\), whose image is \(y^2-y=x_2-y\), gives \((x_2-y)f=x_2(\nu_1-\nu_2)+(x_2\nu_2-\nu_1)y\in J\oplus Jy\), so \(\nu_1\equiv x_2\nu_2\). For \(g=g_0+x_1g_1\) with \(g_0,g_1\) free of \(x_1\) (every \(g\) is so modulo multiples of \(x_1^2-x_1\), a reduction that does not raise degree, on which \(\rho(\cdot)\nu\) lies in \(J^2\) by soundness), \(\rho(g)\nu\) is \((g_0+g_1y)(\nu_1+\nu_2y)=(g_0\nu_1+x_2g_1\nu_2)+(g_0\nu_2+g_1\nu_1)y\equiv x_2\nu_2\,g|_{x_1=1}+\nu_2\,g|_{x_1=x_2}\,y\), using \(\nu_1\equiv x_2\nu_2\) by closure within the degree room. Both components lie in \(J\) for every axiom multiple and weighted error, and survival puts one component of \(\rho(w)\nu\) outside \(J\): a substitution repair by \(x_1\mapsto1\) with \(x_2\nu_2\), or by \(x_1\mapsto x_2\) with \(\nu_2\). (f) Soundness at \(a=x_1^2-x_1\) gives \(\rho(a)\nu=\rho(x_1)u_1-u_1\in J^k\), so \(\rho(x_1)u_1\equiv u_1\) and \(\rho(x_1)u_0=u_1-\rho(x_1)u_1\equiv0\). For \(g=g_0+x_1g_1\), \(g_0,g_1\) free of \(x_1\) (every \(g\) modulo multiples of \(a\), without raising degree), \(\rho(g)=g_0I+g_1\rho(x_1)\), so \(\rho(g)\nu=g_0(u_0+u_1)+g_1u_1=g|_{x_1=0}u_0+g|_{x_1=1}u_1\). If \(\rho(g)\nu\in J^k\), then \(\rho(x_1)\rho(g)\nu=\rho(x_1g)\nu\in J^k\) by closure within the room; write \(x_1g=x_1\,g|_{x_1=1}+a\,c'\) with \(\deg c'\le\deg g-1\); \(\rho(x_1g|_{x_1=1})\nu=g|_{x_1=1}u_1\) exactly, and \(\rho(ac')\nu=\rho(c')\rho(a)\nu\in J^k\), built from \(\rho(a)\nu\in J^k\) by applying the \(\rho(x_i)\) one monomial factor at a time, each intermediate of degree at most \(T(D-K+1)+\deg\nu\le D'\); so \(g|_{x_1=1}u_1\in J^k\), and then \(g|_{x_1=0}u_0\in J^k\). So every coordinate of \(u_1\) is sound and kills with \(x_1\mapsto1\), and every coordinate of \(u_0\) with \(x_1\mapsto0\); \(\rho(w)\nu\notin J^k\) puts \(w|_{x_1=1}u_1\) or \(w|_{x_1=0}u_0\) outside \(J^k\), giving a coordinate that survives.
