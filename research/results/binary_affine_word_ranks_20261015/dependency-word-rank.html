Source comparison and the tested invariant

The retrieved primary Limaye–Srinivasan–Tavenas report defines its relative rank in Section 2.1 and assumes homogeneous noncommutative formulas in Theorem 4. Its Lemma 6 converts an already homogeneous formula computing an ordered set-multilinear output into the ordered model without increasing size or product depth. It is not a generic homogenization theorem. The definition, Lemma 6 proof, Proposition 15 proof and Corollary 16 proof were read; this entry imports no full-paper certification or numerical lower-bound exponent. The recorded binary component obstruction already forbids a generic free fixed-depth component extraction.

For a free polynomial \(C\) in the alphabet \(Z\) of all old variables and all input, Boolean and commutator placeholders, let \(C_r\) be its homogeneous degree-\(r\) part, counting each letter with degree one. Its word tensor has entries \([z_{i_1}\cdots z_{i_r}]C\). For any \(I\subseteq[r]\), flatten it into a matrix \(M_{r,I}(C)\), using the positions in \(I\) for the row index and the remaining positions for the column index, in their original relative order. Contiguous Nisan cuts and the positive/negative position partitions of ordered set-multilinear coefficient matrices are included. The proposed necessary premise to test is that every certificate has a superpolynomial rank in some such matrix, possibly after fixed positionwise linear projections. The witness refutes this premise for arbitrary choices of \(r,I\), not just one selected slice.

Binary word-rank witness

Proposition. Fix \(\Delta\). For every bounded-degree ternary one-hot Tseitin CNF from the complete formula theorem, on \(n\) vertices and \(m\) edges with nonzero total charge, set \(s=n+m+1\). There is an original-input placeholder-linear certificate \(C\in\mathbb F_2\langle X,Y\rangle\) of degree \(O_\Delta(s)\) such that, simultaneously for every \(r\) and \(I\subseteq[r]\), \[ \operatorname{rank}_{\mathbb F_2}M_{r,I}(C)\le O_\Delta(s^2). \] The same bound holds after arbitrary positionwise \(\mathbb F_2\)-linear tensor maps. Moreover \(C\), regarded over \(\mathbb F_4\), has an ordered sum-of-products-of-homogeneous-linear-forms formula of size \(O_\Delta(s^3)\) and constant depth, at most four with scalar constants as leaves (three with weighted sum gates). No constant-depth formula over \(\mathbb F_2\) is asserted.

Proof. Reread the complete earlier construction before its binary product-coordinate descent. Its certificate \(D\) over \(\mathbb F_4\) is a sum of \(T=O_\Delta(s^2)\) scalar-weighted ordered products of length \(O_\Delta(s)\). Expand only the constant-arity local certificates into scalar-weighted word-placeholder-word terms, as that proof already does. Every remaining nonconstant factor is an old variable, one placeholder, or a homogeneous three-term Fourier linear form. Constants are absorbed into the scalar coefficient. Thus each product is individually homogeneous, although their degrees can differ, and the stated constant-depth size bound holds.

Write \(\mathbb F_4=\mathbb F_2(\omega)\), \(\omega^2+\omega+1=0\). Let \(\sigma\) square scalar coefficients while fixing every letter, including placeholders; this is not the polynomial operation \(D\mapsto D^2\). Set \[ C=\omega^2D+\omega\sigma(D). \] If \(D=C_0+\omega C_1\) with binary coefficient polynomials, direct expansion gives \(C=C_0\). Equivalently \(\sigma(C)=C\). Since the original tuple \(F\) is binary, substitution commutes with \(\sigma\); hence \(C(X,F)=\omega^2+\omega=1\) and \(C(X,0)=0\). Placeholder-linearity is retained. Over \(\mathbb F_4\), this expression has at most \(2T\) homogeneous product terms and the same linear degree bound.

For fixed \(r\), discard terms of other degrees. A product of \(r\) homogeneous linear forms has a pure word-coefficient tensor, so every position flattening has rank at most one over \(\mathbb F_4\). Therefore \(\operatorname{rank}_{\mathbb F_4}M_{r,I}(C)\le2T\). Its entries are binary, and matrix rank is unchanged by field extension: a nonzero maximal minor remains nonzero, while larger minors remain zero. This proves the binary rank bound. Positionwise linear maps preserve the pure-tensor decomposition over \(\mathbb F_4\), and the resulting matrix is still binary, proving the last assertion. In particular, projecting each position to its prescribed variable block is covered. \(\square\)

Nonvacuity. This is the same fixed-degree expander family with nonzero charge used for the old PC barrier, at arbitrarily large sizes. The full tuple contains the original clauses, all Boolean generators and all commutators; none is suppressed to make the rank small. Degenerate cases. Absent degrees give zero matrices, an empty side of a flattening gives rank at most one, and an isolated inconsistent vertex has a one-placeholder witness. Zero total charge on a connected graph lies outside the hypothesis.

Scope. This refutes a universally superpolynomial rank lower bound for these matrices, or a polynomial-size sum/max of them, on every complete binary certificate. It does not refute a rank assertion restricted to hypothetical small bounded-depth binary certificates, or to the matched simulation image: the witness is not proved to belong to those classes. Such a restricted assertion would need its own use of the small binary formula, rather than a structural statement about every certificate. Rank over \(\mathbb F_2\) and \(\mathbb F_4\) is the same for the resulting binary matrices, whereas computing their coefficient tensor over those fields need not have the same constant-depth cost. The binary coordinate-depth obstruction remains in force. Affine substitutions followed by homogeneous extraction are not covered by the positionwise linear-map assertion.

Positive flow over an extension

Proposition. Fix \(\Delta\). Let a finite simple graph of maximum degree \(\Delta\), with \(n\ge1\) vertices and \(m\) edges, carry one Boolean orientation bit per edge and the canonical clauses demanding strictly positive signed integer net flow at every vertex. Over some \(K=\mathbb F_{2^a}\) with \(|K|=O(m+1)\), there is an original-input noncommutative IPS formula of size \(O_\Delta((n+m+1)^4)\) and constant unbounded-fan-in depth, including all Boolean and commutator generators. There is also a binary-coefficient certificate polynomial with a constant-depth \(K\)-formula of size \(O_\Delta((n+m+1)^4\log(m+2))\). This does not give a constant-depth formula using binary constants.

Proof. An isolated vertex supplies an empty clause, whose polynomial generator is one; handle it directly. Otherwise \(m\ge1\). Introduce a central Laurent parameter \(z\), not a Boolean or proof variable. For an edge bit \(x_e\), let \[ u_e(z)=z(1-x_e)+z^{-1}x_e,\qquad v_e(z)=z^{-1}(1-x_e)+zx_e. \] Use opposite choices at its endpoints. Let \(T_v(z)\) be the ordered product of its incident factors. Expanding in ordered Boolean point indicators gives the literal free-algebra identity \[ T_v(z)=\sum_{b\in\{0,1\}^{E(v)}}z^{\mathrm{net}_v(b)}\delta_b(X). \] Keep only positive net flows to define \(G_v(z)\). Then \(T_v-G_v\) is exactly a scalar Laurent combination of the vertex's original forbidden-assignment clause polynomials. All exponents of \(G_v\) are strictly positive, and each local expression has constant size depending on \(\Delta\).

Multiply the \(T_v\)'s in vertex order. Adjacent swaps sort the resulting edge factors into \(\prod_e u_ev_e\); as before, their differences have explicit variable-commutator certificates. The local formal identity \[ u_ev_e-1=(z-z^{-1})^2(x_e^2-x_e) \] holds in characteristic two. Telescoping therefore expresses \(\prod_vT_v-1\) using Boolean and commutator generators. Also telescope \[ \prod_vT_v-\prod_vG_v =\sum_v\left(\prod_{w<v}T_w\right)(T_v-G_v)\left(\prod_{w>v}G_w\right). \] Subtract these identities. Replacing each generator occurrence by its placeholder gives \(E(z;X,Y)\), zero at \(Y=0\), with \[ E(z;X,F)=1-\prod_vG_v(z). \] Every scalar Laurent exponent appearing in \(E\) lies in \([-2m,2m]\): each edge factor contributes at most one in absolute value, and the local Boolean or commutator correction uses the two replaced factors' reserve. The product \(\prod_vG_v\) has only strictly positive exponents, so the constant Laurent coefficient of the displayed right side is one.

Choose \(K\) with \(|K|>4m+1\), and \(4m+1\) distinct nonzero elements \(\alpha_t\). Inverting the Vandermonde matrix for \(z^{2m}E(z)\), of degree at most \(4m\), gives constants \(\lambda_t\in K\) extracting its middle coefficient. Consequently \(D=\sum_t\lambda_t E(\alpha_t)\) is a certificate. Each specialized expression has \(O_\Delta(s^2)\) terms and total size \(O_\Delta(s^3)\), where \(s=n+m+1\). Suffix products of \(G_v\)'s are kept factored; they are not expanded into all global assignments. Each \(G_v\) is a constant-size sum of products of affine forms. The extra polynomial number of interpolation copies and outer sum give size \(O_\Delta(s^4)\) and constant depth. Take the least power of two exceeding \(4m+1\), so \(a=O(\log(m+2))\).

Finally choose \(\beta\in K\) with field trace one and put \(C=\sum_{j=0}^{a-1}\sigma^j(\beta D)\), again with coefficientwise Frobenius fixing all letters. Such \(\beta\) exists: the trace polynomial \(t+t^2+\cdots+t^{2^{a-1}}\) is nonzero of degree less than \(|K|\), and its values lie in \(\mathbb F_2\), so some value is one. The sum is fixed by \(\sigma\) and has binary coefficients. Its specializations are zero and \(\operatorname{Tr}(\beta)=1\). At most \(a\) copies of a constant-depth \(K\)-formula give the stated cost. No conversion of those gates or constants into a binary formula is performed. \(\square\)

Nonvacuity. Positive integer net flow at every vertex is inconsistent on every nonempty finite graph because the net flows sum to zero. Graphs of bounded positive degree give unbounded instances with polynomial canonical CNF length. Degenerate cases. Isolated vertices give empty clauses with polynomial generator one. Replacing positivity by a condition allowing zero defeats the constant-coefficient step: a zero exponent can survive. Distinct nonzero interpolation points and the Laurent shift are essential; the two binary points do not supply this extraction.

Route consequence

Architecture test. The complete-certificate rank premise fails on charged Tseitin by an actual binary witness, not by changing a chosen coefficient with a syzygy. Boolean/Jacobi syzygies and axiom-cut normalization remain legal controls; adding them cannot turn a false universal statement into a true one. The source's homogeneous lower bounds cannot simply be applied to arbitrary certificate formulas, and their large-rank target condition is also absent on the explicit witness.

Structural reason. Fixed vertex characters gave the Tseitin construction. Positive flow lacks that property, but polynomially bounded Laurent exponents let interpolation replace it: local positivity removes the constant coefficient, while global edge cancellation restores one. Thus changing to this inequality family does not by itself avoid constant-depth extension-field certificates. The old flow degree ceiling was read in full; it concerns PC degree and does not supply or contradict this size/depth statement. The new flow certificate is not asserted to have all word flattenings small: its local factors are higher-degree sums, unlike the homogeneous linear factors in the Tseitin witness.
