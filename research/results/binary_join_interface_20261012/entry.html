Weight compatibility factorization

Lemma. Work over \(\mathbb F_2\). Let \(J_D\) be a degree-\(D\) Boolean pseudoideal, let \(\deg w\le b\), and let \(U\subseteq\mathbb F_2[x]_{\le K}\) be a linear candidate space. For each state \(\alpha\), let \(\mathcal E_\alpha\) be a finite list of actual old error polynomials of degrees at most \(e\), with \(b+K+e\le D\). Set \[ L_0=\{f\in U:wf\in J_D\},\quad V=U/L_0,\quad H=\dim V, \quad K_\alpha=\{[f]\in V:wfE\in J_D\ \ (E\in\mathcal E_\alpha)\}. \] For two state families with a common fixed compatible boundary, suppose their combined required errors are the union of their lists. Existence of one common surviving weighted annihilator is exactly \(K_\alpha\cap K_\beta\ne0\). Its binary indicator matrix \(M\) factors as \[ M=C_{\rm left}C_{\rm right}^{\mathsf T},\qquad C_{\alpha,v}=\mathbf1_{v\in K_\alpha}\quad(0\ne v\in V), \qquad \operatorname{rank}M\le2^H-1. \] Equivalently \(K_\alpha^\perp+K_\beta^\perp\ne V^*\), the nonspanning version of the recorded binary join predicate.

Proof. If \(wf\in J_D\), bounded absorption gives \(wfE\in J_D\) at the displayed reserve. Thus each error map is well-defined on \(V\), and its kernel is the stated subspace. A nonzero class in both kernels is exactly a multiplier whose weighted class survives and annihilates both lists. If their intersection has dimension \(t\), the displayed matrix product counts its \(2^t-1\) nonzero vectors modulo two. This is one exactly when \(t>0\). The annihilator identity follows from finite-dimensional linear duality. The field being binary is used in the parity count. All lifted multipliers retain degree at most \(K\); no new polynomial variables or unbudgeted multiplication were introduced. Computing the kernels or their incidence rows is not asserted cheap. \(\square\)

Degenerate cases. At \(H=0\), there are no nonzero columns and compatibility is always false. Empty error lists have kernel \(V\). States that disagree on shared coefficient choices, or whose gluing creates additional cross errors, do not satisfy the fixed-boundary/union hypothesis. They need those constraints before applying this matrix. Nonvacuity. Every fixed collection of complete old-only block interpretations with shared lower maps already fixed supplies such lists in its paid window. The following construction supplies explicit families.

Point-state compatibility rank

Proposition. Let a proper binary degree-\(D\) Boolean pseudoideal \(J_D\) on variables \(x\) contain an old system \(\Gamma\) of degree at most \(u\ge2\). Let \[ k\ge1,\quad K\ge k,\quad h\ge k,\quad B\ge\max(u,2h^2+3h),\quad K+B\le D. \] Adjoin \(k\) independent old Boolean variables \(y\). There is a proper extended pseudoideal, and genuine complete accuracy-\(h\) affine-bottom blocks shared by two independent accuracy-\(h\) parents, with \(m=2^k\) point-indicator inputs per parent, as follows. Each parent has legal constant-coefficient states indexed by all \(S\subseteq\Omega=\mathbb F_2^k\). A degree-at-most-\(K\) weight outside the extended pseudoideal kills all mapped source axioms for a pair \((S,T)\) if and only if \(S\cap T\ne\varnothing\). The full binary compatibility matrix has rank \(2^m-1\).

Consequently any exact bilinear factorization needs at least \(2^m-1\) intermediate scalar coordinates. Any exact representation by a nonspanning join predicate on a binary space of dimension \(q\) requires \(q\ge m\). The extra source description has size \(O(h^2 k m^2)\) as written products and sums, even without sharing all repeated products. These are bounds for preserving all state-pair answers. They are not a lower bound on finding one successful pair: the all-points state on both sides succeeds with weight one.

Extend the paid quotient. For polynomials of total degree at most \(D\), define \[ \widetilde J_D=\{f(x,y):f(x,a)\in J_D\text{ for every }a\in\Omega\}. \] This is a linear space containing the old axioms and all old and new Boolean equations. It is proper because one fails its condition. Multiplication by an old \(x_i\) uses the old closure: if \(\deg f\le D-1\), each \(f(x,a)\) has degree at most \(D-1\). Multiplication by \(y_j\) multiplies the evaluated polynomial by the scalar \(a_j\), so also preserves membership. These facts give every allowed bounded product by iteration. This is a proper Boolean pseudoideal, not the ideal of solutions of \(\Gamma\). In particular \(\Gamma\) may be inconsistent. This reuses the independent-coordinate extension principle in the earlier relative-code control.

Legal source and states. Write \[ \delta_a(y)=\prod_{j=1}^k(1+y_j+a_j)\qquad(a\in\Omega). \] For each \(a\), use a genuine affine-bottom block on the input tuple \((y_j+a_j)_{j=1}^k\). Assign its first \(k\) rows to the respective individual inputs and its other rows zero. Its product is literally \(\delta_a\). Share these fixed bottom blocks between the two parents. Each parent reads their genuine products, not fresh independent feature variables.

In parent state \(S\), choose coefficient one on inputs indexed by \(S\) in the first row, zero on the other inputs, and zero in every other row. Its mapped product is \[ P_S=1+\sum_{a\in S}\delta_a. \] Every coefficient field equation maps to zero. At a Boolean \(y=c\), \(\delta_a(c)=\mathbf1_{a=c}\), so the parent companion \(\delta_a P_S\) evaluates to one precisely when \(c=a\notin S\), and otherwise zero. The two states agree on their entire shared bottom boundary. Their combined companions are exactly the union of their parent lists, with the already satisfied bottom/old/field obligations.

Exact surviving-weight predicate. Let \(\deg f\le K\). The definition of \(\widetilde J_D\) shows that all weighted parent companions for \(S,T\) belong to it precisely when \[ f(x,a)\in J_D\qquad(a\notin S\cap T). \] If the intersection is empty, this condition says \(f\in\widetilde J_D\), so no weight survives. If \(a\in S\cap T\), the weight \(f=\delta_a\) has degree \(k\le K\), survives since \(f(x,a)=1\notin J_D\), and meets every companion condition. Old weighted generators also lie in \(\widetilde J_D\): their degrees fit \(K+u\le D\) and old bounded absorption applies after evaluating \(y\). Weighted bottom companions evaluate to zero for every \(y\); their written mapped degree is at most \(K+k+1\). Parent companion degree is at most \(K+2k\). These bounds fit \(K+B\le D\); all fields are already zero. Thus the predicate concerns the complete source, not a subset of its axioms.

Original cost. Before substitution a bottom product has degree at most \(2h\). A parent row has degree at most \(2h+1\), so its companion ceiling is \(h(2h+1)+2h=2h^2+3h\). Fresh fields have degree two. Hence \(B\) covers the original source degrees, independently of cancellation after the constant maps. Their replay uses maximum substitution degree one, including unchanged old variables, and retains \(K+B\le D\). Writing every parent companion separately costs at most \(O(h^2 k m^2)\): there are \(m\) companions, each with \(h\) sums of \(m\) bottom products of written size \(O(hk)\); the bottom companions and field equations fit this bound as well. Sharing can reduce this size but is unnecessary here.

Rank calculation. Index columns by nonempty subsets \(U\subseteq\Omega\), and put \(Z_{S,U}=\mathbf1_{U\subseteq S}\). Then \[ (ZZ^{\mathsf T})_{S,T}=2^{|S\cap T|}-1\pmod2 =\mathbf1_{S\cap T\ne\varnothing}. \] Restricting \(Z\) to its nonempty rows gives the subset-inclusion matrix, triangular with ones on the diagonal when subsets are ordered by size. Thus \(Z\) has full column rank \(2^m-1\). Its transpose is surjective, so the product has that rank. An arbitrary factorization needs at least the matrix rank in its intermediate dimension. The nonspanning join factorization in a binary \(q\)-space has rank at most \(2^q-1\), by the preceding lemma or the same nonzero-annihilator count. Comparison forces \(q\ge m\). \(\square\)

Easy-choice control. For \(S=T=\Omega\), the literal identity \(\sum_a\delta_a=\prod_j((1+y_j)+y_j)=1\) gives \(P_S=P_T=0\). Weight one and the displayed constant maps therefore work. The large all-pairs matrix does not imply that every map is expensive, that the source is hard to remove, or that a small subset of states cannot preserve existence of one solution.

Nonvacuity. Boolean-only \(J_D\) already supplies every \(k\) at arbitrarily large \(D\). The construction also retains any supplied inconsistent old system's proper window. For example the recorded charged theorem, whose full charge-extension proof was reread, supplies bounded-degree nonzero-charge expander systems with a proper binary window linear in their number of vertices. For \(k=\lceil\log_2 n\rceil\), take \(h=K=k\) and \(B=\max(u,2k^2+3k)\). Then \(K+B=O(\log^2 n)\) fits that window eventually, \(m\) is between \(n\) and \(2n\), and the extra source description is polynomial in \(n\), whereas its compatibility rank is \(2^{\Theta(n)}\). Independent old variables were added; this is not an assertion about states occurring in every proof of the original unchanged family.

Degenerate cases. Empty states have no successful partner; singleton intersections have the explicit point weight. The all-points state has the easy control just given. A nonproper old space would invalidate survival and is excluded. The source construction requires \(k\ge1\) and \(h\ge k\) for its literal bottoms. It uses genuine products and arbitrary constant first-row choices; no fixed assumption about optimal states is imposed.
