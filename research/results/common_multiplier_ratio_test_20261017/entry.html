Setting

As in the native restriction: on a reduced board \(B\) with \(p\) pigeons and \(n\) holes, \(A_k=V_k(B)/W_k(B)\) modulo the closure (\(W_k\) the span of the \(x_sQ_i'\) with \(|s|+1\le k\)) and \(A_k=V_k(B)\) modulo the domain, and the native count of the unit error at \((K,L)\) is the least number of functionals \(\lambda_j\) on \(A_{K+L}\) with no nonzero \(\bar u\in A_K\) such that \(\lambda_j(\bar uv)=0\) for all \(v\in A_L\) and all \(j\). By lem:native-restriction (b) and (c) it is the native count of a variable error on the board with \(p+1\) pigeons and \(n+1\) holes when \(1+K+L\le D\le\lceil(n+1)/2\rceil\). A partial matching is a point of \(\{0,1\}^E\), \(E\) the edges; evaluation at it is a ring homomorphism on polynomials and vanishes on the non-matching monomials, and at a total matching (each pigeon in its own hole) it also vanishes on every \(Q_i'\).

Single evaluation functional

Statement. Lemma (single evaluation functional). Over every field, in either model, let \(L\ge K\ge0\) and let \(X\) be a set of partial matchings at which every element of the model's \(W_{K+L}\) vanishes (every set of partial matchings modulo the domain), such that evaluation at \(X\) is injective on \(A_K\). Then one functional detects: for \(S\subseteq X\) with evaluation \(A_K\to F^S\) bijective, \(\lambda=\sum_{\sigma\in S}\mathrm{ev}_\sigma\) leaves no nonzero \(\bar u\in A_K\) with \(\lambda(\bar uv)=0\) for all \(v\in A_L\). Instances: (i) modulo the domain, \(X\) the partial matchings of size at most \(K\), on every board; (ii) modulo the closure, \(X\) the total matchings, on every board where evaluation there is injective on \(A_K\), which holds at \(K=2\) on the reduced boards with three and four pigeons and eight holes over \(\mathbb F_2\) (computed). So a variable error on the functional pigeonhole system with \(1+K+L\le D\le\lceil N/2\rceil\) and \(L\ge K\) has native count \(1\) modulo the domain, and modulo the closure in case (ii).

Proof. Every element of \(W_{K+L}\) vanishes at \(X\), so \(\lambda\) is a functional on \(A_{K+L}\), and since evaluation is multiplicative, \(\lambda(\bar uv)=\sum_{\sigma\in S}u(\sigma)v(\sigma)\). With \(M\) the square, invertible matrix of the evaluation \(A_K\to F^S\), for \(\bar u,\bar v\in A_K\), the second lifted to \(V_K\subseteq V_L\), \(\lambda(\bar u\bar v)=(M\bar u)^\top(M\bar v)\), and \(M^\top M\) is invertible over every field; so for \(\bar u\ne0\) some \(v\in V_K\) has \(\lambda(\bar uv)\ne0\). An \(S\) exists since evaluation at \(X\) is injective on the finite-dimensional \(A_K\). In (i) the evaluation of the basis \(x_t\), \(|t|\le K\), at the matchings of size at most \(K\) is unitriangular for inclusion, so injective, and modulo the domain there is no \(W\). In (ii) each \(Q_i'\) is \(0\) at a total matching, so \(W_{K+L}\) vanishes there; the injectivity at the two boards, over \(\mathbb F_2\), is total_eval.json's rank, \(145=\dim A_2\) and \(275=\dim A_2\). The last sentence is lem:native-restriction (b) and (c). \(\square\)

Hypotheses, each with its step.

  • \(L\ge K\): \(V_K\subseteq V_L\) supplies the \(v\) the proof uses; for \(L<K\) on a board with matchings of size \(L+1\), \(\dim V_K>\dim V_L\) modulo the domain, the ratio exceeds \(1\) and one functional cannot detect.
  • The point set: (i) needs nothing; (ii) needs total matchings, so at most as many pigeons as holes on the reduced board, and the injectivity, computed at two boards. With more pigeons than holes there is no total matching, every partial matching leaves some \(Q_i'\) equal to \(1\), and the lemma gives nothing modulo the closure.
  • The field: no step uses it. The reduction sentence needs lem:native-restriction's \(D\le\lceil N/2\rceil\).

Degenerate cases. \(K=0\): \(\lambda=\mathrm{ev}_\varnothing\) in (i), count \(1\). A board without pigeons: \(V_k\) the constants, count \(1\). \(L<K\): excluded. A board with \(1\in W_{K+L}\) (two pigeons, one hole, \(K+L\ge2\), as the reviewer found): no point of \(X\) exists and \(A_{K+L}=0\); for \(K\le1\), where \(A_K\) is one-dimensional, no functional detects modulo the closure, and for \(K\ge2\), \(A_K=0\) and the count is \(0\); the lemma's hypothesis fails there, and lem:native-restriction's \(D\le\lceil N/2\rceil\) excludes it.

Nonvacuity. (i) The reduced board with three pigeons and eight holes at \(K=L=2\), the board with four pigeons and nine holes at \(D=5\): \(\lambda\) is the sum of the \(1+24+3\cdot56=193\) evaluations at matchings of size at most \(2\), \(\dim V_2=193\). (ii) The same board modulo the closure: \(\dim A_2=145\) and the evaluation of \(V_2\) at its \(336\) total matchings has rank \(145\) (total_eval.json), so \(145\) of them give the functional. Plainest bound. lem:native-restriction (d) gives \(1\le m_{\min}\le193\) and \(1\le m_{\min}\le145\) there, and, as the plainest uniform bound over the family of boards and slacks \(L\ge K\), the same bracket \(1\le m_{\min}\le\dim A_K\) on each member; the lemma decides both.

Native ratio

Statement. Conjecture (native ratio). Over every field, on every reduced board and at every \(K,L\ge0\) with \(1+K+L\le\lceil(n+1)/2\rceil\), the native count of the unit error equals \(\lceil\dim A_K/\dim A_L\rceil\) in both models; so a variable error's native count on the functional pigeonhole system with \(1+K+L\le D\le\lceil N/2\rceil\) is that ratio on its reduced board.

Statement. Finite check (native ratio counts). Over \(\mathbb F_2\), on the reduced boards with eight holes, random functionals attain the ratio in both models: at \(K=3\), \(L=1\) with three pigeons, \(22\) functionals modulo the domain (\(\dim V_3/\dim V_1=529/25\)) and \(16\) modulo the closure (\(336/22\)), on all twenty seeds; with four pigeons, \(52\) (\(1713/33\)) on nineteen seeds of twenty, one needing \(53\), and \(36\) (\(1039/29\)) on all twenty; at \(K=L=2\), where the ratio is \(1\), one functional detects in both models with three pigeons (\(\dim A_2=193\) and \(145\)), on \(15\) and \(2\) of \(200\) seeds, and with four pigeons (\(369\) and \(275\)) on \(7\) and \(1\) of \(20\); and evaluation at the total matchings embeds \(A_2\) modulo the closure on both boards (ranks \(145\) and \(275\)). By lem:native-restriction these are the counts of a variable error on the boards with four and five pigeons and nine holes, at \(D=5\).

Evidence for the conjecture. Every board and model computed attains the ratio: \((2,1)\) on six- and seven-hole boards (check:reduced-native-counts), \((3,1)\) and \((2,2)\) on eight-hole boards (check:native-ratio-counts), \((1,1)\) on the reduced board with five pigeons and four holes (check:one-native-functional), all over \(\mathbb F_2\); and lem:single-evaluation-functional proves the domain's case \(L\ge K\) and the closure's at \(K=L=2\) on the two satisfiable boards over \(\mathbb F_2\).
