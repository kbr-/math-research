Closure degree locality

Lemma (closure degree locality). On the functional pigeonhole system with \(m\) pigeons and \(N\) holes, over any field, let \(C_d\) be the degree-\(d\) closure of all axioms. If \(d\le D\le\lceil N/2\rceil\), then \(C_D\cap V_d=C_d\). In particular, for \(E\) of degree at most \(e\) and \(K+e\le d\), \(\{u\in V_K:uE\in C_D\}=\{u\in V_K:uE\in C_d\}\).

Proof. By Proposition 2.8 and Theorem 2.9 at degrees \(d\) and \(D\) (Impagliazzo, Pudlák and Sgall, as read in the functional-transfer entry), \(C_d\) is \(H_d\) plus the lift of \(K_d=\operatorname{span}(B_d\setminus T_d)\), and \(C_D\) likewise, \(H_d\) being the span of the non-matching monomials of degree at most \(d\). Reduction modulo \(H\) is the projection onto matching monomials, so a polynomial \(f\) of degree at most \(d\) has its non-matching part in \(H_d\) and its matching part in \(\operatorname{span}(T_d)\). The functional-transfer entry's Setting shows that \(B_d=\{x_a\in B_D:|a|\le d\}\) and that an element of \(\operatorname{span}(T_d)\) has the same expansion in \(B_d\) and in \(B_D\), so \(K_D\cap\operatorname{span}(T_d)=K_d\). Hence \(f\in C_D\) exactly when its matching part lies in \(K_d\), that is, when \(f\in C_d\). The kernel statement is the case \(f=uE\), of degree at most \(K+e\le d\). \(\square\)

Against the record. The functional-transfer entry's Setting proves the case \(d=K\) (“\(C\cap V_K\) is \(H\cap V_K\) plus the lift of \(K_K\)”) by the same argument. The lemma is that degree fact at a general \(d\), registered as refining it.

Hypotheses, each with its step.

  • \(d\le D\le\lceil N/2\rceil\): Proposition 2.8 and Theorem 2.9 at degrees \(d\) and \(D\), for the bases and the closures' descriptions.
  • For the kernel form, \(\deg(uE)\le d\), implied by \(K+e\le d\).
  • The number of pigeons and the field: any, as in the cited basis.

Degenerate cases. \(d=D\): trivial. \(E=0\): both kernels are \(V_K\). \(d=0\): \(C_0=0\), no axiom having degree \(0\), so the lemma says \(C_D\cap V_0=0\), that is, \(C_D\) is proper.

Nonvacuity. Six pigeons, five holes, \(D=3=\lceil5/2\rceil\), \(d=2\), \(K=1\), \(e=1\): \(1+1\le2\le3\). The dense errors below are kernels at \(D=3\) that, by the lemma, are those of the degree-\(2\) closure. Plainest bound. Without the lemma, \(C_d\subseteq C_D\cap V_d\) gives only one inclusion.

Baseline. Without an idea: \(C_d\subseteq C_D\cap V_d\). The lemma adds equality below half the holes, the recorded degree fact at a general degree.
