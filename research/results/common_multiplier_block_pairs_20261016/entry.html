General statement. conj:monomial-kernel-transfer extends conj:functional-kernel-transfer from variable errors to monomial errors. It covers the functional pigeonhole system with \(n+1\) pigeons and \(n\) holes, at a proper degree \(D\), \(K\le D-1\), any set of monomial errors \(E\) with \(K+\deg E\le D\). It states that the common kernel in \(V_K\) modulo the closure \(C\) equals the kernel modulo the closure \(H\) of the hole and functionality axioms, plus \(C\cap V_K\). check:block-pair-kernels confirms it on six sets at \(K=1\), \(D=3\), with six pigeons and five holes.

Scope. The conjecture keeps the parent's \(m=n+1\), the only case with evidence (over \(\mathbb F_2\)).

  • It drops the condition that each monomial lie on distinct pigeons and holes. A monomial on one pigeon or one hole lies in \(H\subseteq C\), so \(uE\) lies in \(H\) for every \(u\in V_K\). Such an error constrains neither side, and the equality is unchanged. Dropping the condition makes the statement more general.
  • With \(\deg E=1\) it contains the parent.
  • The field enters only the computation; whether a proof needs \(\mathbb F_2\) is open.
