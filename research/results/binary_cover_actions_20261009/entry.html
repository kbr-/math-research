Allow hidden states, retain actual degrees

Fix a field, \(n\) variables, \(D\ge1\), and a finite supplied source \(\mathcal F\) whose generators under consideration have degrees at most \(D\). Write \(C_D\subseteq V_D\) for its syntactic degree-\(D\) PC consequence space, including all supplied domain equations. Fix a linear observation \(\Lambda:V_D\to W\) with \(\Lambda(1)\ne0\), and its recorded horizons \[ N_b=\{f\in V_b:\Lambda(fq)=0\text{ for all }q\in V_{D-b}\},\qquad S_b=V_b/N_b. \] Inclusion and variable multiplication define maps between consecutive \(S_b\)'s by the horizon lemma.

A source-compatible cover consists of finite-dimensional spaces \(E_0\hookrightarrow\cdots\hookrightarrow E_D\), surjections \(\pi_b:E_b\to S_b\) commuting with inclusions, a vector \(u\in E_0\) projecting to \([1]\), and variable maps \(X_{i,b}:E_b\to E_{b+1}\). The variable maps commute with inclusions and with each other wherever both steps fit through \(D\), and intertwine the corresponding maps on \(S\). Evaluate each polynomial of degree at most \(b\) on \(u\), padding to grade \(b\) by inclusions; commutation makes this linear evaluation \(\mathrm{ev}_b:V_b\to E_b\) well-defined. Require \(\mathrm{ev}_{\deg g}(g)=0\) for each supplied generator \(g\). In particular Booleanity is required on the cyclic states when it is in the source. No global degree-preserving endomorphism outside the paid window is demanded.

Lemma (source compatible cover). Such a cover exists if and only if \(\Lambda(C_D)=0\). When it exists, its smallest possible terminal dimension is \[ \min_I\dim(V_D/I), \tag{1} \] where \(I\) ranges over degree-\(D\) PC pseudoideals containing the supplied generators and contained in \(\ker\Lambda\). Every cover can be replaced by a cyclic one with no larger dimension in any grade. In particular \[ \sum_{b=0}^D\beta_b\ \le\ \min\dim E_D\ \le\ \dim(V_D/C_D), \tag{2} \] with \(\beta_b\) the births of the fixed horizon diagram as in the inclusion-cover theorem. The bounds assert neither a low-degree substitution nor a polynomial source-size bound.

Proof, from a cover. Intertwining and the marked vector give \(\pi_b\mathrm{ev}_b(f)=[f]\) for every \(f\in V_b\), by induction on monomials and linearity. Put \(I=\ker\mathrm{ev}_D\). Since inclusions of \(E\) are injective, for any \(f\in I\) of actual degree \(b<D\), its earlier evaluation \(\mathrm{ev}_b(f)\) is already zero. Consequently \(\mathrm{ev}_{b+1}(x_if)=X_{i,b}\mathrm{ev}_b(f)=0\). Hence \(I\) is closed under legal variable multiplication, including reuse after cancellation. The axiom requirement puts every supplied generator in \(I\), so \(C_D\subseteq I\). The terminal intertwining gives \(I\subseteq N_D=\ker\Lambda\). This proves necessity and properness.

Now restrict \(E_b\) to the image of \(\mathrm{ev}_b\). These images are nested, stable under the paid variable maps, and still project onto \(S_b\), because \(V_b\to S_b\) is onto. Thus this cyclic restriction is a cover with no larger dimensions. Its terminal dimension is \(\dim(V_D/I)\), giving one direction of (1).

Proof, from a pseudoideal. Conversely suppose \(I\) satisfies the displayed conditions. Define \(E_b=V_b/(I\cap V_b)\). Its inclusion maps are injective, and multiplication by variables descends and commutes in the paid window, using pseudoideal closure. If \(f\in I\cap V_b\) and \(\deg q\le D-b\), repeated legal multiplication gives \(fq\in I\); hence \(\Lambda(fq)=0\). Thus \(I\cap V_b\subseteq N_b\), and the quotient maps \(E_b\to S_b\) are well-defined surjections intertwining all required maps. The class of one gives \(u\), all source equations evaluate to zero, and the terminal dimension is \(\dim(V_D/I)\). If \(\Lambda(C_D)=0\), take \(I=C_D\), which is a pseudoideal by PC closure. This proves sufficiency, (1), and the upper bound in (2). The lower bound follows by forgetting the variable maps and applying the inclusion-cover theorem. \(\square\)
