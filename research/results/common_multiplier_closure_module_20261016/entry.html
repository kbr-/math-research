General statement. No claim applies: this entry builds a framework tool.

The build. tools/pc_closure.py computes the degree-\(D\) polynomial-calculus closure over \(\mathbb F_2\) in the multilinear ring, with Booleanity built in, by echelon elimination on the monomials of degree at most \(D\) ordered by degree descending, multiplying every echelon row whose leading monomial has degree below \(D\) by every variable until nothing new enters; stored rows stay reduced at every pivot, so remainders are canonical and linear. It answers membership and remainder queries and gives the refutation degree. Its tests, tools/tests/test_pc_closure.py, compare the refutation degree with tools/truncated_buchberger.py, an independent method, on the pigeonhole system with \(3\) pigeons and on six random width-two clause systems on \(4\) variables, asserting that both refuted (degree \(2\)) and unrefuted systems occur among them; check that remainders are canonical; and include a member arising only from cancelling top terms, the case the first truncated-quotient script missed and the one control that distinguishes that error.
