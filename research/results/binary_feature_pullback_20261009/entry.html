Keep the actual weighted kernel

Let \(J_D\subseteq\mathbb F_2[x_1,\ldots,x_n]_{\le D}\) be a proper degree-\(D\) pseudoideal containing old Booleanity. Let \(w\notin J_D\), \(\deg w\le b\), and choose any binary old polynomials \(z_1,\ldots,z_s\) of degree at most \(d\ge1\). Put \(v=n+s\ge1\), and define \[ \psi:\mathbb F_2[x,Z]\longrightarrow\mathbb F_2[x], \qquad \psi(x_i)=x_i,\quad\psi(Z_j)=z_j. \] There is no algebraic independence assumption. For \[ R=\left\lfloor\frac{D-b}{d}\right\rfloor\ge2, \qquad I_R=\{F\in\mathbb F_2[x,Z]_{\le R}:w\psi(F)\in J_D\}, \tag{1} \] the space \(I_R\) is a proper Boolean pseudoideal.

Verification of (1). Linearity is immediate, and \(1\notin I_R\) is exactly \(w\notin J_D\). If \(\deg F\le R-1\), multiplication of \(w\psi(F)\) by the image of any formal variable has ceiling at most \(b+dR\le D\). Hence \(I_R\) has the required bounded multiplication closure. Every formal Boolean equation pulls back to an old Boolean consequence through \(2d\); multiplication by \(w\) fits \(b+2d\le D\). Thus all Boolean equations belong to \(I_R\). The nontrivial defining relations of the features are included automatically wherever their formal degree fits. We have not replaced them by independent variables.

Affine removal, followed by row grouping

There are \(M\ge1\) finite affine tuples \(g_{a,i}(x,Z)\), intended as actual inputs \(\psi(g_{a,i})\) of complete binary blocks with original accuracy \(h\ge1\). Choose integers \(1\le K\le v\), \(t\ge1\) with \[ K+t+1\le R,\qquad \eta=\frac12\frac{N_v(K-1)}{N_v(K)},\qquad M(1-\eta)^t<1, \quad N_v(k)=\sum_{j\le k}\binom vj. \tag{2} \] Let \(q=\lceil t/h\rceil\) and \(T=\max(1,d(q-1))\).

Lemma (weighted feature removal). Under (1)–(2), there are old polynomial coefficient maps of degree at most \(T\) for the original \(h\) rows and a weight \(W=w\psi(F)\notin J_D\), \(\deg F\le K\), such that every actual weighted companion belongs to \(J_D\). The weight has degree at most \(b+dK\). Weighted coefficient-field equations also belong to \(J_D\) whenever \(b+dK+2T\le D\). Earlier weighted identities persist if their final multiplied degrees fit \(D\).

Proof of approximation in the pullback. Apply the weighted approximation proof with weight one and affine inputs in \(I_R\). Its applicability to Boolean pseudoideals is given by the conditional contraction and squarefree kernel count; here the multiplier domains are full degree spaces, not arbitrary conditioned subspaces. Explicitly, before row \(j\), the prefactor degree is at most \(j-1\), so the contraction reserve is \(K+j+1\le R\). Each error rank contracts by \(1-\eta\); initially it is at most the same quotient-domain dimension \(\dim(V_K/(I_R\cap V_K))>0\). Summing the final \(M\) ranks under (2) leaves a nonzero common kernel class. Hence there are binary scalar rows \(c_{a,u,i}\) and a multilinear \(F\) of degree at most \(K\) such that \[ F\notin I_R,\qquad Fg_{a,i}\prod_{u=1}^{t}(1-\ell_{a,u})\in I_R, \qquad \ell_{a,u}=\sum_i c_{a,u,i}g_{a,i}. \tag{3} \] The definition of \(I_R\) gives survival of \(W\) and weighted old membership for every image in (3).

Fit the original rows. Partition the \(t\) factors into at most \(h\) ordered groups, each of size at most \(q\). For one group the exact existing telescoping identity is \[ 1-\prod_{u\in G}(1-\ell_{a,u}) =\sum_{u\in G}\ell_{a,u}\prod_{\substack{v\in G\\vfactor packing with affine formal inputs and no retained core; no source accuracy or original degree is reset.

The coefficient images are binary polynomials, so their squared-minus-original equations have old Boolean certificates through \(2T\). Weighting adds at most \(b+dK\). Every earlier relation \(wE\in J_D\) can be multiplied by \(\psi(F)\) through its actual final ceiling. This proves the lemma. \(\square\)

Replay criterion. If the resulting single global source substitution has degree at most \(T\), every earlier weighted source image remains valid, the original source proof ceiling is \(B\ge2\), and \[ b+dK+TB\le D, \tag{4} \] then a degree-\(B\) source refutation contradicts \(W\notin J_D\). Multiply its substituted lines by \(W\). The new companions are in \(J_D\) by (3), fields by (4), and earlier generators by the stipulated final ceilings. PC multiplication fits \(b+dK+TB\), since the preceding original line has degree at most \(B-1\). In a multilevel application, \(T\) must also bound all earlier coefficient maps; it is not automatically only the current row-grouping degree. Ordinary NS refutations are covered by their same-degree PC simulation.

A complete two-level consequence

Theorem (bounded feature source exclusion). Let \(\Gamma_n\) be any binary Boolean old family in \(n\) variables with \[ 1\notin\mathcal C_D(\Gamma_n),\qquad D=\lfloor\gamma n\rfloor, \qquad 0<\gamma<1. \] Fix constants \(r_0\ge1\), \(A>0\), \(0<\beta<1\), \(0<\epsilon<\beta/2\), and \(0\le a<2+\epsilon-\beta\). Put \(h=\lfloor n^\epsilon\rfloor\), \(B=\lfloor n^\beta\rfloor\). Consider complete product-only two-level binary ENS sources with at most \(n^a\) bottom blocks, bottom inputs of old degree at most \(r_0\), and at most \(n^A\) parents. Every block has accuracy \(h\); each parent input is old affine plus a constant binary combination of genuine bottom products. There are no raw earlier coefficients outside those products, and shared bottoms are coherent. For sufficiently large \(n\), the full source has no degree-\(B\) PC or NS refutation.

Bottom stage. Work in \(J_D=\mathcal C_D(\Gamma_n)\). Choose \(K_0=\lfloor D/8\rfloor\). The fixed-degree weighted approximation rate for degree-\(r_0\) inputs is bounded below by a positive constant depending only on \(\gamma,r_0\), since \(K_0/n\) tends to a positive constant. Thus one may choose \(t_0=\lceil C\log(2n)\rceil=\Theta(\log n)\), with a sufficiently large fixed \(C\), for the bottom inventory; \(t_0\le h\) eventually. Select those binary constant rows and zero-pad, obtaining \(w\notin J_D\), \(\deg w=b\le K_0\), annihilating all bottom companions. The actual bottom values have degree at most \(d=\max(1,r_0t_0)=O(\log n)\). Empty bottom inventory may be omitted; it reduces to one affine level.

Parent stage. Name all \(s\le n^a\) actual bottom values and retain all \(n\) old variables in the pullback (1). Thus \(v=n+s\), not \(n\). We have \(R=\Theta(n/\log n)\). Set \(K=\lfloor R/8\rfloor\), eventually at least one and below \(v\). The elementary binomial estimate \[ N_v(K)\le(1+v/K)N_v(K-1) \] follows by bounding its newest summand \(\binom vK\le(v/K)\binom v{K-1}\). Hence \(\eta\ge K/(2(v+K))\ge K/(4v)\). Taking \[ t=\left\lceil \frac{4v}{K}\ln(2\max(1,M))\right\rceil \] gives the strict rank deficit in (2). For nonempty parent inventory, \[ t=O((1+n^{a-1})\log^2n). \tag{5} \] Because \(a<2+\epsilon-\beta<2\), this is \(o(R)\), so \(K+t+1\le R\) eventually. Apply weighted feature removal and group its rows into the original \(h\).

Full ledger. The new weight degree is at most \[ b+dK\le b+(D-b)/8\le15D/64. \] Also \(\lceil t/h\rceil-1\le t/h\), giving \[ T\le1+dt/h =1+O((n^{-\epsilon}+n^{a-1-\epsilon})\log^3n). \] Therefore \[ TB=O(n^\beta+ n^{\beta-\epsilon}\log^3n+ n^{a-1-\epsilon+\beta}\log^3n)=o(n), \] by the strict hypotheses. Condition (4) follows. Every old affine part is included in the formal tuples, so no feature-independence or old-part restriction has been imposed.

The bottom coefficient maps are constants and the parent maps have degree at most \(T\), so this is one global degree-\(T\) substitution. Previous bottom memberships can be multiplied by \(\psi(F)\): their actual degree is at most \(b+dK+d+r_0\), which fits \(D\). Old axioms and fields fit the same replay ceiling. Original bottom products have ceiling \(h(r_0+1)\); original parent input ceiling is \(\max(1,h(r_0+1))\), so parent companions have original degree \(O(h^2)\). Since \(2\epsilon<\beta\), these lie below \(B\) eventually. The replay criterion now excludes the full source refutation. This completes the proof. \(\square\)

For example \(\beta=1/3\), \(\epsilon<1/6\) permit any fixed internal exponent \(a<5/3+\epsilon\), with an arbitrary fixed polynomial parent exponent \(A\). This is not every polynomial internal inventory. The count \(n+s\) is exactly where that loss appears; naming dependent features is valid but not free.
