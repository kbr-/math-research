Complementary source merge

Lemma. Over \(\mathbb F_2\), let \(\Gamma(y)\) contain Booleanity for all retained variables, and let \(H(y),t_1(y),\ldots,t_m(y)\) be arbitrary polynomials. Two complete fresh accuracy-\(h\) blocks, \(h\ge2\), have inputs \((H,t)\), \((1+H,t)\), private arrays \(a,b\), and products \(P_+,P_-\). Additional axioms \(\mathcal A(y,a,b,z)\) are allowed. A constant/renaming substitution \(\Phi\), fixing \(y,z\), transfers every degree-\(B\) ordinary NS or PC consequence \(q\) to a degree-\(B\) consequence \(\Phi(q)\) of \(\Gamma\), one complete accuracy-\(h-1\) block on \(t\), and \(\Phi(\mathcal A)\). For empty or identically zero \(t\), omit the residual block. No survival or weight premise is required.

Construction. In row one of both blocks set the distinguished coefficient to one, all other coefficients to zero. In rows two through \(h\), set distinguished coefficients to zero and identify both residual arrays with one fresh array \(u\). Then \[\Phi(P_+)=(1+H)P_t,\qquad \Phi(P_-)=HP_t.\] Residual companions become \((1+H)t_iP_t\) or \(Ht_iP_t\); both distinguished companions become \(H(1+H)P_t\). Every source field becomes zero or a residual field.

Proof. When some \(t_i\ne0\), set \(d=\max_i\deg t_i\), \(e=\deg H\) for nonconstant \(H\) and zero otherwise, \(\delta=\max(d,e)\), \(v=(h-1)(d+1)\). Both source tuples have actual maximum degree \(\delta\), and original product degree \(h(\delta+1)\). A residual companion image has a certificate through \[v+\deg t_i+e\le h(\delta+1)+\deg t_i,\] within its actual source companion degree. For nonconstant \(H\), Boolean division certifies \(H^2+H\) through degree \(2e\): monomial reductions by \(y_j^2+y_j\) do not increase degree; the multilinear remainder vanishes at all Boolean points and is zero by successive interpolation. Its product with \(P_t\) costs \[v+2e\le h(\delta+1)+e,\] within each distinguished companion's original degree. Constant \(H\) gives zero; fields cost two. Zero residual companions impose nothing.

For empty/all-zero \(t\), take \(P_t=1\) and set unused coefficients to zero. The only possible nonzero companion image is \(H(1+H)\), certified through \(2e\) within its original cost. A wholly zero source tuple has only fields, mapped to constants. The substitution increases neither proof-line nor cofactor degree. Insert certificates only for active axioms at their original ceilings, then replay addition and multiplication by a variable or its constant/variable image, or each NS summand. The substituted additional axioms are target axioms with nonincreased degree. This proves the claim. \(\square\)

Readers. With old-affine \(L\) and constant \(\alpha,\beta\), \[\Phi(L+\alpha P_++\beta P_-)=L+[\alpha+(\alpha+\beta)H]P_t.\] Equal coefficients therefore preserve affine-in-whole-products grammar, inductively through later blocks whose coefficients remain fixed. Arbitrary later axioms still transfer. However, for \(H=x,t=(z),h=2\), \(P_t=1+uz\), the coefficient of \(u\) in \(xP_t\) is \(xz\), whereas in an old-affine polynomial plus a scalar multiple of \(P_t\) it is \(\lambda z\). This literal dictionary fails; further reader normalization is not ruled out.

Nonvacuity. Take \(\Gamma=\mathrm{BOOL}\), independent \(x,z\), \(H=x,t=(z),h=2\). Both tuples have proper input spans. Source companions have degree five, the residual companion degree three, and its distinguished-factor multiple and Boolean certificate fit degree four. The reader \(P_++P_-\) becomes \(P_t\). Unused bits give unbounded variable counts; this tests the template, not a hard family.

Degenerate cases. Constants, empty/all-zero residuals and inactive source axioms are covered above. Duplicates retain their arity/encoding costs. At \(h=1\) the same map transfers to \(\Gamma\) plus input axioms \(t_i\), because \(P_t=1\); it is not free elimination. Accuracy zero is excluded. Padding never resets original degrees.
