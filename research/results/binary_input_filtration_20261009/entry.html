Return rank and its degree charge

Work over \(\mathbb F_2\) in a Boolean degree-\(D\) pseudoideal quotient. Take tuple spaces \(X\subseteq Y\subseteq Q_D^m\) with representatives through degree \(e\), binary inputs \(g_i\) of degree at most \(\delta\), and their multiplication span \(\mathcal B=\operatorname{span}(1,g_i)\). Let \(h\ge1\), \(Z=\mathcal B^hX\), and let \(P=\prod_{j=1}^h(1-q_j)\), where every \(q_j\) is a binary linear combination of the inputs. Assume \(e+2h\delta\le D\). Spaces and products are in this single retained quotient; all earlier source/replay obligations are separate.

Proposition (input filtration return). The induced map \(\bar P:Y/X\longrightarrow Q_D^m/Z\), \([y]\mapsto[Py]\), is well-defined. With \[\eta=\dim\bigl((PY\cap Z)/PX\bigr),\] one has \[\operatorname{rank}(P|Y)=\operatorname{rank}(P|X)+\operatorname{rank}\bar P+\eta,\qquad 0\le\eta\le\dim Z-\dim X. \tag{1}\] The upper bound is sharp for actual inputs and the canonical minimum-growth subspace, with arbitrarily large dimensions, in the hard-quotient construction below.

Proof. Since \(PX\subseteq Z\), the quotient map is defined. Projection of \(PY\) modulo \(Z\) has kernel \(PY\cap Z\); rank-nullity gives the equality. The quotient defining \(\eta\) exists because \(PX\subseteq PY\cap Z\).

Every binary polynomial is Boolean modulo the old variable equations with its ordinary squared-degree certificate. In particular \(P^2-P\) is certified through \(2h\delta\). If \(v\in PY\cap Z\), choose \(y\in Y\) and a representative \(z\) of an element of \(Z\) with \(v=Py=z\) in the quotient. The relation \(z-Py\) has degree at most \(e+h\delta\), so multiplying by \(P\) is legal at \(e+2h\delta\). Booleanity then gives \(Pz=P^2y=Py=v\). Thus \(PY\cap Z\subseteq PZ\). The induced linear map \(Z/X\to PZ/PX\) is onto, whence \[\eta\le\dim(PZ/PX)\le\dim Z-\dim X.\] This proves (1) without assuming that \(Z\) is invariant under \(P\), or extending multiplication past the paid window. \(\square\)

The same construction makes the proposed graded input maps legal: \(g_i:\mathcal B^jY/\mathcal B^jX\to\mathcal B^{j+1}Y/\mathcal B^{j+1}X\), whenever those products fit. It does not identify their ranks with the ungraded rank. Summing graded ranks alone silently sets \(\eta=0\), which (1) does not allow. Using actual images \(PX\) as the codomain layer would make additivity tautological, but that layer depends on the chosen prefix and its unknown rank.

A canonical minimizer with a sharp return cost

Fix \(r\ge1\), and put \(v=\lceil\log_2(6r)\rceil\). Let a proper binary Boolean quotient contain an unsatisfiable family of width at most \(u\), with \(D\ge\max(u,4v)\). By the weighted cube at weight one, choose \(v\) old coordinates whose whole Boolean algebra injects linearly. Work entirely inside this algebra. Select \(6r\) distinct assignments on these coordinates. Split them into \(r\) groups, each with four core points \(a_1,a_2,a_3,a_4\) and two tail points \(b,c\). Denote their literal degree-\(v\) indicators by \(e_a\). Points not selected carry value zero in all spaces below.

For one group define \[X_j=\{(a_1,a_2,a_3,a_4,0,0):\textstyle\sum_i a_i=0\},\qquad Y_j=\{(a_1,a_2,a_3,a_4,t,t):t=\textstyle\sum_i a_i\}.\] Here vectors are coefficients in the independent point-indicator basis, not assignments satisfying the old unsatisfiable family. Put \(X=\bigoplus_jX_j\), \(Y=\bigoplus_jY_j\). Then \(\dim X=3r\), \(\dim Y=4r\). Let the input list be all \(6r\) selected indicators; its action \(\mathcal B\) includes every coordinate projector on the selected support. Consequently \[\mathcal BX=\mathbb F_2^{\,4r}\ \text{on the cores},\qquad \mathcal BY=\mathbb F_2^{\,6r}.\tag{2}\]

Canonical minimality. For every nonzero subspace \(L\subseteq Y_j\), \(\dim\mathcal BL\) is precisely the number of point coordinates occurring in its support. If no tail coordinate occurs, let \(k\le4\) be its core-support size. The even-sum relation gives \(\dim L\le k-1\), hence \(\dim\mathcal BL/\dim L\ge k/(k-1)\ge4/3\). Equality requires \(k=4\), \(\dim L=3\), and \(L=X_j\). If a tail occurs, both do, and \(k\ge1\); core projection is injective, so \(\dim L\le k\) and the ratio is at least \((k+2)/k\ge3/2>4/3\).

For any \(L\subseteq Y\), let \(L_j\) be its projections to the groups. Coordinate projectors give \(\dim\mathcal BL=\sum_j|\operatorname{supp}L_j|\), while \(\dim L\le\sum_j\dim L_j\). The preceding inequalities imply \(\dim\mathcal BL\ge(4/3)\dim L\). Equality forces every nonzero \(L_j=X_j\), and equality of dimensions forces \(L\) to be their direct sum. Thus \(X\) is the unique largest minimizer, of ratio \(4/3\), not an arbitrarily chosen favorable layer. The argument works also after scalar extension to \(\mathbb F_4\), agreeing with the recorded binary canonical-minimizer theorem.

One coherent legal row. Choose \(q=\sum_j(e_{b_j}+e_{c_j})\), and \(P=1-q\). Then \(P\) fixes every core and kills every tail. Set \(Z=\mathcal BX\). We have \[PX=X,\qquad PY=Z,\qquad \bar P=0,\qquad \eta=r.\tag{3}\] Indeed arbitrary core values occur in \(Y_j\); their required tail values disappear after multiplication. Thus the true residual rank is \(4r\), while the two graded ranks sum to only \(3r\). The omitted return rank is \(r=\dim Z-\dim X\), exactly the bound in (1). Input maps and this same legal row descend throughout the generated quotients. Their existence therefore does not justify dropping that cost.

Actual tuple and original source ledger. The multiplication-image embedding \(Tf=(e_af)_a\) is injective on all functions supported at the selected points and commutes with every input multiplier. It transfers (2)–(3), all growth ratios and the minimizer to actual tuple images. Every reduced coordinate has degree at most \(v\); the unreduced expression \(e_af\) has degree at most \(2v\). The safe phase reserve for one growth step is \(2v+2v=4v\le D\), and the shortening reserve with \(K=v,\delta=v,h=1\) is also \(4v\). The complete source has \(P_{\rm raw}=1-\sum_a r_ae_a\), original product degree \(v+1\), companion degree \(2v+1\), and coefficient fields of degree two. Constant row choices preserve these original charges; each specialized companion reduces using Boolean certificates through \(2v\). This is a literal complete ENS block, with an allowed degree-\(v\) multiplier subspace. No assertion is made that this subspace is forced by the actual compiler's earlier obligations.

There is also a perfect legal row: \(q_*=\sum_a e_a\). Orthogonality of the indicators gives \(e_a(1-q_*)=0\) for every input. The input span does not contain the global constant one, since \(6r<2^v\) (a positive multiple of six is never a power of two). Thus constant-span cleanup does not remove the comparison, but an optimized row still removes all errors. This remains a counterexample to a proposed bound for a fixed coherent row, not to existential source removal.

What survives the test

The construction answers the new question within a hard quotient and with a canonical minimizer of ratio below \(3/2\). The earlier returning-rank control concerned generic escape specialization and a stronger universal half-retention inequality; the uniform-profile control concerned averaging over rows and flags. Neither is being reinterpreted as the present filtered-rank identity. The common lesson is to retain the actual chosen map and its returning part.

The graded construction can be repaired by paying \(\eta\), bounded by the generated-layer surplus in (1). Here that entire surplus is necessary. It can also be repaired by choosing a prefix with a small actual return, as \(q_*\) demonstrates. Neither repair supplies a source-wide estimate for arbitrary shared nonlinear inputs. Demanding a globally invariant input module would be stronger than necessary and is not adopted. The exact branch extensions similarly give local projections but no common low-cost action on all these layers; their quantified source-composition gap remains relevant.
