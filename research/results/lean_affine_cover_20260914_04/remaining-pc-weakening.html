3. Semantic weakening costs at most \(4h\)

Suppose \(C\models D\). If \(Z(D)\ne\varnothing\), then \(Z(D)\subseteq Z(C)\), and every affine \(g_{C,i}\) vanishes on \(Z(D)\). Elementary affine linear algebra gives constants \(\lambda_{ij}\in\mathbb F_2\) with

\[ g_{C,i}=\sum_j\lambda_{ij}g_{D,j}. \]

Hence \(g_{C,i}P_D\) is a constant linear combination of the \(D\)-companions, through degree \(2h+1\). The identity

\[ P_D=P_CP_D+\sum_iU_{C,i}(g_{C,i}P_D) \tag{RPC-weakening} \]

derives \(P_D\) through \(\max(K,4h)\) from a completed degree-\(K\) proof of \(P_C\). If \(Z(D)=\varnothing\), then \(1\) is in the affine span of its input forms, so a constant combination of its companions derives \(P_D\) directly through \(2h+1\). This includes tautological clauses. Deriving an empty clause means that the proved value is already one.
