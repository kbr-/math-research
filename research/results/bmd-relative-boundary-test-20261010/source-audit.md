# Eisenbud-Harris source scope

David Eisenbud and Joe Harris:
- Limit linear series: Basic theory, Invent. Math. 85 (1986), 337-371. Author-hosted scan: https://eisenbud.github.io/papers/pdfs/1986-001.pdf
- Existence, decomposition, and limits of certain Weierstrass points, Invent. Math. 87 (1987), 495-515. Author-hosted scan: https://eisenbud.github.io/papers/pdfs/1987-002.pdf

The author's index supplied the exact PDF links. The web reader could not open them, but direct curl downloads succeeded. The scans have no mathematical text layer; pdftotext returned only their cover terms. No OCR dependency was installed. Relevant pages were inspected as page images with Poppler.

Reading coverage: 1986 paper pp. 337-339 and 345-348, including compact type, arbitrary possibly incomplete linear series, refined node compatibility, Proposition 1.1 and its full induction proof, the hypotheses of section 2 and Proposition 2.1's statement. The proof of Proposition 2.1 and the later smoothing theory were not imported. 1987 paper pp. 495-498: canonical ramification setup, dimensional properness, Theorems 1-3 and the quoted collision theorem. Their existence proofs and the complete paper were not replayed.

The source is used to identify hypotheses and scope. The notebook's graph and special low-degree extending-lattice proofs are independent calculations in the actual root family. Incompleteness alone is not an obstruction to the 1986 theory; compact type is its hypothesis. The canonical-moduli existence theorem does not give expected codimension inside this fixed cover family.

Unmodified scans and their included terms are preserved locally under ignored private/references. They are not redistributed or relicensed. Hashes are in source-provenance.json. Page images and scratch extractions are removed at checkpoint.
