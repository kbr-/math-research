A supported alternative is to seek a finite exception inventory through the established generic Jacobian certificate theorem. Empty exception sets are unnecessary.

The missing step is to obtain an explicit certificate and evaluate its cutoff D, proving original generic normality for every d>D. Exact tests through D then determine the complete set.

A decisive bounded test is a fixed depth/height certificate search for the smallest unresolved pair. A valid witness advances completeness; exhaustion rejects only that search budget. The supplied results give termination in principle, but no practical runtime bound or uniform grid coverage.
