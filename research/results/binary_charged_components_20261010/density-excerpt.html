3. Exact equations for PC-saturated coefficient components

Return to a retained binary system \(\Gamma_0\) with \(1\notin\mathcal C_B(\Gamma_0)\), and take \(B\ge2D\), \(D\ge2\). A fresh level has arbitrary retained input polynomials \(g_{a,i}\), actual degrees \(d_{a,i}\), maximum \(\delta_a\), accuracies \(h_a\), original companion costs \(e_{a,i}=d_{a,i}+h_a(\delta_a+1)\), and private coefficient variables. There are no dependencies within this fresh level. Ignore zero companions and keep original costs after any cancellation.

Let \(\mathcal R\) be the set of all fresh coefficient positions across all parents. For every subset \(\alpha\subseteq\mathcal R\), \(|\alpha|\le D\), put \(s_\alpha=D-|\alpha|\). Choose the one pairing from the lemma for every cutoff through \(D\), and introduce an independent density class \(\rho_\alpha\in Q_{s_\alpha}\otimes\mathbb K\). Define

\[ \Lambda(r^\alpha u)=\lambda(\rho_\alpha u),\qquad \deg u\le s_\alpha,\qquad \lambda(\rho_\varnothing)=1. \tag{PCM-components} \]

Fresh monomials are reduced by \(r^2=r\). In particular \(\rho_\varnothing\) is an unknown normalized component, not automatically the constant one: the old moments may be chosen after seeing the full source.

For block \(a\), let \(\mathcal T_a\) consist of the subsets of its coefficient positions choosing at most one position in each coefficient row. For \(\tau\in\mathcal T_a\), set \(g_{a,\tau}=\prod_{(v,j)\in\tau}g_{a,j}\). Its product expands as \(P_a=\sum_{\tau\in\mathcal T_a}r^\tau g_{a,\tau}\). The complete companion constraints are precisely

\[ \sum_{\tau\in\mathcal T_a} \lambda\!\left(\rho_{\alpha\cup\tau}\,u\,g_{a,i}g_{a,\tau}\right)=0 \quad\left( |\alpha|\le D-e_{a,i},\ \deg u\le D-e_{a,i}-|\alpha| \right). \tag{PCM-equations} \]

The cofactor support \(\alpha\) ranges over all fresh blocks, including other parents. These are coupled linear equations in the density coefficients, not separate per-parent choices. At accuracy one the sum is \(\lambda(u g_{a,i}\rho_\alpha)+\sum_j\lambda(u g_{a,i}g_{a,j}\rho_{\alpha\cup\{r_{a,j}\}})\). This is the moment-side counterpart of the existing fresh-coefficient identity.

Working coordinate criterion. Solutions of (PCM-components)–(PCM-equations) are exactly normalized degree-\(D\) source functionals whose retained component on \(r^\alpha\) annihilates \(\mathcal C_B(\Gamma_0)\cap\mathcal P_{\le s_\alpha}\). Such a solution suffices to exclude a degree-\(D\) NS source refutation. This saturation requirement is stronger than merely annihilating the original degree-\(D\) NS equations; existence is not asserted.

Proof and degree check. The argument of each density has degree at most

\[ \deg(u g_{a,i}g_{a,\tau}) \le D-|\alpha|-h_a(\delta_a+1)+|\tau|\delta_a \le D-|\alpha\cup\tau|=s_{\alpha\cup\tau}. \]

The second inequality uses \(|\tau|\le h_a\). Thus the pairing in every summand is legal through \(2s_{\alpha\cup\tau}\le2D\le B\), with the original companion cost unchanged. Expansion and fresh Boolean reduction give (PCM-equations). Fresh field multiples vanish by definition. Every legal retained axiom multiple belongs to the corresponding retained PC space, and PC reuse makes its product with a density a consequence through \(B\), so (PCM-components) annihilates it. This proves sufficiency and saturation. Conversely every saturated component is a linear functional on its \(Q_{s_\alpha}\), hence has the unique density from the lemma; expansion gives the displayed equations. \(\square\)
