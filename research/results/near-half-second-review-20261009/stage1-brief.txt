STAGE 1 BRIEF (goal and available results only; the route currently preferred is deliberately withheld)

GOAL. Superpolynomial proof-size lower bounds for every fixed depth of AC0[2]-Frege, through this chain.

(A) Source theorem (proved). Gamma: any set of polynomials over F_2 in v Boolean variables containing the Boolean
axioms; C_D(Gamma): its degree-D polynomial-calculus closure. If 1 is not in C_D for D = floor(v/2 - s),
s = o(v/(log v)^(l-2)), l >= 2 the fixed approximation depth, then a system augmented by polynomially many
approximation ("ENS") blocks in l levels has no PC refutation of degree v^(1/3).

(B) Its engine (imported, audited). Error ranks c_P(k) = rank of f in V_k -> ([P g_i f])_i mod C_D satisfy
c_{P'}(K) <= c_P(K) - (1/2) c_P(K-d) for a new approximation factor, and (A) needs
c_P(K-d)/c_P(K) >= 1/polylog for polylogarithmic d at K = D - v^(1/3).

(C) Missing. A polynomial-size unsatisfiable Boolean family meeting the hypothesis of (A) or of one of the
variants below, and the transfer of an actual Frege proof into the grammar of (A).

PROVED VARIANTS OF THE HYPOTHESIS (each sufficient in place of "D near v/2"):
V1. Free dimension: affine axioms over F_2 can be substituted away without changing refutation degree, so v may be
    replaced by n = v - rank(affine axioms), with v <= n^C. This is a strict weakening.
V2. Thin kernel: the standard monomials of C_D (degree-compatible order) number at most binom(X, j) in degrees
    K - Delta < j <= K, X about (2 + 1/Delta) K, Delta polylogarithmic.
V3. Extension counts: every standard monomial x_S with K - Delta <= |S| < K has at most (1 + 1/Delta)(|S| + 1)
    variables x with x x_S standard.
Necessary for V2 and V3: every set Q of variables all of whose monomials of degree <= K are standard ("shattered")
has |Q| <= about 2K; a proper closure of an unsatisfiable system always shatters D - 1 variables.
Not known: whether V2 or V3 can hold with a proper closure outside the cases where they reduce to near half of
the (free) variable count.

PROVED NEGATIVE OR LIMITING RESULTS (scope stated; none is a general theorem about all systems):
N1. Regular systems of even modular rows of width k (canonical clause encoding): two rows through a common
    unselected variable cannot both carry half their scope, giving degree <= v/2 - Omega(v/k). No general ceiling
    of about v/2 for all unsatisfiable Boolean systems is known to us, and no example exceeding v/2 + O(1) either.
N2. General resolution-type rule: if one row's trace drops on the fibre x = 1 and another's (disjoint) on x = 0,
    their product drops. For CNF with 1 not in C_D: some (D-1)-set contains the scope of no clause derivable by
    resolution in width D-1.
N3. Rows defined as bounded relations on dense random parities of n free variables (bounded-width chain
    encoding, free dimension n): every set of at most n/2 - O(sqrt(n g k log v)) free variables is unreduced on
    joins of g rows, for g up to almost linear. So all local obstructions vanish in this class; a heuristic
    dimension count suggests random instances are nevertheless refutable below n/3.
N4. Frames (satisfiable low-degree structures such as the cube, or the cube with a product variable at each node
    of a binary tree): any condition of type V2/V3 verified on the frame alone needs a cutoff at least half the
    frame's top standard degree tau, while every unsatisfiable extension is refuted by degree tau + w. The cube
    attains the ratio one half; the product tree gives 0.4 n against 0.5 n.
N5. Random k-CNF, first-moment calibrations only: scope-free ceiling 0.54 v at (k, density) = (3, 4.27); V2 holds
    for the input scopes from 0.34 v. Known expansion-type lower bounds are, by an unverified estimate, at most
    about 0.18 v there.
N6. Known lower-bound technology reaches at most about a quarter of the variables on the families examined; no
    method reaching a constant fraction near one of any ceiling is known to us.

REQUEST. Without knowing the route currently preferred, propose a materially different architecture for reaching
the goal, or give a reason why you find none. Consider in particular: whether a family is needed at all in the
form (C) (for instance whether the engine could be run against a different invariant than the rank functions
c_P, or with a rate assumption verified for the specific prefactors that occur rather than for all down-closed
families); whether (B)'s contraction step could be replaced; and what the cheapest decisive test of each
proposal is. Give at most three proposals, each with (i) the precise endpoint, (ii) why the chain would still go
through or what replaces it, (iii) the cheapest falsifier. Be concrete; under 500 words. Run no computations, read
no other files and edit nothing.
