- Giulio Caviglia, *A hyperplane restriction theorem and applications to reductions
  of ideals*, arXiv:2104.11836v1, https://arxiv.org/pdf/2104.11836 . Read the
  introductory Green theorem, Macaulay lowering convention, Definition 1.5,
  Proposition 1.8/Corollary 1.9 with their genericity proofs, and the nearby
  characteristic caveat. Green's general statement permits arbitrary
  characteristic but requires an infinite field and a general linear form.
  Later reduction-of-ideals applications and a binary-grid version are not imported.

- *Gotzmann's persistence theorem for smooth projective toric varieties*,
  arXiv:2405.02275, https://arxiv.org/pdf/2405.02275 . Read Section2.1,
  Lemma2.1, Definition2.2 and the classical statement Theorem2.3.
  For an ideal generated through d, equality H(d+1)=H(d)^{<d>} persists.
  No toric generalization or full proof certification is relied on.

  arXiv:1009.3764v2, https://arxiv.org/pdf/1009.3764 . Read introductory
  definitions/statements (1)–(4), subsequent theorem statements and examples.
  No improved divisibility theorem is applied to the source incidence system.

- Polly Y. Yu and Gheorghe Craciun, *Mathematical Analysis of Chemical Reaction
  Systems* (2018 author manuscript),
  https://pollyyu.web.illinois.edu/manuscripts/2018-Mathematical%20analysis%20of%20chemical%20reaction%20systems.pdf .
  Read mass-action, stoichiometry, reversibility and balance definitions,
  Theorem2.3, Definition2.7 and Theorem2.8. These concern positive real

  arXiv:1207.1925, https://arxiv.org/pdf/1207.1925 . Read the whole-ideal
