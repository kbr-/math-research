Setting

This is the functional pigeonhole system with \(m\ge2\) pigeons and \(N\) holes over a field \(k\): Booleanity, hole and functionality axioms, pigeon axioms \(Q_i=1-\sum_hx_{ih}\), in the multilinear ring. \(C\) is its degree-\(D\) closure and \(H\) that of the hole and functionality axioms, with \(1\le K<D\le\lceil N/2\rceil\), \(W=C\cap V_K\) and \(\rho=\max(2K-1,K+2)\). Two distinct pigeons are written \(1\) and \(2\). The partition errors are \(E_{A_i}=\sum_{j\in A_i}x_{1j}+\sum_{j\notin A_i}x_{2j}\) for sets of holes \(A_1,\dots,A_r\), \(r\ge1\), with \(U=\bigcup_iA_i\) and \(I=\bigcap_iA_i\). The common kernel is \(J=\bigcap_i\operatorname{Ann}_C(E_{A_i})\cap V_K\). A partial matching is a \(0/1\) point at which each pigeon is matched to at most one hole and each hole to at most one pigeon. For a set \(S\) of holes, “pigeon \(p\) is in \(S\)” means that \(p\) is matched to a hole of \(S\).

Partition common kernels

Statement. Proposition (partition common kernels).

  • (a) In characteristic other than \(2\), if \(N-|U|\ge\rho\), then \(J=\Phi+W\). Here \(\Phi\) is the space of \(u\in V_K\) vanishing at every partial matching outside \(Z=\{\text{pigeon }2\text{ in }I,\ \text{pigeon }1\text{ not in }U\}\) (pigeon \(1\) unmatched or matched to a hole of \(U^c\)). In particular, if \(I=\varnothing\), then \(J=W\), and no common surviving killer of degree at most \(K\) exists. More generally, for any choice \(\chi\) of representatives \(R_i=E_{A_i}+Q_2\) (edges in \(A_i\)) or \(E_{A_i}+Q_1\) (edges in \(A_i^c\)) whose edge sets \(L_i\) have \(N-|\bigcup L_i|\ge\rho\), \(J=\Phi_\chi+W\), with \(\Phi_\chi\) the \(u\in V_K\) vanishing off the intersection of the zero sets. Here \(E_A+Q_2\) vanishes at \(\{2\text{ in }A,\ 1\text{ not in }A\}\) and \(E_A+Q_1\) at \(\{1\text{ in }A^c,\ 2\text{ not in }A^c\}\). For two disjoint sets \(A\), \(B\) this gives \(J=W\) also when \(|A|\ge\rho\) or \(|B|\ge\rho\).
  • (b) In characteristic other than \(2\), if \(|I|\ge\rho\), the same holds with pigeons \(1\), \(2\) exchanged and each \(A_i\) replaced by its complement: \(J=\Phi'+W\), with \(Z'=\{\text{pigeon }1\text{ matched to a hole of }U^c,\ \text{pigeon }2\text{ unmatched or in }I\}\). In particular, if \(U\) is all the holes, then \(J=W\).
  • (c) In every characteristic, if \(I\ne\varnothing\), \(U\) is not all the holes and \(K\ge2\), then \(x_{1h}x_{2j}\) with \(j\in I\), \(h\notin U\) is a common surviving killer.
  • (d) In characteristic other than \(2\) and \(3\), for two disjoint sets \(A\), \(B\) with \(|A\cup B|\ge\rho\), \(J=W\). With (a), outside characteristics \(2\) and \(3\) a disjoint pair has \(J=W\) whenever \(\max(N-|A\cup B|,|A\cup B|)\ge\rho\), so at every \(N\ge2\rho-1\).

Proof. First, a polynomial \(p\) of degree at most \(D\) lies in \(H\) exactly when it vanishes at every partial matching. The axioms vanish at partial matchings, and so does every member of \(H\), which lies in their ideal. Conversely, modulo \(H\), \(p\) is a combination \(\sum_tc_tx_t\) of matching monomials, \(|t|\le D\). A monomial that contains two edges sharing a pigeon or a hole is a multiple, of degree at most \(D\), of a hole or functionality monomial, so it lies in \(H\). If \(p\) vanishes at every partial matching and some \(c_t\ne0\), take such a \(t\) minimal. At the point \(t\), \(x_s(t)=[s\subseteq t]\), so \(p(t)=c_t+\sum_{s\subsetneq t}c_s=c_t\ne0\), a contradiction. So \(p\in H\).

(a) Take the representatives \(R_i=E_{A_i}+Q_2=1+\sum_{j\in A_i}(x_{1j}-x_{2j})\), whose edges lie in \(A_i\). With \(N-|U|\ge\rho\), cor:partition-transfer-every-k states that “the common kernel modulo C is the intersection of the \(\operatorname{Ann}_H(R_i)\cap V_K\) plus \(C\cap V_K\)”. For \(u\in V_K\), \(uR_i\) has degree at most \(K+1\le D\), so by the first step \(u\in\operatorname{Ann}_H(R_i)\) exactly when \(u(M)R_i(M)=0\) at every partial matching \(M\). There \(R_i(M)=1+[1\text{ in }A_i]-[2\text{ in }A_i]\), which takes the values \(1,2,0,1\) when \(([1\text{ in }A_i],[2\text{ in }A_i])\) is \((0,0),(1,0),(0,1),(1,1)\). Since \(2\ne0\), \(R_i(M)=0\) exactly when pigeon \(2\) is in \(A_i\) and pigeon \(1\) is not. So \(u\) lies in every \(\operatorname{Ann}_H(R_i)\) exactly when it vanishes outside \(\bigcap_i\{2\text{ in }A_i,\ 1\text{ not in }A_i\}=Z\), that is, when \(u\in\Phi\). If \(I=\varnothing\), then \(Z\) is empty, since pigeon \(2\) sits at one hole. So \(\Phi=H\cap V_K\subseteq W\), and \(J=W\). The general choice \(\chi\) is the same step: the corollary's clause holds for any one representative per error with room, and the evaluation gives each representative's zero set, \(E_A+Q_1=1+[2\text{ in }A^c]-[1\text{ in }A^c]\) vanishing, outside characteristic \(2\), exactly when pigeon \(1\) is in \(A^c\) and pigeon \(2\) is not. For the disjoint pair with \(|B|\ge\rho\), take the mixed choice: \(R_A=E_A+Q_2\), with edges in \(A\), and \(R_B=E_B+Q_1=1+\sum_{j\notin B}(x_{2j}-x_{1j})\), with edges in \(B^c\). Their union is \(B^c\), since \(A\subseteq B^c\), so the room is \(|B|\). \(R_B(M)=1+[2\text{ in }B^c]-[1\text{ in }B^c]\) vanishes, outside characteristic \(2\), exactly when pigeon \(1\) is in \(B^c\) and pigeon \(2\) is not. \(R_A\) vanishes exactly when pigeon \(2\) is in \(A\subseteq B^c\). No matching does both, so the domain common kernel is \(H\cap V_K\), and \(J=W\). The case \(|A|\ge\rho\) is the same with \(A\) and \(B\) exchanged.

(b) Exchanging pigeons \(1\) and \(2\) is an automorphism of the system that fixes \(C\), \(H\) and \(W\) and sends \(E_A\) to \(E_{A^c}\). Apply (a) to the complements \(A_i^c\): their union is \(I^c\), so the room is \(N-|I^c|=|I|\), and their intersection is \(U^c\). Exchanging back gives \(Z'\). If \(U\) is all the holes, then \(U^c=\varnothing\), and \(J=W\).

(c) Take \(j\in A_i\) and \(h\notin A_i\) for every \(i\). Then \(x_{1h}x_{2j}E_{A_i}=\sum_{k\in A_i}x_{1h}x_{1k}x_{2j}+\sum_{k\notin A_i}x_{1h}x_{2j}x_{2k}\). Each term contains \(x_{1h}x_{1k}\) with \(h\ne k\) or \(x_{2j}x_{2k}\) with \(j\ne k\), so it lies in \(H\), at degree \(3\le D\), since \(K\ge2\) and \(K<D\). Survival is the argument of thm:partition-killer-degree (d). Rank the holes \(j\), \(h\) lowest. \(D\ge3\) and \(D\le\lceil N/2\rceil\) give \(N\ge5\), above the \(N\ge4\) that the dance of thm:partition-killer-degree (d) uses, so both pigeons dance to higher holes, and \(x_{1h}x_{2j}\in T_2\cap B_2\) is not in \(C\).

(d) This argument is the reviewer's, checked here. For any sets \(A\), \(B\), \(E_A+E_B=E_{A\cup B}+E_{A\cap B}\) as polynomials, since \([j\in A]+[j\in B]=[j\in A\cup B]+[j\in A\cap B]\) for the coefficient of \(x_{1j}\), and likewise for \(x_{2j}\) with the complements. For disjoint \(A\), \(B\), with \(U=A\cup B\), \(E_\varnothing=\sum_jx_{2j}=1-Q_2\). So \(u\in J\) gives \(u(E_U+1-Q_2)\in C\). Since \(uQ_1,uQ_2\in C\) for \(u\in V_K\), \(K\le D-1\), also \(uF\in C\) with \(F=E_U+1+Q_1=2+\sum_{j\notin U}(x_{2j}-x_{1j})\). \(F\) has degree \(1\), its edges lie in \(U^c\) and on pigeons \(1\), \(2\). lem:low-hole-linear-transfer, with room \(N-|U^c|=|U|\ge\max(2K-1,K+2)\), gives \(u\in\operatorname{Ann}_H(F)\cap V_K+W\). At a partial matching, \(F(M)=2+[2\text{ in }U^c]-[1\text{ in }U^c]\in\{1,2,3\}\), which is nonzero outside characteristics \(2\) and \(3\). So by the first step a \(u\in\operatorname{Ann}_H(F)\cap V_K\) vanishes at every partial matching, so it lies in \(H\cap V_K\subseteq W\). Hence \(J=W\). Finally, \(\max(N-|U|,|U|)\ge\lceil N/2\rceil\), so \(N\ge2\rho-1\) suffices. \(\square\)
