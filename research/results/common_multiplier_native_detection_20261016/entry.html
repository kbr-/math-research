Native detection count

Statement. Over a field \(F\), let \(V_k\) be the polynomials of degree at most \(k\) in \(n\) variables, \(J_D\subseteq V_D\) a degree-\(D\) pseudoideal (closed under multiplication by a variable within degree \(D\)), \(J_k=J_D\cap V_k\), \(W=V_D/J_D\), \(E_1,\ldots,E_{S'}\) errors of degree at most \(e\), \(U\subseteq V_K\), and \(L\ge0\) with \(e+K+L\le D\). Let \(T:U\to W^{S'}\), \(u\mapsto([E_iu])_i\), with kernel \(C\), image \(Y\) and rank \(r\). An old functional is a linear \(\mu:V_D\to F\) vanishing on \(J_D\); for old functionals \(\mu_1,\ldots,\mu_m\), the rectangle forms are the coordinates of \(\Phi_L(u)=\bigl(\mu_j(E_iuv)\bigr)_{i,j,v}\), \(v\) over a basis of \(V_L\). (a) \(C\subseteq\ker\Phi_L\), and \(\ker\Phi_L=C\) implies \(r\le m\dim Z_L\), \(Z_L\) the image of \(\operatorname{span}\{E_iv:i,\ v\in V_L\}\) in \(V_{e+L}/J_{e+L}\); in particular \(r\le m\,S'\dim V_L\), and \(r\le m\,S'\,N_n(L)\) when \(J_D\) contains the Boolean axioms (\(N_n(L)\) multilinear monomials of degree at most \(L\)). (b) Let \(F=\mathbb F_q\), and let \(c\) be the least dimension of \(\operatorname{span}\{y_1,\ldots,y_{S'}\}\subseteq W\) over nonzero \(y=(y_i)\in Y\) (\(c=\infty\) when \(r=0\)). If \(mc\ge r\), some old functionals \(\mu_1,\ldots,\mu_m\) give \(\ker\Phi_L=C\) for every admissible \(L\); if \(1\notin J_D\) and \(m\ge1\), they can be chosen with \(\mu_1(1)=1\). (c) So over \(\mathbb F_q\) the least \(m\) with \(\ker\Phi_L=C\) lies between \(\lceil r/\dim Z_L\rceil\) and \(\lceil r/c\rceil\le r\); for \(S'=1\), \(c=1\), and at \(L=0\) the least \(m\) is \(r\) over every field, the components of \(Y\) then spanning a space of dimension \(r\). (d) For Pang's errors \(P_\mu g_{\mu,i}\), \(e=\Delta+d\), \(w=1\): his selection cutoff \(\Delta+K+d\le D\) allows \(L=0\), where a \(p\)-linear form \(\mu(E\,u_1\cdots u_p)\), of degree \(e+pK\), does not fit; his simulation's cost \(K+\max(2\Delta+2d,\Delta+w)\le D\), stated on the same \(D\) for his Proposition 5.1 in the downstream-realization paragraph of the record of cor:pang-observation-calibration, gives \(L=D-\Delta-d-K\ge\Delta+d\), so thm:common-moment-radical's reserve \(b+e+pK\le D\) holds whenever \((p-1)K\le\Delta+d=(p-1)dt+d\), for instance when \(K\le dt\).

Proof. (a) If \(u\in C\), each \(E_iu\in J_D\), and \(E_iuv\) is a combination of monomial multiples of \(E_iu\) of degree at most \(e+K+L\le D\), so it lies in \(J_D\) and every \(\mu_j(E_iuv)=0\). For fixed \(j\), the coordinate \(u\mapsto\mu_j(zu)\) with \(z=E_iv\) depends only on the class of \(z\) in \(V_{e+L}/J_{e+L}\): if \(z\in J_{e+L}\), \(zu\in J_D\) by the same closure. So for each \(j\) the coordinates span a space of functionals of dimension at most \(\dim Z_L\), and \(\operatorname{rank}\Phi_L\le m\dim Z_L\); if \(\ker\Phi_L=C\), that rank is \(\dim U-\dim C=r\). \(\dim Z_L\le S'\dim V_L\); with the Boolean axioms in \(J_D\), \(v\) and its multilinear reduction differ by multiples of \(x_k^2-x_k\) of degree at most \(L\), so \(\dim Z_L\le S'N_n(L)\). (b) Old functionals \(\mu_1,\ldots,\mu_m\) are the rows of a linear map \(M:W\to F^m\), and every \(M\) arises. The coordinates with \(v=1\) are \((My_i)_i\) for \(y=T(u)\), so \(C\subseteq\ker\Phi_L\subseteq\ker\Phi_0\), and it suffices that \(\ker\Phi_0=C\): that no nonzero \(y\in Y\) has \(M\) vanishing on \(\operatorname{span}\{y_i\}\). For one \(y\), the maps vanishing on that span, of dimension at least \(c\), number at most \(q^{m(\dim W-c)}\); \(y\) and its nonzero multiples give the same span, so at most \((q^r-1)/(q-1)<q^r\) spans occur, and the bad maps number less than \(q^{r+m\dim W-mc}\le q^{m\dim W}\), the number of all \(M\). If \(1\notin J_D\), the maps whose first row takes the value \(1\) at the class of \(1\) number \(q^{m\dim W-1}\); for one \(y\) the bad ones among them number at most \(q^{m(\dim W-c)-1}\), since the condition fixes one coordinate of the first row in a direction independent of those vanishing on the span unless \([1]\) lies in that span, in which case there are none; so fewer than \(q^{r+m\dim W-mc-1}\le q^{m\dim W-1}\) are bad. (c) The lower bound is (a); the upper bound is (b) with \(m=\lceil r/c\rceil\), and \(c\ge1\); for \(S'=1\) every nonzero \(y\) spans a line, so \(c=1\), and at \(L=0\), \(Z_0\) is spanned by \(E_1\), so \(m\ge r\). (d) Pang's errors have degree at most \(\Delta+d\), the approximator of degree \(\Delta=(p-1)dt\) times an input of degree at most \(d\); with \(D\ge K+2\Delta+2d\), \(L=D-\Delta-d-K\ge\Delta+d\), and \(b+e+pK=\Delta+d+pK\le D\) exactly when \((p-1)K\le L\).

Hypotheses, each with its step. \(e+K+L\le D\) and the pseudoideal closure give \(C\subseteq\ker\Phi_L\) and the dependence on classes in (a); the Boolean axioms only replace \(\dim V_L\) by \(N_n(L)\); the finite field is used only in (b)'s count, for every \(q\ge2\) (over an infinite field the same conclusion needs the least rank over the algebraic closure, which can be smaller: \((e_1,e_2),(-e_2,e_1)\) has least rank \(2\) over \(\mathbb R\) and \(1\) over \(\mathbb C\)); \(1\notin J_D\) is used only for the normalization; old functionals are exactly the maps \(M\) on \(W\). The conclusions concern the stacked kernel of fixed errors, with no class protected; survival of a multiplier is a separate condition.

Degenerate cases. \(r=0\): every \(u\) is in \(C\), and \(m=0\) functionals detect it. \(S'=1\): \(c=1\), and at \(L=0\) the least \(m\) is \(r\), which contains prop:repair-cost-controls' rectangle obstruction for one scalar combination, its case \(m=1\). \(r=0\) with \(m=0\): the normalization is void. \(J_D=V_D\): \(W=0\), \(r=0\). A satisfiable domain model, \(J_D\) the degree-\(D\) part of a vanishing ideal: (a) to (c) hold unchanged.

Nonvacuity. Over \(\mathbb F_2\), \(n=3\), \(J_2\) the degree-\(2\) part of the Boolean ideal, \(U=V_1\), errors \(x_1,x_2\), \(e=K=1\), \(D=2\), \(L=0\): \(u=\alpha+\beta x_1+\gamma x_2+\delta x_3\) with \(x_1u\) and \(x_2u\) vanishing on the cube must vanish wherever \(x_1=1\) or \(x_2=1\), which forces \(u=0\), so \(C=0\) and \(r=4\); \(\dim Z_0=2\), so at least two functionals are needed; \(u=1+x_1\) gives \(y=(0,x_2+x_1x_2)\), whose span is a line, so \(c=1\) and (b) guarantees \(m=4\). The instance \(\ell=0\). Here \(W\) is the functions on \(\{0,1\}^3\), \(U\) the functions with \(\sum_au(a)=0\) (the coefficient of \(x_1x_2x_3\)), \(C\) is spanned by \((1+x_1)(1+x_2)\), and \(r=6\). With \(w_j\) the function representing \(\mu_j\), the coordinates are \(\langle f_j,u\rangle\) and \(\langle g_j,u\rangle\), \(f_j\) and \(g_j\) the restrictions of \(w_j\) to \(\{a_1=1\}\) and \(\{a_2=1\}\); \(\ker\Phi_0=C\) exactly when the \(f_j,g_j\) span all functions on the six points with \(a_1=1\) or \(a_2=1\), since the annihilator of \(C\) is those functions plus the constant. Two functionals give at most four vectors, so \(m\ge3\). Three suffice: on the blocks \(A=\{100,101\}\), \(B=\{010,011\}\), \(\Gamma=\{110,111\}\), take \(w_1=(A{:}\,e_1,\ B{:}\,e_2,\ \Gamma{:}\,e_1)\), \(w_2=(A{:}\,e_2,\ B{:}\,e_1,\ \Gamma{:}\,e_2)\), \(w_3=(A{:}\,e_1,\ B{:}\,e_1,\ \Gamma{:}\,0)\); then \(f_3\) is \(A{:}\,e_1\), \(f_1-f_3\) is \(\Gamma{:}\,e_1\), \(g_1-\Gamma{:}\,e_1\) is \(B{:}\,e_2\), \(g_3\) is \(B{:}\,e_1\), \(g_2-B{:}\,e_1\) is \(\Gamma{:}\,e_2\), and \(f_2-\Gamma{:}\,e_2\) is \(A{:}\,e_2\), all six. So the least \(m\) is \(3=r/S'\), the lower bound of (c), while (b) gives \(6\).
