prop:one-sided-half-plane-equivalence
  Statement: Finite configuration, Fejer kernel K_L, off-line pairs gamma_j +- i delta_j of multiplicity m_j: (a) D_off(tau) >= - sum m_j e^{delta_j L} min(2L, 8/(L((tau - gamma_j)^2 + delta_j^2))), so if all delta_j <= theta and theta L >= 2 the density is at least -8 M e^{theta L}/(L theta^2); (b) with delta* the largest distance and tau off the heights and midpoints of the pairs at delta*, for every L_1 some L > L_1 has D_L(tau) <= -q' e^{delta* L}/L, q' < q = sum |c_j|^2/(2 sum |c_j|), c_j = m_j/(tau - gamma_j - i delta*)^2; hence D_L(tau) >= -C e^{theta L} for all large L forces delta* <= theta.
  Assessment: Working proof: two elementary bounds for the kernel at complex arguments; the leading term at the largest distance is a real trigonometric polynomial in L with mean zero, whose negative part has mean at least q.
  Status: working_proof

prop:binomial-cluster-hiding
  Statement: One-sided density with the Fejer kernel: for k+1 off-line pairs at heights gamma_0 + j pi/L with multiplicities binom(k, j) at one distance delta, |G| <= (k+1)! (pi/b)^k/delta^2 and D_off >= -(2 e^b/(L delta^2)) (k+1)! (pi/b)^k - 6 * 2^k/(L delta^2); with real points on a lattice of spacing s <= 2 pi/L the whole density is nonnegative once 2 pi/s is at least that bound, which for k = floor(b/pi) is at most (2 e^2/(L delta^2)) (b/pi + 1)^(3/2) e^((1 - 1/pi) b) + 6 * 2^(b/pi)/(L delta^2).
  Assessment: Working proof: the exponential sum is (1 + q)^k with |1 + q| <= pi x/L. With real density l/(2 pi), for each length with b up to about 1.47 log l (asymptotically) the cluster built for that length is hidden at it, where a single pair shows from about log l; its multiplicities are at most l^0.32 there. One cluster per length; not shown that a fixed cluster hides at shorter lengths, that the family shows soon after, or that no admissible cluster hides longer.
  Status: working_proof
  Record: [Hidden cluster length](https://kbr.is-a.dev/math-research/branches/riemann/#entry-2026-10-11-hidden-cluster-length)
