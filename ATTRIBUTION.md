# Attribution and citation

Forks, adaptations, and independent continuations of this research are welcome.
Credit **Kamil Braun, PHP research notebook**, and identify the source repository
and version or dated result used. Preserve the notices required by the applicable
license; see [LICENSE](LICENSE).

## License obligations

- **Software (MIT):** preserve the copyright and permission notices in copies
  or substantial portions of the software. Include the applicable license when
  redistributing it. MIT does not require a paper citation merely for running a tool.
- **Original research material (CC BY 4.0):** when sharing covered material or
  adaptations, give appropriate credit, retain required notices, link to the
  license, and indicate changes as the license requires. Do not imply endorsement.
- **Third-party results and material:** retain their own attribution and license
  notices. Citing this repository does not replace credit to original sources.

These are a summary; the full license texts govern. The licenses do not impose
copyright restrictions on mathematical facts or ideas, or on uses that do not
require permission under applicable law.

## Scholarly credit for results and research use

If your work relies on a result, argument, computation, or tool learned from this
repository, give appropriate scholarly credit. Cite the specific numbered or
dated notebook entry and commit/version, and cite upstream sources where our work
uses their results. A GitHub fork relationship alone is not a citation in a paper.

Suggested citation:

> Kamil Braun. *PHP research notebook*. Relevant dated entry or tool, repository
> URL, and commit/version consulted.

`CITATION.cff` supplies machine-readable author/title information. Add the public
repository URL once it has been chosen; no URL or publication DOI is fabricated.
Correctness and novelty must be checked before citing a working argument as an
established theorem.

Scholarly attribution is an ethical expectation even when using a mathematical
result does not implicate copyright. This policy does not pretend that a software
or content license can enforce citation of every abstract mathematical fact.
See the [EMS publication code](https://euromathsoc.org/code-of-practice) and
[Creative Commons FAQ](https://creativecommons.org/faq/).

This project uses ChatGPT and Codex for research assistance, exposition, coding,
and checking. Such assistance is not independent peer review or a guarantee of
correctness, novelty, or copyright protection. Human authors remain responsible
for work they publish and for properly acknowledging contributions and sources.
