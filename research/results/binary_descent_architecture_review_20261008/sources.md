# Primary sources and scope — 8 October 2026

These are source-location and scope notes, not redistributed copies.

- Stacks Project, tag 0B2R, projective intersection lemma:
  https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/0B2R . Statement and affine-cone proof read.
  Nonempty projective intersection does not guarantee a point in an affine chart.
- Eisenbud, Huneke, Ulrich, *Heights of Ideals of Minors*, AJM 126 (2004),
  417–438: https://eisenbud.github.io/papers/pdfs/2004-001.pdf . Introduction
  and regular-ring height statements read. Properness of the minor ideal is a
  premise; no application of their numerical bound is claimed here.
- Adolphson, Sperber, *p-adic estimates for exponential sums and the theorem
  of Chevalley–Warning*, Ann. ENS 20 (1987), 545–556:
  https://www.numdam.org/item/10.24033/asens.1543.pdf . The introduction's
  exact classical Katz divisibility exponent was read. The notebook uses that
  quoted classical statement, not a new import of the refined p-adic proof.
- Craciun, Dickenstein, Shiu, Sturmfels, *Toric Dynamical Systems*:
  https://arxiv.org/pdf/0708.3431 . Section 2, complex balancing, saturation,
  moduli and steady-state statements and proofs read. Positive real conclusions
  are not transferred to characteristic two. Only the proposed algebraic chart
  translation is tested.
- Rosenthal, Sottile, *Some Remarks on Real and Complex Output Feedback*:
  https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/9702218 . Sections 1–2, necessary control conditions,
  the Grassmann projection criterion and proof read. The source must satisfy
  coherent parameter and affine-chart conditions; generic free feedback is not
  a source-specific theorem. An author-hosted copy had unusable extracted text;
  the arXiv copy was readable, so no source request remained.

The blind reviewer had only the goal and bounded positive results. The same
reviewer received the current route at the comparison stage and the stable
complete proofs at the final stage. Saved briefs and verdicts give exact scope.

The control program makes no charged-system evaluations. It checks Boolean
identities and independent finite-field matrix/torus controls. Charged survival
and the all-parameter threshold are proved in the notebook via circulation
grading and cut-code independence.
