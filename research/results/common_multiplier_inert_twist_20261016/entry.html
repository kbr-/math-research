General statement. cor:twist-open-band: on the product pigeonhole system with Boolean axioms, over any field, take a twisted repair whose twisted variables are \(t\) hole variables on distinct pigeons and distinct holes, the others untwisted. At window \(n\ge2\) with \(m\ge2\) it is scalar on \(\nu\). When \(TW+tT+\deg\nu\le n\), it reduces to a substitution repair at its full window. So a twisted repair outside the scalar and substitution classes, with \(m,n\ge2\), needs \(W\le n-1\) and \(TW+tT+\deg\nu\ge n+1\).

The open band

Setting. As in lem:twist-window-trade: \(R=k[x]\), \(\rho\) an algebra map given by commuting \(\rho(x_i)\) of entry degree at most \(T\ge1\), \(\rho(x_i)=x_iI\) for the untwisted variables, and \(\nu\) a twisted repair at window \(W=D-K\) with room \(R_0\), \(TW+\deg\nu\le R_0\le D'\). \(R_0\) appears only in the room clause, so any value in that interval may be taken. Here \(D'=n\), and \(J=C\) is the degree-\(n\) closure of the product pigeonhole system with \(m\) pigeons and \(n\) holes and the Boolean axioms. The twisted variables \(x_1,\dots,x_t\) are hole variables on distinct pigeons and distinct holes.

Corollary (twist open band).

  • (a) If \(W=n\), \(n\ge2\) and \(m\ge2\), then the room forces \(T=1\) and \(\deg\nu=0\), and \(\rho(g)\nu=g\nu\) for every \(g\): the repair is scalar.
  • (b) If \(TW+tT+\deg\nu\le n\), then for some \(\sigma\in\{0,1\}^t\) some coordinate of \(u_\sigma\) is a substitution repair of \(x_i\mapsto\sigma_i\) at window \(W\), in \(C\), with multiplier degree at most \(\deg\nu+tT\). This part holds for any twisted variables carrying Boolean axioms, on any system, with \(D'\) in place of \(n\).
  • (c) With \(m\ge2\) and \(n\ge2\), a twisted repair that is neither scalar on \(\nu\) nor reducible to a substitution repair at its own window needs \(W\le n-1\) and \(TW+tT+\deg\nu\ge n+1\).

Proof.

  • (a) is lem:top-window-twist-scalar, whose hypotheses are those stated. The room \(Tn+\deg\nu\le n\) gives \(T=1\) and \(\deg\nu=0\).
  • (b) Take \(R_0=D'=n\). Then \(R_0\ge TW+sT+\deg\nu\) for \(s=t\), and lem:twist-window-trade (a) says the first \(t\) splits keep the window. Its splits use the Boolean axioms \(x_i^2-x_i\), and its other variables are untwisted. After the \(t\) splits the action is scalar, and some coordinate of some \(u_\sigma\) is a substitution repair of \(x_i\mapsto\sigma_i\), measured in \(J=C\), at window \(W\), of vector degree at most \(\deg\nu+tT\).
  • (c) With \(m,n\ge2\), a repair outside both classes is in neither case. Not (a) means \(W\ne n\), and the room \(TW+\deg\nu\le n\) gives \(W\le n\), so \(W\le n-1\). Not (b) means \(TW+tT+\deg\nu\ge n+1\). \(\square\)

Remark, a sketch. A domain-model route would give (b) one degree short. The band. With \(T=1\), the band is \(W\le n-1\) with \(W+\deg\nu\ge n+1-t\), under the room \(W+\deg\nu\le n\).

  • \(t=1\): \(W+\deg\nu=n\), so \(\deg\nu\ge1\): one twist at tight room.
  • \(t=2\): \(W+\deg\nu\in\{n-1,n\}\), with \(W\le n-1\).
  • Larger \(t\): \(W+\deg\nu\ge n+1-t\).
