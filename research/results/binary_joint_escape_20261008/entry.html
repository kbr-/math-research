8 October 2026 — Generic returning rank need not survive a legal row

Working refutation of a stronger relative-intersection estimate; actual source ranks remain open.

Question. On general-step and the binary lower-bound statement, can joint measurement improve the partial-image bound without an estimate for the escaping image? Write \(c\) for current error rank and \(e\) for generic multiplied-image rank modulo the current image. The existing bound is \(c'\le\min(c,c/2+e)\). The test asks whether the generic returning dimension \(c-e\) itself retains half its rank under some legal binary row, giving \(c'\le(c+e)/2\). Such an estimate would help only when the actual source has a sufficiently large returning part; it is not already a source exclusion.

General statement. prop:relative-intersection-loss refutes that universal improvement, even for literal Boolean multiplication. For every integer \(t\ge1\), it constructs an allowed multiplier subspace with \(c=21t\), \(e=14t\), and optimal one-row residual rank \(18t>(c+e)/2\). It also refutes the corresponding common weighted-objective improvement. This is a satisfiable-domain calibration, not an unavoidable hard-source obstruction.

A cyclic orbit combines these local losses into a single multiplication map on six Boolean variables for which every nonzero legal row violates the proposed improvement. A small actual multiplication space

Let \(\Omega=\mathbb F_2^3\setminus\{0\}\), with point \(j\in\{1,\ldots,7\}\) labelled by its three binary digits, least significant first. Let \(\delta_j\) denote its point indicator and put \[ u_1=\delta_1+\delta_6+\delta_7,\quad u_2=\delta_2+\delta_5+\delta_7,\quad u_3=\delta_3+\delta_5+\delta_6,\qquad U_0=\langle u_1,u_2,u_3\rangle. \] Their values at points \(1,2,3\) are the identity matrix, so \(\dim U_0=3\). Multiplication is coordinatewise. Set \(L=\alpha y_1+\beta y_2+\gamma y_3\) over \(E=\mathbb F_2(\alpha,\beta,\gamma)\). Since every point is nonzero, all seven diagonal entries of \(L\) are nonzero in \(E\), hence \(\dim_E LU_{0,E}=3\).

Eliminate coordinates \(1,2,3\) modulo \(U_0\). Coordinate 4 is identically zero on both spaces. The remaining escape matrix, with columns \(5,6,7\), is \[ \begin{pmatrix} 0&\alpha+\beta+\gamma&\beta+\gamma\\ \alpha+\beta+\gamma&0&\alpha+\gamma\\ \beta+\gamma&\alpha+\gamma&0 \end{pmatrix}. \] Its determinant is zero because the two nonzero determinant terms coincide in characteristic two; a principal minor is \((\alpha+\beta+\gamma)^2\ne0\). Thus the generic escape rank is two and the generic returning dimension is one.

For a binary coefficient vector \(a\), let \(q_a=a\cdot y\) and \(H_a=\{y\in\Omega:q_a(y)=0\}\). The actual residual rank is \(\dim (1-q_a)U_0=\operatorname{rank}(U_0|_{H_a})\). Its drop is also \(\dim(U_0\cap q_aU_0)\): because \(q_a^2=q_a\), an element of this intersection is supported on \(q_a=1\), and conversely any element of \(U_0\) with that support equals its own product with \(q_a\).

Symmetry makes every shared choice fail

Proposition (relative intersection loss). For every \(t\ge1\), set \(r=\lceil\log_2t\rceil\). There is a subspace \(U\) of multilinear Boolean polynomials in \(6+r\) variables, of degree at most \(K=6+r\), such that with \(A=1\), \(g=(y_1,y_2,y_3)\), and the Boolean vanishing pseudoideal through any \(D\ge K+3\), the maps of the partial-image lemma have \[ c=21t,\qquad e=14t,\qquad c'(a)=\begin{cases}21t&a=0,\\18t&a\ne0.\end{cases} \] Consequently no legal row satisfies \(c'\le(c+e)/2\). The claim concerns arbitrary permitted multiplier subspaces, not specifically the full degree space or a source-conditioned kernel.

Proof. First take \(t=1\). The linear map \[ R(y_1,y_2,y_3)=(y_3,y_1+y_3,y_2) \] has characteristic polynomial \(X^3+X+1\), irreducible over \(\mathbb F_2\). It identifies with multiplication by a nonidentity element of \(\mathbb F_8^*\), so it has order seven and acts transitively on the nonzero vectors. Its dual action is also transitive: no nonidentity power has eigenvalue one, since that power minus the identity is invertible. Put \(U_j=\{u(R^jy):u\in U_0\}\), \(0\le j<7\). An invertible linear change of the three coefficient indeterminates preserves each generic escape rank, so every \(U_j\) has current rank three and escape rank two. For a fixed nonzero \(a\), the seven restriction problems run through all seven nonzero coefficient vectors in the preceding table. Their rank drops therefore sum to three.

Introduce three selector bits \(z\). Choose a bijection between their seven nonzero assignments and \(j=0,\ldots,6\), and write \(\chi_j(z)\) for each assignment indicator. Extend all functions of \(y\) by zero at \(y=0\), using their unique multilinear representatives of degree at most three. Define \[ U=\bigoplus_{j=0}^6\chi_j(z)U_j(y). \] The selectors have disjoint support, so this is a 21-dimensional space of degree at most six. Multiplication by \(q_a(y)\) acts separately on its seven summands. Generic escaping ranks add, giving 14; for every nonzero \(a\), the drops add to three, giving residual rank 18. The zero row changes nothing.

To identify these function-space ranks with the lemma's actual error maps, use \(T f=(y_1f,y_2f,y_3f)\). On functions supported at \(y\ne0\), \(T\) is injective, and it commutes with multiplication by \(L\) or \(q_a\). All spaces here have that support. Thus \(\dim T(U)=21\), the rank of \(T(LU)\) modulo \(T(U)\) is exactly 14, and the new error rank is exactly the residual rank just computed. Quotienting by the Boolean vanishing pseudoideal gives precisely these evaluations; no inconsistent old ideal or global model of a hard system is used. The input certificates \(y_i^2-y_i\) are present, and \(0+K+3\delta\le D\) holds with \(\delta=1\).

For arbitrary \(t\), take \(t\) disjoint assignment indicators on \(r\) additional selector bits and copy the six-variable construction under each. The resulting degree is at most \(6+r\). The same input tuple uses only the original three \(y\)-bits, so current ranks, escape ranks, and every specialized residual rank all multiply by \(t\). This proves the stated values and the strict gap \(18t-35t/2=t/2\). \(\square\)

Equivalently, the seven spaces \(U_j\) already refute an improved simultaneous weighted objective with equal weights: the best common row has total residual rank 18, above \(\sum_j(c_j+e_j)/2=35/2\). Choosing separate rows for the seven spaces gives total residual rank 14 instead. This explicitly distinguishes one shared legal row from independent per-group choices. It does not claim that independent choices are unavailable in every application, or that coupling always hurts. The existing \(\min(c,c/2+e)\) estimate remains valid and merely gives no decrease on this example.

Decisive test and scope

The search tested two named universal hypotheses, maximum and nonzero-average retention of half the generic returning dimension, on all subspaces of \(\mathbb F_2^7\). It enumerated unique reduced row-echelon forms, with dimension counts \(1,127,2667,11811,11811,2667,127,1\), total 29,212. Every space passed the existential test; seven failed the average test. Instead of testing larger cubes, the existing disjoint-sum operation and a transitive coefficient symmetry converted the first failure into the existential counterexample above.

Generic escape ranks in the search were exact, not sampled heuristically. Every escape minor has size at most three, hence is homogeneous of degree at most three in the three coefficient variables. Substitution \((\alpha,\beta,\gamma)=(1,\zeta,\zeta^4)\), with \(\zeta\) of degree 13 over \(\mathbb F_2\), sends the monomials of each fixed degree injectively to powers of degree at most 12. No nonzero minor vanishes. GAP native exact rank routines supplied both these ranks and the seven binary restriction ranks. The separate composition check verifies all seven transformed spaces and all eight legal rows, with the zero-row control and independent-row comparison following from the verified dimensions and local gains.
