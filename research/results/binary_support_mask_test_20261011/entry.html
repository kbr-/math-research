Boolean support mask

Lemma. Work over \(\mathbb F_2\) in \(v\ge1\) variables, with \(b_j=x_j^2+x_j\) and \(J_s=(\prod_{j\in T}b_j:|T|=s)\); set \(J_{v+1}=0\). Define commuting linear operators \[ L_jf=(1+x_j)f|_{x_j=0}+x_jf|_{x_j=1},\qquad D_j=I-L_j, \quad E_tf=\sum_{|T|=t}\left(\prod_{j\in T}D_j\right)f \] for \(1\le t\le v\). Every polynomial has the unique free-module expansion \(f=\sum_{\epsilon,\alpha}c_{\epsilon,\alpha}x^\epsilon b^\alpha\), with \(\epsilon\in\{0,1\}^v\). On the term with support \(U=\{j:\alpha_j>0\}\), \(E_t\) acts by the scalar \(\binom{|U|}{t}\bmod2\). Consequently, if \(f\in J_s\), \(1\le s\le v\), and \(\binom{s}{t}\) is odd, then \[ M=f+E_tf\in J_{s+1}. \] There is an explicit polynomial \(Q_t(x,Z)\), linear in \(Z\), with \(Q_t(x,b)=E_tf\). If \(f\) has division-free unbounded-fanin circuit size \(S\), counting gates and wires, and depth \(\delta\), then \(Q_t\) has size \[ O\!\left(\binom vt\,[3^t(S+v+t+1)]^4\right) \] and depth at most \(4\delta+20\), with binary constants. The estimate is polynomial for fixed \(t\); it is not a polynomial bound for growing \(t\).

Support proof. The free-module expansion follows by the separate monic relations \(x_j^2+x_j=b_j\), as proved in rank coordinate freedom. If \(\alpha_j=0\), the term is linear in \(x_j\) and \(L_j\) fixes it. If \(\alpha_j>0\), both endpoint evaluations vanish, so \(L_j\) kills it. Thus \(D_j\) is one or zero on each such basis term according as \(j\in U\) or not. Different coordinate operators commute; exactly \(\binom{|U|}{t}\) subsets survive. Membership in \(J_s\) means every basis term uses at least \(s\) distinct \(b_j\). The displayed parity cancels all terms of support exactly \(s\), and creates none of smaller support, proving the assertion about \(M\).

Correction and circuit proof. For each \(T\), let \(f_T=\prod_{j\in T}D_jf\), and choose its least index \(i(T)\). Setting \(x_{i(T)}\) to zero or one kills \(f_T\), so \(f_T=b_{i(T)}h_T\). The formal quotient is given by the two divided differences of coordinate-line coefficients. Put \(Q_t=\sum_{|T|=t}h_TZ_{i(T)}\). This proves the exact specialization and linearity, without extracting any coefficient of an unknown certificate.

Expand each \(D_j=I-(1+x_j)\operatorname{ev}_{j,0}-x_j\operatorname{ev}_{j,1}\). Commutation at different coordinates expresses \(f_T\) as at most \(3^t\) terms, each a coordinate restriction of the original circuit times at most \(t\) linear factors. Separate copies give size at most a constant times \(3^t(S+v+t+1)\), depth at most \(\delta+3\). Two divided differences give the fourth-power size bound and depth at most \(4(\delta+3)+4\). Multiplication by a placeholder and summation over \(T\) add two layers, within the stated bound. No division gates, extension constants, or arbitrary homogeneous-component extraction occur. For \(t=1\), this is simply \(Q_1=\sum_j Z_j\mathcal D_{j,1}\mathcal D_{j,0}f\), of size \(O(v(S+v+2)^4)\).

Nonvacuity. If \(s\) is odd, choose \(t=1\). More generally, if \(2^e\) is the least power of two occurring in the binary expansion of positive \(s\), then \(\binom{s}{2^e}\) is odd: expand \((1+z)^s\) over \(\mathbb F_2\) as the product of \(1+z^{2^j}\) for its set bits. This does not make the cost for growing \(2^e\) cheap. For \(f=\prod_{j=1}^s b_j\), the first-bit mask is zero for odd \(s\), but equals \(f\ne0\) for even \(s\). Rank face mask

Corollary. For the rank inputs \(F_{ik}=\delta_{ik}+(AB)_{ik}\), with \(m>n\ge1\) and even positive \(r=m-n\), take \(s=r+1\) and \(t=1\). Every Hilbert-like refutation has a polynomial-overhead, constant-depth-overhead replacement whose restrictions to all coordinate faces leaving at most \(r+1\) individual matrix entries free equal restrictions of one fixed polynomial-size constant-depth circuit. This circuit is computed solely from the inputs, independent of the refutation.

Proof. The previous cycle proved \(P\in J_{r+1}\), and \(s\) is odd. The lemma gives \(M\in J_{r+2}\) and the patch. Here \(v=2mn\), the input size is \(O(m^2n)\) and depth is constant. Thus its first-bit circuit bound is polynomial in the rank input encoding length and its depth remains bounded. The sharp nonzero faces of the previous cycle show this is not vacuous vanishing of \(P\) itself: \(P\) can be nonzero there, while the new multiplier \(M\) vanishes. \(\square\)

The corollary gives a global certificate conditional on a supplied certificate, with cheap simultaneous face data. It neither assembles the previous facewise certificates into a cheap unconditional refutation nor proves that the rank family is easy. The all-certificate face-hardness strategy fails in this scope; an invariant of the full coset or a restriction to a smaller simulation image can still be pursued.
