The complete observation map

Let \(J_D\subseteq F[x]_{\le D}\) be a degree-\(D\) pseudoideal over any field, let \(\deg w\le b\), and let \(U\subseteq F[x]_{\le K}\) be an \(F\)-linear subspace. Put \(H=\dim[wU]\), with all classes taken in \(Q_D=F[x]_{\le D}/J_D\). The interesting case is \(H>0\).

Take a finite proof DAG using axiom/assumption leaves and MP. Give every actual occurrence a fixed polynomial falsity value of degree at most \(L\ge1\), and normalize the final occurrence to the literal polynomial one. At an MP step let \(a,c,b\) be the representative values of the antecedent, implication and conclusion; let \(a',b'\) be their occurrences inside the implication. Include the two copy discrepancies \(a-a'\), \(b'-b\), and the actual defect \[R=c-(1-a')b'.\] If the representatives coincide literally, those discrepancies are zero. Include every leaf value. These are observations, not additional hypotheses that the defects vanish. One interpretation is used consistently at every occurrence; no row choices are changed during replay. Let \(\mathcal E\) be this complete list.

Corollary (proof observation rank). If \[b+K+2L\le D,\tag{1}\] then \[\ker\bigl(f\in U\longmapsto([wef])_{e\in\mathcal E}\bigr) =\ker\bigl(f\in U\longmapsto[wf]\bigr).\tag{2}\] Thus the actual proof-observation map descends to an injective map on \([wU]\) and has rank \(H\). Booleanity is not needed for this algebraic replay statement.

Proof. Every observation has degree at most \(2L\), so bounded absorption proves the inclusion from the right kernel to the left. Conversely let \(u=wf\) with every \(ue\in J_D\). At a leaf its weighted value vanishes by definition. Suppose the two MP premise values satisfy \(ua,uc\in J_D\). The first copy discrepancy gives \(ua'\in J_D\). Multiply this membership by the known polynomial \(b'\), paying at most \(\deg u+2L\). The literal identity \[b'=c+a'b'-R\] gives \(ub'\in J_D\). The second copy discrepancy then gives \(ub\in J_D\). Linear combinations and every absorption stay within (1); multiplying by \(b'\) does not assume that \(b'\) has already been proved. Induction on the proof DAG reaches its final value one, hence \(u\in J_D\). This proves (2) and the rank assertion. \(\square\)

All leaf values were retained, including logical axiom instances. A leaf whose weighted value is already known in \(J_D\) can be omitted only with its membership and degree cost supplied. The paid complete-source map has the same rank

The comparison part of the corollary is a general PC replay fact. Suppose a complete source has a PC refutation through degree \(C\), and all fresh generators under consideration have original degree at most \(C\). Let one fixed polynomial substitution \(\theta\) of variable degree at most \(T\ge1\) preserve the coefficient field and map every retained old generator into \(J_D\). Assume their weighted multiples and every substituted proof line fit \[b+K+TC\le D.\tag{3}\] Let \(\mathcal G_\theta\) be the substituted fresh companions and coefficient-field generators. Then \[\ker\bigl(f\mapsto([w\theta(g)f])_{g\text{ fresh}}\bigr)=\ker(f\mapsto[wf]),\tag{4}\] and its rank is also \(H\).

Proof. One direction is absorption. For the other, put \(u=wf\) and multiply every line of the substituted PC refutation by \(u\). Fresh weighted axioms vanish by the kernel hypothesis; old weighted axioms belong to \(J_D\) by their supplied memberships and (3). An original multiplication by a source variable becomes multiplication by its fixed polynomial image of degree at most \(T\). Expanding that multiplier into monomials implements the step within the same \(b+K+TC\) ceiling; linear combinations are unchanged. The final line is \(u\), so it belongs to \(J_D\). This is exactly the recorded weighted-substitution/replay argument.

For a substitution by \(\mathbb F_2\)-polynomials with the old Boolean equations in \(J_D\), coefficient-field images are old Boolean consequences through degree \(2T\), so they can be dropped from the map when that certificate and its weighted cost fit (3). This is the complete-companion comparison used here; arbitrary extension-field coefficients require separately supplied Boolean-valued-image certificates. Inactive generators above the original proof ceiling are not silently inserted into an unaffordable map. For the audited unbalanced compiler, the complete profile generators fit its common ceiling, and the original proof-height-free PC compilation supplies the refutation needed in (3).

Under the common reserve \(b+K+\max(2L,TC)\le D\) and these hypotheses, both total ranks are exactly \(H\). No cofactor enlargement is hidden: the same \(U\) occurs in (2) and (4).
