# Elementary fillings and the mixed-selector interface

The [notebook entry](https://kbr.is-a.dev/math-research/#entry-2026-09-15-elementary-fillings-and-selector-gap)
contains the full proof and audit. No new multilevel ENS extension was obtained.

## Exact mathematical scope

The entry gives a direct chain proof that every reduced (r-2)-cycle in the
r-by-N chessboard complex bounds when N >= 2r-1. This is the known vanishing
statement previously imported from the Björner–Lovász–Vrećica–Živaljević
connectivity theorem. It is not a claim of a new topological theorem.

The proof uses the preceding cycle's signed row-trade spanning lemma.
Last-row slices are cycles in links and are filled by induction. Removing
their cones leaves a top cycle on r-1 rows. Row trades span it, and each trade
has an unused column where a last-row cone fills it. The orientation identity,
base case, link width, and spare-column condition are explicit.

The argument works over every field (indeed any commutative coefficient ring).
Together with the existing matching-moment completeness and duality argument,
it replaces the imported filling input in the old stable-filtration theorem.
It does not prove homotopy connectivity, strengthen the old degree range,
establish augmented ENS stability, or settle the main Frege source obligation.

## Coupling audit

The preceding affine theorem extends prescribed old matching-compatible
moments. It does not promise to preserve every earlier joint selector
functional. The next sufficient construction may choose those moments jointly
with later ones. For refutation exclusion, one normalized feasible choice is
enough; a universal moment-preservation statement is stronger than necessary.

The exact costed modular source is a potentially weaker sufficient endpoint
than annihilating every concrete ENS consequence. Supplied module witness
ceilings remain essential.
