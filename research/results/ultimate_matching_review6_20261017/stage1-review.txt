1. Architectures. (a) Dual chaining over the conjugate class: the recorded pieces cover separate
   regimes (fine-grid (c) levels from N^c; flow-coarea (b), (d) with a (log Y)^(1/4) or (log log
   N)^(1/4) loss; guided-chain (d) nested chains of any number of layers when guides exist; band
   families; aligned sums). First lemma: every level set of a class function at level y has a guide
   from a family of N^c unions of coarse cells fixed in advance with P(S)+12W#T <= beta P(A); with
   it the layer-cake decomposition and guided-chain (c), (d) would give the conjecture's order at
   (4,4), at least for single-sign increments at one level. Expected break: counting, about exp(c P
   2^(l_1)) unions of coarse cells of perimeter P, so N^c guides cover only P 2^(l_1) <~ log N, the
   curve-counting origin of the Leighton-Shor (log N)^(3/4); and a general G mixes levels and
   orientations, a gradient truncated to level bands not being a gradient. (b) Primal, anisotropic
   multiscale: on the curve 1/alpha_1 - 1/alpha_2 lies in (-1/2, 1/2), so cells of aspect (log
   N)^|1/alpha_1 - 1/alpha_2| are in the range of lem:anisotropic-leighton-shor-bound. First lemma:
   one matching good at two levels t < t' at once, beyond thm:low-level-mixed-counts. Break:
   coupling the levels; lem:per-level-gluing-fails is for fixed X and Y and does not exclude a
   gluing that uses the randomness. (c) Refutation at an interior pair, mixed levels near y =
   exp(sqrt(log N)), where the recorded min{(log Y)^(1/4), (log N/log y)^(1/4)} peaks at (log
   N)^(1/8). First lemma: a converse of cor:exponential-matching-discrepancy-reduction without the
   tube-density hypothesis of lem:tube-density-converse; without it, refuting the dual conjecture
   does not refute the goal. Break: segments of Leighton-Shor length can give local tube density
   above KN.

2. Most leveraged: the Hopf-Lax reduction; the two class lower bounds (they fix the target order);
   lem:guided-chain-bound (the one bound for any number of layers; it moves the problem onto
   guides); lem:fine-grid-leighton-shor with lem:flow-coarea-level-bound (they fix the hard window
   of levels); lem:anisotropic-leighton-shor-bound (the geometry of the whole curve). Dead ends or

   electrical one, whose roughly Gaussian marginals probably fail as in
   thm:dyadic-flow-obstruction); the method exclusions; lem:common-exponent-cap, implied by
   thm:below-curve-matching-exclusion. Observations: by convex duality the flow route through
   lem:flow-matching-bound is no easier than the dual route, the best flow's cost being the class
   discrepancy, and the dyadic, electrical and greedy flows are trial points;
   lem:mollified-smooth-part leaves the difficulty in the remainder.

3. Where the difficulty lies: the bounds lose nothing at the extremes (levels from N^c, single
   layers, bands, few levels); the loss sits at intermediate levels, polylog(N) <= y <= N^(o(1)),
   with many layers or several levels: in primal terms the coupling of the tail levels in one
   matching, in dual terms chaining over level sets (the guide family); Leighton-Shor chaining with
   an Orlicz weight.
