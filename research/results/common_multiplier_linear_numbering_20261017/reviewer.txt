1. **Joint kernel as an intersection.** The joint C-kernel is always ∩_e Ann_C(E_e). If each single error has the transfer with its own numbering, the joint kernel is ∩_e (A_e + W), where A_e = Ann_H(E_e)∩V_1 and W = C∩V_1 = span{Q_k}. No closure computation is needed after that.
2. **The space A_e.** Take E_e = x_e(1+q_1) with e=(i,j) and i,j ∉ {0,1}. A coefficient count gives A_e as the set of u = c + Σa_f x_f with:
   - c + a_e = 0;
   - a_00 = a_11 = 0 (in characteristic 2, a_00 = a_11 instead);
   - a_f = 0 for every f compatible with e other than 00, 11, e;
   - a_f free for f in row i or column j.

   At 6×5 over F_2 this gives dim A_e = N+m = 11 and A_e∩W = span{Q_i}, so dim(A_e+W) = 16. That matches the check's "16".
3. **Membership in A_e + W.** u ∈ A_e+W if and only if: for every pigeon k≠i, row k is constant off hole j (call that value −λ_k), and c + a_{ij} = Σ_{k≠i} λ_k. The λ_i term cancels.
4. **Intersecting over the family.** Each row is constant off j for at least two holes j (N≥4), so each row is constant. In characteristic 2 the exceptions at 00 and 11 are removed by the ν=2 family, where rows 0 and 1 are ordinary. Then u ≡ a constant γ modulo W, and the remaining condition forces γ=0. Hence the joint kernel is C∩V_1.
5. **Per-error equality.** Use the polynomial lemma with E_e alone, its monomials being x_e, x_e x00 and x_e x11. Rank hole j at 0 and holes 0 and 1 at ranks 1 and 2. For N≥6, every t∪E (at most three pigeons, the added pigeon off the top hole) appears to satisfy the ballot condition of lem:ballot-dance-maps, so it dances in every pigeon order. The binding segment is the whole board: 3 members against N−3 non-members.
   - N=5 needs a numbering search. Alternatively, the per-error equality is already computed there over F_2 (dim 16).

If this checks out, uncollapsed (a) holds for every N≥6 over any field, and at N=5 over F_2. The per-error-then-intersect step also looks general, and is worth registering.
