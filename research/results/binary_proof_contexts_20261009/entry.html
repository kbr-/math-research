What a compressed backward context representation certifies

For a fixed PC derivation, begin with coefficient one on its final line and propagate contexts backward: a linear-combination inference distributes its context with the two scalar coefficients, and a multiplication inference multiplies the predecessor's context by that variable. Combining contributions reaching the same original axiom yields the ordinary identity \[ 1=\sum_j C_j g_j, \] including the field axioms. This is the usual flattening of the derivation. Representing the \(C_j\)'s by a shared circuit or a weighted automaton can shorten their description; it does not establish \(\deg(C_jg_j)\le D\). If every complete summand had that bound, the identity would be a degree-\(D\) ordinary NS refutation by definition.

On the path control, every such degree-two claim is excluded by the displayed design. The PC proof still has degree two because it multiplies a derived final line after cancellation, rather than multiplying every old certificate summand. This is exactly the historical reuse lemma. Allowing a high-degree static context and charging only its compact automaton dimension would therefore change the degree statement that the old separator can justify. Algebraic cancellation between different original-axiom summands does not lower each summand's certificate charge.

A genuinely proof-local state construction may instead keep separate intermediate states and transitions for the actual derivation, including these resets to lower actual degree. Such a construction is not refuted here. However, its unit preservation and those transitions are additional obligations, and neither their existence at a small cost nor a source-size rank estimate follows from minimal realization alone. A complete full-space kernel satisfying the previous horizon criterion would suffice, but is stronger than the required proof-local replay and must not be imposed without justification.
