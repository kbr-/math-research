Charged thin-tail obstruction

Proposition. Use the proper binary charged pseudoideal \(J_D\) on a connected graph with \(n\) vertices and \(m\) edges, in the recorded window \(\max(4,2\Delta)\le D<\varepsilon n/8\), with its graph, charge and one-hot encoding conventions. Let \(C_D\subseteq J_D\) be any degree-\(D\) Boolean pseudoideal, including the old PC closure. Suppose \(m\ge8n\), \(D\le m/8\). For integers \(8\le t\), \(2t\le K\le D\), no degree-compatible monomial order and real \(X\) with \[K\le X\le 2(K-t)+1+(K-t+1)/t\] can make every degree-\(j\) standard-monomial level of \(C_D\) have size at most \(\binom Xj\), for \(K-t<j\le K\).

Proof. By the cut-code dictionary and binary descent, \(h(j)=\dim V_j/(J_D\cap V_j)\) counts ternary cut-code cosets meeting the radius-\(j\) ball. The quotient code dimension is \(R=m-n+1\ge7m/8\). With \(z=1/14\), \[h(D)\le\sum_{j\le D}2^j\binom mj\le z^{-D}(1+2z)^m\le[(8/7)14^{1/8}]^m<3^{7m/16}\le3^{R/2}.\] The strict inequality follows by raising bases to the sixteenth power: it is \(2^{50}<147^7\). Indeed \(147^7>144^7=2^{28}3^{14}>2^{50}\), since \(3^7=2187>2048=2^{11}\). The code-ball growth theorem at \(\eta=1/2\) gives \(h(K)/h(K-t)\ge(3/2)^t\).

Initial-space inclusion shows that every standard monomial for \(J_D\) is standard for \(C_D\), in the same order. The proposed caps thus also hold for \(J_D\). Apply the intrinsic shadow ratio to the singleton error \(E=1\), with degree zero. Its rank at cutoff \(k\) is exactly \(h(k)\). The displayed bound on \(X\) gives \(\rho=(X-K+t)/(K-t+1)\le1+1/t\), hence \[h(K)/h(K-t)\le1+\sum_{i=1}^t\rho^i\le1+e_{\rm Euler}t.\] But \((3/2)^8=6561/256>25\), and induction using \((3/2)(1+3t)>1+3(t+1)\) for \(t\ge2\) gives \((3/2)^t>1+3t>1+e_{\rm Euler}t\) for \(t\ge8\). Properness ensures the denominator is nonzero. This contradiction proves the claim. No global charged algebra or satisfying assignment is used. \(\square\)

Scope. In the current growing family, \(m\ge n\Delta/8\) and \(D=\lfloor n\Delta/64\rfloor\), so \(\Delta\ge64\) supplies both new numerical hypotheses. This excludes the stated whole-old-closure premise throughout its admissible \(t,K\) range. A smaller actual-error standard family, a different containing pseudoideal not contained in this canonical \(J_D\), or another hard family is not excluded. It is a consequence of two existing tools, not a new shadow method.

Correction to the flat-shadow threshold

Corollary. In the earlier flat-shadow proposition, the equivalence in equation (1), for \(r>m\) and \(q=2m-r\ge K\), must read \[q\le 2K-2\delta+1+(K-\delta+1)/\delta.\] Its right side was too large by \(\delta\). The graded cap and complementary-flat control remain valid; the \(r\le m\), \(K<m/4\) exclusion remains valid.

Proof and control. Rearranging \((X-K+\delta)/(K-\delta+1)\le1+1/\delta\) gives exactly the corrected right side as an upper bound on \(X\). When \(q\ge K\), the degree-\(K\) cap forces \(X\ge q\), and \(X=q\) suffices for all caps. For \(r\le m\), the cap still forces \(X\ge m\), while the corrected upper bound is at most \(3K-1<m\). The old iff fails at \(K=2,\delta=1,m=9,r=12,q=6\): the old right side is six, but the correct one is five and the minimal \(X=6\) gives \(\rho=5/2>2\). The pair-counting polynomial \((1+z)^6\) is realized by six single-pivot and three double-pivot pairs. This is a control for the majorant's threshold, not a charged hard instance. \(\square\)

Compression controls

Proposition. The ternary zero-sum predicate on \(r\ge1\) labels has a tensor-train representation of bond dimension three and a polynomial-size Boolean circuit of depth \(O(1+\log r)\). Neither resource implies polynomial-size fixed-depth \(\mathrm{AC}^0[2]\) circuits. Separately, the exact diagonal projector for \((x_1=0)\lor(x_2=0)\) on two qutrits is not a stabilizer-code projector.

Proof. Let \(S\) cyclically permute three basis states. The zero-sum predicate is \(e_0^{\mathsf T}S^{x_1}\cdots S^{x_r}e_0\), over the reals or any field. Cutting this product at any position factors its unfolding through dimension three. For a Boolean implementation, encode each partial residue by three indicators and combine two residues with \(c_s=\bigvee_{a+b=s}(u_a\land v_b)\). Balanced composition uses \(O(r)\) fixed-size gates and \(O(\log r)\) depth; a serial version uses linear depth. Restricting every label to zero or one gives MOD3, to which the recorded fixed-depth lower bound applies. Thus these small representations do not give the missing depth guarantee.

The two-qutrit diagonal predicate accepts \(3+3-1=5\) basis states, so its projector has rank five. A stabilizer projector in dimension nine has rank \(9/|\mathcal S|\), an integer divisor of nine, by the stabilizer size theorem; five is impossible. The dimension identity follows by averaging the stabilizer group and taking the trace: only the identity has nonzero trace. This reuses the exact mechanism of the earlier binary stabilizer-parity control, with qutrit disjunction in place of binary XOR; it is not a new obstruction principle. This is a complex Hilbert-space encoding test, not finite-field PC. Sums of stabilizer projectors, nonadditive codes and larger annotated spaces remain possible. \(\square\)
