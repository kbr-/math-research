conj:ultimate-matching
  Statement: Talagrand's ultimate matching conjecture (Conjecture 5.8 of The Status of my favorite problems): for alpha_1, alpha_2 in (0,infinity] with 1/alpha_1+1/alpha_2=1/2 and a universal L, with probability tending to one as N grows, two independent samples X, Y of N i.i.d. uniform points of [0,1]^2 admit a matching pi with sum_i exp[(sqrt(N/log N)|X_i^j-Y^j_pi(i)|/L)^alpha_j] <= 2N for j=1,2; alpha=infinity means max_i |X_i^j-Y^j_pi(i)| <= L sqrt(log N/N). Research Problem 17.1.2 of Upper and Lower Bounds for Stochastic Processes (2nd ed., 2021) states it with Y evenly spread, a sufficient form by lem:two-sample-matching-transfer.
  Assessment: Open conjecture, imported from its two sources with their read statements. Known in the monograph's setting: the pair (alpha,infinity) for alpha<1/2 with constant K(alpha) (Theorem 17.1.3), and the pair (2,2) with constant sqrt(L) but only with probability 9/10 (Talagrand-Yukich), not with probability tending to one; the three-dimensional analogue with exponent sum 1 is Theorem 18.1.1.

lem:two-sample-matching-transfer
  Statement: For N>=2, alpha in (0,infinity] and point sequences X,Y,Z of length N: if sigma_1 matches X to Z and sigma_2 matches Y to Z with sum_i exp[(sqrt N|P_i^j-Z^j|/(L sqrt(log N)))^alpha] <= 2N (or the maximum bound L sqrt(log N/N) at alpha=infinity), then sigma_2^-1 sigma_1 matches X to Y with the same bound at constant max(2,2^(1/alpha))L. lem:exponential-matching-consequences
  Statement: If a matching pi of point sequences satisfies sum_i exp[(sqrt N|X_i^j-Y^j_pi(i)|/(L sqrt(log N)))^alpha_j] <= 2N, N>=2, then max_i |X_i^j-Y^j_pi(i)| <= L sqrt(log N)(log 2N)^(1/alpha_j)/sqrt N, and for alpha_j>=1 the coordinate distances sum to at most 2L sqrt(N log N).   Assessment: Working elementary proof (each summand at least 1; t <= exp(t^alpha) for alpha>=1); records with constants the deductions both sources sketch.
