1. Direct interpolation on a costed domain
Let \(\Gamma(Y)\) contain the old Boolean equations over a fixed \(\mathbb F_p\). Let \(\Omega\subseteq\{0,1\}^s\) be nonempty, with the uniform reduction property: every old polynomial \(A\) vanishing on \(\Omega\) has an ordinary NS certificate from \(\Gamma\) through \(\deg A+\lambda\), for a specified \(\lambda\ge0\).
Take any finite acyclic complete product-only source, at positive accuracies. Inputs are literal polynomials \(H_{a,i}(Y,(P_c)_{c<a})\); earlier coefficients occur there only through genuine whole products, while proof cofactors may use them freely. Define the induced functions \(\overline g_{a,i}\) and their common-zero indicators \(\zeta_a\) on \(\Omega\) recursively as in the semantic lift.
For each block separately, suppose old polynomials \(\pi_{a,b}\) of degree at most \(t_a\) are zero-one valued on \(\Omega\), with exactly one equal to one at each point. Supply old-affine tuples \(L_{a,b,i}\) satisfying
\[ \pi_{a,b}(Y)=1\quad\Longrightarrow\quad \overline g_{a,i}(Y)=L_{a,b,i}(Y)\quad\text{for every }i,\quad Y\in\Omega. \tag{DP-branches} \]
Different blocks may use different partitions; there is no required global refinement. Suppose all reference tuples, at their original accuracies, have one nonzero old weight \(f\), of degree at most \(k\), and old-only coefficient normalizers \(\gamma_{a,b,u,i}\) of degree at most \(T_0\ge1\), with their companions zero on \(\Omega\cap\{f\ne0\}\). These are actual common-support maps, not merely one-level nonrefutability statements.
Working theorem. Put \(t=\max_a t_a\). Every degree-\(D\) ordinary NS or PC source proof of an old target \(q\) transfers to an old proof of \(fq\) through
\[ T=T_0+t,\qquad B=TD+k+\lambda. \tag{DP-cost} \]
The branch inventory is \(\sum_a|\{b\}|\). The direct map has no extra dependency-depth factor; original expanded source degrees still enter \(D\). If \(f\) is separated from old consequences through \(B\), source refutations are excluded.
2. Compatibility and the original-degree proof
Proof. Fix old variables and give each source coefficient its one old polynomial image
\[ \Phi(r_{a,u,i})=\beta_{a,u,i} =\sum_b\pi_{a,b}\gamma_{a,b,u,i},\qquad \deg\beta_{a,u,i}\le T_0+t. \tag{DP-map} \]
On \(\Omega\cap\{f\ne0\}\), induct through the source. If earlier products have their induced values, the actual current inputs are \(\overline g_{a,i}\). The unique active partition member selects one reference tuple equal to those inputs, and (DP-map) equals its normalizer. The resulting product is one if every input is zero, and zero otherwise: the first conclusion follows from the product formula, and the second from its normalized companions. Thus \(\Phi(P_a)=\zeta_a\), including shared products and zero or empty tuples. This establishes the induction without a simultaneous choice of one leaf for all blocks.
Consequently \(f\Phi(E)\) vanishes on \(\Omega\) for every source companion \(E\). For original degree \(e=\deg E\), the actual polynomial has degree at most \(Te+k\), so the uniform domain property supplies a certificate through \(Te+k+\lambda\). All coefficient images are \(\mathbb F_p\)-valued on the old Boolean cube; their field images vanish there, with cost at most \(pT+k\). An old generator is fixed and has its ordinary weighted certificate. For an NS cofactor of original degree at most \(D-e\), add \(T(D-e)\) to the companion-image cost. This gives (DP-cost).
For PC, first derive the weighted axiom images with those ordinary NS certificates, then simulate each source line \(u\) by \(f\Phi(u)\). Its actual degree is at most \(T\deg u+k\), even if its axiom prelude used the extra \(\lambda\). Multiplication by a source variable becomes multiplication by its degree-at-most-\(T\) image; a nonzero predecessor has degree at most \(D-1\). Reuse of that line therefore fits the same ceiling \(TD+k+\lambda\), without adding \(\lambda\) at every inference. The last line is \(fq\). \(\square\)
This is a sharper composition of the existing interpolation and semantic-lift mechanisms when low-degree old partition polynomials are available. Composing a fresh-branch map of factor \(t+1\) with a degree-\(T_0\) map only records \((t+1)T_0\); constructing (DP-map) directly gives \(t+T_0\). The offset-compression theorem constructs selectors from small images and has its own degree cost; small image alone does not provide the low-degree selectors assumed here.
3. A PHP domain supplies the certificates and removes parent rank
Use the fixed robust graph family from the fixed-graph affine endpoint, and project its distinguished row coordinates globally. Let \(\Gamma'_H(Y)\) be the projected functional graph base. Define \(\Omega\) to be the Boolean incidence vectors of partial matchings using only nondistinguished edges. It is nonempty (the empty matching is allowed). It does not assert that all original PHP axioms hold.
The defining forbidden monomials are \(Y_eY_{e'}\) for distinct nondistinguished edges sharing a row or a hole. They are actual, unchanged exclusion generators of \(\Gamma'_H\). Boolean reduction followed by deleting monomials containing a forbidden pair is degree-nonincreasing. Surviving monomials correspond to matchings; their evaluation matrix on matching supports, ordered by size, is triangular with diagonal one. Thus a polynomial vanishing on \(\Omega\) reduces to zero, giving the uniform property with \(\lambda=0\). This explicitly certifies the domain; it is not agreement on models of an unsatisfiable system.
Consider a complete two-level source on that graph. Every used bottom \(c\) has input tuple \((1-Y_e)_{e\in S_c}\), where \(S_c\) is a nonempty matching of nondistinguished edges. Unused bottoms may be arbitrary old-affine tuples. Parents have inputs
\[ q_{a,i}=L_{a,i}(Y)+\sum_{c\in C_a}\alpha_{a,i;c}P_c, \tag{DP-matching-source} \]
with old-affine \(L_{a,i}\), constant coefficients, no raw bottom coefficients and no parent dependencies. Within each parent's set \(C_a\), assume any two distinct matchings are incompatible: their union contains a pair sharing a row or hole. Sets belonging to different parents need not be incompatible, and a bottom may be shared. Duplicate matchings can first be combined semantically in the constants.
Working corollary. Fix \(p\), polynomial total source inventory, polylogarithmic original proof degree \(D\), and a constant \(0\le\rho<1\). If
\[ t=\max\bigl(1,\max_{c\text{ used}}|S_c|\bigr) \le N^\rho\operatorname{polylog}N, \tag{DP-matching-range} \]
the source has no degree-\(D\) ordinary NS or PC refutation, for sufficiently large \(N\). Accuracies are arbitrary positive integers. There is no nonlinear parent-rank factor in this sufficient bound. A complete-board source is covered when one robust restriction and projection give the stated source format.
4. Construct the parent partitions, not a global one
Proof. A used bottom's induced common-zero indicator on \(\Omega\) is the monomial \(Z_c=\prod_{e\in S_c}Y_e\), independently of its accuracy. In one parent, the \(Z_c\)'s are mutually exclusive by the assumed conflicts. Set
\[ \pi_{a,c}=Z_c\ (c\in C_a),\qquad \pi_{a,0}=1-\sum_{c\in C_a}Z_c. \tag{DP-matching-partition} \]
These are a zero-one partition on \(\Omega\), of degree at most \(t\), over every prime. Use reference inputs \(L_{a,i}+\alpha_{a,i;c}\) on branch \(c\), and \(L_{a,i}\) on branch zero. Each bottom has the trivial partition one and its original affine tuple. Thus (DP-branches) holds for all blocks. No expensive global product of all parent partitions is taken.
The full reference inventory is at most \(M_b+\sum_a(1+|C_a|)\), hence polynomial. Apply the fixed-graph affine construction to this one family. It supplies one separated weight with \(k,T_0=N^{1/2}\operatorname{polylog}N\), normalizing all references on the entire old Boolean cube where \(f\ne0\). The theorem gives
\[ B=(T_0+t)D+k =O_p\!\left( \bigl(N^{1/2}\operatorname{polylog}N+ N^\rho\operatorname{polylog}N\bigr)D\right)=o(N). \]
The graph's linear separation ceiling applies. Projected old proofs lift to \(\Gamma_H\) through the same ceiling at least two by the row-projection telescope. This proves the corollary. The bottom maps need only realize their induced values on the common support; they need not be exactly normalized everywhere before parent interpolation. \(\square\)
Even if many distinct \(Z_c\)'s are independent modulo affine functions, the selector degree in (DP-matching-partition) stays \(t\), not \(t|C_a|\). This removes that particular loss from the rank-product ledger. Some instances remain covered by historical shared-feature conditioning or the high-bottom clamp; no separation from every older method is asserted.
5. Controls and limits of the general implication
The flat-partition directions have induced parent inputs zero, so the direct theorem uses the trivial partition and zero reference. Their recorded retained certificates remain a separate, sharper source deletion. For differences of products on disjoint groups, the bottom indicators can be simultaneously one. The incompatible-matching corollary does not apply merely because those groups are disjoint; the prior high-bottom clamp covers its stated parameter range.
Cheap affine partitions are not a general replacement for parent normalization. The existing independent-flat control already implies that a nonzero full-cube selector making all \(a\) disjoint products \(x_jy_j\) affine on its support must have degree at least \(a\), even if the affine representatives differ between leaves. Indeed choose a largest selector monomial of degree \(t<a\); one variable pair is untouched. Multiplication by that pair produces a unique term of degree \(t+2\), while multiplication by an affine representative has degree at most \(t+1\). Their difference cannot vanish on the cube. Reducing a nonzero selector to its multilinear representative does not increase degree. Hence a partition of unity cannot have every selector degree below \(a\).
This is a full-cube representation obstruction, not a PHP lower bound or a refutation of domain-based partitions. Its old-quadratic tuple is covered by the existing binary quadratic endpoint under that endpoint's hypotheses, illustrating why a universal partition demand would be unnecessarily strong. The proper-domain obstruction additionally retains its exact cube-volume and certificate-room conditions; it cannot be applied to arbitrary graph selectors without checking them.
