Top-degree axiom inertness

Statement. Over a field, in the multilinear ring or with the Boolean axioms among \(G\), let \(G\) and \(P\) be finite sets of polynomials, \(H\) the degree-\(D\) PC closure of \(G\), and \(P_D\) the members of \(P\) of degree exactly \(D\); suppose every member of \(P\) has degree at least \(D\). Then (a) the degree-\(D\) closure \(C\) of \(G\cup P\) equals \(H+\operatorname{span}P_D\), and \(C\cap V_{D-1}=H\cap V_{D-1}\), if and only if \((H+\operatorname{span}P_D)\cap V_{D-1}\subseteq H\); a checkable sufficient case is that the degree-\(D\) homogeneous parts of the members of \(P_D\) are linearly independent modulo the degree-\(D\) homogeneous parts of members of \(H\), which form a subspace since taking the top part is linear on \(H\). (b) For the product pigeonhole system over any field, with \(G\) the hole axioms, \(P\) the pigeon axioms \(\prod_j(1-x_{ij})\) of degree \(n\), and \(D\le n\), the sufficient case holds, \(H\) is the degree-\(D\) part of the vanishing ideal of the set \(Y\) of hole-respecting assignments, and \(C\cap V_{D-1}=H\cap V_{D-1}\).

Proof. (a) Put \(T=H+\operatorname{span}P_D\). It contains every axiom of degree at most \(D\) and lies in \(C\). If \(T\cap V_{D-1}\subseteq H\), \(T\) is closed: a member of degree below \(D\) lies in \(H\), and its products by variables, within degree \(D\), stay in \(H\subseteq T\); as \(C\) is the smallest closed space containing the axioms, \(C=T\). Conversely, \(T\cap V_{D-1}\subseteq C\cap V_{D-1}\), so the conclusion forces the condition. Under independence, a member \(h+\sum_p\lambda_pp\) of degree below \(D\) has \(\sum_p\lambda_p\operatorname{top}_D(p)=-\operatorname{top}_D(h)\), so \(\lambda=0\) and the member is \(h\). (b) The hole axioms are monomials, so their closure \(H\) (with Booleanity) is the span of the monomials of degree at most \(D\) with two variables in one hole, and its degree-\(D\) parts lie in that span; the top monomials \(x_{i0}\cdots x_{i,n-1}\) of the pigeon axioms are distinct and standard, so independent modulo it, and for \(D<n\) the set \(P_D\) is empty. The standard monomials \(x_S\), \(S\) meeting each hole at most once, are as many as the points of \(Y\), and their evaluation matrix on \(Y\) is triangular for inclusion with ones on the diagonal, so they are a basis of the functions on \(Y\); hence a polynomial of degree at most \(D\) vanishes on \(Y\) exactly when its standard part is zero, that is, when it lies in \(H\).

Relations. This is the step the goal review's second-stage reviewer supplied for the product pigeonhole system, in general form; the committed check of that review (php_degrees.json) gives refutation degree \(n+1\) at \(n=2,3,4\), so for those systems every proper window has \(D\le n\). cor:frame-ratio-half (near-half-pc-degree) studies frames whose closure is their truncated vanishing ideal; part (b) says the product pigeonhole system is such a frame below its pigeon axioms' degree.

Degenerate cases. \(P\) empty: \(C=H\). All members of \(P\) of degree above \(D\): \(P_D\) is empty and \(C=H\), since axioms above \(D\) are not used. A dependence among the top parts of \(P_D\) modulo those of \(H\), for example two members with equal top parts: the sufficient case fails, and the combination, of degree below \(D\), is a member of \(C\) that the condition of (a) must place in \(H\) for the conclusion to hold. A member of \(P_D\) already in \(H\) breaks independence but not the condition.

Nonvacuity. In Pang's matched ledger with \(p=2\): the product pigeonhole system over \(\mathbb F_2\) with \(5\) pigeons and \(4\) holes at \(D=4\le n=4\), where \(P_D\) is the five pigeon axioms and the closure is proper (php_degrees.json of the goal review: dimension \(4905\) at degree \(4\), refutation degree \(5\)); the witness is a closure, not a Pang error family, so it verifies only that the lemma's hypotheses are met.

What distinguishes the system is narrower: its closure \(C\) contains \(H\), the closure of the monomial axioms, which is the degree-\(D\) part of the vanishing ideal of the partial matchings \(Y''\) by the triangularity of (b); so \(C\) is still a domain model in the containment sense of lem:factor-ideal-domination (c), and the test asks whether the linear pigeon axioms add common killers beyond that frame. The computation. The committed script functional_factor_kernels.py builds the functional pigeonhole system over \(\mathbb F_2\) (pigeon axioms \(1+\sum_jx_{ij}\), hole and functionality axioms), finds its refutation degrees with tools/f2_closure_degree.cpp, takes the smallest size with a proper window at degree \(3\) (or the size given), and computes with tools/pc_closure.py the common kernel \(\operatorname{Ann}_C(F)\) on \(V_1\) of the factor maps of several factor sets \(F\), beside \(\operatorname{Ann}_H(F)\) for the closure \(H\) of the monomial axioms alone and \(C\cap V_1\). Its hypothesis, written before the run with its refuting output, was equality \(\operatorname{Ann}_C(F)=\operatorname{Ann}_H(F)+(C\cap V_1)\); the one-hole and one-pigeon checks against lem:factor-ideal-domination, and the containment of \(\operatorname{Ann}_H(F)+(C\cap V_1)\) in \(\operatorname{Ann}_C(F)\), could not fail and are code checks. The positive control, added after review, is the reviewer's counterexample: on variables \(a,b,w,z\) with monomial axioms \(ab\), \(bz\) and the linear axiom \(w+a+z\) at \(D=2\), \(b\) kills \(w\) because it kills \(a\) and \(z\) and \(w\equiv a+z\); the script finds \(\dim\operatorname{Ann}_C(w)=3\) against \(2\) for \(\operatorname{Ann}_H(w)+(C\cap V_1)\), so it detects such a transported killer.

Reading. At this size the linear pigeon axioms add no common killer at \(K=1\): constructed maps into the common kernel gave one annihilator where the factors share a cheap generator, supports of the monomial frame otherwise, and the relations. This is not generic for linear axioms: the positive control shows a linear axiom transporting killers. The reviewer's explanation for the pigeonhole case, unverified, is that \(x_{i0}\equiv1+\sum_{j>0}x_{ij}\), so a transported killer would also have to kill the constant. \(K=1\) is the weakest window, and two sizes were run; the result does not exclude a saving at larger \(K\) or another system.
