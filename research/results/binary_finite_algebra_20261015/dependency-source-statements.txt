MIT OCW 18.706 Spring2023 Lecture3, Theorem3.3/Corollary3.5:
https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/18-706-noncommutative-algebra-spring-2023/mit18_706_s23_lec03.pdf
For a simple module finite-dimensional over its endomorphism division ring,
the action algebra surjects onto the full corresponding linear endomorphism
algebra. The draft explicitly uses the opposite ring to make V a right vector
space, obtaining a quotient Mat_d(K). The primary density statement and proof
were read. Composition-series Definition3.12/Lemma3.13 were also read; the
draft supplies an elementary finite-dimensional flag argument itself.

Smolensky1987, Algebraic Methods in the Theory of Lower Bounds for Boolean
Circuit Complexity, Theorem2, printed page81:
https://people.cs.umass.edu/~immerman/cs601/Smolensky.pdf
Fixed-depth unbounded AND/OR/NOT/MOD2 circuits computing a modulus not a
power of two cannot have polynomial size. Only fixed odd primes are needed.
The paper uses nonzero-residue output; a final NOT changes convention.
Author identified the actual PDF, read the statement/displayed proof and gate
definitions, and visually checked the scanned theorem page. No quantitative
exponent or new underlying lower-bound proof is imported.
