# Primary sources and limits

- Alberto Ravagnani, Rank-metric codes and their duality theory,
  arXiv:1410.1333v3 (2 April2015), https://arxiv.org/pdf/1410.1333v3 .
  Read code/trace-dual definitions, Theorem8 statement, and the complete
  Lemmas27–30/Theorem31 counting proof. The Singleton inequality used locally
  is proved by puncturing; no external existence theorem is needed.
- Gorla, Jurrius, López, Ravagnani, Rank-Metric Codes and q-Polymatroids,
  arXiv:1803.10844v4 (5September2019), https://arxiv.org/pdf/1803.10844v4 .
  Read support/generalized-weight distinctions and Example2.5, and row/column
  definitions4.1–4.2 and full Theorem4.3 proof. Version4 corrects the earlier
  matrix typo in Example2.5. No assertion about unexamined constructions.
- Internal full proofs: entry-2026-10-08-rectangular-profile-rank,
  entry-2026-10-07-filtered-prefix-design,
  entry-2026-10-08-full-rank-moment,
  entry-2026-10-09-coefficient-kernel-counts.
