Setup and actual numerator

Use the recorded charged Fourier pseudoideal on a connected graph with \(n\) vertices, \(m\) edges, maximum degree \(\Delta\), edge expansion \(\varepsilon>0\), and charges of nonzero total in \(\mathbb F_3\). Retain \[ \max(4,2\Delta)\le D<\varepsilon n/8, \qquad r=m-n+1\ge m/3. \] Polynomial coefficients are in \(\mathbb F_4\); the binary pullback gives identical filtered ranks for the binary polynomials used below. Let \(h(j)\) be the number of ternary cut-code cosets reached through support \(j\), and \(V_j\) the old degree-at-most-\(j\) polynomial domain. There is no global evaluation model for this charged quotient.

For \(M\) binary tuples \(g_a\) of degree at most \(\delta\), with weight one, put \[ m_a(L)=\operatorname{rank}\bigl(f\in V_L\mapsto([g_{a,i}f])_i\bigr), \quad L=K+t\delta, \quad \mathcal B_t=2^{-t}\frac{\sum_{a=1}^M m_a(L)}{h(K)}. \] The existing theorem bounds summed expected actual prefix error rank by \(2^{-t}\sum_a m_a(L)\), when \(L+2\delta\le D\), the inputs have their certified binary identities, and all original source charges are separately paid. It is sufficient that \(\mathcal B_t<1\), not necessary.

Proposition (actual tuple budget gap). Assume \(\delta\ge12\), \(t\ge1\), \(K\ge0\), \(L\le m/16\), \(L+2\delta\le D\). If each tuple contains a polynomial that survives in the quotient with at most \(s\) nonzero character coordinates, then \[ \mathcal B_t\ge\frac{M}{s}\left(\frac{(17/16)^\delta}{2}\right)^t. \] In particular \(M\ge s\) implies \(\mathcal B_t>1\). This concerns the true enlarged tuple ranks at the specified weight, not their ambient replacement.

Proof. Project the tuple map onto the indicated coordinate. Its rank is at least \(h(L)/s\) by sparse-weight survival, using its degree at most \(\delta\) and \(L+\delta\le D\). Thus every \(m_a(L)\ge h(L)/s\). The actual code-ball inequality gives \(h(L)/h(K)\ge(17/16)^{t\delta}\). Sum, multiply by \(2^{-t}\), and use \((17/16)^{12}>2\). All ranks are in the same charged quotient, and binary descent preserves them. \(\square\)

A complete two-level realization

The following specialization is also part of the proposition. Choose integers \(h\ge\delta\ge12\) and \(M\) such that \[ 3^\delta\le M\le\binom{2h}{h},\quad (2h+1)\delta\le r, \quad K+h\delta\le m/16, \quad K+\delta+h(2h+1)+2h\le D. \] These margins imply the direct-shortening window for every \(1\le t\le h\). Choose a spanning tree and disjoint \(\delta\)-edge sets \(S_0,\ldots,S_{2h}\) in its complement. Fix arbitrary edge labels and write \[ z_j=\prod_{e\in S_j}x_{e,\ell_e}. \] A cut supported in the tree complement is zero: its vertex potential is constant on the spanning tree. Consequently each \(z_j\) has exactly \(3^\delta\) nonzero, distinct character classes and survives in the proper quotient. Distinct \(z_j\)'s are linearly independent and their span excludes one: a nonzero character supported on one edge of \(S_j\) occurs only in \(z_j\), with nonzero coefficient. This uses the truncated cut-code dictionary inside the paid degrees, not assignments satisfying the inconsistent system.

Introduce one complete accuracy-\(h\) bottom block on \((1-x_{e,\ell_e})_{e\in S_j}\) for every \(j\). Select each of its \(\delta\) inputs in a different row and zero-pad the remaining rows. The actual bottom product becomes \(z_j\). Every bottom companion is a Boolean consequence through \(\delta+1\), since its factor \((1-x_{e,\ell_e})x_{e,\ell_e}\) vanishes; each coefficient domain vanishes at its assigned constant. The original bottom product and companion ceilings remain \(2h\) and \(2h+1\).

Choose \(M\) distinct \(h\)-subsets \(I_a\subseteq\{1,\ldots,2h\}\). Parent \(a\) is a complete accuracy-\(h\) block with inputs \(P_0\) and \(P_j\), \(j\in I_a\), where the \(P_j\)'s are the actual original bottom products. Thus the specialized input tuple is \((z_0,(z_j)_{j\in I_a})\). These parents have distinct input spans, width and rank \(h+1\), and no constant in their span. Their monomial inputs have no common nonconstant monomial factor, since their supports are disjoint. This excludes only that simple factor normalization, not every possible weighted or semantic factorization.

Every parent input originally has degree ceiling \(2h\). Its original product charge is \(h(2h+1)\), and its companion charge is \(h(2h+1)+2h\). The shorter specialized degree \(\delta\) is used to analyze the actual map; it does not replace those source charges. The displayed \(D\) pays the original companion, an additional weight degree \(\delta\), and multiplier cutoff \(K\). All rows below are legal binary constants on that single shared source.

For every prefix length \(1\le t\le h\), apply the first part with \(s=3^\delta\). Each true enlarged tuple image contains the image of multiplication by \(z_0\), so \(\mathcal B_t>1\). The same conclusion holds if each parent is assigned its own allowed prefix length: its contribution is greater than \(1/3^\delta\), so the sum over \(M\ge3^\delta\) cannot be below one. Zero padding to the full accuracy does not alter the carried source degrees.

Yet select \(P_0\) in the first row of every parent and set all its other coefficients to zero. Each parent product becomes \(1+z_0\). With the single old weight \(w=z_0\), every weighted parent companion is \[ z_0(1+z_0)z_j=0\pmod{J_D}, \] including \(j=0\). This is the local Boolean identity \(z_0^2=z_0\), with certificates through at most \(3\delta\); multiplication by degree-\(K\) test polynomials fits the stronger original window above. Weighted bottom companions and coefficient domains vanish as well. The weight survives and satisfies \[ h_w(K)\ge h(K)/3^\delta. \] Thus a degree-\(\delta\) weight and coherent constant rows remove the complete extension family, despite failure of the unweighted actual-enlarged-image certificate for all the permitted prefix lengths. The rank fraction is polynomially small in the asymptotic specialization below; it is not asserted to be constant or adequate for an unrestricted recurrence.

The shared-input choice is an already available weighted clamp mechanism. The construction does not say the general source has a shared surviving input, or that this family is forced in any hypothetical short Frege proof. It also does not turn nonzero individual errors into a rank lower bound for their optimized stacked map. Its role is a separation of a sufficient numerical certificate from successful elimination in the actual hard quotient.

Parameter scope and implication for the route

The finite conditions are nonvacuous asymptotically on any fixed bounded-degree expander family satisfying the stated cycle-space ratio. For every fixed \(A>0\) and \(0<\eta<1/2\), take \(M=\lceil m^A\rceil\), \(\delta=\lfloor\log_3 M\rfloor\), \(h=\lfloor m^\eta\rfloor\). Then \(\delta\ge12\), \(h\ge\delta\), \((2h+1)\delta=o(m)\) and \(h(2h+1)+2h+\delta=o(m)\) eventually. Also \(\binom{2h}{h}\ge2^h\ge M\) eventually; choosing one element from each of \(h\) disjoint pairs proves the first inequality. Choose \(D=\Theta(m)\) strictly inside the inherited old window and \(K=\Theta(m)\) with \(K<\min(D/2,m/32)\). These choices satisfy all remaining margins for large \(m\). This is conditional on such a fixed graph family, not a new expander construction. The graph family need not change with \(A\).
