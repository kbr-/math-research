1. **Audited binary compiler.** In the fixed coherent flat-clause presentation, an interpreted
proof DAG with structural value bound L yields a PC refutation of the old CNF axioms plus the
complete retained ENS source through cL, c=max(M_fr,10), with no proof-height term. Literal CNF
width w is covered by accuracy h>=max(1,w). Structural depth d gives L<=(d+1)h^d. One can therefore
choose constant accuracy for a constant-width old CNF and obtain a constant source-proof degree.
Larger accuracies may instead be chosen and must pay their changed original costs.

The constructor has O(S) blocks for total formula-node occurrence count S in this presentation,
possibly O(S^2) flattened input slots and O(hS^2) fresh coefficients. Its dependency levels are
bounded by formula depth. After literal boundary substitution, inputs are affine in old variables,
O(n) fixed-degree clause predicates, and genuine earlier whole products. No raw earlier
coefficients occur outside those products in inputs; proof polynomials can use every coefficient.
Each complete block retains all g_i P companions, coefficient Boolean equations, and
P=product_(u<=h)(1-sum_i r_(u,i)g_i). Original product/companion degrees remain charged.

2. **An actual limited exponent bound.** For a binary n-variable old family with PC barrier
floor(gamma*n), a complete source of ell levels and at most O(n^(1+rho)) nonterminal blocks per
level excludes original degree B<=n^beta log^c(n) when
rho*(2^(ell-1)-1)+beta<1; terminal inventory may be any fixed polynomial.
The simultaneous map's actual product degrees obey
delta_j=O(n^[rho*(2^(j-1)-1)] log^[2^j-1](n)).

0<rho<1/(2^(ell-1)-1). Encoding is Theta(n) symbols or O(n log n) bits.
