The restriction map on the actual kernel

Let \(G\) be a simple connected graph on \(n\ge3\) vertices, maximum degree \(g\), expansion at least \(\varepsilon>0\), and charge vector \(c\in\mathbb F_3^V\) of nonzero total charge. Use the binary one-hot pullback \(J_D(G,c)\) of the exact \(\mathbb F_4\) character kernel, with \[ \max(4,2g)\le D<\varepsilon n/8. \] Let \(F\subseteq E(G)\) be any set for which \(G'=G\setminus F\) has a supplied expansion lower bound \(\varepsilon'>0\). Pin its labels to \(\eta\in\mathbb F_3^F\), by the degree-nonincreasing substitution \(\rho_\eta\), and put \(g'=\max\deg G'\) and \(c'=c-\operatorname{div}_F\eta\). Its total charge is unchanged. One sufficient case, used below, is maximum incident degree of \(F\) at most \(t<\varepsilon\), giving \(\varepsilon'=\varepsilon-t\); the lemma does not require that sufficient condition. Choose \[ \max(4,2g')\le D'\le D,\qquad D'<\varepsilon'n/8. \tag{1} \] Write \(J'_{D'}=J_{D'}(G',c')\). No pin indicator is multiplied into the old weight.

Lemma (weighted restriction transport). For every pin assignment, \[ \rho_\eta\!\left(J_D(G,c)\cap\mathbb F_2[x]_{\le D'}\right) \subseteq J'_{D'}. \tag{2} \] Let \(w\notin J_D\) have original degree at most \(b\le D'\), and local normal monomial incidence at most \(a\), in the sense of incidence cleanup. If \(2a<\varepsilon'\), some \(\eta\) has \[ w_\eta=\rho_\eta(w)\notin J'_{D'},\qquad \deg w_\eta\le b,\qquad \iota(\overline{w_\eta})\le a. \tag{3} \] Every original membership \(p_j=wR_j\in J_D\) with actual original degree at most \(C_j\le D'\) then gives \(w_\eta\rho_\eta(R_j)\in J'_{D'}\). A supplied certificate ceiling \(C_j\) is a sufficient bound on that actual degree, but no smaller NS certificate is inferred. If \(D'=D\), all original kernel memberships transport; nonzeroness still needs (3).

Proof of (2). Affine Fourier substitution and local reduction preserve ordinary degree. The degree-at-most-\(D'\) Fourier normal form of a kernel polynomial is a linear combination of phase-adjusted differences of characters in its own support: in each coordinate class choose an occurring reference term and cancel the rest. Every character used has degree at most \(D'\). For one such relation, \[ \omega^A m_\alpha\sim\omega^B m_\beta,\qquad \alpha-\beta=\partial_Gu,\quad |\operatorname{supp}u|<n/2,\quad c\cdot u=B-A. \] After pinning, its two terms are \(\omega^{A+\eta\cdot\alpha_F}m_{\alpha_{E\setminus F}}\) and \(\omega^{B+\eta\cdot\beta_F}m_{\beta_{E\setminus F}}\). Their exponent difference is \(\partial_{G'}u\), with the same small vertex witness, and \[ (B+\eta\cdot\beta_F)-(A+\eta\cdot\alpha_F) =c\cdot u-\eta\cdot\partial_Fu=c'\cdot u. \tag{4} \] Thus this is a defining relation in the residual proper window (1). This includes a pair whose remaining exponents coincide; properness forbids a nonzero scalar cycle. Local reduction commutes with pinning, and pinned local-domain relations specialize to zero while other local relations remain. Sum the transported differences and pull back to the original one-hot encoding. All terms stay through \(D'\); binary coefficients remain binary. This proves (2) without a global charged function algebra.

Proof of (3). The local normal polynomial \(\bar w\) is nonzero, since local reduction certificates fit its degree and \(w\notin J_D\). It is a nonzero function on the product of local three-state edge domains. Choose a local state where it is one and restrict that state to \(F\); the resulting local branch is nonzero. This uses local states only, not nonexistent global satisfying charged states. Restriction removes edge factors and can cancel terms, but cannot increase their degree or incidence. Distinct residual Fourier frequencies of that branch have union support of incidence at most \(2a<\varepsilon'\). The cut inequality \[ |H\cap C|\le \frac{\max\deg H}{\varepsilon'}|C| \] excludes a nonzero residual cut difference. Hence its nonzero Fourier coefficients occupy distinct residual charged coordinates through degree \(b\le D'\). The branch survives. Applying (2) to each actual \(p_j\) proves the membership conclusion. This proves the lemma.

Distinction from conditioning. The earlier conditional-slice theorem constructs a colon space with a pin indicator and pays its degree.
