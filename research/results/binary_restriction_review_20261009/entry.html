Architecture review

Necessary target. For fixed depth \(d\), choose one family of original encoding lengths \(\mathcal L\) and exclude symbol-size \(\mathcal L^A\) proofs for every fixed \(A\). On the growing-degree candidate, choose \(0<\beta<\alpha<1\), \(\alpha(d-1)+\beta<1\), \(\Delta=\lceil n^\alpha\rceil\), \(p=n^{-\beta}\). Then \(\log\mathcal L=\Theta(n^\alpha)\), the original audited source ceiling is \(B=O_{A,d}(n^{\alpha d})\), and a hard restriction has \(D'=\Theta(n^{1+\alpha-\beta})\). One coherent source interpretation and surviving weight, with all actual proof errors and original replay fitting \(D'\), suffice. A different direct proof contradiction remains allowed.

Extra requirements. Complete companion cleanup, a small rank for each error separately, a polynomial representation of every narrow test, and an exact common fixed-weight scalar prefix are stronger than the necessary endpoint. Narrow CNFs need not have low degree: a conjunction of \(r\) positive unit clauses has unique Boolean polynomial \(\prod_i x_i\), of degree \(r\). Do not impose an impossible width-only degree bound as a prerequisite. Nor may an arbitrary proof formula be renamed a width-\(\kappa\) categorical CNF without its width/size and MOD2 costs. What is needed is a source-compatible aggregate identity, not separate favorable choices for its pieces.

The blind interpolation test

The projection claim for a connected graph side is elementary: after boundary labels are fixed, the remaining incidence equations are solvable exactly when their residual charges sum to zero. A spanning tree solves them from leaves inward. Hence a separator for the two incompatible side conditions would indeed compute the appropriate boundary MOD3 predicate. The unproved part is bounded-depth extraction from the proof DAG, not these projections.

A generic depth-preserving extractor fails on a familiar counter-certificate construction. For shared bits \(x_1,\ldots,x_r\), let two private one-hot three-state sequences \(a_i,b_i\) start at state zero and update by adding \(x_i\) modulo three. Encode each update by one-hot clauses and the implications \(a_{i,s}\land(x_i=0)\Rightarrow a_{i+1,s}\), \(a_{i,s}\land(x_i=1)\Rightarrow a_{i+1,s+1}\), and likewise for \(b\). These are constant-width clauses. Require \(a_r=0\) on the first side and \(b_r\ne0\) on the second. Their projections onto \(x\) are complementary MOD3 answers.

The combined CNF has a polynomial-size Boolean Frege refutation at some absolute depth \(d_0\): derive \(a_i=b_i\) successively. Each induction step is one fixed Boolean tautology on the two old states, two new states and the common input bit, using the local transitions; it has a fixed finite proof by completeness of a chosen fixed Boolean Frege basis, in the Cook–Reckhow definition of a Frege system. This completeness dependency, not the truth-table check alone, supplies the fixed proof. Initial equality is given, and terminal equality contradicts the two final conditions. Formula depth stays constant although the DAG has \(r\) induction stages. If assumptions are discharged into the full CNF antecedent, symbol size is still polynomial and formula depth changes only by a constant. Thus a generic polynomial-size, bounded-depth AC⁰[2] separator extraction would contradict Smolensky's Theorem 2 for MOD3. This does not refute a special Tseitin extraction theorem.

The local encoding was checked on all 8192 Boolean assignments to the 13 bits of one induction step: six satisfy its premise, none violate its conclusion; dropping the equality premise gives 12 failures. The saved GAP control checks the constant-size schema, while the induction above supplies the all-length argument. No claim about an already extracted small Tseitin interpolant is made.

  • Integer-valued approximation: Mahler interpolation. The continuous \(2\)-adic interpolation theorem suggests grouping reciprocal counts before reducing coefficients. Answers. A low-degree integral representation of the aggregate path identity could remove illegitimate division by even counts. Test. Falsified the naive uniform continuous-count shortcut. The function \(f(0)=0,f(c)=1\) for positive integers is not continuous at zero, since \(2^j\to0\). Its finite Newton coefficients are \((-1)^{j+1}\), not a \(2\)-adic null sequence. For \(c=\sum_{i=1}^r v_i\) on Boolean violation bits, the exact finite formula becomes \(1-\prod_i(1-v_i)\), with degree \(r\) over \(\mathbb F_2\). Lifting parity modulo four is a different, continuous task; it does not automatically implement nonzero-count detection. This rejects that direct use, not source-specific cancellations. Primary reprint statement.

Absurd bridges

  • Quantum measurement: Gao's sequential union bound, Theorem 1. Regard successive error filters as projective measurements with small disturbance. Answers. A field-compatible counterpart could control joint errors without independent choices. Test. Falsified direct conversion of small failure probability into small binary error rank, even with odd rational denominators. For odd \(m\), let \(v=(m,1,1)\), \(P=vv^\mathsf T/(m^2+2)\), \(P_0=\operatorname{diag}(1,0,0)\). Then \(1-\operatorname{tr}(PP_0)=2/(m^2+2)\to0\), but modulo two \(P\) is the all-one matrix and \((I-P)P_0\) has rank one on the one-dimensional \(P_0\)-domain. Direct sums of an odd number \(r\) of these blocks, with density matrix \(\rho=(\bigoplus P_0)/r\), have the same arbitrarily small real failure probability and full error rank \(r\) on that domain; every denominator remains odd. This is an exact matrix control, not a source counterexample or a refutation of Gao's theorem. Primary source.
  • Statistical mechanics: Kotecký–Preiss polymer convergence. Treat path corrections as weakly interacting polymers and use convergence to suppress the long tail. Answers. A degree- and field-compatible tail bound could control the whole inventory together. Test. Falsified ungraded real convergence as a binary truncation argument. With \(r\) polymers compatible except with themselves and activity \(1/q\), odd \(q\ge3\), the criterion holds with exponent parameter one since \(e/q<1\). The partition function is \((1+1/q)^r\), arbitrarily close to one when \(q\gg r\), yet is zero modulo two. All denominators are units at two; real smallness does not make the terms disappear. This leaves the already recorded graded/matrix-polymer questions open and supplies no missing paid grading. Primary research source, Section 2 and (2.12)–(2.15).
