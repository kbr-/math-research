A finite same-quotient statement

Use the proper binary charged one-hot graph quotient, with \(n\) vertices, \(m\) edges, expansion \(\varepsilon\), maximum degree \(\Delta_{\max}\), and \[ \max(4,2\Delta_{\max})\le D<\varepsilon n/8. \] Use two independent Boolean indicators per edge; the third is their affine complement. Thus all affine ranks below are measured after this degree-preserving local normalization. Let \(1\le K\le m/3\), \(\beta=2\sqrt{K/m}<1\), and \[ \eta=(1+2\beta^\varepsilon)^n-1<1. \] Let binary \(w\notin J_D\) have degree at most \(b\) and \(s\) nonzero coordinates in the character quotient after extension to \(\mathbb F_4\). Fix \(M\ge1\) binary affine flats, each defined by a proper affine space of rank \(r_j\), \(r_0\le r_j\le d\), with \(1\le r_0\le m\). Its exact indicator is \(F_j=\prod_{i=1}^{r_j}(1-\ell_{j,i})\), for an affine basis. Assume \(b+K+d\le D\).

Corollary (growing expansion removal). If \[ \frac{2Ms}{1-\eta}\exp\!\left(-\frac{r_0K}{2m}\right)<1, \tag{1} \] there is binary \(f\in V_K\) with \(wf\notin J_D\) and \(wfF_j\in J_D\) for all \(j\). Each unweighted indicator map on the actual degree-\(K\) quotient has relative rank at most \[ \frac{2}{1-\eta}\exp\!\left(-\frac{\min(r_j,m)K}{2m}\right). \tag{2} \] Overlaps and noncoordinate affine equations are allowed. Unit affine spans give zero indicators by the local Boolean certificate and can be discarded separately.

Proof. Each \(F_j\ell\) for \(\ell\) in its defining span vanishes by the Boolean identities through \(r_j+1\). The flat-image theorem gives rank at most \(2e^{-\min(r_j,m)K/(2m)}B_m(K)\), where \(B_m(K)=\sum_{i\le K}2^i\binom mi\). All products fit \(K+r_j\le D\). The occupancy bound gives \(h(K)\ge(1-\eta)B_m(K)\), proving (2). Multiplication by \(w\) cannot increase those ranks at \(b+K+r_j\le D\): a zero unweighted image can be multiplied within the same window. Meanwhile sparse-weight survival gives \(h_w(K)\ge h(K)/s\). The sum of weighted ranks is strictly below that same surviving domain by (1), so their common kernel contains a nonzero class \([wf]\). Binary charged descent preserves all these ranks and kernels; since the maps and domain are binary, a binary representative exists. No satisfying assignment of the inconsistent graph is used. \(\square\)

A family where the denominator loss disappears

Take \(\Delta=\lceil(\log n)^2\rceil\), with natural logarithm. For all sufficiently large \(n\), the already proved graph-existence argument supplies a simple graph with maximum degree at most \(2\Delta\), minimum degree at least \(\Delta/4\), and expansion at least \(\Delta/4\). It applies because \(\Delta\ge64\log(2n)\) eventually; its proof does not require a power-law choice of \(\Delta\). Fix one such graph for each \(n\), independently of any proof-size exponent. Then \[ n\Delta/8\le m\le n\Delta,\quad D=\lfloor n\Delta/64\rfloor,\quad K=\lfloor m/1024\rfloor. \tag{3} \] The old window holds eventually: \(D\ge4\Delta\) and \(D<\Delta n/32\le\varepsilon n/8\). Also \(K\le m/1024\), and \(K\ge m/2048\) once \(m\ge2048\). Hence \(\beta\le1/16\), and \[ \eta\le \exp(2n2^{-\Delta})-1\longrightarrow0. \] In particular \(\eta\le1/2\) eventually, uniformly over the later flat family. Set \[ r_0=\left\lceil4096\log(8Ms)\right\rceil. \tag{4} \] Whenever \(r_0\le m\) and the original degree window holds, (1)'s left side is at most \(4Ms e^{-r_0/4096}\le1/2\). This leaves a common kernel of dimension at least half of \([wV_K]\); it does not claim that any selected weight retains that fraction at later cutoffs.

The canonical forbidden-tuple CNF has encoding length \(N_n\) with \(\log N_n=\Theta(\Delta+\log n)=\Theta((\log n)^2)\), by the earlier upper/lower clause count. Thus \(M,s\le N_n^A\), for any fixed \(A\), make (4) \(O_A(\Delta)\), while the graph is unchanged. Inventory is not polynomial in \(n\); no theorem requiring that hypothesis is invoked. For each fixed structural depth \(d_\pi\), the original binary compiler ceiling \(O_{A,d_\pi}((\log N_n)^{d_\pi})\) is \(o(D)\), with accuracy at least the old CNF width. No residual-length or succinct-encoding substitution is made.

A complete affine-layer application, not a nonlinear endpoint

Corollary (growing expansion split). On (3), let at most \(M\le N_n^A\) complete old-affine ENS bottom blocks have common original accuracy \(h\), where \[ h\ge\lceil4096\log(8M)\rceil,\qquad h\le m,\qquad K+h+1\le D. \tag{5} \] There are constant binary coefficient assignments and \(W\notin J_D\), \(\deg W\le K\), killing every bottom companion and field image. Every block whose proper input span has rank at least \(h\) has \(WP_c\in J_D\); every proper lower-rank block has an exact affine-flat product of degree equal to its input rank. This complete one-level source has no PC or ordinary NS refutation through any original \(B\) with \(K+B\le D\).

The complete block is \(E_{c,i}=g_{c,i}P_c\), with all fresh coefficient fields; these are the generators replayed below.

Proof. For a proper input span of rank at least \(h\), choose \(h\) independent affine forms from it, expressed by constant combinations of the actual indexed inputs. Use those as the \(h\) legal rows. Its product \(P_c\) is an exact rank-\(h\) affine-flat indicator, regardless of the larger input span. For a proper rank below \(h\), select its full basis as rows and zero-pad. Every companion then vanishes by Booleanity through degree at most \(h+1\). A zero span needs no rows; all its companions are zero. A unit span uses a row equal to one, making its product zero.

Apply the preceding corollary to the high-rank products with initial weight one, using \(s=1\), \(d=h\), and the upper bound \(M\) on their number. If there are none, choose \(W=1\). Conditions (3)–(5) give a surviving binary \(W\) annihilating all these products. Multiplication by each affine companion input is paid through \(K+h+1\). The low-rank and unit-block certificates fit that ceiling as well, and every coefficient-field image is zero. All assignments are coherent across repeated uses. A source refutation of original degree \(B\), after this constant substitution and multiplication by \(W\), would put \(W\) into \(J_D\); its entire replay fits \(K+B\le D\). Ordinary NS is included by its PC replay. \(\square\)

For \(h=O_A(\Delta)\) satisfying (5), the original affine companion ceiling \(2h+1\) and each fixed-depth compiler ceiling are polylogarithmic in \(n\), well below \(D-K\). These are nonvacuous one-level exclusions. Constant substitution does not reset original joint degrees, accuracies or the degrees of later source lines.

For an actual parent affine in old terms and these bottom products, the high-rank products disappear only after weighting; the low-rank products remain polynomials of degree below \(h\). Their arbitrary mixtures need not have low-degree references of bounded degree, a small common-core cover, or a favorable intrinsic error-growth ratio. Also the new \(W\) may have large character support, so the sparse-weight version cannot simply be iterated with the old value of \(s\). These are the still missing implications.
