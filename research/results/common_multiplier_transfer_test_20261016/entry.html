General statement. conj:functional-kernel-transfer: for the functional pigeonhole system over \(\mathbb F_2\) with \(n+1\) pigeons and \(n\) holes, at every proper degree \(D\) and \(K\le D-1\), the common kernel in \(V_K\) of a set of variables modulo the closure \(C\) is the common kernel modulo the closure \(H\) of the monomial axioms plus \(C\cap V_K\). Evidence: \(K=1\) at \(6\times5\) and \(7\times6\) (check:functional-factor-kernels) and \(K=2\) at \(6\times5\) and \(7\times6\), all at \(D=3\) (check:functional-kernels-k2).
