General statement. cor:multilinear-repair-scalar: over any field, a repair of a product of any set of proof lines by an element of a module over the tensor power of the ring, whose survival is certified by a map into the ring compatible along multiplication, gives a scalar common repair of all the lines' errors at parameter \(K+\sigma\), on the window \(\deg(a_jm)\le D-K\), against the degree-\(D'\) closure; conversely every scalar common repair is one, with \(\sigma=0\). For the least value of \(K+\sigma\), with \(D'=D+\sigma\), multiplication-certified multilinear repairs and scalar common repairs are equally strong.

Multilinear repairs are scalar

Setting. As in lem:module-repair-factorization: a field \(k\), \(R=k[x_1,\dots,x_n]\), axioms \(a_1,\dots,a_r\), \(V_d\) the polynomials of degree at most \(d\). Let \(\Lambda\) be any set of proof lines, finite or infinite, \(R'=R^{\otimes\Lambda}=k[x^{(t)}_i:t\in\Lambda,\ i\le n]\), \(x^{(t)}\) a copy of the variables for line \(t\), and \(\mu:R'\to R\) the multiplication map, \(x^{(t)}_i\mapsto x_i\), a \(k\)-algebra map of degree bound \(1\). Line \(t\) carries errors \(E_{t,1},\dots\in R\) and a protected class \(w_t\), with \(w_t=1\) for all but finitely many \(t\); write \(E_{t,i}^{(t)}\) for the error in the copy of line \(t\), \(w'=\prod_tw_t^{(t)}\) of degree \(b=\sum_t\deg w_t\), and give \(R'\) the axioms \(a_j^{(t_0)}\) of one fixed copy \(t_0\) (more copies' axioms may be added; they are not used). A multilinear repair is a module repair, in lem:module-repair-factorization's sense, in a filtered \(R'\)-module \(N\) with a degree-\(D\) pseudo-submodule \(J_N\): \(\nu\in F_KN\), \(b+K\le D\), \(\deg(w'E_{t,i}^{(t)})+K\le D\), soundness \(a_j^{(t_0)}m\nu\in J_N\) for \(\deg(a_j^{(t_0)}m)\le D-K\), killing \(w'E_{t,i}^{(t)}\nu\in J_N\), and survival \(w'\nu\notin J_N\). Its survival is certified along multiplication when a linear \(\beta:F_DN\to V_{D'}\) has \(\beta(F_jN)\subseteq V_{j+\sigma}\), \(D+\sigma\le D'\), \(\beta(J_N)\subseteq J\), \(\beta(x^{(t)}_iu)-x_i\beta(u)\in J\) for \(u\in F_{D-1}N\) and every \(t\), and \(\beta(w'\nu)\notin J\), where \(J\) is the old degree-\(D'\) closure.

Corollary (multilinear repairs are scalar). If \(\nu\) is a multilinear repair whose survival is certified along multiplication, then \(f=\beta(\nu)\in V_{K+\sigma}\) is a scalar common repair of all the lines' errors at parameter \(K+\sigma\), on the window \(\deg(a_jm)\le D-K\), against \(J\), with protected class \(\prod_tw_t\): \(a_jmf\in J\) for \(\deg(a_jm)\le D-K\), \(\bigl(\prod_tw_t\bigr)E_{t,i}f\in J\) for every \(t\), \(i\), and \(\bigl(\prod_tw_t\bigr)f\notin J\). Conversely, a scalar common repair \(f\) of all the errors with that protected class, at parameter \(K\) on that window, is the multilinear repair \(\nu=f^{(t_0)}\in N=R'\), certified along multiplication by \(\beta=\mu\) restricted to degree \(D\), with \(J_N=\mu^{-1}(J)\cap V'_D\) and \(\sigma=0\).

Proof. The first part is lem:module-repair-factorization (b) with the ring \(R'\), a polynomial ring over \(k\) (in infinitely many variables when \(\Lambda\) is infinite; (b)'s proof is an induction over monomials, each of which involves finitely many copies, so it applies unchanged), the algebra \(S=R\) with its degree filtration, \(\phi=\mu\), \(T=1\) and \(J'=J\): \(J\) is closed under multiplication by each \(\mu(x^{(t)}_i)=x_i\) within degree \(D'\), and the hypotheses of (b) are those listed. Its conclusion is \(\mu(a_j^{(t_0)}m)f\in J\), \(\mu(w'E_{t,i}^{(t)})f\in J\) and \(\mu(w')f\notin J\), and \(\mu(a_j^{(t_0)}m)=a_j\,\mu(m)\), \(\mu(w')=\prod_tw_t\), \(\mu(E_{t,i}^{(t)})=E_{t,i}\); as \(m\) ranges over the monomials of \(R'\) with \(\deg(a_j^{(t_0)}m)\le D-K\), \(\mu(m)\) ranges over the monomials of \(R\) with \(\deg(a_j\mu(m))\le D-K\), since \(m''\mapsto m''^{(t_0)}\) is a degree-preserving section of \(\mu\) on monomials. Conversely, for \(N=R'\) with the degree filtration and \(J_N=\mu^{-1}(J)\cap V'_D\), a pseudo-submodule because \(\mu\) is a ring map preserving degree and \(J\) is closed under variables within degree \(D'\ge D\), \(\nu=f^{(t_0)}\) has \(\mu(g\nu)=\mu(g)f\), so soundness, killing and survival of \(\nu\) are those of \(f\), the degrees agreeing because \(\deg(w'E_{t,i}^{(t)})=\deg\bigl(\prod_tw_t\cdot E_{t,i}\bigr)\) in these domains; \(\beta=\mu\) is compatible exactly. \(\square\)

Structural reason. Certification along \(\mu\) forgets which copy an error or an axiom sits in: every copy's variables act on the certificate as the old variables do. A module over \(R^{\otimes\Lambda}\) read through \(\mu\) is therefore an \(R\)-module, and lem:module-repair-factorization makes its repairs scalar. The reason applies to every certificate compatible along one ring map to \(R\), whatever the number of copies or the size of the module, and not to certificates that keep the copies apart. For the same reason an infinite set of lines adds nothing over one line carrying all the errors \(E_{t,i}\) with protected class \(\prod_tw_t\): \(\mu\) sends every error into \(R\), and the conclusion depends only on that set of errors and that product.

Hypotheses, each with its step.

  • Survival certified along multiplication: the whole of the first part, through (b).
  • The axioms of one copy: soundness for the old system, since \(\mu\) maps the multiples of one copy's axioms onto all the old multiples; further copies' axioms are not used.
  • The degree bound \(D+\sigma\le D'\): (b)'s window. The pseudo-submodule property of \(J_N\) is used only in the converse; (b)'s proof uses the closure of \(J\), not of \(J_N\).
  • All but finitely many \(w_t=1\): \(w'\) is a polynomial. The field, the set of lines and the axioms: any.

Degenerate cases. One line: \(\mu\) is the identity and the corollary is (b) with \(S=R\). A line with no errors contributes only its protected class. All \(w_t=1\): the protected class is \(1\). \(\nu=0\) or \(J_N=F_DN\): no survival.

Nonvacuity. The converse is the part the record does not state; the forward part is (b) itself. Over \(\mathbb F_2\), \(n=2\), the Boolean axioms \(x_i^2+x_i\), two lines with errors \(E_1=x_1\) and \(E_2=x_2\), \(w_1=w_2=1\), \(D=D'=4\), \(K=2\), \(\sigma=0\), \(N=R'\), \(J_N=\mu^{-1}(J)\cap V'_4\), \(J\) the degree-\(4\) part of the Boolean ideal. The scalar repair \(f=(1+x_1)(1+x_2)\) gives \(\nu=f^{(1)}\); so does the element \(\nu'=(1+x^{(1)}_1)(1+x^{(2)}_2)\), which mixes the copies and has \(\mu(\nu')=f\). Soundness is exercised: the axiom multiples of degree at most \(D-K=2\) are the axioms themselves, and \(\mu\bigl((x^{(1)2}_j+x^{(1)}_j)\nu\bigr)=(x_j^2+x_j)(1+x_1)(1+x_2)\in J\); killing gives \(x_1f=x_2f=0\) on the cube, and survival \(f(0)=1\). Plainest bound. On the cube a common scalar repair must vanish where \(x_1=1\) or \(x_2=1\) and not everywhere, so its degree is at least the transversal number \(2\) of the supports (lem:transversal-killer-basis (c), with \(2+1\le D\)), which \(f\) meets; that bound already decides the instance's question.

Baseline. Without an idea, repairing each line separately by \(\nu_t\) and multiplying gives \(\prod_t\nu_t\), whose survival can fail: over \(\mathbb F_2\), \(n=1\), the Boolean axiom, \(w=1\), line \(1\) with \(E=x\) and line \(2\) with \(E=1+x\) have separate repairs \(1+x\) and \(x\), whose product lies in \(J\), and no common repair exists, since it would vanish on the whole cube (historical:ex:mixedfailure is the analogue for design corrections). Every scalar common repair is a multilinear one; the corollary adds the converse.

Smallest case. The next attempt is the combined-error reading. If line \(t\) holds \(w_t+E_t\), the product line has protected class \(w=\prod_tw_t\) and error \(E_c=\prod_t(w_t+E_t)-\prod_tw_t\), and a repair of the product kills \(wE_c\). Every term of \(E_c\) contains some \(E_t\), so when the degrees fit a common repair of the factors' errors also repairs the product: the combined repair's least degree is at most the common one's, and the question is whether it can be smaller.

  • Tried here by hand in a domain model, \(J\) the vanishing ideal of a finite point set \(X\) within degree, with room. A repair of the product must vanish on \(\{p:w(p)\ne0,\ \prod_t(w_t+E_t)(p)\ne\prod_tw_t(p)\}\), a subset of the common repair's set \(\{p:w(p)\ne0,\ E_t(p)\ne0\text{ for some }t\}\). Over \(\mathbb F_2\) the two sets are equal: where \(w(p)=1\) each \(w_t(p)=1\), and \(\prod_t(1+E_t(p))=1\) only when every \(E_t(p)=0\). So over \(\mathbb F_2\), in domain models, the combined-error reading saves nothing.
  • Over \(\mathbb F_3\) the sets differ: \(n=1\), the Boolean axiom \(x^2-x\), \(w_1=w_2=1\), \(E_1=E_2=x\), \(D=2\). Then \(E_c=(1+x)^2-1=2x+x^2\equiv3x=0\) modulo \(x^2-x\), so \(E_c\in J\) and \(f=1\) repairs the product line (survival: \(w f=1\notin J\)). A common repair of \(E_1=x\) must vanish at \(x=1\) and not at \(x=0\), so its degree is \(1\), attained by \(1-x\). The saving comes from the cancellation \((1+E_1)(1+E_2)=1\) at \(x=1\), which needs characteristic other than \(2\); the instance is a case of the reviewer's suggestion below (\(E\) takes the value \(-2\) at \(x=1\)), checked here.
