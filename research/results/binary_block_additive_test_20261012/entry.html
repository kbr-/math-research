Majority row rank floor

Let \(Q\in\{2,3\}\). Each coordinate has \(Q\) states, represented by \(Q-1\) independent binary indicators and their affine complementary indicator. The local degree polynomial is \(1+(Q-1)z\). Let \(\delta\ge2\) be a power of two, \(d=2\delta-1\), and take \(q\ge1\) disjoint blocks of \(d\) coordinates inside \(m\ge qd\) coordinates. On block \(i\), let \(a_e\) indicate that coordinate \(e\) is not in its distinguished zero state, and put \[g_i=e_\delta((a_e)_{e\in S_i}).\] Here \(a_e\) is a linear sum of the nonzero-state indicators. On local points \(g_i\) is the majority of these \(d\) bits, since \(\binom w\delta\bmod2=1\) precisely for \(\delta\le w\le2\delta-1\). This is the previously recorded binary digit identity.

For every binary constant matrix \(C\) with \(0\le h<q\) rows, set \[P_C=\prod_{u=1}^{h}(1+(Cg)_u),\qquad E_i=P_Cg_i.\] For \(0\le K\le m\), in the full local function algebra, \[\operatorname{rank}(f\in V_K\mapsto(E_if)_i) \ge [z^{\le K}](1+(Q-1)z)^{m-qd} \left(\sum_{j=0}^{\delta-1}(Q-1)^j\binom dj z^j\right)^q.\tag{1}\] Writing \(B_{Q,m}(K)=\sum_{j\le K}(Q-1)^j\binom mj\), one obtains \[\frac{\operatorname{rank}E(V_K)}{B_{Q,m}(K)} \ge1-q\binom d\delta(K/m)^\delta \ge1-q(4K/m)^\delta.\tag{2}\] These are local ranks, not automatically charged-quotient ranks.

Both fibers preserve low degree. The Hamming ball of radius \(\delta-1\) around the all-zero state lies in \(g_i=0\). Its evaluation matrix on normal monomials of degree at most \(\delta-1\) is triangular with diagonal one: index both points and monomials by supports of size at most \(\delta-1\) and their nonzero labels, and order first by support size. A monomial evaluates nontrivially exactly when its labeled support is contained in the point. Thus restriction to this ball is injective on that degree space.

Fix a nonzero state, the same on each coordinate. Every point within Hamming distance \(\delta-1\) of the resulting constant word has at least \(\delta\) nonzero coordinates, so this ball lies in \(g_i=1\). Relabeling states independently on coordinates is an invertible affine substitution of their independent indicators and preserves the degree filtration. It takes this ball to the zero-centered one. Consequently restriction to either majority fiber is injective through degree \(\delta-1\), also when \(Q=3\). For \(Q=2\), the earlier majority-fiber record gives an exact cumulative rank, stronger than the lower bound needed here.

Every matrix leaves one product fiber. Since \(h<q\), choose nonzero \(y\in\ker C\), with \(y_i=1\) for some \(i\). On the nonempty product fiber \(g_j=y_j\), one has \(P_C=1\) and \(E_i=1\). Tensoring the injective restrictions of the block degree-at-most-\(\delta-1\) spaces, with the full spaces on unused coordinates, gives an injective restriction. Its total-degree-at-most-\(K\) subspace has the dimension in (1). The full error map is injective on that subspace, proving (1). This uses every possible constant matrix, not only one correlated sampling law.

Count the omitted monomials. A local normal monomial has a support and one of \(Q-1\) labels on each selected coordinate. Among all monomials through degree \(K\), conditional on support size its support is uniform. The probability it contains a particular \(\delta\)-subset is at most \((K/m)^\delta\), since \((k-a)/(m-a)\le k/m\le K/m\). Union-bound over \(\binom d\delta\) subsets in each block. All monomials outside these events appear in (1), proving the first inequality in (2); \(\binom{2\delta-1}\delta\le4^\delta\) proves the second. Neither field positivity nor a point-density-to-rank inference is used.

A paid source-scale control

For sufficiently large \(m\), choose the least power of two \(\delta\ge2\log_2m\), so \(\delta<4\log_2m\), set \(d=2\delta-1\), \(q=\lfloor m/d\rfloor\) and \(K=\lfloor m/1024\rfloor\). For every \(h<q\), \[\operatorname{rank}E(V_K)\ge(1-m^{-15})B_{Q,m}(K),\tag{3}\] because \(q(4K/m)^\delta\le m(1/256)^\delta\le m^{-15}\). In particular (3) holds for polylogarithmic \(h\).

For \(Q=3\), these are the local coordinates of the growing charged family. A literal complete two-level source realizes each \(g_i\): for every \(\delta\)-subset \(T\) of its block introduce an affine bottom on inputs \((1-a_e)_{e\in T}\). With original accuracy \(h\ge\delta\), select each input once and pad; the product is exactly \(\prod_{e\in T}a_e\). The parent inputs are their sums, namely the \(g_i\)'s. The bottom ranks are \(\delta\), and the total bottom inventory is at most \(q\binom d\delta\le m4^\delta<m^9\). Factored descriptions and their accuracy factors remain polynomial in \(m\), hence in the original CNF length.
