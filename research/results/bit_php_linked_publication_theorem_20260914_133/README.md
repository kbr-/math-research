usual CNF bit pigeonhole principle. The proof uses notebook dependencies without
repeating their statements or proofs. It includes both audited rule presentations,
the actual CNF bridge, and explicit closure of the degree and board parameters.

[dependencies.json](dependencies.json) identifies the exact proof-step references,
the checkpoint containing their full arguments, and the parameter choices.
[literature-supplement.json](literature-supplement.json) records the additional
Bhattacharya-Chattopadhyay-Dvorak literature lead supplied by the user.
The checked additional papers retain regularity, depth, or tree-like restrictions.

This is a working publication candidate, not an externally verified theorem.
No new numerical suite was run; the linked component evidence retains its
original scope. The focused checkpoint checks only touched references, stable
labels, metadata, and preservation of earlier entries.
