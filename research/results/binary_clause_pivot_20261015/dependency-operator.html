Boolean cover cost gap

Proposition. For every \(n\ge4\), work over \(\mathbb F_2\), with cutoff \(D=3\) and source consisting only of the original equations \(x_i^2+x_i\), \(1\le i\le n\). Let \(T\subseteq\{0,1\}^n\) consist of the seven points \(0,e_1,e_2,e_3,e_4,e_1+e_2,e_3+e_4\), with all extra coordinates zero, and set \(\lambda(f)=\sum_{a\in T}f(a)\). Then \(\lambda(1)=1\) and \(\lambda(C_3)=0\). Its horizon dimensions are \((1,5,5,1)\), with inclusion ranks \((1,5,1)\). Thus \(\mathcal B(\lambda)=5\), while \(6\le\mathcal M(\lambda)\le7\). The minimum is defined by the source-compatible-cover criterion; no exact value inside that interval is asserted.

Proof: horizons. Every Boolean source equation vanishes on \(T\), and evaluation annihilates every degree-three PC consequence by induction on the derivation. The unit evaluates to seven, hence one. The only nonzero squarefree moments of degree at most three are \(\lambda(1)=1\), \(\lambda(x_1x_2)=\lambda(x_3x_4)=1\). Singleton and triple moments vanish. On \(V_1\), the pairing \((f,g)\mapsto\lambda(fg)\), after deleting the zero extra-coordinate directions, has matrix \(\operatorname{diag}(1,J_2,J_2)\), where \(J_2=\left(\begin{smallmatrix}0&1\\1&0\end{smallmatrix}\right)\); it is nonsingular. Therefore the degree-one horizon has dimension five. The degree-two horizon is the pairing against \(V_1\), so its dimension is at most five and the displayed nonsingular submatrix gives equality. The degree-zero and degree-three horizons have dimension one. Inclusion from degree one to two has rank five by that same pairing; the outer inclusions have rank one since the unit has nonzero moment. The birth formula gives \(1+(5-1)+(5-5)+(1-1)=5\).

Proof: five states are impossible. Suppose a compatible cover has terminal dimension five. Restrict it to its cyclic part using lem:source-compatible-cover. Its degree-one space already surjects onto a five-dimensional horizon. Injective inclusions therefore identify \(E_1,E_2,E_3\) with a single five-dimensional space \(E\). The degree-one evaluation spans \(E\). The paid multiplication maps, through these identifications, become endomorphisms \(X_i:E\to E\): compatibility with inclusions equates their degree-one and degree-two versions. Their two-step commutation applies on all of \(E_1\), so the endomorphisms commute. Each original Boolean equation annihilates the cyclic unit; its legal multiple by every \(x_j\) also does, through degree three. Hence \(X_i^2+X_i\) kills the spanning evaluations of \(1,x_1,\ldots,x_n\), and each \(X_i\) is idempotent on \(E\).

Commuting binary idempotents have joint projectors \(P_a=\prod_{i:a_i=1}X_i\prod_{i:a_i=0}(I+X_i)\), which sum to the identity. This is a finite linear-algebra argument on the now flat space; it does not spend polynomial-calculus degree to form those projectors. At most five joint eigenspaces have nonzero components of the cyclic unit \(u\). Every monomial of degree at most three evaluates on \(u\) by the paid maps. The terminal projection \(E\to S_3\cong\mathbb F_2\) thus expresses \(\lambda\) through degree three as a sum of point evaluations on at most five points, with binary weights. Extra coordinates also satisfy the equations. Restrict this representation to polynomials in the first four variables and combine weights of coincident four-bit projections; it still uses at most five four-bit points.

The fifteen squarefree monomials of degree at most three on four bits are independent functions: they are a subset of the sixteen-monomial basis of the full Boolean function algebra, whose evaluation matrix is triangular under subset containment. Their orthogonal complement in the sixteen-dimensional point-weight space has dimension one. The all-ones weight lies in it, because each such monomial sums to \(2^{4-|S|}=0\) modulo two. Therefore the only four-bit point-weight vectors giving the displayed moments are the characteristic vector of the seven-point set and its complement, of size nine. Neither has support at most five, a contradiction. Consequently \(\mathcal M\ge6\). Conversely the seven-dimensional function algebra on \(T\), filtered by polynomial degree, gives commuting source actions, the cyclic unit and observation \(\lambda\). All Boolean equations vanish, and the terminal algebra is generated by degree-at-most-three evaluations: the full evaluation map on \(T\) has rank seven since a supported weight annihilating all fifteen monomials would have to be the all-ones weight on all sixteen points. Thus it is a compatible seven-state cover and \(\mathcal M\le7\). \(\square\)
