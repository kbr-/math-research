Domain-model stacked rank

Statement. Let \(F\) be a field, \(X\subseteq F^n\) a finite set, \(B_D\) the polynomials of degree at most \(D\) vanishing on \(X\), \(U=V_K/(B_D\cap V_K)\), and \(E_1,\ldots,E_s\in V_e\) with \(K+e\le D\). Write \(S_j\subseteq X\) for the support of \(E_j\) on \(X\), \(S=\bigcup_jS_j\), and \(T_j:U\to V_D/B_D\), \(u\mapsto[E_ju]\). Then \(\operatorname{rank}T_j=\dim U|_{S_j}\) and the stacked map has \(\operatorname{rank}(T_j)_j=\dim U|_S\), where \(U|_A\) is the space of restrictions to \(A\) of the functions in \(U\). The restriction map \(\iota:U|_S\to\bigoplus_jU|_{S_j}\) is injective, so \(\sum_j\operatorname{rank}T_j-\operatorname{rank}(T_j)_j=\dim\operatorname{coker}\iota\).

Proof. Since \(K\le D\), \(B_D\cap V_K\) is exactly the degree-\(\le K\) part vanishing on \(X\), so classes of \(U\) are determined by their functions on \(X\). For \(u\in V_K\), \(E_ju\) has degree at most \(K+e\le D\), so \([E_ju]=0\) exactly when \(E_ju\) vanishes on \(X\); as \(E_j(x)\ne0\) is invertible in \(F\) at every \(x\in S_j\), that is exactly \(u|_{S_j}=0\). So \(\ker T_j=\{u:u|_{S_j}=0\}\) and the stacked kernel is \(\{u:u|_S=0\}\); rank–nullity on the restriction maps gives the ranks. A function on \(S\) vanishing on every \(S_j\) is zero, so \(\iota\) is injective, and \(\sum_j\dim U|_{S_j}\) is the dimension of its target.

Application to Pang. In cor:pang-observation-calibration the errors are \(E_{\nu,i}=P_\nu g_{\nu,i}\), of degree at most \(\Delta+d\), and the window \(\Delta+K+d\le D\) is exactly \(K+e\le D\). Over \(\mathbb F_p\) with only the Boolean axioms, a proper system, the degree-\(D\) PC consequences are exactly \(B_D\) for \(X=\{0,1\}^n\): multilinearization is degree-nonincreasing, each step a monomial times \(x^2-x\) (historical lem:fieldreduction). So with only domain relations the stacked rank of all of Pang's error maps is \(\dim U|_S\) over the union of their supports, and the recorded sum exceeds it by \(\dim\operatorname{coker}\iota\); overlap of supports is one source of that gap, failure of \(U\) to interpolate independently on disjoint pieces another.

Against the open statement. joint-error-savings says, verbatim: “Nor does union-of-supports counting in an evaluation model, nor domination”. In the domain-only model the lemma expresses the joint rank through the union of supports alone, as an interpolation rank, so every saving over the sum there is of that form; whether the exclusion's wording, written for the curve model where “joint rank is the union size by definition”, covers the interpolation deficit on disjoint supports is for the next review to confirm. Two further facts bound what a saving in Pang's proper quotient \(V_K/(C_D\cap V_K)\), with \(C_D\) the consequences of an unsatisfiable system, can be. First, domain-only savings carry into it: \(u|_S=0\) gives \(E u\in B_D\subseteq C_D\), so the proper-quotient kernel contains the image of the domain-only one. Second, a saving beyond that needs the relations of \(C_D\) beyond the domain equations.

Nonvacuity. For the lemma alone: \(F=\mathbb F_2\), \(X=\{0,1\}^n\), \(n\ge2\), \(K=e=1\), \(D=2\), errors \(E_j=x_j\), \(j\le s\), \(2\le s\le n\), on \(U=V_1\): each \(\dim U|_{S_j}=n\) (there \(x_j\) and \(1\) coincide), so the summed rank is \(sn\), while the union is the cube minus exactly \(2^{n-s}\) points, on which \(U|_S\) has dimension \(n+1\). In Pang's matched ledger, a witness of his form: \(p=2\), \(d=t=1\), so \(\Delta=1\); \(K=1\), \(D=3\), so \(\Delta+K+d=3\le D\); \(s\ge2\) families \((x_{2a-1},x_{2a})\) on \(n=2s\) variables with \(q_a=x_{2a-1}+x_{2a}\) and \(P_a=1+q_a\). The errors \(P_ax_{2a-1}\) and \(P_ax_{2a}\) are supported on \(\{x_{2a-1}=x_{2a}=1\}\), where \(U=V_1\) restricts to dimension \(2s-1\); the summed family ranks are \(s(2s-1)\), and the stacked rank is \(\dim U|_S=2s+1\), since an affine function vanishing whenever some pair is \((1,1)\) is zero for \(s\ge2\).

Degenerate cases. \(s=0\): rank zero. \(E_j=0\): empty support. Disjoint supports: \(\iota\) need not be surjective, and the sum can exceed the stacked rank when \(U\) cannot separate the supports, as with \(U\) the constants. \(K+e>D\): outside the statement; products are not paid.

New mathematics. lem:domain-model-stacked-rank, an exact formula at the rediscovery level: in a function quotient, multiplication by \(E\) kills exactly the functions vanishing on its support, so stacked rank is the interpolation rank on the union of supports, and the sum exceeds it by a cokernel. Applied to Pang's errors at his cutoff, it identifies every domain-only saving and shows a further saving needs non-domain relations; where the record sums over parents is identified.
