1. The admissible schedule and its missing uniform inequality
Suppose a proved, unweighted source transformation fixes the old base, maps a degree-\(D\) refutation to a degree-\(AD\) refutation, and leaves a complete acyclic source. Remove an upward-closed set of blocks in reverse dependency order, with current input degrees \(\delta_j\). Historical additive elimination leaves a refutation through
\[ D_1=AD+C,\qquad C=(p-1)\sum_j\delta_j. \tag{MS-eliminate} \]
If the remainder satisfies the affine-reference criterion, its greedy charge \(t\) and coefficient factor \(T\) give a terminal affine proof of a separated old target through
\[ E=T(AD+C)+t. \tag{MS-total} \]
The available full-board old-target endpoint requires target exponent \(\eta\), proof exponent \(\zeta\ge\eta\), and \(2\zeta+\eta<1\). In particular, when \(T,A,D\) are polylogarithmic and \(C\le N^\mu\operatorname{polylog}N\), \(t\le N^\eta\operatorname{polylog}N\), the sufficient condition is \(2\max(\mu,\eta)+\eta<1\). If all coefficients instead disappear under a proved pure-reader map, use that map's stronger old linear barrier; do not replace it by this smaller window.
These are actual sequential implications, not a free optimization over reordered operations. A weighted clamp ends at \(f\), not one, whereas historical elimination assumes a refutation. Applying it afterward without a target-preserving theorem is unjustified. A profile transformation with its own weight also cannot be inserted as the unweighted first step without a separate composition ledger. The missing main-route assertion is a uniform schedule satisfying (MS-total) on the required compiled sources, including every helper and original degree. No such bound follows from the existence of several affordable local operations.
2. A nonzero old class has few annihilating bit-affine directions
Use the full functional system \(\mathcal F^{\mathrm{fun}}_{m,N}\) over any field \(K\), with \(N=2^\ell\), \(\ell\ge1\), \(m\ge t+1\), and \(N>4t\). Write \(I_j=\mathcal I_j(\mathcal F^{\mathrm{fun}}_{m,N})\), and let
\[ W=\operatorname{span}_K\{1,B_{i,u}:1\le i\le m,\ 1\le u\le\ell\}, \qquad \dim W=1+m\ell. \]
Here \(B_{i,u}\) is the decoded label bit in row \(i\). Let \(\deg f\le t\) and \(f\notin I_t\). Define the linear annihilator at the next old cutoff by
\[ K_f=\{L\in W:fL\in I_{t+1}\}. \tag{MS-ann} \]
Working rank lemma. There is an integer \(\kappa\le t\) such that
\[ \dim K_f\le\kappa\ell\le t\ell, \qquad \operatorname{rank}\bigl(W\xrightarrow{L\mapsto[fL]}\mathcal P_{\le t+1}/I_{t+1}\bigr) \ge1+(m-\kappa)\ell. \tag{MS-rank} \]
The bound concerns dimension; it does not assert that every annihilating form is supported on a common set of \(\kappa\) rows.
Proof. The old row-trade detector supplies \(R\) with \(f-R\in I_t\), degree \(\kappa\le t\), and a nonzero constant \(c\) obtained by a disjoint two-label difference in a \(\kappa\)-row set \(U\). At \(\kappa=0\), use \(R=c\). Multiplying the congruence by any affine \(L\) gives \(fL-RL\in I_{t+1}\).
Let \(W_{\bar U}\) be the span of one and the decoded bits outside \(U\). We claim multiplication by \(f\) is injective on this space modulo \(I_{t+1}\). Take a nonzero \(L\in W_{\bar U}\). If it is nonconstant, choose a row \(j\notin U\) where its bit coefficient vector is nonzero. Its row-label function is nonconstant after deleting the \(2\kappa\) reserved labels: every fiber has size at most \(N/2\), since toggling a bit with nonzero coefficient changes the value, and \(2\kappa<N/2\). Choose two remaining labels with different values.
Apply the \(U\)-trade and then this extra row difference to \(RL\). Because \(L\) is independent of the rows in \(U\), the first result is \(cL\), and the final result is nonzero. Set every other row to zero when evaluating the difference. This signed functional annihilates \(I_{\kappa+1}\): Boolean and exclusion generators and assigned-row sums vanish, while an unassigned-row sum leaves a cofactor of degree at most \(\kappa\), missing one of the \(\kappa+1\) difference rows. This is the same direct generator check as in the preceding entry.
Thus \(RL\notin I_{\kappa+1}\). The old stable filtration, valid through \(t+1\) under the stated board room, keeps this degree-at-most-\(\kappa+1\) polynomial outside \(I_{t+1}\). If \(L\) is a nonzero constant, use the original \(\kappa\)-row difference and stability instead; when \(\kappa=0\), nonzero constants survive old \(I_{t+1}\). This proves injectivity on the \(1+(m-\kappa)\ell\)-dimensional space \(W_{\bar U}\), hence (MS-rank). \(\square\)
3. An explicit generic-family bound
Now work over \(\mathbb F_2\), with \(m=N+1\), and let \(W_0\) be the \(v=m\ell\)-dimensional span of the decoded bits without the constant. Draw \(M\le N/8\) independent uniform \(r\)-dimensional subspaces of \(W_0\), \(1\le r\le v\). They are proper nonzero affine input spans. Let \(G={v\brack r}_2\) be the number of such subspaces.
For a fixed nonzero old class of charged degree \(t\le M-1\), (MS-rank) gives
\[ \Pr[V\subseteq K_f] =\frac{{\dim(K_f\cap W_0)\brack r}_2}{{v\brack r}_2} \le 2^{-r(v-t\ell)}. \tag{MS-probability} \]
The numerator is zero when its dimension is below \(r\). Otherwise write the Gaussian-binomial ratio as \(\prod_{j<r}(2^k-2^j)/(2^v-2^j)\), where \(k\le t\ell\); each factor is at most \(2^{k-v}\). This proves the displayed bound without an independence assumption about membership tests within one span.
In characteristic two, a forced-one greedy decision is exactly \(V\subseteq K_f\). For any fixed online rule selecting its next nonzero probe from the current span, independence of the next sampled span and (MS-probability), conditional on the previous history, imply that every block is new and successfully charged except with probability at most
\[ M\,2^{-r(v-(M-1)\ell)}+\binom M2/G. \tag{MS-online} \]
The second term separately bounds repeated subspaces, which memoization would not charge. The first term bounds the first forced-one event, so preceding successful choices ensure the required \(f\notin I_t\).
The conclusion can also cover all orders and probe choices. Fix a canonical listing of the \(2^r-1\) nonzero vectors of each subspace. A fixed permutation and one prescribed vector index per stage define an online rule, with a deterministic valid-probe fallback if its prescribed index annihilates the current class. Every valid offline trace is realized by at least one of these \(M!(2^r-1)^M\) rules. A union bound therefore gives exceptional probability at most
\[ M!(2^r-1)^M M\,2^{-r(v-(M-1)\ell)}+\binom M2/G. \tag{MS-all-traces} \]
Working generic-charge corollary. For \(r=\ell\) and \(M=\Theta(N)\) with \(M\le N/8\), the exceptional probability in (MS-all-traces) is \(2^{-\Omega(N\ell^2)}\). Thus, with that explicit high probability, every unmodified greedy trace on the distinct sampled internal types has charge \(t=M\). Indeed \(G\ge2^{r(v-r)}\), while \(\log_2 M!+rM+\log_2 M=O(N\ell)\) and \(r(v-(M-1)\ell)=\Omega(N\ell^2)\). This is a proved counting estimate for the displayed random model, not an estimate of research prospects. No sampling or large matrix computation was run.
The forms may be dense across rows; the rank lemma did not assume disjoint row supports. Genericity therefore does not make the forced-one alternative common in this procedure. This does not prove that a different common weight, restriction, affine map or source-dependent functional is expensive.
4. Dense dependency and allowed simplifications still leave a gap
A raw two-level schedule can face many distinct terminals, each reading all internal products. The graph-code construction supplies polynomially many pairwise-disjoint parent input spaces with this property; arbitrary old-affine terms can be added without destroying their independence modulo old-only polynomials. Choose \(M=2\ell\lfloor N/(16\ell)\rfloor\) bottom coordinates so the binary graph-code construction applies, and take logarithmic-rank generic bottom spans as above, common accuracy \(h=\Theta(\log N)\), and \(\Theta(N)\) such terminals. Their actual maximum input degree is \(2h\). Removing every terminal one by one costs \(\Theta(Nh)\); leaving a terminal retains all bottoms as internal types, for which (MS-all-traces) gives linear greedy charge. Neither fits the displayed affine-terminal power window. Raw equal-span merging does not identify the pairwise-disjoint parent spaces.
This is a test of those operations on a source grammar, not a claim of essential occurrence in a short proof or a lower bound against all transformations. In particular, at \(h\ge r\) the binary Fermat basis construction can replace each rank-\(r\) bottom by its exact degree-\(r\) flat indicator using constant coefficient rows. Original bottom companion images have old Boolean certificates through \(r+1\le2h+1\). The resulting parent inputs have old degree at most \(r=\Theta(\log N)\); this is a legitimate alternative representation, but it is a nonlinear old-input source, not the affine terminal system in (MS-total). No free reset of the proof degree or deletion of defining equations was used. The earlier shared-bit spread control is already covered and must not be recycled as a hard source.
