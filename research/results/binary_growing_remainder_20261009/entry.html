Statement with the original inventory parameter

Fix the graph/CNF family of growing expansion, with \(\Delta=\lceil(\log n)^2\rceil\), \(n\Delta/8\le m\le n\Delta\), proper binary old quotient through \(D=\lfloor n\Delta/64\rfloor\), and \(K=\lfloor m/1024\rfloor\). Use \(v=2m\) independent Boolean old indicators, and let \(N_n\) be the canonical encoding length, \(\log N_n=\Theta(\Delta)\). All logarithms are natural. Define \[ q=\left\lfloor\frac{\log n}{100\log16384}\right\rfloor,\quad R=\lceil4096\log(8M)\rceil,\quad h=\lfloor n^{1/20}\rfloor,\quad B=\lfloor n^{1/4}\rfloor. \tag{1} \] For large \(n\), \(q\ge1\). Fix any \(A>0\), with \(1\le M\le N_n^A\) and at most \(M\) bottom blocks and at most \(M\) parents. Both levels have original accuracy \(h\), private coefficient domains and all companions. Bottom inputs are old affine forms. Every parent input is an old-affine polynomial plus a constant binary linear combination of genuine bottom products; no raw bottom coefficients or parent-to-parent inputs occur. Products shared by different parents are interpreted coherently.

Theorem (growing rank gap). Suppose each proper bottom input span has rank at most \(q\) or at least \(R\). For all sufficiently large \(n\), depending on \(A\), this complete two-level source has no PC or ordinary NS refutation through original degree \(B\). One global constant coefficient assignment and a surviving old weight of degree at most \(2K\) suffice. The graph, accuracy exponent and degree-bound exponent are independent of \(A\).

“Proper” means that the affine span does not contain one, after local normalization. Zero and unit spans are permitted and handled separately. If the intervals overlap, choose the high-rank treatment first; every remaining nonzero proper span then has rank at most \(q\). The theorem imposes no additional parent arity, overlap or linear-rank bound.

First weight and the already available rank retention

Proof. For every proper bottom rank at least \(R\), choose \(R\) independent affine forms from its actual input span, use their constant indexed representations as rows, and zero-pad to \(h\). For each remaining proper span choose its complete basis, of rank at most \(q\), and zero-pad. A unit span uses a row equal to one; a zero span uses zero rows. Since \(R=O_A(\Delta)\) and \(q=O(\log n)\), all selected rows fit \(h\) eventually.

Apply growing-expansion removal to the high-rank products, starting at weight one. It gives a common kernel \(X\subseteq[V_K]\) of dimension at least \(h_1(K)/2\), killing those products. The original companion reserve is \(K+R+1\le D\). If there are no high-rank products, take \(X=[V_K]\).

The rank-retaining lemma, over the binary Boolean old quotient, applies to \(X\) at \(3K\le D\). We may therefore select a binary \(w\) of degree at most \(K\), with \([w]\in X\), such that \[ h_w(K)\ge\tfrac12\dim X\ge\tfrac14h_1(K)>0. \tag{2} \] This resolves the proposed first-weight support concern using an existing theorem. It requires no character sparsity. For the parent argument only \(w\notin J_D\) is needed; (2) is recorded for possible actual-profile comparisons, not smuggled in as a necessary hypothesis.

All weighted bottom companions vanish: high products are killed and multiplication by each affine input is paid; low products exactly test a basis, so their companions have Boolean certificates; unit and zero cases were handled explicitly. Every coefficient-field image is zero. Put \(\delta=\max(R,q,1)\). Actual specialized parent inputs have degree at most \(\delta\), while after deleting the high products they agree modulo the weight with reference polynomials of degree at most \(q\). Constants from zero spans can be absorbed into the old-affine part. Thus \[ w(g_{a,i}-\widetilde g_{a,i})\in J_D,\qquad \deg\widetilde g_{a,i}\le q, \] through the already paid \(K+\delta\) ceiling. No claim that the low products become affine is made.

The logarithmic-degree rate fits

Write \(N_v(k)=\sum_{j\le k}\binom vj\). For \(1\le k\le v\), \[ \frac{N_v(k-1)}{N_v(k)}\ge\frac{k}{v+k}\ge\frac{k}{2v}, \] because \(\binom vk=((v-k+1)/k)\binom v{k-1}\le(v/k)N_v(k-1)\). Eventually \(q\le K/2\), and \(K\ge m/2048\). Multiplying the \(q\) consecutive ratios therefore gives \[ \eta_q=\frac12\frac{N_v(K-q)}{N_v(K)} \ge\frac12\left(\frac{K}{4v}\right)^q \ge\frac12\,16384^{-q}\ge\frac12 n^{-1/100}. \tag{3} \] Choose \[ t=\left\lceil4 n^{1/100}\log(2M)\right\rceil. \] Then \(M(1-\eta_q)^t\le M e^{-2\log(2M)}=1/(4M)<1\). Since \(\log M=O_A((\log n)^2)\), \[ t=O_A(n^{1/100}(\log n)^2)=o(h),\qquad (t+1)\delta=O_A(n^{1/100}(\log n)^4)=o(n\Delta). \] Also \(2K\le n\Delta/512\), whereas \(D=n\Delta/64+O(1)\). Hence \[ 2K+(t+1)\delta\le D, \qquad 2K+\delta+1\le D, \qquad 2K+B\le D \tag{4} \] eventually. These are inequalities in the actual vertex/edge parameters; the much larger inventory was included through \(\log M\), not replaced by a polynomial in \(n\).

Apply the single-branch removal theorem with branch weight \(w\), degree ceiling \(b=K\), reference degree \(q\), actual degree \(\delta\), cutoff \(K\) and \(t\) rows. Its hypotheses are precisely (3)–(4) and the weighted comparisons. It chooses constant binary rows in the actual parent positions and \(f\in V_K\), with \(W=wf\notin J_D\), annihilating all parent companions. Zero-padding to original accuracy \(h\) is legal. The earlier bottom memberships survive multiplication by \(f\) through \(2K+\delta+1\le D\); all coefficient fields remain zero.

A purported original degree-\(B\) source refutation, after this one global constant assignment and multiplication by \(W\), stays in \(J_D\) by \(\deg W+B\le2K+B\le D\). Its final line is \(W\), a contradiction. The original bottom products have degree at most \(2h\), parent inputs degree at most \(2h\), and parent companions at most \(2h^2+3h\); these are all below \(B\) eventually. The complete source is not excluded vacuously by placing its axioms above the proof ceiling. Ordinary NS refutations replay in PC at their original ceiling. This proves the theorem. \(\square\)
