Verdict: no alternative architecture it can vouch for; one concrete candidate that escapes the deficit bound.
1. Row types. Multi-tuple or mixed-width rows on at most k forms: per-row factor is the level polynomial of
the allowed set, series = table product series, thm:saddle-deficit-bound applies: deficit at least
n(ln k/(2k) - 0.47/k) (index = v max Phi itself conjectural). General polynomial g of degree d in the forms:
top form tau in Lambda^d U; forced relations are those of Ann(tau) that lift; tau itself always does (g(g+1)=0);
a clause has the largest annihilator; higher-rank tau moves towards the semi-regular series: worse (mine).
Rows forbidding more than half: for "at most one of k forms" (factor 1+kz) index about n/k: worse (mine).
Candidate: same-sign sharing. Each linear form occurs with one constant only: literals L_1..L_N, N > n, a
monotone CNF with edge set H, unsatisfiability only from the N-n linear relations. Shared-form relations lift
exactly: f_1 = L A, f_2 = L B give B f_1 = A f_2 (the constant-degree failure needs opposite signs) (mine).
Conjectured criterion: the linear relations are regular on the exterior face ring of Delta = {sets containing
no edge} through D; series f_Delta(z)/(1+z)^(N-n); saddle form Phi n = alpha n - alpha(1-alpha) psi'(alpha),
psi = -ln mu_alpha(Delta). Deficit n^(1-eps) needs psi, psi' of order at most n^(1-eps) at
alpha = 1/2 - n^(-eps), yet psi(1/2) >= n ln 2: a sharp lower-tail threshold with minterms of size O(log n).
Disjoint edges and block-structured H cannot; it would need clusters of about n^eps jointly relevant forms.
Smallest test, on paper: decide whether any H with polynomially many O(log n)-edges has that psi-jump.
2. Amplification: the record gives none (restriction, product frames, padding, disjoint union (mine),
XOR-ification inside the class). Relative deficit ln k/k depends only on k. The two-level form's worth is a
first F_2 bound above n/4, not a step to every depth.
3. Assessment: not reachable with independent-like rows; whether reachable at all by lifting hinges on the
psi-threshold question; that, or a regularity proof at degree growing with n, would most change the picture.
Unexamined: covers with small covering sum (structured codes).
