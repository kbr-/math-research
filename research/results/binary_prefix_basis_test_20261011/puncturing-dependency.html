The same conclusion holds for complete literal bottom blocks realizing the \(Q_j\)'s, followed by these parent tuples after that one bottom assignment, provided each bottom has accuracy at least \(d_j\), every earlier coefficient occurs in parents only as allowed by this specialization, and (1) holds for the actual specialized inputs.
