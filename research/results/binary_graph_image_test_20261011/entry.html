Local graph exclusion

Setup. Work over \(\mathbb F_2\) on \(m\) Boolean-exclusive pairs \((x_e,y_e)\), with \[ I_{\rm loc}=(x_e^2+x_e,\ y_e^2+y_e,\ x_ey_e:e\in[m]). \] The local domain has three states per edge. Square reduction and deletion of mixed monomials never increase degree. The remaining monomials use at most one indicator per edge and form a basis, by tensoring the invertible three-state evaluation matrix. Consequently every degree-\(L\) local-zero polynomial has an ordinary local-axiom certificate through degree \(L\).

Let \(J_D\) be a proper degree-\(D\) binary PC-closed space containing the old axioms and these local axioms, and let \(w\notin J_D\), \(\deg w\le b\). Let \(U\) be a subspace of local normal polynomials of degree at most \(k\ge1\), and write \(H=\dim[wU]>0\). There are \(M\ge1\) terminal complete ENS blocks, with private coefficients, positive original accuracies and old-only inputs. Block \(a\) has a core \(S_a\) of \(s_a\) edges and, for every \(e\notin S_a\), contains the actual input \[ g_{a,e}=x_e+F_{a,e}(x_{S_a},y_{S_a}). \] The offsets are arbitrary binary polynomials on that core. A block-specific swap of the two indicators on an edge is permitted. Extra old-only inputs are allowed. Put \(s=\max_a s_a\), \(T=k+s\), and \[ N_t(k)=\sum_{j=0}^{\min(k,t)}\binom tj. \] Let \(B\ge2\) cover all original old and fresh axioms and the hypothetical source proof, including each original companion ceiling \(e_{a,i}=h_a(\delta_a+1)+d_{a,i}\), where \(\delta_a\) is the actual maximum input degree and \(d_{a,i}\) the individual degree.

Count the actual zero graphs. Fix a local core state \(\sigma\), one of \(3^{s_a}\) choices. Setting the displayed graph inputs to zero fixes each outside \(x_e\) to the bit \(F_{a,e}(\sigma)\). Its partner \(y_e\) is forced to zero if that bit is one; otherwise it is a free Boolean bit. A degree-at-most-\(k\) old normal polynomial restricted to this part of the graph is therefore a degree-at-most-\(k\) polynomial in at most \(m-s_a\) free bits. The restriction rank is at most \(3^{s_a}N_{m-s_a}(k)\). Fixing the core for this count is not a free nonlinear substitution or a removal of partner variables.

Choose a complement \(U_0\) to the kernel of \(f\mapsto[wf]\) in \(U\). It has dimension \(H\). The joint graph-restriction map on \(U_0\) has rank below \(H\), so choose a nonzero \(Q\) in its kernel. Then \([wQ]\ne0\), and \(Q\) vanishes on every actual local zero graph. Core sets and offsets may differ between blocks.

Construct the coefficient maps. Fix \(a,\sigma\), specialize \(Q\)'s core coordinates to \(\sigma\), and call the resulting polynomial \(Q_\sigma\). Put \(c_e=F_{a,e}(\sigma)\). On outside pairs use the linear local projection \[ x_e\longmapsto c_e,\qquad y_e\longmapsto(1+c_e)y_e. \] It maps local points onto the outside zero graph. Telescoping each normal monomial yields \[ Q_\sigma=\sum_{e\notin S_a}\gamma_{\sigma,e}(x_e+c_e)+L_\sigma,\qquad \deg\gamma_{\sigma,e}\le k, \] where \(L_\sigma\) is local-zero with a certificate through \(k+1\). For an \(x_e\) factor the difference is the displayed input. For a \(y_e\) factor only \(c_e=1\) changes it, and \(y_e=(x_e+1)y_e+x_ey_e\). The cofactor has degree at most \(k\) and the extra local term at most \(k+1\). The fully projected polynomial vanishes on the outside local domain; degree-nonincreasing local reduction handles that remainder.

Let \(\chi_\sigma\) be the product of the core state indicators, choosing \(1+x_e+y_e\), \(x_e\) or \(y_e\) on each core edge. Its degree is at most \(s_a\). Put \[ \beta_{a,e}=\sum_\sigma\chi_\sigma\gamma_{\sigma,e}. \] Use these coefficients in the first row at the indicated input positions, and zero in every extra-input position and later row. Then \(\deg\beta_{a,e}\le k+s_a\le T\). At each old local point exactly one selector is one, so \[ H_a:=\sum_{e\notin S_a}\beta_{a,e}g_{a,e}=Q \] as local functions. Thus the mapped product is \(1+H_a\), and every weighted mapped companion vanishes locally: if \(Q=0\) its weight is zero; if \(Q=1\), the product is zero. This includes companions of extra inputs. Every mapped coefficient field also vanishes, since its image is binary on Boolean points.

A companion of original degree \(e_{a,i}\), multiplied by \(wQ\), has degree at most \(b+k+Te_{a,i}\). The local-zero certificate just proved fits that same ceiling. Proof.
