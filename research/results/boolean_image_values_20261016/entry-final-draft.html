The coefficients at a Boolean point

The compiler is the noncommutative depth audit's, read in full: the certificate is \(\sum_iP_{i-1}L_i\), with \(P_i=\prod_{j\le i}(1-L_j)\) in proof order; an input line "write \(L_i=F_k+R_i\) ... Insert its original placeholder plus that correction certificate"; a non-input line has the identity "\(L_i=R_i+\sum_{j<i}a_{ij}L_jb_{ij}\)", whose "multipliers are products of old translated subformulas and constants", and "For a logical axiom or correction \(R_i\), insert its given certificate"; each premise term is rewritten by "\(U(1-L_j)VaL_jb=-U(L_j^2-L_j)Vab+U(1-L_j)[Va,L_j]b\)", where "The lemma supplies both certificates on the right", and "all terms contain exactly one placeholder". The lemma, lem:shallow-Boolean-commutator-certificates, builds the commutator certificate by the product rule, "\([P_1\cdots P_r,Q]=\sum_i P_1\cdots P_{i-1}[P_i,Q]P_{i+1}\cdots P_r\)", recursively, with "\([P_1+P_2,Q]=[P_1,Q]+[P_2,Q]\)" and "Constants commute", the surrounding factors "in their inherited order, never commute them"; its certificates of \([P,Q]\) are "from variable commutators", so the commutator certificates \(\gamma\) and the literal-permutation corrections carry only commutator placeholders, and \(\gamma(1-L,Q)=-\gamma(L,Q)\) by linearity in the first argument. Clause placeholders occur only in the input lines' terms.

Let a refutation \(\pi\) write its used input lines first, translations \(L_1,\ldots,L_m\) with \(L_k=F_k+R_k\), then its non-input lines. Fix a Boolean point \(a\), the old variables commuting scalars and the placeholders free. Every translation takes a value in \(\{0,1\}\) there and every Boolean-generator or commutator element vanishes, so \(R_i(a)=0\) there as a polynomial value while its certificate's coefficients need not vanish. The used inputs have no common Boolean zero, so some \(L_k(a)=1\); let \(i^*\) be the least such \(k\), and \(\varepsilon(a)=1\) if no \(k>i^*\) has \(L_k(a)=1\), zero otherwise. Write \(\beta_k\) for the supplied certificate of \(L_k^2-L_k\), \(\rho_k\) for input \(k\)'s correction certificate, and \(\gamma_{i^*,k}\) for the supplied certificate of \([L_{i^*},L_k]\), each evaluated at \(a\) with its placeholders free. For \(k<i^*\) put \[w_k(a)=\sum_{n\le n_0}\ \sum_{\text{slots of }n\text{ citing }k}a_{nk}(a)\,b_{nk}(a)\in\mathbb F_2,\] where \(n_0\) is the first non-input line with \(L_{n_0}(a)=1\).

Lemma (Boolean image values). For every refutation \(\pi\) the compiler accepts, compiled with its used inputs first, and every Boolean point \(a\), \[C_\pi(a,Y)=\sum_{k\le i^*}\bigl(Y_k+\rho_k\bigr)-\varepsilon(a)\Bigl(\beta_{i^*}+\sum_{k<i^*}w_k(a)\,\gamma_{i^*,k}\Bigr).\] The initial segment of thm:used-node-jet-erasure for the same ordered list has the same form with every \(w_k(a)=1\), and so has every member of the matched jet envelope of lem:matched-jet-envelope. So the clause coordinates and the Boolean-generator coordinates of the image's value are determined by the ordered used input list, and the commutator coordinates are too except for the terms \(w_k(a)\gamma_{i^*,k}\), by which the image differs from the initial segment: \(C_\pi(a,\cdot)-A_t(a,\cdot)=-\varepsilon(a)\sum_{k<i^*}(w_k(a)-1)\gamma_{i^*,k}\).

Proof. (1) Input lines: line \(k\)'s term is \(P_{k-1}(Y_k+\rho_k)\), and \(P_{k-1}(a)\) is one for \(k\le i^*\) and zero for \(k>i^*\). (2) A non-input line \(n\): its own term \(P_{n-1}R_n\) has \(P_{n-1}(a)=0\), since \(P_{n-1}\) begins with all the inputs, among them the factor \(1-L_{i^*}\). (3) A premise term of line \(n\) for premise \(j\), \(-U\beta_jVab+U(1-L_j)\gamma(Va,L_j)b\) with \(\gamma(Va,L_j)\) the supplied certificate of \([Va,L_j]\): if \(j\) is a non-input line, \(U\) begins with all the inputs, so \(U(a)=0\) and the whole term vanishes; if \(j=k>i^*\), \(U\) contains \(1-L_{i^*}\), and the term vanishes; if \(j=i^*\), \(U(a)=1\), the commutator part has the outer factor \(1-L_{i^*}(a)=0\), and the Booleanity part is \(-\beta_{i^*}\) times \(V(a)a(a)b(a)\), with \(V\) the inputs after \(i^*\) and the non-input lines before \(n\), so \(V(a)=\varepsilon(a)\prod_{n'<n}(1-L_{n'}(a))\). If \(j=k<i^*\): the Booleanity part has \(V\) outside \(\beta_k\) and \(V\) contains \(1-L_{i^*}\), so it vanishes; in the commutator part \(U(1-L_k)(a)=1\), and by the product rule \(\gamma(Va,L_k)\) is the sum over the factors \(V_l\) of \(V\) and over \(a\) of (the factors before it)\(\cdot\gamma(V_l,L_k)\cdot\)(the factors after it, then \(a\)); every summand except the one at \(V_l=1-L_{i^*}\) keeps that factor among its surrounding factors, its value zero; the remaining summand has before it the inputs strictly between \(k\) and \(i^*\), value one, and after it the inputs after \(i^*\), the non-input lines before \(n\) and \(a\), value \(\varepsilon(a)\prod_{n'<n}(1-L_{n'}(a))a(a)\); with \(\gamma(1-L_{i^*},L_k)=-\gamma_{i^*,k}\) and the outer \(b\), it contributes \(-\varepsilon(a)\prod_{n'<n}(1-L_{n'}(a))\,a_{nk}(a)b_{nk}(a)\,\gamma_{i^*,k}\). (4) \(n_0\) exists, the final line translating to one; \(\prod_{n'<n}(1-L_{n'}(a))\) is one for \(n\le n_0\) and zero after. For \(n\le n_0\) evaluate line \(n\)'s identity at \(a\): \(R_n(a)=0\), every non-input premise is earlier than \(n\) and so has value zero, and an input premise has value one exactly when it is \(i^*\) with \(\varepsilon(a)=1\); so the sum of \(a_{nj}(a)b_{nj}(a)\) over the slots citing \(i^*\) is \(L_n(a)\), zero for \(n<n_0\) and one at \(n_0\), and summing (3) over the lines the Booleanity parts give \(-\varepsilon(a)\beta_{i^*}\). The slots citing \(k<i^*\) meet a premise of value zero, so the identity does not constrain their sum, which is \(w_k(a)\); summing (3) gives \(-\varepsilon(a)\sum_kw_k(a)\gamma_{i^*,k}\). (5) \(A_t\) is, by the used-node record's definition, "the compiler's sum of contributions from the tag proofs and those rounds"; the tag-proof lines translate to zero and their recursion "gives zero"; round zero gives (1); in round one the copy of input \(k\) is a weakening citing round zero's \(k\), identity \(L=1\cdot L_k\cdot1\), so (3) applies with round zero after \(k\) and round one's earlier copies in the place of the non-input lines: the copy of \(i^*\) gives \(-\varepsilon(a)\beta_{i^*}\), each copy of \(k<i^*\) gives \(-\varepsilon(a)\gamma_{i^*,k}\), its earlier copies having values zero, so \(w_k=1\); every later round's terms have \(U\) beginning with round zero's product, zero at \(a\). (6) Every member of the envelope is \(A_t\) plus a left multiple of \(P^t\) (lem:matched-jet-envelope, \(P=\prod_k(1-L_k)\)), and \(P(a)=0\). \(\square\)
