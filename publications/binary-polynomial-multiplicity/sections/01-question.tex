\section{The question and the standard estimate}

All degrees below are ordinary total degrees of formal polynomials.
For a nonzero polynomial \(P\in\F[x_1,\ldots,x_n]\) and \(a\in\F^n\),
write
\[
 P(a+z)=\sum_{\alpha\in\mathbb Z_{\ge0}^n}c_\alpha z^\alpha.
\]
Its multiplicity at \(a\) is
\[
 \mult_a(P)=\min\{|\alpha|:c_\alpha\ne0\},
 \qquad |\alpha|=\alpha_1+\cdots+\alpha_n.
\]
This is Hasse multiplicity: all Hasse derivatives of total order less
than \(\mult_a(P)\) vanish at \(a\). We set \(\mult_a(0)=\infty\).
In particular, a nonvanishing value has multiplicity zero. We do not
reduce polynomials modulo the field equations, since that operation
need not preserve multiplicities.

In the published version of \emph{Subspace coverings with multiplicities},
Bishnoi, Boyadzhiyska, Das and M\'esz\'aros~\cite[Question~4.3]{BBDM23}
ask the following: for positive integers \(n,k\) with \(k\ge2^{n-2}\),
if \(P\in\F_2[x_1,\ldots,x_n]\) satisfies
\begin{equation}\label{eq:hypotheses}
 \mult_a(P)\ge k\quad(a\in\F_2^n\setminus\{\zero\}),
 \qquad \mult_\zero(P)\le k-1,
\end{equation}
must one have
\begin{equation}\label{eq:question-bound}
 \deg P\ge 2k-\left\lfloor\frac{k}{2^{n-1}}\right\rfloor\,?
\end{equation}
The question is numbered~5.2 in the earlier arXiv version~\cite{BBDM21}.
The distinction matters because Question~4.3 in that version concerns
a different covering problem.

The relevant estimate is already available in the work of Dvir,
Kopparty, Saraf and Sudan~\cite[Definition~2.2 and Lemma~2.7]{DKSS13}.

\begin{lemma}[Multiplicity Schwartz--Zippel]\label{lem:msz}
Let \(\F\) be any field, \(n\ge1\), and let
\(P\in\F[x_1,\ldots,x_n]\) be a nonzero polynomial of total degree \(d\).
For every finite nonempty set \(S\subseteq\F\),
\begin{equation}\label{eq:msz}
 \sum_{a\in S^n}\mult_a(P)\le d\,|S|^{n-1}.
\end{equation}
\end{lemma}
There is no restriction relating \(d\) to the field size in this lemma.
