Compression and complete blocks

\[P_C=1-\sum_{j\in B_C}r_{C,j}t_j,\qquad E_{C,j}=t_jP_C.\]

\[R_A=P_T-r_ft_fP_T=0,\qquad R_B=P_T-r_gt_gP_T=0\]

Relative independent-set count

Let \(G\) be a simple graph on \(s\) vertices of maximum degree \(\Delta\), and let \(H\) consist of \(R\) vertex-disjoint pairs that are not edges of \(G\). Write \(Z_t(J)\) for the number of independent \(t\)-sets of graph \(J\). Put \(D=\Delta+1\), and suppose

\[2\le k\le\frac{s}{8(D+1)}.\]

Then

\[\frac{Z_k(G+H)}{Z_k(G)}\le\left(1-\frac{k(k-1)}{2s^2}\right)^R\le\exp\left(-\frac{Rk(k-1)}{2s^2}\right).\tag{RH-relative}\]

All denominators are positive: the greedy extension count below constructs independent sets up to the stated size. This is an estimate relative to the original graph’s independent sets, not to \(\binom sk\).

Proof by successive pair exclusions

For any graph \(J\) of maximum degree at most \(D\), count extensions of independent \((t-1)\)-sets. Each has at least \(s-(t-1)(D+1)\) available vertices. Hence
