### 552 obs:unary-to-bit-Frege-encoding
unary-to-bit-Frege-encoding-clarification  3. What does transfer from bit PHP to ordinary PHP? 
    Standard reduction, clarified.  Let \(n=2^\ell\), and let \(X_{ij}\) be the variables of ordinary unary PHP. In a depth-\(d\) \(\mathrm{AC}^0[p]\)-Frege refutation, substitute 
   \[
    X_{ij}\longmapsto \Lambda_{ij}
      :=\bigwedge_{t=1}^{\ell}
        \begin{cases}b_{it},&j_t=1,\\ \neg b_{it},&j_t=0.\end{cases}
    \tag{PUB-label-substitution}
  \] 
   This is the familiar bit-label substitution, also used explicitly by Bonacina and Bonet,   On the strength of Sherali-Adams and Nullstellensatz as propositional proof systems , Definition 4.11 and the proof of Corollary 4.12, p. 18 . Its use here is a clarification of the route, not a claim of a new encoding lemma. 
    Argument.  A unary collision clause \(\neg X_{ij}\vee\neg X_{i'j}\) becomes exactly the bit-PHP clause forbidding rows \(i,i'\) from both encoding \(j\), after De Morgan expansion. A pigeon axiom becomes the tautology \(T_i=\bigvee_{j<n}\Lambda_{ij}\). This tautology has a polynomial-size, constant-formula-depth Frege derivation: at every complete assignment to the \(\ell\) row bits, the corresponding disjunct of \(T_i\) is true; derive the clause consisting of \(T_i\) and the negations of that assignment's literals, then cut upward through the full binary assignment tree. There are \(O(2^\ell)=O(n)\) tree nodes, and each line has polynomial length and bounded formula depth. This is an ordinary short truth-table derivation on only \(\ell=\log_2n\) variables, not on the entire PHP variable set. 
   Uniform substitution preserves the Frege inference rules, including MOD\(_p\) occurrences, with polynomial size overhead and formula depth \(d+O(1)\). Replace each substituted pigeon axiom by the preceding derivation and each substituted collision axiom by its bit-PHP clause. Therefore a polynomial-size ordinary-PHP refutation at fixed depth would yield a polynomial-size bit-PHP refutation at another fixed depth. Consequently, bit-PHP lower bounds at  every  fixed \(\mathrm{AC}^0[p]\)-Frege depth suffice for the ordinary-PHP goal. 
   The powers-of-two restriction does not obstruct that implication. For a general \(N\), let \(n\le N&lt;2n\) be a power of two. Assign the \(N-n\) extra pigeons to distinct extra holes and set surviving-pigeon variables in those holes to zero. Restricting unary PHP on \(N+1\) pigeons and \(N\) holes leaves unary PHP on \(n+1\) pigeons and \(n\) holes. Restriction preserves proof size and bounded depth. 
    Why the present subsystem bound is insufficient.  Under (PUB-label-substitution), an affine parity \(\bigoplus X_{ij}\) becomes a parity of bit conjunctions, generally a nonlinear polynomial in the bits. That is outside the affine-query language of Res(\(\oplus\)). No polynomial-size Res(\(\oplus\)) conversion follows from this substitution. Likewise, the  existing source obstruction  concerns preservation of a restricted selector-rank hypothesis, not absence of the standard Frege reduction. Earlier references to an “ordinary-unary conversion gap” should be read with this distinction; this dated clarification leaves their original statements intact.
