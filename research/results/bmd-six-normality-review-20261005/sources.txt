Primary source statements read by the coordinator for the draft's attributions (paraphrased):
Tate, A review of non-Archimedean elliptic functions, pp.14-15: rational functions on the Tate elliptic curve are multiplicatively periodic meromorphic functions, expressed as quotients of theta functions with equal automorphy. Positive-degree theta spaces have that degree as dimension; degree-zero spaces vanish unless the automorphy is a period power. The precise Hasse principal-basis construction used here is proved in paired.txt.
Rosengren-Schlosser, Corollary 4.7, p.12, at https://www.mat.univie.ac.at/~schlosse/pdf/determinants-new.pdf: det[theta(t*a_j*x_i)/(theta(t)*theta(a_j*x_i))] equals theta(t*product(a_i*x_i))/theta(t) times product_(i<j)[a_j*x_j*theta(a_i/a_j)*theta(x_i/x_j)] divided by product_(i,j)theta(a_j*x_i). Independent indeterminates and the analytic theta setting. Draft imports no characteristic-p nonvanishing; its rational Hasse control is proved directly.
Stacks Lemma53.5.2 https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/0B5B: Riemann-Roch with duality for proper Gorenstein pure dimension-one schemes. For genus-one smooth curves, chi(L)=deg(L). Lemma33.44.17 https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/0AYQ: on a proper curve over an algebraically closed field, deg(L)>=2g-1 implies H1(L)=0.
Dusson-Sigal-Stamm Theorem1.2 pp.4-5 at https://arxiv.org/pdf/2105.02058: for complementary projections and invertible eliminated block, the bounded Feshbach map has the same zero-kernel dimension, and the full kernel vector is recovered by the eliminated-block inverse. The draft separately proves the finite-dimensional algebraic version over any field; it imports no analytic positivity or spectral estimates.
Chaiken 1982 https://epubs.siam.org/doi/10.1137/0603033 theorem statement: complementary Laplacian minors enumerate signed rooted forests matching the deleted vertex sets. The draft's test needs only the directly computed two-by-two reduced Laplacian of a triangle, determinant three; it makes no additional forest-identity claim.
