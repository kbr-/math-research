Quadratic critical gap

Proposition (quadratic critical gap). Over \(\mathbb F_2\), let \(J_D\) be a proper Boolean degree-\(D\) pseudoideal containing an unsatisfiable old family \(\Gamma\). Put \(u=\max(2,\max_{\gamma\in\Gamma}\deg\gamma)\). Fix \(r\ge1\) and \(D\ge\max(u+2,4r+3)\). There exist distinct old coordinates \(z=(x_1,y_1,\ldots,x_r,y_r)\) whose entire Boolean coordinate algebra injects into the old quotient. Set \[ Q_r=\sum_{i=1}^r x_i y_i,\quad A=Q_r,\quad \Omega=\{z\in\mathbb F_2^{2r}:Q_r(z)=1\}. \] Let \(U\) be the full multilinear coordinate space on these bits, of degree ceiling \(K=2r\). Then \(A\) survives and \[ H=\dim[AU]=|\Omega|=2^{2r-1}-2^{r-1}. \] For a binary \(h\times2r\) matrix \(C\), \(0\le h\le r\), write \(P_C=\prod_{j=1}^h(1-(Cz)_j)\), and let \(W=\{([Az_i f])_i:f\in U\}\). Enlarging \(U\) by input words of length at most \(h\) leaves its image unchanged after paid Boolean reduction. Writing \(V_D=B_{\le D}/J_D\) for the quotient vector space, \[ \operatorname{rank}\bigl(f\mapsto([Az_iP_Cf])_i\bigr) =\dim(W\cap(\ker C\otimes V_D))=|\Omega\cap\ker C|, \] and \[ \min_C|\Omega\cap\ker C|=2^{2r-h-1}-2^{r-1}. \tag{1} \] Thus the fixed-weight scalar critical exponent is \(r\). Yet an accuracy-\(h\) complete block on the raw tuple \(z\), \(h\ge1\), has a degree-one polynomial coefficient map killing all \(A\)-weighted companions through degree five. A scalar map and surviving weight \(Ax_1\) do so too. Their complete original degree-\(B\) replay costs are \(B+2\) and \(B+3\), respectively, for \(B\ge\max(u,2h+1)\); these ceilings must fit \(D\).

Embedding and ranks. The weighted restriction cube with weight one supplies \(D-1\) free coordinates; select \(2r\). Injectivity applies to all multilinear functions on those coordinates, not arbitrary outside actions. Hence \([AU]\) is the function space on \(\Omega\). At every point of \(\Omega\), \(z\ne0\), so multiplication by the vector \(z\) embeds it as \(W\). Contraction with \(C\) kills a value exactly on \(\ker C\). Also \(P_C\) is the indicator of \(\ker C\). Both ranks therefore count \(\Omega\cap\ker C\). This equality of actual rank and intersection is special to this model. The tensor expression asserts no untruncated quotient algebra. All enlarged input words reduce to functions in \(U\). Actual weighted products have degree at most \(2r+h+3\); the original accuracy-\(h\) companion has degree \(2h+1\), and its product with \(Af\) costs at most \(2r+2h+3\le4r+3\). These ceilings pay every reduction.

Exact count. The polar form \(\mathcal B(s,t)=Q_r(s+t)+Q_r(s)+Q_r(t)\) is nondegenerate alternating. Let \(L=\ker C\), \(j=\operatorname{rank}C\), \(d=2r-j\), and \(R=\operatorname{rad}(\mathcal B|_L)\). Since \(R\subseteq L^\perp\), \(\dim R\le j\). The integer sum \(S_L=\sum_{z\in L}(-1)^{Q_r(z)}\) obeys \[ S_L^2=2^d\sum_{t\in R}(-1)^{Q_r(t)}. \] Expand the square and substitute the second variable by \(z+t\); the character sum in \(z\) vanishes unless \(t\in R\). The restriction \(Q_r|_R\) is linear, so the remaining sum is zero or \(2^{\dim R}\). Conditioned relation. In the Boolean ring, \[ J^{[A]}_{D-2}=\{f:\deg f\le D-2,\ \operatorname{ML}(Af)\in J_D\} \] is proper, contains \(\Gamma\), and is closed under legal variable multiplication, with the two extra degrees paid. It contains \(Q_r-1\), by the following literal certificate. This is a degree-two unit relation in a proper conditioned kernel. We do not assert \(Q_r-1\in J_D\) or a global satisfying assignment of \(\Gamma\).

Affine coefficient map. In row one set the coefficient of \(x_i\) to \(y_i\) and that of \(y_i\) to zero. Set later coefficients to zero. The row value is \(Q_r\), the prefix \(1-Q_r\), and \[ Q_r^2-Q_r=\sum_i\bigl[y_i^2(x_i^2-x_i)+x_i(y_i^2-y_i)\bigr]. \tag{2} \] This certificate has degree four. Multiplication by \(z_j\) kills each \(Az_j(1-Q_r)\) through degree five. Weighted coefficient fields \(A(\beta^2-\beta)\) cost at most four. The global substitution has degree one. The original product costs \(2h\), companion \(2h+1\), and weighted companion \(2h+3\ge5\). Padding does not reset these costs. Multiplying every substituted line of a degree-\(B\) original PC derivation by \(A\) costs at most \(B+2\), including old generators and inference steps.

Scalar repair. Choose row one equal to \(x_1\), zero-pad, and change the weight to \(Ax_1\). It survives: \(x_1=y_1=1\), other selected bits zero, is a nonzero value on the injected cube. Each \(Ax_1z_j(1-x_1)\) is a Boolean-equation multiple through degree five. Coefficient fields vanish identically; replay costs \(B+3\). Such a weight change is allowed by the real goal, so the large fixed-weight critical exponent is not a removal lower bound. Moreover, historical one-block elimination gives the stronger generic local transfer \(B+1\) for degree-one inputs. \(\square\)
