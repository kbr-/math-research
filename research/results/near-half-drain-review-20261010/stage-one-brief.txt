GOAL (side notebook near-half-pc-degree)
Superpolynomial size lower bounds for AC0[2]-Frege at every fixed depth.

PROVED TRANSFER (working proof, thm:near-half-frege-transfer): it suffices to construct polynomial-length
unsatisfiable literal CNFs F_n of width w_n, together with auxiliary affine equations Lambda_n over F_2 (which
need not be part of the formula), of free dimension n -> infinity (number of variables minus rank of the affine
equations), such that clause polynomials + Boolean axioms + Lambda_n have no polynomial-calculus refutation over
F_2 of degree floor(n/2 - s_n), with s_n + w_n <= n^(1-eps). (For a single depth d, (s+w)(log n)^(d+c-2) = o(n)
suffices.) Equivalent pure form (prop:form-clause-encoding): polynomially many affine subspaces of F_2^n of
codimension <= n^(1-eps) covering F_2^n whose indicator polynomials, with Boolean axioms, do not generate 1
within n^(1-eps) of degree n/2.

THE ONLY OPEN STATEMENT: such a family. Unchanged for 51 research entries.

AVAILABLE RESULTS (all over F_2, multilinear PC with Boolean axioms; "degree" = refutation degree; a "row" is the
indicator polynomial of an affine subspace of codimension w; a "cover" is a set of rows whose subspaces cover F_2^n)
Upper bounds / exclusions:
- Affine equations are free: only the free dimension counts. Degree is invariant under invertible affine
  coordinate changes (lem:affine-coordinate-invariance).
- prop:transversal-rank-ceiling: forms of rank rho making every row affine give degree <= rho + w.
- Two-bit four-colouring of any graph: degree <= n/2 + 2. Every concrete non-4-colourable family examined falls
  in constant or logarithmic degree (affine consequences of bounded subgraphs; four-clique sums; game bound).
  Topological screen: antipodal chains on the box complex give degree <= 10; graphs without K_{2,3} have no such
  chains, and absence of chains says nothing about degree (prop:bipartite-free-chain-bound).
- Covers: sum_r 2^(-w_r) >= 1, so polynomially many rows cover in measure by rows of codimension O(log n); an
  exact partition (or any odd cover) is refuted in degree w; a cover in which every point lies in at most c rows
  is refuted in degree c*w; refuting = deriving the degree-<=w indicator of the evenly covered set
  (lem:cover-sum-gap). The multiples of one row within degree D span exactly C(n-w, <=D-w) dimensions, so the
  static span of M rows is proper by dimension below the least D with M*C(n-w,<=D-w) >= C(n,<=D); near
  D = n/2 - s this needs covering sum 1 + O(s w / n). The semi-regular series (1+z)^n/prod(1+z^w) is NOT a model
  for subspace rows.
- Rank expansion for rows on linear forms: "the forms of every r rows are independent" gives no degree bound at
  widths 1, 2 even for r linear in n, and none uniformly in r at any width (three counterexamples).
- Invariant family: for p = n+1 prime with 2 a primitive root, F_{2^n} = two-colourings of Z_p modulo complement
  with AGL(1,p) acting; orbit covers exist for w <= log2(n+1). At n = 18 a single-orbit cover (342 rows, w = 4)
  has maximal static span at every degree and refutation degree exactly 6 (n/2 = 9); at n = 10 degree 4.
  Translates of a multiplier-invariant subspace with covering sum < 2 do not cover (p = 13, 19).
- Lifted spread: 2^w + 1 subspaces of codimension w through 0 with a common core cover with sum 1 + 2^-w; easy.
- Known lower-bound methods: expansion (Alekhnovich-Razborov pseudo-reduction) for rows on variables; in the
  random models examined its certified degree stays below about n/4.
- Impagliazzo-Pudlak-Sgall read: subset-sum ceiling n/2 + 1 is special to one linear axiom and void over F_2;
  its lower bound is over the reals.
- Block-field binomial systems (n = mk bits in m blocks read as nonzero elements of F_{2^k}; rows are clause sets
  of multiplicative equations prod y_i^{c_i} = gamma; includes Tseitin-type flow systems modulo 2^k - 1): every
  unsatisfiable one has degree <= min{n/3, n/4 + w} + o(n) (prop:block-binomial-third-ceiling, -quarter-ceiling;
  derive an equivalence of two monomials where both are light, then carry it by multiplying by variables). Their
  degree-D closure is exactly a labelled equivalence on exponent vectors of weight <= D generated by row steps
  and translation of light pairs (lem:binomial-closure-classes); at a proper D all vectors of weight <= D-k+1 are
  live and each level has at least as many standard monomials as cosets of the row lattice of that least weight
  (lem:binomial-coset-level-bound). All such systems small enough to enumerate fall at their width.
- Restriction: adding an affine equation with a suitably chosen right-hand side lowers the degree by at most 1;
  a subspace raises degree/dimension only if restriction loses less degree per dimension than the current ratio.
- Where "half" enters the transfer: its engine contracts an error rank c(K) (a count of standard monomials of
  degree <= K of a down-closed family inside the closure's standard monomials) by a factor 1 - c(K-d)/(2c(K)) per
  round, d polylogarithmic, and the only general lower bound for c(K-d)/c(K) is the binomial ratio
  N_n(K-d)/N_n(K), which is close to 1 only for K near n/2. Proved alternative (thm:level-size-degree-transfer):
  it suffices that the closure is proper at D and its standard monomials of degree j just below a cutoff K < D
  number at most binom(X, j) with X about 2K ("thin closure"); then K may be far below n/2. Necessary for it: no
  set of more than about 2K variables is shattered through degree K (prop:shattered-dimension-bound), while a
  proper closure shatters some set of D-1 variables. No system with a thin proper closure is known; product
  frames (points in general position per block) do not beat ratio one half of their own top degree.
- Cited from a search summary, not read: Buss-Grigoriev-Impagliazzo-Pitassi, Tseitin mod p on an expander has
  PC degree >= eps*V/8 over fields of other characteristic (multiplicative variables).
- The engine's families (lem:engine-colon-families): its counts come from truncated colons (C_D : P g) and, when
  the inputs generate 1, from the closure itself; any rate better than the ambient binomial one needs the
  closure's own counts h_k = dim V_k/(C_D cap V_k) to plateau below the cutoff. A proper closure at D has level
  sizes at least binom(D-1, j) (thm:truncated-kernel-dimension), so a thin proper closure has levels between
  those of cubes on D-1 and about 2K points. Derived affine consequences may be counted in the free dimension.
- lem:barely-covering-padding: any cover of a codimension-w subspace pads to a cover of the whole space with
  covering sum 1 + 2^-w(Sigma - 1) and at least the same degree; covering sum near one carries no information.
- Table rows (a row = all clauses on a scope of k variables, allowed set R): the degree-D part of the row's
  ideal has dimension sum_i (binom(k,i) - b_i(R)) N_{v-k}(D-i), b_i the level sizes of R, and the span K_D of
  these parts lies in the PC closure at degree max{k,D} (prop:table-row-span). At scale K_D is far from the
  whole space (lem:top-coefficient-functionals). In nine random systems with v <= 20 the constant 1 enters K_D
  exactly at the first non-positive index of the "product series" (1+z)^v prod_a h_a(z)/(1+z)^k, h_a the level
  polynomial; at those sizes this tests only the first-order term (conj:table-series-unit-degree).
- The product series as a model. Its index is conjectured to be v times the maximum of the saddle function
  Phi(alpha) = alpha - sum_j mu_j alpha(1-alpha) q_j'(alpha)/(1-q_j(alpha)), q_j the alpha-biased weight of the
  non-standard monomials of row type j, mu_j rows per variable (exact indices at v = 2048 exceed it by 0.009v
  to 0.016v in eight cases). Proved about Phi: for clause rows on k variables at first-moment density
  sup Phi = 1/2 - ln k/k + O(1/k) (prop:clause-saddle-value); for half-tables it is about 1/2 - sqrt(ln k/2k);
  for ANY mixture of rows on at most k variables, each forbidding at most half, with covering sum at least
  v/2, Phi <= 1/2 - ln k/(2k) + 0.47/k on (0,1/2) (thm:saddle-deficit-bound, via the biased edge-isoperimetric
  inequality, proved in the record). So in the model, systems of polynomial length built from independent-like
  rows on O(log v) variables have deficit of order at least v ln ln v/ln v: enough for the transfer at two
  levels (deficit o(n)), not at three (needs o(n/log n)). The model does not apply to covers with small
  covering sum (rows dependent) or to rows on many variables. No lower bound on PC degree is involved: the
  model would give upper bounds on degree if its two conjectures held.

    m = 3n); injectivity lifts from the projection modulo a vector (hardest case = fewest variables). The full

  * cor:two-level-regularity-reduction (conditional): rows of width k -> infinity, 2^k polynomial, unsatisfiable
