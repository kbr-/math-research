# Primary-source audit for the proof-incidence review

Accessed 8 October 2026 through the web tool. Full third-party text was not added to the repository.

- Britz, Heiseldal, Johnsen, Mayhew, Shiromoto, *Generalizations of Wei's Duality Theorem*, arXiv:0910.2099v1: https://arxiv.org/pdf/0910.2099 . Read Section 3.4 definitions, Proposition 28 and Theorem 29, and the underlying duality statement. Scope: support-weight duality, not a labelled multiplication-rank theorem.
- Berkesch and Schreyer, *Syzygies, finite length modules, and random curves*, arXiv:1403.0581: https://arxiv.org/pdf/1403.0581 . Read the module division setup, construction (1.3), induced order (1.4), and Corollary 1.11 with proof. A supplied module-image Groebner basis is a hypothesis.
- Joellenbeck and Welker, *Minimal Resolutions via Algebraic Discrete Morse Theory*, author preprint: https://www.mathematik.uni-marburg.de/~welker/preprints/kres.pdf . Read Chapter 2 through Theorem 2.2 and its finite-matching scope. The matching weights must be central units; path products enter the differential. No general degree bound is asserted by that theorem.
- Golovin and Krause, *Adaptive Submodularity: A New Approach to Active Learning and Stochastic Optimization*, author copy: https://www.cs.cmu.edu/~dgolovin/papers/colt10.pdf . Read Definitions 1-2 and Theorem 4, with the deterministic diminishing-returns discussion. The raw eliminated-rank objective fails that hypothesis by the existing prefix plateau.
- Bravyi and Terhal, *A no-go theorem for a two-dimensional self-correcting quantum memory based on stabilizer codes*, arXiv:0810.1983: https://arxiv.org/pdf/0810.1983 . Read Lemma 1 and its proof, plus the subsystem caveat. It is a qubit result; the recorded qutrit calculation is a direct test of a proposed translation, not an application to the charged binary pseudoideal.

Exploratory web searches also returned random-CSP/PC references already covered by the existing expansion review. No new theorem from those search snippets is used.
