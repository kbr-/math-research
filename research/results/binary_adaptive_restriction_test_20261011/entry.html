Exact mass and legal postselection

For a real-valued function on the full ternary label cube, let \(\|F\|_\Box\) be the infimum of \(\sum_\nu|\lambda_\nu|\) over finite identities \(F=\sum_\nu\lambda_\nu\mathbf1_{B_\nu}\), with \(B_\nu\) a Cartesian product of coordinate subsets. These indicators are exactly the categorical width-one CNFs, including constant functions. This norm is not a rank in the charged pseudoideal.

Fix once and for all one complete binary ENS block of accuracy \(h\ge1\), with one input \(z_e=[X_e=0]\) per original edge. Its original product cost is \(2h\), companion cost \(2h+1\), and all fresh coefficient equations are retained. On any restriction leaving at least \(2h\) edges live, order the first \(2h\) of them as pairs \((x_i,y_i)\). Choose row \(i\) to select those two input positions, all other row entries zero. This is one shared scalar substitution, not separate choices per error. Its evaluated product and one of its actual companions are \[P=\prod_{i=1}^h(1+x_i+y_i),\qquad E=x_1P,\] where the product is evaluated over \(\mathbb F_2\) and its resulting bit is regarded as a real function. Every other original companion remains in the tuple; coefficient fields vanish under these zero/one choices.

Proposition. For this companion, \(\|E\|_\Box=2^{h-1}\). Under the hard restriction theorem's hypotheses, write \(t=p\Delta\), \(D_H=\lfloor tn/128\rfloor\), and assume \(K\ge0\), \(K+B\le D_H\), \(B\ge2h+1\). Then this policy is available on every good restriction, and the sum \(\Psi\) of the width-one masses of all \(m\) original companion images is at least \(2^{h-1}\) there. All original degree and field costs are respected.

For comparison, let \(\mathcal C\) be any nonempty finite family of fixed scalar row choices whose \(m\) companion bit functions each have categorical CNF width at most two before restriction. If \(p<1/4\), put \(\theta=(3p/(1-p))^2<1\). Then \[\mathbb E\min_{C\in\mathcal C}\Psi_C(\rho)\le\frac{m}{1-\theta},\qquad \mathbb E[\min_{C\in\mathcal C}\Psi_C(\rho)\mid G]\le\frac{m}{(1-\theta)\Pr(G)},\] where \(G\) is the good graph event. In particular \(\Pr(G)\ge1-\eta\) gives the recorded conditional allowance. Taking \(\mathcal C\) to contain every fixed disjoint-pair choice and the all-zero matrix makes both the bad policy and this minimum comparison concern the same fixed input tuple and permissible family.

Proof of exact mass. The error requires the first two labels to be zero. For every other pair it requires either both labels zero or both labels nonzero. Expanding these \(h-1\) disjoint alternatives writes \(E\) as a sum of \(2^{h-1}\) rectangle indicators with coefficient one, proving the upper bound. For the lower bound fix the first pair to zero and all unused coordinates arbitrarily. Restrict each remaining label to \(\{0,1\}\), and define \[\Lambda(F)=\sum_{a\in\{0,1\}^{2h-2}}(-1)^{\sum_j a_j}F(0,0,a).\] For each rectangle, this factors into \(\prod_j(\mathbf1_{B_j}(0)-\mathbf1_{B_j}(1))\), times the fixed-coordinate indicators, so its absolute value is at most one. Each equal pair contributes two to \(\Lambda(E)\), hence \(\Lambda(E)=2^{h-1}\). Applying \(\Lambda\) to any signed expansion proves the lower bound. For \(h=1\) the empty product is the point-evaluation functional and gives one. The argument permits arbitrary real coefficients and arbitrary unary allowed-label sets; it is not limited to one particular path expansion.

Hard-event and cost proof. On \(G\), the live graph has minimum degree at least \(t/8\), hence at least \(nt/16\) live edges. The original-cost assumptions imply \(2h<D_H\le nt/128\), so enough live pairs exist. The row policy only selects constants in original positions; restricting old variables and substituting these coefficients do not increase proof degree. Each companion still carries original cost \(2h+1\), although the specialized polynomial may have smaller degree. Thus the usual common multiplier window is \(K+B\le D_H\), without resetting the compiler's original ceiling. The selected component's exact mass lower-bounds the sum of every component's nonnegative mass. This holds for every fixed-label assignment on \(G\), not merely a favorable sample.

Minimum proof. Fix any \(C_0\in\mathcal C\) before sampling. The categorical restriction theorem gives \(\mathbb E\|E_{e,C_0}|_\rho\|_\Box\le(1-\theta)^{-1}\) for each original companion, so summing gives \(\mathbb E\Psi_{C_0}\le m/(1-\theta)\). Pointwise \(\min_C\Psi_C\le\Psi_{C_0}\), proving the first assertion. Multiply that inequality by \(\mathbf1_G\), bound its expectation by the unconditional expectation, and divide by \(\Pr(G)\). No independence of \(G\) and the mass is assumed. A fixed pair choice has width-two companion CNFs by the old paired-prefix proof; the all-zero choice has unary companions. Finite minimization can be computationally expensive; no efficient algorithm is claimed. \(\square\)

What this says in the actual parameter range

Use the existing nonempty growing family with \(\Delta=\lceil n^\alpha\rceil\), \(p=n^{-\beta}\), \(0<\beta<\alpha<1\), and \(\alpha(d-1)+\beta<1\). Its original encoding satisfies \(\log N=\Theta(n^\alpha)\), \(m\le n\Delta\), and \(D_H=\Theta(n^{1+\alpha-\beta})\). Choose \(h=\Theta_{A,d}(n^\alpha)\) in the compiler's allowed accuracy range, with \(B=O_{A,d}(n^{\alpha d})\) and a remaining \(K\) such that \(K+B\le D_H\). The exponent inequality leaves this window nonempty for every fixed \(A,d\). Then the fixed-choice conditional allowance is \(O(m)\), while every good restriction under the displayed adaptive policy has \(\Psi\ge2^{h-1}\). This is an admissible source-block control in the goal's window, not a claim that a hypothetical small proof must make that bad choice.

The source family is particularly easy to improve. If a fixed edge has label zero, its input is the constant one after restriction; selecting it in one row makes \(P=0\) and kills all companions. The event that no fixed edge has label zero has probability \(((2+p)/3)^m\). Conditional on \(G\), its probability is at most that quantity divided by \(\Pr(G)\). This is a separate good policy in the full legal scalar class. Even without this event, the old paired-prefix record supplies a cheap surviving-weight repair at its free-cube hypotheses. Neither repair is a source-wide statement for arbitrary mixtures. These positive controls prevent turning an adversarial policy into an optimized lower bound.
