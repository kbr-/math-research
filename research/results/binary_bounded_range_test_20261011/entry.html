Certified range exclusion

Theorem. Over a fixed prime field \(\mathbb F_p\), let features \(U_1(x),\ldots,U_r(x)\) have circuits and linear-placeholder certificates \(K_j(x,Z)\) with \(K_j(x,0)=0\) and \(K_j(x,B)=U_j^2-U_j\), where \(B_l=x_l^2-x_l\). Let \(\beta\) lie outside the residues \(\{0,\ldots,r\}\). Then \(F=\sum_jU_j-\beta\) has a full Hilbert-like IPS refutation of size polynomial in the supplied total size plus \(O(r^2 2^r)\), and depth the maximum supplied depth plus a constant. Gates are unbounded-fanin sums/products, counting gates and wires, without division or succinct powering. Cofactors need not be multilinear.

Proof. For \(\epsilon\in\{0,1\}^r\), let \(c_\epsilon=\sum_j\epsilon_j-\beta\ne0\), and write \[ E_\epsilon(z)=\prod_j\bigl(\epsilon_jz_j+(1-\epsilon_j)(1-z_j)\bigr),\qquad D(z)=\sum_\epsilon c_\epsilon^{-1}E_\epsilon(z). \] Since \(\sum_\epsilon E_\epsilon=1\), one has \[ D(z)(\sum_jz_j-\beta)-1=\sum_j a_j(z)(z_j^2-z_j), \quad a_j(z)=\sum_\epsilon c_\epsilon^{-1}(2\epsilon_j-1) \prod_{l\ne j}\bigl(\epsilon_lz_l+(1-\epsilon_l)(1-z_l)\bigr). \] Indeed \(E_\epsilon(z)(z_j-\epsilon_j)\) is the corresponding signed Boolean axiom times the product omitting \(j\). Thus \[ C(x,Y,Z)=D(U)Y-\sum_j a_j(U)K_j(x,Z) \] vanishes at zero placeholders and evaluates to one at \((Y,Z)=(F,B)\). Sharing the supplied circuits and the explicit selector products proves the size and depth bounds. \(\square\)

No independence of features is assumed. Repetitions and overlaps are allowed. If \(r=0\), this is the nonzero constant equation \(-\beta=0\). In \(\mathbb F_p\), the required \(\beta\) does not exist for \(r\ge p-1\); the excluded-value premise is literal residues, not formal integers.

Certify the written multilinearized features

Within each group \(i\), take atoms that are distinct \(x\)-variables or disjunction polynomials \(G_S=1-\prod_{y\in S}(1-y)\). After identifying duplicates, assume their variable supports are pairwise disjoint. Let \(S_i=\sum_\sigma M_{i,\sigma}\), where each \(M_{i,\sigma}\) is a squarefree product of atoms. Different groups may overlap arbitrarily. Put \(L_i=\operatorname{ML}(S_i^{p-1})\), with multilinear reduction in the original variables, and \(U_i=1-L_i\). For fixed \(p\), these \(U_i\) and their full Booleanity certificates have constant-depth, polynomial-size circuits in the explicit atom/term/literal description.

Atom witnesses. A variable atom uses its placeholder \(Z_y\). If \(E=\prod_a\ell_a\), with \(\ell_a=1-y_a\), define \[ K_E=\sum_a Z_{y_a}\prod_{b<a}\ell_b^2\prod_{b>a}\ell_b. \] It evaluates to \(E^2-E\). For \(G=1-E\), set \(K_G=K_E\), since \(G^2-G=E^2-E\). These circuits use \(O(|S|^2)\) wires and constant depth.

Reduction witnesses. Expand only the \(p-1\) copies of \(S_i\), giving at most \(N_i^{p-1}\) ordered term tuples, where \(N_i\) is its number of summands. Do not expand the disjunction atoms. A resulting term is \(t=\prod_bG_b^{a_b}\), \(1\le a_b\le p-1\). Its reduction is \(\bar t=\prod_bG_b\), since distinct atoms are multilinear with disjoint supports. Use \[ K_t=\sum_b K_{G_b}\left(\sum_{q=0}^{a_b-2}G_b^q\right) \prod_{c<b}G_c^{a_c}\prod_{c>b}G_c. \] An empty inner sum is zero. The identity \(G^a-G=(G^2-G)\sum_{q=0}^{a-2}G^q\) and product telescoping give \(K_t(x,B)=t-\bar t\). Summing over the ordered tuples produces \(R_i(x,Z)\) with \[ R_i(x,B)=S_i^{p-1}-L_i. \] For fixed \(p\), the tuple count and each factor list are polynomial in the description size. Powers are bounded by \(p-1\), and unbounded-fanin prefix/suffix products retain constant depth. This proves a special structured multilinearization, not a generic operation on arbitrary circuits.

Feature and aggregate witnesses. Let \(V_i=1-S_i^{p-1}\). The explicit Frobenius gate construction, reread in full, supplies \(K_{S_i}(x,B)=S_i^p-S_i\). Then \[ K_{U_i}=S_i^{p-2}K_{S_i}+(U_i+V_i-1)R_i \] evaluates to \(U_i^2-U_i\), using \(U_i-V_i=R_i(x,B)\) and \(V_i^2-V_i=S_i^{p-2}(S_i^p-S_i)\). For an aggregate \(A_j=\prod_{i\in I_j}U_i\), in any fixed order, \[ K_{A_j}=\sum_{i\in I_j}K_{U_i}\prod_{l<i}U_l^2\prod_{l>i}U_l. \] It certifies \(A_j^2-A_j\) without expanding the aggregate. Each witness is linear in the original Boolean placeholders, polynomial-size and constant-depth. Applying the theorem to a fixed number of aggregates gives the full certificate for \(\sum_jA_j-\beta\).
