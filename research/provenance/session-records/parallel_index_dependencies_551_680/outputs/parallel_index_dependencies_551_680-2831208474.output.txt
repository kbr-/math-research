
### 554 audit:additional-Res-parity-regularity-literature
publication-additional-regularity-literature  5. Supplement prompted by the user: the Dvořák work and subsequent bounds 
   Bhattacharya, Chattopadhyay, and Dvořák,   Exponential Separation Between Powers of Regular and General Resolution over Parities , CCC 2024 , give formulas exponentially hard for bottom-regular Res(\(\oplus\)) but with short proofs even in general ordinary Resolution. This is a separation from the regular subsystem; those formulas cannot witness a lower bound for unrestricted Res(\(\oplus\)). Their published abstract explicitly distinguishes the still-open unrestricted size problem. This comparison was missing from the preceding audit table and is added here. 
   Bhattacharya and Chattopadhyay's   Exponential Lower Bounds on the Size of ResLin Proofs of Nearly Quadratic Depth , ECCC TR25-106, 30 July 2025 , reaches nearly quadratic proof depth using lifted Tseitin formulas. It retains a depth restriction, and identifies ideas originating in the earlier three-author paper. Chattopadhyay and Dvořák's   Super-critical Trade-offs in Resolution over Parities Via Lifting , ECCC TR24-132 , concerns tree-like proofs with width restrictions. These are relevant advances, not unrestricted superpolynomial DAG-size theorems. 
   Together with the  Efremenko–Garlík–Itsykson and 2025–26 comparison , these additional primary sources leave the provisional novelty verdict unchanged. They also show why simply obtaining another regular or shallow-proof result would not resolve the stated benchmark. The candidate above explicitly removes those restrictions.
