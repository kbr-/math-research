Duplicate prefix rank law

Statement. Over \(\mathbb F_2\), let \(J_D\) be a degree-\(D\) pseudoideal containing old Booleanity, let \(\deg w\le b\), and let binary old polynomial \(g\) have degree at most \(\delta\ge1\). Take integers \(K\ge0\), \(h,M\ge1\) with \(b+K+(h+1)\delta\le D\). Assume \[c_0=\operatorname{rank}(f\in V_K\mapsto[wgf])>0.\] There are \(M\) distinct complete accuracy-\(h\) parent blocks, each with the same single interpreted input \(g\). For each row and parent choose independent uniform \(\xi_{a,d}\in\mathbb F_2\), set \(P_{a,j}=\prod_{d\le j}(1+\xi_{a,d}g)\), and define \[c_j=\operatorname{rank}(f\in V_K\mapsto([wP_{a,j}gf])_{a=1}^M).\] Then every realization has \(c_j\in\{0,c_0\}\), and \[\mathbb E c_j=c_0\bigl[1-(1-2^{-j})^M\bigr],\qquad 0\le j\le h.\] Given a history at \(0\le j<h\) with \(r>0\) parents whose first \(j\) coefficients are all zero, the next conditional expected joint rank is \((1-2^{-r})c_0\). Thus no positive contraction constant independent of \(M\) holds uniformly at the initial row. If every parent instead uses the same uniform bit at each round, \(\mathbb E c_j=2^{-j}c_0\). The legal deterministic assignment \(\xi_{a,1}=1\) for all parents makes every companion zero modulo old Booleanity in one row.

Complete proof and degree costs. A binary polynomial satisfies \(g^2+g\in(\mathrm{BOOL})\) through \(2\delta\), by termwise squaring and telescoping monomial powers. If a parent has any coefficient one among its first \(j\) rows, its companion \(gP_{a,j}\) contains \(g(1+g)\) and has a Boolean certificate through \((j+1)\delta\). Weighting and multiplying by \(f\in V_K\) costs at most \(b+K+(j+1)\delta\le D\). If all its coefficients are zero, its companion is literally \(g\). Hence the stacked map consists of zero coordinates and identical copies of \(f\mapsto[wgf]\). Its rank is zero exactly when every parent has been hit, and otherwise \(c_0\). One parent remains unhit with probability \(2^{-j}\); independence across parents gives the displayed probability that all are hit. Conditional on a history with \(r\) unhit parents, each is hit next with probability one half, independently, so all are hit with probability \(2^{-r}\). For synchronized rows there is one common unhit event, of probability \(2^{-j}\); the deterministic choice kills every block by the same Boolean certificate. Every chosen coefficient is a binary constant, so all fresh field equations vanish. Zero-padding after the first deterministic row does not reset any original source charge. This proves the law and its repair.
