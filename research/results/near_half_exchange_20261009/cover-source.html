Setting and statement

Let \(F\) be any field, and let \(t,k,c,D\) be integers with \(t\ge2,k\ge1,c\ge0\). Take disjoint private sets \(A_1,\ldots,A_t\), each of size \(k\), separators \(z_1,\ldots,z_{t-1}\), and \(c\) spectators. Write \(a_i=\prod_{x\in A_i}x\), \(P=\prod_{i=1}^t a_i\), and work in the Boolean function algebra \(\mathcal B\), filtered by multilinear degree. The original clause polynomials are

\[g_1=a_1(1-z_1),\qquad g_i=a_i z_{i-1}(1-z_i)\ (2\le iThese are canonical forbidden-assignment indicators on their scopes, hence a literal CNF with \(t\) clauses, widths at most \(k+2\), and \(kt+t-1+c\) declared Boolean coordinates, including the free spectators. For \(t=2\), both widths are \(k+1\). For \(S\subseteq[t]\), let \(J_S\) be the full Boolean vanishing ideal of the clauses indexed by \(S\), extended to all variables. Let \(V_D(S)\) be the images in \(\mathcal B\) of polynomials derivable in ordinary PC through original degree \(D\) from those clauses and all Boolean axioms. Thus \(V_D(S)\subseteq J_S\cap\mathcal B_{\le D}\). Put

\[K_D=\sum_{S\subsetneq[t]}(J_S\cap\mathcal B_{\le D}),\qquad E_D=\sum_{S\subsetneq[t]}V_D(S).\tag{2}\]

Theorem (guarded chain cover). If \(D\ge kt+1\) and \(c\ge D-kt\), select \(D-kt\) distinct spectators, write \(W\) for the product of the first \(D-kt-1\), and \(w\) for the last. Then

\[ WP\in E_D\cap\mathcal B_{\le D-1}\subseteq K_D,\qquad wWP\notin K_D,\qquad wWP\in V_D([t]).\tag{3}\]

Moreover \(1\notin K_D\). Consequently neither \(E_D\) nor \(K_D\) is closed under legal multiplication at cutoff \(D\), although every proper subfamily is included. Setting \(k=1,c=3,D=t+1\) gives \(v=2t+2\), \(D=v/2\), constant width at most three and \(t\) clauses. This is a satisfiable control, not a PC lower-bound instance.

Proof with original degrees

A simultaneous disabling assignment. For each proper \(S\), choose \(j\notin S\). Set \(z_i=1\) for \(i

A separating coefficient. Consider the coefficient of the monomial \(wWP\), which has degree exactly \(D\) and contains no separator. In any polynomial \(f\) of multilinear degree at most \(D\), no monomial containing a separator can contribute to that coefficient after specializing the separators: it would have to contain all \(D\) coordinates of \(wWP\) and at least one more coordinate. Hence this coefficient is unchanged by any separator specialization. The preceding disabling assignment shows that it is zero for every \(f\in J_S\cap\mathcal B_{\le D}\). It therefore vanishes on \(K_D\), but takes value one on \(wWP\). This proves the middle assertion in (3), over every field. The same argument gives \(P\notin K_{kt}\).

Prefix derivations. Put \(P_l=a_1\cdots a_l\) and \(F_l=P_l(1-z_l)\) for \(1\le l \[F_l=a_l(1-z_l)F_{l-1}+P_{l-1}g_l.\tag{4}\]

The two summands cancel their terms containing \(z_{l-1}\). Each right-hand product is derived by successive variable multiplications and linear combinations, interpreting multiplication by \(1-z_l\) as subtraction from the predecessor. The maximum ordinary degree is \(kl+2\); the previously derived prefix uses no more. Consequently the entire prefix \(F_{t-1}\) is derived from \(g_1,\ldots,g_{t-1}\) within degree \(kt+1\): for \(t=2\) its input degree is \(k+1\), and for \(t\ge3\), \(k(t-1)+2\le kt+1\). Finally,

\[P=a_tF_{t-1}+P_{t-1}g_t.\tag{5}\]

Both summands have degree \(kt+1\), but their sum has degree \(kt\). The first belongs to \(V_{kt+1}([t-1])\), by multiplying the prefix final line by the \(k\) variables of \(A_t\). The second is a monomial multiple of the last clause and belongs to \(V_{kt+1}(\{t\})\). Both subfamilies are proper. Multiplying the two derivations by the \(D-kt-1\) spectator variables of \(W\) yields \(WP\in E_D\), with every original degree at most \(D\). No multiplication of a degree-\(D\) predecessor is used.

Global reuse after the fall. Equation (5) is a global degree-\(kt+1\) derivation of the literal polynomial \(P\). Reuse this final line and multiply by the \(D-kt\) variables in \(wW\). The maximum of the preceding proof degree \(kt+1\) and the resulting degrees is \(D\). Thus \(wWP\in V_D([t])\), proving (3). All these displayed polynomial identities hold before Boolean reduction; the coefficient separation takes place in the unique multilinear image.

Unit survival. Set all private variables to zero. Since \(k\ge1\), this satisfies every clause, with arbitrary separators and spectators. Evaluation at this common model annihilates each \(J_S\) and therefore \(K_D\), and sends one to one. This proves the last assertion. \(\square\)
