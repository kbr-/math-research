Exact matrix erasure

The full dependency proof, not merely its jet statement, establishes \[ C_{\widehat\pi}-A_k=P^kQ,\qquad P=\prod_{i=1}^m(1-F_i), \] in the free algebra with the original placeholder tuple retained. Here \(F_i\) are the used original clause translations in their fixed order. The used list is Boolean-unsatisfiable because it supports the refutation. The proof keeps the first \(k\) complete input-prefix copies as literal exterior factors in all old-dependent corrections; this exact factorization is why scalar-point jet information alone is not substituted for the argument.

Fix a Boolean assignment of \(u\) and one scheme. Sums and products of upper triangular matrices remain upper triangular, and the \(b\)-th diagonal of a polynomial evaluation equals its scalar evaluation at \(((M_j)_{bb})_j\). That tuple is a Boolean old assignment. Some used clause has \(F_i=1\) there, so the corresponding diagonal of \(P(M)\) is zero. All diagonals are therefore zero. A product of \(k\) strictly upper triangular \(k\)-by-\(k\) matrices is zero: a nonzero path would require \(k\) strict increases among \(k\) indices. In particular \(P(M)^k=0\). The binary evaluation cost

Consider a size-\(L\), depth-\(h\) arithmetic formula over \(\mathbb F_2\), with ordered unbounded products, evaluated at upper triangular \(l\)-dimensional matrices. Compute and share the entry circuits at each formula gate. A sum is coordinatewise parity. For a product \(T_1\cdots T_q\), its \((i,j)\) entry is \[ \sum_{i=i_0\le i_1\le\cdots\le i_q=j} \prod_{b=1}^q (T_b)_{i_{b-1},i_b}. \] For \(q\ge1\), the number of paths is \(\binom{q+j-i-1}{j-i}\), at most \((q+l)^{l-1}\). This counts weak compositions of the total increase \(j-i\) among the \(q\) factors, so every path is included once, with its original factor order. A product layer and parity layer suffice. Summing the at most \(q\) wires per path over all product gates and at most \(l^2\) entries, using \(\sum q\le L\), gives a conservative size bound \((L+l+2)^{l+4}\), plus the supplied leaf-entry circuits. The entry depth is at most the leaf depth plus \(2h\). Scalar constants act as scalar identity matrices; empty products and sums use the identity and zero. The output is a Boolean circuit with shared child entries, not an uncharged matrix gate or a formula-size preservation claim.

Placeholder matrices \(N_i\) need not be triangular. To evaluate a polynomial of placeholder degree at most one, use the \(2k\)-dimensional substitution \[ X_j\longmapsto \begin{pmatrix}M_j&0\\0&M_j\end{pmatrix},\qquad Y_i\longmapsto \begin{pmatrix}0&N_i\\0&0\end{pmatrix}. \] Every resulting leaf matrix is upper triangular. The upper-right output block equals the sum of the terms containing exactly one placeholder, including when polynomial linearity results from cancellations in the supplied formula. Terms with two placeholders multiply to zero, and placeholder-free terms have zero upper-right block. The compiled certificate is homogeneous placeholder-linear because its zero-placeholder specialization is zero. Even if \(A_k\) is described only as affine placeholder-linear, the preceding erasure with \(N=0\) gives \(A_k(M,0)=C_{\widehat\pi}(M,0)=0\) at every Boolean parameter assignment. Its upper-right block therefore equals the full desired \(A_k(M,N)\) there. No additional homogeneous-component extraction is used.
