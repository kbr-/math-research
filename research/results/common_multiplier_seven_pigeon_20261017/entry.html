Setting

The functional pigeonhole system with \(m\) pigeons and \(N\) holes (Booleanity, hole and functionality axioms, pigeon axioms \(Q_i=1-\sum_hx_{ih}\), multilinear ring), \(C\) its degree-\(D\) closure, \(H\) that of the hole and functionality axioms, \(W=C\cap V_K\). For two distinct pigeons, written \(1\) and \(2\) (the record's pigeons are numbered from \(0\)), and \(A\subseteq\{0,\dots,N-1\}\), the partition error is \(E_A=\sum_{j\in A}x_{1j}+\sum_{j\notin A}x_{2j}\); its representatives modulo \(C\cap V_1\) are the \(E_A+\sum_ic_iQ_i\), since \(C\cap V_1\) is the span of the pigeon axioms (lem:closure-degree-locality at degree one); its single excess is \(\dim\operatorname{Ann}_C(E_A)\cap V_K\) minus the largest \(\dim(\operatorname{Ann}_H(E')\cap V_K+W)\) over its linear representatives \(E'\) (check:top-degree-partition-kernels's definition).

Partition error transfer

Statement. Corollary (partition error transfer). Over any field, on the functional pigeonhole system with two distinct pigeons \(1\), \(2\) among any number and \(N\) holes, \(2\le D\le\lceil N/2\rceil\) and \(K=1\): if \(N-|A|\ge3\), then \(\operatorname{Ann}_C(E_A)\cap V_1=\operatorname{Ann}_H(E')\cap V_1+W\) for the representative \(E'=E_A+Q_2=1+\sum_{j\in A}(x_{1j}-x_{2j})\); if \(|A|\ge3\), the same holds with \(E'=E_A+Q_1=1+\sum_{j\notin A}(x_{2j}-x_{1j})\). So the single excess of \(E_A\) is \(0\) unless \(|A|\le2\) and \(N-|A|\le2\); for \(N\ge5\) every partition error has single excess \(0\) at \(K=1\). (b) This is the whole reach of cor:linear-error-transfer on partition errors: some representative and some numbering satisfy its criterion (i)–(iii) exactly when \(\max(|A|,N-|A|)\ge3\).

Proof. \(E_A-E'=-Q_2\), of degree \(1\le D\), and for \(u\in V_1\), \(uQ_2\in C\) since \(1+1\le D\); so \(uE_A\in C\) exactly when \(uE'\in C\), and \(\operatorname{Ann}_C(E_A)\cap V_1=\operatorname{Ann}_C(E')\cap V_1\). \(E'\) has degree \(1\le D-K\), and its edge set \(S=\{(1,j),(2,j):j\in A\}\) misses the \(N-|A|\ge3\) holes outside \(A\); cor:linear-error-transfer, whose statement gives the transfer “in particular when S misses three holes”, with \(2\le D\le\lceil N/2\rceil\), \(K=1\) and any number of pigeons, gives \(\operatorname{Ann}_C(E')\cap V_1=\operatorname{Ann}_H(E')\cap V_1+W\). The case \(|A|\ge3\) is the same with \(Q_1\): \(E_A+Q_1\) has edges \((1,j),(2,j)\), \(j\notin A\), missing the \(|A|\ge3\) holes of \(A\). Then the best single representative's domain side is at least \(\dim(\operatorname{Ann}_H(E')\cap V_1+W)=\dim\operatorname{Ann}_C(E_A)\cap V_1\), and at most it since every representative's domain side lies in \(\operatorname{Ann}_C(E_A)\): the excess is \(0\). For \(N\ge5\), \(|A|\le2\) forces \(N-|A|\ge3\). (b) A representative \(E'=E_A+\sum_ic_iQ_i\) has coefficient \(-c_i\) on \(x_{ij}\) for each pigeon \(i\notin\{1,2\}\) and every hole, \([j\in A]-c_1\) on \(x_{1j}\) and \([j\notin A]-c_2\) on \(x_{2j}\); so its edges miss hole \(j\) exactly when \(c_i=0\) for \(i\notin\{1,2\}\) and \((c_1,c_2)=([j\in A],[j\notin A])\). One representative fixes \((c_1,c_2)\), so the holes it misses all lie in \(A\) (when \((1,0)\)) or all outside \(A\) (when \((0,1)\)), at most \(\max(|A|,N-|A|)\) of them. Condition (i) needs the hole of rank \(N-1\) missed, so a representative meeting the criterion has \(c_i=0\) off pigeons \(1\), \(2\) and \((c_1,c_2)\in\{(1,0),(0,1)\}\). Then every hole it uses holds edges of both pigeons \(1\) and \(2\): for \((1,0)\) the coefficients at a hole outside \(A\) are \(-1\) and \(1\), for \((0,1)\) those at a hole of \(A\) are \(1\) and \(-1\). (ii) needs every edge in the hole of rank \(N-2\) to have its pigeon first and (iii) every edge in the hole of rank \(N-3\) to have its pigeon last, impossible for two distinct pigeons at once, so those holes are missed too: the criterion holds for some numbering exactly when the representative misses three holes (ranking them on top gives the if direction), which some representative does exactly when \(\max(|A|,N-|A|)\ge3\). The corollary's second in-particular clause, one pigeon in each of two further holes, is a case of (i)–(iii) and adds nothing here. \(\square\) Part (b) is the reviewer's sketch, proved here with the order of its steps corrected by the reviewer (a representative with some \(c_i\ne0\) off pigeons \(1\), \(2\) can have one-pigeon holes, but misses no hole).

Middle sizes

Statement. Conjecture (excess at the middle sizes). Over \(\mathbb F_2\), on the functional pigeonhole system with \(n+1\) pigeons and \(n\) holes at \(D=\lceil n/2\rceil\), \(K=D-1\), the partition error \(E_A\) has positive single excess exactly when \(|A|\in\{\lfloor n/2\rfloor,\lceil n/2\rceil\}\); the excess depends only on \(|A|\), by the holes' symmetry, and is the same at \(|A|\) and \(n-|A|\), by exchanging the two pigeons. At the top degree \(D=K+1\) the most holes a representative misses is \(\max(|A|,n-|A|)\) (part (b)'s count), at least \(K+2\) exactly off the middle sizes, so the conjecture is equivalent to “excess \(0\) exactly when some representative misses \(K+2\) holes”; the stronger form, that such a representative itself transfers (\(\operatorname{Ann}_C(E_A)\cap V_K=\operatorname{Ann}_H(E')\cap V_K+W\)), is proved at \(K=1\) by part (a) and untested at \(K\ge2\), as are its extensions below the top degree and to other pigeon counts.

Evidence. check:top-degree-partition-kernels: excess by \(|A|\) \(0,1,1,0\) at \(n=3\), \(0,0,1,0,0\) at \(n=4\), \(0,0,12,12,0,0\) at \(n=5\), and at \(n=6\) \(15\) at \(|A|=3\), \(0\) at \(|A|=0\); check:partition-sizes-seven-pigeons: \(0\) at \(|A|=1\) and at \(|A|=2\), \(n=6\). The corollary proves the “only if” direction at \(K=1\), that is \(n\le4\); both directions are settled over \(\mathbb F_2\) at \(n=3\) to \(6\) by the recorded computations; the conjecture is open for \(n\ge7\) and other fields, and no direction is proved at \(K\ge2\). What the evidence does not show: \(n\ge7\), other fields, and why the middle; and it is a statement about single errors, not about the pairs' intersection kernels, which at \(n=6\) are absent at the middle size too.
