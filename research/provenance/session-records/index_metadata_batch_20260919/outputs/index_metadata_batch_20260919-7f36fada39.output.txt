418: Proof. Write \(N=n-q\). First apply the usual partial-matching restriction \(\tau_0\): selected edges become one, all other entries in their rows or columns become zero, and the remaining board stays free. It sends \(M\) to one and our base to \(\mathcal F_N\) or zero. Hence \(f|_{\tau_0}\) is an affine PC consequence through degree \(c\). Since \(p\ge2\), our range implies \(c\le\lfloor(N-2)/2\rfloor\); affine rigidity puts this image in the residual row-equation span.
