The explicit trace certificate retains a binary depth obstruction

Corollary (trace certificate depth). Fix constants \(\Delta,\varepsilon>0\), and a family of finite simple oriented graphs on \(n\to\infty\) vertices, of maximum degree at most \(\Delta\) and edge expansion at least \(\varepsilon\). Give each graph ternary charges with nonzero total charge. For any ordering of its vertices, let \(D\) be the particular extension-field, placeholder-linear certificate constructed in the vertex-product construction, with each vertex's local certificate using only that vertex's forbidden-tuple clauses, incident edge-domain clauses, Boolean equations and commutators. Let \(C=\omega^2D+\omega\sigma(D)\) be its binary constant coordinate, where \(\omega^2+\omega+1=0\) and \(\sigma\) conjugates coefficients while fixing all letters. The commutative Boolean evaluation of \(C\), with old variables and placeholders both regarded as inputs, has no polynomial-size fixed-depth binary arithmetic circuits. Therefore neither does this particular formal polynomial or noncommutative certificate. This is an application of the recorded MOD3 circuit obstruction, not a lower bound for all certificates.

Proof: isolate one coefficient without extraction. Let \(S\) be the first \(\lfloor n/2\rfloor\) vertices and let \(v\) be the next vertex. Choose a valid one-hot assignment to the incident edges of \(v\) that violates its charge. This is possible since expansion gives positive degree for sufficiently large \(n\). Pin those old variables, set the placeholder of that unique forbidden tuple at \(v\) to one, and set every other placeholder to zero. Exactly one local generator evaluates to one; every other forbidden tuple of \(v\), its domain and Boolean equations and its commutators evaluates to zero. Locality and placeholder linearity therefore force its local certificate to evaluate to \(T_v-\alpha_v\ne0\). Certificates of other vertices use no copy of this placeholder. The edge cancellations and sorting corrections use only their own domain, Boolean or commutator placeholders and also disappear.

The surviving global term is consequently \[ \kappa_0\prod_{u\in S}T_u,\qquad \kappa_0=(\Omega-1)^{-1}(T_v-\alpha_v)\prod_{u>v}\alpha_u\ne0, \] where signs disappear in characteristic two. Evaluate old letters commutatively on valid one-hot labels. Internal edges of \(S\) contribute \(U_eV_e=1\); edges outside \(S\) contribute nothing. Pin all edges except those crossing \(S\) and not incident with \(v\) to fixed valid labels. At least \[ t\ge\varepsilon\lfloor n/2\rfloor-\Delta=\Omega(n) \] edges remain free. For each, use the labels \(0,1\) when its factor in the prefix is \(U_e\), and \(0,2\) when that factor is \(V_e\). Its three indicators are then \(0,z_e,1+z_e\) in the appropriate positions, a binary affine substitution. Each free factor is \(\omega^{z_e}\). Thus the restriction of \(D\) is \(\kappa\omega^{\sum_e z_e}\), with fixed \(\kappa\in\mathbb F_4^\ast\).

Proof: return to the binary coordinate. Binary substitutions commute with coefficient conjugation. The restriction of \(C\) is \[ \operatorname{Tr}_{\mathbb F_4/\mathbb F_2} \bigl(\omega^2\kappa\,\omega^{\sum_e z_e}\bigr). \] The trace is zero at \(1\) and one at \(\omega,\omega^2\). Hence this is the complement of one residue class of the Hamming weight modulo three. Fixing at most two of the \(z_e\) to one changes the missing residue to zero; complementing the output gives MOD3 on at least \(t-2=\Omega(n)\) inputs. This is a restriction and affine substitution, not homogeneous-component extraction. Binary arithmetic sums and products become parity and AND on these inputs, with constant overhead. A polynomial-size fixed-depth circuit for \(C\) would contradict the circuit lower bound already used in cor:binary-coordinate-depth-gap. The original bounded-degree CNF has size polynomial in \(n\), so the conclusion is superpolynomial in that encoding length as well. \(\square\)

Degenerate cases. The claim is asymptotic and makes no assertion when the displayed free-edge bound is nonpositive. Zero total charge is excluded because the construction divides by \(\Omega-1\). A graph with a unit isolated-vertex clause cannot belong to the positive-expansion family at large \(n\); the earlier upper theorem's immediate certificate there is no counterexample. Duplicate input occurrences require separate ownership as in the stated construction, not identification of placeholders of distinct vertex constraints.

Nonvacuity. The existing connected bounded-degree expander family, with charge one at one vertex and zero elsewhere, meets the hypotheses for unbounded \(n\). Every vertex ordering has the balanced cut used above. No graph enumeration, numerical rank calculation or new source encoding is claimed.

Scope. The same certificate's polynomial word ranks do not imply shallow binary circuitry. Conversely its hardness does not extend to other certificates. In the commutative Hilbert-like setting the axiom-cut patch \((1-F_{v,b})C+Y_{v,b}\) is a valid certificate, and on this particular slice \(F_{v,b}=1\), so it becomes the constant one. The test thus answers the blind reviewer's explicit-witness calibration, while leaving full-coset and matched-image hardness open. It cannot serve again as the next decisive test.

Proof. The new input occurrences have exactly the fixed literal orders of \(F_i\), so their correction terms are zero. The first copy contributes \(H\), the next \(PH\), and the \(j\)-th contributes \(P^{j-1}H\). For every old input or local correction term, its old prefix gains the left factor \(P^t\). The absorption certificates \[ -U(L_j^2-L_j)Vab+U(1-L_j)[Va,L_j]b \] therefore acquire exactly the same left factor \(P^t\), with the same local Boolean and commutator certificates as before. Summing proves the polynomial identity without changing those certificate choices. Only \(tm\) legal input occurrences are added; no earlier subtree is duplicated. The existing jet argument applies because the input CNF has no common Boolean zero, so \(P(a)=0\). No new calculus rule or semantic substitution is involved. \(\square\)

Terminal padding is different. Once an empty cedent \(L_T=1\) occurs, the later prefix contains the literal zero \(1-L_T\). Later input and correction terms vanish. In a premise absorption, that zero lies in \(U\) or \(V\), unless the premise is \(L_T=1\), whose Booleanity and commutator certificates are zero. The standard recursive certificates respect these zero factors. Thus an appendage after the old empty cedent contributes zero in this prefix construction. The tree-like recursive simulation's final-cut realization cannot be substituted for this different compiler.
