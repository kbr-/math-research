Setup and statement. Work over \(\mathbb F_2\). For each of \(M\ge1\) terminal parents choose independently an invertible binary affine coordinate system \(y_a\) and a proper coordinate subset \(C_a\subsetneq[v]\). Its input positions are exactly \(\mathcal H_{C_a}\) at a common \(2\le r\le v\), with inputs \(G_{a,S}=y_{a,S}+\ell_{a,S}\), arbitrary binary affine \(\ell_{a,S}\). Each monomial is the genuine product of a complete accuracy-\(h\) affine-bottom block on \((1-y_{a,i})_{i\in S}\). Every parent is complete at the same original accuracy. Identical defining bottom tuples may share; there are no raw coefficient readers or parent dependencies. Set \(c=\max_a|C_a|\), \(q=\max(r-1,c)\), \(T=u+q-r\), \(N_v(k)=\sum_{j=0}^k\binom vj\), and \(\eta=N_v(K-2)/(2N_v(K))\). Assume integers \(2\le K\le v\), \(t\ge1\), \(h\ge\max(r,t+1)\), \(B\ge\max(u,2h^2+3h)\), \(M(1-\eta)^t<1\), and \(K+TB\le D\). Downset cofactors. Fix one parent and omit its subscript. The common zero set of its retained monomials, identified with supports, is \[\mathcal D_C=\{U:|U|<r\}\cup\{U:U\subseteq C\}.\] Indeed a support outside this downset has size at least \(r\) and an element outside \(C\), so contains a retained \(r\)-subset; the converse is immediate. Its maximum size is \(q_C=\max(r-1,|C|)\le q\). For \(U\in\mathcal D_C\) define \[e_U(y)=\sum_{V\in\mathcal D_C,\ U\subseteq V}y_V.\] On a point \(\mathbf1_Z\) of the downset, this is zero unless \(U\subseteq Z\), and otherwise sums all subsets of \(Z\setminus U\). Downward closure includes that whole interval. In characteristic two its value is one precisely at \(Z=U\). Thus these are indicator representatives of degree at most \(q_C\). Express \(\Gamma\) in \(y\), preserving degree \(u\). Every downset point violates a generator; choose one such generator for each point, and sum its indicators to form \(b_j\). Then \(F=1+\sum_j b_j\Gamma_j\) vanishes on this downset and has degree at most \(u+q_C\). Its multilinear remainder has no coefficient on any support in \(\mathcal D_C\): evaluate successively at downset supports in increasing size; all proper subsets stay in the downset. Every remaining monomial therefore has a retained \(r\)-divisor. Assign one such divisor per monomial and factor it, giving \[F=\sum_{S\in\mathcal H_C}c_Sy_S+R_{\rm bool},\quad \deg c_S\le u+q_C-r\le T.\] Ordinary Boolean division has a certificate through the input degree \(u+q_C\); it never increases degree. Hence \(R=1+\sum_S c_Sy_S\) has a certificate in the original old generators through \(u+q_C\). Affine substitution back to \(x\) preserves degrees and sends each \(y_i^2+y_i\) to a constant combination of old Boolean equations. This uses cube points only for constructing a certificate, never as models of the inconsistent old system.

Retained exchange paths. For retained \(S,T\), enumerate \(S\setminus T=\{i_1,\ldots,i_k\}\) and \(T\setminus S=\{j_1,\ldots,j_k\}\). If \(S\cap T\) has an outside-core element, it remains throughout any monotone path. Otherwise \(T\setminus S\) has an outside-core element; choose it as \(j_1\). Complete exchange certificate. Put \(s=\ell_S\), \(b_a=\prod_{j\le a}y_{j_j}\), and \(D_a=s\ell_{S_a}+sb_a\). Therefore \[D_k=b_k(s^2+s)+\sum_{a=1}^k\Bigl(\prod_{j>a}y_{j_j}\Bigr)(sE_{S_{a-1},i_a,j_a}+\ell_{S_a}A_{S,i_a}).\] Order \(I=S\cap T\). Telescoping gives \[\ell_SG_T=D_k+b_k\sum_{i\in I}\Bigl(\prod_{i'\in I,\ i'\text{ before }i}y_{i'}\Bigr)A_{S,i}.\] All used positions are retained.
