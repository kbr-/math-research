Setting

As in the linear numbering entry: the functional pigeonhole system over a field, \(m\) pigeons, \(N\) holes, \(C\) its degree-\(D\) closure, \(H\) the closure of the hole and functionality axioms, \(W=C\cap V_1=\operatorname{span}\{Q_k\}\), \(Q_k=1-\sum_hx_{kh}\). For a family \(\nu\), \(P_\nu\) is the set of pigeons and \(J_\nu\) the set of holes not used by \(q_\nu\) (\(P_1=J_1=\{2,\dots\}\), \(P_2=J_2=\{0,1,4,\dots\}\)), and the separated family is \(\{(1+q_\nu)x_e:\ e=(i,j),\ i\in P_\nu,\ j\in J_\nu\}\). Write \(E_e=(1+q_\nu)x_e\) and \(A_e=\operatorname{Ann}_H(E_e)\cap V_1\).

Separated-family kernels

Statement. Corollary (separated-family kernels). Over any field, let \(N\ge6\), \(m\ge4\), \(K=1\) and \(3\le D\le\lceil N/2\rceil\). Then (a) each error of the separated family has \(\operatorname{Ann}_C(E_e)\cap V_1=A_e+W\); (b) \(A_e\) is the set of \(u=c+\sum_fa_fx_f\) with \(c+a_e=0\), \(a_f=0\) for every edge \(f\) sharing no pigeon or hole with \(e\) other than \(q_\nu\)'s two edges, and on those two edges \(a=a'\) in characteristic \(2\) and \(a=a'=0\) otherwise; (c) the common kernel in \(V_1\) of any set of errors of degree at most \(D-1\) that contains the eight errors \(E_e\) of family \(1\) at \(e\in\{2,3\}\times\{2,3\}\) and of family \(2\) at \(e\in\{0,1\}\times\{0,4\}\), the separated family among them, is \(W\); in characteristic other than \(2\) the four of family \(1\) suffice; (d) in characteristic \(2\), the common kernel in \(V_1\) of family \(1\)'s separated errors alone is \(W\oplus\operatorname{span}\{q_1\}\), and, for \(K=2\) and \(4\le D\le\lceil N/2\rceil\), the product \(q_1q_2\) kills every error \((1+q_\nu)x_e\) of both families on every edge and lies outside \(C\).

Proof. (a) By lem:polynomial-numbering-transfer with \(\mathcal E=\{E_e\}\), it suffices to number the holes and pigeons so that each matching monomial of \(E_e\), namely \(x_e\), \(x_ex_g\), \(x_ex_{g'}\) for \(q_\nu=x_g+x_{g'}\), joined with any single edge \(t\) that has a dance, has a dance. Rank the hole \(j\) of \(e\) at \(0\) and the holes of \(g,g'\) at \(1,2\). A matching \(t\cup E'\) uses at most three holes: at most two of ranks \(0,1,2\) from \(E'\), and \(t\)'s hole, which is off the top since \(t\) has a dance. lem:ballot-dance-maps gives a dance in every pigeon order when every top segment holds at most as many of these holes as other holes. The whole board holds at most \(3\) of them against at least \(N-3\ge3\) others; a segment from rank \(1\) holds at most \(2\) against \(N-3\); from rank \(2\), at most \(2\) against \(N-4\ge2\); a segment above rank \(2\) holds at most \(t\)'s hole, not the top, so at most \(1\) against at least \(1\). So the property holds and (a) follows. (b) For \(u=c+\sum_fa_fx_f\), \(uE_e\in H\) exactly when every matching monomial's coefficient vanishes. With \(q_\nu=x_g+x_{g'}\), the coefficient of \(x_e\) is \(c+a_e\); of \(x_ex_g\), \(c+a_e+2a_g\); of \(x_ex_{g'}\), \(c+a_e+2a_{g'}\); of \(x_ex_gx_{g'}\), \(a_g+a_{g'}\); of \(x_ex_f\) for \(f\) compatible with \(e\) and \(f\ne g,g'\), \(a_f\); edges in the row or column of \(e\) give non-matching products. This is the stated set. (c) \(W\) lies in the kernel of every error of degree at most \(D-1\). Let \(u\) lie in the common kernel of the eight errors, so by (a) in every \(A_e+W\): for each \(e=(i,j)\) there are \(\lambda_k\) with \(u-\sum_k\lambda_kQ_k\in A_e\). Its coefficient on an edge \(f=(k,h)\) is \(a_f+\lambda_k\), and by (b) it vanishes for \(k\ne i\), \(h\ne j\), \(f\ne g,g'\). So for \(k\ne i\) the row \(k\) of \(u\) is constant off hole \(j\) and off \(q_\nu\)'s edges. Each row \(k\) is reached so with two holes: for \(k\notin\{0,1\}\), family \(1\) with \(i\in\{2,3\}\setminus\{k\}\) and \(j\in\{2,3\}\), where \(q_1\)'s edges are not in row \(k\); for \(k\in\{0,1\}\), family \(2\) with \(i\) the other of \(0,1\) and \(j\in\{0,4\}\), where \(q_2\)'s edges are not in rows \(0,1\). In characteristic other than \(2\), (b) puts no exception on \(q_1\)'s edges, so family \(1\)'s four errors make rows \(0,1\) constant too. The two holes leave every other hole in common (\(N\ge3\)), so every row of \(u\) is constant, \(a_{kh}=r_k\). Then \(u=c+\sum_kr_k\sum_hx_{kh}=c+\sum_kr_k(1-Q_k)\equiv\gamma\) modulo \(W\), with \(\gamma=c+\sum_kr_k\). The constant \(\gamma\) is in the common kernel; for one error \(e=(i,j)\), \(\gamma-\sum\lambda_kQ_k\in A_e\) needs \(\lambda_k=0\) for \(k\ne i\) (the compatible edges of row \(k\)), and then its constant \(\gamma-\lambda_i\) plus its \(x_e\) coefficient \(\lambda_i\) vanish by (b): \(\gamma=0\). So \(u\in W\). (d) In characteristic \(2\), \(q_1(1+q_1)=q_1+q_1^2=0\) in the multilinear ring, so \(q_1\) kills every error of family \(1\), and likewise \(q_2\) of family \(2\). For family \(1\) alone, the argument of (c) makes rows \(k\notin\{0,1\}\) constant; rows \(0,1\) are constant off \(q_1\)'s edges, and the exception of (b), \(a_{00}+\lambda_0=a_{11}+\lambda_1\), says their deviations there are equal, \(d\); so \(u\equiv\gamma+dq_1\) modulo \(W\), and since \(q_1\) is in every kernel, \(\gamma\) is, and \(\gamma=0\) as in (c). \(q_1\notin W\): a combination \(\sum\lambda_kQ_k\) has its row \(k\) constant, and \(q_1\) has row \(0\) equal to \(1\) at hole \(0\) only. At \(K=2\), \(q_1q_2\) kills both families' errors on every edge, \(q_1\) or \(q_2\) killing each factor. It lies outside \(C\): its monomials \(x_{00}x_{22},x_{00}x_{33},x_{11}x_{22},x_{11}x_{33}\) are matchings whose holes have ranks among \(0,\dots,3\) when holes \(0,\dots,3\) are ranked lowest; for \(N\ge6\) no two of them form the pair of ranks \(\{N-3,N-2\}\) and none uses the top hole, so each has a dance (the two-pigeon analysis of cor:linear-error-transfer), lies in \(T_D\cap B_D\), and a nonzero combination of such monomials is not in \(C\): \(B_D\) is a basis of \(\operatorname{span}(T_D)\) (Impagliazzo–Pudlák–Sgall, Proposition 2.8, as the functional-transfer entry quotes it) and \(C\) is \(H\) plus the lift of \(K_D=\operatorname{span}(B_D\setminus T_D)\) (the same entry), so \(\operatorname{span}(T_D\cap B_D)\cap K_D=0\). \(\square\)
