Statement. Let R be a multiset of points in the open strip, closed under rho -> 1 - conj(rho), whose points
of height above some T_0 are all on the line Re = 1/2, with counting function of the heights in (0, t]
equal to (t/2 pi) log(t/2 pi e) + O(log t), symmetric in the sign of the height. Let w = 1 - 1/rho and, for
n >= T_0^2, let H_n be the sum of Re(1 - w^n) over the points of height above sqrt(n). Then
    H_n = (1/2) n log n + C_1 n + O(sqrt(n) log n),   C_1 = (gamma_0 - 1 - log 2 pi)/2,
so that the whole sum Lambda_n is the sum over the points of height at most sqrt(n) plus this, and
Lambda_n >= 0 is, up to O(sqrt(n) log n), a lower bound -(1/2) n log n - C_1 n for the sum over the
points of height at most sqrt(n).

By the rule that a question its cited statements answer in a few lines is a lemma, this is a lemma with an
expected answer (Proved). It is posed because the review finds nothing on the route that is more, and
because the lead's closure as a restatement rests on it.

Outcomes.
- Proved: positivity of the n-th coefficient is, up to the error, a one-sided bound for the incomplete
  coefficient of height sqrt(n): a statement about the points below that height, weighted. With
  prop:li-height-window the lead is then a restatement of the absence of off-line points below sqrt(n),
  with a weight, and is closed as such once the statement of Bombieri and Lagarias on one-sided bounds is
  read or its reading dropped with a reason.
- Refuted, or the constant or the error differs: the points above sqrt(n) matter at index n beyond a
  polynomial error, or the density's remainder enters at a larger order; then the lead keeps a question
  (what of the high zeros a coefficient sees) and is not closed.

Trivial cases. The empty configuration has H_n = 0: the statement needs the density from below, not only
an upper count; with an upper count alone only the lower bound of prop:li-height-window holds. A
configuration with all points on the line and the density stated is the nontrivial instance; off-line
points above sqrt(n) change H_n by at most 5 A sqrt(n) log(n + 2) downward by prop:li-height-window and
their upward change is of the same order by the same bound per pair. Rescaling the heights changes the
density and the constant.

Power. prop:li-height-window gives the lower side for off-line pairs. For zeta, Lagarias's Theorem 1.1
(arXiv math/0404394), with the sign of its incomplete coefficient corrected as in the last entry on the
lead, states the same two-sided form; it is imported and its proof unread. No computation is planned.

Attempt. For an on-line point of height g, w = e^{i theta} with theta = 2 arctan(1/(2g)) = 1/g + O(g^-3),
and its term is 1 - cos(n theta). Summing 1 - cos(n/g) against the density over g > sqrt(n) and both
signs, with u = n/g: (n/pi) int_0^{sqrt n} (1 - cos u) u^-2 log(n/(2 pi u)) du, and int_0^infinity
(1 - cos u) u^-2 du = pi/2 gives (1/2) n log n; the tail beyond sqrt(n) costs O(sqrt(n) log n); the
density's remainder, by parts against the derivative n g^-2 sin(n/g), costs O(sqrt(n) log n). The attempt
stops at two points: the constant, which needs int_0^infinity (1 - cos u) u^-2 log u du and the density's
second term, and is not computed; and the replacement of theta by 1/g inside the cosine, whose error
n g^-3 I have bounded only by its sum.
