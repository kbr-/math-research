2. Lemma: many dense labels force a satisfied term

Lemma. Conditionally on any \((Q,R')\), if \(|D|\ge\Delta_0\) then some term of \(F\) is satisfied by \(\mu'\) with probability at least \(1-1/(8S)\).

Proof. For a label \(x\notin Q\) let \(A_x\) be the event that the row \(\mu'^{-1}(x)\) is pinned to \(x\). Given \((Q,R')\), \(\mu'\) is a uniform bijection from the \(n-N\) matched rows onto the labels outside \(Q\), so \(\Pr[A_x]=\deg_{R'^c}(x)/(n-N)\), where \(\deg_{R'^c}(x)\ge\deg(x)-(N+1)\) counts matched rows pinned to \(x\). The indicators of a uniform bijection are negatively associated (Joag-Dev and Proschan, Negative association of random variables, with applications, Ann. Statist. 11 (1983), permutation distributions; imported statement), \(A_x\) is an increasing function of the entries of column \(x\), and distinct labels use disjoint columns; hence \(\Pr[\bigcap_{x}A_x^c]\le\prod_x(1-\Pr[A_x])\le\exp(-\sum_x\Pr[A_x])\). At least \(|D|-N\) dense labels lie outside \(Q\), each with \(\deg_{R'^c}(x)\ge D^\ast-N-1\ge D^\ast/2\), so the exponent is at most \(-(|D|-N)D^\ast/(2(n-N))\le-\ln(8S)\). \(\square\)
