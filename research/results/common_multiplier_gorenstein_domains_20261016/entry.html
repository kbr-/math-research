Question. prop:frobenius-marked-separation turns a perfect graded pairing into an annihilator of complementary degree. Do the associated graded algebras of the line's domain models, the cube and the hole-respecting assignments \(Y\), carry such a pairing, and how does graded annihilation bear on annihilators on the domain?

General statement. lem:graded-domain-pairing: for a down-set \(X\) of the cube, the associated graded algebra of the functions on \(X\) is the face ring of \(X\) modulo the squares, whose socle is spanned by the facets, so it is Gorenstein, the graded hypothesis of marked Frobenius separation, exactly when \(X\) has one facet; product domains with one-hot coordinates are such down-sets, Gorenstein exactly when every factor has at most two states; and graded annihilation is a lower bound for annihilation on the domain, not a source of it: the leading form of a killer kills the errors' leading forms.

Graded domain pairing

Statement. Over a field \(F\), (a) for a down-set \(X\subseteq\{0,1\}^N\), a simplicial complex on \([N]\), with the functions on \(X\) filtered by degree, the associated graded algebra is \(G_X=F[x]/(x_v^2\ (v\in[N]),\ x_S\ (S\notin X))\), the face ring of \(X\) modulo the squares, with basis the \(x_S\), \(S\in X\); its socle is spanned by the \(x_M\) over the facets \(M\) of \(X\); so \(G_X\) has a perfect graded pairing into its top degree, the hypothesis of prop:frobenius-marked-separation, exactly when \(X\) is the set of subsets of one set \(M\), with \(s=|M|\). (b) A product \(\prod_jA_j\) of finite nonempty domains with base states and one-hot variables is the down-set of \(\{0,1\}^{\sum_j(|A_j|-1)}\) with at most one variable per block, whose facets choose a non-base state in every block with \(|A_j|\ge2\); its socle has dimension \(\prod_j\max(|A_j|-1,1)\), and it is Gorenstein exactly when every \(|A_j|\le2\). For the product pigeonhole system with \(n\ge1\) holes and \(m\ge2\) pigeons, each hole has \(m+1\ge3\) states, so \(Y\) has no such pairing; with \(m=1\), \(Y\) is the cube \(\{0,1\}^n\). (c) If \(f\) and \(E\) are functions on \(X\) with \(fE=0\), their leading forms satisfy \(\operatorname{lead}(f)\operatorname{lead}(E)=0\) in \(G_X\); so the least degree of a common annihilator of the errors' leading forms in \(G_X\) is a lower bound for the least degree of a common killer on \(X\). An annihilator in \(G_X\) need not lift: on the cube \(x_{[N]}\) kills every \(x_A\), \(A\ne\emptyset\), in \(G_X\), while on \(X\) the product \(x_{[N]}x_A=x_{[N]}\) is nonzero. On the cube both least degrees, for monomial errors \(x_{A_j}\), equal the transversal number \(\tau\): in \(G_X\) the common annihilator of the \(x_{A_j}\) is spanned by the \(x_S\) with \(S\) a transversal, and on \(X\) by lem:transversal-killer-basis.

Proof. This is the standard computation of the socle of a face ring modulo squares; we write it out. (a) On \(X\), \(x_v^2=x_v\), and \(x_S\) vanishes on \(X\) for \(S\notin X\), as \(X\) is a down-set; the \(x_S\), \(S\in X\), are a basis of \(F^X\) by triangularity, and degree at most \(k\) is spanned by those with \(|S|\le k\). In \(G_X\) the product of the classes of \(x_S\) and \(x_T\) is the degree-\((|S|+|T|)\) class of \(x_{S\cup T}\): \(x_{S\cup T}\) when \(S\cap T=\emptyset\) and \(S\cup T\in X\), and \(0\) otherwise, since then \(x_{S\cup T}\) has lower degree or vanishes on \(X\); this is the presentation. The socle is graded, since multiplication by each \(x_v\) is homogeneous; \(g=\sum_Sc_Sx_S\) is in it when \(x_vg=0\) for all \(v\); \(x_vx_S\ne0\) exactly when \(v\notin S\) and \(S\cup\{v\}\in X\), and distinct \(S\) give distinct products, so \(g\) is in the socle exactly when \(c_S=0\) for every non-facet \(S\). If \(G_X\) has a perfect pairing \(G_j\times G_{s-j}\to G_s\), then \(G_s\) is one-dimensional, as \(G_0=F\), and a homogeneous socle element of degree \(j<s\) pairs to zero with \(G_{s-j}\subseteq G_{\ge1}\), so it is zero; the socle is \(G_s\), one facet \(M\), and every face lies in \(M\); conversely \(F[x_M]/(x_v^2)\) pairs \(x_S\) with \(x_{M\setminus S}\). (b) The one-hot variables of a block satisfy \(x_{j,a}x_{j,b}=[a=b]x_{j,a}\), so a polynomial reduces to the monomials with at most one variable per block, \(\prod_j|A_j|\) of them, the number of points; they are a basis, and the domain is that down-set; its facets choose one non-base state in each block with \(|A_j|\ge2\), giving the socle dimension, which is \(1\) exactly when every \(|A_j|\le2\). (c) \(fE=0\) on \(X\) has every degree class zero, and its class in degree \(\deg f+\deg E\) is \(\operatorname{lead}(f)\operatorname{lead}(E)\); for common killers apply this to each error. In \(G_X\) for the cube, \(x_Sx_{A_j}=0\) exactly when \(S\cap A_j\ne\emptyset\), and an element kills \(x_{A_j}\) exactly when each of its monomials does, by distinctness of the products.

Relations. The computation of (a) is standard for face rings modulo squares; the entry registers it for its consequences here, not as new. On \(Y\) the question it was meant to answer, whether a pairing gives a killer below \(\tau\), was already settled by lem:cylinder-killer-degree (a): no function on \(Y\) vanishing on the error supports has degree below \(\tau\), whatever pairing produces it. A non-graded pairing changes nothing: \(F^Y\) is Frobenius under \(\tau_w(a)=\sum_yw_ya(y)\) for any nonvanishing weights, and the separators it gives have no degree control, choosing \(w\) being the same as choosing the separator.

Degenerate cases. \(X=\{\emptyset\}\): \(G_X=F\), Gorenstein with \(s=0\). \(X\) empty: \(F^X=0\). A product with all \(|A_j|=1\): one point, \(G_X=F\). The pigeonhole system with \(m=1\): \(Y=\{0,1\}^n\), Gorenstein with \(s=n\). In (c), \(E\) constant and nonzero: \(\operatorname{lead}(E)\) is a unit, so \(\operatorname{lead}(f)=0\), \(f=0\).

Nonvacuity. For (b): the hole-respecting assignments with \(m=5\), \(n=4\), six states per hole, socle dimension \(5^4=625\), not Gorenstein. For (a) and (c): the cube \(\{0,1\}^3\) with errors \(x_1x_2,x_1x_3,x_2x_3\), Gorenstein with \(s=3\); the least annihilator degree is \(\tau=2\) in \(G_X\) and on \(X\).
