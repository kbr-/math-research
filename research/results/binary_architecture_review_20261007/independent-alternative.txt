### One proposed mechanism—not an established implication

Associate Boolean extension variables to gates occurring in a purported depth-\(h\) AC0[2]-Frege proof. Over \(\mathbb F_2\), parity gate definitions are linear in their input values. The hard part is eliminating the AND/OR extensions **without expanding their full Boolean polynomials and without losing inference preservation**.

A single sufficient theorem to investigate is:

> **Proof-dependent gate-elimination theorem.** For the fixed graph-PHP family, a depth-\(h\) AC0[2]-Frege refutation of total encoding size \(S\) yields an ordinary PC refutation over \(\mathbb F_2\), in the original variables, of degree at most
> \[
> C_hN^{1-\varepsilon_h}\bigl(\log(S+L_N)\bigr)^{a_h},
> \]
> where \(\varepsilon_h>0\), \(a_h>0\), and \(C_h\) depend only on \(h\).

This is a direct proof-to-proof statement, not an interpolation theorem or a probability-to-rank conversion. It would have to exploit the actual proof and the graph-PHP axioms; it cannot be a generic consequence of old PC hardness.

The architectural difference is that it seeks a **global elimination certificate for the extensions used by one proof**, rather than exclusion for every retained tuple or every mixed PC class. No claim is made that such certificates presently exist.

### Full parameter issue

Combining that hypothetical theorem with the certified degree lower bound would give
\[
\log(S+L_N)\ge c_hN^{\varepsilon_h/a_h}.
\]
Consequently \(S\) would be exponential in a positive power of \(N\), hence superpolynomial in \(L_N\).

The uniformity is essential. For a putative proof bound \(S\le L_N^A\), the extracted degree must be \(o(N)\) for **every fixed \(A\)** on the same family. Allowing \(\varepsilon_h\) to vanish with \(N\), or choosing the family according to \(A\), would invalidate this argument. Dependence on fixed depth is permitted.

The output must be an **ordinary old-variable PC refutation**. A low-degree refutation retaining gate variables, a nonzero old quotient, or an old-moment certificate does not meet this endpoint.
