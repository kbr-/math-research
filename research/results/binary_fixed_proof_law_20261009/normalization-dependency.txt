The candidate coefficient map and its residual
Work over \(\mathbb F_2\), with Boolean matrices \(A\in\mathbb F_2^{m\times n}\), \(B\in\mathbb F_2^{n\times m}\), \(m>n\), and base \(\Gamma_{m,n}\) given by \(AB=I_m\). Put \(R=BA\). Direct multiplication gives \[ AR-A=(AB-I_m)A,\qquad R^2-R=B(AB-I_m)A. \] Their original certificate degrees are three and four. For the accuracy-one block with inputs \(A_{ik}\), choose its first row coefficients as \(\beta_i=B_{ki}\). The product becomes \(1-R_{kk}\). Its companion obeys \[ A_{ik}(1-R_{kk})\equiv\sum_{\ell\ne k}A_{i\ell}R_{\ell k}\pmod{\Gamma_{m,n}} \tag{1} \] with a degree-three certificate. Thus the matrix identity redistributes the error into off-diagonal terms; it does not make them vanish. With additional chosen rows, the corresponding factors must be retained with their original costs. Idempotence of \(R\) is not a license to discard its off-diagonal entries.
A bare-matrix selector is impossible in the low window
Proposition (unused column selector obstruction). Let \(m>n\), \(C\ge2\), and \(n\ge2C\). Fix a column \(k\). There is no old polynomial \(P(A,B)\) with \[ P|_{A_{\cdot k}=0}=1 \quad\text{and}\quad A_{ik}P\in\mathcal C_C(\Gamma_{m,n})\text{ for every }i\in[m]. \tag{2} \] In particular no unweighted old-only polynomial coefficient assignment for a complete block on that column can certify all its companion images from the bare matrix base through \(C\), at any accuracy. This makes no assertion about weighted maps or a different proper old strengthening.
Proof. The first condition makes \(P\ne0\). Ordinary polynomial degree is additive in a nonzero product, so the companion membership in the truncated space implies \(\deg P\le C-1\). Set the whole row \(B_{k\cdot}\) to zero and \(A_{ik}=t_i\) for independent binary parameters \(t_1,\ldots,t_m\). For every fixed \(t\), all remaining base equations are exactly \(\Gamma_{m,n-1}\), with its Boolean equations; the removed row contributes zero to \(AB\), independently of \(t\). Each degree-\(C\) derivation specializes without increasing degree.
The residual system has no degree-\(C\) refutation, since \(n-1\ge2C-1\), by the arbitrary-row matrix baseline. Fix one normalized linear annihilator \(\lambda\) of its degree-\(C\) PC space, independent of \(t\). Applying it coefficientwise to the specialized \(P\) gives a binary polynomial \(F(t)\) of degree at most \(C-1\). The specialized companion derivations give \(t_iF(t)=0\) at every binary \(t\), for all \(i\). At \(t=0\), \(P=1\), so \(F(0)=1\). Thus \(F\) is the indicator of the zero point on the \(m\)-cube, whose unique multilinear representative is \(\prod_i(1-t_i)\) of degree \(m\). Boolean reduction cannot increase degree, contradicting \(C-1<m\). \(\square\)
This is the matrix analogue of the handoff's parameterized certificate obstruction, using a PC annihilator of the residual system. It rejects a particular type of complete certificate, not the historical one-block PC elimination theorem. A weight can evade it: for one column, \(W=A_{1k}\), the row selecting that input, and \(P=1-A_{1k}\), give \(WA_{ik}P=0\) modulo Booleanity through degree three. The weight vanishes on the zero-column branch used in the proof, so the obstruction cannot be extended to all weights by the same argument.
The known PHP strengthening gives an affine boundary
For clarity include every fixed tensor order \(s\ge2\). The published diagonal tensor rank system has variables \(Z_{j,k,i}\), \(j\in[s]\), \(k\in[n]\), \(i\in[m]\), and equations \[ \sum_{k=1}^n\prod_{j=1}^s Z_{j,k,i_j} =\begin{cases}1&i_1=\cdots=i_s,\\0&\text{otherwise}.\end{cases} \tag{3} \] Use the same four-forbidden-row CNF for binary AND/XOR gates as in the matrix boundary. Each of the \(m^s\) entries uses \(n(s-1)\) AND gates and \(n-1\) XOR gates, followed by its output unit. Call the CNF \(F_{m,n,s}\). For \(m>n\ge2\), it has \(smn+m^s(sn-1)\) variables and \(m^s(4sn-3)\) clauses. It is polynomial size for fixed \(s\). For unsatisfiability, flatten the first index against the remaining indices: the diagonal columns give an identity matrix of rank \(m\), while a sum of \(n\) rank-one tensors would flatten to rank at most \(n\). This is the published diagonal tensor principle, not a new hardness claim.
Corollary (affine tensor CNF transfer). There is an affine substitution sending every degree-\(D\) ordinary-PC refutation of \(F_{m,n,s}\), \(D\ge3\), to a degree-\(D\) refutation of functional unary PHP on \(m\) rows and \(n\) holes. Consequently no such refutation exists when \(n\ge2D-1\). The same substitution sends a complete remaining ENS source over the CNF to a complete remaining source over the functional PHP base without increasing any original degree. It does not remove arbitrary remaining blocks.
Proof. Use the published identification \(Z_{j,k,i}\mapsto X_{ik}\). Map a partial product gate inside one summand to \(X_{ik}\) if every outer index used so far equals \(i\), and to zero otherwise. Map a partial XOR sum to the sum of these final summand images. All gate-output images are affine.
An AND image is either the identity \(X_{ik}^2=X_{ik}\), a product of two different row variables in the same hole equated to zero, or an identity with a zero input. Each gate-clause image has a Boolean/column-collision certificate through its original degree three: the degree-complete column reduction proves this on the states \(0,e_1,\ldots,e_m\). Every XOR image satisfies its defining parity identically as a binary polynomial. Its clause images vanish on the full Boolean cube and have degree at most three. Gate Boolean equations have degree-two Boolean certificates. Diagonal output units map to \(\sum_kX_{ik}-1\); off-diagonal units map to zero. Thus all old axiom images have certificates within their original degrees.
Functional PHP additionally includes same-row exclusions. This is an allowed strengthening, not an assertion that they follow cheaply from the bare matrix equations. Its proper PC range is supplied by the matching-moment extension and PC closure theorem, for arbitrary \(m\). Affine substitution and the axiom preludes replay the original proof through \(D\). Leave fresh ENS coefficients unchanged and apply the map everywhere in their inputs; every block remains complete and all original charges are preserved as upper bounds. \(\square\)
For \(s=2\), the identification is \(A_{ik}=B_{ki}=X_{ik}\). This improves the CNF boundary's transfer to the known hard functional PHP base; it is not a degree-preserving transfer back to the bare matrix base. Its extra affine and exclusion relations therefore do not contradict matrix affine rigidity.
The whole eligible column batch is now cheap
Corollary (tensor column normalization). In the preceding source, select any number of fresh blocks whose current inputs after the affine boundary map are old-only polynomials in one common PHP column per block. They may have arbitrary input arity, polynomial degree and positive accuracy, and different blocks may use different columns. All selected blocks can be removed simultaneously without increasing the ordinary-PC proof degree; every unselected block stays complete on its actual transformed inputs, with its original degree ledger. The condition is on the selected current inputs, not a property of every compiled source.
Proof and exact ledger. Apply the recorded affine column-state normalizer, whose full proof was reread. Its state-interpolation and degree-reduction proof uses arbitrary row count; the original notation \(m=n+1\) is not needed for this local statement. On states \(0,e_1,\ldots,e_m\), choose one nonzero input when there is one, and interpolate that choice using the affine state indicators. The first coefficient row becomes affine \(\beta_i(X)\), all other rows zero, and the product becomes \(H=1-\sum_i\beta_i g_i\). For original input degree \(d_i\), maximum \(\delta\) and accuracy \(h\), each companion image has a column-ideal certificate through \[ d_i+\deg H\le d_i+\delta+1\le d_i+h(\delta+1). \] Field images \(\beta_i^2-\beta_i\) have old Boolean certificates through two. The global variable map is affine, and all axiom images have certificates at their own original costs. Hence the entire batch, all later input occurrences and every subsequent inference replay at unchanged degree.
For the tested full matrix-column tuples, the explicit choice is \(\beta_i=X_{ik}\) in the first row. Then \(H=1-\sum_iX_{ik}^2\), and \[ X_{ik}H=(X_{ik}+1)(X_{ik}^2-X_{ik}) +\sum_{j\ne i}X_{jk}(X_{ik}X_{jk}) \] over \(\mathbb F_2\), a degree-three certificate. Thus all columns are handled together, not one chosen column at a time. Their selectors have affine column-state representatives. Arbitrary later mixed inputs are retained; claiming that their selectors stay column-local would exceed the theorem. \(\square\)
