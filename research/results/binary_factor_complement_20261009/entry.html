The cheaper degree is already available after a supplied factor

Factors may differ between blocks; there is no common global factor. Let \(d\le K\le v\), \(t\ge1\), and let every original block accuracy be at least \(t\). Define \(N_v(k)=\sum_{j\le k}\binom vj\) and \(\eta_d=N_v(K-d)/(2N_v(K))\).

Corollary (factored batch removal). If \[ M(1-\eta_d)^t<1,\qquad b+K+(t+1)(a_0+d)\le D, \tag{1} \] there are constant binary rows in the original input positions and \(f\in V_K\), with \(wf\notin J_D\), annihilating all actual weighted companions. Coefficient fields vanish. An original degree-\(B\) full-source replay additionally requires \(b+K+B\le D\), with any earlier weighted memberships preserved at their original costs. In the one-level old-only setting with that old-base inclusion, this excludes such PC and ordinary NS refutations.

Proof. Apply the single-branch weighted approximation theorem to all residual tuples \(q_{a,i}\), with the same surviving weight \(w\), cutoff \(K\), and \(t\) rows. Its smaller buffer \(b+K+(t+1)d\le D\) follows from (1). It selects binary matrices \(C_a\) and one \(f\in V_K\), with \(wf\notin J_D\), killing every normalized companion \[ wf q_{a,i}P_{q,a},\qquad P_{q,a}=\prod_{u\le t}(1-\sum_i C_{a,u,i}q_{a,i}). \] These residual inputs are an analysis device, not new legal source positions. Use exactly the same constants in the original tuple, giving \[ P_{g,a}=\prod_{u\le t}(1-H_a\sum_i C_{a,u,i}q_{a,i}). \] The factored-prefix identity gives \[ [wf P_{g,a}g_{a,i}]=[wfH_aP_{q,a}q_{a,i}] \] through (1).
