Proof. On the Fourier polynomial ring let \(\iota\) conjugate coefficients and interchange \(U_e,V_e\). It preserves degree and the local-domain relations. A defining charged relation has \[ \omega^a m_\alpha\sim\omega^b m_\beta,\qquad \alpha-\beta=\partial s,\quad |\operatorname{supp}s|<n/2,\quad c\cdot s=b-a. \] Under \(\iota\), it becomes the relation with phases \(2a,2b\), exponents \(-\alpha,-\beta\), and witness \(-s\). Its phase condition is \(c\cdot(-s)=2(b-a)\) in \(\mathbb F_3\), so it is valid in the same window. Hence \(\iota J_D=J_D\). Direct substitution gives \(\iota\Phi(x_{e,a})=\Phi(x_{e,a})\), and therefore \(\Phi\sigma=\iota\Phi\). Thus \(\widehat J_D\) is coefficient-conjugation stable.
