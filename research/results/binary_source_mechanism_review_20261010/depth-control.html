Degree descent is not circuit-depth descent

Corollary (binary coordinate depth). There are multilinear polynomials of degree at most \(n\) over \(\mathbb F_2\) with linear-size, depth-at-most-four, unbounded-fan-in arithmetic circuits over \(\mathbb F_4\), whose Boolean functions have no polynomial-size fixed-depth arithmetic circuits over \(\mathbb F_2\). Consequently coefficient projection, even restricted to already binary polynomials and Boolean-valued maps, cannot in general preserve polynomial size and constant depth simultaneously. This is a scope corollary of Smolensky's Theorem 2, not a new lower-bound technique.

Proof. Write \(\mathbb F_4=\mathbb F_2(\zeta)\), with \(\zeta^2+\zeta+1=0\), and put \[ P_n=\prod_{i=1}^n(1+(\zeta-1)x_i),\qquad Q_n=\prod_{i=1}^n(1+(\zeta^2-1)x_i),\qquad H_n=P_n+Q_n . \] Coefficientwise Frobenius exchanges \(P_n,Q_n\), so \(H_n\) has binary coefficients. The factors have disjoint variables, hence \(H_n\) is multilinear of degree at most \(n\). Scalar multiplications, affine sums, the two unbounded products and their final sum give depth at most four and linear size over \(\mathbb F_4\).

For a Boolean input of Hamming weight \(k\), the two products evaluate to \(\zeta^k,\zeta^{2k}\). Their sum is zero for \(k\equiv0\pmod3\), and one for \(k\equiv1,2\pmod3\). Thus \(1+H_n\) is the residue-zero MOD3 function. A fixed-depth polynomial-size binary arithmetic circuit agreeing with \(H_n\) on the cube would have only binary values at every gate there: multiplication is AND and addition is parity. With a final NOT this would give polynomial-size fixed-depth \(\mathrm{AC}^0[2]\) circuits for MOD3, contradicting the cited theorem. This argument excludes even agreement on the cube, a weaker demand than computing the polynomial formally. \(\square\)

Connection to the actual descent theorem. Every unital \(\mathbb F_2\)-linear coefficient projection fixes \(H_n\). In the Boolean ideal its fresh-field relation \(H_n^2-H_n\) has an ordinary certificate through degree \(2n\): expand in squarefree monomials and telescope each \(m^2-m\) using the equations \(x_i^2-x_i\). Thus the issue persists for a Boolean-valued map satisfying its field equation; it is not merely the inadmissible nonbinary-valued product \(P_n\).

For an explicit complete-map control, take \(n\ge2\), \(W=1\), \(T=n\), original source \(\{y-H_n(x),\,y^2-y\}\), ceiling \(B=n\), and the Boolean-ideal pseudoideal through \(D=n^2\). The image \(y\mapsto H_n\) meets \(b+TB\le D\). Any degree-at-most-\(n\) descended map satisfying the first equation agrees with \(H_n\) on the cube, so it cannot also have polynomial-size fixed-depth binary circuitry. This is a satisfiable scope control; the possibly large expanded binary description of \(H_n\) is not a qualifying hard CNF or a Frege lower bound.

The existing weighted map theorem promises degrees, memberships and one rank guarantee, not circuit depth. None of those conclusions changes. For the IPS alternative, distinguish the original binary circuit class from compact extension-field representations. Nor does this control exclude a different useful weighted witness for a hard source. Smolensky's own root-of-unity construction underlies the example; no independent novelty is claimed.
