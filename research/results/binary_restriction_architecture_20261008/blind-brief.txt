Goal-level independent architecture stage. Read this brief only, not notebook living sections or the current route. Read-only, no computations, edits or external tools. This is an exploratory architecture proposal, not certification of a theorem.

Goal: For every fixed depth d, exhibit one unsatisfiable family (allowed to depend on d, not on the polynomial size exponent) whose AC0[2]-Frege refutations have size L^omega(1) in encoding length L. No formula family is mandated. The all-prime goal is broader, but binary first is accepted. No bound is currently established.

Available scoped tools:
1. Proper degree-D binary pseudoideals for charged ternary Tseitin on bounded-degree expanders: max(4,2Delta)<=D<epsilon*n/8, arbitrary vertex charges of nonzero total. Quotient coordinates are degree-bounded character/cut-code classes over F4 with a compatible F2 form. This contains old PC consequences, not asserted equal.
2. Binary costed CNF compiler under coherent profile/presentation hypotheses: structural value degree L0 <=(d+1)h^d; proof-height-independent PC source ceiling max(M_fr,10)L0. Complete product blocks keep every companion g_i P and fresh coefficient field equation, with original product weight h(delta+1). No unrestricted source exclusion.
3. On one proper Boolean pseudoideal and a binary weight w of degree b, a subspace X of its degree-K weighted image has a binary multiplying factor f of degree K with wf surviving and next degree-K multiplication rank >=dim(X)/2, at b+3K<=D. This requires the supplied X, not automatic small errors.
4. A complete two-level restricted-source theorem allows arbitrary affine parent mixtures of bottom products, polynomial block inventory, bounded-degree old bottom inputs and parent old parts, if the bottom input spaces admit q=o(v/log v) nested chains. For a fixed old family with PC barrier gamma*v, accuracy h=floor(v^eta), eta<1/6, it excludes PC refutations through v^(1/3). No arbitrary source chain cover or full-depth recurrence.
5. Exact filtered Boolean ranks and coded approximation for homogeneous linear mixtures of disjoint degree-r monomials. For r=Theta(log v), polynomial inventory, K/v fixed below1/2, a sublinear-degree batch makes errors polynomially small relative to full Boolean dimension N_v(K). Transfer to a weighted hard quotient retains log(N_v(K)/h_w(K)); its natural Boolean block projectors do not preserve charged one-hot equations.
6. A side programme has a conditional near-half-variable PC-degree route to the same Frege goal, but no qualifying hard family. Known linear degree barriers have not met its full encoding/parameter condition. Do not treat the family premise as supplied.
7. Narrow clause width is not polynomial degree; this does not give finite-field error ranks or hard-family survival.
