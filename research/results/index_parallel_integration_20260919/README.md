# Parallel index integration and permutation-probability audit

The coordinator integrates disjoint, source-fingerprinted worker proposals into
the canonical registry. Classification, significance and dependency review are
separate dispositions; an inventoried dependency is not a certificate of its truth.

- `classifications.py` and `classification-integration.json`: 504 remaining
  baseline classifications, preserving original claim text and reviewed seeds.
- `register_audit.py` and `audit-registration.json`: the dated elementary
  permutation counterexamples, two retracted historical assertions, and scoped
  proof-status corrections. The full mathematical argument is in the notebook.
- `significance.py` and `significance-integration.json`: nine completed bounded
  literature/scope assessments; unknown novelty remains unknown.
- `formalization_scopes.py`: four obsolete prospective scope phrases replaced by
  the separately verified common-kernel assembly reference, without expanding
  coverage or replaying Lean.

These scripts describe one-time guarded applications to the declared baseline;
they refuse an already-applied classification/audit rather than overwriting it.
Run substantial scripts through `compute.sh` under the shared resource policy.
The worker patch directories preserve exact source selections and provenance.
Their measured intervals overlap and must not be summed into coordinator time.

Dependency proposals are generated in `parallel_index_dependency_merge/` and
applied only after source checks, graph diagnostics and immutable input-hash
comparison. Scope-normalization decisions and original worker locators remain
in its reports. New findings correct the current metadata and a dated notebook
entry; historical statements and proofs are not rewritten.

The probability audit refutes the full permutation-matrix negative-association
premise and the exact generic exponential avoidance bound. It does not refute
every dense-label or reader conclusion. Conditional proof use and downstream
review scope remain distinct from a statement-level counterexample. The main
Frege goal is unchanged and still open.
