Two filtrations must be kept separate

Over any field, fix a source system \(\mathcal F\) with supplied polynomial degrees at most \(B\). Use the existing paid representation: finite-dimensional spaces \[ E_0\hookrightarrow E_1\hookrightarrow\cdots\hookrightarrow E_B, \] variable maps \(X_{i,b}:E_b\to E_{b+1}\) commuting with inclusions and with each other wherever the compositions fit, and \(0\ne u\in E_0\). Padding by inclusions defines \(\operatorname{ev}_b(f)=f(X)u\) for \(\deg f\le b\). Assume every supplied generator evaluates to zero at its original degree. These data are the marked cyclic part of the source-cover setting; no fixed external observation is required here.

Give each \(E_b\) an additional finite increasing filtration \[ 0=F_0E_b\subseteq F_1E_b\subseteq\cdots\subseteq F_tE_b=E_b. \] This is an auxiliary component filtration, not the polynomial-degree filtration. Require each \(X_{i,b}\) to preserve its index, and each degree inclusion \(j_b:E_b\hookrightarrow E_{b+1}\) to be strict: \[ j_b(F_aE_b)=j_b(E_b)\cap F_aE_{b+1}. \tag{1} \] A single flag on \(E_B\), restricted by intersection to each \(E_b\), ensures (1); preservation by the actual variable maps remains an additional requirement.

Lemma (marked filtration descent). Under these hypotheses, let \(a\) be the least index with \(u\in F_aE_0\). The diagonal maps on \[ G_b=F_aE_b/F_{a-1}E_b \] have injective degree inclusions and a nonzero marked vector \(\bar u\). Their cyclic subspaces generated by \(\bar u\) give a source-compatible paid representation through \(B\). In particular their terminal evaluation kernel is a proper degree-\(B\) PC pseudoideal containing \(\mathcal F\). Thus a strict triangular correction of fixed diagonal actions cannot create a marked source witness if none of the diagonal components admits one.

Proof. The choice of \(a\) makes \(\bar u\ne0\). Strictness says that an element of \(F_aE_b\) whose image falls into \(F_{a-1}E_{b+1}\) was already in \(F_{a-1}E_b\). Hence each induced \(G_b\to G_{b+1}\) is injective, and the image of \(\bar u\) remains nonzero through \(B\). Preservation of both \(F_a\) and \(F_{a-1}\) gives the induced variable maps. Their commutation identities descend.

Every polynomial evaluation on \(u\) stays in \(F_a\). Its class in \(G_b\) is exactly the same polynomial evaluated on \(\bar u\), by induction on monomials and then linearity. In particular each original source generator still evaluates to zero. Restricting \(G_b\) to the span of polynomial evaluations of degree at most \(b\) preserves the inclusions, the paid variable actions and the marked vector.

For completeness, if a polynomial \(f\) of actual degree \(b<B\) has zero terminal evaluation, injectivity of the induced degree inclusions makes its evaluation in \(G_b\) zero. Applying the induced variable map makes \(x_if\) zero at grade \(b+1\), hence at \(B\). The terminal kernel is therefore closed under legal multiplication, including cancellation/reset, as well as linear combinations; it contains the original generators. It is proper because the marked image of one is nonzero. This is the same PC argument as the source-cover theorem, now on the single diagonal component. \(\square\)

The lemma is a necessity statement for this construction. It does not say that every compatible graded witness lifts, or that a nilpotent ideal automatically supplies a strict auxiliary filtration. Nor may ordinary polynomial degree be substituted for \(F_a\): a variable increases polynomial degree and need not preserve its index. It also does not require preservation of a prescribed scalar functional.

Why strictness and the marked vector matter

Without (1), associated grading can lose an injective inclusion. For example, take \(E_0=E_1=k u\), inclusion the identity, \(F_0=0,F_2=E_b\), but \(F_1E_0=0\) and \(F_1E_1=E_1\). The degree-two auxiliary graded class of \(u\) is nonzero at \(E_0\) and zero at \(E_1\). Thus ordinary injectivity before grading does not justify PC reset after grading. This is a flag control, not a source lower bound.

The earlier filtered-complex control also remains in force: survival on an early spectral-sequence page does not prove survival in the final object. The present lemma goes from an already valid strict marked representation to a diagonal one. It gives no reverse lifting theorem and assumes no spectral-sequence degeneration.

Local components do not automatically become a joint reference

Take two separate complete accuracy-one blocks, each with single constant input one and its own fresh coefficient \(r_1,r_2\). Their companions are \(1-r_1,1-r_2\); their original field equations have degree two. The joint source is satisfiable by \(r_1=r_2=1\). Compare two reference components: one uses \((r_1,r_2)=(1,0)\), the other \((0,1)\). Each handles one block and fails the other.

The most general binary two-dimensional upper-triangular coefficient actions with those fixed diagonal choices are \[ R_1=\begin{pmatrix}1&a\\0&0\end{pmatrix},\qquad R_2=\begin{pmatrix}0&b\\0&1\end{pmatrix},\qquad a,b\in\mathbb F_2. \] Each is idempotent. Commutation, required for a full polynomial action, holds exactly when \(a=b\). Even without using commutation, \[ (I-R_1)+(I-R_2)=I+(a+b) \begin{pmatrix}0&1\\0&0\end{pmatrix} \] is invertible, because the off-diagonal matrix squares to zero. A vector killed by both companions must therefore be zero. No off-diagonal choice supplies the missing joint marked witness. Replacing the reference by \(R_1=R_2=I\) solves both blocks immediately; the obstruction concerns the specified diagonal data, not the source or all possible local-to-global methods.

This is the same distinction captured by the lemma at arbitrary cutoff: the nonzero graded class at the first filtration level containing the marked vector already has to satisfy every source equation, not just the equations assigned to its local block. A triangular extension can preserve or obstruct such compatibility, but the specified setup cannot manufacture it from components that have no joint cyclic witness. Non-strict constructions, filtered maps that change the auxiliary index, or a different diagonal representation require separate proofs and remain outside this conclusion.
