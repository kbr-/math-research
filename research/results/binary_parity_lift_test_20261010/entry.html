The exact invariant factors

Proposition (parity kernel lift gap). For every \(r\ge3\) and \(s\ge2\) in this setup, multiplication by \(G_s\) on \(U\) has Smith form \[ \operatorname{diag}(\underbrace{1,\ldots,1}_{r},2) \tag{2} \] over \(A_s\), with other rows zero. Its reduction has rank \(r\) on the \((r+1)\)-dimensional domain and kernel \(\langle1+\sum_i z_i\rangle\). Its integral affine kernel is exactly \[ \left\{c\left(1-\sum_i z_i\right):c\in 2^{s-1}A_s\right\}. \tag{3} \] Consequently every such affine kernel vector reduces to zero; no surviving binary affine kernel lifts inside the same affine domain. The domain itself has a unit \((r+1)\)-minor.

Proof. No nonzero cut is supported on the selected complement edges. Their \(3^r\) local character labels are distinct in the quotient, and all have representatives of degree at most \(r\). The local Fourier evaluation matrix is invertible over \(A_s\), since three and differences of distinct third roots are units. Therefore it is a legitimate invertible change of output coordinates on this summand. Every binary indicator pattern occurs among local ternary labels. Remove duplicate evaluation rows by invertible subtraction, as in the two-edge control, leaving one row for each binary pattern. This is local interpolation inside an independent summand, not evaluation on global solutions of the inconsistent graph.

The domain rows are \((1,z_1,\ldots,z_r)\). The zero pattern and the \(r\) singleton patterns give a unit domain minor. In the multiplication matrix, even patterns give zero rows, and an odd subset \(S\subseteq[r]\) gives \[ (1,\mathbf1_S). \] The singleton rows have an identity minor on the \(r\) variable columns. Subtract their sum for \(i\in S\) from the row indexed by \(S\); this leaves \((1-|S|,0,\ldots,0)\). Every such remaining scalar is even, and a three-element subset supplies \(-2\). Hence the residual one-column ideal is exactly \((2)\), proving (2) by unit pivots and row elimination.

Alternatively solve for an affine vector \(f=c_0+\sum_i c_i z_i\). Singleton rows give \(c_i=-c_0\). A triple row gives \(-2c_0=0\), equivalent in \(A_s\) to \(c_0\in2^{s-1}A_s\). This condition suffices because \(1-|S|\) is even on every odd subset. This proves (3). Reduction of (2) gives rank \(r\), and the reduced singleton equations give precisely the claimed one-dimensional residue kernel. \(\square\)

The restriction \(r\ge3\) supplies a triple and is substantive; for two inputs the earlier canonical lift has an actual affine kernel and zero final invariant factor. This is why that two-input control could not decide the present universal lifting hypothesis. The statement now covers all larger arities analytically, with no size sweep.

Binary removal remains cheap

The binary weight \(w=1+g\) has degree one and survives: its independent local character expansion is nonzero. Its product with the companion is \(g+g^2\), an old Boolean consequence through degree two. Original weighted replay still costs \(1+B=4\), which fits the inherited window here. The binary kernel is therefore exactly the useful solution that primitive integral lifting would discard.

A higher-degree integral lift exists: \(1-G_s\) reduces to \(1+g\) and annihilates \(G_s\) pointwise modulo \(2^s\). The recorded Boolean certificate convention gives the identity through degree \(2d_s\), so this alternative is available in the charged quotient if that larger window also fits. This is an upper bound, not a claim that \(d_s\) is the optimal degree of an integral annihilator. The affine impossibility proves only that degree one is insufficient under (2). Do not optimize a local weight-degree constant as if it advanced the unrestricted bound.

Over the characteristic-zero coefficient field, the exact parity-bit polynomial on these \(r\) indicators has the same odd-subset rows, so its affine-domain rank is \(r+1\). Thus a rational-rank upper bound cannot see this binary deficit even though the domain has a unit minor and the lift is canonical. Smith form does see it as the single nonunit pivot two. No contradiction arises with the earlier inequality that binary rank is at most rational rank.

Likewise full-cube idempotence does not force a chosen truncated domain's multiplication matrix to be a split projector. The input domain here is affine, while its output lies in the larger local summand. The existing joint branch representation supplies a different enlarged operator model and retains its input-word growth; it does not produce the missing primitive affine kernel. The weaker deflation remains valid and only needs the binary rank, not such an integral lift.
