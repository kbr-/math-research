Pigeon-direction FI-modules of closure windows

16 October 2026 · Working proof (imports two Church–Ellenberg–Farb–Nagpal theorems) · FI-modules of closure windows.

It bears on joint-error-savings, whose evidence on this line is finite checks at a few sizes, and it comes first because the localization item had to rotate after two entries without bearing; the question is whether a functorial structure makes such checks settle every size.

Best outcome. A theorem making kernel dimensions of a symmetric family of systems polynomial in the number of indices, with a range one can compute, so that finitely many computed sizes determine the rest.

Question. The item's first test: are the degree-\(D\) closures functorial along injections, adding a pigeon or a hole, at fixed degree? And are the windows and the common kernels of factor maps finitely generated FI-modules?

General statement. lem:fi-system-windows: for a family of systems on a set of variables carrying an action of injections, under which the axioms of degree at most \(D\) go to members, the degree-\(D\) windows and the common kernels of factor maps on fixed variables form finitely generated FI-modules over any field, with dimensions eventually polynomial by the Church–Ellenberg–Farb–Nagpal theorem; for the product pigeonhole systems with \(K<D\le n\) the windows have an explicit monomial basis, so the dimensions are explicit polynomials in the pigeons at every size, and along \(m=n+1\) for \(n\ge D\).

FI-system windows

Setting. Fix a field \(k\), degrees \(K\) and \(D\), and a finitely generated FI-set of variables: for each \(t\ge0\) a finite set \(X_t\) of variables and for each injection \(f:[t]\to[t']\) an injective map \(X_t\to X_{t'}\), functorial, such that every variable lies in the image of \(X_s\) for some \(s\le r\), a fixed bound (for instance \(X_t=[t]\times Y\) with \(Y\) fixed, \(r=1\)). For each \(t\) let \(\Gamma_t\) be a set of polynomials in \(X_t\), and let \(\phi_f\) be the induced ring map. Call the family an FI-system when \(\phi_f(\Gamma_t\cap V_D)\subseteq C_D(\Gamma_{t'})\) for every injection, \(C_D\) the degree-\(D\) closure. Put \(Q_t=V_K(t)/(C_D(\Gamma_t)\cap V_K(t))\).

Statement. (a) Each \(\phi_f\) maps \(C_D(\Gamma_t)\) into \(C_D(\Gamma_{t'})\), so \(t\mapsto Q_t\) is an FI-module. (b) It is finitely generated, in degree at most \(rK\). (c) For a fixed finite set \(F\) of polynomials in the variables over a fixed index set \(P_0\), with \(\deg w+K\le D\) for \(w\in F\), the common kernels \(W_t=\{[u]\in Q_t:wu\in C_D(\Gamma_t)\ \forall w\in F\}\) are well defined and form a sub-FI-module under the injections fixing \(P_0\). (d) Over any field, \(\dim Q_t\) and \(\dim W_t\) are eventually polynomial in \(t\) (Church–Ellenberg–Farb–Nagpal, arXiv 1210.1854, its Noetherian property and polynomial-dimension theorems, read from the source). (e) The product pigeonhole systems at a fixed number \(n\) of holes, \(X_m=[m]\times[n]\), \(\Gamma_m\) the pigeon axioms \(\prod_j(1-x_{ij})\) and the hole axioms, and the functional systems with linear pigeon axioms and functionality, are FI-systems in the pigeons. In the holes the inclusion need not carry pigeon axioms into the closure: at \(D=n\) the smaller system's pigeon axiom has degree \(D\), and by lem:top-degree-axiom-inertness on \(n+1\) holes it is not in the larger system's closure; the substitution \(x_{i,n}=0\) is functorial in the opposite direction. (f) For the product pigeonhole systems with \(K<D\le n\), \(C_D\cap V_K\) is spanned by the monomials of degree at most \(K\) with two variables in one hole, so \(Q\) has the basis of the monomials with distinct holes and \(\dim Q_{m}=\sum_{s\le\min(K,n)}\binom ns m^s\) for every \(m\) and \(n\). Along the diagonal, pigeons \(\{*\}\sqcup[t]\) and holes \([t]\), an FI-set with \(r=2\), the hole-axiom windows form an FI-module for every \(t\), generated in degree at most \(2K\), and agree with \(Q_{t+1,t}\) for \(t\ge D\), where \(\dim Q_{t+1,t}=\sum_{s\le K}\binom ts(t+1)^s\).

Proof. (a) The closure is the smallest subspace of the polynomials of degree at most \(D\) containing the axioms of degree at most \(D\) and closed under multiplying members of degree below \(D\) by variables. The preimage \(\phi_f^{-1}(C_D(\Gamma_{t'}))\cap V_D(t)\) is such a subspace: it contains \(\Gamma_t\cap V_D\) by hypothesis, as \(\phi_f\) preserves degree, and if \(\phi_f(c)\in C_D(\Gamma_{t'})\) with \(\deg c<D\), then \(\phi_f(xc)=\phi_f(x)\phi_f(c)\) is a member, \(\phi_f(x)\) being a variable. So it contains \(C_D(\Gamma_t)\), and \(\phi_f\) induces \(Q_t\to Q_{t'}\), functorially. (b) A monomial of degree at most \(K\) involves at most \(K\) variables, each in the image of \(X_s\) for some \(s\le r\), hence lies in the image of \(X_{rK}\) under an injection; the finitely many monomials over \(X_{rK}\) generate \(V_K\), and \(Q\) is a quotient. (c) If \(c\in C_D\cap V_K\) and \(\deg w\le D-K\), multiplying \(c\) by the variables of each monomial of \(w\) one at a time keeps every intermediate degree below \(D\), so \(wc\in C_D\) and \(W_t\) is well defined on classes. For \(f\) fixing \(P_0\), \(\phi_f(w)=w\), and \(wu\in C_D\) gives \(w\phi_f(u)=\phi_f(wu)\in C_D\) by (a). (d) Under the injections fixing \(P_0\), a monomial of degree at most \(K\) involves at most \(rK\) indices outside \(P_0\), so it is the image of a monomial on \(P_0\sqcup[rK]\) under an injection fixing \(P_0\); so \(Q\) restricted to these injections is a finitely generated FI-module, \(W\) is a sub-FI-module, finitely generated by the Noetherian property over the Noetherian ring \(k\), and the polynomial-dimension theorem gives eventual polynomiality of both dimensions. (e) An injection of pigeons carries a pigeon axiom to a pigeon axiom and a hole axiom \(x_{ij}x_{kj}\), \(i\ne k\), to a hole axiom; the same holds for functionality and linear pigeon axioms. For holes, the substitution \(x_{i,n}=0\) does not raise degree, sends variables to variables or \(0\), and carries the larger system's axioms to the smaller's or to \(0\), and the argument of (a) applies to it. (f) By lem:top-degree-axiom-inertness, for \(D\le n\) the members of degree below \(D\) are those of the hole-axiom closure, the span of the monomials of degree below \(D\) with two variables in one hole; as \(K<D\), \(C_D\cap V_K\) is the span of those of degree at most \(K\). The remaining monomials, with distinct holes, are independent modulo it: the hole-respecting assignments form a down-set of \(0/1\) vectors, and a combination of distinct-hole monomials that vanishes on it has, at a minimal monomial \(M\) with nonzero coefficient \(c_M\), the value \(c_M\) at the indicator of \(M\), a hole-respecting point, so all coefficients are zero; a monomial of degree \(s\) with distinct holes chooses \(s\) holes and a pigeon for each. For the diagonal, an injection acting on pigeons and holes carries hole axioms to hole axioms, so (a) applies to the hole-axiom systems at every \(t\), and for \(t\ge D\) their windows are the \(Q_{t+1,t}\).

What it gives. At a fixed number of holes, window and common-kernel dimensions are eventually polynomial in the pigeons, by (d), without an explicit range. In the regime \(K<D\le n\) of the line's pigeonhole checks, (f) gives the window dimension exactly at every number of pigeons, and along \(m=n+1\) for \(n\ge D\), so there the FI-module statement is dominated by the explicit basis and is kept with that relation. Outside that regime, for \(D>n\) or other systems, the relation degree of \(Q\) can be as large as the first size at which a new low-degree member appears, and the record gives no bound; that explicit ranges exist in terms of generation and relation degrees (Church–Ellenberg) is an untested hypothesis here, since the source was not read, and nothing below rests on it.

Degenerate cases. A size with \(1\in C_D(\Gamma_t)\): \(Q_t=0\), and by (a) the family stays improper as \(t\) grows, so the polynomial is then \(0\). \(F\) empty: \(W=Q\). \(K=0\): \(Q_t\) is \(k\) or \(0\). \(D\) below every axiom's degree: \(\Gamma\) contributes nothing and \(Q=V_K\).

Nonvacuity. In Pang's matched ledger with \(p=2\), checking only the lemma's hypotheses, over \(\mathbb F_2\): the product pigeonhole systems with \(n=4\) holes at \(D=3\), \(K=1\); the pigeon axioms have degree \(4>D\) and are excluded by \(\Gamma\cap V_D\), the hole axioms go to hole axioms, and by (f) \(\dim Q_m=4m+1\); for \(F=\{x_{00}\}\), the common kernel contains \(1+x_{00}\) and the \(x_{k0}\), \(k\ne0\), \(m\) independent classes, with dimension exactly \(5\) at \(m=5\) in check:clique-factor-kernels.

New mathematics. lem:fi-system-windows: symmetric families of systems whose injections carry the low-degree axioms to members give finitely generated FI-modules of closure windows and of fixed-factor common kernels, with eventually polynomial dimensions over any field; for the product pigeonhole systems with \(K<D\le n\) the windows have an explicit monomial basis, which dominates the FI statement there.
