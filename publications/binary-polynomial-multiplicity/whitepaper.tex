\begin{abstract}
Bishnoi, Boyadzhiyska, Das and M\'esz\'aros ask whether a polynomial over
\(\F_2\) that vanishes with multiplicity at least \(k\) at every nonzero
point of \(\F_2^n\), and with multiplicity at most \(k-1\) at the origin,
has degree at least \(2k-\lfloor k/2^{n-1}\rfloor\), when
\(k\ge 2^{n-2}\). We observe that the requested inequality is an immediate
consequence of the multiplicity Schwartz--Zippel lemma of Dvir, Kopparty,
Saraf and Sudan. The deduction works for all positive integers \(n,k\),
without the stated restriction on \(k\).
\end{abstract}
