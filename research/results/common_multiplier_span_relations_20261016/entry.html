The proof learns each input \(f\) of the current block as a consequence: every later block's span contains \(f\), so a selector substitution weighted by \(\chi_\alpha(f)\) turns the original refutation into a derivation of \(\chi_\alpha(f)\), and the learned inputs are reused. Its Lemma 8.2 relaxes nesting to nesting modulo a space \(H\), \((V_1+H)/H\subseteq\cdots\subseteq(V_s+H)/H\), provided every element of \(H\) has a supplied derivation from the old system within degree \(c\), at degree \(\max\{c,D+(p-1)\delta,(p+1)\delta\}\). The shared-span hypothesis allows incomparable spans: in the shared-span entry's family the spans \(T_1\oplus\Lambda\) of equal dimension are pairwise incomparable.
