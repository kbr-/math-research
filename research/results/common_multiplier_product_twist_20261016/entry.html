Top-degree twist scalarity

Statement. Let \(J=C\) be the degree-\(n\) closure of the product pigeonhole system with \(m\) pigeons and \(n\) holes, over any field, in the multilinear ring or with Boolean axioms. Let \(Y\) be the hole-respecting assignments and \(x=x_{ab}\) a hole variable. Let \(S\) be a set of variables other than \(x\), let \(P=\{x_{kb}:k\ne a\}\cup S\), and let \(1\le K'\le n-1\).

  • Put \(F=\{f\in V_{K'}:pf\in C\text{ for all }p\in P\}\).
  • Let \(\tau\) be the number of distinct holes the variables of \(P\) lie in: \(1\) plus the number of holes other than \(b\) that \(S\) touches, when \(m\ge2\).
Then \(F\not\subseteq C\) if and only if \(\tau\le K'\). When it holds and hole \(b\) is touched (\(b\in H\), for instance \(m\ge2\)), \(f=x\prod_{j\in H\setminus\{b\}}\bigl(1-\sum_kx_{kj}\bigr)\), with \(H\) the touched holes, lies in \(F\setminus C\) and has degree \(\tau\). That is the scalar repair with multiplier \(m=\prod_{j\in H\setminus\{b\}}(1-\sum_kx_{kj})\), of degree \(\tau-1\le K'-1\): \(xm\) satisfies the same conditions and survives. Consequence for twisted repairs. On this system at any window \(W=D-K\ge2\) with room \(TW+\deg\nu\le n\), take a twisted repair whose weight is \(x\), whose errors are the variables of \(S\), and whose twisted variable is \(x\) with the other variables untwisted. By the identity of lem:twist-window-trade, some coordinate gives an \(f_1\in F\setminus C\) with \(K'=T+\deg\nu\le n-1\). So, with \(b\in H\), a scalar repair with multiplier of degree \(\tau-1\le T+\deg\nu-1\) exists for the same errors. At \(T=1\), or whenever \(\tau\le\deg\nu+1\), that degree is at most \(\deg\nu\), and the twist adds nothing beyond a scalar repair. For \(T\ge2\) the scalar multiplier can exceed \(\deg\nu\), though it is still a valid scalar repair. Only the necessary direction is used.

Proof.

  • Each \(p=x_{kj}\in P\) is a monomial error of degree \(1\) on one hole. For \(f\) of degree at most \(K'\) with \(K'+1\le n\), lem:top-degree-killer-transfer (monomial errors on distinct holes, all \(m,n\)) gives \(pf\in C\) exactly when \(f\) vanishes on the support of \(p\) on \(Y\), the cylinder \(\{s_j=k\}\).
  • By lem:top-degree-axiom-inertness, as recorded in cor:hole-occupancy-kernels, \(C\) below degree \(n\) is the vanishing ideal of \(Y\). So \(f\notin C\) exactly when \(f\) is nonzero on \(Y\).
  • Hence \(F\not\subseteq C\) exactly when some function of degree at most \(K'\) vanishes on the union of the cylinders and is nonzero on \(Y\). On \(Y\), with base state empty, lem:cylinder-killer-degree makes the least degree of such a function the transversal number of the cylinders' coordinate sets. These are single holes, so the transversal number is \(\tau\).
  • The killer. At hole \(b\) take the state "pigeon \(a\)": no cylinder assigns it, since \(x\notin S\) and the partners \(x_{kb}\) have \(k\ne a\). Its indicator is \(x\). At every other touched hole take the state "empty", which no cylinder assigns, with indicator \(1-\sum_kx_{kj}\). By the same lemma their product attains degree \(\tau\). It vanishes on every cylinder, through the factor at that cylinder's hole, and it is nonzero at the assignment putting pigeon \(a\) in hole \(b\) and leaving the others empty, which lies in \(Y\).
  • The consequence. A twisted coordinate's \(f_1\) satisfies \(x_{kb}f_1\in C\) (soundness at the hole axioms \(xx_{kb}\)) and \(Ef_1\in C\) (killing \(xE\)), and some coordinate survives with \(f_1\notin C\). That is the twist-window entry's pair identity with \(g_0=0\).

Hypotheses, each with its step.

  • The top degree \(D'=n\) and \(K'+1\le n\) are the hypotheses of lem:top-degree-killer-transfer.
  • \(K'\le n-1\) gives the vanishing-ideal description of survival.
  • Errors that are variables other than \(x\) make each condition a degree-one monomial error and leave the state "pigeon \(a\)" free at hole \(b\).
  • \(b\in H\), given by \(m\ge2\), carries the degree of the killer: without it the product has degree \(\tau+1\), and on the slice \(x=1\) any multiplier must vanish on the \(\tau\) cylinders, so no scalar repair of degree at most \(K'\) exists when \(\tau=K'\).
  • \(T=1\), or \(\tau\le\deg\nu+1\), is what makes the scalar multiplier's degree at most \(\deg\nu\) in the consequence.
  • The field is arbitrary. \(\mathbb F_2\), and Booleanity of products, enter only the comparison with substitutions \(x\mapsto r\) in the computation, where every \((r^2-r)m\) vanishes in the multilinear ring.
  • The number of pigeons is arbitrary.
Not covered: a weight that is not a hole variable; errors through \(x\) or of degree above \(1\); the other weight, \(E=x\) with \(w=v\), which is served by the substitution \(x\mapsto0\) with multiplier \(1\) only when every error is divisible by \(x\); and more than one twisted variable.

Degenerate cases.

  • \(S\) empty with \(m\ge2\): \(\tau=1\), and the killer is \(x\) itself, the scalar repair \(m=1\).
  • \(m=1\): no partners, \(\tau\) counts the holes of \(S\) alone, and when \(S\) does not touch hole \(b\) the scalar half fails: the killer has degree \(\tau+1\) (with \(S\) empty, \(\tau=0\) and the killer \(x\) has degree \(1\)).
  • \(\tau>K'\): no survivor of degree at most \(K'\), so no twisted repair of this form exists.
  • \(K'=n\): outside the hypotheses; the transfer and the survival description fail there.

Nonvacuity. Four pigeons, three holes, \(x=x_{00}\), \(K'=2\), \(S=\{x_{01}\}\): \(\tau=2\le2\). Over \(\mathbb F_2\) the killer is \(f=x_{00}(1+x_{01}+x_{11}+x_{21}+x_{31})\).

  • \(x_{k0}f\in C\), since each term contains the hole axiom \(x_{00}x_{k0}\).
  • \(x_{01}f=x_{00}x_{01}(x_{11}+x_{21}+x_{31})\in C\), since each term contains a hole axiom \(x_{01}x_{k1}\) (and \(x_{01}(1+x_{01})=0\)).
  • \(f\) is nonzero at the assignment putting pigeon \(0\) in hole \(0\) alone.
The computation found the different member \(x_{00}(1+x_{01})\) of \(F\setminus C\), of the same degree. Plainest bound. The scalar repairs of degree \(\tau-1\) are the plainest class, and the corollary shows they decide every set; recorded results decide the computation. No parameter ledger applies: the witness verifies no budget of the goal.

Baseline. Without the twist, scalar repairs; lem:top-degree-killer-transfer and lem:cylinder-killer-degree already give their least degree for monomial errors. The corollary adds that the twisted pair conditions with weight a hole variable reduce to the same killer problem, so that a twist cannot do better there.
