Setting

The functional pigeonhole system with \(m\) pigeons and \(N\) holes over a field \(k\) (Booleanity, hole and functionality axioms, pigeon axioms \(Q_i=1-\sum_hx_{ih}\), multilinear ring), \(C\) its degree-\(D\) closure with \(D\le\lceil N/2\rceil\), \(H\) that of the hole and functionality axioms, two distinct pigeons written \(1\), \(2\), and the partition error \(E_A=\sum_{j\in A}x_{1j}+\sum_{j\notin A}x_{2j}\). A surviving killer of \(E\) of degree \(K\le D-1\) is \(u\in V_K\setminus C\) with \(uE\in C\); \(\kappa(E)\) is the least such \(K\), when one exists. For \(A=\varnothing\) or \(A\) all holes, \(E_A\) is \(1\) minus a pigeon axiom, and no surviving killer exists.

Partition killer degree

Statement. Theorem (partition killer degree). Let \(1\le|A|\le N-1\) and \(D\ge2\).

  • (a) In characteristic \(2\), \(\kappa(E_A)=1\), with the killer \(\sum_{j\in A}(x_{1j}+x_{2j})\).
  • (b) In every characteristic, if \(A=\{j\}\), \(\kappa(E_A)=1\), with the killer \(x_{2j}\); if \(A^c=\{h\}\), the killer \(x_{1h}\).
  • (c) In characteristic other than \(2\), if \(2\le|A|\le N-2\), and \(N-|A|\ge3\) or \(|A|\ge3\), no linear killer survives: \(\operatorname{Ann}_C(E_A)\cap V_1=C\cap V_1\).
  • (d) In characteristic other than \(2\), if \(2\le|A|\le N-2\) and \(D\ge3\) (so \(N\ge5\) and the condition of (c) holds), \(\kappa(E_A)=2\), with the killer \(x_{1h}x_{2j}\) for any \(h\notin A\), \(j\in A\).
  • (e) In characteristic other than \(2\), for partition errors \(E_A\), \(E_B\) with \(A\cap B\ne\varnothing\), \(A\cup B\) not all holes, at least one of them of size between \(2\) and \(N-2\), and \(D\ge3\), the least degree of a common surviving killer is \(2\), by \(x_{1h}x_{2j}\) with \(j\in A\cap B\), \(h\notin A\cup B\).

Proof. Degree \(0\): a constant \(c\ne0\) with \(cE_A\in C\) would put \(E_A\) in \(C\cap V_1\), the span of the pigeon axioms (lem:closure-degree-locality at degree one); \(E_A=\sum_ic_iQ_i\) forces \(c_1=c_2=0\) by the coefficients of \(x_{1h}\) and \(x_{2h'}\) for a hole \(h\notin A\) and a hole \(h'\in A\), the other \(c_i=0\) by their variables, and then \(E_A=0\), false. So \(\kappa\ge1\). Survival of a linear form \(u\): it is outside \(C\) unless it is in the span of the pigeon axioms, and \(\sum_{j\in A}(x_{1j}+x_{2j})\), \(x_{2j}\) and \(x_{1h}\) are not, by the same coefficient count (a hole outside \(A\), or outside \(\{j\}\), has coefficient \(0\) on pigeon \(1\) or \(2\) while a hole inside has \(1\)). Write \(E'=E_A+Q_2=1+S\), \(S=\sum_{j\in A}(x_{1j}-x_{2j})\); since \(uQ_2\in C\) for \(u\in V_{D-1}\), \(u\) kills \(E_A\) modulo \(C\) exactly when it kills \(E'\).

(a) Modulo Booleanity, hole and functionality axioms, \(x_{pj}^2=x_{pj}\), \(x_{1j}x_{2j}=0\), \(x_{1j}x_{1k}=x_{2j}x_{2k}=0\) for \(j\ne k\), so \(S^2=\sum_{j\in A}(x_{1j}+x_{2j})-2\sum_{j\ne k\in A}x_{1j}x_{2k}\), which in characteristic \(2\) is \(S\), with \(S=\sum_{j\in A}(x_{1j}+x_{2j})\). Then \(SE'=S+S^2=2S=0\) modulo \(H\), a polynomial of degree \(2\le D\). So \(S\) kills, and survives.

(b) \(x_{2j}E'=x_{2j}(1+x_{1j}-x_{2j})=x_{2j}+0-x_{2j}=0\) modulo \(H\); symmetrically \(x_{1h}(E_A+Q_1)=x_{1h}(1+x_{2h}-x_{1h})=0\). They survive.

(c) Lower bound. Assume \(N-|A|\ge3\); cor:partition-transfer-every-k at \(K=1\), whose \(\rho\) is \(\max(1,3)=3\), gives \(\operatorname{Ann}_C(E_A)\cap V_1=\operatorname{Ann}_H(E')\cap V_1+W\). Let \(u=c_0+\sum_ec_ex_e\in V_1\) with \(uE'\in H\). Every member of \(H\) vanishes at every partial matching, the axioms do; so \(u(M)E'(M)=0\) at every partial matching \(M\), where \(E'(M)=1+[1\text{ in }A]-[2\text{ in }A]\) takes the values \(0,1,2\), \(0\) exactly when pigeon \(2\) is at a hole of \(A\) and pigeon \(1\) is not; \(2\ne0\) here. At \(M=\varnothing\), \(c_0=0\); at a single edge \(e\) other than \((2,j)\), \(j\in A\), \(E'\ne0\), so \(c_e=0\); at \(M=\{(2,j),(1,k)\}\) with \(j\ne k\) in \(A\) (as \(|A|\ge2\)), \(E'(M)=1\), so \(c_{2j}=0\). So \(u=0\), \(\operatorname{Ann}_C(E_A)\cap V_1=W\), and no linear killer survives. If instead \(|A|\ge3\), the same with \(E_A+Q_1=1+\sum_{j\notin A}(x_{2j}-x_{1j})\), the roles of the pigeons and of \(A\), \(A^c\) exchanged, \(|A^c|\ge2\). This evaluation is the mechanism of lem:idempotent-common-denominator (b) with \(p=1\), \(H\cap V_D\) being the degree-\(D\) vanishing ideal of the partial matchings: a linear killer modulo \(H\) is a linear function on them supported where \(E'\) vanishes.

(d) The bound \(\kappa\ge2\) is (c), whose condition holds: \(D\ge3\) and \(D\le\lceil N/2\rceil\) give \(N\ge5\), and \(2\le|A|\le N-2\) then gives \(N-|A|\ge3\) or \(|A|\ge3\). Upper bound: \(x_{1h}x_{2j}E_A=\sum_{k\in A}x_{1h}x_{2j}x_{1k}+\sum_{k\notin A}x_{1h}x_{2j}x_{2k}\), where \(x_{1h}x_{1k}=0\) for \(k\in A\) (as \(h\notin A\), \(h\ne k\)) and \(x_{2j}x_{2k}=0\) for \(k\notin A\) (as \(j\in A\)), by the functionality axioms: a product of degree \(3\le D\), zero modulo \(H\), in every field. Survival: rank the holes \(j\), \(h\) lowest; \(\{(1,h),(2,j)\}\) has a dance, its two pigeons moving to two higher holes (\(N\ge4\)), so \(x_{1h}x_{2j}\in T_2\cap B_2\), \(B_2\) the basis of lem:admissible-numbering-transfer's record at \(2\le D\le\lceil N/2\rceil\). There \(C\cap V_2\) is \(H\cap V_2\) plus the lift of \(K_2=\operatorname{span}(B_2\setminus T_2)\), and a nonzero combination of members of \(T_2\cap B_2\) is not in it. So the killer survives and \(\kappa=2\). The killer \(x_{2j}(1-\sum_{k\in A}x_{1k})\) of the first draft is \(x_{2j}\sum_{h\notin A}x_{1h}\) modulo \(C\), since \(x_{2j}Q_1\in C\): a sum of these.

(e) Say \(2\le|A|\le N-2\). A common killer of degree \(0\) or \(1\) would kill \(E_A\) alone, which degree \(0\) and (c) exclude; \(x_{1h}x_{2j}\) with \(j\in A\cap B\), \(h\notin A\cup B\) kills each error by (d)'s computation, \(j\) lying in each set and \(h\) outside each, and survives as there. \(\square\)

Hypotheses, each with its step. \(D\le\lceil N/2\rceil\): lem:closure-degree-locality and the basis \(B_2\); \(D\ge2\): the products \(uQ_2\) and \(SE'\) of degree \(2\); \(D\ge3\) in (d), (e): the product of the killer, degree \(2\), with the error; \(1\le|A|\le N-1\): a hole on each side for the coefficient counts; characteristic \(2\) in (a): \(2S=0\) and the cross term \(-2\sum x_{1j}x_{2k}\); characteristic other than \(2\) in (c): \(E'(M)=2\ne0\) at \(M=\{(1,k)\}\), \(k\in A\); \(|A|\ge2\) in (c): the matchings \(\{(2,j),(1,k)\}\); \(N-|A|\ge3\) or \(|A|\ge3\) in (c): the transfer at \(K=1\), implied in (d) by \(D\ge3\); \(|A|\le N-2\) in (c): \(|A^c|\ge2\) on the mirrored branch, \(|A^c|=1\) falling under (b); \(N\ge4\) for the dance of the killer of (d), implied by \(D\ge3\); in (e), \(A\cap B\ne\varnothing\) and \(A\cup B\) not all holes for the holes \(j\), \(h\) of the killer, and the size condition on one of the two errors only, for (c), the upper bound using neither. The conclusions hold for every number of pigeons at least two and, in (b) and (d)'s upper bound, every field.

Degenerate cases. \(|A|\in\{0,N\}\): excluded, \(E_A\equiv1\) modulo \(W\) and no killer survives. \(D=2\) in characteristic other than \(2\), \(2\le|A|\le N-2\): the window \(K\le1\) has no surviving killer when \(N-|A|\ge3\) or \(|A|\ge3\), by (c); at \(N=4\), \(|A|=2\) neither holds, not covered, the middle size where conj:middle-excess-from-three predicts positive single excess over \(\mathbb F_2\).

Nonvacuity. Six pigeons and five holes, \(D=3\), \(A=\{0,1\}\), over \(\mathbb Q\): \(2\le|A|=2\le3\), \(N-|A|=3\ge3\), so \(\kappa(E_A)=2\) by (d) (the branch \(N-|A|\ge3\) of (c)), and over \(\mathbb F_2\) the same error has \(\kappa=1\) by (a); for (e), \(A=\{0,1\}\), \(B=\{1,2\}\), killer \(x_{1,3}x_{2,1}\). Plainest bound. The monomial \(x_{1h}x_{2j}\), \(h\notin A\), \(j\in A\), one product at a zero of \(E_A\), gives \(\kappa\le2\) in every field; \(\kappa\ge1\) because \(E_A\notin C\).
