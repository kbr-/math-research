Required changes:
1. "Consequently ... d(G) <= max{d(G^cl), 2w} and d(G^cl) <= max{d(G), 2w+1}": swapped. The proof's
   containments give d(G^cl) <= max{d(G), 2w} and d(G) <= max{d(G^cl), 2w+1}. The stated first inequality is
   unproved; on the pigeonhole instance (d(G) = 5, 2w = 4) it would assert d(G^cl) = 5.

   d(G^cl) <= max{2 d(H), 2w}, below 2(d(H)+1). The citation of lem:gadget-lift-degree-bound is itself a
   correct instance.
3. Entry-meta "the expansion route is closed for clause systems", and the Bearing's "the method ... does not
   apply": overstated. Part (d) defeats only the clause-variable-graph theorem on G^cl, for s >= 2^p. The
   immunity criterion applied to the constraints c^ on H's graph is untried; immunity exactly p + 2q there,
   against p + q for the clause (unverified).
4. "the suggestion fits fields of odd characteristic": wrong as put. Linear equations are degree one over
   every field; parities are what is hard in odd characteristic. Tseitin clauses over F_2 are no dodge,
   their degree being about the width.
5. Follow-up, "doubling follows": needs a lower bound on d(G) equal to twice the exact d(H); the registered
   r(l/4 - ...) form gives no such constant.
