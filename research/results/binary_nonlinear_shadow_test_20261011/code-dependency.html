1. Exact error ranks

Split \(v=mr\) Boolean variables into \(m\ge1\) disjoint blocks of equal size \(r\ge2\). Let \(z_i\) be the product of all variables in block \(i\). For any binary matrix \(A\in\mathbb F_2^{a\times m}\), put \(g=Az\). For \(C\in\mathbb F_2^{t\times a}\), define

\[P_C=\prod_{j=1}^t(1+(Cg)_j),\qquad c_C(K)=\operatorname{rank}\bigl(f\in V_K\longmapsto(P_Cg_if)_i\bmod\mathrm{BOOL}\bigr).\]

Here \(V_K\) is the multilinear degree-at-most-\(K\) space; \(t=0\) is allowed and gives \(P_C=1\). No old unsatisfiable axioms are imposed yet. For \(T\subseteq[m]\), let \(A_T\) and \((CA)_T\) denote column restrictions and set

\[d_T=|T|-\operatorname{rank}(CA)_T,\quad e_T=|T|-\operatorname{rank}A_T,\quad \delta_T=d_T-e_T,\quad H_r(u)=(1+u)^r-1-u^r.\]

Theorem (monomial code ranks). For every \(0\le K\le v\),

\[c_C(K)=[u^{\le K}]\sum_{T\subseteq[m]}H_r(u)^{m-|T|} \left((1+u^r)^{d_T}-u^{r\delta_T}(1+u^r)^{e_T}\right).\]

The operator \([u^{\le K}]\) sums coefficients through \(K\) in this integer dimension polynomial, not a polynomial reduced modulo two.

Proof. On one block, use the filtered basis \(1,z_i\) and \(1+x_S\) for all nonempty proper subsets \(S\) of that block. It is a degree-compatible basis: each vector has its distinct squarefree leading monomial, with only an added constant. Multiplication by \(z_i\) kills every \(1+x_S\), while on \(\langle1,z_i\rangle\) it is multiplication by the Boolean coordinate \(z_i\), of weight \(r\). The killed basis vectors have degree enumerator \(H_r\).

Tensor these decompositions. Each component is indexed by its active two-dimensional factors \(T\) and choices of killed basis vectors on the other blocks. If their total degree is \(b\), its degree-\(K\) part is the Boolean polynomial space in \(|T|\) coordinates of degree at most \(\lfloor(K-b)/r\rfloor\). Every multiplication by an input or approximation factor preserves the component. On it, the joint error map has the same kernel as evaluation on

\[\ker((CA)_T)\setminus\ker(A_T).\]

Indeed \(P_C\) indicates the first linear space and the tuple \(g\) vanishes exactly on the second. The two spaces have dimensions \(d_T,e_T\), and the second is contained in the first. Linear changes of binary coordinates and restriction to a linear subspace preserve total-degree filtrations; choosing free coordinates shows surjectivity onto all degree-bounded functions of the first space.

In coordinates on that space, arrange the second as the locus where \(\delta_T\) coordinates vanish. Functions supported there are uniquely its indicator \(\prod_{j\le\delta_T}(1+y_j)\) times an arbitrary function of the remaining \(e_T\) coordinates. Their degree is exactly \(\delta_T\) plus the degree of that function, because the top term in those first coordinates cannot cancel. The quotient restriction to the set difference therefore has degree enumerator \((1+u)^{d_T}-u^{\delta_T}(1+u)^{e_T}\). Reinsert weight \(r\), the inactive degree shifts and their multiplicities, then sum components. The case \(\delta_T=0\) gives zero, as required. \(\square\)

2. Kill the low-active components in one batch

Fix \(1\le K<v/2\), write \(\rho=K/v\), and put

\[N_v(K)=\sum_{j\le K}\binom vj,\qquad \alpha=(1-\rho)^r+\rho^r,\qquad \mu=m\alpha.\]

Corollary (monomial code approximation). For any integer \(1\le s\le m\), one can choose \(C\) with at most \(t=\min\{m,s\lceil\log_2(m+1)\rceil+1\}\) rows so that

\[\deg P_C\le rt,\qquad \frac{c_C(K)}{N_v(K)}\le(v+1)\Pr\{J>s\},\qquad J\sim\operatorname{Bin}(m,\alpha).\]

In particular, for an integer \(M\ge1\), if \(s\ge2e\mu\) and \(s\ge\log_2(2M(v+1))\), the ratio is at most \(1/(2M)\). These are sufficient conditions, not claims that such an \(s\le m\) exists for every parameter choice.

Proof of the code. Choose rows of \(C\) independently and uniformly. For each \(x\in\mathbb F_2^m\) of support at most \(s\) with \(Ax\ne0\), the event \(CAx=0\) has probability \(2^{-t}\). There are at most \((m+1)^s\) such vectors: encode a subset of size at most \(s\) as a sorted length-\(s\) list padded with a zero symbol. Thus \(s\lceil\log_2(m+1)\rceil+1\) rows make the union-bound failure probability at most one half. Alternatively, choose a basis of the rows of \(A\), using at most \(m\) input combinations; their common kernel already equals that of \(A\). Padding with zero rows is harmless. The chosen check matrix consequently has \(\operatorname{rank}(CA)_T=\operatorname{rank}A_T\) for every \(|T|\le s\).

Those components contribute zero to the exact formula. Each remaining component's rank is at most its full filtered dimension. Hence the rank is bounded by the cumulative coefficients of

\[\sum_{j>s}\binom mj(1+u^r)^j H_r(u)^{m-j}.\]

Set \(\theta=\rho/(1-\rho)\in(0,1)\). Nonnegative coefficients bound this cumulative sum by \(\theta^{-K}\) times its value at \(\theta\). Since \(H_r(\theta)+1+\theta^r=(1+\theta)^r\), that value is \((1+\theta)^v\Pr(J>s)\), with the stated \(\alpha\). Moreover \(K\) is a mode of \(\operatorname{Bin}(v,K/v)\), so its mass is at least \(1/(v+1)\). Thus

\[N_v(K)\ge\binom vK\ge\frac{\theta^{-K}(1+\theta)^v}{v+1},\]

proving the ratio bound. For completeness, the exponential-moment estimate \(\mathbb E e^{\lambda J}\le\exp(\mu(e^\lambda-1))\), with \(e^\lambda=s/\mu\), gives \(\Pr(J\ge s)\le(e\mu/s)^s\) for \(s\ge\mu\). When \(s\ge2e\mu\), this is at most \(2^{-s}\). The remaining displayed condition gives the claimed \(1/(2M)\). \(\square\)

Logarithmic blocks. Along \(v=mr\to\infty\) with \(r=\Theta(\log v)\), \(K/v\) bounded away from zero and from one half, and \(M\le v^A\) for fixed \(A\), we have \(\alpha=v^{-\Omega(1)}\). Choose \(s=O(\mu+\log(Mv))\) satisfying the inequalities. Eventually \(s\le m\), and

\[rt=O\bigl(v\alpha\log v+(\log v)^3\bigr)=o(v).\]

Thus simultaneous factors can make the error rank polynomially small relative to \(N_v(K)\) at sublinear approximation degree. The factors are linear combinations of the actual input tuple \(Az\); the argument does not assume access to individual \(z_i\) when their span is unavailable.

3. The weighted old-PC interface retains a real loss

Let an old Boolean system have consequence space \(C_D\), and let \(w\notin C_D\), \(\deg w\le b\). Define \(h_w=\operatorname{rank}(f\in V_K\mapsto[wf]\bmod C_D)>0\). Suppose there are \(M\) tuple families of the form above, each with a disjoint partition of the same \(v=mr\) variables; their mixing matrices and partitions may differ. If the chosen \(s,t\) satisfy

\[b+K+rt+r\le D,\qquad s\ge2e\mu,\qquad s\ge\log_2\left(\frac{2M(v+1)N_v(K)}{h_w}\right),\qquad1\le s\le m,\]

then there are codes \(C_\nu\) and one degree-\(K\) multilinear \(f\) with \(wf\notin C_D\) and \(wfP_{C_\nu}g_{\nu,i}\in C_D\) for every error.

Proof. A Boolean-zero error product has a Boolean certificate through its total degree; multiplying by \(w\) stays through \(D\). Thus each weighted error rank modulo \(C_D\) is at most its Boolean error rank, bounded above by \(h_w/(2M)\). Every weighted error map kills \(\ker(f\mapsto[wf])\), by PC reuse within the same degree window. The sum of ranks on that common quotient is at most \(h_w/2<h_w\), leaving a nonzero kernel class. This is the weighted common-kernel argument with the new absolute rank estimate. \(\square\)

The price \(\log(N_v(K)/h_w)\) cannot be silently dropped. A full Boolean quotient has \(h_1=N_v(K)\), but it is satisfiable and supplies no Frege contradiction. The available old graph separated-space estimates can lose an exponential factor when the multiplier degree is a fixed positive fraction of the variables; the present argument does not restore that factor. Nor does the block decomposition hold for arbitrary overlapping matching supports, old-affine offsets in individual block variables, or deeper gate polynomials. No PHP endpoint follows without resolving those issues.
