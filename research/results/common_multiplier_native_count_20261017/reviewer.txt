## Open point: m_min at 5 pigeons, 4 holes, K = 2, L = 1 (unverified; my argument, nothing computed)

I believe m_min = 6, the lower bound.

- Put c for the constant coefficient, c_{hi} for the 15 linear coefficients and c_e for the 75 quadratic ones.
- ν_0 = the ball sum at the empty centre (16 points). Its 16 forms recover c and the c_{hi}.
- The quadratic coefficients are the edges of K_{5,5,5}: 15 vertices (h,i), with edges between different holes.
- A 0/1 measure on the weight-two points of an edge set G gives, with v = 1 and v = x_k, the total sum and the 15 star sums of the c_e over G, modulo the known lower part.
- If G is a connected spanning graph with 15 edges containing an odd cycle, this map is injective on the coordinates of G:
  - in characteristic other than 2, because the unsigned incidence matrix has kernel of dimension E − V + (number of bipartite components) = 0;
  - over F_2, because the kernel is the cycle space spanned by the odd cycle, and the total sum is nonzero on it.
- The edge sets are disjoint, so the ranks add: 16 + 5·15 = 91.
- A decomposition of K_{5,5,5} into 5 such pieces gives m_min = 6:
  - In characteristic other than 2, an explicit one: identify the vertices with Z_15, the hole being the residue mod 3. The difference classes ±1, ±2, ±4, ±7 are Hamiltonian 15-cycles, which are odd. The class ±5 is five disjoint triangles; this works when the characteristic is not 2, but over F_2 its rank is 11.
  - Over every field: Laskar–Auerbach (1976) state that K_{n,…,n} with r parts is Hamiltonian-decomposable when (r−1)n is even. This is recalled, not read.
- In general this suggests m_min = 1 + ⌈E/V⌉ with E = C(f,2)m², V = fm, for f free holes and m pigeons. On the cube it agrees with ⌊a/2⌋+1 at odd a.
