On effective-selection and joint-error-savings, the next entry runs the survival review's Decisive test: on six pigeons and five holes at \(D=3\), \(K=1\), whether the common kernel modulo the closure of the \(24\) errors \((1+q_\nu)x_e\) of two Pang families, on the edges that share nothing with \(q_\nu\)'s edges, strictly contains \(C\cap V_1\). It develops pang-contraction-joint-count, chosen because the test is on Pang-form errors: “Find a structural joint bound on Pang's actual error maps that gives such a c on an unbounded family, or show the stacked rank of Pang's error families is generically the sum.” It first registers the review's observation, a sufficient condition and not an equivalence: the support bound decides selection only when \(\dim(C\cap V_K)<\dim Z\), and a domain common killer outside the closure decides it by itself.
