# Independent architecture stage

Goal: for every fixed formula depth, prove a superpolynomial AC0[2]-Frege
proof-size lower bound for one appropriate family independent of the proposed
polynomial size exponent. Any family is allowed. The all-prime goal remains,
with binary first accepted. No qualifying general lower bound is currently proved.

Bounded available statements (do not read current route/next-step sections):

- A fixed-depth coherent compiler produces complete binary product-only ENS
  sources and a PC refutation through cL, with L <= (d+1)h^d and no proof-height
  term. Original companions and fresh Boolean coefficient fields must be kept.
- An existing iteration excludes the recorded source presentation at symbol
  sizes n^(1+rho) only when rho < 1/(2^(ell-1)-1), for ell source levels. It does
  not exclude every polynomial exponent. Its loss is global named-feature growth.
- A weaker sufficient endpoint is one surviving old weight W, coherent values of
  degree <=L and annihilation of all used axiom/copy/MP defects through
  degW+2L<=D. Complete observations of any normalized proof have full weighted
  rank, so a contradicting upper estimate must use the assumed small proof size.
- Near-half's conditional transfer needs polynomial-length literal CNFs whose
  proper binary PC closure reaches free dimension n/2-s, with the recorded
  deficit/width bounds. No qualifying family is known.
- A new alternative sufficient quantitative premise from that thread: standard
  monomials of a proper closure have at most binom(X,j) elements in each degree
  K-t<j<=K, X>=K and X<=2(K-t)+1+(K-t+1)/t. The Lovasz shadow theorem then bounds
  every contained down-closed family's cumulative growth over that window by
  1+e*t. The engine's actual error kernels are such families. No family satisfying
  the needed properness/thinness combination is known.
- On a connected ternary charged epsilon-expander, a refutation restricted to
  scalar multiplication and binary linear combinations of vertex equations
  must expose >=epsilon*n/6 active edge labels. Exact fixed-depth AC0[2] circuits
  for that predicate are large by MOD3 hardness. No annotation/extraction of an
  arbitrary Frege proof into that restricted model is proved.

Suggest a materially different complete architecture or a concrete necessary
implication/test supported by these results. Explicitly report when no supported
alternative is found. Do not infer source coverage, near-half hardness, a useful
thin closure, or a depth-preserving extraction from their conditional statements.
This is only stage one; the current route will be disclosed in stage two.
