1. A product of bottom values has a union-rank comparison cost

Work over \(\mathbb F_p\), with an old Boolean base and complete old-affine bottom blocks. For a finite set \(S\) of blocks let \(H_S=\prod_{c\in S}P_c\), \(W_S=\sum_{c\in S}w_c\), and let \(V_S\) be the affine-polynomial span of all their inputs. Put \(r_S=\dim V_S\), \(\tau_S=(p-1)r_S\). For a proper span choose a basis \(F_j\) and let \(\chi_S=\prod_j(1-F_j^{p-1})\); for a unit span take \(\chi_S=0\), and for the zero span take \(\chi_S=1\).

Working comparison. In the retained bottom system \(\Gamma_0\),

\[ H_S-\chi_S\in\mathcal I_{W_S+\tau_S}(\Gamma_0). \tag{IS-comparison} \]

Proof. In the proper nonzero case, each nonzero input is genuinely affine of degree one. Every original input times \(H_S\) is a retained companion times the other products, through \(W_S+1\); the same holds for each basis \(F_j\). The Fermat prefix for \(1-\chi_S\) therefore certifies \(H_S(1-\chi_S)\) through \(W_S+\tau_S\).

The exact product prefixes also write \(H_S-1\) as a combination of the original inputs with coefficient degree at most \(W_S-1\). Each input times \(\chi_S\) vanishes on the old Boolean cube, with a Boolean NS certificate through \(\tau_S+1\). Consequently \((H_S-1)\chi_S\) has a retained certificate through \(W_S+\tau_S\). Add these two expressions to obtain (IS-comparison). If \(1\in V_S\), a constant input combination gives \(H_S\) directly from its companions through \(W_S+1\le W_S+\tau_S\). The zero and empty cases are identities. \(\square\)

Thus a retained functional through \(E\) identifies \(\lambda(qH_S)\) with \(\lambda(q\chi_S)\) only when \(\deg q+W_S+\tau_S\le E\). The extra rank charge is explicit. The new map below avoids silently inserting this comparison into a smaller original companion budget.
