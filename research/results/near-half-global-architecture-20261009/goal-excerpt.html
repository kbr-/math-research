Open statements

  1. Near-half Boolean PC family — open. For each fixed relevant approximation depth \(\ell\ge2\), construct a polynomial-size, preferably constant-width unsatisfiable Boolean CNF family on \(v\to\infty\) variables, independent of the proof-size exponent \(A\), with \(1\notin C_D\) over \(\mathbb F_2\) for \(D=\lfloor v/2-s\rfloor\), \(s=o(v/(\log v)^{\ell-2})\). A stronger near-square-root-deficit barrier also suffices. Growing width needs a new complete cost ledger, not automatic application of the fixed-input-degree theorem.
  2. Complete Frege transfer — open. For that same family and every fixed Frege depth and polynomial exponent, verify a source/proof interpretation covered by Pang\'s argument or the moving-cutoff theorem, including encoding length, original axiom and approximation degrees, coefficient domains and coherent source grammar. Derive the superpolynomial bound in formula length.
