Sparse pattern removal

Work over \(\mathbb F_2\). Let \(J_D\) be a proper Boolean degree-\(D\) pseudoideal in \(v\) old variables, and \(V_K\) the old degree-at-most-\(K\) space, \(1\le K\le v\). Put \(H=\dim[V_K]>0\). For each of \(M\ge1\) tuples choose \(q_a\le q\) old binary features \(z_{a,j}(x)\) of degree at most \(d\ge1\), and a supplied polynomial tuple \[\widehat g_{a,i}(x,Z)=b_{a,i}(Z)+\sum_{k=1}^v x_k b_{a,i,k}(Z),\qquad g_{a,i}(x)=\widehat g_{a,i}(x,z_a(x)).\] Coefficients are binary. Give each old variable weight one and each formal feature weight \(d\); assume every supplied \(\widehat g_{a,i}\) has weighted degree at most \(\delta\ge d\). Thus each actual input has old degree at most \(\delta\), and each reference obtained by assigning \(Z\) constants is old-affine. No independence, disjointness or faithful formal feature cube is assumed. This includes old-affine parts plus constant feature mixtures with \(\delta=d\), and affine old coefficient functions multiplying features, at their larger written cost. Put \(z_{a,S}=\prod_{j\in S}z_{a,j}\).

Fix integers \(0\le s\le q\), \(t\ge\max(1,s)\), and suppose \(K+\delta(t+1)\le D\). Define \[ L_s=\sum_{j=0}^s\binom qj,\quad \eta=\frac{N_v(K-1)}{2N_v(K)},\quad N_v(k)=\sum_{j\le k}\binom vj, \] \[ R_s=\sum_a\sum_{\substack{S\subseteq[q_a]\\|S|=s+1}} \operatorname{rank}\bigl(f\in V_K\mapsto[z_{a,S}f]\bigr). \tag{1} \] All these maps factor through the same \([V_K]\), since \(d(s+1)\le\delta(t+1)\); an empty inner sum is zero.

Lemma. If \[M L_s(1-\eta)^t H+R_s<H,\tag{2}\] there are binary constant matrices \(C_a\) with \(t\) rows and one old multilinear \(W\), \(\deg W\le K\), \(W\notin J_D\), such that every \[Wg_{a,i}\prod_{u=1}^t(1+(C_ag_a)_u)\in J_D.\] The row positions are the original input positions. For any original accuracy \(h\ge1\), the same product can be realized in \(h\) rows by binary old coefficient maps of degree at most \[T=\max\{1,\delta(\lceil t/h\rceil-1)\}.\tag{3}\] Complete source replay additionally retains \(K+TB\le D\), earlier generators and all coefficient fields; (2) alone is not a source exclusion.

One matrix for the affine references. For one matrix \(C_a\) define \[E_{a,i}(x,Z)=\widehat g_{a,i}(x,Z)\prod_{u=1}^t(1+(C_a\widehat g_a(x,Z))_u).\] For each binary \(\xi\) of support at most \(s\), the reference \(\widehat g_a(x,\xi)\) is an affine old tuple. Uniform independent rows of \(C_a\) give expected rank at most \((1-\eta)^tH\) for its complete error map on \(V_K\). Here is the precise use of the recorded conditional contraction. Before row \(j\), the reference prefix has old degree at most \(j-1\). The binary expected gain is at least half the preceding error rank at cutoff \(K-1\), with reserve \(K+j+1\le D\). The error kernel on full old degree spaces is closed under allowed variable multiplication and contains Booleanity. Its degree-compatible standard monomials are downward closed, so the ratio of ranks at \(K-1\) and \(K\) is at least \(N_v(K-1)/N_v(K)\), unless both ranks are zero. Thus conditional expected rank contracts by \(1-\eta\). Initially it is at most \(H\), by bounded absorption. This proves the expectation after \(t\) rows.

The same random matrix is used for all \(\xi\) of one parent; no independent choice for each reference is made. Linearity of expectation bounds their summed ranks by \(L_s(1-\eta)^tH\), so one matrix attains at most that sum. Choose such a matrix for each parent. The stacked reference maps, together with the product maps (1), have total rank below \(H\) by (2). Their common kernel therefore has a nonzero old class. Choose a binary multilinear representative \(W\) of degree at most \(K\). It kills every reference error and every \(z_{a,S}\) with \(|S|=s+1\), within the displayed degree window.

Use the actual polynomial expansion. Boolean-reduce \(E_{a,i}\) in \(Z\) only: \[E_{a,i}(x,Z)\equiv\sum_{S\subseteq[q_a]}c_{a,i,S}(x)Z_S, \qquad c_{a,i,S}(x)=\sum_{U\subseteq S}E_{a,i}(x,\mathbf1_U). \tag{4}\] Its weighted degree before reduction is at most \(\delta(t+1)\). Hence \(c_{a,i,S}=0\) when \(d|S|>\delta(t+1)\), and \(\deg c_{a,i,S}\le\delta(t+1)-d|S|\). These are identities over \(\mathbb F_2[x]\), not evaluations on a model of the inconsistent old system.

For \(|S|\le s\), (4) expresses \(Wc_{a,i,S}\) as a sum of killed reference errors. Its actual degree is at most \(K+\delta(t+1)-d|S|\). Multiplication by \(z_{a,S}\) therefore remains in \(J_D\) through \(K+\delta(t+1)\); cancellation in the coefficient is used only after its membership is established. For every larger \(S\), choose one \((s+1)\)-subset \(T\subseteq S\) and collect those terms as \(Z_T R_{a,i,T}(x,Z)\). After feature substitution, \[\deg R_{a,i,T}(x,z_a)\le\delta(t+1)-d(s+1).\tag{5}\] Each constituent term has degree at most \(\delta(t+1)-d|S|+d(|S|-s-1)\), proving (5). Since \(Wz_{a,T}\in J_D\), multiplication by this cofactor stays within \(K+\delta(t+1)\).

The reduction difference in (4) has a certificate in the formal Boolean equations through weighted degree \(\delta(t+1)\). The substituted equation \(z_j^2+z_j\) has an old Boolean certificate through \(2d\); substituting its weighted cofactor does not raise weighted degree. Multiplication by \(W\) consequently gives an old Boolean certificate through \(K+\delta(t+1)\). Combining low and high terms proves the actual weighted companions. Neither a component projector nor a separately surviving reference weight enters.

Return to the original rows. Group the \(t\) factors into at most \(h\) bins, each of size at most \(\lceil t/h\rceil\). For each bin, exact product telescoping expresses \[1+\prod_{u\in G}(1+(C_ag_a)_u)=\sum_i\beta_{a,G,i}(x)g_{a,i}, \qquad\deg\beta_{a,G,i}\le\delta(|G|-1). \] Put these coefficients in its one original row and zero-pad the remaining rows. The resulting product equals the virtual product literally. This is the existing local-feature packing identity, not an increase of original accuracy. Its binary polynomial field images have Boolean certificates through \(2T\). Full source replay also needs the original global ceiling \(K+TB\le D\) and all earlier weighted memberships. This proves the lemma.

Conjunction mixture exclusion

A dictionary-free parent is allowed by taking \(q=0,s=0\). Let \(u\) bound the old axiom degrees.

Theorem. If the original source-proof ceiling and paid window satisfy \[ B\ge\max\{u,2h^2+3h\},\qquad K+\max\{d(t+1),TB\}\le D, \tag{7} \] this complete source has no degree-\(B\) PC or ordinary NS refutation. Proof of the product-image bound. Set every bottom to its literal rows, then zero-pad. Its product is the specified conjunction and its companions are Boolean-zero through \(d+1\). For a set \(S\) of \(s+1\) features of one parent, the product is zero if its labels conflict. Otherwise it reduces to their union label conjunction. The union has at least \(r(s+1)/\nu\) edges and at most \(d(s+1)\). The local reduction from the written product has a certificate through \(d(s+1)\). The full punctured conjunction theorem bounds its multiplication rank on \(V_K\) by \[H\lambda^{\gamma|\cup_{j\in S}S_j|}\le H a^{s+1}.\] Its support and multiplication windows fit (7). Hence \(R_s/H\le M\binom q{s+1}a^{s+1}\). If \(s=q\), this is zero. Otherwise (6) gives \(s\ge16qa\) and \(s\ge\log_2(8M)\), so \[M\binom q{s+1}a^{s+1}\le M\left(\frac{eqa}{s+1}\right)^{s+1}\le M4^{-s-1}<1/8.\tag{8}\] Also \(L_s\le(q+1)^s\), by sorted support lists padded to length \(s\), and (6) implies \(M L_s(1-\eta)^t\le1/8\).
