- Layer side: pairs with |Delta^j| >= s t number at most 2N exp(-t^alpha_j), points at depth s t in

  for all but O(1) pairs). Rescaling x_j by u_j turns it into the isotropic sup-norm Leighton-Shor

  exponential moments.
