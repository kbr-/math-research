Use the full earlier orbit proof

Keep the recorded charged quotient over \(F=\mathbb F_4\), on a connected oriented graph with \(n\) vertices, edge set \(E\), maximum degree \(\Delta\), and expansion \(\varepsilon>0\), in the window \[ \max(4,2\Delta)\le D<\varepsilon n/8. \] Use the recorded charges and encoding, \(\mathcal B=\operatorname{im}\partial\subseteq\mathbb F_3^E\), \(\mathcal G=\mathbb F_3^E/\mathcal B\), and the phase-adjusted map \(R_D\) into the degree-\(D\) quotient. This is a truncated vector space, not a global charged algebra. Let \(\overline{W_T}\) denote the image in \(\mathcal G\) of the coordinate space \(W_T=\mathbb F_3^T\), and let \(L_{T,a}\) be a label conjunction on \(T\).

The exact conjunction-rank proof says more than its numerical rank: for a degree-at-most-\(k\) character multiplier, the output column is either zero, or nonzero on every coordinate of one \(\overline{W_T}\)-orbit. Columns in the same orbit are proportional. These statements hold at \(k+|T|\le D\), by comparing bounded exponents and their small cut witnesses; they do not multiply a finished high-degree relation by another entire conjunction.

Corollary (weighted conjunction sectors). For every polynomial \(F_0\) of degree at most \(a_0\), with \(a_0+|T|\le D\), the support of \[ H=R_D(F_0L_{T,a}) \] is a union of complete \(\overline{W_T}\)-cosets. If \(H\ne0\), the subgroup generated by differences of its nonzero support labels contains \(\overline{W_T}\). Consequently, for any finite collection \(H_i=R_D(F_iL_{T_i,a_i})\), each within its own displayed product window, let \[ E_{\rm live}=\bigcup_{i:H_i\ne0}T_i. \] Every subgroup \(S\le\mathcal G\) making every nonzero \(H_i\) homogeneous in one coset satisfies \[ \overline{W_{E_{\rm live}}}\subseteq S, \qquad \operatorname{codim}_{\mathcal G}S \le |E\setminus E_{\rm live}|-n+c(G\setminus E_{\rm live}). \tag{1} \] Isolated vertices are counted in \(c\). In particular, if the surviving supports span \(\mathcal G\), including when they cover all edges or a spanning-tree complement, then \(S=\mathcal G\).

Proof. Locally reduce \(F_0\) into characters of degree at most \(a_0\). If the conjunction labels violate a local cut character, every column of its multiplication map is zero by the old theorem, hence \(H=0\). Otherwise decompose that map's columns by \(\overline{W_T}\)-orbits. In each orbit they are proportional to one vector with every orbit coefficient nonzero. A linear combination either cancels this vector completely or leaves all its coordinates nonzero. Distinct orbits have disjoint supports, proving the first assertion. All coordinates in each relevant orbit have representatives of degree at most \(a_0+|T|\), so truncation omits none of them.

If one complete coset \(\gamma+\overline{W_T}\) survives, its pairwise differences contain all of \(\overline{W_T}\). A support contained in one \(S\)-coset has every difference in \(S\). Apply this to every nonzero error and take their span to get the first inclusion in (1). For the dimension bound, quotient by \(\overline{W_{E_{\rm live}}}\): \[ \mathcal G/\overline{W_{E_{\rm live}}} \cong\mathbb F_3^{E\setminus E_{\rm live}}/\pi(\mathcal B). \] Here \(\pi(\mathcal B)\) is the cut space of the spanning subgraph with edge set \(E\setminus E_{\rm live}\). Its dimension is \(n-c(G\setminus E_{\rm live})\), since vertex potentials are in its kernel exactly when constant on each connected component. Subtracting gives (1). This uses only a quotient of label vector spaces; it introduces no global charged multiplication. \(\square\)

No matching assumption, disjointness assumption, Fourier sparsity of \(F_i\), or independent-coordinate hypothesis is needed for this conditional nonzero-error statement. The old quotient hypotheses supply the phase dictionary. The conclusion concerns support differences and an upper bound on possible sector codimension, not a rank bound inside a sector and not a weight-degree lower bound.

Preserve the existing weighted escape and the mixture gap

The later prefix-collapse and weighted-opening record was read before this proof. On a spanning-tree-complement partition \(T_1,\ldots,T_a\), its weight \(w=1+x_{e,\ell_e}\), \(e\in T_1\), and prefix \(1+z_1\) erase the first error and leave \(wz_i\ne0\) for \(i\ge2\). Thus \(E_{\rm live}=T\setminus T_1\). The residual graph is the spanning tree plus \(T_1\), so (1) allows codimension at most \(|T_1|\). Requiring the weight itself to be homogeneous also forces \(\bar e\in S\), because its two Fourier support labels are \(\bar e,-\bar e\). Their direction is independent of the other surviving supports, tightening the bound to \(|T_1|-1\), exactly attained in the older proof. Its half-rank survival and conjunction-removal range remain unchanged. A degree-one weight can therefore open many sectors: sector codimension is not a lower bound for the weight degree.

The conclusion does not pass from a sum of conjunctions to each summand. For example choose two independent spanning-tree-complement edges \(e,f\), write \(z_e=x_{e,a}\), \(z_f=x_{f,b}\), and take \(w=z_ez_f\). The compatible conjunction survives by the old orbit theorem. Yet \[ w(z_e+z_f)=0\pmod{J_D},\qquad wz_e=wz_f=w\ne0, \] through degree three, by the two indicator idempotence certificates. Thus a mixed input can have zero weighted error although both conjunction pieces survive; componentwise coverage cannot be used as the actual error coverage. These are source-admissible old inputs with an all-zero accuracy-one prefix; the original replay must still be paid separately. This local algebraic calibration is not a hard-source lower bound. General affine mixtures of retained nonlinear values require their actual combined errors, not a termwise application of (1).
