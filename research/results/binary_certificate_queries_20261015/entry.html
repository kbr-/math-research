Complete original-input certificate

Corollary (certificate word queries). For every \(n\ge1\), take the original nonempty unit CNF \(x_0,\neg x_0,x_1,\ldots,x_n\). Its indexed encoding length is \(N=\Theta(n\log(n+2))\). Include all original clause falsities, Boolean generators \(x_i^2+x_i\), and variable commutators \(x_ix_j+x_jx_i\), with separate original placeholders. There is a placeholder-linear free-algebra certificate over \(\mathbb F_2\) of full formula size \(O(n^4)\), ordered unbounded-fan-in depth at most six and joint degree at most \(n^2+2n+2\), whose coefficient at \(x_{b_1}\cdots x_{b_n}x_0Y_1\) equals MOD-3 of \(b\). Here \(Y_1\) names the original positive unit \(x_0\), not its Boolean field. Thus these growing-word queries admit no polynomial-size fixed-depth \(\mathrm{AC}^0[2]\) circuit family, even nonuniformly.

Proof. Retain the exact run polynomial \[ Z_n=\sum_{j=0}^n x_0^j,\qquad Q_n=\sum_{t=0}^{\lfloor n/3\rfloor}Z_n(x_1Z_n)^{3t}. \] Let \(F_1=1+x_0,F_2=x_0\), and define \[ C_n=Y_1+Y_2+Q_n\bigl(x_0Y_1+Y_2(1+x_0)\bigr). \tag{1}\] The added term at the full original tuple is \(Q_n(x_0(1+x_0)+x_0(1+x_0))=0\) literally in the free algebra. The first two terms become one. At all placeholders zero the expression is zero; every term has exactly one placeholder. Every other original label remains in the source tuple with coefficient zero. For a length-\(n\) word \(w\), only \(Q_nx_0Y_1\) contributes at \(wx_0Y_1\), with coefficient \([w]Q_n\); the isolated \(Y_1\) cannot contribute and all other terms use \(Y_2\). The reviewed unique zero-run decomposition makes this coefficient one exactly when the number of \(x_1\)'s is divisible by three. Full formula copies and product wires give the stated size, depth and joint degree. Substituting the bit-selected word into a hypothetical polynomial-size fixed-depth query circuit would compute MOD-3 on \(n\) bits, contradicting the recorded circuit consequence. The query has \(n+2\) letters; its input encoding has \(O(n\log(n+2))\) bits and is obtained from \(b\) by constants and wires. All original label counts are polynomial in \(n\). \(\square\)

An exact used proof image

Theorem (compiled word queries). For every \(n\ge1\), take instead the original unit CNF \(z,\neg z,x_0,\ldots,x_n\), on \(r=n+2\) variables, still with encoding length \(N=\Theta(n\log(n+2))\). Retain its \(n+3\) clause labels, \(n+2\) Boolean labels and \(\binom{n+2}{2}\) commutator labels. In the exact shallow prefix compiler, with antecedent complements first and then ordered succedent factors, there is a refutation \(\pi_n\) with twenty-two used nodes, no repeated multiset cedent, symbol size \(O(n^4)\) and formula depth at most seven. Its full compiled certificate \(D_n\) has size \(O(n^{48})\), depth at most seventeen and actual joint degree at most \(O(n^{48})\), with all original old variables and placeholders of degree one. For the original positive-unit placeholder \(Y_z\), \[ [z x_{b_1}\cdots x_{b_n}Y_z]D_n=\mathbf1\{\textstyle\sum_i b_i=0\pmod3\}. \tag{2}\] Consequently there is no generic polynomial-size, fixed-depth \(\mathrm{AC}^0[2]\) word-query guarantee for these exact compiler images, even when all proof nodes must be used and distinct. The polynomial bounds are in the original encoding and query length, with a depth bound depending only on the fixed source depth. This is an easy-CNF, nonminimum-proof control, not a lower bound on proofs.

Arithmetic encoding. Write \(a=\neg x_0,b=\neg x_1\), with falsities \(x_0,x_1\), and let \(E=\bigvee\varnothing\), \(O=\neg E\), of falsities one and zero. Realize a product by ordered OR. Realize a sum of \(k\) terms by their XOR, appending \(O\) if \(k\) is even. The resulting odd arity has falsity equal to the desired sum under the exact convention \(t(\operatorname{XOR}_k)=1+k+\sum_i t(A_i)\). Apply this explicitly to the two sums in \(Q_n\), representing each power by all its repeated OR arguments. Call the resulting formula \(U\). Then \(t(U)=Q_n\), \(|U|=O(n^4)\), and \(\operatorname{depth}(U)\le5\): the letter negations, power OR, first XOR, alternating-factor OR and final XOR form five layers. Padding constants do not increase that ceiling. Its outermost connective is a nonempty XOR, and it contains no \(z\).
