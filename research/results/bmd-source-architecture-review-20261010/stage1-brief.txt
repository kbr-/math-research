Thresholds are initial degrees
Work on the cube over a field \(F\), with \(R=F[y_1,\ldots,y_n]\), the root curve \(z_i\), and \(X_{a,r}\) the degree-\(r\) part of \(\prod_ir_0(y_i)^{a_i}\), as in the stable per-order law and its finite-grid form. For \(h\ge1\) and \(m\ge0\), let \(\mathrm{Sol}^{(h)}_m\subseteq R^{m+1}\) be the set of \((W_0,\ldots,W_m)\) with
\[ \sum_{c\le m}W_cX_{a,c-t}=0\qquad(a\in\{0,1\}^n,\ |a|+2t\le h-1), \]
and let \(I^{(h)}_m=\{W_m:W\in\mathrm{Sol}^{(h)}_m\}\).
Working lemma (lem:cube-threshold-ideal).
\(I^{(h)}_m\) is a homogeneous ideal of \(R\), decreasing in \(h\). For \(k=\ell+m+1\), the savings satisfy \(h(k,\ell)\ge h\) if and only if \(I^{(h)}_m\) has a nonzero element of degree \(\ell\), that is, \(\ell\ge\ell_0(h,m):=\mathrm{indeg}\,I^{(h)}_m\). Hence \(h(k,\ell)=\max\{h:\ell_0(h,m)\le\ell\}\), and \(\kappa(m)=\max\{h:I^{(h)}_m\ne0\}\), with \(\mathrm{indeg}\,0=\infty\) and \(\max\emptyset=0\). For example, \(I^{(1)}_0=0\), since the equation for \(a=0\), \(t=0\) is \(W_0=0\).
The first order outside \(\mathrm{Ord}_n(h-1)\). Suppose \(\mathrm{Ord}_n(h-1)=\{0,\ldots,N-1\}\) with \(N=N_{2,n}(h-1)\), and take \(m=N\). Here \(\kappa(N)\ge h\), with equality when \(N\in\mathrm{Ord}_n(h)\); in that case we call \(m=N\) the \(\kappa\)-boundary. Then \(\mathrm{Sol}^{(h)}_m\) is free of rank 1, and \(\ell_0(h,m)=\deg\Delta_{n,h-1}-\deg g\), where \(g\) is the gcd of the \(N\times N\) minors of the constraint matrix.

An exact degree formula from the actual prefix cokernel
Theorem. Let \(k\) have characteristic zero, \(N\ge5\), \(S=k[c_2,\ldots,c_N]\), with \(\deg c_j=j\). Put \[ q=\binom{N-2}{2},\quad w=2N-1,\quad p=w+q=\binom N2+2,\quad \lambda=3\binom N4. \] In the free marked core \(F\), let \(v_i=D^iv\), of weight \(w+i\), and define the actual finite prefix \[ C^+=\operatorname{coker}\left[ E=\bigoplus_{i=0}^{q+1}S(-w-i)\xrightarrow{(v_0,\ldots,v_{q+1})}F\right]. \] Write \(e_N=\operatorname{indeg}\operatorname{Ext}^2_S(C^+,S)\), with initial degree \(+\infty\) for the zero module. Then the original first-step degree is \[ \boxed{\ \alpha_N:=\ell_0(3,p+1) =\min\{\lambda,\ \lambda+p+e_N\}\ }. \tag{ED} \] In particular \[ 2\le\alpha_N\le \min\left\{\lambda,\lambda+\binom N2-4N+7\right\}. \tag{UB} \] If the kernel of the natural surjection \(C^+\to D_{22}(-4)\) has codimension at least three, then \[ \alpha_N=\min\left\{\lambda,\lambda+\binom N2-4N+7\right\}. \tag{CE} \] The condition is sufficient, and is not assumed proved. It predicts twelve at \(N=5\), forty-three at \(N=6\), and \(\lambda_N\) for every \(N\ge7\).
