The [notebook entry](https://kbr-.github.io/math-research/#entry-2026-09-13-retained-source-scope-audit)
contains the full proof and dated correction of the recent MP source
application. This cycle needed no numerical computation.

The selected direct NS endpoint simulates an assumption-free theorem
proof. Over F2, formulas with no OR gates have exact affine approximations.
A theorem of that kind has approximation zero. An original level-one OR
tautology has affine inputs with no common Boolean zero, so their constant
span contains one. A false OR has all inputs identically zero. Existing
scalar cleanup therefore removes semantically constant level-one ORs,
including their proper copies, through every original NS/PC degree ceiling.

An original level-one MP implication has a level-zero antecedent. Hence
the recent theorem requiring an independent nonzero affine antecedent
does not apply nonvacuously to those boundaries in the direct theorem
simulation. Its conditional algebraic statement and finite examples remain
valid. The formal MP coefficient extraction remains correct as well.
The source-application claim, not those algebraic proofs, is corrected.

The retained hard level-one objects are nonconstant proper OR subformulas,
whose exact Boolean values are indicators of nonempty proper affine
subspaces. They can appear in comparisons, Booleanity certificates, MOD
value lines, and higher-level inputs. Their occurrence is not itself proof
that a nonzero cofactor survives all preprocessing; that remains to be
checked for the selected source representation.

The pair (u,v) and (u,u+v) illustrates the role: the OR functions agree,
but independent approximation products differ as ordinary polynomials.
The notebook writes their degree-4h difference certificate explicitly from
the existing prefix identity and a constant change of input basis. It does
not assume certificates for the individual, nonzero input differences.
