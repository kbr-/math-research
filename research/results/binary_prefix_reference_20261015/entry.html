Then \[ [P]_D\ne0,\qquad \ker\bigl(U_a\longrightarrow Q_D/[PV_1],\ x\longmapsto[P T x]_D\bigr)=C.\tag{1} \] Here \(G_a=TU_a\), the reference space \([PV_1]\) uses all original affine old polynomials, and the resulting weighted input image modulo that space has dimension \(p-k\). Consequently no such prefix supplies affine references for every original input of its parent, because \(p>h\ge k\). The coefficients of T are selected once before any row history, but may satisfy this stronger predicate instead of the old lexicographic predicate. Condition before exposing another direction
