Filtered and graded objects

Let \(B=\mathbb F_2[x_1,\ldots,x_n]/(x_j^2-x_j)\), filtered by multilinear degree. Its associated graded algebra is \(E=\mathbb F_2[x_1,\ldots,x_n]/(x_j^2)\). For \(1\le i\le m\), let \[ F_i=\prod_{j=1}^{r_i}a_{i,j},\qquad a_{i,j}=\ell_{i,j}+\epsilon_{i,j}, \quad\epsilon_{i,j}\in\mathbb F_2, \] where the binary linear forms within each row are independent and \(r_i\ge1\). Its degree-\(r_i\) top is the nonzero decomposable element \(\omega_i=\prod_j\ell_{i,j}\in E\). Set \(I_i=(\omega_1,\ldots,\omega_i)\subset E\), \(I_0=0\), and \[ K_t=\sum_i F_i B_{\le t-r_i}\subseteq B_{\le t}, \] with negative-degree coefficient spaces zero. These are static degree-bounded row spans; products are reduced in \(B\). Raw ordinary source and Boolean-certificate degrees are retained below.

Theorem (binary colon lifting). Let \(D\ge\max(2,r_1,\ldots,r_m)\). Suppose for each row \(i\), in all degrees \(0\le e\le D-r_i\), \[ (I_{i-1}:\omega_i)_e =\bigl(I_{i-1}+(\ell_{i,1},\ldots,\ell_{i,r_i})\bigr)_e. \tag{1} \] Then for every \(t\le D\), \(K_D\cap B_{\le t}=K_t\), and the filtered quotient through \(D\) has graded pieces \((E/I_m)_t\). Ordinary degree-\(D\) PC closure of the Boolean axioms and the rows, after multilinear reduction, equals \(K_D\); in particular it does not contain one.

A single-degree variant holds: if (1) is assumed only at \(e=D-r_i\) for each \(i\), then \(K_D\cap B_{\le D-1}=K_{D-1}\), the same PC-closure equality holds, and \(1\notin K_{D-1}\) is sufficient for degree-\(D\) nonrefutability. Earlier strictness or graded Hilbert equality is not asserted by this variant.
