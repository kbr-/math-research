Clustered flat batch bound

Work in the binary local algebra on \(m\) three-state pairs, with \(B_m(K)=\sum_{j\le K}2^j\binom mj\). Take \(q\ge1\) disjoint nonempty edge blocks \(S_i\), each of size at most \(d\ge1\), leaving other edges unused if desired. Block \(i\) carries at most \(L\ge1\) affine-flat indicators \(z_{i,j}\), each defined there by independent affine constraints of rank at least \(r\ge1\). Their supports and direction spaces within a block may overlap arbitrarily; empty local intersections are permitted. Let actual polynomial inputs of degree at most \(\delta\ge1\) have local values \(g_v=\ell_v+F_v(z)\), with homogeneous old-linear \(\ell_v\) and arbitrary Boolean feature functions. Constants are included in \(F_v\).

For integers \(1\le K<m/2\), \(s,H\ge1\), set \[\theta=\frac{K}{2(m-K)},\qquad a=1-\frac{K}{2m},\qquad \alpha=\min(1,La^r),\qquad t=s\left\lceil\log_2(q3^d+1)\right\rceil+1.\] Theorem. Some at most \(H+t\) constant binary rows in the actual input positions give a product \(P\) whose whole companion rank satisfies \[\frac{\operatorname{rank}(f\in V_K\mapsto(Pg_vf)_v)}{B_m(K)} \le (m+1)\Pr\{\operatorname{Bin}(q,\alpha)>s\} +(m+1)(q+1)^s(3/\theta)^{sd}a^H.\tag{1}\] For \(s\ge2eq\alpha\), the first term is at most \((m+1)2^{-s}\). In a binary degree-\(D\) PC pseudoideal containing the local axioms, weight degree at most \(b\), this local rank bounds the weighted quotient rank at \(b+K+(H+t+1)\delta\le D\), compared with the actual \(\dim[wV_K]\). Original source replay is separately retained.

Union Hilbert polynomial. On block \(i\), let \(U_i\) be the union of the supports of all its feature indicators. The inactive subspace is the kernel of restriction to \(U_i\), equivalently the intersection of their multiplication kernels. Choose a degree-adapted basis of that kernel and extend it to one of the full local algebra. Let \(A_i(u)\) be the polynomial counting added, active basis vectors by degree, and \(G_i(u)=(1+2u)^{|S_i|}\) the full count. If \(h_U(j)\) denotes the cumulative restriction rank through degree \(j\), restriction to the union embeds into the direct sum of restrictions to its flats, whence \(h_{U_i}(j)\le\sum_v h_{iv}(j)\) for every \(j\ge0\). Consequently, for \(0<\theta<1\), \[A_i(\theta)=(1-\theta)\sum_{j\ge0}h_{U_i}(j)\theta^j \le\sum_v A_{iv}(\theta).\tag{2}\] The series converge because these cumulative dimensions stabilize. This does not differentiate a cumulative inequality or add exact-degree ranks across different quotients.

For each constituent flat, the existing pivot-variable Hilbert bound gives \(A_{iv}(\theta)\le G_i(\theta)a^{r_{iv}}\). Thus \[0\le A_i(\theta)/G_i(\theta)\le\min(1,La^r)=\alpha.\tag{3}\] Tensor the degree-adapted block bases with ordinary bases on unused edges. A component with active set \(T\) is annihilated by every feature on blocks outside \(T\), hence supported where all those feature vectors are zero. Its complement need not be invariant under every multiplication. Evaluating the nonnegative degree polynomials at \(\theta\) makes the active choices independent for this counting argument. The degree-\(K\) dimension of components with \(|T|>s\) is at most \(B_m(K)(m+1)\Pr\{\operatorname{Bin}(q,\alpha)>s\}\), by the same binomial-mode bound as in the local-flat theorem. No probability measure on charged solutions is used.

Low affine rank. If the homogeneous span of \(\ell_v\) has rank below \(H\), select a basis using actual inputs as rows, forming \(Q\), and subtract their constant combinations from all other inputs. The resulting legal tuple \(g'\) is a function only of the feature vector, and agrees with \(g\) on \(Q=1\). In any block there are at most \(3^d\) attainable feature vectors. The number of vectors that are zero outside at most \(s\) blocks is at most \((q3^d+1)^s\): encode the nonzero blocks and their states in sorted padded lists. This upper bound may include fictitious zero block states; harmless extra patterns can be included in the code test. Uniform binary rows miss any fixed nonzero value of \(g'\) with probability \(2^{-t}\). The union bound is below one, so some \(t\) rows detect all those values. Express them in the original positions. Every low-active component is killed pointwise by the full companion map, even after multiplying by \(Q\). Its rank is therefore bounded by the first term of (1).

High affine rank. Otherwise select \(H\) actual inputs with independent homogeneous parts and take their complementary product \(P\). Its companion map factors through multiplication by \(P\). On high-active components charge the preceding tail. For any \(|T|\le s\), fix the local states of all edges in its active blocks. There are at most \(3^{sd}\) such states, with local one-hot selectors of degree at most \(sd\). On the support of each selector times a component vector, every feature is fixed, so \(P\) agrees there with the indicator of \(H\) independent old-affine equations. Those equations retain the raw old-linear parts; they are not projected to a coordinate system that preserves the charged quotient.

For a rank-\(H\) flat and a multiplier of degree at most \(K+sd\), evaluate its local pivot count at the original \(\theta\). The multiplication image is bounded by \[\theta^{-(K+sd)}(1+2\theta)^m a^H \le B_m(K)(m+1)\theta^{-sd}a^H.\] There are at most \((q+1)^s\) active sets and \(3^{sd}\) selectors per set. Summing these cumulative image bounds and the tail proves (1). If a flat misses the local domain its image is zero. All auxiliary decomposition and selector operations are local rank analysis, not additional PC proof lines.

Actual quotient transfer. The chosen actual companions have degree at most \((H+t+1)\delta\). Every local-zero relation among their degree-\(K\) columns has a local certificate at its actual degree by square reduction and pair exclusion. Weighting fits the stated window, and bounded absorption makes each map well-defined on \([wV_K]\). Thus the quotient rank is at most (1). If a finite family of these bounds sums to less than \(\dim[wV_K]\), their common kernel supplies a surviving multiplier. Constant coefficient rows kill fresh field images; all original companion and replay ceilings still apply. This proves the theorem.

A complete two-level range

Use the fixed growing-expansion charged family with \(\Delta=\lceil(\log n)^2\rceil\), \(n\Delta/8\le m\le n\Delta\), \(D=\lfloor n\Delta/64\rfloor\), \(K=\lfloor m/1024\rfloor\), and \(\log N=\Theta((\log n)^2)\). Fix \(A>0\), \(M\le N^A\). Take complete two-level sources with at most \(M\) affine bottoms and \(M\) parents. Each parent is affine in old variables and genuine bottom products, with no raw coefficient readers or parent dependencies. Within each parent, all its used nonconstant proper bottom flats can be grouped into disjoint coordinate blocks of size at most \(d=\lceil(\log n)^2\rceil\), with at most \(n\) features per block and ranks at least \(r=\lceil32768\log n\rceil\). Every proper bottom span, including unused bottoms, has rank at most \(2d\). Different parents may use different partitions; shared bottoms have one interpretation. Zero and unit bottoms give constants. Put \(h=\lceil(\log n)^5\rceil\), \(B=\lceil(\log n)^{11}\rceil\).

Every bottom and parent has the common original accuracy \(h\). Every bottom rank is at most \(2d<h\) eventually, so constant full-basis maps make all bottoms exact and kill their companions. Parent inputs have actual old degree at most \(2d\). Since \(K/m\ge1/2048\) eventually, \(La^r\le n\exp(-r/4096)\le n^{-7}\), and \(q\le m\) gives \(q\alpha=o(1)\). Set \[s=\lceil\log_2(8M(m+1))\rceil,\quad t=s\lceil\log_2(m3^d+1)\rceil+1,\] \[H=\left\lceil\frac{\log(8M(m+1))+s\log(m+1)+sd\log(3/\theta)}{-\log a}\right\rceil.\tag{4}\] The tail is at most \(1/(8M)\), as is the second term in (1). Also \(s=O_A(\log^2 n)\) and \(t,H=O_A(\log^4 n)\), so all rows fit the original \(h\). Summed parent ranks are at most \(B_m(K)/4\), whereas the actual charged dimension is at least \(B_m(K)/2\). A common \(W\in V_K\setminus J_D\) therefore kills every parent companion. The specialized reserve is \(K+(H+t+1)2d\le D\). Original two-level companions retain \(2h^2+3h<B\), and \(K+B<D\); a constant global map and weighted replay exclude every degree-\(B\) PC or NS source refutation. The graph, accuracy and source ceiling are independent of \(A\).

Nonvacuity. For unbounded large \(n\), partition edges into blocks of size \(d\), ignoring leftovers. Within a block use the invertible direction basis \(v_j=x_1+\cdots+x_j\). Choose \(n\) distinct rank-\(r\) subspaces spanned by \(r\)-subsets of this basis, each defining its homogeneous zero flat. Such subsets exist eventually since \(\binom dr\ge(d/r)^r>n\). These flats overlap at the all-zero local point and have noncoordinate, overlapping supports. Parents can use arbitrary old-affine terms and mixtures of all these features, and different parents can use different dictionaries. This is a legal unbounded source class, not a global solution of the charges.

For this supplied dictionary of \(L=n\) consistent flats on one block, the ungrouped high-rank profile from the union theorem is at least \(3^L a^{\max(H,2d)}\), because every union has rank at most \(2d\). For polylogarithmic \(H,d\) that upper-bound expression diverges. The grouped support estimate is instead at most \(La^r\le n^{-7}\). This compares these two sufficient estimates on the supplied representation; it does not rule out a different representation or prove that every old method fails on the source.
