# Graph existence source scope

Joel Friedman, *A Proof of Alon's Second Eigenvalue Conjecture and Related Problems*.
Primary author PDF: https://www.cs.ubc.ca/~jf/pubs/web_stuff/alon.pdf
Read 9 October 2026: Theorem 1.1, Corollary 1.4, model definitions and the
simple-graph discussion on PDF pages 2–5. No full-proof audit or local PDF copy.

The imported existence consequence is used only for fixed even degree and even
vertex counts: simple regular graphs can have second adjacency eigenvalue at most
2 sqrt(d-1)+1 for arbitrarily large counts. The source explicitly discusses the
uniform simple model after Corollary 1.4. The elementary Laplacian calculation and
the choice of sufficiently large fixed d are proved in our entry. Charges are
chosen independently with nonzero total; no odd-order restriction is needed by
the charged pseudoideal theorem used here.
