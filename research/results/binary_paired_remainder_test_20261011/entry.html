Paired residual kernel

Lemma. Over \(\mathbb F_2\), let \(J_D\) be a Boolean degree-\(D\) pseudoideal. Let \(q_i\) be old affine polynomials and \(H,r_i\) old binary polynomials, for \(1\le i\le m\), \(m\ge1\). Set \[ g_i=Hq_i+r_i,\quad g'_i=(1+H)q_i+r_i,\quad A_i=r_i+q_i r_1. \] Choose \(\delta\ge1\) with \(\deg H+1\le\delta\) and \(\deg r_i\le\delta\). Let \(\deg w\le b\), \(U\subseteq V_K\), and \(W=wq_1\). Select actual input position one in the first row of each complete block and zero all other coefficients, so its effective products are \(1+g_1\) and \(1+g'_1\); all fresh fields vanish. If \[ b+K+2\delta+2\le D, \] the two maps on \(U\) \[ f\longmapsto([Wfg_i(1+g_1)],[Wfg'_i(1+g'_1)])_i, \qquad f\longmapsto([WfA_i])_i \] have the same kernel and rank. Both factor through the same weighted image \([WU]\). Original source ceilings and earlier obligations remain separate application requirements; no degree is reset after specialization.

Proof. At a Boolean point with \(q_1=0\), every weighted expression vanishes. At a point with \(q_1=1\), one has \(g'_1=1+g_1\), \(g'_i=q_i+g_i\), and \(g_i=q_i g_1+A_i\). Consequently, modulo Booleanity, \[ Wfg_i(1+g_1)=WfA_i(1+g_1),\qquad Wfg'_i(1+g'_1)=WfA_i g_1, \] and their sum is \(WfA_i\). Every difference has degree at most \(b+K+2\delta+2\). Degree-nonincreasing Boolean division therefore certifies these identities at the stated ceiling. Both errors vanishing implies their sum vanishes. Conversely \(WfA_i\) has degree at most \(b+K+\delta+2\), so multiplication by either displayed factor fits \(D\), and bounded closure gives both errors. Multiplication of a relation \(Wf\in J_D\) by the error factors or by \(A_i\) fits the same window, establishing factorization through \([WU]\). Rank-nullity proves the equal-rank conclusion. \(\square\)

The residual on \(q_1=1\) is a proportionality defect: after multiplication by \(W\), it agrees with the minors \(q_1r_i+q_ir_1\). This description removes \(H\) from the formula, not from a proved quantitative cost bound.
