STAGE 1 BRIEF (goal and available results only; the route currently preferred is deliberately withheld)

GOAL. Superpolynomial proof-size lower bounds for every fixed depth of AC0[2]-Frege, through this chain.

(A) Source theorem (proved). Gamma: polynomials over F_2 in v Boolean variables with the Boolean axioms; C_D its
degree-D polynomial-calculus closure. If 1 is not in C_D for D = floor(n/2 - s), s = o(n/(log n)^(l-2)), l >= 2
the approximation depth, where n = v - rank of any set L of auxiliary affine equations added to Gamma (L need not
be part of the formula; v <= n^C), then a system augmented by polynomially many approximation blocks in l levels
has no PC refutation of degree n^(1/3). A polynomial-size family suffices if it is hard at half the dimension of
SOME affine subspace.
(B) Its engine (imported, audited) contracts error ranks at a rate that needs the cutoff at about half of a
dimension the closure cannot exceed.
(C) Missing: a polynomial-size unsatisfiable family meeting (A), and the transfer of an actual Frege proof into
the grammar of (A). The second has had no research cycle.

WHAT THE LAST SIX ENTRIES ESTABLISHED (all proved unless marked):
S1. Certificates. 1 is not in C_D iff there is a set G without constant, containing the axioms of degree <= D in
    its degree-D span, all of whose pairs with lcm of degree <= D have resolved S-polynomials. If the non-Boolean
    elements have pairwise coprime leading monomials and their Boolean pairs resolve, the system is satisfiable;
    so a certificate for an unsatisfiable system has an element of degree D or two overlapping elements of total
    degree >= D+2.
S2. Two jointly unsatisfiable relations are refuted by a constant combination of their canonical axioms (degree
    at most the larger width).
S3. Tool (tested against brute force on 3-variable systems): exact refutation degree by truncated completion.
    Measured: 3n random products of two affine forms have degree exactly 3 at n = 8, 10, 12, 16, three instances
    each; n = 20 did not finish (pure Python).
S4. Ceiling on a method: if every variable lies in >= 2 rows, any boundary expansion parameters (r, s, c) of the
    row-variable matrix satisfy rc <= v/2 + s, so Alekhnovich-Razborov's Theorem 3.13 (degree > rc/2) gives at
    most v/4 + s/2 and Theorem 3.8 at most 0.0335 v + s/4. Rows on variables only.
S5. Rows on k linear forms, one forbidden tuple each, any at most r rows independent (full "rank expansion"):
    satisfiable if n = kr (k >= 2, r >= 3); unsatisfiable needs more than r 2^k/4 rows and, for k >= 4,
    n >= 2k floor(r/2) + 0.156 floor(r/2) - log. A hypothetical theorem "degree > rk/2" for such rows (not
    proved; its leading-term proof step is known false) would then fall short of n/2 by about 0.02 n/k, which
    with polynomially many rows (k = O(log n)) is at least of order n/log n: enough for l = 2 only.
S6. Earlier, standing: a ceiling v/2 - (8/9) v/k for regular even modular sources with canonical rows of width k
    (so l = 2 only there too); fixed-width occurrence ceilings with linear deficit; no general ceiling near n/2
    for all unsatisfiable systems is recorded, and no example hard at n/2 - o(n) of any dimension.
