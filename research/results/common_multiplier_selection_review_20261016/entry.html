Extra requirements. Two demands go beyond what repair needs. First, a rank saving: lem:exact-common-kernel-selection shows that repair needs only the exact criterion, a protected class surviving on the common kernel, which can hold when no saving does. The user's goal names savings beyond summed ranks, so the statement keeps the saving and the review records that the exact criterion is the weaker sufficient endpoint. Second, the domination clause, “the common kernel being the annihilator of one element of the window”, which principal joint kernels left open. The readings its words allow are these (the reviewer's stage-one list, checked here against cor:generic-principal-kernel and lem:factor-ideal-domination): - Field: an element over the base field, or one over an extension. Over an extension, every reserve-window family and every domain-model family is excluded (cor:generic-principal-kernel (a), (c)). Only proper closures without the reserve stay admissible, so the endpoint becomes narrow and turns on an artefact of the method. - Degree of the element: at most \(e\), at most \(\sigma\) with \(K+\sigma+e\le D\), at most \(D-K\), or at most \(b\); the window of degree at most \(D-K\) contains the others, which are not otherwise ordered (\(b\le D-K\) since \(b+K\le D\)). Its example (d) is principal over \(\mathbb F_2\) at degree \(2=D-K\) and not at degree \(1\). - Equality against containment: under containment the clause becomes a common-killer condition, which overlaps the exclusion of products of killers. - Property against mechanism. As a property, the clause excludes every family whose kernel happens to be principal. As a mechanism, it excludes a saving whose proof only exhibits the common kernel as one element's annihilator and then bounds that element's rank by its own single count. The review adopts the mechanism reading, over any field, with equality. Under it the endpoint stays attainable and nontrivial in the reserve window of proper closures (domain models stay excluded by the support clause, every domain-model saving being a union-of-supports count by lem:domain-model-stacked-rank), and it excludes what “merely representing the same kernel” already aims at. A bound on one generic combination's rank counts only when it comes from the errors' joint structure and is neither a support count nor a product of killers. This keeps open the head item's next attempt on the linearity of Pang's choice. What can be weakened is the requirement that the saving hold on arbitrary families: a saving on the actual error families of one application is enough.
