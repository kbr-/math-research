The full-rank moment

Lemma (full-rank moment). Let \(V=\mathbb F_q^k\), let \(Q\) be finite-dimensional, and \(W\le V\otimes Q\). Take integers \(1\le t<k\) and choose \(C\) uniformly among full-row-rank \(t\times k\) matrices. For \(z\in W\), let \(\rho(z)\) be the rank of its contraction map \(Q^*\to V\), and put \(I_C=W\cap(\ker C\otimes Q)\). Define \[ \pi_r=\begin{cases}\displaystyle\prod_{j=0}^{r-1}\frac{q^{k-t}-q^j}{q^k-q^j},&0\le r\le k-t,\\0,&r>k-t,\end{cases}\qquad \pi_0=1. \] Then \[ Z=\mathbb E_C q^{\dim I_C}=\sum_{z\in W}\pi_{\rho(z)}, \qquad\min_C\dim I_C\le\lfloor\log_q Z\rfloor. \] If \(W\) contains \(v\otimes H\), where \(0\ne v\in V\), \(H\le Q\) and \(\dim H=m\), then \[ Z\ge1+(q^m-1)\pi_1\ge \pi_1q^m, \quad \log_q Z\ge m+\log_q\pi_1, \quad\pi_1=\frac{q^{k-t}-1}{q^k-1}>q^{-t-1}. \] No deficient matrix is used in these inequalities.

Proof. The kernel of a uniform full-rank matrix is uniform among the \((k-t)\)-dimensional subspaces of \(V\): every such kernel has the same number of surjective quotient maps to \(\mathbb F_q^t\). By transitivity on ordered independent \(r\)-tuples, the probability that a fixed \(r\)-space lies in this kernel is the number of ordered independent tuples in a \((k-t)\)-space divided by their number in \(V\). This is \(\pi_r\), and it is zero when \(r>k-t\). The event \(z\in I_C\) is exactly containment of its contraction image. Count elements of \(I_C\), then take expectation and interchange the finite sums. Some matrix has cardinality at most this expectation, proving the minimum bound.

In the indicated pure-direction subspace, every nonzero tensor has rank one, so the zero tensor and its \(q^m-1\) nonzero tensors give the lower bound on \(Z\). The second inequality uses \(\pi_1\le1\). Finally \(q^{k-t}-1\ge q^{k-t-1}\) for \(q\ge2\), \(k-t\ge1\), whereas \(q^k-1<q^k\), giving the strict stated bound. \(\square\)
