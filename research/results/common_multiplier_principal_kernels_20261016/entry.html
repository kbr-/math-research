General statement. cor:generic-principal-kernel: in the reserve window of lem:two-sided-frobenius-compression, and in every domain model with no reserve, a generic combination \(E(\lambda)=\sum_i\lambda_iE_i\) over a large enough extension has as annihilator the scalar extension of the common kernel, so a joint saving is one combination's rank deficit; over the base field no element of the errors' degree need do so, though one of higher degree may.

Generic principal kernel

Statement. In (a) and (b) polynomials and \(V_K\) follow the convention of lem:two-sided-frobenius-compression; in (c) \(V_K\) is all polynomials of degree at most \(K\); in (d), on the Boolean cube, polynomials are multilinear. Let \(J\) be an \(\mathbb F_p\) degree-\(D\) pseudoideal containing old Boolean or \(\mathbb F_p\)-domain consequences, \(w\) of degree at most \(b\) and \(E_1,\dots,E_r\) of degree at most \(e\), all with \(\mathbb F_p\) coefficients, \(U\le V_K\) an \(\mathbb F_p\)-subspace, \(M_i:U\to V_D/J\), \(u\mapsto[wE_iu]\), \(N=\bigcap_i\ker M_i\), and \(\rho\) the rank of \((M_i)_i\). (a) If \(b+K+e+(p-1)\min(K,e)\le D\), then for \(\mathbb F_q\supseteq\mathbb F_p\) with \(q>\rho\) and \(\lambda\in\mathbb F_q^r\) uniform, with probability at least \(1-\rho/q\), \(\{u\in U\otimes\mathbb F_q:wE(\lambda)u\in J\otimes\mathbb F_q\}=N\otimes\mathbb F_q\), and \(u\mapsto[wE(\lambda)u]\) has rank \(\rho\). (b) Under (a), \(\sum_i\operatorname{rank}M_i-\rho=\sum_i\operatorname{rank}M_i-\operatorname{rank}(u\mapsto[wE(\lambda)u])\). (c) Domain models, with no reserve: if \(J\) is the space of polynomials of degree at most \(D\) vanishing on a finite \(X\subseteq\mathbb F_p^n\), \(w=1\), \(K+e\le D\), and the vectors \((E_i(x))_i\), \(x\in S=\bigcup_i\operatorname{supp}E_i\), fall into \(h\) projective classes, then for \(q\ge h\) some \(\lambda\in\mathbb F_q^r\) has \(\operatorname{supp}E(\lambda)=S\), and for it the annihilator of \(E(\lambda)\) in \(U\otimes\mathbb F_q\) is \(N\otimes\mathbb F_q\). (d) Over \(\mathbb F_p\) itself no element of degree at most \(e\) need have annihilator \(N\): for \(p=2\), \(X=\{0,1\}^3\), \(D=4\), \(U=V_2\), \(E_1=x\), \(E_2=y\), \(N\) is spanned by \((1+x)(1+y)\), no \(g\) of degree at most \(1\) over \(\mathbb F_2\) has annihilator \(N\), and \(x+\omega y\) over \(\mathbb F_4\) does; the element \(x+y+xy\) of degree \(2=D-K\) over \(\mathbb F_2\) does too.

Proof. (a) Under the reserve, lem:two-sided-frobenius-compression gives the pencil \(\sum_it_iM_i\) over \(\mathbb F_p(t)\) the kernel \(N\otimes\mathbb F_p(t)\), so its rank is \(\dim U-\dim N=\rho\), and its specialization clause keeps the kernel for uniform \(\lambda\in\mathbb F_q^r\) with probability at least \(1-\rho/q\) (a nonzero \(\rho\times\rho\) minor is homogeneous of degree \(\rho\) in \(t\)). The specialization is \(u\mapsto[wE(\lambda)u]\) on \(U\otimes\mathbb F_q\) into \((V_D/J)\otimes\mathbb F_q\); its kernel always contains \(N\otimes\mathbb F_q\), and rank \(\rho\) forces equality. (b) follows. (c) Membership in \(J\) is vanishing on \(X\), and \(K+e\le D\) keeps every product \(E_iu\), \(u\in U\), within degree \(D\); so \(\ker M_i\) is the set of \(u\in U\) vanishing on \(\operatorname{supp}E_i\), and \(N\) the set vanishing on \(S\). For \(x\in S\), \(H_x=\{\lambda:\sum_i\lambda_iE_i(x)=0\}\) is a hyperplane of \(\mathbb F_q^r\), and it depends only on the projective class of \((E_i(x))_i\); \(h\) hyperplanes through \(0\) cover at most \(h(q^{r-1}-1)+1<q^r\) points when \(h\le q\), so some \(\lambda\) avoids all of them, and then \(\operatorname{supp}E(\lambda)=S\) (off \(S\) every \(E_i\) vanishes). The annihilator of \(E(\lambda)\) in \(U\otimes\mathbb F_q\) is then the set vanishing on \(S\), which is \(N\otimes\mathbb F_q\), since vanishing on \(S\) is a set of \(\mathbb F_p\)-linear conditions. (d) Here \(\dim V_2=7\), restriction from \(V_2\) to functions on the cube is injective, and by (c)'s description \(N\) is the set of \(u\in V_2\) vanishing off the two points \((0,0,z)\): \((1+x)(1+y)(\alpha+\beta z)\), of degree at most \(2\) only when \(\beta=0\). For \(g\) of degree at most \(1\) the annihilator is the set of \(u\in V_2\) vanishing on \(\operatorname{supp}g\). Over \(\mathbb F_2\) such \(g\) is zero, one, or the indicator of an affine half-space, of \(0\), \(8\) or \(4\) points: support \(0\) gives \(V_2\), support \(8\) gives \(0\), and a half-space is an affine square on which every function has degree at most \(2\), so restriction from \(V_2\) onto its four points is surjective and the annihilator has dimension \(3\). None is \(N\). The projective classes of \((x,y)\) on \(S\) are \((1,0),(0,1),(1,1)\), so \(h=3\) and (c) applies over \(\mathbb F_4\): \(x+\omega y\) takes the values \(1,\omega,1+\omega=\omega^2\) there. Finally \(x+y+xy\) is the indicator of \(S\) over \(\mathbb F_2\).

Hypotheses, each with its step. The reserve and the prime field are used only in (a), through lem:two-sided-frobenius-compression, whose proof needs them; \(q>\rho\) is its specialization bound. The \(\mathbb F_p\) coefficients of \(w\) and the \(E_i\) are that lemma's actual errors. In (c), \(K+e\le D\) is the step identifying kernels with vanishing; \(w=1\) only shortens the statement (a weight multiplies every map by the same vanishing condition on \(\operatorname{supp}w\)). (b) needs only (a).

Degenerate cases. \(r=1\): \(E(\lambda)=\lambda_1E_1\), trivially. \(\rho=0\): every \(u\) kills, and any \(\lambda\) works. \(N=0\): a combination whose annihilator is \(0\). Errors in \(J\): \(M_i=0\), and they drop out. \(q\le\rho\): (a) gives nothing, as in (d) at \(q=4<\rho=6\), where (c) still applies.

Nonvacuity. The example of (d) satisfies every hypothesis of (a) and (c): \(p=2\), the degree-\(4\) vanishing ideal of \(\{0,1\}^3\), which contains the Boolean domain consequences, \(w=1\), \(U=V_2\) of dimension \(7\), errors of degree \(1\) with \(\mathbb F_2\) coefficients, \(0+2+1+1\cdot1=4\le D\), individual ranks \(4\) and \(4\), stacked rank \(\rho=6\), a saving of \(2\); (a) applies over \(\mathbb F_8\) and larger, (c) over \(\mathbb F_4\). No parameter ledger applies: the witness verifies no budget of the goal.

Endpoint consequence. joint-error-savings lists excluded kinds of saving, among them “domination, the common kernel being the annihilator of one element of the window”. Two readings go to the next scheduled review. As a property of a family's kernel, over an extension, the clause excludes every family in the reserve window by (a), and every domain-model family by (c), so the endpoint would be unattainable there by its wording; read at the base field it depends on the degree allowed to the element: at the errors' degree \(e\), and at the degree \(\sigma\) with \(K+\sigma+e\le D\) that lem:factor-ideal-domination allows, which in (d) is \(\sigma\le1=e\), it is a genuine restriction, as (d) shows; at degree \(D-K\), which needs only \(K+\deg g\le D\), (d)'s family is excluded by \(x+y+xy\), directly by vanishing. As a mechanism, a saving obtained only by exhibiting one annihilator, the clause leaves families in the reserve window admissible when a bound comes from elsewhere, and (a) is the reformulation it rules out. The entry does not choose between them.
