Conventions and source audit
Use free associative polynomials over \(\mathbb F_2\). Addition and ordered multiplication have unbounded fan-in; size counts every gate, input occurrence and wire in the expression tree. This is essential: a long product has one layer here, whereas its binary-fan-in depth grows logarithmically. Certificates are sums of products containing exactly one placeholder per term, possibly with old-variable factors on both sides. They need not have the left-coefficient-only form of commutative Hilbert-like IPS.

The recorded parity correction is retained: with falsity translation \(t\), use \(t(x)=1-x\), \(t(\neg A)=1-t(A)\), ordered \(t(\bigvee_i A_i)=\prod_i t(A_i)\), and standard odd parity \(t(\bigoplus_{i=1}^r A_i)=1+r+\sum_i t(A_i)\). In particular empty parity translates to one. The source below is exactly these sequent rules, with optional explicit exchange and contraction; no new presentation equivalence is assumed.
Shallow Boolean commutator certificates
Lemma. Let old-variable arithmetic formulas \(P,Q\) over \(\mathbb F_2\) have total size at most \(M\ge2\) and depth at most \(h\). The polynomials \([P,Q]=PQ-QP\) and \(P^2-P\) have placeholder-linear free-algebra certificates from variable commutators and, for Booleanity, \(x_i^2-x_i\), of size \(O(M^8)\) and depth at most \(h+2\). More precisely they can be written as polynomially many ordered products, each containing one generator and otherwise only constants and subformulas of \(P,Q\); replacing that generator by its placeholder gives the stated depth. No arbitrary semantic identity is asserted to have such a certificate.
Commutator proof. Expand recursively with \[ [P_1+P_2,Q]=[P_1,Q]+[P_2,Q],\quad [P_1\cdots P_r,Q]=\sum_i P_1\cdots P_{i-1}[P_i,Q]P_{i+1}\cdots P_r, \] and the analogous second-argument identities, notably \([P,Q_1Q_2]=[P,Q_1]Q_2+Q_1[P,Q_2]\). Constants commute. Terminal terms are signed variable commutators, converted to the fixed \(i<j\) orientation or zero. There is at most one term for each ordered pair of variable-leaf occurrences, hence at most \(M^2\) terms. Each term's surrounding factors are subformulas on the two leaf paths, with total copied size bounded by \(O(M^2)\). Flatten their products in their inherited order, never commute them. This gives size \(O(M^4)\), one outer product layer and one sum layer.
Booleanity proof. At a variable use its Boolean generator; binary constants give zero. For a sum \(P=\sum_i P_i\), the exact identity in characteristic two is \[ P^2-P=\sum_i(P_i^2-P_i)+\sum_{i<j}[P_i,P_j]. \] For a product \(P=P_1\cdots P_r\), first transform the factor word \(PP\) to \(P_1^2\cdots P_r^2\) by at most \(r^2\) adjacent swaps, paying each difference with the preceding commutator construction. Then use \[ \prod_iP_i^2-\prod_iP_i =\sum_i\left(\prod_{j<i}P_j^2\right)(P_i^2-P_i)\left(\prod_{j>i}P_j\right). \] Recursively insert each child Booleanity certificate once in this last sum. Each formula node contributes at most \(O(M^4)\) terminal commutator terms under the coarse bounds \(r\le M\) and \(M^2\) terms per commutator. There are at most \(M\) nodes, so at most \(O(M^5)\) terminal terms. Ancestor contexts add only subformula factors, with at most \(O(M^2)\) factors per term and size at most \(M\) per factor. Thus \(O(M^8)\) bounds the entire expanded certificate tree. Products such as \(P_j^2\) mean two copies of the same old subformula; flatten them as factors. Every old factor has depth at most \(h\), proving the \(h+2\) bound. \(\square\)
Nonvacuity. Ordered products and sums of arbitrarily many distinct variables are allowed, so the lemma covers unbounded size at fixed depth. Degenerate cases. Constants and identical variable commutators give the zero certificate. Over \(\mathbb F_4\), the constant \(\omega\) has \(\omega^2-\omega=1\), so arbitrary extension-field constants cannot satisfy this Booleanity assertion. Omitting commutators already fails for \((x+y)^2-(x+y)\) in the free algebra.
Shallow noncommutative sequent simulation
Theorem. Let a size-\(S\ge2\) DAG refutation in the binary sequent presentation just specified have formula depth at most \(d\), where antecedent formulas are counted after negation as in the source. Size counts all formula-symbol and sequent occurrences. Let \(F\) be its original CNF clause-falsity polynomials in a fixed literal order, together with all Boolean and variable-commutator generators. There is a placeholder-linear noncommutative IPS formula \(C\) over \(\mathbb F_2\) of size \(O(S^{12})\) and unbounded-fan-in depth at most \(d+10\), satisfying formally \(C(X,0)=0\), \(C(X,F)=1\). The constants are absolute for this fixed rule presentation. The generous exponent and depth allowance are not claimed sharp.
Local rule identities. Translate a cedent to the ordered product of its right-hand formula falsities and the complements of its left-hand formula falsities. Write its polynomial as \(L_i\), in topological proof order, with \(L_T=1\) for the empty final cedent. Every translated formula has depth at most \(d+1\), every cedent at most \(d+2\), and total translation size \(O(S)\). Reordering cedent factors is paid by adjacent commutators and the lemma. For a common ordered context product \(H\), put \(a=t(A_1)\), \(b=t(\bigvee_{j>1}A_j)\), or \(b=t(\bigoplus_{j>1}A_j)\), as appropriate. The complete nontrivial identities are \[ (1-a)H+aH=H,\qquad (1-a)H+a(1-b)H=(1-ab)H, \] \[ (1-a)bH+a(1-b)H=(a+b)H,\qquad (1-a)(1-b)H+abH=(1+a+b)H. \] They cover cut, OR-left and the two parity rules without commuting \(a,b\). Weakening multiplies by the added factor, with reordering paid if necessary; negation and OR-right are identical after factor grouping and ordering. The logical axiom \(A\to A\) is \((1-t(A))t(A)=-(t(A)^2-t(A))\), supplied by the lemma. Empty-disjunction and negated-empty-parity axioms translate to zero. Contraction is a contextual multiple of \(P^2-P\), and exchange a contextual commutator. Original input clauses map to their own placeholders, after a paid literal permutation if their written order differs. These operations yield, for every non-input line, an exact identity \[ L_i=R_i+\sum_{j<i}a_{ij}L_jb_{ij}, \] with at most two premise terms for the displayed rules, where \(R_i\) has an explicit shallow Boolean/commutator certificate. For the supplied structural rules there is one premise. The multipliers are products of old translated subformulas and constants, and every ordering correction is paid; no certificate for an earlier proof-line is substituted recursively.
Global prefix construction. Set \(P_0=1\), \(P_i=\prod_{j=1}^i(1-L_j)\), with this exact order. Since \(L_T=1\), the telescoping identity is \[ 1=\sum_{i=1}^T P_{i-1}L_i. \] For an input occurrence, write \(L_i=F_k+R_i\), where \(F_k\) is the original clause in its fixed order and \(R_i\) has the paid literal-permutation commutator certificate (zero if the orders agree). Insert its original placeholder plus that correction certificate. For a logical axiom or correction \(R_i\), insert its given certificate. For a premise term \(P_{i-1}aL_jb\), write \(P_{i-1}=U(1-L_j)V\), where \(U,V\) are the preceding and following ordered prefix factors. The exact free-algebra absorption identity is \[ U(1-L_j)VaL_jb =-U(L_j^2-L_j)Vab+U(1-L_j)[Va,L_j]b. \] The lemma supplies both certificates on the right. Thus every summand of one is expressed from the original inputs, Boolean generators and commutators. Replacing their occurrences by the corresponding placeholders gives the required \(C\); all terms contain exactly one placeholder. The proof DAG is never unrolled, and a premise may be used by arbitrarily many later lines.
Cost ledger. There are at most \(S\) lines and \(O(S)\) premise occurrences. Original translations, context products and each \(Va\) above have size \(O(S^2)\) under a loose copied-input bound, but more sharply \(O(S)\): one prefix uses a subset of line translations whose aggregate size is \(O(S)\), and each local multiplier has size \(O(S)\). A lemma application therefore has size \(O(S^8)\). At most \(O(S^3)\) local factor swaps over all lines and at most \(O(S)\) absorption applications, with their context copies, fit \(O(S^{12})\). Each factor \(1-L_j\) has depth at most \(d+3\), a prefix product or \(Va\) at most \(d+4\). A lemma certificate has depth at most \(d+6\); outer context multiplication and the final sum add at most two, below the stated \(d+10\). Products of adjacent factors are flattened in their given order. There is no conversion of unbounded products to binary gates at constant depth, no extension-field descent, and no low-PC-degree conclusion. \(\square\)
Nonvacuity. Contradictory unit inputs \(x\) and \(1-x\) with one cut give a refutation; arbitrary dummy variables and repeated legal weakenings give unbounded \(S\). Non-tree proof reuse is explicitly allowed. Degenerate cases. A unit input gives its one-placeholder certificate; zero polynomial lines cause no problem. Without \(L_T=1\), the telescope is \(1-P_T\), not one. On a satisfiable CNF all valid cedents vanish at a satisfying Boolean assignment, so the final empty cedent cannot occur. The construction cannot refute every formula irrespective of proof existence.
