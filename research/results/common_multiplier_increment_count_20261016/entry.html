General statement. thm:increment-count-exclusion is Braun's affine exclusion with the common-kernel count replaced by the increment bound of lem:restriction-rank-overlap along any ordering of covering subspaces; it contains the published theorem and thm:shared-span-affine-exclusion, and it improves the rank-aware sum of some cover by at most a factor \(\max_c(d_c+k)/k\). prop:common-kernel-ceiling gives, at each degree \(D\), coordinate blocks whose flats have no common nonzero kernel in \(\mathcal L_{k'}\) for every \(k'\) that \(D\) allows, with \(\ln M\) about \(3K_D^2/m+\ln(K_D\ln v)\), against the published \(\ln(4M)\le K_D^2/m\).

Increment count exclusion

Setting. As in shared-span affine exclusion: Braun's \(\mathbb F_2\) one-level complete accuracy-\(h\) old-affine family over \(\mathcal Q_{m,\ell}\), \(n=2^\ell\), \(h=3\ell\), \(v=m\ell\), the row-linear space \(\mathcal L_k\), \(B=k(D+1)\), and ideals generated by affine spans in \(\mathbb F_2[x]\).

Statement. Let \(k\ge1\). Let \(T_1,\ldots,T_C\) be subspaces of old affine polynomials with \(1\notin T_c\), of dimensions \(t_c\), such that the span of every proper block of rank greater than \(h(k+1)\) contains some \(T_c\). Order them and choose parents \(b(c)<c\). Put \(d_c=v-t_c\), and \(e_c=\dim(T_c+T_{b(c)})-t_c\) when \(1\notin T_c+T_{b(c)}\). Define

\[N=\binom{d_1+k}{k}+\sum_{c\ge2}\min\Bigl\{\binom{d_c+k}{k},\;e_c\binom{d_c+k-1}{k-1}\Bigr\},\tag{1}\]

where the term is \(\binom{d_c+k}{k}\) when \(1\in T_c+T_{b(c)}\). If \(N<\dim\mathcal L_k\), \(D\ge2h+1\), \(2B-1\le n\) and \(4(k-1)<n\), the augmented system has no ordinary-PC refutation through degree \(D\). If moreover every \(t_c>h(k+1)\) and \(k\le m\), then with \(d=v-r_*\), \(r_*=3\ell(k+1)+1\), it suffices that

\[m\ln(4C_{\rm inc})\le k^2,\qquad C_{\rm inc}=1+\sum_{c\ge2}\min\Bigl\{1,\frac{e_ck}{d+k}\Bigr\},\tag{2}\]

with the term \(1\) when \(1\in T_c+T_{b(c)}\).

Proof. With no proper high-rank block take \(f=1\). Otherwise discard every \(T_c\) contained in no proper high-rank span; this only removes terms of (1). Since \(1\notin T_c\), each flat \(W_c\) is nonempty of dimension \(d_c\). By (2) and (4) of lem:restriction-rank-overlap with \(U=\mathcal L_k\) and the ideals \((T_c)\), the joint restriction rank to the \(W_c\) is at most \(N\): the first ideal costs \(r_{\mathcal L_k}((T_1))\le\binom{d_1+k}{k}\), and each later one adds at most its increment over its parent, bounded by \(e_c\binom{d_c+k-1}{k-1}\) through the full degree-\(k\) space, or by its own rank \(\binom{d_c+k}{k}\). If \(N<\dim\mathcal L_k\) a nonzero \(f\in\mathcal L_k\) has zero restriction to every \(W_c\). The coverage, cofactor, weighted-removal and separation steps are those of the shared-span proof verbatim. For (2), \(t_c>h(k+1)\) gives \(d_c\le d\), and both \(\binom{d_c+k}{k}\) and \(\binom{d_c+k-1}{k-1}\) increase with \(d_c\), so \(N\le C_{\rm inc}\binom{d+k}{k}\) using \(\binom{d+k-1}{k-1}=\frac{k}{d+k}\binom{d+k}{k}\). The published comparison, which uses \(k\le m\), gives \(\binom{d+k}{k}\le e^{-k^2/m}\binom mk\ell^k\), and \(e^{-k^2/m}\le1/(4C_{\rm inc})\) gives \(N\le\frac14\binom mk\ell^k<\dim\mathcal L_k\).

Containment. Every term of \(C_{\rm inc}\) is at most \(1\), so \(C_{\rm inc}\le C\) and (2) is implied by the condition \(m\ln(4C)\le k^2\) of thm:shared-span-affine-exclusion, after the same discarding of unused subspaces; and (1) is at most the shared-span sum \(\sum_c\binom{d_c+k}{k}\). Taking each \(T_c\) to be a block span gives the published theorem.

Limit of the increment count. For any cover and any choice of parents, let \(S\) consist of index \(1\) and every \(c\) whose parent is not contained in \(T_c\). Every \(c\notin S\) contains its parent, so following parents it contains some member of \(S\); hence \(S\) is again a cover. For \(c\in S\), \(c\ge2\), either \(e_c\ge1\) or the term is \(\binom{d_c+k}{k}\), so each such term of (1) is at least \(\frac{k}{d_c+k}\binom{d_c+k}{k}\), and so is the first. So \(N\ge\min_c\frac{k}{d_c+k}\sum_{c\in S}\binom{d_c+k}{k}\): the increment count is at least a factor \(\min_c k/(d_c+k)\) times the rank-aware shared-span sum of the cover \(S\). When that sum is about \(M\) times one binomial, this is an additive \(\ln\max_c((d_c+k)/k)\) in \(\ln M\). It bounds what (1) can certify, not the true joint rank.

Nonvacuity. The increment ledger is met at \(\ell=16\) by a chain of \(19\) windows: block \(a\), \(1\le a\le19\), has as inputs the \(r_*\) consecutive bits \(x_a,\ldots,x_{a+r_*-1}\). Each span is a proper coordinate span of rank \(r_*>h(k+1)\); consecutive windows have \(e_a=1\) and sum without one. Two distinct windows meet in dimension at most \(r_*-1=h(k+1)\), so at this \(k\) no subspace of dimension above the threshold lies in two of them, and the shared-span count is \(C=19\). The saved check kernel_count_numbers.json gives \(k=302\), \(d=1034047\), \(k/(d+k)\approx2.92\cdot10^{-4}\), largest admissible chain length \(19\) with margin \(k^2-m\ln(4C_{\rm inc})\approx6.90\), and the published condition false already at \(M=2\). Degree \(D=107\) as in the shared-span entry. This witness shows that the hypotheses can hold; it is not a strict gain. At \(k'=k+1=303\) these windows are low-rank, so both the published and the shared-span counts exclude the family through the same degree.

Ceiling on the common-kernel step

Statement. In the same setting fix a degree \(D\ge2h+1\) and let \(K_D=\lfloor(n+1)/(2(D+1))\rfloor\), the largest \(k'\) with \(2k'(D+1)-1\le n\); assume \(K_D\ge1\). Put \(r_D=h(K_D+1)+1\), assume \(r_D+K_D<v\), and let \(q_D=\exp\bigl(-r_DK_D/(v-r_D-K_D)\bigr)\) and \(M_D=\lfloor K_D\ln(v+1)/q_D\rfloor+1\). There is a family of at most \(M_D\) distinct proper blocks, each with inputs \(r_D\) distinct old bits, such that for every \(k'\) with \(1\le k'\le K_D\), that is every \(k'\) the published theorem's degree condition \(2k'(D+1)-1\le n\) allows at this \(D\): every block has rank above \(h(k'+1)\), and no nonzero \(f\in\mathcal L_{k'}\) has zero ordinary restriction to all their flats. So at degree \(D\) the common-kernel step of the published proof, with any certificate of the joint rank, fails for this family at every \(k'\) its other conditions could allow. Here

\[\ln M_D\le\ln\bigl(K_D\ln(v+1)+1\bigr)+\frac{r_DK_D}{v-r_D-K_D},\qquad\frac{r_DK_D}{v-r_D-K_D}=\frac{3K_D^2}{m}\Bigl(1+O\Bigl(\frac1{K_D}+\frac{K_D}{m}\Bigr)\Bigr),\tag{3}\]

while the published theorem at degree \(D\) admits at most \(\ln(4M)\le K_D^2/m\), since it needs \(m\ln(4M)\le k'^2\) with \(k'\le K_D\).

Proof. For \(k'\le K_D\), \(h(k'+1)\le h(K_D+1)<r_D\) and \(\mathcal L_{k'}\subseteq\mathcal L_{K_D}\); it suffices to make the joint kernel in \(\mathcal L_{K_D}\) zero. For a set \(P\) of bits, ordinary restriction to \(x_P=0\) sends a row-linear monomial meeting \(P\) to zero and the others injectively to distinct monomials, so its kernel on \(\mathcal L_{K_D}\) is spanned by the row-linear monomials meeting \(P\); the joint kernel of several is spanned by those meeting every \(P_a\), since an intersection of spans of subsets of one basis is the span of their intersection. The constant meets nothing, so the joint kernel is zero exactly when no nonconstant row-linear monomial of degree at most \(K_D\), a set \(X\) of at most \(K_D\) bits, meets every \(P_a\). Choose \(P_1,\ldots,P_{M_D}\) independently and uniformly among the \(r_D\)-subsets of the \(v\) bits. For \(|X|=j\le K_D\),

\[\Pr[X\cap P=\varnothing]=\prod_{i=0}^{r_D-1}\Bigl(1-\frac{j}{v-i}\Bigr)\ge\Bigl(1-\frac{K_D}{v-r_D}\Bigr)^{r_D}\ge q_D,\tag{4}\]

using \(\ln(1-x)\ge-x/(1-x)\) at \(x=K_D/(v-r_D)\). So \(X\) meets all the sets with probability at most \(e^{-q_DM_D}\). The number of nonconstant row-linear monomials of degree at most \(K_D\) is below \(\dim\mathcal L_{K_D}\le\sum_jv^j/j!\le(v+1)^{K_D}\), and \(q_DM_D>K_D\ln(v+1)\), so by the union bound some choice has zero joint kernel. Repeated sets may be removed; inputs are linear forms, so each block is proper of rank \(r_D\). For the logarithm, \(M_D\le(K_D\ln(v+1)+1)/q_D\) since \(q_D\le1\). For the asymptotic form, \(r_DK_D=3\ell K_D^2(1+1/K_D+1/(3\ell K_D))\) and \(v-r_D-K_D=m\ell(1-O(K_D/m))\).

At \(\ell=16\). The saved run output kernel_count_numbers_run.txt gives, at \(D=107\): \(K_D=303\), \(r_D=14593\), \(\ln M_D\le12.62\) against the published \(K_D^2/m\approx1.40\); at \(D=97=2h+1\): \(K_D=334\), \(r_D=16081\), \(\ln M_D\le13.64\) against \(1.70\). The ratios, about \(9.0\) and \(8.0\), are dominated at this size by the logarithmic term; in a regime where \(K_D^2/m\) grows faster than \(\ln(K_D\ln v)\), such as the published polylogarithmic one, the ratio tends to \(3\).

Degenerate cases. In (1), a single covering subspace gives \(N=\binom{d_1+k}{k}\); \(e_c=0\) is the case \(T_{b(c)}\subseteq T_c\), handled by the cover \(S\) above; \(1\in T_c+T_{b(c)}\) gives the plain term. With no proper high-rank block the theorem takes \(f=1\). In the ceiling statement \(r_D+K_D<v\) keeps (4) positive, and \(K_D\ge1\) is needed for any \(k'\) to exist; the blocks are constructed, so the statement is not vacuous.

New mathematics. Braun's exclusion with the common-kernel count paid by span increments along any ordering of covering subspaces, containing the published and shared-span counts, together with the proof that this count is at least a factor \(\min_ck/(d_c+k)\) times the rank-aware sum of some cover. A first-moment family of coordinate blocks with no common row-linear kernel at any degree parameter allowed by a fixed degree \(D\), so that at degree \(D\) no certificate of the common-kernel step extends the admissible \(\ln M\) beyond \(3K_D^2/m\,(1+o(1))+\ln(K_D\ln(v+1)+1)\), against the published \(K_D^2/m\). Both proofs are elementary: the first composes lem:restriction-rank-overlap with the published ledger, the second is a union bound over row-linear monomials.

Selection gain. The kernel count can be paid per unit of span distance from a parent, but this gains at most a factor \(\max_c(d_c+k)/k\) over the rank-aware sum of some cover. At a fixed degree \(D\), any improvement of the joint-rank certificate in the common-kernel step reaches at most \(\ln M\approx3K_D^2/m+\ln(K_D\ln v)\) on unrestricted families, a factor tending to \(3\) over the published reach where \(K_D^2/m\) dominates, and about \(8\) to \(9\) at \(\ell=16\), where the logarithmic term dominates. Larger gains for Braun's adapter need structure of the family, such as shared subspaces, or a step other than the common kernel.
